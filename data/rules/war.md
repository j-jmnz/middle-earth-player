“Wrath and Ruin” is played with an encounter deck built with all the cards from the following encounter sets: Wrath and Ruin, Easterling Raiders, and City of Rhûn. (Easterling Raiders and City of Rhûn can be found in A Shadow in the East deluxe expansion to The Lord of the Rings: The Card Game.)
Controlling Locations
Wrath and Ruin is a battle to liberate the ancient capital of Gondor from the forces of Mordor. To represent Gondor’s struggle to drive the enemy from every last corner of the ruined city, the players are instructed to take control of locations when they leave play.
Stage 1B and 2B both read: “Forced: When a location is explored, the first player takes control of it.” To take control of a location, the first player removes all progress from the just-explored location and places it in front of him in his play area, instead of discarding it. Locations under any player’s control are still in play. Their game text is active and they can be affected by card effects.
Losing Control of a Location
There are many encounter card effects that force players to return locations they control to the staging area. Additionally, some of the locations have triggered effects that will cause players to return them to the staging area.
When a player returns a location he controls to the staging area, he loses control of that location and removes all progress from it. If a player is eliminated from the game, each location controlled by that player is returned to the staging area.
