# Celebrimbor's Secret

The Ring-maker part 8 (pack `CS`), standalone only, on Easy and Normal (Q04.8, E04.8). Encounter sets Celebrimbor's Secret, Misty Mountain Orcs and Broken Lands (`rm.setup-celebrimbors-secret`; the two other sets are from The Voice of Isengard). The rulesheet defines the Scour keyword (`rm.scour`, with its example) and The Orcs' Search (`rm.the-orcs-search`).

- **Setup.** Stage 1A prints "Add Bellach, The Orcs' Search, and The Secret Chamber to the staging area. Attach Celebrimbor's Mould to The Secret Chamber. Each player adds a different Ost-in-Edhil location to the staging area. Shuffle the encounter deck." (`08122:A:1`). The Setup reader gives a staging step, an attach step and the location step for each player (trait Ost-in-Edhil).
- **Piles.** The staging pile holds Bellach (08124), The Orcs' Search (08125), The Secret Chamber (08126) and the Ost-in-Edhil locations (Ruined Plaza 08130, Collapsed Tower 08131, Ancient Foundation 08132, City Remains 08133). DragnCards stages Celebrimbor's Mould (08127), but the Setup attaches it to The Secret Chamber, so an override (`scenarioPiles`) puts it in the set-aside pile. The quest pile holds two stages.
- **Errata.** None for a card of this scenario.

## Data

- Stage 1B and 2B print Time 3 and trigger each Scour effect currently in play when the last counter leaves (`08122:B:2`, `08123:B:2`). Bellach is immune to player card effects and cannot be engaged until stage 2B holds 12 progress.
- Scour is printed in bold on Bellach, Bellach's Scout, Prowling Orc, Collapsed Tower and Ancient Foundation (`rm.scour`), and four cards of the scenario read "underneath The Orcs' Search". Scour is not in the `keywords` of `data/cards`; the records name it by the lines `08124:A:2`, `08128:A:2`, `08129:A:2`, `08131:A:2` and `08132:A:1`.
- Bellach (08124) prints X as the number of cards underneath The Orcs' Search.

## Readings

The statuses are those of the Readings bullet of `docs/scenarios/the-ring-maker.md`. Each key is the scan-read ledger line (`data/printed/the-ring-maker.json`), `<code>:<side>:<n>`.

- Scour (span-settled). The keyword does nothing alone; when a quest card or encounter card tells the players to trigger a scour effect, they resolve the effect that follows the keyword on each card named, in an order the first player chooses; each player resolves per-player effects ("Each player must search ...") in player order. A scour effect on a card in the discard pile triggers only when a card says so (`08133:A:1`). Not taken: the effect triggers by itself. Basis: `rm.scour` and its example (Bellach and Collapsed Tower).
- "Trigger each Scour effect currently in play" (`08122:B:2`, `08123:A:1`, `08123:B:2`, `08134:A:1`) (span-settled). Each card in play with the keyword is a trigger, whether it is in the staging area, engaged or the active location. Basis: `rm.scour`.
- `08133:A:1` (span-settled). "Trigger the topmost Scour effect in the discard pile, if able": the card nearest the top of the encounter discard pile that has the keyword; the card stays in the discard pile and its effect is resolved as if it were in play. With no such card nothing happens. Not taken: the effect of the first card found from the bottom. Basis: `rm.scour` (the effect that follows the keyword on that card) and the printed word "topmost", which in a discard pile is the card discarded last. Sources: none found for this card; https://rulespal.com/the-lord-of-the-rings-the-card-game-celebrimbors-secret/rulebook and https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/06/30/celebrimbors-secret/ give the rulesheet text only.
- The Orcs' Search (`08125:A:1`, `08125:A:2`) (span-settled). A location with damage equal to its printed quest points goes facedown underneath The Orcs' Search; any tokens and attachments on a card are discarded first; a card there is not in play and only interacts with effects that name the cards underneath. Each card underneath raises each player's threat by 1 at the end of the refresh phase. Basis: `rm.the-orcs-search`.
- Damage on a location (`08130:A:1`, `08131:A:2`, `08132:A:1`, `08128:A:2`, `08129:A:1`, `08135:A:1`) (span-settled). Damage placed on a location is tracked on the location and counts for the check of `08125:A:1`. Basis: `rm.the-orcs-search`, `rr.damage`.
- `08126:A:3` (span-settled). If The Secret Chamber is placed underneath The Orcs' Search the players lose the game. Basis: the printed line.
- `08127:A:1`, `08127:A:2` (span-settled). The first player claims Celebrimbor's Mould when it is free of encounters and attaches it to a hero; if that hero leaves play the players lose. The stage cannot advance unless it is attached to a hero. Basis: the printed lines.
- `08123:A:1` (span-settled). Stage 2A makes Bellach attack the player who controls the Mould, then attaches the Mould to Bellach. Basis: the printed line.
- `08123:B:3` (span-settled). Bellach is immune to player card effects and cannot be engaged unless stage 2B holds at least 12 progress; the players cannot win unless the Mould is attached to a hero. Basis: the printed line. The ledger corrects "at last" to "at least" and keeps the two sentences as separate lines.
- `08132:A:1` (span-settled). Ancient Foundation's scour assigns X damage among locations in play, X being the number of locations in play; two copies in play assign once. Basis: `rm.does-not-stack`.
- `08136:A:1`, `08135:A:2`, `08130:A:2`, `08133:A:2`, `08134:A:2` (span-settled). X is the number of cards underneath The Orcs' Search (or the cost of the destroyed ally for `08135:A:2`), counted when the shadow effect resolves. Basis: the printed lines.
- Data cross-check against the pinned scans (`.cache/scans/08126.A.jpg`, `08124.A.jpg`). The Secret Chamber prints Threat X (the number of players), quest points 6 and Victory 3: `data/cards` questPoints 6 is right and its threat field is null because X is not a number. The check of The Orcs' Search for the Chamber is therefore 6 damage; X is its Threat. Bellach prints Threat X (the cards underneath The Orcs' Search), attack 5, defense 4, health 7, engagement cost 50, Victory 5, as the data lists. No override is needed. Both X threats are counted live, in the staging area (`rr.the-letter-x`).

  - `08125:A:1` (researched): a location goes underneath when its damage is equal to or more than its printed quest points, checked as damage is placed. Not taken: exactly equal.
  - `08122:A:1` (span-settled): Setup adds the Plaza in player order; Ruined Plaza's Forced is heard at Setup (`rr.enters-play`) and on a return to the staging area (`recommended`: no source names it).
  - `08122:B:2` (span-settled): the set of Scour cards is fixed when the trigger starts; the first player orders them, per-player effects run in player order.
  - `08124:A:2` (`recommended`): each player chooses the Orc he finds; the house rule of the Mines of Moria file (highest threat, attack, nearest the top) applies where the engine chooses.
  - `08122:B:4`, `08123:B:4` (`recommended`): a stage with its quest points in progress but no Mould on a hero holds the progress and advances (or wins) the moment the Mould is attached; no ruling names it.
  - `08122:B:2`, `08123:A:1`, `08123:B:2`, `08134:A:1` (span-settled: `rm.scour`, `rm.the-orcs-search`): a Scour card that is no longer in play when its turn comes does not resolve. "Examine each encounter card in play" finds the card where it lies as its turn comes, and a card underneath The Orcs' Search is not in play. A card that moved inside play (staging area to the active location) still resolves; for `08133:A:1` the card stays in the discard pile.
  - `08132:A:1` (`recommended`, no source names it): when the first Ancient Foundation resolves and buries itself, the second copy then holds the `scoursAlone` flag (the oldest copy in play) and resolves in the same trigger, as it is still in the set the trigger fixed. X is read as it resolves. Sources read: https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/06/30/celebrimbors-secret/ and https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/137881-scour-effect-on-bellach-and-collapsed-tower-in-celebrimbors-secret (neither covers two copies). Not taken: the second copy skipped for the rest of the trigger.
  - `08123:A:1` (researched; the attach `span-settled`, `rr.then`): scour effects, then Bellach attacks the controller of the Mould from the staging area (shadow card dealt, defender may use sentinel), then the Mould attaches to Bellach. A hero killed by the attack loses the game first. "Then, attach" depends on the attack, so the attach runs only when a hero holds the Mould; with none, nothing moves.
  - `08127:A:1` (researched): free of encounters means no encounter card holds it (`faq.saruman-out-of-play-guarded`); the Secret Chamber or Bellach leaving play frees it and the first player claims it at once. It stays on a damaged hero.
  - `08127:A:2` (span-settled): the loss line holds for a hero only. The existing record `08127:constant:1` gives `lossOnLeavingPlay` to its host, so with the Mould on Bellach (`08123:A:1`) Bellach leaving play would lose the game; Build restricts the record to a hero host.
  - `08129:A:2` (researched; tie `recommended`): the Orc engages the highest-threat player (ties: first player chooses, as `10041:A:1`) and makes an immediate attack. In the discard pile (`08133:A:1`, `recommended`): it engages from the discard pile and attacks.
  - `08131:A:2` (researched): both parts resolve; Collapsed Tower in the discard pile returns to the staging area and takes 2 damage.
  - `08132:A:2` (researched): the travel damage may send the location underneath.
  - `08129:A:1` (researched): Forced after each attack of Prowling Orc, immediate and additional ones too.
  - `08135:A:1` (`recommended`, no source names it): the surge is the alternative to the damage, so "If there is no active location" is checked on the table as the card resolves, before the 3 damage; a location the damage sends underneath The Orcs' Search does not give surge. Not taken: the check after the damage. Sources read: https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/06/30/celebrimbors-secret/ and https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/101214-a-few-questions-concerning-certain-cards/ (neither rules on the order); the record checks first, as Trouble in Tharbad's 08083 does.

## Table

Each piece a record creates, with the line that creates it (`docs/agents/agent-brief.md`, Scenario procedure, The Table step). The engine decides each number; the table only says it. Words in `src/play/words/scenarios/ring-maker/celebrimbor-table.ts`, readouts in `src/play/readouts/ring-maker/celebrimbor.ts`, previews in `src/play/cards/ring-maker/celebrimbor-preview.ts`.

- **Time dial** (`08122:B:1`, `08123:B:1`, `08122:B:2`, `08123:B:2`; `rm.time-x`): the counters left against Time 3, the cycle's time readout (`src/play/readouts/ring-maker/fords.ts`, `FORDS_STAGES`; `fords-table.ts`). It warms at 2 counters left and is never critical; the last counter says that each Scour effect in play is triggered and 3 counters return. One dial for all seats (one stage is current). The stage's standing rule leads the note (Bellach's immunity, `08122:B:3`, `08123:B:3`).
- **Stage gate** (`08122:B:4`, `08123:B:4`): the dial's note says that, with no hero holding the Mould, the stage keeps its progress and advances (stage 2: is defeated) the moment a hero holds it. The stage preview names the printed line through the generic hold line (`advanceHold`, `src/play/readouts/objective.ts`), as Gríma's gate does.
- **Cards underneath The Orcs' Search** (`08125:A:1`, `08125:A:2`, `08124:A:1`; `rm.the-orcs-search`): the Search dial, a count and never which cards (they lie facedown). Its note says the count, what each player's threat rises by at the end of the refresh phase and Bellach's Threat X; Bellach's preview says it too, so the X values of Bellach, Ruined Plaza and Foundation read one count. Facedown cards have no zone on the table: the dial is the readout.
- **Warning before a location goes under** (`08125:A:1`, `08126:A:3`): the dial warns, before the damage that does it, for each damaged location in play within 2 of its printed quest points ("The Secret Chamber has 4 of 6 damage. At 6 it goes underneath The Orcs' Search, and the players lose the game."); `critical` when it is The Secret Chamber, `near` otherwise. The Secret Chamber's preview says the same with its travel cost (`08126:A:2`) and its Threat (one for each player, `08126:A:1`).
- **Celebrimbor's Mould** (`08127:A:1`, `08127:A:2`, `08122:A:1`, `08123:A:1`): the held card drawn under its host in every zone it can lie in. On a hero: the preview says losing the hero loses the game. On The Secret Chamber: not free of encounters while the Chamber is in play. On Bellach: no hero holds it, so the stage cannot advance. In the staging area, free: the first player claims it. The host wears the pill "held" (`src/play/cards/ring-maker/held-mark.ts`) and its preview says the same, so a hero's, the Chamber's and Bellach's line each name the Mould.
- **Bellach's immunity** (`08122:B:3`, `08123:B:3`): Bellach's preview says that he is immune to player card effects and cannot be engaged, and on stage 2 until the stage has 12 progress with the progress now; at 12 it says players can engage him. It reads the engine's flags (`immuneToPlayerCards`, `cannotBeEngaged`), not the stage.
- **The Scour order** (`08122:B:2`, `08123:A:1`, `08123:B:2`, `08134:A:1`): no zone or tracker; the first player's pick of the Scour card that resolves next is worded in the asks file (`src/play/words/asks/celebrimbors-secret.ts`) and says each effect finishes before the next. The engine asks it from `TriggerScour`, not from a record's Pick, so the words test does not reach it.
- **Owner on a pick that spans seats**: the Ost-in-Edhil and Orc searches (`08122:A:1`, `08124:A:2`), the damage of Ruined Plaza (`08130:A:2`) and Ancient Foundation (`08132:A:1`), the discard of `08136:A:2`, Prowling Orc's tie (`08129:A:2`) and the Scour order are `owned`, so two-handed each sentence leads with the player.
- **Set aside, staged and held cards** (`08122:A:1`): the Mould waits set aside; Bellach, The Orcs' Search and The Secret Chamber enter the staging area at the Setup; ordinary cards in the zones the setup puts them in, with no widget of their own.
- **The One Ring** (`141085:A:1` setup, another box's card): an attachment on a hero in a play area; no tracker, zone or held card of this scenario.
