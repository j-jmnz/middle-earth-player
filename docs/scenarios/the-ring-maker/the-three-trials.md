# The Three Trials

The Ring-maker part 5 (pack `TTT`), standalone only, on Easy and Normal (Q04.5, E04.5). Encounter sets The Three Trials and Ancient Forest (`rm.setup-the-three-trials`; Ancient Forest is shared with Into Fangorn). The rulesheet defines the current trial and the current trial's Key objective (`rm.current-trial`) and prints Secrecy X (`rm.secrecy`).

- **Setup.** Stage 1A prints "Set aside all 3 Guardian enemies, all 3 Key objectives, all 3 Barrow locations, and Hallowed Circle." (`08035:A:1`). The Setup reader takes the cards of the set by trait and type: the ten set-aside cards.
- **Piles.** The `setAside` pile holds Boar's, Wolf's and Raven's Guardian (08040 to 08042), the three Keys (08043 to 08045), the three Barrows (08046 to 08048) and Hallowed Circle (08049). The quest pile holds five stages: 1A, the three stage 2 quests (08036 to 08038) and 3A (08039). No override.
- **Errata.** None for a card of this scenario.

## Data

- Stage 1B advances "to a stage 2A quest of their choice" (`08035:B:1`). Each stage 2A sets cards aside into the staging area and attaches a Key to a Guardian or Barrow; each stage 2B says what the players do when they control the current trial's Key objective and advances to a stage 2 of their choice, or to stage 3A when all stage 2 quests have been defeated.
- The three Guardians print Time 2, 3 and 4 as an ability line (`08040:A:2` to `08042:A:2`: "Time 2. Forced: After the last time counter is removed from Boar's Guardian ..."), each re-placing its counters.
- Hallowed Circle (08049) is immune to player card effects and has the Travel cost "The players must exhaust 3 Key objectives."

- `08036:A:1`, `08037:A:1`, `08038:A:1` (span-settled). "Randomly choose 1 of the remaining set aside" picks among the cards still set aside, which excludes those of earlier trials. ## Table

Each piece a record creates, with the line that creates it (`docs/agents/agent-brief.md`, Scenario procedure, The Table step). The engine decides each number; the table only says it. Test: `src/play/scenarios/the-three-trials-table.test.ts`; screen moments: `test/scenarios/the-three-trials/screens.ts`.

- **The choice of the next trial** (`08035:B:1`, `08036:B:2`, `08037:B:2`, `08038:B:2`; `rm.current-trial`): the Choose that `AdvanceToChosenStage` queues belongs to no record, so no asks entry words it. `trialsChoice` (`src/play/words/scenarios/ring-maker/trials-table.ts`, called once from `src/play/choice/sheets.ts`) says that the first player answers for the group (the sentence names him with two players: "Player 1, for the group: choose the next trial."; alone: "Choose the next trial.") and the trials left and done. Each row is one line of the same three parts, the trial, what it puts on the table and its 2B rule, and carries its stage code so a long press opens the card.
- **The current trial's Key** (`08036:B:2`, `08037:B:2`, `08038:B:2`; `rm.current-trial`): a goal dial "Key" in the top bar (`readouts/ring-maker/trials.ts`, `trialDials`), empty while no player controls the Key the stage marked (`trialKey`) and full once a hero holds it (`trialKeyControlled`, the engine's own test). Its note says where the Key lies: on a Guardian or Barrow, with no controller; in the staging area, for the first player to claim; in the encounter deck or discard pile; or on a hero, naming the player in a game of several.
- **The Guardians' time counters** (`08040:A:2`, `08041:A:2`, `08042:A:2`; `rm.time-x`): a Time dial for each Guardian in play, labelled by its animal (Boar, Wolf, Raven) and ordered with the fewest counters first, the counters against Time 2, 3 and 4 (`timeLimit`), warming, only while a player is engaged, when the next refresh phase (`refreshTakes`) takes the last counter (the warning says the first sentence, the note the rest), like the dials of the Fords and Fangorn stages (`readouts/ring-maker/fords.ts`). The note names what the last counter does (a discarded ally, an immediate attack, 1 damage to each character), that the counters return, and the engaged player it hits; a Guardian in the staging area says no player is engaged and the effect does nothing.
- **The Key on its Guardian or Barrow** (`08043:A:1`, `08043:A:2`, `08044:A:1`, `08045:A:1`; `rr.attachment-cards`): the pill under a Guardian or Barrow says the Key's animal ("Boar", "Wolf", "Raven"; the reader hears "Key of the Boar attached"). A hero wears no pill: the Key is drawn as an attachment on it (`cards/ring-maker/held-mark.ts`, `heldMark`). The host's preview says no player controls the Key and that it goes to the staging area when the host leaves play; on a hero it says who controls it ("You control it" alone) and that the Key counts toward Hallowed Circle's cost while ready (`cards/ring-maker/trials-preview.ts`).
- **The Guardians in the victory display** (`08039:A:1`; `rr.reveal`, `rm.time-x`): a Victory dial that counts the Guardians in the victory display and opens the pile; its note says stage 3A reveals each again with fresh time counters and adds it to the staging area. It is absent when no Guardian lies there.
- **Hallowed Circle's Travel cost** (`08049:A:2`, `08049:A:3`; `rr.travel`, `rr.cost`): its preview says the cost is 3 ready Key objectives (not their heroes), how many ready Keys the players hold, whether the travel is possible, and that each engaged Guardian then attacks. The travel list already gives the engine's refusal and the printed Travel line. Each of the three exhaust Picks says its number ("Key 1 of 3") and names the Keys (`src/play/words/asks/the-three-trials.ts`).
- **Owner on a choice**: the discard of Boar's Guardian (`08040:A:2`) and the time counter of Cursed Forest's shadow (`08051:A:2`) are `owned`, the engaged or defending player's own, so two-handed each sentence leads with the player. The first player answers for the group at the trial choice, the Travel cost and Cursed Forest's search.
- **Choices that can lose the game**: none carry a caution. A last counter costs an ally, an attack or damage, and the dial and the sentence say so.
