# The Three Trials

The Ring-maker part 5 (pack `TTT`), standalone only, on Easy and Normal (Q04.5, E04.5). Encounter sets The Three Trials and Ancient Forest (`rm.setup-the-three-trials`; Ancient Forest is shared with Into Fangorn). The rulesheet defines the current trial and the current trial's Key objective (`rm.current-trial`) and prints Secrecy X (`rm.secrecy`).

- **Setup.** Stage 1A prints "Set aside all 3 Guardian enemies, all 3 Key objectives, all 3 Barrow locations, and Hallowed Circle." (`08035:A:1`). The Setup reader takes the cards of the set by trait and type: the ten set-aside cards.
- **Piles.** The `setAside` pile holds Boar's, Wolf's and Raven's Guardian (08040 to 08042), the three Keys (08043 to 08045), the three Barrows (08046 to 08048) and Hallowed Circle (08049). The quest pile holds five stages: 1A, the three stage 2 quests (08036 to 08038) and 3A (08039). No override.
- **Errata.** None for a card of this scenario.

## Data

- Stage 1B advances "to a stage 2A quest of their choice" (`08035:B:1`). Each stage 2A sets cards aside into the staging area and attaches a Key to a Guardian or Barrow; each stage 2B says what the players do when they control the current trial's Key objective and advances to a stage 2 of their choice, or to stage 3A when all stage 2 quests have been defeated.
- The three Guardians print Time 2, 3 and 4 as an ability line (`08040:A:2` to `08042:A:2`: "Time 2. Forced: After the last time counter is removed from Boar's Guardian ..."), each re-placing its counters.
- Hallowed Circle (08049) is immune to player card effects and has the Travel cost "The players must exhaust 3 Key objectives."

## Readings

The statuses are those of the Readings bullet of `docs/scenarios/the-ring-maker.md`. Each key is the scan-read ledger line (`data/printed/the-ring-maker.json`), `<code>:<side>:<n>`.

- The current trial (`08036:B:2`, `08037:B:2`, `08038:B:2`) (span-settled). The stage 2 in play is the current trial; its Key objective is the one chosen by its stage 2A's When Revealed effect. The stage 2B ends when the players control that Key, whichever other Keys they control. Basis: `rm.current-trial`.
- `08035:B:1` (recommended). The players choose the stage 2A by group decision, modelled as the first player's choice. Not taken: another chooser. Basis: the printed line, `rm.current-trial`. Read: https://hallofbeorn.com/LotR/Details/The-Trials-Begin-TTT ; https://www.unboxedtheboardgameblog.com/post/2018/09/28/lotr-lcg-the-three-trials (no ruling on who decides).
- `08036:B:2`, `08037:B:2`, `08038:B:2` (span-settled). Stage 2 has no quest points and is defeated only by this effect. A defeated stage is not offered again; when all three are defeated the quest advances to stage 3A. "Control" is control after the claim: the Key attached to a hero. Basis: `rm.current-trial`, `rr.objective-cards`, `rr.ownership-and-control`.
- Random choices (`08036:A:1`, `08037:A:1`, `08038:A:1`) (span-settled). "Randomly choose 1 of the remaining set aside" picks among the cards still set aside, which excludes those of earlier trials. The Key that shares a Trait with the Guardian (Boar, Wolf or Raven) is found among the set-aside Keys. Basis: `rm.current-trial` and its example.
- `08038:A:1` (span-settled). The Key is shuffled into the bottom 10 cards of the encounter deck after the encounter discard pile is shuffled into the deck. The ledger corrects "suffle" to "shuffle". Basis: the scan.
- `08038:B:1` (span-settled). "Any time players would place progress on this quest, discard an equal number of cards from the encounter deck instead (progress is placed on the active location before triggering this effect)": the progress still goes to the active location first and only the excess is replaced, one card for each progress. Not taken: all the progress is replaced. Basis: the printed parenthesis.
- `08040:A:1` to `08042:A:1` (span-settled). The Guardians cannot have non-Key attachments, so the Key attached by the stage stays. Basis: the printed lines.
- `08037:B:1` (span-settled). The Guardians cannot be damaged and do not leave play except by a quest effect. Basis: the printed line.
- `08043:A:1` to `08045:A:3` (span-settled). Each Key must be claimed by the first player and attached to a hero; a discarded Key returns to the staging area; a Key dealt as a shadow card shuffles back into the encounter deck. Basis: the printed lines.
- `08049:A:2` (span-settled). The three Keys are exhausted for the Travel cost of Hallowed Circle; the hero each is attached to is not exhausted. Basis: the printed line.
- `08052:A:1`, `08052:A:3` (span-settled). Grim Foothills has Doomed 2, which raises each player's threat by 2 when it is revealed (`rr.doomed-x`), and progress is placed on it before the active location while it is in the staging area. Basis: `rr.doomed-x`, the printed line.
- `08055:A:1` (span-settled). Wild Tenacity removes X time counters from each enemy that has the Time keyword, where X is the number of players. Basis: the printed line, `rm.time-x`.
- `08036:B:2` timing (recommended). The advance is immediate, also in the combat phase; the new Guardian engages the first player at once and may attack in that phase. Not taken: no attack until next round. Basis: the printed "immediately advance". Read: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/185194-the-three-trials-final-stage-timing ; https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/258303-several-questions/ (no official text on a same-phase attack).
- `08036:A:1`, `08038:A:1` (span-settled). "Reveal them" is a full reveal: Time counters are placed, then the cards are added to staging. Basis: `rr.reveal`, `mec108.time-x`, `faq.reveal-and-add`. Read: https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/08/02/the-three-trials/.
- `08037:A:1` (span-settled). The Barrow goes to staging, a random remaining Key is attached to it, and the Guardian that shares the Key's trait is revealed. Basis: the printed line, `rm.current-trial`.
- `08038:A:1` Key and discard (span-settled). A Key discarded from the deck goes to staging and is claimed (Key text). Not taken: the Key to the discard pile. Basis: `rr.objective-cards`. Read: https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/08/02/the-three-trials/.
- `08036:B:1`, `08037:B:1`, `08038:B:1`, `08039:B:1` (researched). The Guardians engage the first player passively: at once, moving with the token in the refresh phase; at 3B each engages the player who controls the Key of its trait. Not taken: engage once. An uncontrolled Key leaves the Guardian in staging (community only, recommended for that case). Read: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/111740-three-trials-guardians ; https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/177038-timing-at-the-three-trials (designer answer relayed in the thread).
- `08039:A:1` (researched). Guardians in the victory display are revealed again with fresh counters; none carry over; a Guardian still in play is untouched. Basis: `rr.reveal`, `rm.time-x`. Read: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/185194-the-three-trials-final-stage-timing ; https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/08/02/the-three-trials/.
- `08040:A:2` to `08042:A:2` (recommended). Counters are placed at reveal; one comes off at the end of the refresh phase after the token passes; the Forced effect hits the player engaged at that moment; none engaged: nothing, counters still placed. Not taken: the old first player. Basis: `rm.time-x`, `rr.step-7-4`. Read: https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/08/02/the-three-trials/ ; https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/177038-timing-at-the-three-trials (no official source on the engaged player's timing).
- `08040:A:2` ally (span-settled). The engaged player chooses and discards one ally; none: nothing.
- `08041:A:2` (span-settled). The immediate attack deals a shadow card (plus Hill Barrow, Wild Tenacity), even if the Guardian attacked already or is exhausted. Basis: `rr.attacks-outside-the-combat-phase`, `faq.peril-attacks`.
- `08042:A:2` (span-settled). One damage to each hero and ally of the engaged player.
- `08036:B:1` Barrows (span-settled). A Barrow cannot be travelled to and is immune to player card effects only while stage 2 Strength is the stage in play; quest effects still work. Basis: `faq.immune-expanded`, `rm.immune-to-player-card-effects`. Read: https://www.unboxedtheboardgameblog.com/post/2018/09/28/lotr-lcg-the-three-trials.
- `08046:A:1` (span-settled). The extra shadow card is dealt when the attack begins; a Barrow in staging counts. Basis: `faq.errata.01091`, `rr.step-6-2`.
- `08047:A:1` (recommended). Allies are discarded down to 5 on entry and whenever above 5, all seats counted together; the first player chooses which. Basis: the printed line. Read: https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/08/02/the-three-trials/ (no rule on the chooser).
- `08048:A:1` (recommended). Each player raises threat by the attacker's current Threat, once per attack, also immediate or additional attacks, cancelled or not. Not taken: printed Threat only. Read: https://www.unboxedtheboardgameblog.com/post/2018/09/28/lotr-lcg-the-three-trials (no official source on modifiers).
- `08049:A:1` (span-settled). Threat X is the number of players; the card is immune. Basis: `faq.immune-expanded`. Read: https://hallofbeorn.com/LotR/Details/Hallowed-Circle-TTT.
- `08049:A:3` (recommended). Each engaged Guardian attacks its engaged player once, outside combat; the first player orders. Basis: `rr.attacks-outside-the-combat-phase`. Read: https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/08/02/the-three-trials/ (no official source on order).
- `08039:B:2` (span-settled). Stage 3 is defeated only with Hallowed Circle in the victory display; defeating it wins.
- `08050` to `08053` Keys (span-settled). +1 per Key controlled for the attack on a shadow; surge at 3 Keys; non-unique characters of all players take 1 damage. Key text works only unattached.
- `08051:A:1` (recommended). The Spirit enemy found by the search is added to staging, not revealed; the deck is shuffled after the search. Basis: `rr.search`, `faq.reveal-and-add`.
- `08051:A:2` (recommended). The defending player chooses the enemy with a counter and removes one. Basis: `rr.defending-player`.
- `08054:A:1` (recommended). Each Guardian attacks its engaged player; a non-engaged Guardian attacks the first player; none in play: one random Guardian from the victory display is revealed. Basis: `rr.attacks-outside-the-combat-phase`. Read: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/177038-timing-at-the-three-trials (no official source on a non-engaged Guardian).
- `08054:A:2`, `08055:A:2` (span-settled). One more attack with a new shadow card; Wild Tenacity gives 2 additional shadow cards.
- `08055:A:1` (span-settled). Stops at 0, one trigger per card. Basis: `rm.time-x`.
- `08043:A:1` Key attached (span-settled). A Key attached by a 2A lies on its host and cannot be claimed there; when the host leaves play the Key is discarded, goes to staging and the first player claims it. Basis: `rr.attachment-cards`, `faq.errata.131043`.
