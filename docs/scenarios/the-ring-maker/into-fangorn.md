# Into Fangorn

The Ring-maker part 3 (The Voice of Isengard, `VoI`), standalone only, on Easy and Normal (Q04.3, E04.3). Encounter sets Into Fangorn, Ancient Forest and Weary Travelers (`rm.setup-into-fangorn`; the insert spells the last "Weary Travellers"; Ancient Forest is shared with The Three Trials, Weary Travelers with The Dunland Trap and The Nîn-in-Eilph). The scenario adds the Hinder keyword (`rm.hinder`) and the "does not stack" clause (`rm.does-not-stack`).

- **Setup.** Stage 1A prints "Add Edge of Fangorn to the staging area and attach Mugash to that location. Shuffle the encounter deck. Reveal X encounter cards where X equals the number of players in the game minus 1." (`07065:A:1`, read from the scan; Hall of Beorn's text adds the word "additional"). The Setup reader gives it a staging step with an attach step, a shuffle and the step `revealPlayersLessOne`.
- **Piles.** The staging pile holds Edge of Fangorn (07032). DragnCards stages the objective Mugash (07031), but the Setup attaches him to Edge of Fangorn, so an override (`scenarioPiles`) puts him in the set-aside pile. The quest pile holds the three stages.
- **Errata.** None for a card of this scenario.

## Data

- Mugash here is the objective (07031) with the Guarded keyword; the enemy of To Catch an Orc is another card. Stage 1B prints Time 4, 2B Time 4 and 3B Time 3 (`07065:B:1`, `07066:B:1`, `07067:B:1`).
- Hinder is on the three Huorn enemies (07033, 07034, 07035) and is not in the `keywords` of `data/cards`; the records name it by their first lines. Immune to player card effects is on Edge of Fangorn (`faq.immune-expanded`).
- Ancient Forest (07050) and Tangled Woods (07051) print "This ability does not stack with other copies".

## Readings

The statuses are those of the Readings bullet of `docs/scenarios/the-ring-maker.md`. Each key is the scan-read ledger line (`data/printed/the-ring-maker.json`), `<code>:<side>:<n>`.

- Hinder (`07033:A:1`, `07034:A:1`, `07035:A:1`) (span-settled). While engaged with a player, an enemy with Hinder is not dealt a shadow card and does not make an attack during the combat phase. At the beginning of the combat phase each player removes 1 progress from the current quest for each Hinder enemy engaged with him; when the quest holds no progress the progress is removed from the active location, and when neither holds any there is no effect. Not taken: the enemy still attacks, or the progress comes off a location first. Basis: `rm.hinder`.
- Hinder and an attack by effect (recommended; no official source answers it, the community thread agrees). A Hinder enemy is stopped from attacking in the combat phase only; the Forced attack of Angry Huorn at the beginning of the resource phase (`07034:A:2`) and the immediate attack of The Forest's Malice (`07037:A:1`) outside the combat phase are not made "during the combat phase" and resolve. An engaged Hinder enemy that attacks outside the combat phase is dealt a shadow card as any such attack is. In the combat phase Hinder bars every attack, an attack by a card effect included: The Forest's Malice revealed in the combat phase gives no attack and no shadow card. Not taken: Hinder stops every attack, or an attack outside the combat phase is dealt no shadow card. Basis: `rm.hinder` (the no-shadow and no-attack clauses describe the combat phase), `rr.attacks-outside-the-combat-phase`, `faq.enemy-attacks-outside-combat`. URLs read: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/127326-the-forests-malice (four posters agree), https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/06/11/into-fangorn (a blog, not a source of rulings).
- `07051:A:1`, `07050:A:1` (span-settled). Two copies of Ancient Forest in the staging area give each Forest location there +1 Threat and +3 quest points in all, not +2 and +6; the same holds for Tangled Woods. Basis: `rm.does-not-stack`.
- `07051:A:1` (span-settled). The gained Travel cost is on each Forest location in play (the staging area and the active location); the ledger reads "in play". Basis: the printed line.
- `07031:A:1` to `07031:A:3` (span-settled). The Setup attaches Mugash to Edge of Fangorn, which guards him; a hero exhausts to claim him when he is free of encounters and he attaches to that hero. If the attached hero takes damage, Mugash returns to the top of the encounter deck. Basis: the printed lines, `rr.objective-cards`.
- `07065:B:3`, `07066:B:2` (span-settled). The players cannot advance from stage 1 unless Mugash is attached to a hero; when the last counter leaves stage 1B or 2B, Mugash is shuffled into the encounter deck and the stage advances. Basis: the printed lines, `rm.time-x`.
- `07057:A:2` (span-settled). Off Track attaches to a location in play as a Condition with the text the card gives: the location gets +2 quest points and gains a Forced effect that removes an additional time counter from the current quest at the end of each refresh phase, if able. Basis: the printed line.
- `07052:A:1` (span-settled). "Either remove 1 time counter from a card in play, or return the active location to the staging area": the first player selects the card when more than one card holds a counter, and the players decide as a group which of the two the effect does (an either-or is no listed matter of the first player's final say); the first needs a card that holds a time counter. Not taken: the players choose the card. Basis: `rr.first-player` (an encounter effect that targets a single card with several eligible targets: the first player selects it), the printed line.
- `07058:A:1` (span-settled). In Need of Rest removes a hero from the quest and attaches to it; the Condition damages the attached hero after a time counter leaves the current quest. Basis: the printed line.
- Surge (`07057:A:1`) (span-settled). Basis: `rr.surge`.
- `07037:A:1` (recommended; only a blog and one forum thread answer it). "If no attack was made" is one test over the whole table: when no Huorn attacked, every player searches the encounter deck and discard pile for a Huorn and puts it into play engaged with him; when any attack was made, no player searches. Not taken: the test per player. The effect cannot be canceled (the card is in the staging area while it resolves). URLs read: https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/06/11/into-fangorn, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/127326-the-forests-malice.
- `07031:A:1` (recommended; one community thread answers it, no official source). Guarded is not resolved at Setup: Edge of Fangorn is Mugash's guard. It resolves each time Mugash is revealed from the encounter deck (after he is shuffled in, returned to the top, or found in the discard pile by `07067:B:2`): the next card is attached to him and both go to the staging area. URL read: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/104584-into-fangorn-setup.
- `07031:A:2` (span-settled). While Edge of Fangorn is in play Mugash is attached to it and cannot be claimed. When it leaves play by any means he lies in the staging area, free of encounters, and any player may claim him. Basis: `rr.guarded`, `rr.objective-cards`, `rr.attachment-cards`.
- `07031:A:3` (researched, official relay). A hero discarded without damage leaves Mugash in the staging area; a hero that takes damage, one destroyed by it included, sends him to the top of the encounter deck, once for each hero. The engine releases a destroyed hero's cards before the damage event is heard, so a second record of Mugash hears the hero's destruction by damage (`hostDefeated`, announced only then) and returns him to the deck. A hero that leaves play any other way, with damage on it or not, leaves him in the staging area. Only a hero's damage counts, not damage to the location he lies on. Source: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/100145-mugash-objective-and-caldera (FFG answer, 27 Feb 2014).
- `07065:A:1` (span-settled). Setup reveals one card for each player but the last (X, the players less 1); with one player none.
- `07065:B:2`, `07066:B:2` (span-settled). Mugash is shuffled into the encounter deck from wherever he is (attached to Edge of Fangorn or a hero, lying in the staging area, or in the discard pile); his old guard stays where it is. The quest jumps to stage 3 (3A): a jump ignores the advance restriction of `07065:B:3` and `07066:B:3`, which bind the advance by progress. Source: https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/06/11/into-fangorn.
- `07067:B:2` (recommended; no official or community answer on one card taken twice). The first player chooses first; each player chooses a different enemy or objective in the encounter discard pile (the cards the effect discarded and the cards already there), reveals it in full (Guarded resolves for Mugash) and adds it to the staging area; a player with none left takes none. Then 3 time counters go on the stage. Basis: the printed line, `rr.guarded`.
- `07067:B:3` (span-settled). At least 6 progress, per the scan ledger (the blog says 12; the scan wins), with Mugash attached to a hero, after progress is placed on the stage (by questing or by a card): the quest jumps back to stage 2A, whose When Revealed effect and time counters resolve again. Source: https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/06/11/into-fangorn.
- `07036:A:1` (recommended; no source names who chooses). The cap of 5 is for each player: the player chooses which characters stay exhausted, one at a time, at the beginning of the refresh phase. A second copy changes nothing once the cap is met.
- `07032:A:2` (recommended; `rr.search` says the player may add a found card). The Travel completes whether or not a Huorn is found. Found Huorns join the staging area and are not engaged; the encounter deck is shuffled after the search. Source: https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/06/11/into-fangorn.
- `07057:A:2` (recommended). The first player chooses the location, in the staging area or active, that holds no Off Track; with none the card is discarded and its surge still applies. The extra counter comes off at the end of each refresh phase wherever the location lies in play, before the stage's own removal; the removal counts for `07058:A:1`.
- `07058:A:1` (recommended). The player who reveals it removes a hero committed to the quest, of any player, that holds no In Need of Rest; with none it is discarded. Each removal from the current quest, by the refresh phase, Off Track or Turned Around, is heard.
- `07056:A:2` (recommended). The extra +1 needs a defending character that is undamaged; an undefended attack gets +1.
- `07052:A:1` (span-settled). "A card in play" with a time counter is the current quest in this scenario; the option is offered only while it holds a counter.
- Immune (`07032:A:1`) (span-settled). Encounter effects (Hinder's progress, Off Track, Ancient Forest, Tangled Woods) act on Edge of Fangorn; only player card effects are stopped. Basis: `rm.immune-to-player-card-effects`, `faq.immune-expanded`.
- `07033:A:2`, `07035:A:2` (span-settled). The threat raise is once for each Dark-Hearted Huorn engaged; the 3 damage of Deadly Huorn go to one character as one instance. Basis: the printed lines.
- `07065:B:2`, `07066:B:2` (recommended; no source answers it). When Mugash is already in the encounter deck, the encounter deck is shuffled: the card's aim is a random place for him. Whichever way he joins the deck, it is shuffled.
- `07066:B:4` (span-settled). Stage 3 (07067) is reached only by the Time jump, so no stage follows stage 2 by progress: defeating stage 2 (with Mugash attached, `07066:B:3`) wins the game and does not advance. Basis: the printed line.
- `07037:A:1` (recommended; a blog only, `faq.search-effects` gives the rule). The shuffle of the encounter deck follows a search only (`faq.search-effects`): when any attack was made no player searches and the deck is not shuffled.

## Table

Each piece a record creates, with the line that creates it (`docs/agents/agent-brief.md`, Scenario procedure, The Table step). The engine decides each number; the table only says it.

- **Time dial** (`07065:B:1`, `07066:B:1`, `07067:B:1`, `07065:B:2`, `07066:B:2`, `07067:B:2`; `rm.time-x`): the Fords readout (`src/play/readouts/ring-maker/fords.ts`, `FORDS_STAGES`) also reads stages 1B, 2B and 3B: the counters against Time 4, 4 and 3. It warms at 2 counters left and is never critical, because the last counter of these stages loses nothing. The words say what the last counter does (`src/play/words/scenarios/ring-maker/fords-table.ts`): Mugash is shuffled into the encounter deck, even from a hero, and the quest advances to stage 3 (stages 1B and 2B); 5 cards from the top of the encounter deck are discarded, each player adds an enemy or objective from the discard pile to the staging area, and 3 counters return (stage 3B). Turned Around's time row (`07052:A:1`) carries its count and the last-counter caution the same way (`src/play/choice/ring-maker-sheets.ts`, `LAST_COUNTER`). With two players the bar holds both threat dials and one Time dial.
- **Mugash under Edge of Fangorn, then on a hero** (`07031:A:2`, `07031:A:3`, `07065:A:1`; `rr.guarded`, `rr.objective-cards`, `rr.attachment-cards`): the pill on Edge of Fangorn and on the hero names him ("Mugash"; `card-effects.ts`, `heldMark`, through `mugashLyingOn` in `readouts/ring-maker/orc.ts`, which reads both Mugash cards of the box). The preview on Edge of Fangorn and on Mugash says no player can claim him while Edge is in play and that he stays in the staging area when it leaves; on the hero it says damage returns him to the top of the encounter deck and any other exit returns him to the staging area; free in the staging area it says a ready hero can claim him (`src/play/cards/ring-maker/preview.ts`, words in `words/scenarios/ring-maker/fangorn-table.ts`).
- **Hinder Huorn** (`07033:A:1`, `07034:A:1`, `07035:A:1`; `rm.hinder`): the preview says that in the combat phase the enemy makes no attack and is dealt no shadow card, and removes 1 progress from the current quest, else from the active location, at the start of that phase; in the staging area it says what it does once engaged.
- **Off Track and In Need of Rest on their hosts** (`07057:A:2`, `07058:A:1`): the host's preview says Off Track gives +2 quest points and takes 1 more time counter at each refresh phase, and that a hero with In Need of Rest takes 1 damage each time a time counter comes off the current quest; a hero with Mugash and In Need of Rest shows both lines.
- **Heart of Fangorn's cap** (`07036:A:1`): the choice of the exhausted characters that stay exhausted names the cap (5), how many exhausted characters the player has, and, two-handed, the player (`owned`, `src/play/words/asks/into-fangorn.ts`).
- **Choices that can lose the game**: none. The last counter and a hero destroyed by damage lose nothing here (Mugash only returns to the deck or the staging area); the time rows carry a harm caution for the counters running out, as the Fords stages do.
- **Owner on a choice**: the picks of a player's own (Deadly Huorn, Ancient Forest, Low on Provisions, The Forest's Malice, Escape from Fangorn's search, Heart of Fangorn, the claim of Mugash, the choice from the discard pile of stage 3B) are `owned`, so two-handed each sentence leads with the player. The first player answers for the group at Edge of Fangorn's Travel, Turned Around, Off Track and In Need of Rest.
