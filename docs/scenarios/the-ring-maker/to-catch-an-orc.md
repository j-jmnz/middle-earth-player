# To Catch an Orc

The Ring-maker part 2 (The Voice of Isengard, `VoI`), standalone only, on Easy and Normal (Q04.2, E04.2). Encounter sets To Catch an Orc, Misty Mountain Orcs and Broken Lands (`rm.setup-to-catch-an-orc`; Misty Mountain Orcs is shared with Trouble in Tharbad and Celebrimbor's Secret, Broken Lands with Celebrimbor's Secret). The scenario adds the out-of-play deck (`rm.out-of-play-deck`), Mugash and his Guard with player card backs (`rm.mugash-player-card-backs`) and the Searches X keyword (`rm.searches-x`).

- **Setup.** Stage 1A prints "Each player removes the top 20 cards of his deck and places them aside, as an out-of-play deck. The first player takes Mugash plus 1 Mugash's Guard for each other player in the game and randomly shuffles one facedown into each player's out-of-play deck. Place any remaining copies of Mugash's Guard aside, out of play." (`07062:A:1`). The Setup reader gives it two verbs: `CreateOutOfPlayDecks`, and `ShuffleIntoOutOfPlayDecks`, which also leaves the remaining Guards aside.
- **When.** The twenty cards are the top of each deck after the starting hand is drawn and any mulligan is taken: Setup step 5 (draw the setup hand) comes before step 7 (follow the scenario Setup instructions). Basis: `rr.setup`, `rr.mulligan`.
- **Piles.** The `setAside` pile holds Mugash (07024) and Mugash's Guard (07025); the Setup puts them into the players' out-of-play decks, so they are never in the encounter deck. The Guard copies the Setup does not deal stay aside. No override.
- **Errata.** None for a card of this scenario.

## Data

- Mugash (07024) and Mugash's Guard (07025) are encounter cards with a player card back; they cannot be put into a player's deck (`rm.mugash-player-card-backs`). Mugash prints "Cannot have attachments" and a Forced effect that attaches him to a hero after he is defeated, as a Captive attachment with the text the card gives.
- Stage 1B has each player search the encounter deck for a Mountain location and then advances (`07062:B:1`); stage 2B and 3B print Time 2 and Time 3 (`07063:B:1`, `07064:B:1`). Methedras (07027) and Orc Cave (07028) print the Searches keyword on their leaving-play lines; Mugash's Lair (07029) reveals the top card of each out-of-play deck at the beginning of the encounter phase.
- Searches is not in the `keywords` of `data/cards`; the records name it by the lines `07027:A:2` and `07028:A:1`.

## Readings

The statuses are those of the Readings bullet of `docs/scenarios/the-ring-maker.md`. Each key is the scan-read ledger line (`data/printed/the-ring-maker.json`), `<code>:<side>:<n>`. Sources read: the Voice of Isengard insert (`rm.searches-x`, `rm.out-of-play-deck`), https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/100233-mugash-forced-effect-on-to-catch-an-orc (an official response by Caleb), https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/05/15/to-catch-an-orc/ (a player's preview, not a rules authority; it links an official ruling on a thread at boardgamegeek.com that the fetch could not read).

- Out-of-play deck (span-settled). Each player's twenty cards are one facedown deck out of play, apart from the draw deck, the hand and the discard pile; a card there is not in play and counts for no effect that names the player's deck. Basis: `rm.out-of-play-deck`, `rr.in-play-and-out-of-play`.
- Searches X (`07027:A:2`, `07028:A:1`) (span-settled). When a location with the keyword leaves play (here as an explored location), each player the card names reveals the top X cards of his out-of-play deck, adds each revealed enemy to the staging area, chooses 1 of the other cards for his hand and discards the rest. Methedras names each player, Orc Cave the first player. Not taken: the search keeps the enemies hidden. Basis: `rm.searches-x`.
- Searches with fewer than X cards (recommended). The player reveals all the cards the deck holds; with none nothing happens. Not taken: a loss for an empty deck (no rule makes an out-of-play deck a draw deck; `rr.running-out-of-cards` is about the player deck). Basis: `rm.searches-x`; no rule orders more, so the effect does as much as the deck allows.
- `07024:A:2` (researched). Mugash's Forced effect attaches him to a hero and exhausts that hero; the exhaustion is an effect, not a cost, so he attaches to an exhausted hero. The hero stays exhausted and cannot ready while Mugash is attached. Basis: the official response by Caleb dated 2014-03-03 on https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/100233-mugash-forced-effect-on-to-catch-an-orc ("Exhausting the hero Mugash is attached to is an effect, not a cost.").
- `07024:A:2` (span-settled). The Captive attachment is the attachment with the text the card gives: "Attached hero cannot ready. If attached hero leaves play, the players lose the game." Basis: the printed line, `rr.attachment-cards`.
- `07025:A:1`, `07025:A:2` (span-settled). While a player controls a hero with Mugash attached, each Guard engages that player; when a Guard destroys a character and Mugash is attached to a hero, Mugash returns to the staging area. Basis: the printed lines.
- `07026:A:1` (span-settled). "Shuffles 1 random card from his hand into his out-of-play deck": the card goes in at a random place, face down. Not taken: the card goes on top. Basis: the printed verb "shuffles" (a shuffle puts the card at a random place), `rm.out-of-play-deck`.
- `07029:A:1` (span-settled). The top card of each player's out-of-play deck is revealed; an enemy goes to the staging area and any other card is discarded to its owner's discard pile. Basis: `rm.out-of-play-deck`, `rr.discard-pile-and-victory-display`.
- Time on stages and enemies (`07063:B:1`, `07064:B:1`) (span-settled). The counters are placed when the stage is revealed and one is removed at the end of each refresh phase. Basis: `rm.time-x`.
- Setup timing (span-settled). See Setup, When.
- Out-of-play deck, ownership (span-settled). Mugash and his Guards are encounter cards and have no owner; the twenty cards belong to their player and go to that player's discard pile (a reveal discards to the owner of the pile it lay in). Basis: `rr.ownership-and-control`, `rm.mugash-player-card-backs`, `rr.discard-pile-and-victory-display`.
- Out-of-play deck, empty (span-settled). An empty out-of-play deck costs the player nothing: it is not the player deck of `rr.running-out-of-cards`. Basis: `rr.in-play-and-out-of-play`, `rr.running-out-of-cards`.

- `07027:A:2`, `07028:A:1` (span-settled). - `07027:A:2`, `07028:A:1` (span-settled). Only a location that leaves play as an explored location searches. The `leftPlay` event the exploration queues carries the explored mark and the card hears it from the discard pile (`subject: self` with `subjectExplored`, `rr.explored`); a location discarded or returned another way does not search. ## Build rulings

- A card moved into an out-of-play deck leaves its seat's deck, lies facedown, has no controller, keeps its owner (the verb stamps the seat for a deck card and clears it for Mugash and the Guards) and joins the bottom of the pile list (`core/zones.ts` `moveCard`, `core/piles.ts` `joinPile`). A card revealed out of it is turned up, its owner cleared, and goes where the verb sends it.
- The two zones are `outOfPlay0` and `outOfPlay1` because a game has one or two seats (`StartScenario` `seatCount`).
- The random hand pick of `07026:A:1` leaves out a card that cannot leave the hand (`heldInHand`, `families/ring-maker/hand.ts`), as a random discard does; with every card held nothing moves.

## Table

Each piece a record creates, with the line that creates it (`docs/agents/agent-brief.md`, Scenario procedure, The Table step). The engine decides each number; the table only says it.

- **Out-of-play deck of each seat** (`07062:A:1`, `07029:A:1`, `07027:A:2`, `07028:A:1`; `rm.out-of-play-deck`): one top-bar dial, "Out of play", with the count of cards left, never which cards (`src/play/readouts/ring-maker/orc.ts`, words in `src/play/words/scenarios/ring-maker/orc-table.ts`, hooked in `readouts/scenario-dials.ts`). Solo it is the player's deck. Two-handed it is both decks added together: two dials would crowd the bar beside both threat dials and the Time dial. A tap opens a panel in both games, a count for each deck, with the warning and the note at its foot; with four dials the row goes wordless. It warms when a deck holds 5 cards or fewer while Mugash is still hidden in a deck (a reveal can take what is left), and the warning names the player whose deck is low. Once Mugash is on the table it stays calm. An empty deck loses nothing (`rr.running-out-of-cards` is about the draw deck).
- **Mugash held under his hero** (`07024:A:2`): on the hero he is attached to, in every zone that hero can lie in, the pill names him ("Mugash"), and a screen reader hears "Mugash held underneath" (`src/play/cards/card-effects.ts`, `heldMark`). The preview of Mugash says the hero is holding him, cannot ready, and that the players lose if it leaves play; the preview of the hero says the same from its side (`src/play/cards/ring-maker/preview.ts`, hooked in `dungeon-preview.ts`). A Mugash attached to no hero has no line.
- **The Guards' engagement** (`07025:A:1`): the preview of a Guard in the staging area or engaged says whom it engages: any player who controls a hero with Mugash attached, whatever its engagement cost; with Mugash attached it names the player (two-handed) and says it engages him.
- **Time dial** (`07063:B:1`, `07064:B:1`, `07063:B:2`, `07064:B:2`; `rm.time-x`): the Fords readout (`src/play/readouts/ring-maker/fords.ts`) also reads stages 2B and 3B: the counters against Time 2 and Time 3. It warms at 2 counters left and is never critical, because the last counter of both stages returns counters and loses nothing. The words say what the last counter does: the encounter discard pile joins the deck, 2 encounter cards are revealed for each player, and 2 counters return (stage 2B); Mugash returns to the staging area, the players reveal 1 encounter card for each player after the first, and 3 counters return (stage 3B). The time row of a Choose (Orc Skirmisher, Orc Hunter, Take Cover!, Searching for Mugash) carries its count and the last-counter caution the same way (`src/play/choice/ring-maker-sheets.ts`).
- **Choices that can lose the game**: a hero that holds Mugash cannot leave play (`07024:A:2`). Orc Skirmisher's damage pick arms a second tap on the row of that hero when 3 damage destroys it (`07038:A:1`), and Take Cover!'s damage row arms one when a hero that holds Mugash has 1 hit point left (`07055:A:2`); both say the players lose the game (`src/play/words/asks/to-catch-an-orc.ts`).
- **Owner on a choice**: the picks of a player's own (Mugash's Lair, Prowling Wolf, Orc Hound, Orc Skirmisher, each player's search for a location and Searches X) are `owned` in the asks file, so two-handed each sentence leads with the player. The first player answers for the group at Mugash's attachment, Orc Hunter, Take Cover! and Searching for Mugash.
