# The Fords of Isen

The Ring-maker part 1 (The Voice of Isengard, `VoI`), standalone only, on Easy and Normal (Q04.1, E04.1; DragnCards labels the decks "Fords of Isen"). Encounter sets The Fords of Isen, Dunland Warriors and Dunland Raiders (`rm.setup-the-fords-of-isen`; the two Dunland sets are shared with The Dunland Trap and The Antlered Crown). The cards print the Time X keyword on the three quest stages (`rm.time-x`) and the Gríma objective-ally (`rm.grima-objective-ally`).

- **Setup.** Stage 1A prints "Add The Islet to the staging area and attach Gríma to that location. Each player searches the encounter deck for 1 different Dunland enemy and adds it to the staging area. Shuffle the encounter deck." (`07059:A:1`, read from the scan). The Setup reader turns it into a staging step with an attach step and a search of one Dunland enemy for each player (`scripts/data/normalize/setup-the-ring-maker.ts`).
- **Piles.** The staging pile holds The Islet (07017). DragnCards stages Gríma (07016), but the Setup attaches him to The Islet, so an override (`scenarioPiles`) puts him in the set-aside pile and the scenario author attaches him at Setup. The quest pile holds the three stages.
- **Errata.** None for a card of this scenario.
- **Cards of other scenarios.** The Dunland Warriors and Dunland Raiders cards (07042 to 07048) keep one record in each scenario that deals them (`docs/scenarios/the-ring-maker.md`).

## Data

- Stage 1B has Time 5 (`07059:B:1`), stage 2B Time 2 (`07060:B:2`) and stage 3B Time 3 (`07061:B:1`). Each stage 2B and 3B prints the Forced effect that resolves when the last counter is removed and puts the counters back. The ledger holds the printed order of stage 2B (When Revealed first, then Time 2), where the source text lists the Time line first.
- Gríma (07016) has the objective-ally type: an objective and an ally (`rm.grima-objective-ally`). Fords of Isen (07018) and The King's Road (07019) read the cards in a player's hand; Gap of Rohan (07020) removes a time counter from the current quest with its shadow effect.
- Immune to player card effects is printed on The Islet (`faq.immune-expanded`).

## Readings

The statuses are those of the Readings bullet of `docs/scenarios/the-ring-maker.md`. Each key is the scan-read ledger line (`data/printed/the-ring-maker.json`), `<code>:<side>:<n>`.

- Gríma hero (span-settled). While the scenario is played no player can use the hero card Gríma (07002); the scenario author builds the deck check that refuses it. Not taken: both Gríma cards in play. Basis: `rm.grima-objective-ally`, `rr.unique-cards`.
- `07016:A:1` to `07016:A:3` (span-settled). Gríma is attached to The Islet at Setup, claimed and controlled by nobody. He is not free of encounters while he lies on The Islet. When The Islet leaves play (explored or discarded) he is not discarded with it: he stays in the staging area, and the first player gains control of him at once. The first player gains control of him when he is free of encounters; he then sits in that player's play area and acts as any ally (his Action: exhaust to draw a card; the draw is the first player's and triggers "after a player draws"). If Gríma leaves play, the players lose. Not taken: Gríma is discarded with The Islet (the players lose). Basis: `rm.grima-objective-ally`, `rr.guarded`, `wor.guarded-x`, the printed lines. Source: https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/05/03/the-fords-of-isen/ ("you cannot choose which player draws with him"; he is read as free once the Dunlendings are driven from The Islet, not as a loss).
- `07016:A:1` (researched, community only). The control clause is a passive: Gríma moves to the new first player each time the first player token passes. Not taken: a one-time claim that stays with the first controller. Basis: `rm.grima-objective-ally`, `mec108.objective-allies` (a Response claim does not follow the token; Gríma's clause is no Response), `07059:B:3`. Sources: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/256719-fords-of-isen-grima-with-first-player ; https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/192811-clarification-on-objective-allies-moving-with-first-player-icon (no official post).
- `07016:A:1`, `07016:A:3` (researched, relayed). A first player who holds Gríma and is eliminated: the eliminated player's cards leave play at once, Gríma leaves play and the players lose, though the token then passes. Not taken: the token passes first, Gríma follows it and the game goes on. Basis: `07016:A:3`, `faq.first-player-elimination`. Source: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/256737-first-player-elimination-timing-question (a poster relays the designer's answer: the cards are removed at once when the player is eliminated).
- `07059:B:2` (span-settled). When the last time counter leaves stage 1B, at the end of the refresh phase after the other Forced effects of that step or by a card (Gap of Rohan, Down from the Hills), Gríma is discarded from play, which loses the game by `07016:A:3`. Stage 1B does not refill its counters. Not taken: a refill at 0. Basis: the printed lines, `rm.time-x`. Sources: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/195353-time-x-keyword ; https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/05/03/the-fords-of-isen/ ("the effect that actually loses is on the Gríma ally").
- `07059:B:3` (recommended). The players cannot advance unless the first player controls Gríma: progress stays on the stage when quest points are met or passed, and the stage advances at once when the first player gains control of him. Not taken: the excess progress is lost, or the advance waits for the next quest phase. Basis: `rr.quest-advancement`, `faq.progress-without-advancing`. No source covers waiting progress on this stage; the same reading is taken for Alcaron's Scroll (`05066:B:2`). Source: https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/05/03/the-fords-of-isen/ (no ruling on waiting progress).
- `07059:A:1` (span-settled). The Setup runs at step 7 of `rr.setup`; the search takes enemies from the encounter deck only; nothing is drawn, so no draw is heard at Setup. Not taken: the discard pile too. Basis: the printed line, `rr.setup`.
- `07059:B:1`, `07060:B:2`, `07061:B:1` (span-settled). The counters go on when the stage is turned to side B (stage 1 at the end of Setup, the others when the players advance to them) and leave with the stage. Not taken: counters that carry over to the next stage. Basis: `ltp.quest-advancement`, `rm.time-x`. Sources: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/195353-time-x-keyword ; https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/98441-time-x-problem-with-the-phrasing-or-intentional.
- `07060:B:3`, `07061:B:2` (span-settled). X is counted when the Forced effect resolves; the enemies of `07061:B:2` are added to the staging area, not revealed. Not taken: X counted at the reveal. Basis: `rr.lasting-effects`.
- `07061:B:3` (recommended). While any enemy is in play the players cannot defeat the stage; progress stays on it and the stage is defeated the moment the last enemy leaves play; defeating it wins the game. Not taken: progress is capped at the quest points. Basis: `rr.quest-advancement`. No source covers waiting progress on this stage. Source: https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/05/03/the-fords-of-isen/ (no ruling on waiting progress).
- Cards in hand (`07019:A:1`, `07019:A:2`, `07042:A:1`, `07042:A:2`, `07043:A:1`, `07043:A:2`, `07044:A:1`, `07047:A:1`, `07060:B:3`, `07061:B:2`) (span-settled). A condition on "any player" reads every player's hand, and a value that counts the cards in a hand is counted live. Not taken: counted once at the reveal. Basis: `rr.constant-ability`, `rr.lasting-effects`.
- "After a player draws any number of cards" (`07046:A:2`, `07048:A:1`) (span-settled). Cards drawn at the same time are one draw: the effect triggers once for the draw. Basis: `rr.drawing-cards`.
- `07020:A:2`, `07022:A:1` (span-settled). "Remove 1 time counter from the current quest" removes it from the stage in play; when it removes the last one the Forced effect of that stage triggers as it does at the end of a refresh phase. Basis: `rm.time-x`, `mec108.time-x`.
- `07045:A:2`, `07049:A:2` (researched, relayed official answer). Old Hatreds and Wild Men of Dunland attach to the current quest as a Condition with the text the card gives. The attachment leaves play with the stage it is attached to when the quest advances, as an attachment does when its host leaves play (`rr.attachment-cards`); the next stage does not inherit it. Not taken: the attachment moves to the next stage. Basis: `rr.attachment-cards`, `rr.quest-advancement`. Sources: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/100323-voi-att (an official answer relayed by the thread's author after an official query: treachery cards that attach to the current quest are discarded when the stage they are attached to leaves play); https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/108615-treachery-cards-attaching-to-quest.
- `07045:A:2`, `07049:A:2`, `07046:A:2`, `07048:A:1` (span-settled). The Forced text belongs to the Condition in play on the current quest: the drawing player is "his" or "that player", and for Wild Men that player picks the character. Berserker answers only the draw of the player it is engaged with, makes the attack outside the combat phase with a shadow card, and the defender may be declared. Not taken: any player's draw triggers Berserker. Basis: `rr.forced`, the printed lines. Source: https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/05/03/the-fords-of-isen/ ; https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/100910-fords-of-isen-and-dunland-berserker.
- `07046:A:2`, `07048:A:1`, `07045:A:2`, `07049:A:2`, `07018:A:2` (researched, community only). A draw of several cards in one step is one draw and triggers once; a draw of 0 cards triggers nothing; in the resource phase each player's draw triggers once, in player order. Not taken: one trigger for each card; one trigger for all players together. Basis: `rr.drawing-cards`. Sources: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/100910-fords-of-isen-and-dunland-berserker ; https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/126186-after-a-player-draws-any-number-of-cards-clarification.
- `07022:A:1` (span-settled). Each player chooses between removing a time counter from the current quest and searching for a Dunland enemy; if any player has 5 or more cards in hand the effect cannot be canceled. Basis: the printed line.
- `07022:A:1` (recommended, several players). The players choose in turn; the counters come off one at a time; a stage emptied by them resolves its Forced effect after the When Revealed effect has finished; the encounter deck is shuffled once. Not taken: the Forced effect resolves between two players' choices. Basis: `rr.forced`, `rr.after`. Source: https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/05/03/the-fords-of-isen/ (names only the removal of counters); no source covers several players removing the last counters.
- `07021:A:1` (recommended). Each player draws first, then each player raises his threat by 1 for each card in his hand at that point; the Forced draw triggers of the Conditions in play resolve after the When Revealed effect. Not taken: the threat rises before the draw triggers. Basis: `rr.after`, `rr.forced`. Source: https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/05/03/the-fords-of-isen/ (no ruling on the order).
- `07023:A:1` (researched). Ill Tidings goes into the first player's hand as the card he draws in the printed words; it cannot leave the hand and counts in every hand count; the surge check is made once, after it is in the hand, itself included. Not taken: surge each time the hand grows. Basis: the printed line, `rr.staging`. Sources: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/202960-ill-tidings-and-erestor/ ; https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/100910-fords-of-isen-and-dunland-berserker.

Basis: the printed line; the card comes from the encounter deck. Sources: the two pages of the line above (no word on draw triggers); no source settles it.

## Readings to confirm

- `07016:A:1`: researched, community only. Gríma follows the first player token each time it passes.
- `07016:A:1`, `07016:A:3`: researched, relayed. An eliminated first player who holds Gríma loses the game for all players.
- `07059:B:3`, `07061:B:3`: recommended. Progress waits on a blocked stage and the stage advances or is defeated at once when the block ends.
- `07022:A:1`: recommended. With several players the counters come off one at a time and the stage's Forced effect resolves after the When Revealed effect.
- `07021:A:1`: recommended. Every player draws before any player raises his threat.
- `07023:A:1`: recommended. Ill Tidings going into the hand is not a draw.
- `07045:A:2`, `07049:A:2`: researched, a relayed official answer. The Condition leaves play with the stage; the next stage does not inherit it.
- `07046:A:2`, `07048:A:1`, `07045:A:2`, `07049:A:2`, `07018:A:2`: researched, community only. One draw is one trigger; each player's draw in the resource phase is its own.
