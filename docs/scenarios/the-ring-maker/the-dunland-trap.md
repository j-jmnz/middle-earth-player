# The Dunland Trap

The Ring-maker part 4 (pack `TDT`), standalone only, on Easy and Normal (Q04.4, E04.4). Encounter sets The Dunland Trap, Dunland Warriors and Weary Travelers (`rm.setup-the-dunland-trap`; the two other sets are in The Voice of Isengard). The rulesheet prints the Secrecy X keyword of the player cards (`rm.secrecy`) and a strategy tip about the many "after a player draws" Forced effects.

- **Setup.** Stage 1A prints "Set Chief Turch and Munuv Dûv Ravine aside, out of play. Make Old South Road the active location. Each player searches the encounter deck for a Boar Clan enemy and puts it into play engaged with him. Shuffle the encounter deck." (`08011:A:1`; Hall of Beorn's text spells the ravine "Du^v"). The Setup reader gives it an aside, an active location and the step `eachPlayerEngagesTraitEnemy` (trait Boar Clan).
- **Piles.** The `setAside` pile holds Chief Turch (08014) and Munuv Dûv Ravine (08015), the `activeLocation` pile Old South Road (08016). Chief Turch is the enemy card of this scenario. No override.
- **Errata.** None for a card of this scenario.

## Data

- Stage 1B prints Time 2 (`08011:B:1`); stage 3B prints Time X, where X is 5 times the number of players (`08013:B:1`). Stage 2A makes Munuv Dûv Ravine the active location and returns the previous one to the staging area (`08012:A:1`).
- Chief Turch (08014) prints "Chief Turch engages the first player" and a Forced effect on every enemy that engages a player (`08014:A:2`, `08014:A:3`). Old South Road (08016) removes a time counter from the current quest at the end of the refresh phase.
- Hills of Dunland (08020) and Hithaeglir Foothills (08019) read the draw of cards; Hills of Dunland prints the "does not stack" clause.

## Readings

The statuses are those of the Readings bullet of `docs/scenarios/the-ring-maker.md`. Each key is the scan-read ledger line (`data/printed/the-ring-maker.json`), `<code>:<side>:<n>`.

- Draw triggers (`08017:A:2`, `08018:A:1`, `08019:A:3`, `08020:A:1`) (span-settled). A draw of several cards is one draw: "after a player draws any number of cards" triggers once, and the rulesheet's tip orders the Forced effects of several cards that share the trigger one after the other. Basis: `rr.drawing-cards`, the rulesheet tip (`tdt`).
- `08014:A:3` (span-settled). Each time any enemy engages a player a time counter is removed from the current quest; the Boar Clan enemies the Setup puts into play engaged are engaged by an effect and trigger it. Not taken: only an engagement check triggers it. Basis: `rr.engaged`, the printed line.
- `08021:A:1`, `08022:A:1` (span-settled). While the location is the active location the resource phase draw is none (Plains of Enedwaith) or 2 cards (Hithaeglir Stream) for every player. Basis: the printed lines.
- `08005:A:1` (Close Call, Doomed X) (recommended). The reading is in the cycle file, Player card readings, under the key `08005:A:1`.
- `08011:A:1`, `08012:B:1` (recommended). Each player, in player order, searches for a Boar Clan enemy and puts it into play engaged with him; the Setup searches the encounter deck, stage 2B the deck and the discard pile. The printed lines do not say "different", so a later player may take a second copy of a title an earlier player took. The deck is shuffled once, after the last player. A player with none left takes none. Alternative: a title no earlier search took. Source: agent recommendation (the printed lines; no source online); `rr.engaged`, `rr.setup`.
- `08011:B:2` (span-settled). At the last counter each player discards his hand and draws 2 cards; the draws are heard after the effect, so after the 2 counters are placed. It happens whoever removes the last counter. Basis: the printed line, `rr.drawing-cards`.
- `08011:B:2`, `08016:A:1` (order at the end of the refresh phase) (span-settled). The stage's own counter comes off after the Forced effects of the end of the refresh phase (Old South Road, Off Track), which the first player orders; the same reading as Angmar Awakened and Into Fangorn Off Track (`docs/scenarios/angmar-awakened.md`, Angmar Awakened). The research file reads the stage's removal as one of those Forced effects; the totals are the same with 1 or 2 counters left. Basis: `rm.time-x` (the keyword, not a Forced effect), `faq.simultaneous-effects`.
- `08016:A:1` (recommended). Old South Road removes a counter at the end of each refresh phase wherever it lies in play, active or back in the staging area, from the stage that is current then. Alternative: the active location only. Source: agent recommendation; https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/07/03/the-dunland-trap/ and https://www.unboxedtheboardgameblog.com/amp/2018/09/11/lotr-lcg-the-dunland-trap (blogs, no answer on the zone).
- `08012:A:1` (literal). The Ravine becomes the active location, the old one goes back to the staging area; each player discards each Item and each Mount attachment he controls on any card, then keeps 1 ally of his choice and discards the others (a player with no ally picks nothing). Basis: the printed line, `rr.active-location`.
- `08012:B:2`, `08013:A:1` (literal). Stage 2B advances to 3A at the end of the combat phase; 3A puts Chief Turch into play engaged with the first player.
- `08013:A:1` (order) (recommended). The counters of stage 3B go on when the stage turns, before 3A's When Revealed resolves, so Turch's own engagement takes the first counter (X less 1 left). Alternative: the counters after Turch enters. Source: agent recommendation; https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/109452-full-dunland-trap-spoilers/page/2 (a player answer, no official one).
- `08013:B:1` (recommended). Time X on the final stage: X is 5 times the players still in the game when stage 3B turns; the counters are put on once and never counted again (the verb `Time` with `perPlayer`). Basis: `rm.time-x`. Alternative: counted again, or the players who started. Source: agent recommendation; https://hallofbeorn.com/LotR/Details/No-Way-Out-TDT (card text only) and https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/217919-dunland-trap-3-or-4-players (no talk of elimination).
- `08013:B:2` (span-settled). A hero destroyed at stage 3B loses the game at once; a player eliminated by threat or by an effect has not had a hero destroyed. Basis: the printed line, `rr.player-elimination`. The loss resolves before the win of `08013:B:3` when an attack destroys a hero (checked in a test).
- `08013:B:3` (recommended). After the last counter: engagement checks until none engages (`rr.step-5-3`), then each engaged enemy attacks, dealt a shadow card (`faq.enemy-attacks-outside-combat`); the players win when the attacks are done and no hero was destroyed. Alternative: the win checked after each attack, or one round of checks. Source: agent recommendation; https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/219373-dunland-trap-timing-of-forced-effects (a player answer, no official one).
- `08013:A:1`, `08014:A:1` (recommended). Chief Turch is put into play engaged with the first player. He cannot have attachments and cannot leave play, destruction included: damage past his hit points leaves him engaged (`cannotLeavePlay` and `indestructible`). Alternative: "cannot leave play" blocks discard and removal only. Source: agent recommendation; https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/07/03/the-dunland-trap/ (the author finds it unclear how this differs from Indestructible) and https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/217919-dunland-trap-3-or-4-players ("can't be killed or leave play").
- `08014:A:2` (recommended). Turch always engages the first player: when the first player token passes, or his player is eliminated, he moves to the new first player and has engaged him (his Forced effect then takes a counter); in a one-player game nothing passes. Alternative: he engages once, at 3A. Source: agent recommendation; https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/109452-full-dunland-trap-spoilers/page/2 (a player: he re-engages the new first player; one poster disagrees for solo).
- `08015:A:1` (recommended). Dunland enemies (the trait, so the Boar Clan enemies and Turch too) get +1 Attack and +1 Defense while the Ravine is in play, active or back in the staging area. Alternative: active location only. Source: agent recommendation; https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/07/03/the-dunland-trap/ (Turch 5 Attack +1), no source for the staging area.
- `08017:A:2`, `08018:A:1` (recommended). The draw triggers fire for the player the enemy is engaged with; the Warrior's shadow card stays until its next attack, on top of the regular deal (each deal is an additional shadow card). Alternative: the card discarded at once. Source: agent recommendation (none found online); `faq.enemy-attacks-outside-combat`, `faq.additional-attacks`, `rr.shadow-effects`.
- `08018:A:2` (recommended). +1 Attack for each shadow card on the attacker when the effect resolves, this card included. Alternative: excluding itself. Source: agent recommendation (none found online); `rr.shadow-effects`.
- `08019:A:2`, `08019:A:3` (span-settled). The X is the number of resource tokens on the card, read live; each draw by any player puts 1 token on it in any zone of play. Basis: `rr.constant-ability`, `rr.drawing-cards`.
- `08020:A:1` (span-settled; recommended for the empty deck). Hills of Dunland in the staging area gives itself the Forced effect: after a player draws, discard the top card of the encounter deck; if it is a Dunland enemy it enters play engaged with that player. A second copy there adds nothing (the flag `huntsOnDraw`, held by the oldest copy there); a copy not in the staging area gives nothing. With the encounter deck empty the discard pile is reshuffled in the quest phase only. Basis: `rm.does-not-stack`, `rr.running-out-of-cards`. Source for the empty deck: agent recommendation (no source online); https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/07/03/the-dunland-trap/.
- `08020:A:2` (recommended). The draw that pays for Travel is made while Hills of Dunland is still in the staging area, so its Forced effect and the Foothills hear it. Alternative: the cost draw triggers nothing. Source: agent recommendation; https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/07/03/the-dunland-trap/ ("traveling to the Hills triggers this").
- `08021:A:2` (literal). Plains of Enedwaith's shadow gives +1 Attack, +3 instead when the attack is undefended (not +4).
- `08022:A:2` (recommended). Hithaeglir Stream's shadow gives +1 Attack and deals the attacker one more shadow card, which resolves in the same step. Alternative: dealt and not resolved. Source: agent recommendation (none found online); `rr.shadow-effects`.
- `08023:A:1` (recommended). Each player draws 1 (player order), then each discards each ally in his hand; surge only if no player discarded an ally. Alternative: surge for each player with none. Source: agent recommendation (none found online).
- `08024:A:2` (recommended). It attaches to the active location (limit 1 per location; with none, or a copy there, it is discarded and its surge still applies); after the host is explored each player searches the encounter deck and discard pile for a Dunland enemy and puts it into play engaged with him, one shuffle at the end. The attachment hears the host leave play explored. Alternative: the Forced effect lost with the location. Source: agent recommendation (none found online); `rr.explored`, `rr.attachment-cards`.

## Table

Each piece a record creates, with the line that creates it (`docs/agents/agent-brief.md`, Scenario procedure, The Table step). The engine decides each number; the table only says it.

- **Time dial on 1B and 3B** (`08011:B:1`, `08011:B:2`, `08013:B:1`, `08013:B:3`; `rm.time-x`): the Fords readout (`src/play/readouts/ring-maker/fords.ts`, `FORDS_STAGES`) reads the counters against Time 2 on stage 1B and against Time 5 x the players on stage 3B (`timeLimit`; the limit words say "Time 5 x 2 players: it starts with 10 time counters"). Stage 2B prints no Time, so it has no dial. The dial warms at 2 counters left and is never critical (`src/play/words/scenarios/ring-maker/fords-table.ts`): the last counter on 1B makes each player discard the hand and draw 2 and returns 2 counters; on 3B it makes the checks and attacks, and the players win if no hero is destroyed and lose if one is.
- **Chief Turch** (`08014:A:1`, `08014:A:2`, `08014:A:3`; `rr.cannot`, `rr.engaged`, `rr.first-player`): his preview says he cannot leave play or be destroyed, engages the first player and follows the first player token, and that each time an enemy engages a player 1 time counter comes off the current quest (`dunlandDetail`, `src/play/cards/ring-maker/preview.ts`; words in `src/play/words/scenarios/ring-maker/dunland-table.ts`). The sheet's Indestructible pill comes from the `indestructible` flag.
- **Hills of Dunland's draw effect** (`08020:A:1`; `rm.does-not-stack`): its preview says that in the staging area each draw discards the top encounter card and a Dunland enemy there enters play engaged with the player who drew, and that copies do not stack; out of the staging area it says the effect does not work there.
- **Munuv Dûv Ravine's bonus** (`08015:A:1`): the Ravine's preview says Dunland enemies in play, staged or engaged, get +1 Attack and +1 Defense; a Dunland enemy's preview names the Ravine while it is in play, above the existing "Effects on it" rows.
- **Old South Road** (`08016:A:1`; `rr.refresh-phase`): its preview says that at the end of each refresh phase 1 time counter comes off the current quest, besides the one the stage takes.
- **Choices that can lose the game**: none. The searches and the ally pick of A Well Laid Trap lose nothing by themselves; the dial carries the caution for the counters.
- **Owner on a choice**: the searches of 08011, 08012 and Dunlending Ambush and the ally pick of 08012 are `owned` with a `why`, so two-handed each sentence reads "Player 1, <card>: <why> <note>" and never "Player 1: <card>: ..."; Off Track's pick is the first player's, for the group (`src/play/words/asks/the-dunland-trap.ts`; each scenario reads only its own asks file, so Off Track's entry is written here as in Into Fangorn).
