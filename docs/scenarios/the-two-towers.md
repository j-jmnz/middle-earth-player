# The Two Towers

The rulings, readings and table pieces of The Two Towers, scenario by scenario. Product rules, the Domain and the Core round are in `docs/brief.md`; the authority order is in `.claude/rules/authority.md`.

MEC112 (2023) reprints **The Treason of Saruman** (RingsDB pack `ToS`) and **The Land of Shadow** (pack `LoS`) as the second saga box: six scenarios, numbered 7 to 12. Its catalog pack code `TTwT` is Hall of Beorn's slug suffix, as `TFotR` is the Fellowship's; each of its 156 cards takes the code of its twin (143001 to 143081, 144001 to 144076), a title both boxes print (Aragorn, Frodo Baggins, The One Ring, Gollum) pairing with its own box's twin. The campaign id is `the-two-towers`: its own campaign and save with the one `CampaignLog` type (user decision, 2026-11-07). Its data is built (`data/campaigns/the-two-towers.json`, six scenario files) and the app offers it as a campaign and as a box on the Scenarios tab (`src/screens/data/campaign-data.ts`) with its own booklet (MEC112); only the scenarios `src/campaign/playable.ts` lists are playable, and its campaign tile unlocks with the first of them (`data/campaigns/index.json`, `playable`), which is when the continuation offer below reaches the Start screen. The six campaign cards' Setup and Resolution lines are effect records (`src/effects/authored/the-two-towers.ts`; "The campaign cards" below).

- **Saga start per scenario** (`mec112.setting-up-the-game`). The first player takes control of ☺ Aragorn (143001) in scenarios 7 to 9, and of a ☺ hero with the Ring-bearer trait with The One Ring (144063) attached in 10 to 12. Aragorn does not count against the hero limit, so no Ring-bearer slot is asked for 7 to 9 (`mec112.aragorn`); the One Ring's rules are the Fellowship's (`mec112.the-one-ring`, `mec112.multiplayer-the-one-ring`). The scenario data records it (`ScenarioData.sagaStart`); the campaign's `prelude` is empty. The briefing, the loadout, the checklist and the start read the scenario's own saga start (`src/campaign/saga.ts`), and the Fellowship, whose six scenarios all use the Ring-bearer, reads as before. ☺ Aragorn is dealt to the first player and never written to the log's hero list; the log keeps the Fellowship heroes it holds (the Ring-bearer Frodo while ☺ Aragorn is granted), and a hero of the Fellowship sphere is never a hero change.
- **Fellowship heroes** (`mec112.the-fellowship-sphere`, `mec112.fellowship-sphere-heroes`). One ☺ hero in play at a time, and no other version of Aragorn or of Frodo Baggins as a starting hero or in the deck. The Ring-bearer Frodo stays on the log but is not dealt as a hero in scenarios 7 to 9, and returns for 10 to 12 (user decision, 2026-11-07, from the Treason of Saruman campaign-mode rule that players cannot use a card titled Frodo Baggins in those scenarios; the pinned MEC112 rulebook words only the one-☺-hero rule).
- **The Uruk-hai** (`the-uruk-hai`; records in `src/effects/authored/the-uruk-hai.ts`, the pursuit tracker in `src/rules/families/pursuit/`, the Captives in `src/rules/families/captives/`). The pursuit value (`mec112.pursuit`) is `ScenarioState.pursuit`, set to 10 by the quest card's Setup, raised and lowered by the cards, and the X of both quest stages; To Isengard! raises it at the end of each refresh phase and the players lose at 30 or more. The stages print X for their quest points on side B (`questPointsX`, set by `scripts/data/normalize/cards.ts` for a Two Towers quest), and a stage advances as soon as its progress reaches X, so lowering the value can advance it. Uglúk (143069:B:2) cannot be engaged, take damage or be moved out of the staging area by anything (`cannotLeaveStaging`, `moveCard`); "each enemy engaged with you adds its Threat" (143065:A:2) is read live to the end of the round (`engagedThreatSeat`). A Captive (143069:A:1) is a hero faceup under To Isengard! or attached to Grishnákh: in play, controlled by no one, blank, out of its owner's heroes yet not killed, so its owner stays in the game while it lies there (`rr.player-elimination`); a Captive that leaves the captive zone, destroyed or otherwise, is a Captive no more. With no Captive in play the players lose (143022:A:1). The table adds the pursuit dial in the top bar (declared by `widgets.pursuitTracker`, the value at which the players lose, read from To Isengard!'s record; it warms toward it and shows its change as the threat dial does), every Captive drawn under its host (under To Isengard! or Grishnákh, wherever he is), a held dial on each seat that has a Captive, and the owner on a Captive's row in Grishnákh's pick when more than one seat's are offered; the choices that name the pursuit value say its value and the loss at 30 (`docs/design-system-table.md`, Cards). Rulings (user, 2026-11-07):
  - A seat whose Campaign Log names a captive takes that hero as its Captive without being asked; a seat with no logged captive (a fresh log, or a mixed one) chooses 1 non-Fellowship hero it controls, as in standalone.
  - Strange Weariness (143031:A:1): the characters committed when it resolves keep -1 Willpower until the end of the round, even after the quest phase.
  - Hands of a Healer (143016:A:3): "a hero" is any hero in play, a Captive included.
  - Grishnákh (143024:A:1): any way he leaves play puts the attached Captive faceup underneath To Isengard!; a Captive is never discarded that way.
  - A Captive keeps its tokens and attachments, a Permanent boon included; the damage on it counts in the score, and a Captive destroyed goes to its owner's discard pile.
  - The Skill boons (143015 to 143018) attach only to a hero with their Trait (Warrior, Healer, Ranger, Noble), also when earned at The Uruk-hai's Resolution (143071:B:1): a seat is offered a Skill boon only on a hero it controls with the Trait, and a seat with no such hero is offered none (user ruling C1, 2026-11-08; FFG preview "Now May I Make a Right Choice": "Each of these boons can only be attached to a hero with the corresponding trait"; `boonHeroes`, `src/rules/families/campaign/resolution.ts`).
  - A seat whose last non-Fellowship hero is its Captive is not eliminated while that Captive is held (`rr.player-elimination`; user ruling C2, 2026-11-08).
  - Rescue the Captives' Response (143070:B:2) lowers the pursuit value no lower than 0: the card names no floor (user ruling C3, 2026-11-08).
  - Defeating the last stage rescues each Captive (143070:B:3, "they have rescued each Captive hero"): it returns among its owner's heroes in the place it held, controlled again, its text box no longer blank and its attachments kept, as soon as the stage is defeated, in a standalone game as in a campaign one and before the score or the Resolution reads the heroes (`rescueCaptives`, a settle hook of `src/rules/families/captives/index.ts`), so a Skill boon may go on it; the score counts it as any hero of its owner's (its damage, or its threat cost when its owner was eliminated) and the log lists it among the heroes, not the fallen.
  - Eastemnet (143027:A:1): "each hero's resource pool" includes the Captives, as Hands of a Healer's "a hero" does (user ruling, 2026-11-10).
  - A Captive rescued back to a seat that was eliminated scores as that seat's other heroes do: its threat cost, as a destroyed hero (`src/campaign/score.ts`; user ruling, 2026-11-10).
  - An additional attack (Strange Weariness' Shadow) is dealt a new shadow card (`faq.additional-attacks`). A location the progress explores hears nothing, as Ettenmoors does (Banks of Entwash, 143029:A:1).
- **Helm's Deep** (`helms-deep`; records in `src/effects/authored/helms-deep.ts`, the Defense keyword in `src/rules/families/fortress/`, Poisoned Counsels in `src/rules/families/burden/player-deck.ts`). While the current quest stage has Defense (`ModifyStat defenseKeyword` on stages 2B to 4B, `mec112.defense`), questing places no progress for the players, and a staging Threat above the committed Willpower raises nobody's threat: the first player places the difference on the current quest through the active location first (a tie or a players' success places nothing). Progress from a player card effect is still placed: the keyword bars only questing. The quest-resolution step goes through `familyQuestResult` (`src/rules/interpret/family-hooks.ts`), which the fortress family answers.
  - Table (`docs/design-system-table.md`, Cards, Helm's Deep): The Defense of Helm's Deep's tokens are a Tokens dial with the limit 8 and the stage it advances to read from its record (143033:A:3; `widgets.defenseTracker`), filling toward the limit in the accent and never warning, since reaching it is the players' way out; while the stage has Defense (143073:B:1) the quest meter's marker ("+2 to stage", "Held") and the dock's result line say the encounter deck places the difference as progress, no player's threat rises, and what that does to the stage (advance, or the loss on 4B, or the card the first player reveals on 3B); the previews and advance screen say at what progress the encounter deck advances the stage, and that filling 4B loses the game (143075:B:3), as the quest card's progress strip on the table does ("5 / 8 · lose", in threat red). No seat has a held dial.
  - The Hosts of Isengard (143074:B:2): progress the encounter deck places on the active location only is not progress "placed on this stage", so the Forced reveal still fires (confirmed by Vision of the Palantir's review of Helm's Deep, a fan review and no official ruling: https://visionofthepalantir.com/2019/03/12/helms-deep).
  - Devilry of Saruman (143043:A:1) and Postern Door (143040:A:2): an encounter card's lasting effect reaches the characters that enter play later in the round too (`rr.lasting-effects`): the blank text box is a class holder of every character, and "cannot declare attacks" is held by the table.
  - Helm's Gate (143041:A:2): each enemy gets -20 engagement cost and +2 Attack for the round, enemies that enter play or engage later included (`rr.lasting-effects`; agent recommendation, the card names no snapshot).
  - Night Without End (143045:A:2): with no token on The Defense of Helm's Deep the token option cannot be chosen, so no question is asked and the revealer reveals 2 encounter cards (`rr.effects`; a blog reading agrees).
  - The King of the Golden Hall (143072:B:1): a neutral ally and a Fellowship hero match no sphere (`rr.neutral-cards`); a player with no ally in hand that matches the sphere of one of their heroes puts nothing into play. The ally is put into play, not played, and a unique title already in play is refused (`rr.play-and-put-into-play`, `rr.unique-cards`); the choice is mandatory. The first player answers for the group at the Setup choice (`rr.the-word-choose`): Poisoned Counsels in each deck after the starting hands, or the bypass of stage 1B to 2A, whose When Revealed does not resolve.
  - Soldier of Isengard (143036:A:2) and Scaling Ladders' Shadow (143042:A:3) place progress on the current quest through the active location (`mec112.defense`, `rr.active-location`); Devilry of Saruman (143043:A:1), stage 2B (143073:B:3) and stage 4B (143075:B:2) bypass it.
  - The Hosts of Isengard (143074:A:1): when no Helm's Gate is found in the deck, discard pile or victory display but a copy is staged, that copy becomes the active location (agent recommendation: the sentence names Helm's Gate, not "it").
  - The Wall is Breached! (143075:B:3): any defeat of stage 4B loses the game, whoever placed the last progress, a player card effect included (the card's words; the booklet says "if the encounter deck defeats").
  - The Defense of Helm's Deep (143033:A:1, A:3): its tokens join the total Threat in the staging area as a live amount held by the table, not a Threat stat of the objective (confirmed that each token adds 1 Threat to the staging area by https://visionofthepalantir.com/2019/03/12/helms-deep, a fan review; live against snapshot is not argued there, so that is an agent recommendation); 8 or more tokens end it (the Forced token at the end of the quest phase is the only way to gain one).
  - The Hornburg (143037:A:2): the first player assigns all X damage among any players' characters, X the enemies in play, staging and engaged, when it is explored (`rr.first-player`, as High Falls above); its archery cut (143037:A:1) is `archeryBonus` below 0 and the archery total never falls below 0.
  - Reckless Hate (143044:A:3): its additional attack is dealt a new shadow card (`faq.additional-attacks`).
  - Deeping Culvert (143039:A:1): its Threat X is 1 more than the allies the first player controls, never 0 (the data prints no Threat).
  - Poisoned Counsels (143021, `mec112.poisoned-counsels-searching-eye`, shared with The Searching Eye): an encounter card with a player back in a player's deck, drawn, held and discarded from the hand or the deck like a player card, never played (it prints no cost); after it is drawn the rest of the hand is discarded.
  - Soldier of Isengard: each player sets 1 copy aside; the other copies stay in the encounter deck (`data/scenarios/helms-deep.json`, `perPlayer`).
- **The Road to Isengard** (`the-road-to-isengard`; records in `src/effects/authored/the-road-to-isengard.ts`, Wizardry in `src/rules/families/wizardry/`, the pressure on the hands in `src/rules/families/deck/pressure.ts`). Wizardry (`mec112.wizardry`) is a location's own line: its record is a Forced effect whose trigger is `wizardry` (the printed timing in `data/` stays a constant line), answered as the location leaves play explored, in the staging area as well as the active location, and run by `TriggerWizardry` for Machines of Isengard and for Saruman. Each reading below is applied as researched or recommended; none was confirmed by the user.
  - Poisoned Vapour (143061:A:2): every damaged character is exhausted when it resolves, and until the end of the combat phase (`endOfCombat`) every character that is damaged at that moment, also one damaged or healed later, has a blank text box with its Traits kept: a class holder `damagedCharacters`, so a healed character is no longer blank (`rr.lasting-effects`; `faq.lasting-effects` names the card).
  - Saruman's Voice (143068:A:2): the revealing player chooses a hero committed to the quest, any player's ("a hero", not "a hero you control"); with none committed nothing is removed and nothing attaches (agent recommendation; `mec112.peril`, `mec112.saga-staging-rules`; no ruling found). "Triggers its ability" is the hero's own Action or Response initiated by its controller and its own Forced effect when it triggers (`rr.triggered-ability`; the `abilityTriggered` event); each one is a discard of 1 card of the controller's choice.
  - The Voice of Saruman (143080:B:3): "if a player has 0 cards in his hand" is a constant "if" (`rr.constant-ability`), checked at each settle once the effect or step that emptied the hand has finished, not in the middle of an instruction; a player who plays their last card is eliminated when it has resolved (agent recommendation; `rr.player-elimination`; no discussion found). Discarding the last card is legal on 3B and eliminates the player.
  - Saruman's Staff (143048:A:1): "draws any number of cards" is any draw of 1 or more cards, the resource phase's included, once for each player in player order; a draw of 0 cards and an effect that adds a card to a hand do not trigger it (the FFG forum, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/126186-after-a-player-draws-any-number-of-cards-clarification, and Vision of the Palantir, https://visionofthepalantir.com/2021/09/18/the-road-to-isengard/, by analogy; agent recommendation for the per-player order). Stage 3A's draw of 6 is not heard: the Staff is attached at the end of the same effect and a Forced effect must be in play and active when its trigger occurs (`rr.forced`; `faq.triggering-forced-effects`). The attack deals 2 shadow cards, then 1 damage is removed from Saruman.
  - Saruman's Staff (143048:A:2) and the last stage: while Saruman has any hit points left (6 hit points; he is indestructible) the stage cannot be defeated, so 6 damage on Saruman clears the gate and defeating 3B then wins (the stage waits, defeated, as other win gates do); with 15 progress and Saruman unhurt it does not win (the card's words; a playthrough, https://talesfromthecards.wordpress.com/2016/11/30/campaign-mode-road-to-isengard-part-2/, wins the same way).
  - Rouse the Ents (143078:B:1): "cannot gain resources" bars the resource phase's collection and every effect that adds resources to a pool, not the heroes' starting resources (placed before 1B is the current stage); no commitment, no staging, no draw (the card's words; the FFG forum, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/180386-road-to-isengard-stage-1-b, and Vision of the Palantir, https://visionofthepalantir.com/2021/09/18/the-road-to-isengard/, agree).
  - Ent of Fangorn (143046:A:1): taken into control, it is an ally under the triggering player and out of the staging area, so stage 1B's "remove each Ent objective in the staging area" no longer reaches it (`rr.objective-cards`). The Quest Action is any player's: "a hero you control" is the triggering player's, the compare reads the hero's current Willpower against the discarded card's printed or modified Threat (no Threat is 0), and 3 resources are discarded as the player takes control (the card's words, `rr.the-word-choose`).
  - Saruman (143047:A:2) "as if he is the only player": each "each player" of the Wizardry reads the defending player alone, a number of players reads 1 (Steaming Vent's X is 2) and "characters in play" reads the defending player's characters (confirmed for "as if he is the only player" by https://visionofthepalantir.com/2021/09/18/the-road-to-isengard/, a fan review; the card's words for the rest).
  - Saruman and Wolf of Isengard (143047:A:2, 143052:A:1) "is dealt a shadow card with a Wizardry effect": the Forced fires when the shadow card is flipped in the attack (a facedown card's text is unknown, and a card discarded unflipped never triggers it), once for each such shadow card; the Wizardry line of the shadow card does nothing by itself, a shadow card having only its Shadow text active (the FFG forum, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/233249-shadow-cards-and-when-they-are-dealt-effects: two community answers agree; no official ruling).
  - Wolf of Isengard (143052:A:1): with an empty hand the discard cannot be chosen (`rr.effects`: an effect that can change the game must be selected), so only the additional attack is offered; the additional attack is dealt a new shadow card (`faq.additional-attacks`). The attack option exists only while an attack is under way, which is always the case when the Forced effect fires (agent recommendation for a Wizardry card dealt outside an attack).
  - Machines of Isengard (143062:A:1) triggers the Wizardry of the active location only, never a location in the staging area and without exploring it; it gains surge when there is no active location or it prints no Wizardry line (Gates of Isengard, Orthanc), also when the Wizardry changes nothing (the card's words).
  - Wizardry on exploring: it fires for a location explored in the staging area as for the active one (`mec112.wizardry`: "even if it is not the active location"); with Breaking Isengard's Forced effect (143079:B:2) on the same explore the first player orders them (`rr.forced`).
  - Orthanc (143080:B:2): when it is explored all the progress placed on the quest this phase is cancelled, the overflow of the quest resolution that explored it included (it is placed on the quest before the Forced effect), and Orthanc returns to the staging area with its progress tokens removed; the tokens would explore it again at once (FAQ 1.18). Agent recommendation; Hall of Beorn, Vision of the Palantir and BoardGameGeek were searched (BoardGameGeek answered 403) and none discusses it.
  - Orthanc (143053:A:2): the first player looks at the top 3 cards, reveals 1 and then discards the others, in the printed order: the chosen card is revealed and resolved first (a surge or an extra reveal on it reaches the looked-at cards still on top), then the looked-at cards left on top are discarded. Agent recommendation (the printed order); no official ruling: the only discussion found, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/190299-encounter-deck-drawing-and-reshuffling-questions, is about a deck with 1 card left, and Hall of Beorn, https://www.hallofbeorn.com/LotR/Details/Orthanc-ToS, only prints the card.
  - Ring-wall of Isengard (143055:A:1): "its owner" is the controller of the healed character (an Ent objective has no owner); that player discards 1 card of their choice; a heal of 0 does not trigger it (agent recommendation).
  - Open Pit (143059:A:1): "from a player card effect" is a draw made by the ability of a player card (hero, ally, attachment, event or a gained ability), not the resource phase's draw (a framework effect) and not an encounter or quest card's (`rr.effects` defines card effect and framework effect).
  - Isengard Orc (143051:A:2): with an empty hand the discard cannot be chosen, so the encounter card is revealed (`rr.effects`).
  - Palantír of Orthanc (143020:A:2): Aragorn and the Palantír are exhausted as the cost, paid also when the compare fails; adding it to the victory display and removing it from the campaign pool is optional ("may"), and the controller chooses the player (`rr.the-word-choose`); that player skips 1 of their reveals this phase, to a minimum of 0, and nobody else's reveals are shortened; the removal from the campaign pool is skipped in a standalone game.
  - Breaking Isengard (143079:B:2, B:3): the damage token counts every non-unique location explored, in the staging area or the active one (Orthanc and Gates of Isengard are unique and do not count); the Quest Action places 2 progress on a non-unique location in the staging area or the active one, with an Ent ally the player controls and has ready.
  - Steaming Vent (143057:A:1): the committing player deals the damage to a questing character they control; with none, nothing. Blast of Sorcery (143060:A:1): X is the players plus 1, assigned by the revealing player among the characters committed to the quest; its Shadow reads the defending player's hand (1 or fewer cards).
  - Plain of Isengard (143058:A:1, A:2): its Threat is the number of players, and while it is in the staging area archery damage goes to allies only when the players control one.
  - Table (`docs/design-system-table.md`, Cards, The Road to Isengard; `src/play/readouts/isengard.ts`): the top bar's dials for the resources on stage 1B, labelled as the encounter cards revealed on advancing (143078:B:2), the damage on stage 2B against the 5 that advance the players (143079:B:2, a goal dial, no warning), one dial for the Ent of Fangorn closest to control against the 3 that give a player control (143046:A:1, a goal dial, every Ent's count in its spoken words), Saruman's gate while the Staff is on him (143048:A:2, a goal dial: his damage fills the ring toward his hit points, the hit points left on the disc) and, on stage 3B, each seat's cards in hand in its own threat dial's label ("P1 · 3 cards"), with a danger edge and a spoken warning at 1 ("last card") and 0 (143080:B:3, the elimination being the loss); a discard of the last card says it eliminates (the Isengard Orc's and the Wolf's options). The choice of a Wizardry names its location, and the one resolved from Saruman's shadow card says the defending player resolves it as the only player (143047:A:2). Saruman's Voice is drawn under its hero (143068:A:2).
- **The Passage of the Marshes** (`the-passage-of-the-marshes`; records in `src/effects/authored/the-passage-of-the-marshes.ts`, Mire in `src/rules/families/mire/`, Gollum and Sméagol in `src/rules/families/gollum/`). Mire X (`mec112.mire`) is the flag `mire` a location's constant record sets to its X (the printed keyword is not read): at the end of the refresh phase (`crossBoundary`, after the first player token pass and the frames it pushed, `rr.step-7-4` before `rr.step-7-5`, and before that moment's Forced effects) each Mire location in the staging area gets 1 mire token, a resource on the card, and a location whose tokens reach X is discarded at once (`PlaceMireToken`), to the encounter discard pile, never the victory display, and hears `discardedByMire` (not an explored or otherwise discarded location). Gollum and Sméagol are one physical card whose sides are the catalog cards 144075 and 144076 (`mec112.gollum-smeagol`; `FlipGollum`). Gollum's printed Immune keyword is turned off by an override (`data/overrides.json`): the flag `immuneToNonFellowship` holds the rule, read by `playerCardEffectCanChoose` with the source card's sphere on every path a player card's effect takes (picks, attachment hosts, damage, the enemy list). Each reading below is applied as researched or recommended; none was confirmed by the user.
  - Mire tokens (144019:A:1, 144020:A:2, 144021:A:1, 144022:A:1, 144023:A:1): the tokens come from the end of the refresh phase or any effect and are checked after each token placed, at least X discards; simultaneous discards resolve their Forced effects oldest card first, each completely (agent recommendation; `mec112.mire`, `rr.forced`). Soft Mires' reveal is the first player's, also when the discard comes during another player's defense (Groping Dead's Forced or Shadow): `RevealEncounter` `who: firstPlayer`, as 144065:B:3's (card words, `rr.first-player`).
  - Groping Dead (144016:A:2, A:3): a token placed on the active location counts, so at X the active location is discarded by Mire (its progress is lost, the Forced fires); with no active location nothing is placed. The Forced fires when the character is destroyed by Groping Dead's attack, the Shadow when the attack destroys one (agent recommendation; Nightmare Land of Shadow places tokens on the active location, https://visionofthepalantir.com/2022/05/02/the-passage-of-the-marshes/ , https://rulespal.com/lotr-nightmare-the-land-of-shadow/rulebook ; no ruling found).
  - Creeping Marshes (144024:A:2): attached to the current quest (Doomed 1 still raises each player); at the end of the refresh phase, after the keyword's own placement, it puts 1 mire token on each location in the staging area (one without Mire keeps it, which does nothing but add to Stagnant Pools' Threat) and is discarded; "Then, discard" is not held back by there being no location in the staging area (placing a token on each of none is complete, `rr.then`), so it is discarded at every refresh phase it is attached for; if the stage changes first it goes with the old stage. No official ruling or community answer was found (the Soft Mires thread, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/225023-soft-mires-location-question , and Vision of the Palantir's review of the scenario do not touch it): agent recommendation, not user-confirmed (`mec112.mire`, `rr.then`, `rr.attachment-cards`).

- **Aragorn in a campaign** (`mec112.campaign-mode-aragorn`). When a scenario features ☺ Aragorn and a player recorded Aragorn as a hero, that player loses control of that version and may choose another hero without the +1 threat penalty, records it, and moves the Permanent cards of the old Aragorn to ☺ Aragorn. If Aragorn is on the list of fallen heroes he is removed from it and each player takes a permanent +1 threat penalty. The Campaign Setup Checklist does this (`src/campaign/checklist.ts`): the lost Aragorn's replacement is the one free change (a second hero traded away is the ordinary +1 trade), the fallen Aragorn's +1 is not a hero change and needs no acceptance, and the log it returns is the one a win writes.
- **Continuing the saga.** A new Two Towers save may take in a finished Fellowship save's log while the Fellowship save stays as it was (`src/campaign/continue.ts`): the heroes, fallen heroes, threat penalty, campaign pool (each card's burden set and the hero it is attached to), captives and notes are copied, `completed`, `earned` and the scenario scores start empty, the new save takes the finished save's seat count (a log is per seat), and `log.continuedFrom` is the finished save's id, so each Fellowship save is offered once (`carriedFrom` is kept for a finished save's carry-over, so a save may take both). The Campaign Total carries on from the Fellowship log in a continued save (`log.scoreBase`, added to the scenario scores) and starts at zero in a fresh one (user decision, 2026-11-07: no source settles it, and the saga is one campaign across the boxes).
- **A fresh start** (user decision, 2026-11-07). A Two Towers save may start with no Fellowship behind it, from an empty log: no captives (a seat with no logged captive chooses its own in The Uruk-hai, as below), an empty pool and the per-scenario saga hero. The briefing says the save starts fresh.
- **Standalone** (user decision, 2026-11-07), as the Fellowship box is (`mec112.campaign-mode`): no campaign card, no boons or burdens, the per-scenario saga start.
- **The Black Gate** (Journey to the Cross-roads Resolution). The name and quantity of each enemy under The Black Gate is a structured log fact that the log screen shows in the notes section, since The Black Gate Opens (MEC113) may read it (user decision, 2026-11-07).
- **The per-seat threat penalty.** The Passage of the Marshes' Resolution has each player take a permanent +2 starting threat penalty or earn The Searching Eye, "record each player's choice in the campaign log": the printed text is per player, so the log keeps `threatPenaltyBySeat` beside the table's `threatPenalty` and the choice in `marshesChoice`. A seat's starting threat is its heroes' threat cost plus `threatPenalty` plus its own penalty (`StartSeat.threatPenalty`); the Threat Modifiers row reads one number until a seat has its own. The log's columns and the checklist read them; the Resolution of The Passage of the Marshes writes them ("The campaign cards" below).
- **The campaign cards** (`src/effects/authored/the-two-towers.ts`, verbs in `src/catalog/families/campaign.ts`). Setup, dealt by the Campaign Setup Checklist at step 6 (`src/campaign/checklist-two-towers.ts`): The Uruk-hai removes Gildor Inglorion and Mr. Underhill from the pool for good; the burdens of the Fellowship's A Shadow of the Past, Flight to the Ford and The Ring Goes South are left out of the encounter deck in scenarios 7 to 9, and those of Helm's Deep and The Road to Isengard out of the encounter deck and each player's deck in 10 to 12 (the cards stay in the pool: the Setup only leaves them out of the decks it builds); scenarios 8 and 10 waive the +1 hero-change penalty; The Passage of the Marshes shuffles a Searching Eye into each deck; Journey to the Cross-roads has each player add 1 resource to each hero or shuffle a Brace of Coneys into their deck (`StartSeat.resources`, `ScenarioState.chosenAtSetup`); Shelob's Lair attaches A Heavy Burden to the Ring-bearer. Resolution (`src/rules/families/campaign/resolution.ts`): with the pursuit value at 25 or lower (`ScenarioState.pursuit`, set by the scenario's own Setup) The Uruk-hai's players choose, the first player answering for the group, between a Skill boon for each player and Beyond All Hope for one player, who takes a hero off the list of fallen heroes and has the boon attached to him (no +1 penalty; the choice is offered only while the list holds a hero); a player left with more than 3 non-Fellowship heroes removes heroes from the log, one at a time, never a Fellowship hero. Helm's Deep earns Poisoned Counsels for each player holding a copy in hand, deck or discard pile. The Road to Isengard earns Saruman's Voice if any player holds 3 or fewer cards and the Palantír of Orthanc for the first player if any holds 3 or more. The Passage of the Marshes asks each player in turn for the +2 penalty or a copy of The Searching Eye, and writes `threatPenaltyBySeat` and `marshesChoice`. Journey to the Cross-roads earns each Brace of Coneys chosen at Setup and writes the enemies under The Black Gate to `blackGate`. Shelob's Lair earns A Heavy Burden. Every choice is a window on the resolution screen (`src/campaign/resolution-two-towers.ts` words it); no control types a number. Burdens that function like a player card (Poisoned Counsels, The Searching Eye: `mec112.poisoned-counsels-searching-eye`) are earned for a player and shuffled into that player's deck after the starting hand later.
