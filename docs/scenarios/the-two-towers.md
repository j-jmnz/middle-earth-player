# The Two Towers

The rulings, readings and table pieces of The Two Towers, scenario by scenario. Product rules, the Domain and the Core round are in `docs/brief.md`; the authority order is in `.claude/rules/authority.md`.

MEC112 (2023) reprints **The Treason of Saruman** (RingsDB pack `ToS`) and **The Land of Shadow** (pack `LoS`) as the second saga box: six scenarios, numbered 7 to 12. Its catalog pack code `TTwT` is Hall of Beorn's slug suffix, as `TFotR` is the Fellowship's; each of its 156 cards takes the code of its twin (143001 to 143081, 144001 to 144076), a title both boxes print (Aragorn, Frodo Baggins, The One Ring, Gollum) pairing with its own box's twin. The campaign id is `the-two-towers`: its own campaign and save with the one `CampaignLog` type (user decision, 2026-11-07). Its data is built (`data/campaigns/the-two-towers.json`, six scenario files) and the app offers it as a campaign and as a box on the Scenarios tab (`src/screens/data/campaign-data.ts`) with its own booklet (MEC112); only the scenarios `src/campaign/playable.ts` lists are playable, and its campaign tile unlocks with the first of them (`data/campaigns/index.json`, `playable`), which is when the continuation offer below reaches the Start screen. The six campaign cards' Setup and Resolution lines are effect records (`src/effects/authored/the-two-towers.ts`; "The campaign cards" below).

- **Saga start per scenario** (`mec112.setting-up-the-game`). The first player takes control of ☺ Aragorn (143001) in scenarios 7 to 9, and of a ☺ hero with the Ring-bearer trait with The One Ring (144063) attached in 10 to 12. Aragorn does not count against the hero limit, so no Ring-bearer slot is asked for 7 to 9 (`mec112.aragorn`); the One Ring's rules are the Fellowship's (`mec112.the-one-ring`, `mec112.multiplayer-the-one-ring`). The scenario data records it (`ScenarioData.sagaStart`); the campaign's `prelude` is empty. The briefing, the loadout, the checklist and the start read the scenario's own saga start (`src/campaign/saga.ts`), and the Fellowship, whose six scenarios all use the Ring-bearer, reads as before. ☺ Aragorn is dealt to the first player and never written to the log's hero list; the log keeps the Fellowship heroes it holds (the Ring-bearer Frodo while ☺ Aragorn is granted), and a hero of the Fellowship sphere is never a hero change.
- **Fellowship heroes** (`mec112.the-fellowship-sphere`, `mec112.fellowship-sphere-heroes`). One ☺ hero in play at a time, and no other version of Aragorn or of Frodo Baggins as a starting hero or in the deck. The Ring-bearer Frodo stays on the log but is not dealt as a hero in scenarios 7 to 9, and returns for 10 to 12 (user decision, 2026-11-07, from the Treason of Saruman campaign-mode rule that players cannot use a card titled Frodo Baggins in those scenarios; the pinned MEC112 rulebook words only the one-☺-hero rule).
- **Banned titles** (`mec112.saruman-grima-smeagol`). No ally or hero titled Saruman, Gríma or Sméagol in any of the six scenarios.
- **Saga staging** (`mec112.saga-staging-rules`) is the Fellowship's rule: each player reveals one encounter card in player order.
- **Aragorn in a campaign** (`mec112.campaign-mode-aragorn`). When a scenario features ☺ Aragorn and a player recorded Aragorn as a hero, that player loses control of that version and may choose another hero without the +1 threat penalty, records it, and moves the Permanent cards of the old Aragorn to ☺ Aragorn. If Aragorn is on the list of fallen heroes he is removed from it and each player takes a permanent +1 threat penalty. The Campaign Setup Checklist does this (`src/campaign/checklist.ts`): the lost Aragorn's replacement is the one free change (a second hero traded away is the ordinary +1 trade), the fallen Aragorn's +1 is not a hero change and needs no acceptance, and the log it returns is the one a win writes.
- **Continuing the saga.** A new Two Towers save may take in a finished Fellowship save's log while the Fellowship save stays as it was (`src/campaign/continue.ts`): the heroes, fallen heroes, threat penalty, campaign pool (each card's burden set and the hero it is attached to), captives and notes are copied, `completed`, `earned` and the scenario scores start empty, the new save takes the finished save's seat count (a log is per seat), and `log.continuedFrom` is the finished save's id, so each Fellowship save is offered once (`carriedFrom` is kept for a finished save's carry-over, so a save may take both). The Campaign Total carries on from the Fellowship log in a continued save (`log.scoreBase`, added to the scenario scores) and starts at zero in a fresh one (user decision, 2026-11-07: no source settles it, and the saga is one campaign across the boxes).
- **A fresh start** (user decision, 2026-11-07). A Two Towers save may start with no Fellowship behind it, from an empty log: no captives (a seat with no logged captive chooses its own in The Uruk-hai, as below), an empty pool and the per-scenario saga hero. The briefing says the save starts fresh.
- **Standalone** (user decision, 2026-11-07), as the Fellowship box is (`mec112.campaign-mode`): no campaign card, no boons or burdens, the per-scenario saga start.
- **The Black Gate** (Journey to the Cross-roads Resolution). The name and quantity of each enemy under The Black Gate is a structured log fact that the log screen shows in the notes section, since The Black Gate Opens (MEC113) may read it (user decision, 2026-11-07).
- **The per-seat threat penalty.** The Passage of the Marshes' Resolution has each player take a permanent +2 starting threat penalty or earn The Searching Eye, "record each player's choice in the campaign log": the printed text is per player, so the log keeps `threatPenaltyBySeat` beside the table's `threatPenalty` and the choice in `marshesChoice`. A seat's starting threat is its heroes' threat cost plus `threatPenalty` plus its own penalty (`StartSeat.threatPenalty`); the Threat Modifiers row reads one number until a seat has its own. The log's columns and the checklist read them; the Resolution of The Passage of the Marshes writes them ("The campaign cards" below).
- **The campaign cards** (`src/effects/authored/the-two-towers.ts`, verbs in `src/catalog/families/campaign.ts`). Setup, dealt by the Campaign Setup Checklist at step 6 (`src/campaign/checklist-two-towers.ts`): The Uruk-hai removes Gildor Inglorion and Mr. Underhill from the pool for good; the burdens of the Fellowship's A Shadow of the Past, Flight to the Ford and The Ring Goes South are left out of the encounter deck in scenarios 7 to 9, and those of Helm's Deep and The Road to Isengard out of the encounter deck and each player's deck in 10 to 12 (the cards stay in the pool: the Setup only leaves them out of the decks it builds); scenarios 8 and 10 waive the +1 hero-change penalty; The Passage of the Marshes shuffles a Searching Eye into each deck; Journey to the Cross-roads has each player add 1 resource to each hero or shuffle a Brace of Coneys into their deck (`StartSeat.resources`, `ScenarioState.chosenAtSetup`); Shelob's Lair attaches A Heavy Burden to the Ring-bearer. Resolution (`src/rules/families/campaign/resolution.ts`): with the pursuit value at 25 or lower (`ScenarioState.pursuit`, set by the scenario's own Setup) The Uruk-hai's players choose, the first player answering for the group, between a Skill boon for each player and Beyond All Hope for one player, who takes a hero off the list of fallen heroes and has the boon attached to him (no +1 penalty; the choice is offered only while the list holds a hero); a player left with more than 3 non-Fellowship heroes removes heroes from the log, one at a time, never a Fellowship hero. Helm's Deep earns Poisoned Counsels for each player holding a copy in hand, deck or discard pile. The Road to Isengard earns Saruman's Voice if any player holds 3 or fewer cards and the Palantír of Orthanc for the first player if any holds 3 or more. The Passage of the Marshes asks each player in turn for the +2 penalty or a copy of The Searching Eye, and writes `threatPenaltyBySeat` and `marshesChoice`. Journey to the Cross-roads earns each Brace of Coneys chosen at Setup and writes the enemies under The Black Gate to `blackGate`. Shelob's Lair earns A Heavy Burden. Every choice is a window on the resolution screen (`src/campaign/resolution-two-towers.ts` words it); no control types a number. Burdens that function like a player card (Poisoned Counsels, The Searching Eye: `mec112.poisoned-counsels-searching-eye`) are earned for a player and shuffled into that player's deck after the starting hand later.
- **Data.** Gollum / Sméagol is one card with two sides (`mec112.gollum-smeagol`): Gollum faceup in scenario 10, Sméagol in 12. A burden or boon the campaign deals, and the copies each player sets aside (Soldier of Isengard), are not in the encounter pile. Easy counts come from the DragnCards `E0C.07` to `E0C.12` decks, each disagreement with Hall of Beorn recorded as an override with its reason (user decision, 2026-11-07). The six Two Towers Nightmare decks stay unattached.
