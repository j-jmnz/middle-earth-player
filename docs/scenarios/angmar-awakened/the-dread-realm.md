# The Dread Realm

Part 9 and the last (`10193`; `mec108.reanimated-dead`, `mec108.indestructible`, `mec108.campaign-mode`, `mec108.campaign-log`, `rr.campaign-pool`, `rr.removed-from-game`, `rr.winning-the-game`). Stages 10150 (1A/1B, 18 quest points on B), 10151 (2A/2B, none) and 10152 (3A/3B, none); Daechanar (10153) and the Altar of Midwinter (10154) are set aside by the Setup (the data does it), the other encounter cards are The Dread Realm (101156-101164, Dwimmerlaik 10155), Cursed Dead and Dark Sorcery; Easy drops Tombs of Carn Dûm and Possession (Hall of Beorn's scenario page agrees card by card: https://hallofbeorn.com/LotR/Scenarios/The-Dread-Realm). Neither Thaurdir, Iârion nor the objective Amarthiúl is dealt. The campaign ends with this win: the card's Resolution is the line "The players win the campaign!" and nothing else (`10193:B:1` is a copy of 11215:B:4): it asks nothing, writes nothing but the win and marks nothing, so the path reads 9 of 9 completed with nothing to begin ("Angmar Awakened won. The players win the campaign!", `src/screens/campaign/campaign-end.ts`); a loss writes nothing. Amarthiúl is usable here (no ban after part 7).

- **Mechanics.** Family `angmar` (`src/catalog/families/angmar.ts`, `src/rules/families/angmar/reanimate/`; the records are `src/effects/authored/angmar-awakened/the-dread-realm.ts`, the campaign card's in `angmar-awakened.ts`). A Reanimated Dead is the card itself, renamed to `<code>~dead` (`src/rules/core/printed.ts`): a facedown Undead enemy with 0 engagement cost, 2 Threat, 2 Attack, 2 Defense and 2 hit points (`mec108.reanimated-dead`), engaged with a player or in the staging area, that goes to its owner's discard pile, faceup, on every exit (`core/zones.ts`). `Reanimate` (`deckTop`, `handRandom`, `discardTop`, `card`, `destroyed`; `who` the actor, the first player, the controller of the ally an effect destroyed or the defending player), `DealSourceAsShadow`, `ResolveWhenRevealedOf`, `AttachEnemyToPicked`, `DestroyHighestCostAlly`, `DiscardOtherInPlay`, `RemovePickedFromGame`; `DealShadowInPlay` gains `trait`; the flags `passesSorcery`, `discardsHost`, `mustCommit` and the triggers `shadowsResolved` and `attachmentLeft` are engine use. `PlaceProgressInStaging divided` divides progress freely. Every change is gated on the new card state, flags or triggers.
- **Seats.** One to four seats in the data; the goldens and fixtures play one or two.
- **Readings.** The local FAQ spans (`faq.*`) say nothing about reanimating; `mec108.reanimated-dead` is the box's own rule.   1. 10150:A:1 Setup "Each player reveals an encounter card from the top of the encounter deck. Each player reanimates the top card of his deck.": after the opening hands and the campaign Setup (`rr.setup`), in the text's order: each player in player order reveals one card (staged, When Revealed resolved), then each reanimates the top card of their own deck (none from an empty deck). One card, not 3: the QOTW blog lists "Stage 1A should read 'top 3 cards'" among its achievements, but the scan, Hall of Beorn and the 2018 review say the top card and the printed card wins (`.claude/rules/authority.md`). Literal; the order span-settled.
  2. `mec108.reanimated-dead`: the card is an Undead enemy of 0/2/2/2/2 for every rule that reads an enemy (attacking, defending, shadow cards `rr.step-6-2`, "each Undead enemy", the staging threat, "engaged with you" counts, Amarthiúl's text), "in play" although facedown (the box's own rule and Unholy Crypt's "Reanimated Dead in play" prevail over `rr.in-play-and-out-of-play`; a shadow card is the thing facedown that is not in play), with no printed text, trait or icon while reanimated, able to hold attachments (Possession). It leaves play to its owner's discard pile on any exit, never the encounter discard (Dead Lord, Seal the Tomb and Cursed Dead do not find one). The stage reminders of 10150:B:1, 10151:B:1 and 10152:B:1 are inert marks: the engine applies the box rule itself. Span-settled; "in play" is agent recommendation (no source: the cards would never count them otherwise).
  3. Reanimating counts as engaging the player (`rr.engaged`), so "after an enemy engages you" Responses hear it; a Reanimated Dead put into the staging area (Possession) is not engaged. Researched, community (clear and agreed, no staff): https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/196299-do-reanimated-dead-enemies-engage-you.
  4. Which card: the deck's top cards of any type (as many as it holds, none from an empty deck), the destroyed hero or ally taken out of its owner's discard pile (`rr.destroy`: a destruction first, so its leaves-play triggers and its attachments' discarding happen; nothing is reanimated when an earlier effect of the same attack already did), a random card from the hand (the game's seed), Tombs' discarded card only when an ally, a copy for Dark Halls. Span-settled for a destroyed character, literal otherwise.
  5. "Deal it 1 shadow card" (Dwimmerlaik, Unholy Crypt, Dark Halls, Sinister Dungeon): at once, from the top of the encounter deck, facedown; the Reanimated Dead keeps it until it attacks and step 6.2 gives it one more, so it can hold two; every shadow card it holds resolves in its attack and is discarded after it (`faq.enemy-attacks-outside-combat`); one it holds when the combat phase ends is discarded (`rr.step-6-11`); one dealt during the enemy attacks step may let the engaged Reanimated Dead attack in the same step (`rr.step-6-4a`). The same-step attack and the two cards: agent recommendation (the 2018 review is community).
  6. "If this attack destroys a character, reanimate that character" (Unholy Crypt, Dwimmerlaik's shadow, Dark Halls): the defending player reanimates (`rr.defending-player`), so it stands in front of them also when another player's sentinel defended; Dwimmerlaik's Forced says "the defending player" outright. Agent recommendation for the shadow.
  7. Possession (101162:A:2): the highest printed cost ally of every player (an X or no cost is 0; the first player breaks a tie) is destroyed, that ally's controller reanimates it into the staging area (not engaged) and Possession attaches to it; the card counts as a Sorcery card and as a Condition attachment while attached, so an effect discarding a Condition attachment finds it. It is a triggered record, not a Forced (the line has no label): discarding Possession discards the Reanimated Dead to its owner's discard pile; removing it from the game (Daechanar's Forced) leaves the Reanimated Dead in the staging area with nothing attached (`rr.removed-from-game`). With no ally in play nothing happens (Doomed 1 still raises each player's threat). Literal; the removal case agent recommendation.
  8. A Fell Dread (101163:A:1): the player with the highest threat attaches it to a hero they control without a copy; ties go to the first player; with no eligible hero nothing is attached. Willpower is set to 0 (`rr.modifiers`) and the hero must commit to the quest at 3.2 if able. Both effects hold only while the host is a hero. Literal; the ties agent recommendation.
  9. Death and Calamity (101164:A:1): attached to the current quest, then, only if it was attached (`rr.then`), the first player reanimates the top X cards, X the faceup copies in play. Shadow: +X Attack, X the Sorcery cards in play, the shadow card itself not counted. Literal.
  10. Witch of Angmar (101156:A:1): after each shadow effect has resolved, the When Revealed of each Sorcery shadow card dealt to her resolves, the defending player revealing it, in the order dealt; her text gives the trait of a facedown card back (the Golden Rule exception). Researched: the official FAQ entry on Banks of the Anduin, "When a card is drawn as a Shadow card, only its Shadow text is considered to be active", read as quoted in https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/189380-clarification-on-faq-about-shadow-cards, and https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/201643-in-play-and-shadow-cards.
  11. "Sorcery cards in play" (Daechanar, Death and Calamity's shadow): faceup cards with the trait in play, attachments on heroes, allies, enemies, quest cards and a Reanimated Dead included; not a facedown shadow card, a discard pile or the victory display; not Daechanar (a Sorcerer). Researched, community (cmabr002, Ecthelion III, Seastan, GrandSpleen, DukeWellington agree): https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/201643-in-play-and-shadow-cards.
  12. Daechanar (10153:A:3): each damage instance that would put 1 or more damage on him is canceled whole while a Sorcery card is in play and the first player removes one from the game (not discarded: no leaves-play Forced of the Wraith fires); with none in play the damage lands. Indestructible while one is in play stays as the second layer. The chooser and the whole-instance reading are agent recommendation (the 2018 review says "may", the card "remove").
  13. Wraith of Carn Dûm (101157:A:1): when it would leave play (destroyed, discarded or shuffled away) it instead attaches to a hero or ally of any player chosen by the first player, with no character in play it leaves as it was going; attached it is an attachment with the quoted text only (-1 Willpower, -1 Attack, -1 Defense, to a character host only), counts as a Sorcery card, and returns to the staging area as an enemy (undamaged) when the host leaves play; discarded as an attachment it is simply discarded. Agent recommendation (the 2018 review's "you'll need to remove it in stage 2" supports that it can be removed).
  14. Sinister Dungeon (101158): whenever explored it is dealt facedown to a new Reanimated Dead (a random card of the first player's hand) as a shadow card instead of going to the discard pile; with an empty hand it goes to the encounter discard as ever. Its shadow discards the top 2 encounter cards and resolves the When Revealed of each Sorcery card among them (attaching from the discard pile). Literal; the Dungeon kept out of the discard pile agent recommendation (https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/11/11/the-dread-realm/, community-sourced).
  15. Tombs of Carn Dûm (101159): each copy in play resolves at the end of the round in an order the first player chooses; each player discards the top card of their deck and reanimates it when an ally. Travel deals each Undead enemy in play 1 shadow card, a cost payable only when the encounter deck holds one for each (`rr.travel`, `rr.cost`). Literal.
  16. Dark Halls (101160): while in the staging area each Reanimated Dead has +1 Attack and +1 Defense, per copy; its shadow reanimates a copy (same printed title, `rr.unique-cards`) of the defending character from the defending player's own discard pile with 1 shadow card ("it" can only be that copy); nothing when undefended. Agent recommendation for the shadow.
  17. Unholy Crypt (101161): +1 Threat for each Reanimated Dead in play, engaged and staged, read live. Literal.
  18. Stage 1B (10150:B:2): the Sorcery attachments of the main quest card move to stage 2B when it advances (attachments on a side quest are not "this stage"); from 2B to 3B none are carried. Literal. Stage 2 (10151:A:1): Daechanar joins the staging area, the Altar becomes the active location (the previous one returns to the staging area, no Travel) and each player reanimates; 2B advances when Daechanar is destroyed (his Victory 10 puts him in the victory display). Literal.
  19. Altar of Midwinter (10154): no progress can be placed on it while Daechanar is in play (what it cannot take goes to the quest card: agent recommendation); at the end of the staging step the first player reveals 1 additional card or reanimates the top card of their own deck (the second not offered with an empty deck, `rr.effects`).
  20. Stage 3 (10152:A:1): the first player searches the encounter deck and discard pile for locations (no reveal), adding them until the staging area holds one for each player, then the deck is shuffled (precedent, agent recommendation). 10152:B:2 "divided as you choose": progress that would be placed on the quest after the active location took its share (`rr.active-location`) is divided by the first player among the staged locations, a location and then an amount from 1 up to what it still needs, again and again until none is left or no location can take more (the rest stays on the quest, where it does nothing, `faq.progress-without-advancing`); a Sinister Dungeon the first player keeps unexplored is a division the card allows. Agent recommendation (the 2018 review: "can be placed on locations in the staging area"). 10152:B:3 at the end of every quest phase while stage 3 is the main quest (`faq.triggering-forced-effects`): with any location in play (the staging area and the active location; no location is player-controlled) every hero and ally takes 1 damage as a card effect; with none in play the players win the game and 10193's Resolution ends the campaign. Literal.
  21. 10193:A:2 Setup: each player may search their deck for a boon and add it to their opening hand, then each hero starts with the damage the Campaign Log noted for it (after the quest card's Setup and the reanimations; a changed hero takes none). Literal. The pool at this deal: Thaurdir's burdens are removed from this game only and stay in the pool, Amarthiúl's Courage attaches to the hero Amarthiúl or is removed from this game only (`rr.removed-from-game`, the Wastes of Eriador reading), Arnor Ravaged is staged, Omens, Determination, Fend Off Despair and the Loot and Clue boons deal as in the earlier parts.
