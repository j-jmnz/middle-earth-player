# Across the Ettenmoors

Part 6 (`10182`; `mec108.safe`, `mec108.objective-locations`, `mec108.objective-allies`, `mec108.side-quests-in-play`, `mec108.immune`, `mec108.campaign-setup-across-the-ettenmoors`, `mec108.campaign-mode`, `mec108.campaign-log`, `rr.guarded`, `rr.campaign-pool`). Stages 10064 (1A/1B, 10 on B), 10065 (2A/2B, 20) and 10066 (3A/3B, 17), read from the scans; Amarthiúl (10067), the four safe objective-locations (10068 to 10071) and the three side quests Lie Low, Forage for Food and Scavenge for Supplies (10081 to 10083) are set aside by the deal; the Savage Trollspawn (10072) are dealt staged, one for each player, and their other copies stay in the encounter deck; the encounter deck is the Across the Ettenmoors cards (10073 to 10080), the Eriador Wilds (09047 to 09051) and Foul Weather (09052 to 09055). Hall of Beorn exports no quest points for the safe locations and the three side quests, so `data/overrides.json` holds them, read from the scans (Secluded Cave 7, The Hoarwell 3, Abandoned Camp 1, Patch of Woods 5, Lie Low 8, Forage for Food 6, Scavenge for Supplies 7); every other number matches its scan. The campaign card (10182) is dealt in Campaign Mode only; Losing Time (10183, five copies) is its burden, and the Loot the Dungeons boons (10176 to 10181) are in the pool from here on (`overrides.scenarioCampaignPlaced`).

- **Mechanics.** The Safe keyword is the constant marker `Safe` on the four objective-locations (family `angmar`, `src/rules/families/angmar/safe.ts`); the pick filter `safe` and the condition `safeActive` read it, and a record that searches names it (`SearchMove` with `safe`). While a safe location is the active location the table holds the flags `noEngagementChecks` and `treacheryRevealIgnored` and every encounter side quest's text is blank (`blankText` with the holder class of encounter side quests); the treachery is still revealed and keeps its keywords, so Surge and Doomed work. When the players travel to it, `ReturnToStaging` brings every engaged enemy back at once, and at the end of the quest phase it goes to the victory display. A guarded safe location (an encounter card attached to it as its guard, `guard: true`) cannot be the active location (`guardedSafeLocation` in `families/angmar/safe.ts`, read by `travelBlocked`), and it returns to the staging area unguarded when the guard leaves play. The Setup pair (10064:A:1, `StageRandomGuardedPair`) reveals a random safe location and a random side quest by the game's seed, the side quest is put on top of the encounter deck so the location's Guarded keyword takes it as its guard, and the other side quests and locations are shuffled in. `RevealCard` reveals a card an effect found (a discarded side quest, a set-aside Losing Time) through the full reveal path; `DiscardUntil` takes `type: "encounter-side-quest"`; `ReleaseGuards` detaches the guards of a card and leaves them staged (10075:A:2); `RevealSetAsideForTokens` and `EarnEncounterCopies` are the campaign card's clock and Resolution, each copy earned numbered so the pool keeps all of them. `heroResourcesSpent` is one event for each hero whose pool paid (10082:A:1), `progressPlacedHere` is noted for every side quest that takes progress (10081:A:1, cleared each round), `excessToEachQuest` is the Cruel Mountain-troll's, `damagedCannotFace` the Goblin Pursuer's and `noCollection` No Rest's. `SearchMove` takes `engagedSeat: "picked"` (10083:A:2). The engine decided every number; no control types one.
- **Table** (code: `src/play/readouts/ettenmoors.ts`, `src/play/cards/ettenmoors-preview.ts`, `src/play/words/scenarios/angmar/ettenmoors-table.ts`, `src/play/words/asks/across-the-ettenmoors.ts`, `src/play/layout/row-place.ts`; `docs/design-system-table.md`, Cards, Across the Ettenmoors; test `src/play/scenarios/across-the-ettenmoors-table.test.ts`). Each cited line creates a piece and each piece is on the table. 10182:A:3's clock is a top-bar dial on the campaign card ("Losing Time", `angmar-losing`): its resources against 3, warming at 2 while a copy is set aside, and its note says what the quest phase does and how many copies remain; with none set aside it never warns and says nothing more is revealed (10182:A:2, 10182:A:3). A safe location's preview says what it does while it is the active location and what travelling to it does (10068:A:1 to 10071:A:1); an encounter side quest whose text is blank says so while a safe location is active; a treachery revealed while one is active gets a banner line that its When Revealed effects are ignored. The guard of a safe location (a side quest, Troll-fells) is drawn over the location it guards in the staging row, whichever was staged first (10064:A:1, 10077:A:2), and each preview names the other. No Rest is drawn under its hero as any attachment is, and the preview of each says what it does (10080:A:1). A pick that spans seats names the owner on each row. Words are in the asks file; Amarthiúl's damage row is armed "Tap again: Amarthiúl is destroyed and the game is lost" when one more damage destroys him.
- **Seats.** One or two seats: one Savage Trollspawn is staged for each player (10064:A:1); "each player" counts the seats.
- **Decisions.** The Coldfell Giant's damage is one round of a single-option `Choose` for each of the 3 characters, so a round with no character left to pick does nothing and the damage is gated on that round's pick (`rr.effects`). Troll-fells' search takes the safe filter, so the pool's other objectives (Arnor Ravaged, Thaurdir's burdens) are never found. Scavenge for Supplies puts the Troll into play engaged with the player with the highest threat directly (the first player chooses among ties, `rr.first-player`), with no stop in the staging area. Travel to a location that guards a safe location is not travel to the safe location: `traveled` is heard by the card travelled to only.

  3. 10064:B:1 at the beginning of the quest phase with no encounter side quest in play (the staging area, a guard attached to a location and the current quest all count): cards are discarded from the encounter deck until an encounter side quest is discarded, which is revealed and added to the staging area; with none in the deck the discard runs the deck out and reveals nothing (the empty deck resets at the next reveal, `rr.running-out-of-cards`). It resolves before the first player's current-quest choice (`faq.simultaneous-effects`; forum https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/187520-ettenmoors-safe-zones-and-questions).   4. 10064:B:2 when any side quest is defeated (not removed or leaving play any other way), its printed quest points are placed on the main stage (Into the Ettenmoors), not on the side quest the first player made current and not through the active location; a stage that is full advances and the surplus is lost (`faq.progress-without-advancing`); heard while 1B is the stage; a safe location's blank text does not stop it, it is the stage's.   5. 10065:A:1 and 10066:A:1: the encounter discard pile is shuffled into the encounter deck, cards are discarded until an encounter side quest is, which is revealed and added to the staging area, "if able": the shuffle always happens; with no side quest left nothing is revealed. Alternative (one forum player): with no side quest in the deck, discard nothing. Recommended (the thread's only answer is one player's).
  6. 10065:B:1 reveals one additional card at the beginning of the quest phase (the first player reveals it, one for the table) unless a safe location is the active location then. 10066:B:1: each damaged character, hero or ally, Amarthiúl included, gets -2 Willpower unless a safe location is the active location (read live); 10066:B:2 wins the game when it is defeated.   7. 10067 Amarthiúl: his controller may give him to the player an enemy engages; his leaving play loses the game and cannot be modified (`mec108.objective-allies`), as 10016. Span-settled.
  8. 10068 to 10071 Safe locations: Guarded and Safe; no Threat; immune to player card effects. The Responses after the players travel to the card are the first player's to use and each player gets the effect: Secluded Cave each player draws 3; The Hoarwell heals 1 damage from each character in play; Abandoned Camp each player may return 1 card from their discard pile to their hand; Patch of Woods each player adds 1 resource to each of their heroes. Safe (`mec108.safe`): travelling to a safe location returns every engaged enemy at once (the cards' shadow cards are discarded, no engagement check, not canceled); while it is the active location enemies make no engagement checks (an optional engagement is still allowed, `rr.optional-engagement`), the When Revealed effects of treacheries are ignored and the printed text of encounter side quests is blank (`rr.blank`: their Forced, Response and constant effects do nothing, including for one already in play and for the Forced effects of the end of the quest phase, judged when collected, `faq.triggering-forced-effects`); at the end of the quest phase it goes to the victory display with its progress and attachments, unexplored. Span-settled; recommended that a treachery's Surge stays (the rule says only "when revealed"); forum and play-through support for the blank side quests (community-sourced).

A guarded objective-location cannot be the active location and returns to the staging area when the guard leaves play (defeated, explored, discarded, shuffled away); the players are not travelling to the safe location when they travel to the location that guards it. `mec108.objective-locations`. Span-settled. A second objective with the Guarded keyword that is next in the deck while another's Guarded draws is revealed after the card behind it has guarded the first, and its own Guarded keyword then takes the card after that (`rr.guarded`).
  11. 10072 Savage Trollspawn: +1 Attack for each damaged character the engaged player controls while it is engaged (heroes and allies, Amarthiúl while that player controls him); its Shadow gives the attacker +1 Attack for each damaged character the defending player controls, counted when the shadow resolves. Literal.
  12. 10073 Coldfell Giant: after it engages you, 1 damage to each of 3 different characters you control, chosen one at a time by that player; with fewer, each takes 1 (`rr.effects`). Shadow: if the defending character has damage when the shadow is flipped, an additional attack follows against the same player with a new shadow card (`faq.additional-attacks`); an undefended attack makes none. Span-settled.
  13. 10074 Cruel Mountain-troll: for each excess point of combat damage it deals (beyond the hit points the damaged character had left, defended or undefended, hero or ally), 1 progress comes off each quest card in play (the main stage and each staged side quest, encounter or player); damage reduced to 0 is not damage and direct damage is not combat damage. Literal; the blog (https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/10/27/across-the-ettenmoors/) reads it the same (community-sourced).
  14. 10075 Ruthless Hill-troll: +2 Attack and +2 Defense with 3 or more damage; its Shadow has the defending player choose a location with the safe keyword in play (the staging area, guarded or not, or the active location, never the victory display or the discard pile) and shuffle it into the encounter deck; its guard stays in the staging area. Forum https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/192500-ruthless-hill-troll-shadow-effect ("card effects do not interact with cards in an out of play state"), `rr.in-play-and-out-of-play`. Span-settled.
  15. 10076 Goblin Pursuer: a damaged character cannot be declared as an attacker against it or as a defender against its attack (checked at the declaration, this enemy only); with every character damaged the attack is undefended; its Shadow is +1 Attack, +3 instead when the attack is undefended. Literal.
  16. 10077 Troll-fells: while it is the active location each Weather card gains Doomed 1 when revealed, stacking with a printed Doomed (`rr.doomed-x`); its When Revealed, unless it is revealed as the guard of a safe location, has the first player search the encounter deck and discard pile for a location with the safe keyword (not the victory display, not one in play), add it to the staging area guarded by Troll-fells and shuffle the deck; with none found only the shuffle. Troll-fells is the guard and the safe location the guarded objective. Span-settled for the direction; researched (community-sourced) for the search: https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/10/27/across-the-ettenmoors/.
  17. 10078 Barren Moorland: while in the staging area, every ally entering play by any route makes its controller exhaust it or deal it 1 damage, two copies stacking; its Travel line deals 1 damage to each exhausted character as a Travel cost that is always payable. The trigger and stacking are researched (community-sourced: the blog); recommended that the ally's controller chooses (no source names "you" for a card in the staging area); the Travel line is span-settled (`rr.travel`).
  18. 10079 Arador's Bane: each player in turn, the first player first, either engages an enemy in the staging area with an engagement cost above their threat (dealt 2 shadow cards at once) or searches the encounter deck and discard pile for a Troll or Giant and adds it to the staging area (not revealed; the deck shuffled); an option that cannot change the game state is not offered (`rr.effects`, `rr.the-word-choose`). Span-settled; a blog comment reads either option as allowed (community-sourced).
  19. 10080 No Rest: the first player chooses its host among the exhausted heroes of every player without No Rest, else among the ready heroes without one, else it is discarded; the host cannot ready and collects no resources in the resource phase (effects may still add them); it is discarded when the players travel to a location with the safe keyword. Recommended for the limit and the chooser (no source; the blog says "attaches to an exhausted hero if one exists, otherwise to a ready hero").
  20. 10081 Lie Low: at the end of the quest phase, if no progress was placed on it this round (any placement counts, even when it is not the current quest), each enemy gets -20 engagement cost until the end of the round, an enemy entering later that round included (`faq.lasting-effects`); its Response after it is defeated has the first player shuffle an enemy of the staging area into the encounter deck. Span-settled.
  21. 10082 Forage for Food: after resources are spent from a hero's pool as a payment (a cost, not an effect that moves or removes them), 1 damage to that hero, once for each hero whose pool paid; its Response heals 1 damage from each hero. Literal.
  22. 10083 Scavenge for Supplies: each player card costs 1 more to play; when defeated the first player searches the encounter deck and discard pile for a Troll, which comes into play engaged with the player with the highest threat (the first player chooses among ties) with no When Revealed and no engagement check; the deck is shuffled; then each player in turn may play 1 card from their hand at no cost (the 1 more included). Recommended for the ties and for the free play skipping the +1 (no source); literal otherwise.
  23. 10183 Losing Time: Surge; its When Revealed has each player in turn raise their threat by 3 or exhaust a hero they control (the second is not offered with no ready hero); its Shadow has the defending player choose whether the attacking enemy gets +3 Attack or +3 Defense until the end of the phase. Recommended for the Shadow's chooser (the active player resolves shadow text, `rr.step-6-4-2`; a play-through shows a player choosing: https://cardtalk2018.com/category/scenario/across-the-ettenmoors/, community-sourced).
  24. 10182:A:2 sets the five Losing Time aside, out of play, in Campaign Mode only (`mec108.campaign-setup-across-the-ettenmoors`); a standalone game deals no campaign card and has no clock. Literal.
