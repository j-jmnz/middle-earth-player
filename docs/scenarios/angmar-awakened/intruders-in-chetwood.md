# Intruders in Chetwood

Part 1 (`10156`; `mec108.setup-intruders-in-chetwood`, `mec108.campaign-setup-intruders-in-chetwood`, `mec108.objective-allies`, `mec108.side-quests`, `mec108.side-quests-in-play`, `mec108.time-x`). Stage 09068 (30 quest points), Iârion set aside, one Orc War Party in the staging pile and the rest in the deck, four encounter side quests in the encounter deck (Orc Rearguard 3, Rescue Iârion 6, Orc Ambush 4, Lost in the Wilderness 4 quest points, read from the scans: `data/overrides.json`), and in campaign mode Iârion's Pendant (10157) and Protect the Innocent (10158, no quest points: it is never defeated). The game is dealt as having side quests when its encounter deck holds one (`markSideQuests`, `state.sideQuests`), which Fire in the Night's side quest deck does not need. Code: the `angmar` family (`src/catalog/families/angmar.ts`, `src/rules/families/angmar/`), the shared side quest reads (`src/rules/families/wilderland/night-quest.ts`), records `src/effects/authored/angmar-awakened/intruders-in-chetwood.ts`; tests `src/rules/families/angmar/index.test.ts` and `src/campaign/scenarios/intruders-in-chetwood/`.

- **Mechanics.** The amount `questCards` (the main quest card, each side quest in the staging area, encounter or player; at least 1) is the X of Iârion, Shrouded Hills, Rugged Country, Weight of Responsibility and Borders of Bree-land's Shadow. `Time X` (`Time`, a constant marker; `RemoveTimeCounters`, `lastTimeCounterRemoved`) puts X resource tokens on the card when it is revealed, takes one off each Time card at the end of each refresh phase (after the Forced effects of that phase) and announces the last. `HoldUnder` puts Iârion facedown under Rescue Iârion, in play and controlled by nobody, with his Pendant still on him; the defeat of the side quest gives him back to the first player, exhausted, and `DiscardHeld` discards him (he leaves play: the players lose). `ChooseCurrentQuest` and the `currentQuest` conditions make a quest card the current quest until the end of the phase; the progress placed on the current quest is bounded by the cap and the round's tally (`questRoom`) and a side quest with `progressRemovesDamage` takes it as damage removed. The campaign card's Setup attaches the Pendant (`AttachNewTo`) and offers each player, the first player first, the trade of 4 damage on Protect the Innocent for 1 resource on each of their heroes (`TradeDamageForResources`). `attackDamagedCharacter` is heard once for each attack that damages a character, as the attack ends.
- **Table** (code: `src/play/readouts/angmar.ts`, `src/play/cards/side-quest-mark.ts`, `src/play/cards/angmar-preview.ts`, `src/play/choice/sheet-parts.ts` `seatedLabel`, `src/play/words/scenarios/angmar/angmar-table.ts`; test `src/play/scenarios/intruders-in-chetwood-table.test.ts`). Three dials after the seats' in the top bar, each only when its card is in play: `angmar-protect` (Protect the Innocent's damage against 10, 10158:A:3; it warms from 8 and is critical at 9), `angmar-time` (the time counters left on Rescue Iârion, 09024:A:1 and A:3, a countdown; it warns at 2 left and is critical at 1) and `angmar-cap` (the progress placed on the current quest this round against Orc Rearguard's 3, 09023:A:1). A staged encounter side quest wears the strip a quest card does (progress over quest points); the one that is the current quest wears an accent "Current" pill inside its top edge, left of the damage token (09049:A:2, 09047:A:1, 09023:A:2) and Protect the Innocent a pill with its damage against the number that loses ("4/10", threat red at 9). Iârion is drawn under Rescue Iârion in the staging area by the same peek an attachment is (the held card of `captiveUnder`), and his preview says he is facedown in play with nobody controlling him; the held dial of a seat does not apply, since nobody controls him. Lost in the Wilderness' preview names each seat's hand under it and says they return when it is defeated; two seat marks say "Hand under" and, for the seat that controls him, "Iârion". A pick that spans seats names the owner on each row: Borders of Bree-land's enemy ("Angmar Orc \u00b7 engaged with Player 2"), Angmar Orc's ally ("Iârion \u00b7 Player 1's ally"); a first player's choice for the group is not owned, and the sheet adds who chooses.
- **Seats.** The engine plays one or two seats (`seatCount: 1 | 2`, `src/rules/core/state-types.ts`; the app's seat picker offers the same, `docs/brief.md`, Product), so the loss at 20 damage on Protect the Innocent with 3 or 4 players (10158:A:3) can not be reached in the app; the record keeps both numbers, the second read from the amount `players`, so a game with more seats would use it.
- **Readings** (agent recommendation unless a source is named).
  1. "Quest cards in play" count the main quest card, the side quests in the staging area, encounter and player, none in a victory or discard pile, at least 1; "quest stage" (Borders of Bree-land) counts the same. Alternative: the main quest card only. Rules: `rr.in-play-and-out-of-play`, `mec108.side-quests-in-play`; community: https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/08/25/intruders-in-chetwood/ ("Shrouded Hills ... including player side-quests"). Recommended.
  2. Iârion's Response (09016:A:3) is heard from his controller for a side quest revealed by the staging step, a surge or an effect, and for one a search of the encounter deck found, not for one found in the encounter discard pile (the judge's reading of "from the encounter deck", `notFromDiscard`); not while he lies under Rescue Iârion. Printed line; https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/08/25/intruders-in-chetwood/. Recommended.
  3. Placing Iârion under Rescue Iârion is that card's own instruction and does not make him leave play (he stays in play, uncontrolled, facedown, the Pendant still attached); its Forced discard is the loss; the defeat of the side quest returns him exhausted under the first player. `rr.in-play-and-out-of-play`, `mec108.objective-allies`; https://hallofbeorn.com/LotR/Details/Rescue-Iarion-TLR and https://rulespal.com/the-lord-of-the-rings-the-card-game-the-lost-realm/rulebook (neither rules on it); the local FAQ 1.9 has no entry. Recommended.
  4. Time X: X resource tokens when the card is revealed, also by a search; one comes off each Time card at the end of every refresh phase, after the end-of-refresh Forced effects (Stop the War Party's raise); "after the last time counter is removed" is the removal that leaves 0. `mec108.time-x` settles the counters; the order against the Forced effects is recommended.
  5. Orc Rearguard's cap (09023:A:1) holds while it is in the staging area, for the main quest card or the side quest that is current, one tally for the round (it resets each round); progress that would go on Protect the Innocent counts. `faq.side-quest-current-quest-text` (the FAQ: "the text on each quest card in play is active"); https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/08/25/intruders-in-chetwood/ (a blog, not one of the authority's forums). The active text is researched (the FAQ); the cap on the current quest follows it and the blog. Community-sourced. Tension: Fire in the Night's ruling 5 (only the placing of progress is redirected) is kept for its own cards; Rugged Country, Orc Rearguard and Pressing Needs read a side quest as the current quest by their own words.
  6. Orc Rearguard's gained Forced effect fires at the end of the staging step when it is the current quest then, and reveals an additional card. Literal.
  7. Rugged Country's +2 Threat holds while a side quest is the current quest. `mec108.side-quests-in-play`. Span-settled.
  8. Chetwood Forest's Travel line is a cost the players must pay (`rr.travel`): they cannot travel to it while no enemy is in the staging area; when they can, the first player decides which player engages, and that player chooses the enemy. `rr.travel`, `rr.first-player`. Span-settled.
  9. Chetwood Forest's Shadow: the defending player chooses; the enemy returns after the attack resolves. Literal.
  10. Borders of Bree-land's gained Forced effect returns ONE enemy engaged with a player, chosen by the first player, per copy in the staging area; the printed card says "1 enemy engaged with a player" (the Vision of the Palantir page says each player). Scan `.cache/scans/09019.A.jpg`. Span-settled.
  11. Outlying Homestead stops the players reducing their threat by player card effects only (`noThreatReduction`, as 143076:B:1). Recommended.
  12. Sudden Assault: when the committed Willpower is greater than the total Threat in the staging area now, each enemy there makes an immediate attack against the first player, each dealt a shadow card (`faq.enemy-attacks-outside-combat`), and they stay in the staging area; the Orc War Party is searched for and added only when no attack was made, and after the attacks, so it does not attack (`noneReturned`, a canceled attack counts as made); the effect cannot be canceled. Recommended for the no-attack case; https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/08/25/intruders-in-chetwood/ ("If no enemies are in the staging area, the players add an Orc War Party").
  13. Surprising Speed prints the text of the Dark of Mirkwood's card of the same name (41017:A:1), so its record is a copy. Literal.
  14. Angmar Orc: the first player chooses to discard an ally from play, any ally in play, Iârion included (the engine offers him, and the sheet warns), or to reveal an additional card; with no ally the second is forced. Recommended.
  15. Angmar Captain: after it destroys an ally, the top card of the defending player's deck is discarded; an ally card discarded makes it attack again, against the defending player, with the earlier shadow cards discarded and a new one dealt; X is the allies discarded from the deck, not the characters destroyed. FAQ 1.9 (the additional attack deals a new shadow card, `faq.additional-attacks`). Researched.
  16. Orc Ambush: constant +1 Threat, Attack and Defense to every Orc enemy in play while it is staged; its When Revealed searches the deck and discard pile for an Orc enemy for each player in turn. Span-settled (`mec108.side-quests`).
  17. Pressing Needs: the first player chooses to search the encounter deck and discard pile for an encounter side quest and reveal it, or to make a quest card other than the current quest (the main quest card or a staged side quest, encounter or player) the current quest until the end of the phase; an option with nothing to do is not offered. Recommended.
  18. Weight of Responsibility reveals one card for each quest card in play at resolution, each in a staging of its own; its Shadow discards that many attachments the defending player controls, his choice. Count recommended (reading 1).
  19. Lost in the Wilderness: each player in turn places their hand under it; only its own defeat returns them (a card under it when it leaves play any other way is not sent to a discard pile: no path of this scenario does). Literal.
  20. Stop the War Party's Setup puts Iârion into play under the first player, adds the staged Orc War Party and has each player in turn, the first player first, search the encounter deck for a location with a different title to add to the staging area (not revealed). Recommended (as 19109:A:1 and 144064:A:1).
  21. No engagement checks (09068:B:1, the players may still engage); the refresh phase's Forced effect raises each player's threat by the number of enemies in the staging area (counted when it resolves); the stage cannot be defeated unless the first player controls Iârion, nor while an Orc War Party is in play (that one binds the main quest only: an encounter side quest is still defeated). Literal; `mec108.objective-allies` and the Parth Galen record.
  22. "The first player gains control of Iârion" (09016:A:2) follows the first player token: control passes with it when the token passes in the refresh phase, as it does for Aragorn and Calphon, the same printed sentence (`mec112.aragorn`, `mec113.multiplayer-aragorn`); `mec108.objective-allies` states that control of Amarthiúl does not pass because his own Response moves it, and says nothing of Iârion. Implemented as the constant `ControlledBy` of 11028:A:1. Precedent by the same wording; community https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/08/25/intruders-in-chetwood/ (a blog, not one of the authority's forums; read: the ally "will stay with the first player during the entire quest, switching between players if there are more than 1 player"). Community-sourced. The condition of Stop the War Party's 09068:B:3 reads the first player's control after the sweep.
  23. Iârion's Pendant (10157:A:2): the controller of Iârion adds it to the victory display to cancel the When Revealed effects of a card just revealed from the encounter deck, or a shadow effect just triggered; not a side quest's (`mec108.side-quests`), not one that cannot be canceled (Sudden Assault); nobody controls it while Iârion lies under Rescue Iârion; its Willpower bonus needs a host that is a character (the host held facedown is not one). Span-settled.
  24. Protect the Innocent's Forced effect places exactly 1 damage after each attack that deals at least one damage to a character (enemy attacks of any kind, not Archery or direct damage); progress that would be placed on it removes that much damage, no more than it holds; it is lost at 10 damage (20 with 3 or 4 players), checked continuously. https://hallofbeorn.com/LotR/Details/Protect-the-Innocent-AAC (no rulings). Recommended for the "1 an attack" reading.
  25. The campaign card's Setup (10156:A:2) attaches the Pendant, adds Protect the Innocent, and offers each player once the trade of 4 damage for 1 resource on each hero; the engine does not stop the group trading itself to a loss. Literal.
