# The Treachery of Rhudaur

Part 7 (`10184`; `mec108.side-quests-in-play`, `mec108.the-investigation`, `mec108.time-x`, `mec108.objective-allies`, `mec108.indestructible`, `mec108.campaign-mode`, `rr.campaign-pool`). Stages 10094 (1A/1B, Time 5 on B, no quest points) and 10095 (2A/2B, 30 on B), the Great Hall (10097, the active location), Amarthiúl (10096) and Thaurdir (10098, set aside) and the three double-sided side quests Quiet the Spirits, Sift through the Debris and Decipher Ancient Texts (10109, 10111, 10113, staged quest side up; their other sides are the Clue objectives 10110, 10112, 10114) are dealt by the piles; the encounter deck is The Treachery of Rhudaur (10099 to 10108), Cursed Dead (09060 to 09063) and Ruins of Arnor (09056 to 09059). Hall of Beorn exports no quest points for the three side quests, so `data/overrides.json` holds them, read from the scans (Quiet the Spirits 16, Sift through the Debris 18, Decipher Ancient Texts 14). The Setup stages 1 Ancient Causeway (09057), 2 with 3 or 4 players, searched from the encounter deck: the staging pile holds both copies (`overrides.scenarioCounts`, a `normal` of 0 drops the encounter pile's entry) and `byPlayers` stages 1 or 2, the rest staying in the deck; The Weather Hills, which also deals 09057, is unchanged. The campaign card (10184) is dealt in Campaign Mode only; its Resolution lets each player add the Clue boons (10185 to 10187, placed by `overrides.scenarioCampaignPlaced`) for the Clues they control.

- **Mechanics.** Family `angmar` (`src/catalog/families/angmar.ts`, `src/rules/families/angmar/`). The main quest card holds `cannotBeCurrentQuest` (10094:B:3): in the quest phase `currentQuestId` (`wilderland/night-quest.ts`) is the side quest the first player chose, else none; progress beyond the active location is then lost and `currentQuestProgress` is false for both readings. A constant "if" on the stage sets `advanceNow` and a settle sweep (`stage.ts`) advances once nothing is resolving and the Setup is over (10094:B:4); `RemoveSideQuestsFromGame` removes the three titles quest side up (10094:B:2). `FlipWhenDefeated` on each side quest makes `defeatSideQuest` flip it into its Clue in the staging area instead of the victory display. A Clue is claimed by an Action that exhausts Amarthiúl or a hero (`ClaimObjective` takes an objective ally as host); `giveControl` moves a claimed Clue with its host. `progressSponge` (10101:A:1, `sponge.ts`) takes the first 2 progress each round for each copy before a quest card gets any, in `putOnStage`, `placeOnCurrentSideQuest` and `placeOnSideQuest`. `returnsUndeadOnRunOut` (10103:A:1, `run-out.ts`) returns the topmost Undead enemy before `resetEncounterDeck` shuffles the discard pile in. `DiscardEncounterTop reset` resets a deck that runs out in the quest phase and goes on (10097:A:1, 10107:A:1); `StageLooked own` adds one Undead enemy of the acting player's own discard. `RemoveProgressAmong` and the amount `discardedThreat` are Dark Covenant's first option; `destroyedWillpower` is the Willpower a character had when the attack damaged it (`last-known.ts`). `leftStagingArea` is heard by Forbidden Descent when it is explored, discarded or removed while in the staging area, and not when the players travel to it. A stage with Time X gets its counters as it is revealed, at the Setup (side B turned up) and when the quest advances to it (`timeOnReveal`). A search that puts a card into the staging area from out of play announces `searchedIntoPlay` (only a game whose records name it), which Quiet the Spirits hears beside `encounterEnteredPlay`, the announcement of the other ways in. Shadow lines that read a Clue carry the condition on the instruction (`when`), since a shadow record's own `condition` is not read. Pool Setup lines that attach to a card the scenario sets aside (`AttachSelfToNamed` with `outOfPlay`, Thaurdir's burdens) wait as `pendingAttach` until the Setup has set him aside, and only during the Setup and the mulligan. The first settle sweep of `advanceNow` waits for round 1, so the Setup's staging area (empty at its first settle) does not advance the stage. 10095:B:2 and 10095:B:1 are `Restrict advance` under `hitPointsLeft` and a negative `count` amount; the defeat of 2B wins the game by the `WinGame` marker of its `questResolution` record (Deadmen's Dike's 09074:B:2 carries the same marker).
- **Seats.** One or two seats: Ancient Causeway is staged once for 1 or 2 seats (the data counts 1 to 4 seats). The Ancient Causeway count is the piles' (`ancient-causeway.test.ts`).
- **Readings** (agent recommendation unless a source is named). No FAQ entry, Hall of Beorn ruling or designer answer exists for this scenario; every source is community (blog, forum), and Vision of the Palantir is a blog: https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/11/18/the-treachery-of-rhudaur, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/206403-current-quest, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/193098-reshuffling-the-encounter-deck; a search of the Treachery of Rhudaur forum thread (https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/175659-treachery-of-rhudaur/) and the web found nothing on Forbidden Descent with two copies or on declining the side quest choice. Orchestrator rulings are marked.
  1. 10094:A:1 Setup: Amarthiúl goes to the first player (`PutSetAsideInPlay`); the rest is the piles'. Literal.
  2. 10094:B:1 Time 5: the counters are put on 1B when it is revealed (side B turned up after the Setup), one comes off at the end of each refresh phase from round 1. Precedent (09024).
  3. 10094:B:2 removes each Treachery of Rhudaur side quest still quest side up from the game, not to the victory display, with its attachments; flipped Clues stay; then the quest advances. Researched (Vision of the Palantir: "any unexplored Treachery of Rhudaur side quests are then placed out of play").
  4. 10094:B:3: in the quest phase the first player may choose a staged encounter side quest to be the current quest; declining leaves no current quest (progress beyond the active location is lost; a Wight revealed then gains neither surge nor doomed), and the table says "None". Outside the quest phase the main quest card is the current quest. Source for the main quest card's exclusion: the forum thread above. Recommended for declining (agent recommendation: the rule says "may", and no source settles a forced choice; the alternative is a compulsory choice while a side quest is staged).
  5. 10094:B:4 (the constant "if"): counts the three titles quest side up in play, an unclaimed one in the staging area included, so the stage does not advance while one is staged; advances at once at the next settle, in the middle of the quest phase when the last side quest flips; the current quest after the flip is the main quest 2B (agent recommendation, no source).
  6. 10095:A:1: Thaurdir is added to the staging area, each player in order searches the encounter deck and discard pile for an Undead enemy and adds it (not revealed), the deck is shuffled once. Literal.
  7. 10095:B:1: -5 quest points for each Clue attached to a character, read live (a Clue in the staging area does not count); a stage left at or above its points by a change is defeated at the next check unless 10095:B:2 holds it. Literal.
  8. 10095:B:2: held while Thaurdir has hit points left; the stage needs its points and 9 damage on him; progress keeps being placed (`faq.progress-without-advancing`); the stage's defeat wins the game (the `WinGame` marker, as 10066:B:2 and 09074:B:2). Precedent (Deadmen's Dike).
  9. 10096 Amarthiúl: an optional Response, his lose line cannot be modified (`mec108.objective-allies`). Span-settled.
  10. 10097 The Great Hall: each player in order discards the top 5 cards of the encounter deck and adds an Undead enemy of his own five to the staging area (not revealed); a deck with fewer than 5 cards is reset and the discard goes on (`rr.running-out-of-cards`, `faq.last-encounter-card`). Recommended for the reset in the middle of a discard.
  11. 10098:A:3 Thaurdir: as 09033:A:3, only in play (not set aside). Precedent.
  12. 10099 Wight of Rhudaur: surge and doomed are read as it is revealed from the current quest's progress; never both. Literal.
  13. 10100:A:1, 10100:A:2, 10104:A:2: progress comes off the main quest card and each staged side quest, encounter or player, not below 0; the Shadow counts every point of damage placed. Literal.
  14. 10101 Forbidden Descent: every progress that would land on a quest card (questing surplus, a card effect) first fills the sponges in the staging area, 2 each round for each copy, so two copies take 4 (each copy takes its own first 2; agent recommendation: no source, and the other reading, both copies acting on the same 2, would make the second copy useless; `faq.simultaneous-effects` would then have the first player order them); progress on a location is not redirected. The progress a sponge takes explores it when it reaches its quest points (`rr.explored`, orchestrator ruling, span-settled): it goes to the encounter discard pile, and being explored in the staging area is leaving play from the staging area, so 10101:A:2 raises each player's threat by 2. Travelling to it (it stays in play) raises nothing. Researched for the text (Vision of the Palantir); recommended for all placed progress.
  15. 10102 Eerie Halls: the Action raises your threat by 1 (a cost) and is not offered at 0 Threat; the Shadow's additional attack gets a new shadow card (`faq.additional-attacks`). Literal.
  16. 10103 Ghostly Ruins: the return happens before the reset and is a cost of the Travel line (unpayable with no Undead enemy there). Researched (Vision of the Palantir; community).
  17. 10104:A:1 Decrepit Remains: +2 quest points for each location and encounter side quest in the staging area, itself and its copies included. Literal.
  18. 10105 Centuries of Sorrow: the first player must choose another quest card to be the current quest, if able; the Shadow makes the attack undefended without a Clue. Literal.
  19. 10106 Curse of the Years: damage first, then each player with 35 or more threat discards his exhausted attachments (boons included); the Shadow is +1, +3 without a Clue. Literal.
  20. 10107 Dark Covenant: X is the total printed Threat of the three discarded cards; the first player removes X progress from among the quests in play (any split) or assigns X damage among his characters; an option that changes nothing is not offered. The Shadow raises threat by the destroyed character's Willpower as it was in play. Span-settled for the choice; recommended for the last-known Willpower.
  21. 10108 Haunting Fog: each player exhausts a character, it attaches to the current quest (discarded with none) and stays on a side quest that flips into a Clue: the flip is not leaving play, an attachment is discarded only when its host leaves play and its "Attach to" restriction is checked only as it enters play (`rr.attachment-cards`). Span-settled.
  22. 10109 Quiet the Spirits: each Undead enemy that enters play by any route (revealed, added or returned to the staging area, found by a search, put into play, put into play engaged with a player) engages the player with the highest threat while it is the current quest (the first player breaks a tie), and adds its Threat to the staging area until the end of the phase; an engaged enemy merely returned to the staging area did not enter play and stays there (orchestrator ruling). Its Forced places 2 progress on it after any enemy is destroyed, even when it is not current. Researched for the engagement (Vision of the Palantir); recommended for the tie.
  23. 10111 Sift through the Debris: +1 Threat for each staged location while current; its Forced places 2 progress on it after any location is explored. Literal.
  24. 10109:A:3, 10111:A:3, 10113:A:3: a defeated side quest flips into its Clue, in the staging area, unclaimed (`mec108.the-investigation`). Span-settled.
  25. 10113 Decipher Ancient Texts: allies (Amarthiúl too) get -2 Willpower while current; the Action's limit of 3 a round counts every player's uses together. Literal; researched for the shared limit (Vision of the Palantir).
  26. The Clues: an Action claims one by exhausting Amarthiúl or a hero its player controls; it moves with Amarthiúl when control of him passes and goes to the encounter discard pile when its host leaves play: it prints no return to the staging area (`rr.attachment-cards`). Researched, community (semi-official FAQ 1.23: an objective that attaches to a card is also an attachment; 1.17 returns only a guarded objective to the staging area; https://alongextendedparty.com/semi-official-errata-faq-1-10/).
  27. 10184:B:1: for each Clue a player controls that player may add the boon of the same name to the Campaign Pool as that player's card, so it is in their deck for the next scenario (one optional choice for each Clue); a lost game writes nothing. Literal for "may"; recommended for the controller's deck (agent recommendation, as the Mysterious Omens boons).
  28. 10185 to 10187: the Brand gives +1 Attack, +2 against Undead, its Response adds a Sorcery treachery from the top 5 to the victory display; the Heirloom heals all damage; Orders gives +1 Defense, +2 against Undead, its Response discards a non-unique Undead enemy with 2 or less remaining hit points (discarded, not destroyed). Literal.
  29. 10185 to 10187 are Restricted (the limit of 2 applies, `rr.restricted`). Pool cards: Amarthiúl's Courage attaches after 1A's Setup; Thaurdir's burdens attach to the set-aside Thaurdir at the deal and stay on him when 10095:A:1 adds him; Fend Off Despair is an encounter side quest here and is not a Treachery of Rhudaur side quest. Span-settled.
- **Table** (code: `readouts/rhudaur.ts`, `readouts/angmar.ts`, `cards/rhudaur-preview.ts`, `cards/angmar-preview.ts`, `words/scenarios/angmar/rhudaur-table.ts`, `words/scenarios/night.ts`, `words/asks/the-treachery-of-rhudaur.ts`; `docs/design-system-table.md`, Cards and Choice tray; test `scenarios/the-treachery-of-rhudaur-table.test.ts`). Secrets of Rhudaur's time counters are the Time dial (`angmar-time:<card id>`, "Time") worded for this card (10094:B:1, 10094:B:2): the note says what the last counter does ("With the last gone, 2 Treachery of Rhudaur side quests still quest side up leave the game and the quest advances to stage 2.", the count read from the table, true for 1 and 0), and it warns near at 2 and 1 left and is never critical, since the last counter is no loss. While the main quest card cannot be the current quest (10094:B:3) and no side quest was chosen in the quest phase, the dial `angmar-current` ("Quest") says "None" in its disc, as the Day and Night dial says its word, and its note says progress beyond the active location's share is lost; the first player's question words the decline as "have no current quest this phase", and the main quest card's name comes from the stage, so Fire in the Night and the other side quest scenarios name their own. On stage 2B the `angmar-clues` dial ("Clues", a `goal` dial: it fills in the accent and never warms) counts the Clue objectives attached to characters against the 3 that exist, its note giving the quest points left with the 5 off for each claimed Clue ("1 Clue objective attached to a character. Stage 2B has 5 fewer quest points for each, 25 now."), a Clue in the staging area counting for nothing; the stage's preview says the same. Each Forbidden Descent in the staging area has its own dial (`angmar-sponge:<card id>`, "Descent", "Descent · 1" and "Descent · 2" with two copies): the progress it took this round against the 2 it takes (10101:A:1), warning near once full, with the progress it holds against its quest points in the note, and its preview says what leaving the staging area costs and that travelling to it raises nothing. A Clue objective is an attachment of its host in every zone the host can be in: a hero or Amarthiúl in a play area carries it drawn under it (an unclaimed one is a card of the staging row), and its preview says where it lies, who controls it two-handed and what it gives its host. Thaurdir's dial (`angmar-thaurdir`), his preview and his burden's are Deadmen's Dike's, read for his code here and for stage 2B of this scenario ("Stage 2 needs its 25 progress and 9 damage on him"). A pick that spans seats names the owner on each row (Orders from Angmar's enemies, any player's); the Clue claims, Dark Covenant's damage and Decipher Ancient Texts are one player's or the first player's and are worded in `words/asks/the-treachery-of-rhudaur.ts`. No control types damage, resources, threat, progress or life.
- **Goldens** (`test/scenarios/the-treachery-of-rhudaur/game.ts`): four games after Across the Ettenmoors' win, one hero questing and the others attacking Thaurdir with every attack undefended on a hero (never on Amarthiúl): true solo making the last side quest the current quest each round (two side quests flip into Clues, the third is removed when the time runs out), two-handed with no side quest ever chosen (all three removed with the time), a loss at threat 50 and a loss when Amarthiúl leaves play. The auto-player plays no Action, so no Clue is claimed in a golden; the campaign test claims them and reads the Resolution. Only `rhudaur-lost` is a fixture; the win flow waits for a seed that wins.
