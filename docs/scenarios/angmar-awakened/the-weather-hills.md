# The Weather Hills

Part 2 (`10159`; `mec108.setup-the-weather-hills`, `mec108.campaign-setup-the-weather-hills`, `mec108.orc-deck`, `mec108.side-quests`, `mec108.side-quests-in-play`, `mec108.time-x`, `rr.campaign-pool`, `ltp.permanent`). Stage 09069 (1A/1B, no quest points) and 09070 (2A/2B, 20 quest points); Hunting the Orcs (09071, back 09072 Savage Counter-attack, a two-code card) and a Weathered Hilltop in the staging area, Exposed Ridge the active location, Amon Forn (09026) and Orc Ambush (09046) set aside, the Orc deck (`angmarOrcDeck`: Concealed Orc-camp, Angmar Orc, Angmar Marauder and Angmar Captain, with the Easy counts) beside the encounter deck, and four encounter side quests (Find Shelter 6, Make Camp 6, Search the Ruins 8 and, in campaign mode, Mysterious Omens 9 quest points, read from the scans: `data/overrides.json`). In campaign mode the campaign card's Setup (10159:A:2) adds Mysterious Omens to the Campaign Pool and shuffles it into the encounter deck, and adds Protect the Innocent to the staging area with the damage the log noted for it; Protect the Innocent lies in the pool from part 1, so Campaign Setup step 8 skips it and shuffles in no second Mysterious Omens (`encounterDeal`, `src/campaign/checklist.ts`). The five boons (10161 to 10165) are dealt set aside with the scenario (`scenarioCampaignPlaced`, `data/overrides.json`), since Mysterious Omens' own text names them. Code: the `angmar` family (`src/catalog/families/angmar.ts`, `src/rules/families/angmar/`), the Orc deck pile (`src/rules/core/piles.ts`, `src/catalog/scenario-data.ts`), records `src/effects/authored/angmar-awakened/the-weather-hills.ts`; tests `src/rules/families/angmar/weather-hills.test.ts`, `weather-hills-cards.test.ts`, `src/campaign/angmar-awakened-shell.test.ts` and `src/campaign/scenarios/the-weather-hills/`.

- **Mechanics.** The Orc deck (`mec108.orc-deck`) is a plain pile with no discard pile of its own: its cards go to the encounter discard pile, and an effect that names it is ignored while it is empty. `ShufflePile` shuffles it at the Setup (09069:A:1), `AddFromPile` with `reveal` reveals its top card for the first player when the active location is explored (09069:B:2; two cards with three or more players) and `ShufflePileIntoEncounterDeck` moves it into the encounter deck (09070:A:1). `StageSetAside` with `reveal` reveals a set-aside card (keywords and When Revealed resolve, a trigger on a reveal hears it) and adds it to the staging area: Orc Ambush and Amon Forn at stage 2A. `FirstTraitSurge` gives the first Weather treachery of each round surge. `EndPhase` ends the phase in progress once the effect and any attack under way are done (a table flag `phaseEnds` read by the machine). `FlipInPlace` with `entersPlay` flips Hunting the Orcs to Savage Counter-attack with its tokens, which then enters play and is heard (09072:A:1). The Mission objective is a Pick (`{type: objective, trait: Mission}`) followed by `PlaceResourceTokens` or `RemoveResourceTokens` with `on: picked`; Sheltered Valley's Travel cost is unpayable while the Pick finds no card holding a token (`travel.unpayable`, `travelCostUnpayable`). `AttackFromStaging` with `target: subject` makes the Orc that was added attack. `ModifyStat blankText` with `keepsKeywords` blanks the text box of a damaged character and leaves its keywords (and Traits, which a blank text box never removes: `rr.blank`). `PlaceLoggedDamageOn` places the damage the log noted on Protect the Innocent. `ChooseBoon` asks each player, the first player first, which of the boons still set aside to take or none, then which hero he controls carries it (`AttachSetAside`); the Resolution's `EarnAttached` records each boon with its hero. `attachmentFromDiscard` is Stalwart's permission: its Action marks the hero until the end of the phase, and the player then plays the attachment from his discard pile with the ordinary Play intent, paying its cost, onto that hero alone; the play uses the permission up.
- **Table** (code: `src/play/readouts/angmar.ts`, `src/play/cards/angmar-preview.ts`, `src/play/store/pile-ids.ts`, `src/play/words/scenarios/angmar/angmar-table.ts`, `src/play/words/asks/the-weather-hills.ts`; test `src/play/scenarios/the-weather-hills-table.test.ts`; `docs/design-system-table.md`, Piles and Cards). Hunting the Orcs' tokens against 3 more than the players warm toward the flip (`angmar-hunt`, 09071:A:1); Savage Counter-attack's tokens count down to the loss at 0 and warm as they fall (`angmar-savage`, 09072:A:2 and A:3); the Orc deck is a pile of the second slot with its count (09069:A:1, 09069:B:2, 09070:A:1); Protect the Innocent's damage dial is Intruders in Chetwood's (10158:A:3), drawn again; the damage on a character is its damage token; a boon is an attachment drawn under its hero (10160:A:3); each pick that spans seats names the owner on each row (09029:A:1, 09052:A:1, 10160:A:3's hero), and a first player's choice for the group is not owned.
- **Seats.** The engine plays one or two seats, so the Orc deck's second card with three or more players (09069:B:2) and Hunting the Orcs' 3 + players above two are not reached in the app; the records read the amount `players`, so a game with more seats would use it.
- **Readings** (agent recommendation unless a source is named; the section Readings to confirm, at the end of this file, lists those not user-confirmed; "community-sourced" is a reading whose only support beyond the card is a blog or a forum).
  1. 09069:B:2 "resolve its staging as if it was just revealed from the encounter deck" (the ledger reads the scan: "its staging" is the Rules Reference's term, `rr.staging`): the top card of the Orc deck is revealed for the first player and resolved as a card revealed from the encounter deck (keywords, When Revealed, placed in the staging area); an Orc deck card never reveals the encounter deck's Surge by itself, since Surge reveals the next card of the encounter deck; with the Orc deck empty nothing happens (`mec108.orc-deck`). The Weather Hills' Vision of the Palantir page https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/09/20/the-weather-hills/ agrees ("the top card of the orc deck"). Span-settled.
  2. 09054:A:1 Cold from Angmar "Attach to the current quest": the current quest card, which is the chosen side quest when one is the current quest at that moment (the quest phase's staging step), otherwise the main quest card; it leaves with that card. The card blanks the text box of each damaged character while it is attached (not while it lies in the staging area), except for its keywords and Traits; the engine keeps the keywords (`keepsKeywords`) and a blank text box never removes Traits. `mec108.side-quests-in-play`, `faq.side-quest-current-quest-text`. Community-sourced for the side quest: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/169926-lost-realm-cold-from-angmar (two members agree; no staff answer) and https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/09/20/the-weather-hills/; Hall of Beorn lists no ruling (https://hallofbeorn.com/LotR/Details/Cold-from-Angmar-TLR). Researched.
  3. 09072:A:1 "After Savage Counter-attack enters play, advance to stage 2A": flipping Hunting the Orcs makes Savage Counter-attack enter play (the Forced effect is heard on the flip, the tokens already moved onto it), and Hunting the Orcs leaves play. `mec112.gollum-smeagol` (a flipped card: "the Gollum enemy leaves play, and the Sméagol objective-ally enters play"); the Vision of the Palantir page above ("once enough enemies have been killed, the objective flips over, and players advance to stage 2"). Span-settled by analogy.
  4. 09071:A:1 Hunting the Orcs "After an enemy is defeated": any enemy destroyed in any way, in play or staged (an attack, Archery, a player card), one token for each enemy, one at a time; the flip check follows each token; X is 3 + the players (4 solo, 5 for two). A token placed by Concealed Orc-camp does not flip it itself; the next defeat does. `rr.destroy`. Literal.
  5. 09029:A:2 Sheltered Valley "Travel: Remove 1 resource token from the Mission objective" and 09030:A:1: "the Mission objective" is the card in play with the trait Mission (Hunting the Orcs, or Savage Counter-attack after the flip); the Travel line is a cost (`rr.travel`: a Travel cost must be paid), so the players cannot travel to Sheltered Valley while that card holds no token; Concealed Orc-camp's Travel (a search) is payable even when the search finds no Orc enemy (as 19161:travel:1), and its Response places the token on whichever Mission objective is in play. The locations' Responses ("While it is the active location, it gains: Response: When it is explored") are heard when the card is explored as the active location (`activeExplored`), not when it is explored in the staging area. The Vision of the Palantir page above ("its travel cost removes 1 token from the mission objective"). Span-settled.
  6. 09031:A:1 Ice Storm: each player assigns X damage among the characters he controls, X the quest cards in play (reading 1 of Intruders in Chetwood), then every damaged character in play, whoever controls it and however it was damaged, is exhausted. Its Shadow: the defending player assigns X damage among the characters he controls. Literal (the quest card count recommended, as there).
  7. 09052:A:1 Biting Wind: one assignment by the first player among all committed characters of any controller, one damage at a time, X the number committed when it resolves (as 143057:forced:2). `rr.first-player`; the Vision of the Palantir page above ("assigns X damage to players committed to the quest"). Span-settled.
  8. 09070:A:1 "End the current phase": the phase ends once this effect has finished (the rest of the When Revealed resolves, the remaining steps are skipped, such as the other attacks of the combat phase, and the next phase begins; end-of-phase Forced effects still resolve as it ends); mid-combat, after the attack of the enemy that was just defeated. The alternative reads it as the quest phase only (`EndQuestPhase`). No ruling was found: the FAQ 1.9 has no entry, and https://visionofthepalantir.com/2023/09/16/nightmare-the-weather-hills/ and the FFG forum archive say nothing. Recommended.
  9. 09026:A:2 Amon Forn's gained Forced effect: any Orc enemy entering or returning to the staging area (revealed, searched into it, added by an effect, returned by Angmar Marauder or Angmar Orc's Shadow) while Amon Forn is the active location makes an immediate attack against the first player, a shadow card dealt (`faq.enemy-attacks-outside-combat`); as 146017:forced:1 and 2 hear "enters the staging area". Recommended.
  10. 09026:A:3 "The players cannot win the game while Amon Forn is in play": the full stage 2B is not defeated while it is in play (progress keeps being placed, `faq.progress-without-advancing`) and is defeated at once when Amon Forn leaves play; the bar falls on the main quest stage only (Amon Forn is set aside until 09070:A:1 reveals it at stage 2A). Span-settled (Parth Galen's `Restrict advance`).
  11. 09069:B:1 "The first Weather treachery card revealed each round gains surge": counted per round, any reveal (staging, additional, a Surge chain), the trait Weather; the Orc deck holds none. Literal.
  12. 10160:A:3 Mysterious Omens "each player may choose 1 of the following boons and attach it to a hero they control": starting with the first player (`rr.first-player`), each player may decline; each boon exists once, so none is taken twice; the Resolution records each boon with its hero in the Campaign Pool (`EarnAttached`), since a Permanent boon is recorded with the hero it is attached to (`rr.campaign-pool`, `ltp.permanent`), and Mysterious Omens itself leaves the pool. In a later scenario the Campaign Setup Checklist's step 5 attaches each boon to the hero recorded for it, with no choice (`ltp.permanent`: "that card must be attached to the target that earned that boon"); if the hero is fallen the boon is removed from the pool (step 2). Recommended: no ruling or discussion was found (Hall of Beorn https://hallofbeorn.com/LotR/Details/Mysterious-Omens-AAC lists none).
  13. 10160:A:2 "After Mysterious Omens becomes the current quest, each player discards 1 random card": heard when the first player makes it the current quest at the start of the quest phase, or Pressing Needs does. 10159:A:2 "Add Mysterious Omens to the Campaign Log" is the Campaign Pool (the Angmar Awakened campaign card reading above). Literal.
  14. 10159:A:2 Protect the Innocent is added to the staging area with the damage noted for it (0 when none was logged) after the quest card's Setup; it skips step 8 (it lies in the pool from part 1). Literal.
  15. 09032 Find Shelter: Time 4 (revealed also by Surge), one counter off at the end of each refresh phase after the end-of-refresh Forced effects (reading 4 of Intruders in Chetwood); when the last is removed each player deals 4 damage to ONE hero he controls (his choice, all 4 on it), then 4 time counters are placed on it again. Alternative: 4 damage spread among the heroes. Literal (scan; the Vision of the Palantir page above: "each player deals 4 damage to a hero").
  16. 09055 Make Camp: "Characters cannot be healed" holds while it is in the staging area (a staged side quest is in play, `mec108.side-quests-in-play`); its Response after it is defeated happens with it in the victory display: each player heals up to 3 damage from a hero he controls, all of it when the hero holds less (`Heal` removes no more than the damage there is). Literal.
  17. 09056 Ruins of Arnor: its Forced effect after the "when revealed" effect of a treachery is canceled (any cancel, the Pendant of Iârion included) works while the card is in play, in the staging area or as the active location: each player raises his threat by 2 for each cancel. Its Shadow: +1 Attack and, if the attack destroys a character, the defending player raises his threat by 2. Literal.
  18. 09058 Tragic Discovery: each player raises his threat by the Threat of the active location (0 with none); its Shadow: the defending player either raises his threat by 3 or the attack is considered undefended. Literal.
  19. 09025 Cornered Orc: its Forced effect fires when it engages a player, also when it is put into play engaged by a search (Concealed Orc-camp's Travel, Orc Ambush; `rr.engaged`). Its Shadow adds +2 Attack instead of +1 when the defending character is damaged at that time; an undefended attack has no defending character, so it adds +1. Span-settled.
  20. 09070:B:2 the first player chooses at the beginning of the quest phase to remove 1 resource token from Savage Counter-attack or to reveal an additional encounter card this phase; an option that cannot change the game is not offered (`rr.effects`: with no token on Savage Counter-attack the game is already lost). 09072:A:2 (an enemy attacks and destroys a character) discards 1 token each time; 09072:A:3 loses the game at 0 tokens, checked continuously; the defeat of stage 2B wins (09070:B:3). Literal.
  21. 09059 Search the Ruins: its Forced effect at the end of the refresh phase raises each player's threat by 2 while it is staged; its Response after it is defeated lowers each player's threat by 3. Literal.
  22. 10165 Stalwart (Planning Action, once a game): the player may play one attachment from his discard pile onto the attached hero, paying its cost, until the end of the phase; the Action marks the hero and the player plays the attachment with the ordinary Play intent, and the play uses the permission up. The alternative has the Action itself play the attachment. Recommended.
