# Deadmen's Dike

Part 3 (`10166`; `mec108.setup-deadmens-dike`, `mec108.campaign-setup-deadmens-dike`, `mec108.indestructible`, `mec108.side-quests`, `mec108.side-quests-in-play`, `mec108.time-x`, `rr.player-elimination`, `rr.campaign-pool`, `ltp.permanent`). Stage 09073 (1A/1B, 11 quest points on B) and 09074 (2A/2B, 13 quest points on B); Fornost Square (09038) in the staging area, Thaurdir (09033) and Iârion (09016) set aside, the encounter deck of Deadmen's Dike, Dark Sorcery and Cursed Dead with three encounter side quests in it (The Shadow World 5, Seal the Tomb 6, The Power of Angmar 8 quest points, read from the scans: `data/overrides.json`), and in campaign mode Iârion's Pendant (10157), Protect the Innocent (10158) and Arnor Ravaged (100158, its other side: one physical card), Amarthiúl's Courage (10167) and the three burdens (10168, 10169, 10170); the five boons of Mysterious Omens (10161 to 10165) are listed for this scenario and every later one while Omens can lie in the pool. This is the last scenario of The Lost Realm; The Wastes of Eriador follows it. Code: the `angmar` family (`src/catalog/families/angmar.ts`, `src/rules/families/angmar/`: `burdens.ts`, `quest.ts` `hitPointsLeft`, `lock.ts`, `blank.ts`), the `deck` family (`src/rules/families/deck/`: `discarded.ts`, `pressure.ts`), records `src/effects/authored/angmar-awakened/deadmens-dike.ts` and the box's `angmar-awakened.ts` (10166:B:1); tests `src/campaign/scenarios/deadmens-dike/` and `src/play/scenarios/deadmens-dike-table.test.ts`.

- **Mechanics.** A stage that eliminates a player with no cards in his deck (09073:B:3, 09074:B:2) is the table flag `eliminatedAtEmptyDeck`, a constant "if" checked at each settle once nothing is resolving (`eliminateEmptyPiles`, as the empty hand of The Road to Isengard): the seat leaves as `rr.player-elimination` says (its cards discarded, its enemies back to the staging area, the first player token passing, `faq.first-player-elimination`) and the last seat out loses. Stage 2B's "cannot defeat this stage while Thaurdir has any hit points remaining" is `Restrict advance` under the condition `hitPointsLeft` (a card with that code in play with damage below its hit points); Thaurdir is Indestructible, so the stage is defeated the moment the 9th damage is on him while its quest points are full (`faq.progress-without-advancing`: progress keeps being placed meanwhile). The trigger `deckTopDiscarded` is one event for each discard effect or cost that moved a card from the top of a deck (Fornost Square counts the first player's), and the amount `discarded` (`cost`, `types`) adds up what the running effect discarded from every deck, a Shadow effect with `DiscardFromDeck` running in a frame so it can read it (Thaurdir's Damned, Haunted Keep, Terror of the North). `IncreaseCost` is a constant on a card in play that every player's cost reads, for any card played from any zone (Heavy Curse). The flag `discardLocked` is held by The Power of Angmar in the staging area: a player card's effect cannot take a card out of a discard pile (a Pick `from: discard`, `MoveCard`, `ShuffleDiscardIntoDeck`, `PlayFromDiscard`, the Stalwart grant); an encounter card's effect can. `playerAbilityTriggered` is heard by Deadmen's Gate for each Action or Response a player triggers (an event played for its Action included; not a Forced effect, a Travel line or an encounter card's Response such as Iârion's: `announcePlayerAbility`). The pick filter `copyInDiscard` is the controller's discard pile (Dark Sorcery's When Revealed: his), or with `owner` a card of the same title in the card's owner's discard pile, the card itself counting when it lies there (Cursed Dead's and Dark Sorcery's Shadow, Heavy Curse). Thaurdir enters play through `AddSetAsideToStaging` (the encounter move helper, so the cards that hear a card added to the staging area hear him). Thaurdir's burdens: `AttachRandomBurden` (the seeded shuffle, one of the burdens not in the game, spawned from the supply when none is set aside), `MoveToHero` (the hero with the most resources, non-objective attachments or damage, the burden's current host counting; the first player chooses among heroes that tie), `AttachSelfToNamed` for the pool burdens' Setups at a later scenario, `blanksTraits` (a blank text box has no Traits either), `MoveDamageTo` with an amount (Arnor Ravaged). The Resolution is `10166:resolution:1`: the damage on Protect the Innocent is noted, it leaves the pool for Arnor Ravaged, the first player picks the Pendant or Amarthiúl's Courage and the player who takes it, each Omens boon attached to a hero is recorded with that hero (`EarnAttached`, as The Weather Hills does; a boon the pool already held on that hero is not recorded twice) and the burden Thaurdir carried joins the pool (`EarnAllBurdens`); a lost game writes nothing.
- **Table** (code: `src/play/readouts/angmar.ts`, `src/play/cards/angmar-preview.ts`, `src/play/words/scenarios/angmar/angmar-table.ts`, `src/play/words/asks/deadmens-dike.ts`; test `src/play/scenarios/deadmens-dike-table.test.ts`; `docs/design-system-table.md`, Cards). Thaurdir's damage against the 9 hit points, with the stage's own 13 progress, is what wins stage 2B: a goal dial (`angmar-thaurdir`, "Thaurdir"; 09074:B:2: it fills in the accent, never warms and has no warning; it reads his hit points as the stage's hold does, `hitPointsOf`), and his preview says the damage, the Indestructible rule, the 3 he heals on each Sorcery treachery, what the stage needs and the burden he carries. Each seat's deck count is a seat mark while a stage eliminates a player with no cards in his deck ("P1 · Deck 12", solo "Deck 12"; 09073:B:3, 09074:B:2): it warns near at 5 cards and is critical at 2, "At 0 this player is eliminated." Broken Battlements' time counters are Find Shelter's dial worded for this card (09036:A:2), one dial for each of its copies (`angmar-time:<card id>`, named by where it lies): the last counter makes each player discard 5 cards (6 on stage 1B) and place 1 counter, so it warns near, and critical at the last counter when a living seat's deck is at or under the discard (the seat is named). Fornost Square's resource tokens are drawn as any card's tokens, and its preview says "+N Threat" for them and what places one (09038:A:1, 09038:A:2). A burden is drawn under its host wherever it moves (Thaurdir in the staging area or an engagement area, the hero it moved to), and its preview says where it lies and what it does there ("On Aragorn: -2 Willpower and its text box, Traits included, is blank. After a Sorcery treachery is revealed it moves to the hero with the most resources."; on Thaurdir "with no effect on him"). The Power of Angmar's preview says what its lock stops. Protect the Innocent's damage dial (`angmar-protect`) is Chetwood's, drawn again because the card is staged here too, and Iârion marks the seat that controls him ("P1 · Iârion"), merged with the deck mark. The choices are worded in the asks file: Unnatural Fog's either/or and A Fell Wraith's discard or return are each player's own (two-handed they lead with the player; the discard, and Unnatural Fog's, warn "Tap again: your deck would be empty and you would be eliminated. If you are the last player, the game is lost" when the deck is at or under the cards they take), the burden's tie among heroes is the first player's for the group and names each hero's owner on its row, and the searches name the pile they read (Baleful Shade from the encounter deck only, Cursed Dead from the discard pile only). Mysterious Omens, which may lie in this encounter deck from the pool, is worded by the word chain: its record is not one of this scenario's cards. No control types damage, resources, threat, progress or life.
- **Seats.** The engine plays one or two seats, so the reveal of 1 encounter card per player and the search of each player for a Baleful Shade are read from the number of players; a game with more seats would use it.
- **Decisions** (the orchestrator's, applied as given). A Travel line is a cost the players must be able to pay (`rr.travel`; Chetwood Forest's 09018:A:1 reads the same way, Intruders in Chetwood Reading 8): with no enemy in the encounter discard pile the players cannot travel to Norbury Tombs. A boon Mysterious Omens offers is offered while no card of that title is in the game outside the set-aside zone and its printed card is loaded, taken from the set-aside zone or spawned from the supply (the boons were never dealt set aside), so a boon the pool holds on a hero is not offered twice; the Dike deals the boons only when Omens lies in the pool. A boon taken in this scenario's Omens is recorded at its Resolution (10166:B:1), which the box's printed text does not say (it records them in The Weather Hills only): agent recommendation, listed in Readings to confirm.

  1. 10166:A:3 + 09033 "Cannot have attachments": the campaign burden attaches to Thaurdir anyway (the campaign card orders it, the burdens' Setup says "even if he is out of play", and each burden's Forced effect only works while it is in play; Majesty, Spite and Legacy exist for no other purpose). Alternative: `rr.cannot` and the FAQ's 1.56 ("cannot have attachments" is absolute) read strictly, so the burden would never attach, never enter play and do nothing but join the pool. A second search for a ruling (the FFG forum archive, BoardGameGeek and r/lotrlcg, Hall of Beorn's pages of the three burdens and the campaign card, RingsDB's card pages, the Vision of the Palantir posts of this scenario and of The Battle of Carn Dûm, which only repeat that Thaurdir "cannot have attachments") found no word on the burdens. Recommended.
  2. 10166:A:3 "Add that card to the Campaign Pool": written when the scenario is won (`EarnAllBurdens` in the Resolution), after a random pick by the engine's seeded shuffle among the three burdens not in the game; a lost game writes nothing, so a replay rolls again; standalone play has no burden. Alternative: added at once and kept after a loss. `rr.campaign-pool` ("After defeating a scenario and recording its results in the Campaign Log, list all boons and/or burdens earned"). Span-settled.
  3. 10168:A:2, 10169:A:2, 10170:A:2 "move [the burden] to the hero with the most ...": any player's hero, the card's current host counting (a hero already holding it that has the most keeps it), ties chosen by the first player (`rr.first-player`); "non-objective attachments" counts attachments that are not objectives (the burdens themselves are objectives); the burden follows each Sorcery treachery reveal (any reveal, not only from the deck), and Thaurdir's own Forced effect (from the deck only) is ordered with it by the first player (`rr.forced`). Alternative: only heroes other than the current host; ties by the controller. Recommended.
  4. 10168:A:2 "treated as if its printed text box were blank": abilities, keywords and Traits are blank (`rr.blank`), unlike Cold from Angmar, which says "except for keywords and Traits" (The Weather Hills, reading 2); a Trait another card grants stays. Span-settled.
  5. 09074:B:2 "cannot defeat this stage while Thaurdir has any hit points remaining": the stage needs its 13 quest points and Thaurdir's damage at or above 9; progress keeps being placed meanwhile; he is Indestructible, so the stage is defeated as soon as the 9th damage is on him (Vision of the Palantir: "13 progress ... and 9 damage"). Alternative: only quest points. `mec108.indestructible`, `faq.progress-without-advancing`. Researched (community-sourced).
  6. 09073:B:3, 09074:B:2 "If a player has no cards in his deck, he is eliminated": a constant check at each settle (precedent 143080:B:3), so the last card drawn or discarded eliminates the player once the effect has finished, which a test shows for the Forced discard, Thaurdir's choice and a played game; all seats eliminated is a loss. Alternative: checked only when a card is needed and none is left. `rr.player-elimination`, `rr.constant-ability`; Vision of the Palantir (players "can be eliminated when their deck runs out"). Researched (precedent plus community-sourced).
  7. 09037:A:2 Norbury Tombs "Travel: Return the topmost enemy in the encounter discard pile to the staging area": a requirement the players must meet (`rr.travel`; the FFG forum archive and RulesPal pages above, "pay or meet every travel effect"; Vision: "travel is impossible if there are no enemies there"), the topmost enemy being the last one put there. Alternative: an effect without "to travel here" (`rr.cost`), so travel always happens. Researched (community-sourced); the same reading as The Weather Hills' Sheltered Valley.
  8. 09036:A:2 Broken Battlements "each player discards the top 5 cards of his deck and places 1 time counter here": each player places 1 (two counters with two players); the Forced effect fires after the removal of the last counter, at the end of the refresh phase, after the other end-of-refresh Forced effects (Intruders in Chetwood, reading 4). Alternative: one counter in all. Recommended (Vision words it "a new time counter is placed", a paraphrase).
  9. 09036:A:3 Broken Battlements' Travel "Each player discards the top card of his deck": a requirement always met (a player with an empty deck is already eliminated). Literal.
  10. 09038:A:2 Fornost Square "After the first player discards any number of cards from the top of his deck, place 1 resource token here": one token for each discard effect or cost that moves at least one card from the top of the first player's deck (a 3-card discard is one token), not for a discard from the bottom, not for the other players' decks; the Threat grows by 1 for each token and tokens never leave. Alternative: one token per card. The printed "any number of cards"; Vision ("whenever the first player must discard cards from the top of their deck, a resource is placed"). Recommended.
  11. 09039:A:1 Haunted Keep "the first Undead enemy revealed each round gains surge": while it is the active location, the first Undead enemy revealed in a round (any reveal: staging, additional, a Surge chain, a search; not an enemy put into play without a reveal) gains surge; one tally for the round, counted from its start even if the Keep became active later in it. Alternative: counted only from the moment it became active. Precedent: `FirstTraitSurge` (The Weather Hills, reading 11). Recommended.
  12. 09040:A:1 Deadmen's Gate "After a player triggers an 'Action' or 'Response' effect, he must discard the top card of his deck": the player who triggered it discards one card for each Action or Response he triggers (a hero's, an attachment's, a boon's, an ally's, an event played for its Action or Response; each trigger once), not a Forced effect, a Travel line or an encounter card's effect. Alternative: a played event is "played", not "triggered". `rr.triggered-ability` ("Action, response, forced ... abilities are triggered abilities"). Recommended.
  13. 09041:A:2 Unnatural Fog: each player chooses for himself: discard 1 card from the top of his deck for each questing character he controls, or remove each character he controls from the quest; an option that cannot change the game state is not offered (`rr.effects`), so with no questing character he has nothing to choose; with fewer cards than questing characters he discards what is there. Precedent 41025:A:2. Span-settled.
  14. 09060:A:1 Dead Lord "After Dead Lord engages you, put the topmost Undead enemy in the discard pile into play engaged with you": the encounter discard pile's topmost Undead enemy, put into play engaged (not revealed); the enemy so put has engaged the player, so its own "engages you" Forced effect fires (Thaurdir's Damned, another Dead Lord); none: nothing. The Weather Hills, reading 19 (`rr.engaged`). Span-settled.
  15. 09061:A:1 Cursed Dead "Put each copy of Cursed Dead in the discard pile into play in the staging area": every copy in the encounter discard pile, not a reveal (no When Revealed, no Surge). Literal.
  16. 09061:A:2, 09065:A:3 "a copy of the defending character in its owner's discard pile": a card with that title in the discard pile of the card's OWNER (not its controller; a hero's owner is its player); 09065:A:2 "a card in his discard pile": the controlling player's pile. Literal.
  17. 09062:A:1, A:2 Restless Evil: its When Revealed affects each Undead enemy in play at that moment, staged or engaged, until the end of the round, and the surge test is "no Undead enemy in play" after the bonus; its Shadow affects each Undead enemy engaged with the defending player, the attacker included (an immediate attack from the staging area gives none). Literal.
  18. 09063:A:2 Seal the Tomb: discard the top 3 cards of the encounter deck (as many as it holds), then return the topmost Undead enemy in the encounter discard pile (the last put there, the discarded cards included) to the staging area (not a reveal). Literal.
  19. 09064:A:1 Terror of the North "for each different card type discarded this way": the card types of everything the effect discarded from every player's deck, pooled (each type counts once in all: at most 4, so +8); its Shadow: the one defending player's 3 cards. Alternative: per player, summed. Vision ("+2 threat for each different player card type discarded"). Recommended (community-sourced).
  20. 09066:A:2 Heavy Curse "When a player plays a card, increase its cost by 1 if there is a card that has the same title in its owner's discard pile": attaches to the current quest (The Weather Hills, reading 2); the cost is increased for every card a player plays, from hand or from the discard pile (an event with Stalwart's or a card's own permission), whose title matches a card in its owner's discard pile when it is played; a card played from the discard pile is itself a card of its title in that pile, so it pays 1 more (the printed text says "a card", not "another card"); several Curses stack; it goes with the quest card. Alternative: only cards other than the one being played. Vision: "raises the cost of playing any card that has a copy in your discard pile by 1, and multiple copies stack ... this also raises the cost to play cards like Elven-light and Lords of the Eldar ... since you are playing it from out of your discard pile". Researched (community-sourced).
  21. 09067:A:2 The Power of Angmar: while it is staged, effects of player cards cannot move a card out of any discard pile; encounter effects can (Norbury Tombs); its Forced effect after it is defeated shuffles the top 5 cards of each player's discard pile into his own deck from the victory display. Vision ("shuts down effects like Will of the West"). Researched (community-sourced).
  22. 09033:A:3 Thaurdir: after a Sorcery treachery is revealed from the encounter deck while he is in play, he heals 3 damage and makes an immediate attack against the first player from where he is (staging or engaged with any player; a shadow card is dealt, `faq.enemy-attacks-outside-combat`; not part of the combat phase); not while set aside (stage 1). Vision ("he isn't yet engaged"). Literal.
  23. 09074:B:1 "After Thaurdir attacks and destroys a character": the characters his attack destroyed (the defender, or the hero an undefended attack landed on, that is in the discard pile with its hit points of damage), not a character discarded by an effect such as Dark Sorcery's Shadow; that character's controller chooses (`rr.first-player` does not apply); "return Thaurdir to the staging area" is not offered when he is already there and the 3-card discard is not offered with an empty deck (`rr.effects`). Span-settled.
  24. 09074:A:1 / 10166:A:3: Thaurdir is added to the staging area when 2A is revealed (set aside before: out of play) and "enters play" is that move; the reveal of 1 encounter card per player is "then", so it resolves only when he was added (`rr.then`). Literal.
  25. 09073:B:1 "Each player must search the encounter deck for a Baleful Shade": the encounter deck only (not the discard pile), each player in turn, the first player first, one Baleful Shade each (none left: nothing), put into play engaged (no reveal), then one shuffle and 1 reveal per player. Literal.
  26. 10166:A:2 Setup: after the quest card's Setup (Iârion in play under the first player), Iârion's Pendant is attached to him and Protect the Innocent is added to the staging area with the logged damage (0 when none); it skips step 8 (a pool side quest the Setup adds); as 10159:A:2 and 10156:A:2. Literal.
  27. 100158:A:2 Arnor Ravaged "After an attack damages a character but does not destroy it, move 1 damage from Arnor Ravaged to that character": once for each attack (the first character damaged, as 10158:A:1, Intruders in Chetwood, reading 25), only while that character is still in play; the damage is placed, not dealt (nothing cancels it, no "when damaged" effect, it can destroy a character at its limit); with no damage on Arnor Ravaged nothing moves. Alternative: one for each damaged character. `rr.damage`. Recommended. 100158:A:1: the Setup adds it to the staging area with the logged damage (the card has no loss line and no progress line: it is not a quest, and it is never in play in this scenario, where Protect the Innocent is).
  28. 10167:A:1 Amarthiúl's Courage: dealt here only as the Resolution's choice; its Setup runs when it is in the pool at a later scenario (Amarthiúl is an objective-ally from The Wastes of Eriador and a possible hero from The Battle of Carn Dûm): attach to the card in play with that title, otherwise remove it from the game. Literal.
  29. 09042, 09063 and 09067, the three encounter side quests: Surge is a keyword (the ledger's keyword line), their Forced effects run at the end of the refresh phase while they are staged, and the side quest rules are Chetwood's; 09042:A:2's X is counted for each player: the Undead enemies engaged with him.
