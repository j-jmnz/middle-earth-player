# The Battle of Carn Dûm

Part 8 (`10188`; `mec108.thaurdir`, `mec108.battle`, `mec108.indestructible`, `mec108.side-quests-in-play`, `mec108.campaign-mode`, `rr.campaign-pool`). Stages 10125 (1A/1B, 15 quest points on B) and 10126 (2A/2B, 15 on B); Thaurdir (10127 Captain side, 10128 Champion side, one card flipped over the other, dealt to the staging area, Captain side faceup), one Carn Dûm Garrison (10129) for each player (staged: `perPlayer: 1` on the staging pile, the other copies staying in the encounter deck), and Accursed Battlefield (10132, the active location: the data places 1 copy and leaves 2 in the deck, the outcome of searching and shuffling, as the Setup says). The encounter deck is The Battle of Carn Dûm, Angmar Orcs (09043 to 09046, with the side quest Orc Ambush) and Dark Sorcery (09064 to 09067, with the side quest The Power of Angmar), and Furious Charge (10139, a side quest in the encounter deck). Hall of Beorn exports no quest points for Furious Charge, so `data/overrides.json` holds 30, read from the scan (Orc Ambush 4 and The Power of Angmar 8 are there already). The campaign card (10188) is dealt in Campaign Mode only: its Setup lets each player take one of the four boons (10189 to 10192, placed by `overrides.scenarioCampaignPlaced`) into their opening hand and adds it to the Campaign Pool, with no +1 threat for changing heroes, and Amarthiúl (10115) may be a hero from this part on (`HERO_BANS` stops at part 7); its Resolution notes the damage on each hero (`RecordHeroDamage`, a win only) and marks the characters carrying a Determination. Standalone play deals no campaign card.

- **Mechanics.** Family `angmar` (`src/catalog/families/angmar.ts`, `src/rules/families/angmar/`; the records are `src/effects/authored/angmar-awakened/the-battle-of-carn-dum.ts`). Thaurdir's two sides are two cards: `FlipTwoSided {front, back}` and `FlipInPlace` keep his damage, shadow cards and attachments (a Thaurdir burden stays on him across every flip) and announce `flippedOver`, which the new side's Forced hears. `shadowDealtTo` announces a shadow card dealt to one enemy outside an attack; `DealShadowInPlay` deals 1 shadow card to each enemy in play (staging first, then each seat's) or to one enemy, and as a Travel line it is a cost the encounter deck must be able to pay in full (`rr.cost`, `rr.travel`). Flags: `keepShadowCards` (stage 1B), `blankShadowGains` (Mountains of Angmar), `questProgressReduced` (Orc Grunts), `leavesStagingWhenDefeated` and `resolvesHeldShadows` (Thaurdir on stage 2B); amounts `shadowCardsInPlay` and `discardPileMaxCost`; the verb `DiscardCopiesFromDeck`; `QuestProgress {main}` adds progress to the main quest card. Stage 2B's attacks reuse the south family's `engagedWithAll` machinery (the Watcher in the Water): the enemy attacks from the staging area against each seat in turn.
- **Seats.** One to four seats in the data; the goldens and fixtures play one or two. One Garrison for each player is staged (`garrison.test.ts` proves 1 to 4 players, Normal and Easy).
- **Readings.** The local FAQ spans (`faq.*`) have no entry on Thaurdir, flipping, the Midwinter's Crux attacks, Orc Grunts or the boons, and Hall of Beorn's pages list no rulings; Vision of the Palantir is a blog and every forum source other than the one marked is community-sourced (the section Readings to confirm, at the end of this file, lists those not user-confirmed). Sources read: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/194582-flippin-thaurdir, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/194518-thaurdir-and-feint-in-the-battle-of-carn-dun, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/237545-thaurdir-and-sorcery, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/194161-shadow-cards-for-enemies-considered-to-be-engaged, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/274103-orc-grunt-and-blade-of-gondolin, https://visionofthepalantir.com/2019/01/24/the-battle-of-carn-dum/, https://visionofthepalantir.com/2025/04/19/qotw-the-battle-of-carn-dum, https://hallofbeorn.com/LotR/Details/Furious-Charge-TBoCD (no rulings).
  1. 10125:B:3 Forced "If there are 3 shadow cards dealt to Thaurdir, flip him" (and 10131:A:2, the Werewolf): exactly 3, checked as each shadow card is dealt to him (`shadowDealtTo`), so he flips once as the third arrives. Researched, official: FFG's Caleb, 1 Dec 2015, "You should only flip Thaurdir if he has exactly 3 shadow cards on him." (https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/194582-flippin-thaurdir). The Werewolf's twin line takes the same reading by analogy (agent recommendation, no effect on play).
  2. 10127:A:4 / 10128:A:4 "After Thaurdir is flipped or a treachery card with the Sorcery trait is revealed": a flip by any effect (Daechanar's Will, Furious Charge, Midwinter's Crux, his own B:3, the end-of-round flips) announces `flippedOver` on the new side (`mec108.thaurdir`: "after he is flipped to his new side, trigger his new Forced effect"); "revealed" is a Sorcery treachery revealed from the encounter deck, not as a shadow card and not from the discard pile. Captain: 1 shadow card to each enemy in play, Thaurdir included (kept: stage 1B). Champion: heal 3 and an immediate attack on the first player from where he is (`faq.enemy-attacks-outside-combat`: a shadow card is dealt, every shadow card dealt to him resolves, all are discarded after). Span-settled for the flip and the attack; community for the shadow-card exclusion (https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/237545-thaurdir-and-sorcery).
  3. 10125:B:1 "cannot leave the staging area and cannot take damage. At the end of the round, if Thaurdir is Champion side faceup, flip him." Literal, stage 1B only; stage 2B repeats "cannot leave the staging area" and drops "cannot take damage".
  4. 10125:B:2 "Do not discard unresolved shadow cards at the end of the combat phase": a table flag while stage 1B is in play. The facedown cards dealt to enemies that did not attack stay and accumulate; an attacking enemy's cards are discarded after its attack as ever (`faq.enemy-attacks-outside-combat`), an additional attack discards the earlier ones first (`faq.additional-attacks`). Literal. Stage 2B has no such line, so the cards are discarded again.
  5. 10126:A:1 When Revealed "Raise each player's threat by X, where X is the number of shadow cards in play": every shadow card dealt to any enemy in play (staging or engaged), counted once for all players, before the flip. Literal.
  6. 10126:B:1 "cannot leave the staging area, is considered to be engaged with each player, and attacks each player in turn during the combat phase (deal and discard a new shadow card for each attack)": Thaurdir stays in the staging area (nothing moves him out: not an engagement, a return, a discard or any effect; only damage that destroys him takes him out, which wins the game: `cannotLeaveStaging` with `leavesStagingWhenDefeated`, and `cannotBeEngaged`). For attacks he is engaged with every seat: in the combat phase each seat, in player order, resolves one attack by him among its enemy attacks, and players may attack him as an engaged enemy. No engagement happens, so no "after an enemy engages you" effect hears him. The shadow cards he already holds when he makes his first attack of the combat phase (left over from stage 1B, or dealt by his Captain Forced) are flipped and resolved with that attack and discarded after it (`rr.step-6-4-2`: the attack step flips the shadow card dealt to the attacking enemy; `faq.enemy-attacks-outside-combat`), together with the card dealt for it; each later attack that round is dealt a new shadow card and discards the earlier ones first (`faq.additional-attacks`); his first attack outside the combat phase is the same, held cards plus one new card, and a card an earlier attack turned up is discarded, never resolved again. Only one card is dealt for each attack. Span-settled for the held cards (orchestrator ruling); recommended for the order and the absence of an engagement event (community thread without agreement: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/194161-shadow-cards-for-enemies-considered-to-be-engaged).
  7. 10126:B:1 as to threat: Thaurdir is in the staging area, so his Threat counts toward the staging threat (`rr.staging-area`: each enemy in the staging area contributes its threat strength). Span-settled (orchestrator ruling).
  8. 10126:B:2 "While Midwinter's Crux has 15 or more progress on it, Thaurdir loses indestructible. You cannot defeat this stage while Thaurdir is in play.": read on the quest-deck stage card itself, never on a side quest that is the current quest (Furious Charge's 30 does not count). Progress keeps being placed on the stage (`faq.progress-without-advancing`); at 15 or more the keyword is off (`mec108.indestructible`) and damage can destroy him; the stage cannot advance while he is in play (a `Restrict advance`, as 09074:B:2). Literal.
  9. 10126:B:3 "If Thaurdir is defeated, you win the game": `enemyDestroyed` on Thaurdir, either side, runs `WinGame`; he goes to the encounter discard pile (he cannot be shuffled into the encounter deck, `mec108.thaurdir`) and 10188's Resolution follows. Literal.
  10. 10127:A:3 / 10128:A:3 "Each other enemy gets +10 / -10 engagement cost": every enemy without the Wraith trait, in any zone, so Thaurdir's other side is left out; the floor at 0 is the engine's (`modifiedStat`). Literal; the floor is agent recommendation.
  11. 10129:A:1 / A:2 Carn Dûm Garrison: +1 Threat for each shadow card dealt to it. The Shadow's "it" is the attacking enemy (the nearest antecedent; the Garrison as a shadow card has no shadow cards of its own): +1 Attack for each shadow card currently dealt to the attacker, this card included. Agent recommendation (no ruling found).
  12. 10130:A:2 Orc Grunts "Any time progress is placed on the current quest, reduce the amount of progress placed by 1": applied to the progress placed on the quest card itself (the current quest, the main quest or the chosen side quest), after the active location and a sponge took theirs; each Orc Grunts in play reduces once (several add, to 0 at the least). Progress placed on the main quest card while a side quest is the current quest is not reduced (the main quest is not then the current quest). Researched, community only: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/274103-orc-grunt-and-blade-of-gondolin ("placing" against "assigning": two community members, no staff).
  13. 10131:A:1 Werewolf of Angmar: any event played by any player (a canceled event was still played), after it resolves; the card is dealt from the top of the encounter deck facedown. A:2: while in the staging area at exactly 3 shadow cards it engages the first player and attacks at once with no new shadow card (the 3 resolve and are discarded); engaged already, nothing. Literal; any player's event is agent recommendation.
  14. 10132:A:1 Accursed Battlefield: each quest card in play (the main quest card and the staged encounter side quests) gains the battle keyword while it is the active location; the committed characters count Attack in place of Willpower for whichever quest is current, staged enemies and locations still count Threat (`mec108.battle`). A:2 Shadow: +1 Attack, +3 instead when the defending player's threat is higher than the attacking enemy's engagement cost, read live. Literal.
  15. 10133:A:1 Mountains of Angmar: while it is the active location each shadow card with no shadow effect (an enemy without a Shadow line, a location, a treachery or side quest with none) gains "Shadow: Raise defending player's threat by 2", read as the card is flipped. Literal.
  16. 10134 Blight of Carn Dûm: A:1 after an enemy has finished an attack (a canceled or undefended one included), heal 1 damage from it, while Blight is in the staging area or the active location. A:2 Travel: deal each enemy in play 1 shadow card, a cost that is payable only when the encounter deck can supply a card for every enemy (`rr.cost`, `rr.travel`). A:3 Shadow: if the attacker is Thaurdir, heal 3 from him. Literal.
  17. 10135:A:1 Fortress Walls: raises a player's threat by 1 for each resource that player spends in a payment, hero pools and an ally's pool that pays for a card alike (`resourcesSpent`, one event for each seat that paid, with the number); resources moved or removed by an effect are not spent; a raise to 50 eliminates as ever. A:2 Shadow: with threat 40 or higher the attacker makes an additional attack (`faq.additional-attacks`). Literal; agent recommendation for an ally's pool counting (the printed text says "spent by a player").
  18. 10136 The Sky Darkens: each player in player order discards a random card from hand, then searches their deck for each other card with that title and discards them, then shuffles the deck; a player with no hand does nothing. Literal. A:2 Shadow: discard a random card from the defending player's hand.
  19. 10137 Vile Affliction: each player in player order distributes damage among the questing characters they control equal to the printed cost of the highest cost card in their own discard pile (a printed X or no cost is 0; no cap on a character). A:2 Shadow: +1 Attack; if the attack destroys an ally, the defending player searches their deck for another copy of that ally (the first ally the attack destroyed) and discards it. Literal; the cost of an X card is agent recommendation (`rr.printed`).
  20. 10138 Daechanar's Will: flip Thaurdir (either side), then if he is Captain side faceup now this card gains surge; the Shadow flips him after the attack. Literal.
  21. 10139 Furious Charge: A:2 "When at least 1 progress is placed on Furious Charge" (each placement, only on this card): each character in play gets -2 Defense until the end of the round, then Captain Thaurdir flips. A:3 Response: after it is defeated, add 10 progress to the main quest: it goes on the main quest card, even while another side quest is the current quest. While the main quest is the current quest (no side quest chosen) the active location takes its share first (`rr.active-location`: it buffers the progress that would be placed on the current quest); while a side quest is the current quest the main quest card is not the current quest, so the active location keeps none of it and no Orc Grunts reduces it (`mec108.side-quests-in-play`). Span-settled (orchestrator ruling).
  22. 10189 Elrond's Champion: Response after an enemy engages you (the engagement check, an optional engagement, an engagement by an effect; Thaurdir's "considered" engagement is none), ready it; not offered while it is ready. Literal.
  23. 10190 Lore of Ancient Arnor: each player in player order returns 1 card from their own discard pile to their hand (2 when they are the only player in the game) and shuffles the rest of their own discard pile into their own deck; then it is removed from the game for this game only (it stays in the pool, `rr.removed-from-game`). Literal.
  24. 10191 Raiment of the Second Age: +2 Attack and +2 hit points while the host is a character; Response after the attached character attacks and destroys an enemy, draw 1 card for its controller. Literal.
  25. 10192 Rivendell Skirmishers: after it exhausts to defend an attack from a non-unique enemy, discard each shadow card dealt to that enemy, before the shadow effects are flipped (`rr.step-6-4-1`); unique enemies are not touched (Thaurdir is unique). Literal.
  26. Setup order (10125:A:1): Thaurdir staged Captain side faceup, the Garrison copies, the active location, then the shuffle (the data does it all), then the burdens and Amarthiúl's Courage. A Thaurdir burden attaches to the staged Thaurdir, whichever side is up (the earlier parts' reading, agent recommendation). Amarthiúl may be a hero here and stays usable in The Dread Realm (agent recommendation: https://visionofthepalantir.com/2025/04/19/qotw-the-battle-of-carn-dum shows nobody banning him again).
- **Table** (code: `readouts/carn-dum.ts`, `readouts/scenario-dials.ts`, `cards/carn-dum-preview.ts`, `cards/angmar-preview.ts`, `words/scenarios/angmar/carn-dum-table.ts`, `words/asks/the-battle-of-carn-dum.ts`; `docs/design-system-table.md`, Cards and Choice tray; test `scenarios/the-battle-of-carn-dum-table.test.ts`). Thaurdir's side and the shadow cards dealt to him are one dial (`angmar-side`, labelled "Thaurdir" with the side he shows in its name and note; 10125:B:3, 10127:A:4, 10128:A:4): a count against the 3 that flip him on stage 1B ("3 flip him"), with no limit once he holds 3 or more (a flip fires on exactly 3), and a note that says what each flip does (the Captain side deals each enemy a shadow card, the Champion side heals 3 and attacks the first player at once); a Captain one card short warns. On stage 2B shadow cards flip nobody, so it has no limit and says when he flips and that he attacks each player. Stage 2B's `angmar-crux` dial ("Crux", 10126:B:2) is a `goal` dial: the progress on Midwinter's Crux against the 15 that take Thaurdir's Indestructible away, the number read from the records' constant, filling in the accent and never warming, and at 15 its note gives the next goal, the damage on Thaurdir against his hit points. The Garrison's preview says its Threat as the engine reads it now (10129:A:1); Furious Charge's preview says its quest points as the engine reads them (`questPointsOf`) and that its defeat adds 10 progress to the main quest card, the active location taking its share first unless another side quest is the current quest (10139:A:3); Thaurdir's preview says his side, the shadow cards dealt to him, his damage against his hit points as the engine reads them, whether damage touches him, and the burden he carries, which stays on him when he flips (it is an attachment drawn under him wherever he lies). A boon attached to a hero (Raiment of the Second Age) is an ordinary attachment under that hero. The pick each player makes from their own discard pile (Lore of Ancient Arnor) and Vile Affliction's damage lead with the player two-handed, and Vile Affliction's sentence says the highest printed cost in the player's own pile; the pool boons' picks are worded once for every scenario that can deal them (`words/pool-boon-asks.ts`). No control types damage, resources, threat, progress or life.
- **Goldens** (`test/scenarios/the-battle-of-carn-dum/game.ts`): games after The Treachery of Rhudaur's win, one hero questing and the others attacking Thaurdir with every attack undefended on a hero: true solo and two-handed wins, each ending with Thaurdir destroyed on stage 2B once 15 progress took his Indestructible away (the two-handed one has him attack each of the two players during the game), a loss at threat 50 (heroes too small to quest) and a loss with every hero destroyed (heroes too brittle for Thaurdir's attacks). Only `dum-lost` is a fixture.
