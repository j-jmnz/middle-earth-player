# The Wastes of Eriador

Part 4 (`10171`; `mec108.setup-the-wastes-of-eriador`, `mec108.campaign-setup-the-wastes-of-eriador`, `mec108.day-and-night`, `mec108.objective-allies`, `mec108.immune`, `mec108.side-quests-in-play`, `mec108.campaign-log`, `rr.campaign-pool`, `rr.attachment-cards`). Stages 10013 (1A/1B, 20 quest points on B), 10014 (2A/2B, 15) and 10015 (3A/3B, 5), read from the scans (Hall of Beorn's quest points match them: no override); the Time objective, one double-sided card (Daybreak 10011, Nightfall 10012), faceup on Daybreak in the staging area; Shrouded Hills (09048) the active location; Amarthiúl (10016, an objective ally) and Pack Leader (10017) set aside; the encounter deck of The Wastes of Eriador (10018 to 10027), Eriador Wilds and Foul Weather, with three encounter side quests in it (Lost in the Wilderness, Make Camp and Fend Off Despair); and in campaign mode Determination (10172), Fend Off Despair (10173), Mysterious Omens' five boons (10161 to 10165) and, from the pool, Arnor Ravaged (100158), Amarthiúl's Courage (10167) and a burden (10168 to 10170). Determination and Fend Off Despair stay in the pool, so every later Angmar Awakened scenario loads them (`scenarioCampaignPlaced`, `data/overrides.json`). While the next part, Escape from Mount Gram, is not in `src/campaign/playable.ts`, the path says it is coming soon. Code: the `angmar` family (`src/catalog/families/angmar.ts`, `src/rules/families/angmar/`: `day-night.ts`, `determined.ts`, `check-cost.ts`), the `deck` family (`gains-doomed.ts`), `campaign` (`RecordDetermined`), records `src/effects/authored/angmar-awakened/the-wastes-of-eriador.ts` and the box's `angmar-awakened.ts` (10171, 10188); tests `src/campaign/scenarios/the-wastes-of-eriador/` and `src/play/scenarios/the-wastes-of-eriador-table.test.ts`.

- **Mechanics.** Day and Night (`mec108.day-and-night`): the Time objective is one card whose code is 10011 (Day) or 10012 (Night), so a side's records are heard only while it is up. The condition `timeOfDay` reads the side (false with no Time objective), the verb `FlipTimeObjective` swaps the code and announces `becameDay` or `becameNight` to every card in play (a flip to the side already up changes nothing), and the Night flags (`noQuestProgress`, `encounterUncancelable`) and Day's `noEngagementChecks` are constants of the two sides. `GainsDoomed` adds to each event's Doomed (Fend Off Despair); `RecordStartingThreat` writes a delta to `ResolutionLog.startingThreat`, which a won game adds to the log's `threatPenalty` (shown as "-2", floored at 0 at each start); `RecordDetermined` is the Resolution verb that marks the host of each Determination still attached in play (also `ResolutionLog.determined`, unioned into the log's `determined`), and the pick filter `determined` reads the log. `AttachSelfToPicked` takes `unlimited` (a card with no limit of one for each host: Determination), the condition `sourceIn attached` ties the "Then, ready ..." steps to the attach having happened, `DiscardUntilEnemies` takes `trait` and `less` (X is the players, or one fewer), `PutTopmostFromEncounterDiscard` takes `dealShadow`, `RemoveAllQuestProgress` takes `of: "stage"` and otherwise empties the current side quest while one is current, `SearchReveal` takes `cost` (Warg's Den's Travel cannot be paid with no Warg to find) and the stat flag `checkCostPerDamaged` is Hunting Pack's -1 for each damaged character of the player making the check, never below 0. `ScenarioState.pendingAttach` holds a pool card whose Setup names a card the quest card's Setup has not put into play yet (Amarthiúl's Courage): it waits set aside and attaches in `finishSetup`.
- **Table** (code: `src/play/readouts/wastes.ts`, `src/play/cards/wastes-preview.ts`, `src/play/cards/determined-mark.ts`, `src/play/words/scenarios/angmar/wastes-table.ts`, `src/play/words/asks/the-wastes-of-eriador.ts`; `docs/design-system-table.md`, Cards and Choice tray). The Day and Night dial (`angmar-daynight`, "Time") says the side up in its disc, "Day" or "Night", in place of a number (`word`, `ThreatRing`), and its note says what the side does and that it flips at the end of the round (10011:A:1, 10011:A:3, 10012:A:1); Daybreak's and Nightfall's previews say the same. Pack Leader's gate (`angmar-gate`, "Gate"; 10015:B:1) reads the progress on stage 3B against the 5 that let him take damage as a goal dial: it fills in the accent, never warms and has no warning, since it is the players' way out (with 5 destroy him to win the game; all progress is discarded when it becomes Day); it shows while stage 3B is current and he is in play, and his preview and the stage's say it. Eriador Wastes' cap is Orc Rearguard's dial drawn for either card (`angmar-cap`, "This round"; 10023:A:1: the progress placed on the current quest this round against its 5, one dial for two copies). Arnor Ravaged's damage is a dial with no limit and no warning (`angmar-ravaged`, "Ravaged"; the card prints no loss line, so there is no ring to fill and nothing to warm), and its preview says that 1 damage moves to a character after an attack damages it. A hero or ally that the Campaign Log marks determined wears the pill "Determined", and one that carries a Determination the pill "Determination" (10172:A:1), on the table, in its preview and and in the reader table's words (`determined` of the slot's numbers: "The Campaign Log marks this character determined, so Determination readies it." and "It carries Determination: it is marked determined in the Campaign Log if you win this scenario."). Once Fend Off Despair is defeated in a game, each living seat carries the seat mark "Start −2" (solo "Start −2", two-handed "P1 · Start −2"; 10173:A:2: "If you win, this player's starting threat is 2 lower in the next scenarios, noted in the Campaign Log."); its preview says that each event gains Doomed 1 and what its defeat writes. The Resolution says what the win wrote ("Each player's starting threat is 2 lower from now on, noted in the campaign log.", "Marked determined in the campaign log: ..."), and the Campaign Log's Threat Modifiers row reads "−2". The choices (Nightfall's discard, Pack Leader's exhaust, Eriador Wastes' Travel, Predatory Wolves, Determination's attach) are asks-file entries: a choice of one player's own is owned, a pick that spans seats names the owner on each row ("ally · Player 2's ally"), and Predatory Wolves' discard option, or the row of Amarthiúl when he ties for the highest cost, is armed "Tap again: discarding Amarthiúl loses the game", and the discard option names the ally it takes when one ally is the highest cost. No control types damage, resources, threat, progress or life.
- **Seats.** The engine plays one or two seats, so the reveal of 1 encounter card per player, the Wargs of stages 2A and 3A (the players, and one fewer) and Nightfall's second discarded ally (3 or more players) are read from the number of players.
- **Decisions** (the orchestrator's, applied as given). Arnor Ravaged's Setup takes the pool copy: the checklist shuffles the pool copy into the encounter deck and the Setup's search stages that one, so one card lies in the staging area with the logged damage and the checklist keeps its step 8 as it was. Amarthiúl's Courage waits set aside until its host is in play: a player-deck pool card's Setup runs at the deal, before the quest card's Setup puts Amarthiúl into play, so the Courage attaches in `finishSetup`, after it (`faq.simultaneous-effects`: the first player orders simultaneous effects, the quest card's first); with no host coming it is removed from the game as before. Thaurdir's burdens (10168 to 10170) are removed from this game only, since Thaurdir is not in it, and stay in the pool. Determination is written at every later win: 10188:B:1 runs `RecordDetermined` as 10171:B:1 does, and the Resolutions of 10174, 10182 and 10184 take it when their parts are written (10193 ends the campaign and writes nothing). A flip of the Time objective to the side already up changes nothing and announces nothing.

  1. 10011:A:1, 10012:A:1 Day and Night: the faceup side of the Time objective is the state and has no effect of its own (`mec108.day-and-night`); it is Day at the Setup with no change announced, and "becomes Day/Night" is heard when it flips. A flip to the side already up does nothing (it cannot arise at stage 3A: no progress is placed at Night, so the stage is revealed at Day). Span-settled; the no-op is recommended.
  2. 10011:A:3, 10012:A:3 "At the end of the round, flip this objective": it flips once each round; the other side's own end-of-round Forced effect does not also resolve that round (it was not in play and active when the trigger occurred, `faq.triggering-forced-effects`). Span-settled.
  3. 10011:A:1 "Enemies do not make engagement checks" (Day): the whole table; optional engagement (`rr.optional-engagement`) and an engagement an effect makes (Eriador Wastes, Warg's Den, Blood-thirsty Warg) still happen. Literal.
  4. 10011:A:2 "return each enemy in play to the staging area": each engaged enemy, whoever it is engaged with (precedent 141048:A:2); Pack Leader is back in the staging area every Day, so the Night he engages is the Night he is destroyed. Literal.
  5. 10012:A:1 "Progress cannot be placed on quest cards": the main quest and every side quest (a quest card) take none at Night; progress from questing goes first onto the active location, a location, which still takes it and can be cleared. Alternative: no progress anywhere. Researched (community-sourced: two forum members agree, no official answer; precedent 41039:A:1).
  6. 10012:A:1 "Encounter card effects cannot be canceled": When Revealed and Shadow effects alike (`encounterUncancelable`, held while Nightfall is up). Literal.
  7. 10012:A:2 "When it becomes Night, reveal an encounter card": the first player reveals it (`rr.first-player`, `rr.reveal`), after the refresh phase at the end of the round. Alternative: each player. Span-settled.
  8. 10013:A:1 Setup: the first player takes control of Amarthiúl, the encounter deck is shuffled and each player reveals 1 card (each "you" is the revealer). Literal. 10013:B:1 gives each hero +1 Willpower while it is Day (Amarthiúl, an objective ally, is no hero), 10013:B:2 raises each player's threat by 1 when it becomes Night. Literal.
  9. 10014:A:1, 10015:A:1: shuffle the encounter discard pile into the deck, discard from the top until X Warg enemies are discarded (X the players, and one fewer at 10015: none alone, the pile only shuffled) or the deck is empty, and add each Warg so discarded to the staging area (added, not revealed: no When Revealed, no Surge); 10015 first flips the Time objective to Nightfall and adds Pack Leader. `rr.running-out-of-cards`. Literal; researched (community: added to the staging area, not revealed).
  10. 10014:B:2 "discard 1 non-objective ally from play (2 with 3 or more players)" when it becomes Night: the first player chooses among the allies of every player (Amarthiúl is never chosen); with fewer allies than the number it discards what there is. Alternative: each player discards one. `rr.first-player`. Span-settled.
  11. 10015:B:1 Pack Leader cannot take damage unless 5 or more progress tokens are on the stage (any damage; "this stage" is the quest card), 10015:B:2 discards all progress from the stage when it becomes Day, 10015:B:3 the stage cannot be defeated while he is in play (progress beyond 5 stays, `faq.progress-without-advancing`) and the players win when he is destroyed (precedent 146068:B:3). The community's play-through: progress at Day, Pack Leader engages and is destroyed at the next Night. Span-settled.
  12. 10016:A:1 Amarthiúl's Response is optional and control does not pass with the first player token; the lose line cannot be modified (`mec108.objective-allies`). Span-settled.
  13. 10017:A:2 "Cannot be optionally engaged": an effect may still engage him; A:3 "exhaust a character you control for each Warg enemy engaged with you" counts Pack Leader himself, so at least one character exhausts, each a ready character you choose. Alternative: other Wargs only. Literal (precedent 146058:A:2). At Night the engagement check engages the highest engagement cost it can, one enemy for each player (Pack Leader's cost is 1), so the players engage the other Wargs first.
  14. 10018:A:1 "Each Warg enemy gets +1 Threat until the end of the phase" is recalculated: a Warg that enters play later in the phase, the Northern Warg itself, gets it (`faq.lasting-effects`).   15. 10018:A:2 "attacking enemy engages the next player, then makes an immediate attack": the attack is against the player just engaged, as the engine plays it for 141072:A:2 and 906105:A:3; with one player there is no next player and no attack (`faq.next-player`, `faq.additional-attacks`, `faq.enemy-attacks-outside-combat`); it does not attack a second time in the combat phase.   16. 10019:A:1: while in the staging area, when it becomes Night it engages the first player and makes an immediate attack (a shadow card is dealt, `faq.enemy-attacks-outside-combat`); two such Wargs, each, in the first player's order (`rr.forced`). Literal.
  17. 10020:A:2 Hunting Pack: -1 engagement cost for each damaged character (hero or ally, Amarthiúl too) the player making the check controls, only in that player's engagement check (not optional engagement, `rr.optional-engagement`), never below 0. Span-settled.
  18. 10021:A:1 deals 1 damage to a character you control after it engages you (2 at Night); A:2 at Night an additional attack after this one, the earlier shadow cards discarded and a new one dealt (`faq.additional-attacks`).   20. 10024:A:1 immune to player card effects (`mec108.immune`); A:2 Travel: the first player searches the encounter deck and discard pile for a Warg, reveals it (its When Revealed resolves), puts it into play engaged with them and shuffles; with none to find the Travel cannot be paid. Literal.
  21. 10025: +2 Threat at Night; Shadow +1 Attack, and if the attack destroys a character, all progress is removed from the current quest (a side quest when one is current, `mec108.side-quests-in-play`). Literal.

  23. 10027:A:1 Predatory Wolves: each player, in player order, discards the highest-cost ally they control (a tie is theirs to break; Amarthiúl, with no printed cost, counts as cost 0 and is chosen only when no other ally ties or beats him, and losing him loses the game, 10016:A:2) or searches for a Warg and adds it to the staging area; a player with no ally has only the search; the encounter deck is shuffled once at the end. Alternative: Amarthiúl is never eligible. `rr.the-word-choose`. Recommended (a forum thread with one reply: the player may always take the search; community-sourced).
  24. 10172:A:1 Determination: attached to a unique character in play, any player's hero or unique ally (Amarthiúl too), where it stays as a Condition attachment; any number of copies may be attached to one character (`rr.attachment-cards`: no limit); then, only if it attached (`rr.then`), each character with a Determination attached and each character the Campaign Log marks determined is readied. Span-settled; the kept and cumulative mark is recommended.
  25. 10173:A:1 "Each event card gains doomed 1": while it is in play, every event a player plays raises each player's threat by 1 more, stacking with the event's own Doomed X (`rr.keywords`, `rr.doomed-x`), Determination included. Span-settled.
