# Escape from Mount Gram

Part 5 (`10174`; `mec108.setup-escape-from-mount-gram`, `mec108.campaign-setup-escape-from-mount-gram`, `mec108.captured-deck`, `mec108.captured-cards`, `mec108.capture-x`, `mec108.create-staging-area`, `mec108.joining-another-player`, `mec108.time-x`, `mec108.side-quests-in-play`, `mec108.campaign-log`, `rr.campaign-pool`, `rr.player-elimination`, `rr.deckbuilding`). Stages 10038 (1A/1B, no quest points), 10039 (2A/2B, none; printed four times, one for each player, the engine deals one for each seat) and 10040 (3A/3B, 16 on B), read from the scans; Jailor Gornákh (10041) and Southern Gate (10042) set aside, the Angmar Orcs (09043 to 09046) set aside for 3A; the encounter deck of Escape from Mount Gram (10043 to 10053). Hall of Beorn gives Stop the Executioners (10053) no quest points where the scan prints 6 (`data/overrides.json`, as 09046 and 09051). The campaign card's boons (10176 to 10181) are in `campaignPlaced` by an override for this scenario only; each is one copy. A standalone game deals no campaign card, so no Loot the Dungeons.

- **Mechanics.** Captured cards (`mec108.captured-cards`) are the zone `captured`: out of play, facedown, controlled by nobody, each still owned, under a host (many to a host, any card type) or in a seat's captured deck (`Seat.captured`, top first). `Capture X` is a constant marker (the encounter family's `Time X` is the model): a card that enters play (revealed, added to the staging area, put into play engaged, a quest card that becomes a stage) captures X cards for each player sharing its staging area, before its When Revealed. A card leaving play rescues what lies under it, one `capturedRescued` event for the set; a rescued hero returns to play under its owner, ready, with no tokens. Progress on stage 2B rescues random cards instead of counting (`progressRescues`), the active location taking its share first. Each seat can hold its own staging area and stage (`StartSeparateStage`, Breaking of the Fellowship's pieces), and `JoinAnotherPlayer` (stage 2B's beginning-of-travel Forced) joins a seat whose own 2B holds nothing to the next area clockwise, or advances everyone to 3A together when none is left; a joined seat (`Seat.joined`) draws its staging, stage and active location from the area it joined. The engine files are `src/rules/families/captured/` (`pile.ts`, `entry.ts`, `rescue.ts`, `instead.ts`, `deck.ts`, `play.ts`, `join.ts`, `values.ts`) and `families/breaking/areas.ts`; the catalog is `src/catalog/families/captured.ts`.
- **Table** (code: `src/play/readouts/gram.ts`, `src/play/readouts/angmar.ts`, `src/play/cards/gram-preview.ts`, `src/play/words/scenarios/angmar/gram-table.ts`, `src/play/words/asks/escape-from-mount-gram.ts`, `src/play/store/selectors.ts`; `docs/design-system-table.md`, Cards and Choice tray). Each cited line creates a piece and each piece is on the table. 10038:A:1 prepares each player's captured deck: each living seat's threat dial wears the mark "Captured 12" (the count, "P1 · Captured 12" two-handed) with a note that names whose deck it is, and in round 1 the starting threat the line set to the starting hero's cost; 10039:B:2's joined seat adds "· joined P2" to its mark and draws the area it joined; the separate areas are labelled by seat and stage as Breaking of the Fellowship's. 10039:A:1, 10039:A:2 and `mec108.capture-x` put cards under a host (a stage, a Prison Cell, an enemy, a side quest, Loot): they are drawn facedown under it as one counted peek, the host wears the pill "3 captured", and its preview and the reader table say the count and the owners ("3 cards captured underneath, out of play and facedown (2 of Player 1's, 1 of Player 2's)"). 10175:A:1 Loot the Dungeons is one card in every seat's staging row and its preview says so. 10053:A:3 Stop the Executioners' time is a dial worded for this card (the last counter discards its captured cards and raises threat, never the loss of the game), warming near at 2 and 1. 10174:B:3's stolen cards are the Campaign Log's "Unique Cards Stolen by Goblins" row, and the deck builder refuses a stolen card in that player's deck with the sentence "the Goblins of Mount Gram stole this unique card (the campaign log notes it), so it cannot be in this player's deck for the rest of the campaign". A pick that spans seats (Dungeon Guard's, Prison Cell's) names the owner on each row; the owner-only picks (the starting hero, Captives of Gornákh, Feeble and Weary, Loot's boon) are owned in the sentence. No control types damage, resources, threat, progress or life. Test: `src/play/scenarios/escape-from-mount-gram-table.test.ts`.
- **Seats.** The engine plays one or two seats: stage 2A's printed four copies are dealt one for each seat, Prison Cell's four copies stay in the deck (two in a two-seat game), and "each player" counts the seats.
- **Decisions** (the orchestrator's and the builder's, applied as given). The Campaign Setup's Loot the Dungeons capture and the setting aside of the captured heroes' boons (10174:A:2) run in the new setup role `beforeReveal` (`ScenarioDefinition.beforeReveal`): after the quest card's Setup (10038:A:1) and before stage 1B's When Revealed (10038:B:1), so Loot takes the bottom 5 of each captured deck before stage 2A's Capture 7 takes the top 7; an `afterSetup` verb would resolve after 10038:B:1. The area seats of a game whose stages are separate (the quest phase, the travel phase, the failed-quest threat, the willpower of the seats sharing an area) read through `viewOf`; both equal the old values when no seat joined. The change to the engine is gated on fields only this scenario sets (`ScenarioState.capture`, `Seat.captured`, `Seat.joined`, `Seat.startHero`), so no shipped scenario's golden, fixture or step log moves and `ENGINE_VERSION` is unchanged. A player whom a Travel cost eliminates (Patrol Room at threat 48) travels nowhere: the Travel changes nothing and the effect's later instructions still run.

Span-settled for the choice, the random hero and the top; recommended for the not-leaving-play and for dropping the Easy resource.

Literal for the dial and no later raise; recommended for dropping the log modifier (not user-confirmed).

  22. 10051:A:1 Captives of Gornákh: each player in turn chooses one ally they control and captures it under the encounter card with the highest Threat among the encounter cards in play in that area (staged cards, the active location, enemies engaged with players there; a card with no Threat counts 0); the first player picks among ties; nothing with no card in play.   23. 10052 Interrogation: each player in turn discards the top card of their captured deck to their discard pile and raises threat by its printed cost (a hero costs 0; a discarded hero is dead, the blog agrees); if a player had none to discard Interrogation gains Doomed 2. Orchestrator decision taking the agent's recommendation (no source found: the Nightmare blog says only that heroes can still be in your discard pile if they died); recommended.
  32. The boons: Berelind's "a quest card in play" is any quest card in play in the player's area, which the player chooses: the current quest stage or a side quest in the staging area, encounter or player; Coruhaer's "defends" is a declared defender with the resource spent from any hero; Laermuin heals any damaged character; Mail of Eärnur hears any player's threat lowered; Silverthorn reads a Trait shared with an enemy in the victory display and adds the destroyed enemy to it once a game. Recommended (not user-confirmed).
  33. An eliminated player whose area other players joined (`mec108.joining-another-player`): the players that joined are still in the game and in that area, so it passes to the first of them clockwise, with its staging cards, active location and stage, and the others share it; an area nobody joined is discarded with its encounter cards as `mec109.multiple-staging-areas` says. The booklet and the local entries are silent on the host of a joined player being eliminated; a designer answer on a player eliminated at their own stage (the stage and its encounter cards are discarded) is the rule for an area nobody joined (https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/262016-questions-about-a-player-getting-eliminated; https://rulespal.com/the-lord-of-the-rings-the-card-game-escape-from-mount-gram/rulebook), and nothing covers a joined player (agent recommendation).
  34. Advancing together (`mec108.joining-another-player`, "If there is no player to join, then players must continue to stage 3"): the staging areas merge into one and each area's active location returns to the staging area with the cards of the area, since only one active location can exist at a time (`rr.active-location`) and the booklet discards an active location only for a joiner (https://rulespal.com/the-lord-of-the-rings-the-card-game-escape-from-mount-gram/rulebook; no ruling found). Agent recommendation.
