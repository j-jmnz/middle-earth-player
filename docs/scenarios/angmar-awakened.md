# Angmar Awakened

The rulings, readings and table pieces of Angmar Awakened, scenario by scenario. Product rules, the Domain and the Core round are in `docs/brief.md`; the authority order is in `.claude/rules/authority.md`.

The Angmar Awakened campaign is nine scenarios played in order: The Lost Realm (Intruders in Chetwood, The Weather Hills, Deadmen's Dike), the six Adventure Packs of the cycle (The Wastes of Eriador, Escape from Mount Gram, Across the Ettenmoors, The Treachery of Rhudaur, The Battle of Carn Dûm, The Dread Realm) and, for campaign mode, the Angmar Awakened Campaign Expansion (MEC108, rules doc `mec108`, RingsDB `AACE`), which adds the nine campaign cards, the boons, the burdens and the campaign-only cards. The campaign is registered (`data/campaigns/angmar-awakened.json`: `saga` false, no `xp`, `bookletId` `angmar-awakened`, each scenario's page of the book) and its nine scenarios are one box on the Scenarios tab, `angmar-awakened`. Which of them are playable is `src/campaign/playable.ts`; the campaign is a locked tile on the Scenarios tab until the last one is (`data/campaigns/index.json`). The campaign start is built and tested without the deal: `buildStart` deals each scenario's campaign card in Campaign Mode and the checklist reads its Setup. There is no XP and no Hard/Expert Mode card: the campaign runs on boons, burdens, side quests in the pool and the Damage Tracking rows of the log.

- **Campaign mode** (`mec108.campaign-mode`). The nine are played in order with their campaign cards, boons and burdens, under the Learn to Play's campaign rules. "The Amarthiúl hero cannot be used until indicated by a campaign card": the loadout refuses him (`HERO_BANS`, `src/campaign/loadout.ts`) in the first seven scenarios, and The Battle of Carn Dûm's campaign card (10188:A:2, "Amarthiúl may be included among a player's heroes") lifts it. No campaign card says to ban him again, so he stays usable in The Dread Realm (`recommended`: the card says "until", and a hero already on the log from part 8 would otherwise be refused; see Readings, Angmar Awakened campaign cards).
- **The log** (`mec108.campaign-log`, `mec108.campaign-log-tracking`, MEC108 Campaign Log sheet, PDF page 27). The Learn to Play's rows (Heroes, Fallen Heroes, Threat Modifiers, Boons, Burdens, Scenarios Completed, Score, Campaign Total) and four Damage Tracking rows (`src/campaign/log-angmar-awakened.ts`): the damage on Protect the Innocent / Arnor Ravaged (`protectDamage`, one number each Resolution replaces), the damage on each hero (`heroDamage`, by hero code, a hero with none left out), the unique cards stolen by Goblins (`stolenCards`, by seat) and the determined characters (`determined`). An older log reads with the defaults. A row shows once it holds something, and nothing types a number; the rows are laid out in the design system (Campaign log). The game reads them as `CampaignFacts.angmar`, in this campaign only.
- **Resolutions written by the shell.** `RecordDamageOn` notes the damage on the cards in play with the codes (Protect the Innocent 10158, and Arnor Ravaged 100158 on its other side; 0 when not in play); `RecordHeroDamage` notes the damage on each hero in play. 10156:B:1 notes it and earns Protect the Innocent; 10159:B:1 replaces the number; 10166:B:1 notes the damage on Protect the Innocent, takes it out of the pool for Arnor Ravaged and the first player chooses Iârion's Pendant or Amarthiúl's Courage for the group and which player takes it (`ChooseCards` to `player`, fixed to that seat's deck, as 11197:B:1 does); 10171:B:1 earns Fend Off Despair; 10188:B:1 notes the damage on each hero; 10193:B:1 prints the line 11215:B:4 and 12195:B:1 print ("The players win the campaign!") and is copied from them: it asks nothing and writes nothing, and the end screen reads the printed line. "Any player may add Iârion's Pendant to their deck" (10166:B:1) is read as the first player naming the player, the form the user ruled for the Ered Mithrin boon allies (see Boon allies, user ruling 2026-11-05); `recommended`, see Readings, Angmar Awakened campaign cards.
- **Setup written by the shell.** 10171:A:2: no +1 for changing heroes, a Determination in each starting hand and in the pool for each player, Fend Off Despair shuffled into the encounter deck. 10188:A:2: no +1 for changing heroes, and each player in turn may take one of Elrond's Champion, Lore of Ancient Arnor, Raiment of the Second Age or Rivendell Skirmishers into the opening hand (`ChooseCards`, each card once), which joins the pool. 10193:A:2: each player may search the deck for a boon card for the opening hand (`SearchBoonToHand`: no treasure is in this box), and each hero begins with the damage the log noted for it (`PlaceLoggedHeroDamage`, after the quest card's Setup).

The Prison Cell, Savage Trollspawn and Amarthiúl (Wastes) piles follow each quest's Setup over DragnCards' load layout (agent recommendation).

## Intruders in Chetwood

Part 1 (`10156`; `mec108.setup-intruders-in-chetwood`, `mec108.campaign-setup-intruders-in-chetwood`, `mec108.objective-allies`, `mec108.side-quests`, `mec108.side-quests-in-play`, `mec108.time-x`). Stage 09068 (30 quest points), Iârion set aside, one Orc War Party in the staging pile and the rest in the deck, four encounter side quests in the encounter deck (Orc Rearguard 3, Rescue Iârion 6, Orc Ambush 4, Lost in the Wilderness 4 quest points, read from the scans: `data/overrides.json`), and in campaign mode Iârion's Pendant (10157) and Protect the Innocent (10158, no quest points: it is never defeated). The game is dealt as having side quests when its encounter deck holds one (`markSideQuests`, `state.sideQuests`), which Fire in the Night's side quest deck does not need. Code: the `angmar` family (`src/catalog/families/angmar.ts`, `src/rules/families/angmar/`), the shared side quest reads (`src/rules/families/wilderland/night-quest.ts`), records `src/effects/authored/angmar-awakened/intruders-in-chetwood.ts`; tests `src/rules/families/angmar/index.test.ts` and `src/campaign/scenarios/intruders-in-chetwood/`.

- **Mechanics.** The amount `questCards` (the main quest card, each side quest in the staging area, encounter or player; at least 1) is the X of Iârion, Shrouded Hills, Rugged Country, Weight of Responsibility and Borders of Bree-land's Shadow. `Time X` (`Time`, a constant marker; `RemoveTimeCounters`, `lastTimeCounterRemoved`) puts X resource tokens on the card when it is revealed, takes one off each Time card at the end of each refresh phase (after the Forced effects of that phase) and announces the last. `HoldUnder` puts Iârion facedown under Rescue Iârion, in play and controlled by nobody, with his Pendant still on him; the defeat of the side quest gives him back to the first player, exhausted, and `DiscardHeld` discards him (he leaves play: the players lose). `ChooseCurrentQuest` and the `currentQuest` conditions make a quest card the current quest until the end of the phase; the progress placed on the current quest is bounded by the cap and the round's tally (`questRoom`) and a side quest with `progressRemovesDamage` takes it as damage removed. The campaign card's Setup attaches the Pendant (`AttachNewTo`) and offers each player, the first player first, the trade of 4 damage on Protect the Innocent for 1 resource on each of their heroes (`TradeDamageForResources`). `attackDamagedCharacter` is heard once for each attack that damages a character, as the attack ends.
- **Table** (code: `src/play/readouts/angmar.ts`, `src/play/cards/side-quest-mark.ts`, `src/play/cards/angmar-preview.ts`, `src/play/choice/sheet-parts.ts` `seatedLabel`, `src/play/words/scenarios/angmar/angmar-table.ts`; test `src/play/scenarios/intruders-in-chetwood-table.test.ts`). Three dials after the seats' in the top bar, each only when its card is in play: `angmar-protect` (Protect the Innocent's damage against 10, 10158:A:3; it warms from 8 and is critical at 9), `angmar-time` (the time counters left on Rescue Iârion, 09024:A:1 and A:3, a countdown; it warns at 2 left and is critical at 1) and `angmar-cap` (the progress placed on the current quest this round against Orc Rearguard's 3, 09023:A:1). A staged encounter side quest wears the strip a quest card does (progress over quest points); the one that is the current quest wears an accent "Current" pill inside its top edge, left of the damage token (09049:A:2, 09047:A:1, 09023:A:2) and Protect the Innocent a pill with its damage against the number that loses ("4/10", threat red at 9). Iârion is drawn under Rescue Iârion in the staging area by the same peek an attachment is (the held card of `captiveUnder`), and his preview says he is facedown in play with nobody controlling him; the held dial of a seat does not apply, since nobody controls him. Lost in the Wilderness' preview names each seat's hand under it and says they return when it is defeated; two seat marks say "Hand under" and, for the seat that controls him, "Iârion". A pick that spans seats names the owner on each row: Borders of Bree-land's enemy ("Angmar Orc \u00b7 engaged with Player 2"), Angmar Orc's ally ("Iârion \u00b7 Player 1's ally"); a first player's choice for the group is not owned, and the sheet adds who chooses.
- **Seats.** The engine plays one or two seats (`seatCount: 1 | 2`, `src/rules/core/state-types.ts`; the app's seat picker offers the same, `docs/brief.md`, Product), so the loss at 20 damage on Protect the Innocent with 3 or 4 players (10158:A:3) can not be reached in the app; the record keeps both numbers, the second read from the amount `players`, so a game with more seats would use it.
- **Readings** (agent recommendation unless a source is named; the section Readings to confirm, at the end of this file, lists those not user-confirmed).

  2. Printed line; https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/08/25/intruders-in-chetwood/. Recommended.
  3. Placing Iârion under Rescue Iârion is that card's own instruction and does not make him leave play (he stays in play, uncontrolled, facedown, the Pendant still attached); its Forced discard is the loss; the defeat of the side quest returns him exhausted under the first player. `rr.in-play-and-out-of-play`, `mec108.objective-allies`; https://hallofbeorn.com/LotR/Details/Rescue-Iarion-TLR and https://rulespal.com/the-lord-of-the-rings-the-card-game-the-lost-realm/rulebook (neither rules on it); the local FAQ 1.9 has no entry. Recommended.
  4. Time X: X resource tokens when the card is revealed, also by a search; one comes off each Time card at the end of every refresh phase, after the end-of-refresh Forced effects (Stop the War Party's raise); "after the last time counter is removed" is the removal that leaves 0. `mec108.time-x` settles the counters; the order against the Forced effects is recommended.
  5. Orc Rearguard's cap (09023:A:1) holds while it is in the staging area, for the main quest card or the side quest that is current, one tally for the round (it resets each round); progress that would go on Protect the Innocent counts. `faq.side-quest-current-quest-text` (the FAQ: "the text on each quest card in play is active"); https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/08/25/intruders-in-chetwood/ (a blog, not one of the authority's forums). The active text is researched (the FAQ); the cap on the current quest follows it and the blog. Community-sourced. Tension: Fire in the Night's ruling 5 (only the placing of progress is redirected) is kept for its own cards; Rugged Country, Orc Rearguard and Pressing Needs read a side quest as the current quest by their own words.
  6. Orc Rearguard's gained Forced effect fires at the end of the staging step when it is the current quest then, and reveals an additional card. Literal.
  7. Rugged Country's +2 Threat holds while a side quest is the current quest. `mec108.side-quests-in-play`. Span-settled.
  8. Chetwood Forest's Travel line is a cost the players must pay (`rr.travel`): they cannot travel to it while no enemy is in the staging area; when they can, the first player decides which player engages, and that player chooses the enemy. `rr.travel`, `rr.first-player`. Span-settled.
  9. Chetwood Forest's Shadow: the defending player chooses; the enemy returns after the attack resolves. Literal.
  10. Borders of Bree-land's gained Forced effect returns ONE enemy engaged with a player, chosen by the first player, per copy in the staging area; the printed card says "1 enemy engaged with a player" (the Vision of the Palantir page says each player). Scan `.cache/scans/09019.A.jpg`. Span-settled.
  11. Outlying Homestead stops the players reducing their threat by player card effects only (`noThreatReduction`, as 143076:B:1). Recommended.
  12. Sudden Assault: when the committed Willpower is greater than the total Threat in the staging area now, each enemy there makes an immediate attack against the first player, each dealt a shadow card (`faq.enemy-attacks-outside-combat`), and they stay in the staging area; the Orc War Party is searched for and added only when no attack was made, and after the attacks, so it does not attack (`noneReturned`, a canceled attack counts as made); the effect cannot be canceled. Recommended for the no-attack case; https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/08/25/intruders-in-chetwood/ ("If no enemies are in the staging area, the players add an Orc War Party").
  13. Surprising Speed prints the text of the Dark of Mirkwood's card of the same name (41017:A:1), so its record is a copy. Literal.
  14. Angmar Orc: the first player chooses to discard an ally from play, any ally in play, Iârion included (the engine offers him, and the sheet warns), or to reveal an additional card; with no ally the second is forced. Recommended.
  15. Angmar Captain: after it destroys an ally, the top card of the defending player's deck is discarded; an ally card discarded makes it attack again, against the defending player, with the earlier shadow cards discarded and a new one dealt; X is the allies discarded from the deck, not the characters destroyed. FAQ 1.9 (the additional attack deals a new shadow card, `faq.additional-attacks`). Researched.
  16. Orc Ambush: constant +1 Threat, Attack and Defense to every Orc enemy in play while it is staged; its When Revealed searches the deck and discard pile for an Orc enemy for each player in turn. Span-settled (`mec108.side-quests`).
  17. Pressing Needs: the first player chooses to search the encounter deck and discard pile for an encounter side quest and reveal it, or to make a quest card other than the current quest (the main quest card or a staged side quest, encounter or player) the current quest until the end of the phase; an option with nothing to do is not offered. Recommended.
  18. Weight of Responsibility reveals one card for each quest card in play at resolution, each in a staging of its own; its Shadow discards that many attachments the defending player controls, his choice. Count recommended (reading 1).
  19. Lost in the Wilderness: each player in turn places their hand under it; only its own defeat returns them (a card under it when it leaves play any other way is not sent to a discard pile: no path of this scenario does). Literal.
  20. Stop the War Party's Setup puts Iârion into play under the first player, adds the staged Orc War Party and has each player in turn, the first player first, search the encounter deck for a location with a different title to add to the staging area (not revealed). Recommended (as 19109:A:1 and 144064:A:1).
  21. No engagement checks (09068:B:1, the players may still engage); the refresh phase's Forced effect raises each player's threat by the number of enemies in the staging area (counted when it resolves); the stage cannot be defeated unless the first player controls Iârion, nor while an Orc War Party is in play (that one binds the main quest only: an encounter side quest is still defeated). Literal; `mec108.objective-allies` and the Parth Galen record.
  22. "The first player gains control of Iârion" (09016:A:2) follows the first player token: control passes with it when the token passes in the refresh phase, as it does for Aragorn and Calphon, the same printed sentence (`mec112.aragorn`, `mec113.multiplayer-aragorn`); `mec108.objective-allies` states that control of Amarthiúl does not pass because his own Response moves it, and says nothing of Iârion. Implemented as the constant `ControlledBy` of 11028:A:1. Precedent by the same wording; community https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/08/25/intruders-in-chetwood/ (a blog, not one of the authority's forums; read: the ally "will stay with the first player during the entire quest, switching between players if there are more than 1 player"). Community-sourced. The condition of Stop the War Party's 09068:B:3 reads the first player's control after the sweep.
  23. Iârion's Pendant (10157:A:2): the controller of Iârion adds it to the victory display to cancel the When Revealed effects of a card just revealed from the encounter deck, or a shadow effect just triggered; not a side quest's (`mec108.side-quests`), not one that cannot be canceled (Sudden Assault); nobody controls it while Iârion lies under Rescue Iârion; its Willpower bonus needs a host that is a character (the host held facedown is not one). Span-settled.
  24. Protect the Innocent's Forced effect places exactly 1 damage after each attack that deals at least one damage to a character (enemy attacks of any kind, not Archery or direct damage); progress that would be placed on it removes that much damage, no more than it holds; it is lost at 10 damage (20 with 3 or 4 players), checked continuously. https://hallofbeorn.com/LotR/Details/Protect-the-Innocent-AAC (no rulings). Recommended for the "1 an attack" reading.
  25. The campaign card's Setup (10156:A:2) attaches the Pendant, adds Protect the Innocent, and offers each player once the trade of 4 damage for 1 resource on each hero; the engine does not stop the group trading itself to a loss. Literal.


## The Weather Hills

Part 2 (`10159`; `mec108.setup-the-weather-hills`, `mec108.campaign-setup-the-weather-hills`, `mec108.orc-deck`, `mec108.side-quests`, `mec108.side-quests-in-play`, `mec108.time-x`, `rr.campaign-pool`, `ltp.permanent`). Stage 09069 (1A/1B, no quest points) and 09070 (2A/2B, 20 quest points); Hunting the Orcs (09071, back 09072 Savage Counter-attack, a two-code card) and a Weathered Hilltop in the staging area, Exposed Ridge the active location, Amon Forn (09026) and Orc Ambush (09046) set aside, the Orc deck (`angmarOrcDeck`: Concealed Orc-camp, Angmar Orc, Angmar Marauder and Angmar Captain, with the Easy counts) beside the encounter deck, and four encounter side quests (Find Shelter 6, Make Camp 6, Search the Ruins 8 and, in campaign mode, Mysterious Omens 9 quest points, read from the scans: `data/overrides.json`). In campaign mode the campaign card's Setup (10159:A:2) adds Mysterious Omens to the Campaign Pool and shuffles it into the encounter deck, and adds Protect the Innocent to the staging area with the damage the log noted for it; Protect the Innocent lies in the pool from part 1, so Campaign Setup step 8 skips it and shuffles in no second Mysterious Omens (`encounterDeal`, `src/campaign/checklist.ts`). The five boons (10161 to 10165) are dealt set aside with the scenario (`scenarioCampaignPlaced`, `data/overrides.json`), since Mysterious Omens' own text names them. Code: the `angmar` family (`src/catalog/families/angmar.ts`, `src/rules/families/angmar/`), the Orc deck pile (`src/rules/core/piles.ts`, `src/catalog/scenario-data.ts`), records `src/effects/authored/angmar-awakened/the-weather-hills.ts`; tests `src/rules/families/angmar/weather-hills.test.ts`, `weather-hills-cards.test.ts`, `src/campaign/angmar-awakened-shell.test.ts` and `src/campaign/scenarios/the-weather-hills/`.

- **Mechanics.** The Orc deck (`mec108.orc-deck`) is a plain pile with no discard pile of its own: its cards go to the encounter discard pile, and an effect that names it is ignored while it is empty. `ShufflePile` shuffles it at the Setup (09069:A:1), `AddFromPile` with `reveal` reveals its top card for the first player when the active location is explored (09069:B:2; two cards with three or more players) and `ShufflePileIntoEncounterDeck` moves it into the encounter deck (09070:A:1). `StageSetAside` with `reveal` reveals a set-aside card (keywords and When Revealed resolve, a trigger on a reveal hears it) and adds it to the staging area: Orc Ambush and Amon Forn at stage 2A. `FirstTraitSurge` gives the first Weather treachery of each round surge. `EndPhase` ends the phase in progress once the effect and any attack under way are done (a table flag `phaseEnds` read by the machine). `FlipInPlace` with `entersPlay` flips Hunting the Orcs to Savage Counter-attack with its tokens, which then enters play and is heard (09072:A:1). The Mission objective is a Pick (`{type: objective, trait: Mission}`) followed by `PlaceResourceTokens` or `RemoveResourceTokens` with `on: picked`; Sheltered Valley's Travel cost is unpayable while the Pick finds no card holding a token (`travel.unpayable`, `travelCostUnpayable`). `AttackFromStaging` with `target: subject` makes the Orc that was added attack. `ModifyStat blankText` with `keepsKeywords` blanks the text box of a damaged character and leaves its keywords (and Traits, which a blank text box never removes: `rr.blank`). `PlaceLoggedDamageOn` places the damage the log noted on Protect the Innocent. `ChooseBoon` asks each player, the first player first, which of the boons still set aside to take or none, then which hero he controls carries it (`AttachSetAside`); the Resolution's `EarnAttached` records each boon with its hero. `attachmentFromDiscard` is Stalwart's permission: its Action marks the hero until the end of the phase, and the player then plays the attachment from his discard pile with the ordinary Play intent, paying its cost, onto that hero alone; the play uses the permission up.
- **Table** (code: `src/play/readouts/angmar.ts`, `src/play/cards/angmar-preview.ts`, `src/play/store/pile-ids.ts`, `src/play/words/scenarios/angmar/angmar-table.ts`, `src/play/words/asks/the-weather-hills.ts`; test `src/play/scenarios/the-weather-hills-table.test.ts`; `docs/design-system-table.md`, Piles and Cards). Hunting the Orcs' tokens against 3 more than the players warm toward the flip (`angmar-hunt`, 09071:A:1); Savage Counter-attack's tokens count down to the loss at 0 and warm as they fall (`angmar-savage`, 09072:A:2 and A:3); the Orc deck is a pile of the second slot with its count (09069:A:1, 09069:B:2, 09070:A:1); Protect the Innocent's damage dial is Intruders in Chetwood's (10158:A:3), drawn again; the damage on a character is its damage token; a boon is an attachment drawn under its hero (10160:A:3); each pick that spans seats names the owner on each row (09029:A:1, 09052:A:1, 10160:A:3's hero), and a first player's choice for the group is not owned.
- **Seats.** The engine plays one or two seats, so the Orc deck's second card with three or more players (09069:B:2) and Hunting the Orcs' 3 + players above two are not reached in the app; the records read the amount `players`, so a game with more seats would use it.

## Deadmen's Dike

Part 3 (`10166`; `mec108.setup-deadmens-dike`, `mec108.campaign-setup-deadmens-dike`, `mec108.indestructible`, `mec108.side-quests`, `mec108.side-quests-in-play`, `mec108.time-x`, `rr.player-elimination`, `rr.campaign-pool`, `ltp.permanent`). Stage 09073 (1A/1B, 11 quest points on B) and 09074 (2A/2B, 13 quest points on B); Fornost Square (09038) in the staging area, Thaurdir (09033) and Iârion (09016) set aside, the encounter deck of Deadmen's Dike, Dark Sorcery and Cursed Dead with three encounter side quests in it (The Shadow World 5, Seal the Tomb 6, The Power of Angmar 8 quest points, read from the scans: `data/overrides.json`), and in campaign mode Iârion's Pendant (10157), Protect the Innocent (10158) and Arnor Ravaged (100158, its other side: one physical card), Amarthiúl's Courage (10167) and the three burdens (10168, 10169, 10170); the five boons of Mysterious Omens (10161 to 10165) are listed for this scenario and every later one while Omens can lie in the pool. This is the last scenario of The Lost Realm; The Wastes of Eriador follows it. Code: the `angmar` family (`src/catalog/families/angmar.ts`, `src/rules/families/angmar/`: `burdens.ts`, `quest.ts` `hitPointsLeft`, `lock.ts`, `blank.ts`), the `deck` family (`src/rules/families/deck/`: `discarded.ts`, `pressure.ts`), records `src/effects/authored/angmar-awakened/deadmens-dike.ts` and the box's `angmar-awakened.ts` (10166:B:1); tests `src/campaign/scenarios/deadmens-dike/` and `src/play/scenarios/deadmens-dike-table.test.ts`.

- **Mechanics.** A stage that eliminates a player with no cards in his deck (09073:B:3, 09074:B:2) is the table flag `eliminatedAtEmptyDeck`, a constant "if" checked at each settle once nothing is resolving (`eliminateEmptyPiles`, as the empty hand of The Road to Isengard): the seat leaves as `rr.player-elimination` says (its cards discarded, its enemies back to the staging area, the first player token passing, `faq.first-player-elimination`) and the last seat out loses. Stage 2B's "cannot defeat this stage while Thaurdir has any hit points remaining" is `Restrict advance` under the condition `hitPointsLeft` (a card with that code in play with damage below its hit points); Thaurdir is Indestructible, so the stage is defeated the moment the 9th damage is on him while its quest points are full (`faq.progress-without-advancing`: progress keeps being placed meanwhile). The trigger `deckTopDiscarded` is one event for each discard effect or cost that moved a card from the top of a deck (Fornost Square counts the first player's), and the amount `discarded` (`cost`, `types`) adds up what the running effect discarded from every deck, a Shadow effect with `DiscardFromDeck` running in a frame so it can read it (Thaurdir's Damned, Haunted Keep, Terror of the North). `IncreaseCost` is a constant on a card in play that every player's cost reads, for any card played from any zone (Heavy Curse). The flag `discardLocked` is held by The Power of Angmar in the staging area: a player card's effect cannot take a card out of a discard pile (a Pick `from: discard`, `MoveCard`, `ShuffleDiscardIntoDeck`, `PlayFromDiscard`, the Stalwart grant); an encounter card's effect can. `playerAbilityTriggered` is heard by Deadmen's Gate for each Action or Response a player triggers (an event played for its Action included; not a Forced effect, a Travel line or an encounter card's Response such as Iârion's: `announcePlayerAbility`). The pick filter `copyInDiscard` is the controller's discard pile (Dark Sorcery's When Revealed: his), or with `owner` a card of the same title in the card's owner's discard pile, the card itself counting when it lies there (Cursed Dead's and Dark Sorcery's Shadow, Heavy Curse). Thaurdir enters play through `AddSetAsideToStaging` (the encounter move helper, so the cards that hear a card added to the staging area hear him). Thaurdir's burdens: `AttachRandomBurden` (the seeded shuffle, one of the burdens not in the game, spawned from the supply when none is set aside), `MoveToHero` (the hero with the most resources, non-objective attachments or damage, the burden's current host counting; the first player chooses among heroes that tie), `AttachSelfToNamed` for the pool burdens' Setups at a later scenario, `blanksTraits` (a blank text box has no Traits either), `MoveDamageTo` with an amount (Arnor Ravaged). The Resolution is `10166:resolution:1`: the damage on Protect the Innocent is noted, it leaves the pool for Arnor Ravaged, the first player picks the Pendant or Amarthiúl's Courage and the player who takes it, each Omens boon attached to a hero is recorded with that hero (`EarnAttached`, as The Weather Hills does; a boon the pool already held on that hero is not recorded twice) and the burden Thaurdir carried joins the pool (`EarnAllBurdens`); a lost game writes nothing.
- **Table** (code: `src/play/readouts/angmar.ts`, `src/play/cards/angmar-preview.ts`, `src/play/words/scenarios/angmar/angmar-table.ts`, `src/play/words/asks/deadmens-dike.ts`; test `src/play/scenarios/deadmens-dike-table.test.ts`; `docs/design-system-table.md`, Cards). Thaurdir's damage against the 9 hit points, with the stage's own 13 progress, is what wins stage 2B: a goal dial (`angmar-thaurdir`, "Thaurdir"; 09074:B:2: it fills in the accent, never warms and has no warning; it reads his hit points as the stage's hold does, `hitPointsOf`), and his preview says the damage, the Indestructible rule, the 3 he heals on each Sorcery treachery, what the stage needs and the burden he carries. Each seat's deck count is a seat mark while a stage eliminates a player with no cards in his deck ("P1 · Deck 12", solo "Deck 12"; 09073:B:3, 09074:B:2): it warns near at 5 cards and is critical at 2, "At 0 this player is eliminated." Broken Battlements' time counters are Find Shelter's dial worded for this card (09036:A:2), one dial for each of its copies (`angmar-time:<card id>`, named by where it lies): the last counter makes each player discard 5 cards (6 on stage 1B) and place 1 counter, so it warns near, and critical at the last counter when a living seat's deck is at or under the discard (the seat is named). Fornost Square's resource tokens are drawn as any card's tokens, and its preview says "+N Threat" for them and what places one (09038:A:1, 09038:A:2). A burden is drawn under its host wherever it moves (Thaurdir in the staging area or an engagement area, the hero it moved to), and its preview says where it lies and what it does there ("On Aragorn: -2 Willpower and its text box, Traits included, is blank. After a Sorcery treachery is revealed it moves to the hero with the most resources."; on Thaurdir "with no effect on him"). The Power of Angmar's preview says what its lock stops. Protect the Innocent's damage dial (`angmar-protect`) is Chetwood's, drawn again because the card is staged here too, and Iârion marks the seat that controls him ("P1 · Iârion"), merged with the deck mark. The choices are worded in the asks file: Unnatural Fog's either/or and A Fell Wraith's discard or return are each player's own (two-handed they lead with the player; the discard, and Unnatural Fog's, warn "Tap again: your deck would be empty and you would be eliminated. Mysterious Omens, which may lie in this encounter deck from the pool, is worded by the word chain: its record is not one of this scenario's cards. No control types damage, resources, threat, progress or life.
- **Seats.** The engine plays one or two seats, so the reveal of 1 encounter card per player and the search of each player for a Baleful Shade are read from the number of players; a game with more seats would use it.
- **Decisions** (the orchestrator's, applied as given). A Travel line is a cost the players must be able to pay (`rr.travel`; Chetwood Forest's 09018:A:1 reads the same way, Intruders in Chetwood Reading 8): with no enemy in the encounter discard pile the players cannot travel to Norbury Tombs.
