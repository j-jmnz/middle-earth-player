# Angmar Awakened

The rulings, readings and table pieces of Angmar Awakened, scenario by scenario. Product rules, the Domain and the Core round are in `docs/brief.md`; the authority order is in `.claude/rules/authority.md`.

The Angmar Awakened campaign is nine scenarios played in order: The Lost Realm (Intruders in Chetwood, The Weather Hills, Deadmen's Dike), the six Adventure Packs of the cycle (The Wastes of Eriador, Escape from Mount Gram, Across the Ettenmoors, The Treachery of Rhudaur, The Battle of Carn Dûm, The Dread Realm) and, for campaign mode, the Angmar Awakened Campaign Expansion (MEC108, rules doc `mec108`, RingsDB `AACE`), which adds the nine campaign cards, the boons, the burdens and the campaign-only cards. The campaign is registered (`data/campaigns/angmar-awakened.json`: `saga` false, no `xp`, `bookletId` `angmar-awakened`, each scenario's page of the book) and its nine scenarios are one box on the Scenarios tab, `angmar-awakened`. Which of them are playable is `src/campaign/playable.ts`; the campaign is a locked tile on the Scenarios tab until the last one is (`data/campaigns/index.json`). The campaign start is built and tested without the deal: `buildStart` deals each scenario's campaign card in Campaign Mode and the checklist reads its Setup. There is no XP and no Hard/Expert Mode card: the campaign runs on boons, burdens, side quests in the pool and the Damage Tracking rows of the log.

- **Campaign mode** (`mec108.campaign-mode`). The nine are played in order with their campaign cards, boons and burdens, under the Learn to Play's campaign rules. "The Amarthiúl hero cannot be used until indicated by a campaign card": the loadout refuses him (`HERO_BANS`, `src/campaign/loadout.ts`) in the first seven scenarios, and The Battle of Carn Dûm's campaign card (10188:A:2, "Amarthiúl may be included among a player's heroes") lifts it. No campaign card says to ban him again, so he stays usable in The Dread Realm (`recommended`: the card says "until", and a hero already on the log from part 8 would otherwise be refused; see Readings, Angmar Awakened campaign cards).
- **The log** (`mec108.campaign-log`, `mec108.campaign-log-tracking`, MEC108 Campaign Log sheet, PDF page 27). The Learn to Play's rows (Heroes, Fallen Heroes, Threat Modifiers, Boons, Burdens, Scenarios Completed, Score, Campaign Total) and four Damage Tracking rows (`src/campaign/log-angmar-awakened.ts`): the damage on Protect the Innocent / Arnor Ravaged (`protectDamage`, one number each Resolution replaces), the damage on each hero (`heroDamage`, by hero code, a hero with none left out), the unique cards stolen by Goblins (`stolenCards`, by seat) and the determined characters (`determined`). An older log reads with the defaults. A row shows once it holds something, and nothing types a number; the rows are laid out in the design system (Campaign log). The game reads them as `CampaignFacts.angmar`, in this campaign only.
- **Resolutions written by the shell.** `RecordDamageOn` notes the damage on the cards in play with the codes (Protect the Innocent 10158, and Arnor Ravaged 100158 on its other side; 0 when not in play); `RecordHeroDamage` notes the damage on each hero in play. 10156:B:1 notes it and earns Protect the Innocent; 10159:B:1 replaces the number; 10166:B:1 notes the damage on Protect the Innocent, takes it out of the pool for Arnor Ravaged and the first player chooses Iârion's Pendant or Amarthiúl's Courage for the group and which player takes it (`ChooseCards` to `player`, fixed to that seat's deck, as 11197:B:1 does); 10171:B:1 earns Fend Off Despair; 10188:B:1 notes the damage on each hero; 10193:B:1 prints the line 11215:B:4 and 12195:B:1 print ("The players win the campaign!") and is copied from them: it asks nothing and writes nothing, and the end screen reads the printed line. "Any player may add Iârion's Pendant to their deck" (10166:B:1) is read as the first player naming the player, the form the user ruled for the Ered Mithrin boon allies (see Boon allies, user ruling 2026-11-05); `recommended`, see Readings, Angmar Awakened campaign cards.
- **Setup written by the shell.** 10171:A:2: no +1 for changing heroes, a Determination in each starting hand and in the pool for each player, Fend Off Despair shuffled into the encounter deck. 10188:A:2: no +1 for changing heroes, and each player in turn may take one of Elrond's Champion, Lore of Ancient Arnor, Raiment of the Second Age or Rivendell Skirmishers into the opening hand (`ChooseCards`, each card once), which joins the pool. 10193:A:2: each player may search the deck for a boon card for the opening hand (`SearchBoonToHand`: no treasure is in this box), and each hero begins with the damage the log noted for it (`PlaceLoggedHeroDamage`, after the quest card's Setup).
- **Readings, Angmar Awakened data (scans, counts and piles).** The MEC108 rulebook is pinned from the FFG host (`cdn.svc.asmodee.net`), identical to Hall of Beorn's S3 copy (https://hallofbeorn.s3.amazonaws.com/Images/LotR/Rules/MEC108.pdf). The Easy counts of Angmar Captain 09045 (1), Angmar Marauder 09044 (2) in Escape from Mount Gram and Ruins of Arnor 09056 (2) in The Treachery of Rhudaur follow Hall of Beorn's `EasyQuantity` against DragnCards' E05 decks, on the golden-bordered set icon (https://www.fantasyflightgames.com/en/news/2016/8/12/travel-the-darkest-lands-of-the-north/): recommended. `MEC108_CHANGED` is empty because no scan shows the MEC108 printing of the reprinted encounter cards (agent recommendation). The Prison Cell, Savage Trollspawn and Amarthiúl (Wastes) piles follow each quest's Setup over DragnCards' load layout (agent recommendation).
- **Readings, Angmar Awakened campaign cards.** No official ruling, FAQ entry or agreed community answer was found for these (read: Hall of Beorn's campaign card pages https://www.hallofbeorn.com/LotR/Details/Deadmen's-Dike-Campaign-AAC (no rulings listed), the Vision of the Palantir question-of-the-week posts https://visionofthepalantir.com/2025/03/09/qotw-the-weather-hills and https://visionofthepalantir.com/2025/04/19/qotw-the-battle-of-carn-dum (game reports only), the FFG news posts of the cycle https://www.fantasyflightgames.com/en/news/2015/9/24/sins-of-the-past/ and https://www.fantasyflightgames.com/en/news/2015/11/6/the-battle-is-joined/; r/lotrlcg and BoardGameGeek searches found no thread), so each is `recommended`. (1) "Any player may add Iârion's Pendant to their deck if it is chosen" (10166:B:1) is the first player naming the player who takes it, as for Ered Mithrin's boon allies. (2) "Add the Mysterious Omens side quest to the Campaign Log" (10159:A:2) reads as the Campaign Pool, the only place the Campaign Setup list and Mysterious Omens' own "remove it from the Campaign Pool" (10160:A:3) put it. (3) Amarthiúl after The Battle of Carn Dûm: "until indicated by a campaign card" (mec108.campaign-mode) ends at 10188:A:2 and no later card bans him.
