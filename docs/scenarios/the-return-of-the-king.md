# The Return of the King

The rulings, readings and table pieces of The Return of the King, scenario by scenario. Product rules, the Domain and the Core round are in `docs/brief.md`; the authority order is in `.claude/rules/authority.md`.

MEC113 (2024) reprints **The Flame of the West** (RingsDB pack `FotW`) and **The Mountain of Fire** (`MoF`) as the third saga box: six scenarios, numbered 13 to 18, continuing The Two Towers. The campaign id is `the-return-of-the-king`: its own campaign and save with the one `CampaignLog` type. Its data is built (`data/campaigns/the-return-of-the-king.json`, six scenario files, the ledger `data/printed/the-return-of-the-king.json`, its `mec113.*` spans in `data/rules/index.json`) and the app offers it as a campaign and as a box with its own booklet (MEC113, `bookletId`). Its scenarios are playable as `src/campaign/playable.ts` lists them; a Two Towers save that finished offers its continuation once the first is.

- **Rules the box adds.** The saga hero, one ☺ hero at a time, no other version of Aragorn or Frodo as a starting hero or in a deck, Aragorn outside the hero limit and the Ring-bearer's rules are The Two Towers' (`mec113.aragorn`, `mec113.fellowship-sphere-heroes`, `mec113.the-one-ring`, `mec113.campaign-mode-aragorn`; `src/campaign/saga.ts`, `checklist-saga.ts`). The box adds `mec113.campaign-mode-ring-bearer`: in a scenario that grants Aragorn no card may share a title with the campaign's Ring-bearer. The only Ring-bearer is Frodo Baggins, so the ban is the Two Towers' own (`titleBansOf`) and, as there, holds in standalone play too. The box bans no ally or hero title.
- **Continuing the saga.** A new Return of the King save may take in a finished Two Towers save (`SAGA_PREVIOUS`, `src/campaign/continue.ts`), as a Two Towers save takes in a Fellowship one ("The Two Towers", Continuing the saga). The enemies under The Black Gate that Journey to the Cross-roads recorded are copied too (`blackGate`): The Battle of the Pelennor Fields sets the Harad ones aside. A fresh start is an empty log. The Campaign Total carries on from the Two Towers save.
- **The campaign cards** (`src/effects/authored/return-of-the-king/the-return-of-the-king.ts`, verbs in `src/catalog/families/campaign.ts`). Written: the Setup of 145066, 145070 and 146074 (no +1 for changing heroes on 145066 and 146074; the first player may raise each player's threat by 1 to search for Andúril; the burdens of A Shadow of the Past, Flight to the Ford, The Ring Goes South, The Passage of the Marshes and Shelob's Lair are left out of the encounter deck, `playerDecks` unset since the line says "from the encounter deck"), and every Resolution: 145066 adds each Overcome by Fear on a threat dial to the pool for its player and earns the Army of the Dead boon with the Army in the victory display; 145070 records the resources on The Corsair Fleet and removes the Army boon from the pool; 145078 earns a Wraith on Wings for each player and an Esquire on a hero for its controller; 146069 removes the boons attached to Shagrat from the pool; 146074 records the tokens on The Black Gate, removes a Wraith on Wings from the pool for each copy in the victory display, adds Gwaihir to the pool for the players when the first player controls him (the boon has no seat of its own) and leaves a hero in a discard pile off the fallen list; 146079 has the first player keep or destroy The One Ring, which loses or wins the campaign. Not written, left to the scenarios that play them since each needs a mechanic that scenario builds: the Setup line that places tokens in the game (146079:A:1) and the Forced line of the campaign card in play (146079:A:2); 145078's Setup and Forced are written with its scenario (`pelennor/stages.ts`), 146069's with The Tower of Cirith Ungol's (`cirith-ungol/stages.ts`, the campaign family's `SetAsideBoon`, `GuardWithSetAside` and `MoveAttachment`).
- **The log's notes** (`src/campaign/log-return-of-the-king.ts`): `corsairFleet`, `blackGateTokens` and `campaignEnd` ("won" or "lost"), each null until recorded, shown as rows on the log screen once they hold something, and handed to the game as `CampaignFacts.notes` with the Black Gate enemies. The log screen reads the Two Towers columns (the per-seat threat penalty, the enemies under The Black Gate) in this box too.

## The Passing of the Grey Company

Scenario 13 (`mec113.phantom`, `mec113.army-of-the-dead`, `mec113.saga-staging-rules`; records `src/effects/authored/return-of-the-king/the-passing-of-the-grey-company.ts`, family `grey-company`, `src/rules/families/grey-company/`, the dial copies in `attachments/dial.ts`). The players start with an Overcome by Fear on each threat dial (a card in the seat's play area under its control, attached to no card) and the first player controls Aragorn (`mec113.multiplayer-aragorn`: he goes to whoever is first player, so only that player's pool holds a Fellowship resource). Every reading below is applied as written; the ones marked agent recommendation (no ruling found) are not user-confirmed. Sources: [A] https://visionofthepalantir.com/2023/08/02/the-passing-of-the-grey-company/, [B] https://theroadlotr.wordpress.com/2019/03/22/the-passing-of-the-grey-company-playthrough/, [C] https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/228190-campaign-mode-overcome-by-fear-battle-of-pelennor-fields, [D] https://hallofbeorn.com/LotR/Details/The-Dead-are-Following-TRotK, [E] https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/230355-selecting-from-or-option-that-does-nothing-once-youve-tried-it, [F] the FFG FAQ v1.9 (no entry for this box).

- **Readings.**
  - 145065:B:2 (stage 3B). 5 or more progress never defeats 3B and does not win; the only win is "When The Stone of Erech leaves play as an explored location". The record is a `Restrict advance` beside the win. Alternative: the literal `rr.quest-advancement` and `rr.winning-the-game`, where 5 progress on the last stage wins, which makes the Stone's Travel ("Remove 5 progress from stage 3B") unreachable. Source: the card's own Travel line (145017:A:3), `faq.progress-without-advancing` (progress has no upper limit), [A], [B] (plays 3B at 8 of 5 without winning and clears 5 by travelling); [F] is silent. Status: agent recommendation (no ruling found; two playthroughs apply it). That the Stone of Erech is the win is confirmed by the printed line.
  - 145064:B:2 (initial threat level). The floor is the threat set at Setup step 2 (the heroes' threat cost and the campaign penalty; before any hero Setup such as Éowyn's and before the Andúril +1, a step 7 raise), `startingThreat`; a reduction stops at the floor and does nothing below it, while stage 2B is current. Alternative: the heroes' combined threat cost now. Source: `rr.initial-threat-level`, `rr.setup`, `rr.campaign-setup-checklist`, the Andúril reading above, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/228696-flame-of-the-west (Éowyn's Setup reduction and a later raise do not move the floor). Status: confirmed.
  - 145064:B:1 (X). The highest threat among living players, read live as the quest points of stage 2B; the stage advances when progress is placed that reaches X (`rr.quest-advancement`), and a fall in X does not advance it by itself. Alternative: X fixed when 2B is revealed. Source: `rr.quest-advancement`, [A]. Status: confirmed for the live X ([A], [B]); agent recommendation (no ruling found) that a fall in X does not advance the stage without a placement (one report in https://visionofthepalantir.com/2025/01/05/qotw-the-passing-of-the-grey-company-results/ is ambiguous).
  - Phantom (145022-145025 A:1, 145067 A:1). When an attack by a Phantom enemy reaches "determine combat damage" (`rr.step-6-4-3`) with damage above 0, the damage is canceled before any damage window and the defending player (also when a sentinel defends) raises their threat by as much, "raise your threat" and not an encounter-card raise. Defended, undefended, immediate, from-staging and additional attacks all do; archery and card damage do not. Alternative: raise only for the damage left after Response cancels. Source: `mec113.phantom` (the Shadow Host against Gimli example), `rr.defending-player`; [A], [B] and [F] give no cancel window. Status: agent recommendation (no ruling found).
  - "Loses the Phantom keyword" (145065:B:1 for each Oathbreaker enemy, in the staging area or engaged, while The Stone of Erech is the active location; 145023:A:3 for the attacking enemy for that attack). A flag that removes Phantom alone (`losesPhantom`), not all keywords. Status: literal.
  - "Either attach a set aside Overcome by Fear ... or ..." (145022:A:2, 145024:A:2, 145025:A:2, 145027:A:2, 145028:A:2, 145029:A:1 and A:2, 145067:A:3). An option that cannot be done (no copy set aside, the dial already holds one, no ally to discard) is not offered; with one option left it runs without asking. Alternative: a player may pick an impossible option and do nothing. Source: `rr.effects`, "Options that cannot be fulfilled" above, [E]. Status: researched.
  - 145016:A:1 (Limit 1 per threat dial) limits every attach of the card, in Setup and by those options, to one copy per dial. Status: literal ([A]).
  - 145063:A:1. The four dealt copies are set aside; each player attaches one and every copy left is removed from the game (three in a solo game, two with two players); a copy detached later (145016:A:3, 145065:A:1) goes back to the set aside cards and any player may attach it. A pool copy in a later part runs 145016:A:1 at Campaign Setup. Status: literal.
  - 145016:A:3 (Fellowship resource). The raise resolves first (`rr.then`); "you" is the dial's controller; only the first player's pool holds a Fellowship resource, so only that player's dial can be detached (and at the end of the round, after the token passes, the new first player's). 145020:A:2 (Travel: spend 1 Fellowship resource) is paid by the first player the same way. Alternative: any player may spend from the first player's pool. Source: `mec113.fellowship-sphere-heroes`, `mec113.multiplayer-aragorn`, `rr.then`, [A] ("spend a Fellowship resource from Aragorn").   - 145023:A:2 chooser. The revealing player chooses (Peril resolves it alone; saga staging gives "you" and the extra card to the revealer); the threat raise reaches each player. Source: `mec113.saga-staging-rules`, `mec113.peril`. Status: span-settled.
  - 145022:A:2, 145025:A:2 and 145067:A:3 timing. Heard after the enemy is in the player's play area (so "it makes an immediate attack" has an engaged attacker) and at the start of Army of the Dead's attack, "you" the defending player; also when Trysting Place puts the enemy into play engaged (145019:A:2 searches with `to: engaged`, so the engagement is announced). Alternative: before engagement. Source: the Deep-dweller (19128) and Crowley precedents, `rr.defending-player`. Status: agent recommendation (no ruling found).
  - 145024:A:2 Ghosts of Men attacks from the staging area as it is revealed (Peril; dealt a shadow card, Phantom applies; the other players may defend by Sentinel) and is placed in the staging area after. Source: `faq.peril-attacks`, `faq.enemy-attacks-outside-combat`. Status: span-settled.
  - 145027:A:2 and 145028:A:2. The lasting effects apply to every character you control until the end of the round, one that enters later too, and "the current quest" is whichever stage is current while it lasts; progress on the active location is still placed. Source: `rr.lasting-effects`, `faq.lasting-effects`, `rr.active-location`. Status: span-settled.
  - 145026:A:2 Blood Runs Chill. Doomed 1 resolves first; then each player at 35 or higher puts 1 damage on each exhausted character they control, one exhausted by questing included; 145026:A:3: "your threat" is the defending player's and there is no defending character when undefended. Status: literal.
  - 145063:B:1 and B:2. Each player decides once; yes is +1 resource on each hero they control and one +3 threat raise. At the end of the planning phase every hand is discarded entirely, then the quest advances to stage 2A. Status: literal.
  - 145064:B:3 "cannot play cards this phase" bars playing a card from hand until the end of the phase for that seat; abilities of cards in play and put-into-play effects still work. Source: `rr.play-and-put-into-play`. Status: span-settled.
  - 145017:A:2, 145019:A:2 and 145064:A:1 add cards, not reveal them (no When Revealed, Peril or Surge), each player in order from the first player; a search that finds nothing adds nothing and a Travel search still lets the players travel; 145064:A:1 searches the deck only (not the discard pile) for a different title each. Source: `rr.search`, `rr.staging`. Status: span-settled.
  - 145018:A:1 Dark Door bars progress on every location in the staging area (itself included, not the active location); 145019:A:1 Trysting Place makes every enemy in the staging area unable to take damage; both only while the card is in the staging area. 145021:A:1: +1 Threat for each living player at 35 or higher, counted live. 145067:A:2: X is twice the living players. Status: literal.
  - 145065:A:1. The Stone and Army join the staging area from the set aside cards (not revealed), then each player in order may raise their threat by 3 to detach their copy, offered only with a copy on the dial. Status: literal.

## The Siege of Gondor

Scenario 14 (`mec113.saga-staging-rules`, `mec113.peril`, `mec113.archery`, `mec113.army-of-the-dead`, `mec113.multiplayer-aragorn`; records `src/effects/authored/return-of-the-king/the-siege-of-gondor.ts`, campaign card 145070 in `the-return-of-the-king.ts`; the heal cap in `src/rules/families/dreamchaser/heal-cap.ts`, the `highestCost` pick filter in `families/costs/pick.ts`). Great Corsair Ship and The Corsair Fleet are placed in the staging area by the scenario's staging pile (no record places them); the Army of the Dead objective ally is in play only when the campaign pool holds it, under the first player. Every reading below is applied as written; the ones marked agent recommendation (no ruling found) are not user-confirmed. Sources: [A] https://visionofthepalantir.com/2024/02/05/the-siege-of-gondor/, [B] https://theroadlotr.wordpress.com/2019/04/23/the-siege-of-gondor-playthrough/, [C] https://hallofbeorn.com/LotR/Details/The-Siege-of-Gondor-Campaign-TRotK (setup and resolution text, no rulings), [D] https://hallofgondor.com/rulings/ (nothing on these cards), [E] the FFG FAQ v1.9 (no entry for this box); searches of BoardGameGeek, the lotrlcg subreddit and the Hall of Beorn card pages for Fell Folk of Umbar, Raiding the Coasts and The Corsair Fleet found no ruling.

- **Readings.**
  - 145069:B:2 (stage 1B). Progress goes on past 20 while Great Corsair Ship is in play and the stage is not defeated then (`Restrict advance`); the moment the Ship leaves play (explored, so in the victory display) with 20 or more progress on the stage, the stage is defeated and the players win (advancing past the only stage is the win, `rr.winning-the-game`; no `WinGame` record). Alternative: the stage is checked only when more progress is placed after the Ship is gone. Source: `faq.progress-without-advancing`, `rr.quest-advancement` ("as soon as"), [B] (plays 23 of 20 with the Ship active and wins on exploring it), [A]. Status: span-settled.
  - 145069:B:1. Ship locations are immune to player card effects only in the staging area (the Ship location that becomes active is not), and one that enters the staging area later is immune too (a quest card's constant). Source: the card, `rr.lasting-effects`. Status: literal.
  - 145069:A:1 (Setup). Each player, the first player first, searches the encounter deck, not its discard pile (`rr.search`), for a Ship location of any title and adds it to the staging area; nothing found adds nothing; the deck is then shuffled. Alternative: also the discard pile, or different titles. Source: the line, 145064:A:1 (the deck-only form). Status: literal.
  - 145071:A:1 (The Corsair Fleet). At the beginning of the staging step it gets 1 resource, then (`rr.then`) each player in turn, the first player first, chooses either to reveal an encounter card (alone, in a staging of their own: `mec113.saga-staging-rules`, `mec113.peril`) or to assign X damage, X the resources on the Fleet after that one, among the characters they control (the Army of the Dead and Aragorn included). An option that cannot change the game is not offered (`rr.effects`): a player with no character has only the reveal. Each player chooses and resolves in turn (143067:A:1 and 143063:A:2 do). Alternative: all choose first, or the first player assigns for everyone. Source: `rr.then`, `rr.effects`, `mec113.saga-staging-rules`, [A], [B]. Status: span-settled for the option rule and X; the sequential order is agent recommendation (no ruling found; [A], [B] and [D] do not speak to it).
  - 145030:A:1. Great Corsair Ship's Threat X is the resources on The Corsair Fleet, read live (it grows each round while the Ship is in the staging area); 145042:A:2 reads the same number. The data prints no Threat for it, so the record adds X to 0. Source: the scan, [A]. Status: literal.
  - 145030:A:2. The players cannot travel to Great Corsair Ship unless 3 or more Ship locations (location type, trait Ship: the Umbar War Galley and Corsair Dromon) are in the victory display, any owner. Source: the line, 12092:B:3 and 18068:B:1 (the same form). Status: literal.
  - 145030:A:3 (Travel). Each player in turn, the first player first, searches the encounter deck and discard pile for a Corsair enemy and adds it to the staging area; "one of those enemies must be Corsair Captain, if able": the first search must take a Captain while one is in the deck or discard pile and the later searches are free (`SearchMove` `require` binds until the effect has taken the code once). Alternative: every search must take a Captain while any remain, or only the last. Source: the line; [A] confirms only the "if able" wording. Status: agent recommendation (no ruling found; no source speaks to two copies).
  - 145031:A:2 and 145032:A:2 (Travel). The discard pile is shuffled into the encounter deck and cards are discarded one at a time until a Corsair enemy is discarded, which is added to the staging area; with no Corsair enemy left the whole deck is discarded, nothing is added and the travel still happens (the deck is reset at the next quest phase, `rr.running-out-of-cards`). Source: the lines, 19018:A:2 and the-ghost-of-framsburg 191, [B] (the deck ran out and was reset). Status: span-settled; the edge with no Corsair left is agent recommendation (no ruling found; agent recommendation).
  - 145031:A:1 and 145032:A:1. While the Galley or the Dromon is the active location every Corsair enemy in play (staging or engaged, one that arrives later too) gets -10 engagement cost or gains Archery 1, which deals damage at the beginning of each combat phase for each such enemy, the staging area's included (`mec113.archery`). ## Mount Doom

Scenario 18, the saga's last (`mec113.fortitude-tests`, `mec113.player-actions-during-fortitude-tests`, `mec113.dire`, `mec113.the-one-ring`, `mec113.setting-up-the-game`, `mec113.saga-staging-rules`, `mec113.setup-mount-doom`, `rr.then`, `rr.active-location`, `rr.travel`, `rr.first-player`, `rr.in-player-order`, `rr.the-word-choose`, `rr.running-out-of-cards`, `rr.winning-the-game`, `faq.lasting-effects`, `faq.additional-attacks`, `faq.progress-without-advancing`; records `src/effects/authored/return-of-the-king/mount-doom.ts` and `mount-doom/{shared,locations,enemies,stages}.ts`, campaign card 146079 in `the-return-of-the-king.ts`; the family `mount-doom` in `src/catalog/families/mount-doom.ts` and `src/rules/families/mount-doom/`, with verbs in the encounter and campaign families). Sources: VotP is https://visionofthepalantir.com/2025/09/15/mount-doom/, [BGG1] is https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3556685/mount-doom-questions, [BGG2] is https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3591157/lotr-saga-questions, [FFG] is https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/287588-mount-doom-strategy-advice/page/2/ and [ENG] is https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/112007-engages-the-first-player. - **The end.** The players win when stage 4B's Fortitude test is passed after Mount Doom is explored (`WinGame`: the campaign card's Resolution runs and the saga is over); they lose the game when it is failed (`LoseGame`, "The Ring-bearer claimed The One Ring": nothing is written), at threat 99 under Dire, when The One Ring leaves play, and when the campaign card's last token comes off (146079:A:2). A loss writes nothing in any case. The Resolution (146079:B:1 to B:3) asks the first player to destroy The One Ring (the players win the campaign, "The One Ring is destroyed. The players win the campaign.") or keep it (they lose the campaign, "The One Ring is kept. The players lose the campaign."): the Resolution screen words each option above Confirm (the keep option in the danger tone), and the path and the Resolution word the end from the log. After the Ring is kept the settled Resolution is the scene of an end, calm, and says once, over Confirm, "Confirming records the end: The One Ring is kept and the players lose the campaign." Either way the campaign is finished and the path offers no next part. Source: the card. Status: literal.
- **The Fortitude test (`mec113.fortitude-tests`).** A frame like the Hide test's (`FortitudeTest`, window `fortitudeCommit`, intent `CommitFortitude`; `src/rules/families/mount-doom/fortitude.ts`): the tester exhausts any number of ready heroes to commit them (heroes only; none of them is asked when no hero can commit), then discards the top X cards of the encounter deck, X the tens digit of their own threat dial (0 below 10), not revealed; the Sauron's Eye icons the cards print (the catalog's `keywords.eyeIcons`, read from the ledger) and the icons a card adds are counted; an action window follows the count; then the Willpower of the committed heroes still in play is compared: strictly greater passes, equal or less fails (so X of 0 with no hero committed fails, with a hero it passes). A failed test is heard (`fortitudeFailed`, `lastFortitudeFailed`, the round's `fortitudeFailures`) before the card that made it goes on. Several players' tests inside one effect are played in player order, each test with its consequence before the next (not forced by the card; [BGG2] says the order is free). Source: `mec113.fortitude-tests`, `mec113.player-actions-during-fortitude-tests`. Status: span-settled; researched for "no window before the commit" ([FFG], [BGG1] for a tie failing in VotP).
- **A reshuffle in a test (`mec113.fortitude-tests`).** When the encounter deck is empty during a test the discard pile, the test's own discards included, is shuffled into it, so a card may be discarded twice in one test and its icons are counted at each discard. The deck emptied by the test's last discard is refilled at once, so the reveal a failed test makes (Orc Captain) has a card. Alternative: the test's discards stay out of the shuffle, or count once. Source: the span says only to shuffle the discard pile into the deck; no ruling found (VotP, BGG, the FFG forum archive, the card pages). Status: confirmed for the reshuffle (`mec113.fortitude-tests`); agent recommendation (no ruling found) for counting a card again.
- **Gollum (146049:A:2, A:3).** The unprefixed line is a passive: he engages the first player when he is added to the staging area, when the first player token passes (the same as 144075) and at once whenever an effect returns him to the staging area (`returnedToStaging`; Orc Captain's shadow). Alternative: he engages only on being added and on the token pass, and a returned Gollum stays in the staging area. Source: [ENG] ("unprefixed text is a passive ability; the enemy follows the first player token"; community), `mec112.gollum-smeagol` ("already engaged"). Status: researched. The Forced of A:3 is heard once every card of the test is discarded and before the icons are counted: he comes into play engaged with the first player (an engaged-by-effect enemy, so engagement Forced effects are heard), then the first player, who chooses as the controller of an encounter effect, chooses an encounter card discarded for the test (Gollum himself is no longer in the discard pile) and its icons are not counted. The asterisk is emphasis only. Alternative: the failing or testing player chooses. Source: `rr.the-word-choose`, `rr.first-player`, [BGG1] ("the asterisk is just emphasis").
