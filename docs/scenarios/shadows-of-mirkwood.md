# Shadows of Mirkwood

The rulings, readings and table pieces of Shadows of Mirkwood, scenario by scenario. Product rules, the Domain and the Core round are in `docs/brief.md`; the authority order is in `.claude/rules/authority.md`.

The first cycle (2011): The Hunt for Gollum, Conflict at the Carrock, A Journey to Rhosgobel, The Hills of Emyn Muil, The Dead Marshes and Return to Mirkwood, each an Adventure Pack of the cycle (pack codes `HfG`, `CatC`, `JtR`, `HoEM`, `TDM`, `RtM`; the Core Set's encounter sets they reuse are built through the Revised Core Set's). The cycle is `kind: "cycle"` (`data/campaigns/shadows-of-mirkwood.json`): no campaign card, no booklet, no Campaign Mode, so every game of it is a standalone game (Standalone play, above). The user chose all six scenarios on Normal, Easy and Nightmare (user decision, 2026-11-16). The app lists the cycle as one fold on the Scenarios tab, named "Shadows of Mirkwood", with its scenarios in pack order, once one of them is playable (`src/campaign/playable.ts`); the scenarios not yet playable stay in Coming later, captioned "Coming soon · Shadows of Mirkwood · n", and the cycle is one locked tile captioned "Cycle · 6 scenarios · Coming soon" until the first is. It never appears on the Campaigns tab.

- **Data.** Each scenario's file has no `campaignCard`, and a `nightmare` block for decks 9004 to 9009 (`scripts/data/nightmare-defs.ts`). The rulesheets of A Journey to Rhosgobel, The Hills of Emyn Muil, The Dead Marshes and Return to Mirkwood are pinned as rules docs with `sotm.*` spans (Data sources). The app opens no booklet for the cycle: the four rulesheets are separate one-scenario files, and the reader opens one stored book per campaign (`src/screens/booklets/books.ts`), so the briefing's booklet link and the table's Booklet row are absent (`bookletOf` returns none for a cycle).
- **Harness.** A cycle scenario's game is `standaloneGame` (`test/harness/game-kit.ts`: the world, the seats, the start and a golden, with no checklist, log or earlier part); its golden test is `goldenSuite` with `standalone: true` (the suite checks the cycle position, that no campaign card is dealt and the scenario is playable, that a win is scored, settles with nothing earned and is recorded as a standalone run, and that a loss ends with its reason and is recorded); `src/campaign/scenarios/<id>/standalone.test.ts` holds only what is the scenario's own (its Nightmare deck); its fixtures are one open standalone game each (`kind: "standalone"` in `src/campaign/fixtures/registry.ts`, found by `standaloneWonLost` in `scripts/e2e/seed-kit.ts`); a stage test that needs the real cards deals the Spirit starter deck with `coreSpiritGame` (`test/harness/core-spirit-game.ts`); `pnpm scenario new <id>` writes all of them.
- **Readings.** Each scenario adds a `###` subsection below for what its cards and rulesheet leave open, with its sources (`.claude/rules/authority.md`, Open points).

## The Hunt for Gollum

Shadows of Mirkwood part 1 (pack `HfG`), standalone only. Every printed line of every dealt card, the Nightmare deck's included, has a record (`src/effects/authored/shadows-of-mirkwood/the-hunt-for-gollum.ts`, `src/effects/authored/nightmare/the-hunt-for-gollum.ts`). Journey Along the Anduin's and Sauron's Reach's cards the scenario deals are the Core Set's. The Signs of Gollum are the four Clue objectives; a claimed one is a card attached to a hero and an enemy that "has a Clue attached" or "a Clue attached to a Mordor enemy" is, in the engine, the enemy attached to the Clue as its guard (`rr.guarded`), so the table shows no zone or counter beyond the cards. The MEC02 rulesheet (https://hallofbeorn-resources.s3.amazonaws.com/Images/LotR/Rules/MEC02.pdf) and `sotm.reset-the-quest-deck` give the rules of the Signs and of the reset.

- Signs of Gollum claim (02014:A:2). Response to the players questing successfully, offered once the quest is known successful and before the stage that the progress completed can reset the quest deck: a claim on the quest that completes stage 2 keeps stage 3's Clue condition met (the reset waits for the open Response window, `rules/families/quest/reset.ts`). Any player may claim it: the first player names the hero, any hero committed to that quest, any player's, and the card's controller is that hero's controller. Free of encounters is required (`rr.guarded`). The Forced return to the top of the encounter deck covers the hero damaged, destroyed or leaving play for any reason (MEC02 rulesheet; the rulesheet is the source for this line); a Signs that comes back is Guarded again when revealed (https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/55848-hunt-for-gollum-questions); a Response needs its card in play when the window opens (FFG staff, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/223378-hunt-for-gollum-timing-question-hunt-begins-signs-of-gollum). The first player is the one who names the hero: agent recommendation (no source says who).
- The Hunt Begins and A New Terror Abroad (02011:B:1, 02012:B:1): the cards looked at but the revealed one are discarded before the card is revealed, so a revealed Signs' Guarded takes the next card of the deck beneath, not one of the discarded (agent recommendation; the line prints reveal first).
- On the Trail (02013:B:1): a player who controls no hero with a Clue attached cannot commit characters, the other players commit freely (https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/81623-on-the-trail-question); characters already committed stay so when a Clue is lost during the phase (https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/223378-hunt-for-gollum-timing-question-hunt-begins-signs-of-gollum). "If there are ever no heroes with Clue objectives attached in play" is checked at every moment no effect is resolving, stage 3 just revealed included (https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/55848-hunt-for-gollum-questions, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/183397-hunt-for-gollum-questions); the quest deck then returns to stage 2B as `sotm.reset-the-quest-deck` says: the progress on the quest is removed, the progress on the active location stays, no Setup or When Revealed resolves again. A Clue dealt as a shadow card is not attached to a hero and does not count (https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/53347-hunters-of-mordor).
- The Old Ford (02015): X is the number of ally cards in play, every player's, objective allies excluded; the shadow discards the allies with a printed cost lower than the number of Riverland locations in play (staging area and active location, the Old Ford itself counted); a card with no printed cost or an X cost has printed cost 0 (agent recommendation, `rr.printed`).
- The East Bank and The West Bank (02018:A:1, 02019:A:1): the extra cost is paid under the ordinary resource match (`rr.resource-match`) and only for a card played from the hand. West Bank shadow (02019:A:2): the enemy's "base Attack" is its printed Attack, which is added once more for this attack; other modifiers, such as the Hunters from Mordor's +2 for each Clue, are not doubled (agent recommendation; no source found).
- River Ninglor (02017:A:1): "River Ninglor" in its own text is this card: with another copy in play (the staging area), the progress comes off the copy that is the active location (agent recommendation; a card's title in its own text names itself).
- Hunters from Mordor (02021:A:1): every Clue card in play counts, whether in the staging area, guarding or guarded, attached to a hero or attached to a Mordor enemy (https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/183397-hunt-for-gollum-questions, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/53347-hunters-of-mordor). Shadow: 1 damage (3 if undefended) to every hero with a Clue attached, any player's.
- False Lead (02022:A:1): the first player picks any card with the printed Clue trait in play (staging area, guarded or not, or attached to a hero) and shuffles it into the encounter deck; the cards guarding it stay in the staging area; Surge when no Clue card is in play when it is revealed, read before the shuffle (https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/134244-does-false-lead-hunt-for-gollum-gain-surge-if-theres-only-one-clue-in-play, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/51188-false-lead-treachery-card).
- Old Wives' Tales (02024:A:1): a hero with no resource to discard exhausts; an exhausted hero stays exhausted.
- Nightmare setup card (900401:A:2): the first player chooses both the Clue and the Mordor enemy (`rr.first-player`); the Clue is "unclaimed" (in the staging area, not attached to a hero) and not already guarded by a Mordor enemy; the cards that guarded it are detached and stay in the staging area; a Mordor enemy engaged with a player stays engaged (agent recommendation: the card never says to return it, https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/09/25/nightmare-hunt-for-gollum/ does not say); with no Mordor enemy in play (or none free to guard) the effect does nothing. Four Clue cards guarded by Mordor enemies lose the game (900401:A:3).
- Ravenous Hawk (900402:A:1): it acts when any card effect looks at cards of the encounter deck and it is among them (stage 1B and 2B included); it comes out of the cards looked at and into play engaged with that player, and the effect goes on with the remaining cards, so stage 1B with the Hawk among 3 reveals 1 of the other 2 and discards the other (https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/09/25/nightmare-hunt-for-gollum/; the count of the remaining cards is an agent recommendation).
- Hunting Warg (900403:A:1): "entered play this round" is noted when it enters play; at the beginning of the refresh phase, if it is still in play, it adds 1 Hunters from Mordor from the encounter deck or discard pile to the staging area, and the encounter deck is shuffled either way.
- Watched Path (900404:A:1): the player who controls no Clue card (one attached to a hero he controls) cannot draw through card effects; the resource phase's and the round's draws are the framework's, not card effects (agent recommendation).
- Dark Pools (900406:A:2): each player discards the ally with the highest printed cost among the allies he controls; the owner chooses among a tie; with no ally, nothing.
- The Enemy's Trail (900407): the first player (the defending player as a shadow card) chooses the Mordor enemy; the Clue comes from the encounter discard pile and is attached with the enemy's engagement kept; Surge when none was attached, as the Nightmare setup card's.
- Restless Hunters (900408:A:2, A:3): the first player takes a Clue off a hero and attaches it to a Mordor enemy in play, if able (the hero loses it; the reset of On the Trail follows when it was the last). Shadow: the damage of an undefended attack goes on a hero the defending player controls with a Clue attached, if he has one (the player names it among those); a defended attack still damages its defender (agent recommendation: `rr.step-6-4-3` is the only text, no source found).
- The Trail Goes Cold (900409): attached to the active location (Limit 1 per location: a location that already has one is not attached to, so Trail Goes Cold gains surge) with its progress removed; the attached location contributes its Threat to the staging area as the active location (the `activeThreat` flag, as Flooded Ruins), https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/09/25/nightmare-hunt-for-gollum/.

## Conflict at the Carrock

Shadows of Mirkwood part 2 (pack `CatC`), standalone only. Every printed line of every dealt card, the Nightmare deck's included, has a record (`src/effects/authored/shadows-of-mirkwood/conflict-at-the-carrock.ts`, `src/effects/authored/nightmare/conflict-at-the-carrock.ts`). Journey Along the Anduin's and Wilderlands' cards the scenario deals are the Core Set's. The rulesheet MEC03 (https://hallofbeorn-resources.s3.amazonaws.com/Images/LotR/Rules/MEC03.pdf) sets what Setup does: the four unique Trolls and four "Sacked!" are set aside (out of play, the build's piles), one "Sacked!" per player is shuffled back (the deck begins with 1 more "Sacked!" than the number of players), and The Carrock is staged. Grimbeorn the Old is an objective ally: revealed in the quest phase he enters the staging area; when the players claim him (his text) he becomes an ally under the first player's control, who may use him as any ally he controls; he goes to the encounter discard pile if he leaves play. The Carrock's "Immune to player card effects" keyword is the rulesheet's: players cannot select it as the target of any card effect and it ignores a player card that directly interacts with it.

- Grimbeorn's Quest (02035:B:1): the stage has 7 quest points, so the 7th progress token defeats it, and The Carrock becomes the active location when that happens; the previous active location is discarded from play (https://hallofbeorn.com/LotR/Details/Grimbeorn's-Quest-CatC, https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/01/25/conflict-at-the-carrock/).
- Against the Trolls (02036:B:2): any Troll enemy in play, in the staging area or engaged, holds the stage; defeating it wins the game on Normal and Easy, where it is the last stage (the Nightmare deck's stage 3 follows it on Nightmare).
- Grimbeorn the Old (02037:A:2, A:3): the Leadership resources are tokens on him and stay there; with 8 or more he joins the first player (https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/74103-grimbeorn-the-old, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/100094-grimbeorn-the-old-conflict-at-the-carrock). He joins once and stays with that player when the first player token moves on ("joins the first player" is one change of control, and a card that follows the token says "the first player gains control", as Wilyador does; researched, community: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/77580-grimbeorn-vs-arwen-and-wilyador, where one poster reports an FFG clarification that he stays "for the rest of the game", and https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/192811-clarification-on-objective-allies-moving-with-first-player-icon; no official ruling found). The Action is any player's, in the staging area or under control, with no limit, paid from the acting player's own heroes' Leadership resources; it does not exhaust him (the same threads; `rr.action`). The table creates no tracker or zone for the scenario: the tokens lie on his card like any resource tokens (02037:A:2 sets the 8), and a "Sacked!" is an attachment under its hero.
- "After defeating X" Responses (02038 to 02041 A:2, 900501:A:5): "defeating" is destroyed by damage; a "Sacked!" "in play" is one attached to a hero (the card in the deck or discard pile is out of play) and goes to the encounter discard pile (https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/260817-conflict-at-the-carrock-trolls). "You" is the player the defeated enemy was engaged with, whoever is the first player (`answeredBy`; agent recommendation: no source says who "you" is).
- Muck Adder (02042:A:1): the character is discarded only when damage was actually placed on it, so canceled or prevented damage does not trigger it; any character, hero or ally, an attack's damage or a shadow-boosted attack's (https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/69996-frodos-muck-adder-resolved, Caleb, FFG). It is discarded after the attack, as the other cards that hear "an attack damages a character" resolve. A discarded hero goes to its owner's discard pile like a destroyed one.
- Oak-wood Grove (02046:A:1): while it is the active location every hero's pool counts as Leadership for what spends or matches a Leadership resource, Grimbeorn's Action and Leadership cards' costs alike (https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/100094-grimbeorn-the-old-conflict-at-the-carrock for Grimbeorn; the extension to Leadership cards' costs is the card's own text, agent recommendation: no ruling). It is the heroes' `leadershipIcon` flag, so the hero also counts as having a Leadership icon only for paying.
- A Frightened Beast (02047:A:1): the total Threat of the staging area is read when the effect resolves, the staging modifiers included, and neither the active location's Threat (`rr.active-location`: "The active location does not contribute its threat strength to the total threat strength in the staging area") nor the engaged enemies'; each player in turn from the first player may discard 1 Creature ally he controls to cancel the effect for everyone, the first who does ends the offers (agent recommendation for the cancel offers: no official ruling, https://visionofthepalantir.com/2022/01/30/encounter-set-review-conflict-at-the-carrock/, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/51871-aaaggghhhh-conflict-at-the-carrock-session-report).
- Sacked! (02048): the first player chooses the hero, among the heroes he controls with no "Sacked!" attached; with none the card is discarded (agent recommendation: a community answer, no official ruling). "(Cannot be canceled.)" is its When Revealed. The attached hero cannot attack, defend, commit to a quest, collect resources, nor initiate its own Action and Response abilities; its constant and Forced abilities still work (https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/302355-cannot-trigger-effects-sacked). As a shadow card with a Troll attacker its When Revealed effect attaches it to a hero of the first player's choice, not the defender's; with no Troll attacking it does nothing (https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/74599-muck-adder), and the shadow effect can be canceled as a whole (https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/51029-sacked-faq-required-spoiler-for-catc; agent recommendation: community answers, no official ruling).
- Roasted Slowly (02049:A:1): every hero of every player with a "Sacked!" attached is destroyed (`Destroy`: it lies in its owner's discard pile and is not "discarded", `rr.destroy`); it is shuffled back into the encounter deck even when none was.
- Nightmare setup card (900501:A:2 to A:4): Rob and Bob is set aside by the setup card's Setup (the fifth unique Troll; Louis, Morris, Rupert and Stuart are the Nightmare versions the build sets aside) and joins the staging area at stage 3A; stage 2B's own search finds none, the setup card's Forced effect places the four Nightmare Trolls when stage 2B is revealed (https://visionofthepalantir.com/2020/07/11/nightmare-conflict-at-the-carrock). "Progress placed on the current quest this phase" is progress on the quest card itself in the quest phase (the overflow of an explored active location included, progress an active location took excluded); the current quest is the main quest stage (agent recommendation: a side quest "can actually target side-quests as well", the same blog, would make it the current quest only when chosen, and this scenario deals no encounter side quest). Its progress bypasses the active location (`AddStageProgress`).
- Rob and Bob (900503:A:1): after its first attack of the round, with two or more players, it leaves its engagement, engages the player after the defending player in turn order (`rr.next-player`), and attacks them with a new shadow card (the old one discarded); it makes no third attack (agent recommendation: the blog, https://visionofthepalantir.com/2020/07/11/nightmare-conflict-at-the-carrock, says "a second attack each round", and no source says it attacks again in the next player's step).
- Hives and Hives (900509:A:1): once for each placement of progress on it, whichever player's, and not when the placement explores it and it leaves play (a Forced effect needs its card in play when it triggers, `rr.forced`; agent recommendation: no source found); the damage goes on every hero and ally of every player.
- Bee Pastures (02045:A:1): the Response is the first player's after the players travel to Bee Pastures itself; Grimbeorn is searched for in the encounter deck and discard pile and added to the staging area, and the encounter deck is shuffled whether or not he was found (researched, community: a search of a deck always ends with a shuffle unless a card says otherwise, the official clarification quoted at https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/261299-shuffle-back-into/; `rr.then` does not apply, the search itself resolves).
- Time for Supper (900510:A:1): the first player chooses the hero in any player's discard pile; with none, the part before "then" does not resolve (`rr.then`), so nothing is healed and, as no damage was removed from any Troll, the card gains Surge. Surge is decided before the healing (no Troll in play, or none damaged): https://visionofthepalantir.com/2020/07/11/nightmare-conflict-at-the-carrock.
- Grimbeorn's Folly (900511, the Nightmare deck's quest card, stage 3 after Against the Trolls): no quest points, so the quest stops there; 3A adds Rob and Bob and each player's non-unique Troll to the staging area, 3B wins the game when Rob and Bob is defeated, destroyed by damage (https://www.hallofbeorn.com/LotR/Details/Grimbeorn's-Folly-CatCN, https://visionofthepalantir.com/2020/07/11/nightmare-conflict-at-the-carrock). Against the Trolls' own Troll restriction still holds the stage 2.

## A Journey to Rhosgobel

Shadows of Mirkwood part 3 (pack `JtR`), standalone only. Every printed line of every dealt card, the Nightmare deck's included, has a record (`src/effects/authored/shadows-of-mirkwood/a-journey-to-rhosgobel.ts`, `src/effects/authored/nightmare/a-journey-to-rhosgobel.ts`). The rulesheet (the Journey to Rhosgobel rules, `sotm.setup-a-journey-to-rhosgobel`, `sotm.ally-objective-wilyador`, `sotm.removed-from-game`) sets what Setup does: Wilyador and Rhosgobel are in the staging area (the build's piles), Wilyador gets 2 damage tokens and the encounter deck is shuffled (02060:A:1); the first player takes control of Wilyador and uses him as an ally, the token passes to the new first player at the end of each round and so does the control; players cannot play or move attachment cards onto him; if he leaves play, or the player who controls him is eliminated, the players lose; a card "removed from the game" is set aside and ignored, never in the discard pile. The quest has three stages; stage 3 prints 0 quest points and no progress is ever placed on it: the quest does not advance it as the phase that reveals it ends, so its When Revealed heals Wilyador and wins or loses the game (`advanceReady`, `src/rules/quest/quest.ts`).

- Table: no record of this scenario creates a tracker, a zone or a held card (the Table step, `docs/agents/agent-brief.md`, Scenario procedure). Wilyador is an ally in the first player's play area with his damage on his card; a claimed Athelas and the Condition attachments (Shot from the Sky, Noxious Supplies) lie under their host; the cards Radagast's Request removes from the game are set aside and shown nowhere.
- Wilyador's control (02064:A:1): he leaves the staging area at Setup and joins the first player's play area, faceup and ready, then follows the first player token each round (`ControlledBy` with `intoPlay` and `follows`; `sotm.ally-objective-wilyador`, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/239006-wilador-in-staging-area, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/54743-rhosgobel-rules-questions-glorfindelwilyadorathelasetc; researched, community). Grimbeorn the Old's `intoPlay` stays a one-time change. "No attachments" bars player attachments only; Noxious Supplies and Shot from the Sky, whose printed text names him or an Eagle, may attach to him (https://visionofthepalantir.com/2020/05/20/nightmare-a-journey-to-rhosgobel/; agent recommendation: the text that names him wins over the general rule).
- Objective allies and "return to its owner's hand" (02064:A:3; 09016:A:4, 10096:A:4, 11077, 11079; `rr.ownership-and-control`, `sotm.ally-objective-wilyador`, `mec108.objective-allies`). The scenario owns every encounter card, so an objective ally has no player owner and no hand to return to. A player card that returns one to "its owner's hand" (Meneldor's Flight, Born Aloft, `ReturnToHand`, `PutOnDeck`) takes it out of play into the encounter discard pile, the area the scenario owns, and "if X leaves play, the players lose the game" is checked after every action on whatever way it left (`loseWhenLeftPlay`); a card put out of the game by a stage's own text (the Ring-bearer at stage 2A of Breaking of the Fellowship, whose One Ring text is blank) loses nothing. Control changes (`giveControl`) never make a player the owner of an encounter card. Alternative: the effect does nothing to a card with no owner's hand. Source: no ruling found (searched the FAQ, Hall of Beorn, BoardGameGeek, the FFG forum archive; https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/747048/meneldors-flight, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/56437-vassal-of-the-windlord-born-aloft-timing/ and https://alongextendedparty.com/semi-official-errata-faq-1-10/ say only that the card returns to its owner's hand); the placement follows `rr.ownership-and-control`'s "equivalent out of play area". Status: recommended (agent recommendation, not user-confirmed).
- Wilyador's damage (02064:A:2, A:3): the 2 damage at the end of each round is dealt like any damage (Honour Guard may cancel it, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/221924-wilyador-and-honour-guard, community). "Cannot be healed of more than 5 wounds by a single effect" caps each heal effect at 5 (`healCapPerEffect`); his leaving play loses the game however he leaves (`lossOnLeavingPlay`, the destroyed, discarded and eliminated-controller routes).
- Radagast's Request (02061:B:1): the first player places X damage tokens (X is the number of players) on Wilyador as the cost, before the look; the tokens are placed, not dealt, so no shield or cancel applies, and the first player may place them although they would destroy him (https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/63053-during-radagasts-request, community). The 3 cards are looked at, 1 revealed and the other 2 discarded, as The Hunt Begins does.
- Radagast's Request (02061:B:2): "heals" means at least 1 damage was removed (a heal of 0 from a cap or a prevented heal is not a heal; agent recommendation, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/221924-wilyador-and-honour-guard); "that card" is the card whose effect healed, whatever its type, and it leaves the game from wherever it lies (an event, an ally or hero that leaves play and is not destroyed, an attachment such as Athelas, 09011, discarded as its cost: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/63053-during-radagasts-request, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/56741-rhosgobel-wilyador-healing-question; researched, community). A second copy may be played later. The Forced is a stage 2B ability: stage 3's heal is not affected, as 2B has left play.
- Return to Rhosgobel (02062:B:1): an Athelas objective counts when a hero carries it (claimed); one in the staging area or guarded does not, and the player attachment Athelas (09011) is not an objective (https://visionofthepalantir.com/2022/07/15/a-journey-to-rhosgobel-2/, community). The cap of 5 per effect is read per heal, one heal for each Athelas objective, so N objectives heal 5N in all (agent recommendation: the forum is split, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/52983-rules-questions-wilyador-and-return-to-rhosgobel, where Troymk1 reads 10 for two, and the literal reading of one effect would make a second Athelas pointless; the Visions of the Palantir review counts 20 healed for four). The heal counts as an Athelas heal for Noxious Supplies (`athelas`; https://visionofthepalantir.com/2020/05/20/nightmare-a-journey-to-rhosgobel/, community).
- Return to Rhosgobel (02062:B:2): after the heals, Wilyador with no damage on him wins the game, otherwise the players lose; a blocked heal (Rhosgobel in the staging area, Treetop Bird-eater) healed nothing. With no Athelas controlled and no damage on him the players win (https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/53109-lore-of-imladris-and-rhosgobels-3b-effect, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/58289-rhosgobel-vs-athelas; community, the card text settles it).
- Athelas (02063:A:1): Guarded triggers only when the card is revealed from the encounter deck (the official FAQ 1.8, quoted by https://visionofthepalantir.com/2022/07/15/a-journey-to-rhosgobel-2/ and https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/54743-rhosgobel-rules-questions-glorfindelwilyadorathelasetc): one added by Forest Grove or the Nightmare setup shuffle, which are searches, is not guarded; researched, the FAQ quoted by community.
- Athelas (02063:A:2): any player's Action; the acting player exhausts a ready hero he controls, and only when no encounter card guards it; the claimed objective is an attachment on that hero (Swarming Insects does not count the hero as "without any attachments", the same forum thread; researched, community).
- Rhosgobel (02065:A:2, A:3): while it is in the staging area Wilyador cannot be healed, by every effect (https://visionofthepalantir.com/2022/07/15/a-journey-to-rhosgobel-2/, community); it is not immune to player card effects, so progress may explore it without traveling. The players cannot travel to it while stage 1 is the current stage (a Travel line that bars the trip is also a constant, so one record owns it; the bar is on Rhosgobel itself, no other location).
- Forest Grove (02066:A:1): the Response is heard however Forest Grove is explored, as the active location or in the staging area; the encounter deck is shuffled whether or not an Athelas was found (the Carrock's Bee Pastures reading).
- Exhaustion, Swarming Insects, Festering Wounds (02067:A:1, 02068:A:1, 02069:A:1): "each character" is every hero and ally of every player and Wilyador, not enemies or locations; the damage is dealt. A claimed Athelas objective and every condition are attachments (agent recommendation, community only). Swarming Insects' shadow (02068:A:2) deals 3 more to the one character with more damage than each other character; a tie for the most deals none, and a character with no damage is never that character (agent recommendation).
- Mirkwood Flock, Black Forest Bats (02070:A:1, 02071:A:1): only Eagle characters or characters with ranged declare as attackers or defenders against them; the Eagle trait counts as printed (a card that lost it to Shot from the Sky does not), and ranged as the keyword is held now (Pitch-dark Thicket removes it). A ranged character of another player may attack them while they are engaged with someone else. Their shadow puts the damage of an undefended attack on Wilyador, whichever player controls him, and does nothing when he is not in play.
- Nightmare setup card (900601:A:2 to A:4): the Goblin Eagle-hunter joins the staging area at Setup. "The first Creature enemy revealed during each quest phase" is revealed from the encounter deck during the quest phase (not dealt as a shadow, not found by a search), once each quest phase, and only enemies with the Creature trait count; the Goblin Eagle-hunter is not one (https://visionofthepalantir.com/2020/05/20/nightmare-a-journey-to-rhosgobel/; researched, community). The Response is the first player's and optional; "an Athelas card placed in the discard pile" is the encounter discard pile, whatever it came from (the player attachment Athelas goes to its owner's pile and is not an encounter card); the ledger's Response ("you may", "Athelas card") is what the scan prints, which the blog words as a replacement (agent recommendation).
- Goblin Eagle-hunter (900602:A:2, A:3): the Forced works in the staging area and engaged alike; the shadow checks the damage on Wilyador when the effect resolves, 10 or less counting 10, and the damage is dealt.
- Treetop Bird-eater (900603:A:1, A:2): the characters of the player it is engaged with cannot be healed, Wilyador too while that player controls him, which stops stage 3's heal (https://visionofthepalantir.com/2020/05/20/nightmare-a-journey-to-rhosgobel/). At the beginning of the encounter phase it engages the player who controls Wilyador, leaving the player it was engaged with (the card says "engages"; agent recommendation), ignoring the engagement cost.
- Dark-wood Grove (900604:A:1, A:2): every card named Athelas in play counts, in the staging area, guarded or claimed, and the player attachment (the printed card wins over the blog's "objective the players control"); the shadow's additional attack is made once, with a new shadow card, when the defending player controls an Athelas card (a claimed objective or an Athelas attachment on a card he controls).
- Labyrinthine Woods (900605:A:1, A:2): every copy adds; with one player there is no next player, so the shadow does nothing (`rr.next-player`; agent recommendation for the whole sentence).
- Pitch-dark Thicket (900606:A:2): characters with ranged cannot be declared as attackers against the attacking enemy until the end of the combat phase.
- Shot from the Sky (900607:A:2): the first player chooses among every Eagle character in play with no Shot from the Sky on it, Wilyador included (agent recommendation: no exception is printed, the blog says it is easy to avoid with other Eagles); with none it is discarded. The host loses the Eagle trait and takes 1 damage, dealt, each time it readies (the refresh phase, a card effect), not when it enters play.
- Noxious Supplies (900608:A:2): it attaches to Wilyador despite "No attachments" (up to 3 copies); the next heal of him by a card effect other than Athelas heals 1 (a heal of 1 stays 1), and then one Noxious Supplies is discarded; with several copies the first is discarded each time (agent recommendation; https://visionofthepalantir.com/2020/05/20/nightmare-a-journey-to-rhosgobel/, community, says "can end up with all 3 on him"). Every heal by a card effect is reduced, whichever verb it uses: a heal among characters (The Long Defeat) heals Wilyador 1 for the whole effect, and Elrond's Response heal is a heal of 1 already (agent recommendation).

## The Hills of Emyn Muil

Shadows of Mirkwood part 4 (pack `HoEM`), standalone only. Every printed line of every dealt card, the Nightmare deck's included, has a record (`src/effects/authored/shadows-of-mirkwood/the-hills-of-emyn-muil.ts`, `src/effects/authored/nightmare/the-hills-of-emyn-muil.ts`). Rules: `sotm.setup-the-hills-of-emyn-muil`, `sotm.emyn-muil-victory-points`, `rr.victory`, `rr.blank`, `rr.step-6-4-3`, `rr.doomed-x`, `faq.lasting-effects`. No official ruling on this scenario's cards was found; the readings below rest on the printed lines, those spans, and the community pages named.

- Stage 1 (02082:B:1): "the first treachery card revealed during the quest phase" is the first one the encounter deck reveals in a quest phase; the surge is gained only when no location is in the staging area as it is revealed, and a later treachery is not "the first" either way. It surges every quest phase and stacks with a printed Surge, each instance revealing one more card (`rr.surge`; https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/190974-emyn-muil-newbie-questions, https://visionofthepalantir.com/2022/11/27/the-hills-of-emyn-muil-2/, community; agent recommendation for the first-or-first-while-empty choice, no source separates them).
- Stage 1 (02082:B:2): "in play" is the staging area and the active location; "collected" is the Victory points printed on the cards in the victory display now (`rr.victory`; `sotm.emyn-muil-victory-points`: both conditions must hold). Progress placed while the stage is held stays on it, and the stage is defeated as soon as both hold; claiming the last location in an action window before the quest resolves wins at once (https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/190974-emyn-muil-newbie-questions, community).
- Amon Lhaw (02084:A:2): the text box includes Traits (`rr.blank`, and this line has no "except for Traits" as Sunken Temple has): every attachment, player, encounter or location-attached, is blank and has no Traits while it is active. Text an attachment gains from another source stays; Thror's Key on Amon Lhaw blanks Amon Lhaw and stops the line (https://visionofthepalantir.com/2022/11/27/the-hills-of-emyn-muil-2/, community).
- The North Stair (02086:A:1): the top card of the encounter discard pile moves to the staging area and its When Revealed effects resolve for the first player. It is not revealed from the deck, so no Surge, Doomed or cancel of a card just revealed applies (FAQ 1.8, quoted by https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/89518-galloping-boulders-and-surge and https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/64822-the-north-stair/, community, clear and agreed). A treachery on top resolves and stays in the discard pile; an enemy or location stays in the staging area; an empty pile does nothing.
- The Highlands (02090:A:1): the card is revealed, so Surge and Doomed resolve (`rr.doomed-x` holds in any phase); a treachery resolves and is discarded (https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/190974-emyn-muil-newbie-questions, community).
- Rockslide (02092:A:2): "Remove defending character from combat. This attack is considered undefended." is one effect (`rr.step-6-4-3`): the defender stays exhausted, takes no damage, no longer counts as defending, and the damage goes to a hero the active player chooses. The engine's `ConsiderUndefended` is that effect (https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/300477-what-happens-when-a-shadow-effect-discards-the-defender, community).
- Slick Footing (02093:A:1): every location in play (the staging area and the active location) loses 1 progress; each player then discards from the top of his own deck as many cards as tokens were removed in all; nothing happens when none were removed (https://visionofthepalantir.com/2022/11/27/the-hills-of-emyn-muil-2/, community, one source; consistent with the printed "each player's deck for each ... removed").
- Tunneling Nameless Thing (900702:A:2): a standing state, not a Forced effect: while no location is active it returns to the staging area. The printed line adds no bar on engaging it, so none is written: an engagement with no active location (optional, or a check; the card's engagement cost is 50, which no player in the game has) is undone at once and loops nowhere (agent recommendation, orchestrator ruling; https://visionofthepalantir.com/2019/01/05/nightmare-the-hills-of-emyn-muil/, community, says only that clearing the active location returns it).
- Brambles and Thorns (900704:A:1): every copy in play hears each commitment; each character takes at most 1 damage a round from the copies together, the first to resolve dealing it (https://visionofthepalantir.com/2019/01/05/nightmare-the-hills-of-emyn-muil/, community). A damage a shield or cap prevents is not "dealt", so a later copy deals it (agent recommendation: the card says "dealt").
- A Hurrying Darkness (900706:A:1): each player chooses in turn, the first player first, and a revealed encounter card resolves before the next player chooses (agent recommendation for the sequencing; "not as a group", https://visionofthepalantir.com/2019/01/05/nightmare-the-hills-of-emyn-muil/, community).
- Blast of Savage Wind (900707:A:2): the +2 Threat reaches Emyn Muil locations that arrive later in the round, in the staging area or active (`faq.lasting-effects`, `rr.lasting-effects`).
- Hazardous Terrain (900708:A:3): "Then, deal another shadow card" is dealt whether or not a Hazard card was shuffled back, the shuffle counting as resolved with none to move (the same reading as Bee Pastures in Conflict at the Carrock; `rr.then` does not say whether shuffling nothing resolves; no source speaks to the shadow, and the When Revealed's own "whiff" is described by https://visionofthepalantir.com/2019/01/05/nightmare-the-hills-of-emyn-muil/, community; agent recommendation).
- The table (the Table step): no record of this scenario creates a tracker, a zone or a held card, so it adds no readout, dial or held-card drawing; the victory display and the stage's restriction (02082:B:2) are drawn as every scenario's are.

## The Dead Marshes

Shadows of Mirkwood part 5 (pack `DM`), standalone only. Every printed line of every dealt card, the Nightmare deck's included, has a record (`src/effects/authored/shadows-of-mirkwood/the-dead-marshes.ts`, `src/effects/authored/nightmare/the-dead-marshes.ts`); the escape test is the `escape` family (`src/catalog/families/escape.ts`, `src/rules/families/escape/`). Rules: `sotm.setup-the-dead-marshes`, `sotm.escape-test`, `sotm.escape-test-example`, `sotm.reset-the-quest-deck`, `rr.then`, `rr.forced`, `rr.running-out-of-cards`, `rr.destroy`, `rr.shadow-effects`, `faq.simultaneous-effects`, `faq.lasting-effects`. Hall of Beorn garbles the title of 900806 ("Lost Soul of L≤rien"); `data/overrides.json` names it as the scan and the ledger print it.

- The escape test (`sotm.escape-test`): a ready hero or ally of the testing player is exhausted to commit, and counts its Willpower (Attack for Through the Mist, Defense for Swarming Mosquitos; a character with 0 of it may commit). Order: commit, one action window, the cards are dealt, the totals are compared; no action window after the deal (the span; official designer answer, Caleb, 2013, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/85811-escape-tests/). A tie fails, so nothing committed always fails. Each card dealt counts its printed Escape (0 with none) and nothing else of it happens; all go to the encounter discard pile afterwards, and an empty deck is refilled from the discard pile without the cards already dealt (`rr.running-out-of-cards`; Caleb, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/248142-the-dead-marshes-sneak-attack).
- "A player makes" a test: only that player's characters commit. "The party" (02107:A:2): every player commits, the first player first, to one test with one action window, one card for each player and one total. "Each player makes" (02111:A:1, 900808:A:1): separate tests in player order, each failure applying to that player alone (community for the order, https://visionofthepalantir.com/2020/01/18/nightmare-the-dead-marshes/ ; `rr.first-player`).
- Gollum's end-of-phase test (02107:A:2) resolves after quest resolution, once the effects that end with the phase have ended (Caleb, 2017, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/248142-the-dead-marshes-sneak-attack; `faq.simultaneous-effects`, `faq.lasting-effects`); it is a Forced effect, so with Gollum shuffled back there is no test (`faq.triggering-forced-effects`). When stage 2 is defeated by the quest's own progress, stage 2B's test resolves before it (community, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/193596-the-dead-marshes-2b-escape-tests ; https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/248220-the-dead-marshes-escape-test).
- Stage 2B (02106:B:1, B:2): the first player chooses the player who tests; the cards dealt are the tokens on Gollum read as the test starts. Passing that test wins (only the test this stage's own effect made: a treachery's or Gollum's test passed meanwhile does not), failing resets the quest deck to stage 1B (`sotm.reset-the-quest-deck`: the stage goes back to the quest deck, stage 1 returns with no progress, no Setup or When Revealed again, progress on other cards stays). The stage is the last one, but the win waits for the test: the flag `holdsWin` keeps it the current stage, defeated, until its Forced effect ends in `WinGame` or `ReturnToStage` (`src/rules/quest/quest.ts`, `advanceStage`; the card says the players win when the test is passed, `rr.winning-the-game`). With Gollum out of play no test is made and the capture fails: the quest deck is reset (agent recommendation; the community plays it as a reset, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/55891-dead-marshes-questionerrata and https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/56205-dead-marshes-gollum-question, no ruling; the alternative lets the 8-token rule win the game).
- Tokens "on Gollum" while he is out of play (the deck or the discard pile): none are placed (the Mere of Dead Faces takes them in Nightmare); the other consequences on the same card still happen, joined by "and", not "then" (`rr.then`; agent recommendation, the forum threads above have no agreed answer). Gollum at 8 or more tokens is shuffled into the deck at once, tokens gone, and returns as a new card when revealed.
- Impassable Bog (02113:A:1): counts the other locations in the staging area; the Bog is placed after its When Revealed resolves (`rr.staging`), so it is not counted, nor is the active location (`rr.active-location`).
- A Wisp of Pale Sheen (02108:A:1): any player, in player order from the first player, may exhaust a ready Lore hero they control (printed sphere) to reduce the tokens to 1; at most one reduction, decided before the tokens go on. The reduction is offered whenever the tokens will land: Gollum in play, or on Nightmare the Mere of Dead Faces holding them instead (`900802:A:2`); with neither there is no effect to reduce (literal from the printed line and 900802:A:2; the order of offer and the one-reduction limit are agent recommendation, no ruling found).
- The Heart of the Marshes (02114:A:1): while it is the active location every card dealt counts 1 more Escape, a card with no printed value too, in every test (literal).
- Nightmare setup (900801:A:3): the Mere is added to the staging area after the quest card's Setup has searched for Gollum, then the deck is shuffled (agent recommendation for the order; `rr.setup`). 900801:A:4 hears Gollum placed in the discard pile after the cards of a test are all there.
- The Mere of Dead Faces (900802): X is the tokens on it, it cannot leave the staging area, takes the tokens meant for Gollum while he is out of play, and after Gollum enters play he gets 2 tokens, then all its tokens move to him; the move can send him back at 8 (`rr.then`; community agrees, https://visionofthepalantir.com/2020/01/18/nightmare-the-dead-marshes/).
- Stagnant Quagmire (900803:A:1): while in play, in the staging area or active, it adds its Escape value (2) to every test, once for each copy (community, https://visionofthepalantir.com/2020/01/18/nightmare-the-dead-marshes/: "it will add its Escape value to every Escape test"). Its Shadow test is made by the defending player; the defending character is exhausted by defending, so it cannot commit; failing discards it (a discard, not a destroy, `rr.destroy`).
- Things in the Pools (900804:A:1): every copy in play deals its own 1 damage to each character as it commits (community, same article); each resolves as the character commits, before the action window and the deal, and a character it destroys no longer counts (agent recommendation for the timing; `rr.forced`).
- Lost Soul of Lórien (900806:A:1): attaches to any player's hero with 1 or more Willpower, chosen by the first player, one copy to a hero, and stays in the encounter discard pile when none qualifies; attached, it is a Condition that sets the hero's Willpower to 0 (community, https://visionofthepalantir.com/2020/01/18/nightmare-the-dead-marshes/).
- Ill-fated Guard (900807:A:1): "engaged with a player" is any player; several Guards do not stack lower. Candles of Corpses (900808:A:1): the failing player chooses between discarding a hero they control (a discard, not a destroy) and raising their own threat by 5 (community for the order).
- Devilry of the Dark Land (900810:A:1): the topmost X Undead enemies of the encounter discard pile return to the staging area, not revealed (no When Revealed, no engagement); with none returned it gains surge, with fewer than X but one or more it does not (https://visionofthepalantir.com/2020/01/18/nightmare-the-dead-marshes/, community); each instance of surge reveals one more card.
- The table (the Table step): no record of this scenario creates a tracker, a zone or a held card, so it adds no readout, dial or held-card drawing; Gollum's tokens are ordinary resource tokens on a card in play, and the Mere draws its tokens as its Threat. The commit step of an escape test is a choice: the Choice tray names the cards to be dealt, the stat counted, the most that may commit and, for a party test, that every player commits (`src/play/words/scenarios/escape.ts`, `src/play/choice/escape-notes.ts`); the action window's note gives the stat committed so far and what the table adds to each card, and arms Continue while nobody is committed (`docs/design-system-table.md`, Choice tray).

## Return to Mirkwood

Shadows of Mirkwood part 6 (pack `RtM`), standalone only, no campaign card. Every printed line of every dealt card, the Nightmare deck's included, has a record (`src/effects/authored/shadows-of-mirkwood/return-to-mirkwood.ts`, `src/effects/authored/nightmare/return-to-mirkwood.ts`); the player guarding Gollum is the `guardian` family (`src/catalog/families/guardian.ts`, `src/rules/families/guardian/`). Rules: `sotm.guarding-gollum`, `sotm.setup-return-to-mirkwood`, `rr.first-player`, `rr.forced`, `rr.then`, `rr.cannot`, `rr.destroy`, `rr.active-location`, `rr.running-out-of-cards`, `faq.triggering-forced-effects`. The encounter deck is Return to Mirkwood, Spiders of Mirkwood and Wilderlands; Gollum (02130) is set aside, not in the deck (`data/overrides.json`), and the Nightmare deck prints no quest card.

- The guard (`sotm.guarding-gollum`): the player who controls the Gollum card, an objective ally that card effects reach like any ally (Mirkwood Bats, Hummerhorns, attachments) and that cannot attack, defend or commit. A change of guard is a change of control, announced as one (Pursuing Warg-rider hears it); choosing the same player is no change. At Setup (02126:A:1) the first player chooses the guard (`rr.first-player`; agent recommendation for who chooses, the span says "the players choose one player"). A card that forbids the change forbids it for every route, a card's "must" included (`rr.cannot`).
- Gollum (02130:A:1): the damage of an undefended attack against the guard goes to Gollum, as the attack's own damage. Damage a shadow effect or a treachery deals itself (Gollum's Bite, Hummerhorns) is not the attack's damage and is not sent to him (agent recommendation; no ruling). His leaving play for any reason, or the elimination of the guard, loses the game; a guard who changes loses nothing.
- Gollum (02130:A:2): the +3 threat comes after the refresh phase's +1, so the guard raises 4 in a round (community, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/198019-golum-in-return-to-mirkwood, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/57475-return-to-mirkwood-gollums-extra-threat, https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/02/04/return-to-mirkwood/).
- "Choose a new player" is a different player still in the game; with none (a lone player) the effect is unable: a "must ... if able" does nothing and a "may" is not offered (agent recommendation; no ruling found, https://visionofthepalantir.com/2022/12/21/return-to-mirkwood-2/ and https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/02/04/return-to-mirkwood/ say a solo player cannot meet it).
- The Tantrum cards (02135:A:1, 02136:A:1, 02137:A:1): the second sentence is a sentence, not a "then": only the player who guarded Gollum when the card was revealed chooses, after the first sentence and whether or not it did anything (agent recommendation). The hero for Gollum's Bite is the guard's own choice among his heroes, not Gollum (the printed card; a blog quotes "controlled by the first player", the scan wins).
- Dry Watercourse (02132:A:1): the printed line says "all treachery card effects that target the player guarding Gollum" also target each other player, so it covers a treachery card's When Revealed and its Shadow effect alike (literal from the printed line). The guard is first, then the others in player order from the first player; the new guard is still chosen by the original guard alone. A blog's narrower reading, that most Tantrum shadow effects still affect only the guard (https://visionofthepalantir.com/2022/12/21/return-to-mirkwood-2/), is one voice, not agreed community discussion, and is not followed against the printed line.
- The Spiders' Ring (02131:A:1, A:2): the lock is the same flag Isolation (900907) sets. The shadow card is moved from the shadow area to the active slot (it is no longer the enemy's shadow card); the location it replaces returns to the staging area with its progress; the shadow resolves only if the attack is undefended (agent recommendation; the printed text names this card).
- Escape Attempt (02127:B:1, B:2): the guard cannot commit unless he is the only player remaining in the game, eliminated players not counting. "Quest unsuccessfully" is the quest's own result: total Willpower below the staging area's Threat, so a tie does not lose (`ltp.quest-resolution`).
- To the Elvin King's Halls (02128:B:1): the guard cannot play a card from his hand, Quick and Response events included; a card put into play is not played, so it is still allowed (agent recommendation for the put-into-play exception). A new guard is barred at once.
- Ambush (02129:B:1, B:2): every enemy in play engages the guard, the ones in the staging area and the ones engaged with another player (`rr.in-play-and-out-of-play`); an engagement made this way makes no attack. The engine takes them in play order, staging area first (agent recommendation: the first player's choice of order changes nothing the cards read). The stage cannot be defeated while any enemy is in play, in the staging area or engaged.
- Mirkwood Bats (02138:A:2): after it engages any player, 1 damage to each character the guard controls, Gollum included (community, https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/02/04/return-to-mirkwood/, https://visionofthepalantir.com/2022/12/21/return-to-mirkwood-2/).
- Attercop, Attercop (02139:A:1): from the staging area it engages the guard whatever his threat; one already engaged with another player moves to the guard (agent recommendation; https://visionofthepalantir.com/2022/12/21/return-to-mirkwood-2/ says only "regardless of threat").
- Woodman's Glade (02133:A:1, A:2): only the guard pays the Travel cost, and with no ready hero the players cannot travel there. The Response is heard only when Woodman's Glade itself is explored as the active location (another location's exploration does not trigger it while the Glade lies in the staging area), is the first player's to use and lowers the threat of each player not guarding Gollum by 2 (agent recommendation for who answers). Wood Elf Path (02134:A:1) is the guard's to use.
- Nightmare, Forced at the beginning of the quest phase (900901:A:2), Web-spinner (900904:A:1), Wickedness and Mischief (900909:A:1): "must either" offers only an option that can be fully resolved: a hero to exhaust needs a ready hero, a Tantrum needs one in the encounter discard pile, and the two more cards and surge always can be; with neither of the first two nothing is asked (official, Caleb, 2014, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/108063-return-to-mirkwood-nightmare-forced-question/). The Tantrum's When Revealed resolves for the guard and the card stays in the discard pile; its own choice of a new guard resolves too. The topmost Tantrum is the last card of the pile with the trait, not the top card.
- Ambushing Orcs (900902:A:1): a shadow card dealt to it that targets the guard is resolved for each other player after the guard (community, https://visionofthepalantir.com/2020/03/11/nightmare-return-to-mirkwood/). Pursuing Warg-rider (900903:A:1, A:2): cannot be optionally engaged but still engages by an engagement check at 48; after the guard changes it engages him, from the staging area or from another player, and attacks (community, same article).
- Web-spinner (900904:A:2): the guard exhausts a ready character he controls; Gollum is one (agent recommendation; community, https://visionofthepalantir.com/2022/12/21/return-to-mirkwood-2/, says Gollum can be exhausted for King Spider).
