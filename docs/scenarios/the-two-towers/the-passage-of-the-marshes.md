# The Passage of the Marshes

- **The Passage of the Marshes** (`the-passage-of-the-marshes`; records in `src/effects/authored/the-passage-of-the-marshes.ts`, Mire in `src/rules/families/mire/`, Gollum and Sméagol in `src/rules/families/gollum/`). Mire X (`mec112.mire`) is the flag `mire` a location's constant record sets to its X (the printed keyword is not read): at the end of the refresh phase (`crossBoundary`, after the first player token pass and the frames it pushed, `rr.step-7-4` before `rr.step-7-5`, and before that moment's Forced effects) each Mire location in the staging area gets 1 mire token, a resource on the card, and a location whose tokens reach X is discarded at once (`PlaceMireToken`), to the encounter discard pile, never the victory display, and hears `discardedByMire` (not an explored or otherwise discarded location). Gollum and Sméagol are one physical card whose sides are the catalog cards 144075 and 144076 (`mec112.gollum-smeagol`; `FlipGollum`). Gollum's printed Immune keyword is turned off by an override (`data/overrides.json`): the flag `immuneToNonFellowship` holds the rule, read by `playerCardEffectCanChoose` with the source card's sphere on every path a player card's effect takes (picks, attachment hosts, damage, the enemy list). Each reading below is applied as researched or recommended; none was confirmed by the user.
  - Mire tokens (144019:A:1, 144020:A:2, 144021:A:1, 144022:A:1, 144023:A:1): the tokens come from the end of the refresh phase or any effect and are checked after each token placed, at least X discards; simultaneous discards resolve their Forced effects oldest card first, each completely (agent recommendation; `mec112.mire`, `rr.forced`). Soft Mires' reveal is the first player's, also when the discard comes during another player's defense (Groping Dead's Forced or Shadow): `RevealEncounter` `who: firstPlayer`, as 144065:B:3's (card words, `rr.first-player`).
  - Groping Dead (144016:A:2, A:3): a token placed on the active location counts, so at X the active location is discarded by Mire (its progress is lost, the Forced fires); with no active location nothing is placed. The Forced fires when the character is destroyed by Groping Dead's attack, the Shadow when the attack destroys one (agent recommendation; Nightmare Land of Shadow places tokens on the active location, https://visionofthepalantir.com/2022/05/02/the-passage-of-the-marshes/ , https://rulespal.com/lotr-nightmare-the-land-of-shadow/rulebook ; no ruling found).
  - Creeping Marshes (144024:A:2): attached to the current quest (Doomed 1 still raises each player); at the end of the refresh phase, after the keyword's own placement, it puts 1 mire token on each location in the staging area (one without Mire keeps it, which does nothing but add to Stagnant Pools' Threat) and is discarded; "Then, discard" is not held back by there being no location in the staging area (placing a token on each of none is complete, `rr.then`), so it is discarded at every refresh phase it is attached for; if the stage changes first it goes with the old stage. No official ruling or community answer was found (the Soft Mires thread, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/225023-soft-mires-location-question , and Vision of the Palantir's review of the scenario do not touch it): agent recommendation (`mec112.mire`, `rr.then`, `rr.attachment-cards`).
  - Shifting Quagmire (144020:A:2): the first player chooses; returning needs a location in the victory display (random with the game's seed, back with no tokens), the threat option raises each player by the number of locations there, and with none neither is selectable and nothing happens (researched: `rr.effects`, `rr.first-player`, the designer's answer that an option that cannot be fulfilled is not chosen, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/230355-selecting-from-or-option-that-does-nothing-once-youve-tried-it).
  - Haunted Mere (144021:A:1): the first player searches (there is no "you"); showing the Undead enemy is not staging it (no When Revealed or keyword), it joins the staging area, and the deck is shuffled whether or not one was found (agent recommendation; `rr.search`, `rr.reveal`).
  - Stagnant Pools (144022:A:2): +1 Threat for each mire token on it, read live while it is in the staging area; tokens placed on it as the active location add nothing (card words, `rr.active-location`).
  - Evil-smelling Fens (144019:A:1): 1 damage to each character in play, every player's heroes and allies and Sméagol, no enemy (agent recommendation; `mec112.gollum-smeagol`, `dcce.ship-objectives` by analogy).
  - Travel costs (144019:A:2, 144023:A:2, 144020:A:3): every player raises their threat by 1 whoever has the threat, and the travel stays legal when a seat would be eliminated (agent recommendation; `rr.travel`); Shifting Quagmire's Fellowship resource is paid by any hero of the players with the Fellowship sphere, and with none the travel cannot be paid (card words, `rr.travel`; `SpendResources` takes the sphere).
  - The engaged player's threat (144016:A:1, 144017:A:1, 144018:A:1): while the enemy is engaged, that player's threat cannot be reduced by any effect, the others' can (card words, `rr.cannot`: the flag `threatReductionBarred`).
  - Dead Things (144017:A:2, A:3): the threat is raised for the defending player of the attack, also when a sentinel of another player defended (`RaiseThreat` `defendingPlayer`, `rr.defending-player`); the Shadow counts each Undead enemy engaged with the defending player, the attacker included.
  - Candle-bearer (144018:A:2, A:3): "cannot take damage" covers all damage until the end of the round, for itself and, from its Shadow, for the attacking enemy (card words).
  - The Searching Eye (144015:A:2): the drawing player chooses to exhaust each hero they control (not selectable when all are exhausted) or to reveal the top card of the encounter deck; a copy drawn in the kept opening hand fires once the mulligan is settled, a mulliganed-away copy does not; it stays in hand, never played (agent recommendation; `mec112.poisoned-counsels-searching-eye`, `rr.effects`; no ruling on setup draws found: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/46027-drawing-treachery-during-setupobjectiveetc , https://ringsdb.com/card/144015).
  - Bitter Reek (144025:A:2): the revealing player chooses between the threat for each character they control committed to the quest and 1 damage to each committed character, any player's; an option with no committed character is not selectable (span-settled: `mec112.saga-staging-rules`). Its Shadow raises the defending player's threat by the attacking enemy's current Threat (card words).
  - A Fell Light (144026:A:2, A:3): the topmost Undead enemy of the encounter discard pile is put into play engaged with the revealing player, then each Undead enemy engaged with them, the new one included, makes an immediate attack, also when no card came; the Shadow returns the topmost Undead enemy of the discard pile to the staging area (card words; Vision of the Palantir's comment reads staging).
  - Morgul Wraith (144053:A:2): while The One Ring is exhausted it gets -30 engagement cost and is immune to player card effects (card words, `mec109.immune-to-player-card-effects`).
  - Call of the Ring (144054:A:1): the first player chooses; searching deck and discard pile for a Nazgûl needs one there (the victory display never holds one in this scenario) and adds it to the staging area with no shuffle, the other option exhausts The One Ring (an already exhausted Ring still lets the reveal happen) and reveals the top card (researched: `rr.effects`, `rr.first-player`, the designer's answer above).
  - We Hates Them! (144055:A:2, A:3): the first player chooses between flipping Sméagol (only while he is in play) and Gollum's immediate attack against them (only while he is), or cancels the whole effect by spending 1 Fellowship resource and exhausting The One Ring, an option only while there is an effect to cancel and the cost can be paid (Surge still applies; agent recommendation: the printed cancel is an option of the choice, as `rr.cancel` has no When Revealed cancel of its own, and Gildor Inglorion's Fellowship cancel, 141077:A:2, is the pattern). The Shadow gives +1 Attack and +3 instead when the attacker is Gollum.
  - Gollum (144075:A:1, A:2, A:3): immune to the effects of player cards that are not Fellowship cards; he is always engaged with the first player (he enters play engaged and moves to the new first player as the token passes), and the Forced attack fires only when he moves to a different first player in the refresh phase (true solo moves nothing, so no attack; entering play by a flip does not trigger it); defeated, he discards his damage, flips and enters play exhausted as Sméagol under the first player, never the discard pile or the victory display, and counts as defeated for listeners (span-settled: `mec112.gollum-smeagol`, `rr.first-player`; entering engaged is an agent recommendation, https://visionofthepalantir.com/2022/05/02/the-passage-of-the-marshes/ says the token never moves in solo play).
  - Sméagol (144076:A:1 to A:3): controlled by the first player (it moves with the token), immune to player card effects, flipped to Gollum after the players quest unsuccessfully (not on a tie), discarding damage and readying him, engaged with the first player; destroyed Sméagol loses the game, a flip does not (span-settled: `mec112.gollum-smeagol`).
  - Gollum and the first player's elimination (144075:A:1, 144076:A:1): when the first player is eliminated, Gollum is engaged with the new first player and Sméagol is controlled by them, each keeping exhaustion and damage (agent recommendation; the printed words say only "engages the first player" and "controlled by the first player"). The two never go to the staging area or the discard pile, and neither is ever shuffled into the encounter deck when it is refilled (`mec112.gollum-smeagol`).
  - The Taming of Sméagol (144064): Setup puts Gollum (set aside) into play engaged with the first player, then each player in order searches the deck for any 1 location ("different" is not printed, so two copies of a title may be taken), shown and added to staging with no When Revealed, and the deck is shuffled (card words). The players cannot advance unless the first player controls Sméagol, that is, Gollum has been defeated and not flipped back (card words). Each declaration of attackers against Gollum by a player discards the top encounter card and Gollum gets +X Defense until the end of that attack, X the discarded card's Threat (0 for a treachery or an empty deck, with no reshuffle: `rr.running-out-of-cards` is the quest phase only; with an empty deck the last card of the discard pile is read instead, a known edge); declarations by several players each trigger and X adds (agent recommendation; `rr.forced`, the pattern of Urdug, 19157:A:2; https://visionofthepalantir.com/2022/05/02/the-passage-of-the-marshes/ , "after a player declares any number of attackers").
  - Across the Marshes (144065): "players in the game" is the seats in the game; cards are discarded until a location is discarded (stopping when the deck is empty, with no reshuffle) and that location is added to staging, nothing when the count already meets it (agent recommendation; `rr.effects`, `rr.running-out-of-cards`). Progress cannot be placed on locations in the staging area (a copy of 19047:B:1). The players cannot advance unless the first player controls Sméagol and at least 4 locations are in the victory display, explored locations only, since Mire discards never go there (card words). At the end of the staging step with fewer staging locations than players the first player reveals one encounter card, one and not one per missing location (agent recommendation; `rr.first-player`).
  - Don't Look at the Lights! (144066): in player order each player searches the deck and discard pile for an Undead enemy and puts it into play engaged with them (a seat finding none gets none), then every location in the victory display is shuffled into the deck, which is shuffled once (card words, `rr.search`, `rr.engaged`). While any player is engaged with an Undead enemy no progress can be placed on this stage, from the quest or an effect (`noQuestProgress`); progress staying on the active location is not progress on the stage (card words, `rr.cannot`, `rr.active-location`). The stage cannot be defeated unless the first player controls Sméagol, and the stage waits defeated as other win gates do; defeating it wins (card words).
  - Table (`docs/design-system-table.md`, Cards, The Passage of the Marshes; `src/play/readouts/marshes.ts`): the top bar's Mire dial, the mire tokens on the location closest to its Mire X against that X, warming toward it, each location's count in its spoken words (144019:A:1, 144020:A:2, 144021:A:1, 144022:A:1, 144023:A:1; the tokens are the location's resource tokens), Gollum's hit points as a goal dial while he is an enemy in play (144064:B:1, 144075:A:3), and the held mark on the threat dial of the seat engaged with Gollum or controlling Sméagol, which follows the first-player token (144075:A:1, 144076:A:1). We Hates Them!'s cancel is worded as its own option (144055:A:2). No control types a number.
