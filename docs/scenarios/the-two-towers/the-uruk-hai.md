# The Uruk-hai

- **The Uruk-hai** (`the-uruk-hai`; records in `src/effects/authored/the-uruk-hai.ts`, the pursuit tracker in `src/rules/families/pursuit/`, the Captives in `src/rules/families/captives/`). The pursuit value (`mec112.pursuit`) is `ScenarioState.pursuit`, set to 10 by the quest card's Setup, raised and lowered by the cards, and the X of both quest stages; To Isengard! raises it at the end of each refresh phase and the players lose at 30 or more. The stages print X for their quest points on side B (`questPointsX`, set by `scripts/data/normalize/cards.ts` for a Two Towers quest), and a stage advances as soon as its progress reaches X, so lowering the value can advance it. Uglúk (143069:B:2) cannot be engaged, take damage or be moved out of the staging area by anything (`cannotLeaveStaging`, `moveCard`); "each enemy engaged with you adds its Threat" (143065:A:2) is read live to the end of the round (`engagedThreatSeat`). A Captive (143069:A:1) is a hero faceup under To Isengard! or attached to Grishnákh: in play, controlled by no one, blank, out of its owner's heroes yet not killed, so its owner stays in the game while it lies there (`rr.player-elimination`); a Captive that leaves the captive zone, destroyed or otherwise, is a Captive no more. With no Captive in play the players lose (143022:A:1). The table adds the pursuit dial in the top bar (declared by `widgets.pursuitTracker`, the value at which the players lose, read from To Isengard!'s record; it warms toward it and shows its change as the threat dial does), every Captive drawn under its host (under To Isengard! or Grishnákh, wherever he is), a held dial on each seat that has a Captive, and the owner on a Captive's row in Grishnákh's pick when more than one seat's are offered; the choices that name the pursuit value say its value and the loss at 30 (`docs/design-system-table.md`, Cards). Rulings (user, 2026-11-07):
  - A seat whose Campaign Log names a captive takes that hero as its Captive without being asked; a seat with no logged captive (a fresh log, or a mixed one) chooses 1 non-Fellowship hero it controls, as in standalone.
  - Strange Weariness (143031:A:1): the characters committed when it resolves keep -1 Willpower until the end of the round, even after the quest phase.
  - Hands of a Healer (143016:A:3): "a hero" is any hero in play, a Captive included.
  - Grishnákh (143024:A:1): any way he leaves play puts the attached Captive faceup underneath To Isengard!; a Captive is never discarded that way.
  - A Captive keeps its tokens and attachments, a Permanent boon included; the damage on it counts in the score, and a Captive destroyed goes to its owner's discard pile.
  - The Skill boons (143015 to 143018) attach only to a hero with their Trait (Warrior, Healer, Ranger, Noble), also when earned at The Uruk-hai's Resolution (143071:B:1): a seat is offered one only on a hero it controls with the Trait, and none without (user ruling C1, 2026-11-08; FFG preview "Now May I Make a Right Choice"; `boonHeroes`, `src/rules/families/campaign/resolution.ts`).
  - A seat whose last non-Fellowship hero is its Captive is not eliminated while that Captive is held (`rr.player-elimination`; user ruling C2, 2026-11-08).
  - Rescue the Captives' Response (143070:B:2) lowers the pursuit value no lower than 0: the card names no floor (user ruling C3, 2026-11-08).
  - Defeating the last stage rescues each Captive (143070:B:3, "they have rescued each Captive hero"): it returns among its owner's heroes in the place it held, controlled again, its text box no longer blank and its attachments kept, as soon as the stage is defeated, in a standalone game as in a campaign one and before the score or the Resolution reads the heroes (`rescueCaptives`, a settle hook of `src/rules/families/captives/index.ts`), so a Skill boon may go on it; the score counts it as any hero of its owner's (its damage, or its threat cost when its owner was eliminated) and the log lists it among the heroes, not the fallen.
  - Eastemnet (143027:A:1): "each hero's resource pool" includes the Captives, as Hands of a Healer's "a hero" does (user ruling, 2026-11-10).
  - A Captive rescued back to a seat that was eliminated scores as that seat's other heroes do: its threat cost, as a destroyed hero (`src/campaign/score.ts`; user ruling, 2026-11-10).
  - An additional attack (Strange Weariness' Shadow) is dealt a new shadow card (`faq.additional-attacks`). A location the progress explores hears nothing, as Ettenmoors does (Banks of Entwash, 143029:A:1).
