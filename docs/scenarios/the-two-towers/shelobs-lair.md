# Shelob's Lair

- **Shelob's Lair** (`shelobs-lair`; records in `src/effects/authored/shelobs-lair.ts`, the Great Spider set as the wilderland pile `greatSpider` dealt as `greatSpiderDeck`, tests in `src/campaign/scenarios/shelobs-lair/`). The Great Spider set lies beside the encounter deck, hidden, and is shuffled in by stage 2A (`ShufflePileIntoEncounterDeck`, 144071:A:1). Stage 1 (144070) has no quest points: its Forced advances it at the end of the quest phase once the Secret Way is in the victory display; stage 3 (144072) is the last stage, so defeating it wins (`rr.winning-the-game`, as Marshes 144066). Each reading below is applied as researched or recommended. Vision of the Palantir's review (https://visionofthepalantir.com/2023/05/08/shelobs-lair) is a fan review: it states readings and cites no official ruling.
  - Shelob (144040:A:2, A:3) and her resource tokens: +1 Threat for each resource, read live. The Forced at the beginning of the quest phase is the first player's choice, place 1 resource on her or an immediate attack against the first player, each only while she is in play (nothing at stage 1); the attack is dealt a shadow card (`faq.enemy-attacks-outside-combat`) and holds while she is engaged with the first player after Cleft (agent recommendation: no source names the chooser of a Forced "either ... or"; `rr.first-player` is the convention for a card with no player, `rr.effects`; the FFG forum thread https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/224597-when-revealed-do-this-or-that names none). Her resources are tokens on the card, the only new counter; the table adds a Shelob dial (below).
  - Choosers (144041:A:2, 144042:A:2, 144061:A:2): "you" on a When Revealed is the revealing player (`mec112.saga-staging-rules`, span-settled); an option that cannot change the game state is not offered (`rr.effects`). Orc of Cirith Ungol: each player discards 1 random card, offered while some hand holds one, a player with an empty hand discarding nothing (span-settled: "as much as able"); or the Orc attacks the revealer. Lurking Malice: a resource on Shelob or her attack, each only while she is in play, and none at stage 1 (the card keeps Peril and Surge).
  - Morgul Vale (144043:A:2): the search of the encounter deck and discard pile for a Morgul Wraith adds it to the staging area (no When Revealed), the deck is shuffled, and then the first player chooses to exhaust The One Ring (only while it is ready) or each player discards 1 random card (only while some hand holds one). The choice follows whether or not a Wraith was found. Unsettled (agent recommendation, no ruling found): the "Then" of the card follows the sentence "Shuffle the encounter deck", which always resolves (`rr.then`), and a search that finds nothing has been carried out (as Marshes' Haunted Mere); the designer's answer in https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/230355-selecting-from-or-option-that-does-nothing-once-youve-tried-it is about an option that cannot be selected, not about a "Then", and no ruling on a fruitless search under "Then" exists.
  - The Secret Way (144044:A:2, A:3): its Travel cost is the first player's choice of exhausting Sméagol (only while he is in play and ready) or each player discarding 1 random card (only while every living player holds one); with neither payable the players cannot travel there (`rr.travel`; the cost is paid in full, as 41011:A:2). Exploring it, each player in turn exhausts a ready character they control (Sméagol is the first player's) or discards 1 random card (agent recommendation; `rr.effects`, `mec112.gollum-smeagol`; https://visionofthepalantir.com/2023/05/08/shelobs-lair says the author always exhausts Sméagol).
  - Travel costs that cannot be paid (144045:A:3, 144049:A:2, 144048:A:2): a resource on Shelob needs her in play, and exhausting Sméagol needs him ready, or the players cannot travel there (`rr.travel`; `travelCostUnpayable` in `src/rules/interpret/restrictions.ts`, tested for each). Cleft of Cirith Ungol (144045:A:2): while it is in the victory display Shelob gets +2 Attack, loses Indestructible and is engaged with the first player, moving with the token (the Saruman and Gollum pattern `engagedWithFirst`; span-settled by analogy, `rr.victory`, `mec112.indestructible`; agent recommendation for Shelob). The Spider's Lair (144046:A:1, A:2): 1 resource comes off Shelob when it is explored, and Shelob attacks the first player when it becomes the active location, neither while she is out of play.
  - The Under-way (144047:A:1): each copy in the staging area gives every Orc enemy -5 engagement cost and a Forced effect that deals it a shadow card after it engages a player, so copies add up (agent recommendation; `faq.lasting-effects`). Its Shadow deals the attacking enemy 2 additional shadow cards, resolved in the same attack (https://visionofthepalantir.com/2023/05/08/shelobs-lair, agent recommendation). Deep Dark Passage (144048:A:1): each placement of progress on a location in the staging area, itself included, is reduced by 1 (the placement is the unit, copies add up; `progressReduced`; the quest's own placement on the active location is not reduced; agent recommendation, https://visionofthepalantir.com/2023/05/08/shelobs-lair).
  - Den of Night (144049:A:1): while in the staging area, after a "When Revealed" effect is canceled 1 resource goes on Shelob, one for each copy; a cancel of a revealed card with no When Revealed effect does not count; We Hates Them!'s own printed cancel counts (`CancelEffect` in its option, so `whenRevealedCanceled` fires; `rr.cancel`; agent recommendation).
  - Exhalation of Decay (144050:A:1): in player order each player discards 1 random card and 1 non-objective attachment they control, each only when they have one; the attachment is the controller's, wherever it lies (`rr.ownership-and-control`; agent recommendation). Many Openings (144051:A:1): each location in the staging area, later arrivals that round included, gets +1 Threat until the end of the round (span-settled, `faq.lasting-effects`), then the revealer reveals another card with fewer locations than players. Its Shadow adds the active location's Threat to the attacker, 0 with none.
  - A Heavy Burden (144052:A:3): the first player, who controls the Ring-bearer (`mec112.multiplayer-the-one-ring`), chooses to raise each player's threat by 1 or exhaust the Ring-bearer, the latter only while it is ready (agent recommendation; https://visionofthepalantir.com/2023/05/08/shelobs-lair paraphrases it per player).
  - The Great Spider set (144059 to 144062): Blocked Tunnel's When Revealed makes it the active location and the old one returns to the staging area keeping its progress, with no Travel cost for it (researched, https://visionofthepalantir.com/2023/05/08/shelobs-lair); its Action is any player's, exhausting a ready character they control with a Weapon attachment to place 3 progress on it before any reduction (span-settled, `rr.cost`). Shelob's Poison attaches to a hero the revealing player controls that carries none (Limit 1 per hero, on the record as `AttachesTo`), exhausts it (it may already be exhausted), blanks its text box except Traits and keeps it from readying; with no such hero it is discarded; its Shadow resolves the same effect for the defending player when Shelob attacks and the card stays attached (agent recommendation for the no-hero outcome). Lurking Malice's Shadow places a resource on Shelob and then she gets +1 Attack for each resource, the new one included. Rage and Hunger: +2 Attack until the end of the phase and an immediate attack against each player in player order, first player first (span-settled, `rr.in-player-order`, `faq.peril-attacks`); its Shadow is an additional attack dealt a new shadow card (`faq.additional-attacks`).
  - Stage 1 (144070): Setup puts Sméagol, set aside, into play under the first player (a setup placement, not a play) and shuffles the encounter deck (`mec112.gollum-smeagol`). No progress can be placed on an Underground location, quest progress included. The Response after the Secret Way is explored, offered to any player only while a Nazgûl enemy is in the staging area and every living player holds 3 cards, makes every player discard 3 random cards (the cost is paid in full, `rr.cost`, "to" marks a cost) to discard each Nazgûl in the staging area (researched, community answer: https://visionofthepalantir.com/2023/05/08/shelobs-lair, not official, so not user-confirmed).
  - Stage 2 (144071): Shelob and the Great Spider set join, with the encounter discard pile shuffled into the encounter deck (Gollum / Sméagol is never in it). Shelob cannot leave the staging area; Sméagol cannot attack or defend. Each player cannot play or put into play more than 1 ally each round: a per-player count of every ally a player plays or puts into play that round, one that leaves play again included, counted by the controller as it entered (`LimitEntries` with `perPlayer`, `src/rules/families/sea/entries.ts`; Sméagol's flip and Setup placement do not count; agent recommendation, https://visionofthepalantir.com/2023/05/08/shelobs-lair); no more than 4 progress can be placed on the stage each round (`questProgressCap`, a per-round count that resets with the round, the active location taking its progress first, `faq.progress-without-advancing`).
  - Stage 3 (144072): Cleft joins, Sméagol flips to Gollum (engaged with the first player), Shelob is healed and each player places 1 resource on her. While she holds a resource every instance of damage dealt to her, one attack or one effect, is canceled and 1 resource is discarded instead, not 1 per point (researched, https://visionofthepalantir.com/2023/05/08/shelobs-lair: "each resource acts as a Feint"; `resourceCancel`, `rr.damage`, `rr.cancel`). A defeated Gollum goes to the victory display faceup as Gollum, not flipped (the card words, 144072:B:1), the flip of 144075:A:3 skipped on this stage. The stage cannot be defeated unless Shelob and Gollum are both in the victory display.
  - Additional attack of Call of the Ring (144054:A:2): the record copies 19081:A:2; the additional attack is dealt a new shadow card, the old ones discarded first (`faq.additional-attacks`).
  - Table (`docs/design-system-table.md`, Cards, Shelob's Lair; `src/play/readouts/lair.ts`): Shelob's resources dial, the "This round" dial of stage 2B's cap, each seat's ally count as a mark on its seat dial, and Gollum's own dial on stage 3B (144072:B:1), each cited to its line above; Gollum / Sméagol's held mark is Marshes'. The Great Spider set is hidden and set aside, so it has no zone widget.
