# Journey to the Cross-roads

- **Journey to the Cross-roads** (`journey-to-the-cross-roads`; records in `src/effects/authored/journey-to-the-cross-roads.ts`, the Harad deck as the wilderland pile `harad`, the enemies under The Black Gate in `src/rules/families/harad/`). The Men of Harad set is dealt as the pile `haradDeck` and shuffled under The Cross-roads by the stage's Setup (`mec112.setup-journey-to-the-cross-roads`). Each reading below is applied as researched or recommended; none was confirmed by the user.
  - The Harad deck's reveals (144068:B:1, 144032:A:2, 144036:A:2): "reveal the top facedown card from under The Cross-roads" is a reveal (`rr.reveal`, `AddFromPile` with `reveal`), so the card's keywords and When Revealed effects resolve: Man of Harad's Surge reveals one more card from the encounter deck. It is staged, or put into play engaged with the revealing player, who has then engaged it (`rr.engaged`: its Forced effect fires). A card of the deck cannot be canceled by a Response to a reveal from the encounter deck (agent recommendation).
  - Options that cannot be fulfilled (144036:A:2, 144037:A:1, 144038:A:2): an option that cannot be fulfilled cannot be selected; with none selectable nothing happens (`rr.effects`: an effect that can change the game state must be selected; FFG developer reply on Marching Up the Road, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/230355-selecting-from-or-option-that-does-nothing-once-youve-tried-it).
  - Marching Up the Road (144037:A:1): the search option needs a Harad enemy in the encounter deck (the Men of Harad set lies under The Cross-roads, so only the Man and the Captain of Harad are there); a search that finds nothing is not an option to take, and the other option is resolved (developer reply, same thread). The search covers the encounter deck only, not its discard pile, and shuffles nothing, the card printing neither (card text, `rr.search`; https://visionofthepalantir.com/2022/10/24/journey-to-the-cross-roads/).
  - The Dark Lord's Summons (144036:A:2): the first option needs a Harad enemy engaged with the player, the second a card in the Harad deck; ties on the lowest Attack go to the revealing player (`rr.effects`, `rr.engaged`; https://theroadlotr.wordpress.com/2018/12/07/journey-to-the-crossroads-playthrough/).
  - Fierce Southrons (144038:A:2): the first option is offered only while a Harad enemy is in the staging area (lowest Threat, ties to the revealing player, then the extra reveal), the second only while an enemy is (agent recommendation; no ruling found; `rr.effects`; https://visionofthepalantir.com/2022/10/24/journey-to-the-cross-roads/ words the Threat as "lowest attack", the ledger read from the scan says Threat).
  - Archery 2 (144039:A:1; Man of Harad 144028, Haradrim Archer 144058): the Archery gained adds to the printed Archery (1 + 2 on a Man, X + 2 on an Archer), and reaches the Harad enemies that arrive later in the round (`rr.keywords`, `mec109.archery` cumulative, `rr.lasting-effects`; https://visionofthepalantir.com/2022/10/24/journey-to-the-cross-roads/ agrees on stacking). The class holder `enemies:<Trait>` carries it.
  - Captain of Harad (144029:A:1): +1 Attack and -10 engagement cost to every Harad enemy for the round, those that arrive later and the Harad deck's included (`rr.lasting-effects`).
  - Marching Up the Road, Arrows Thick in the Air (144037:A:2, 144039:A:2): the enemy is placed under The Black Gate after the attack resolves (Marching: only if the attack destroyed a character), and the defending player chooses Arrows Thick's option (`rr.step-6-4-2`: the active player resolves the shadow effect; agent recommendation for the timing).
  - Brace of Coneys (144014:A:1, A:2): "control the Ring-bearer" is a hero the player controls with the Ring-bearer trait (`mec112.multiplayer-the-one-ring`: in multiplayer the first player controls it). "(choose 2)" is two different options; an option no change can come of (no damaged hero) cannot be chosen (agent recommendation, as 22074:B:2; one unanswered comment at https://ringsdb.com/card/22074 asks the same).
  - The Black Gate (144027:A:1, A:2): X is the living players plus 3, and the loss holds whenever the cards under it reach that number, so it also falls when a seat is eliminated with the cards already there (card text; a constant, as 143022:A:1's loss). The Forced effect is ignored while Henneth Annûn is the active location, the loss line is not.
  - Henneth Annûn (144031:A:1): ignores only the Forced effects on stage 1B (144068:B:1) and The Black Gate (144027:A:1), not the loss line; the quest phase still happens (card text; https://visionofthepalantir.com/2022/10/24/journey-to-the-cross-roads/).
  - The Cross-roads (144030:A:2): Threat 9 less one for each card left in the Harad deck (never below 0), and the players cannot travel here while a card is left there or before 20 progress is on stage 1B; the progress is kept past 20, since the stage cannot be defeated while it is in play (card text).
  - Southward Road, Foothills of Ephel Dúath, Ithilien Woods, Hills of Ithilien (144032:A:2, 144033:A:2, 144034:A:1, 144035:A:1): Southward Road's cost is revealing the top Harad deck card into the staging area, and with the deck empty it cannot be paid (`rr.travel`); Foothills' cost is the first player engaging any enemy in the staging area, paid before the location becomes active, so Ithilien Woods' Forced effect is not heard for it (`rr.engaged`); the Forced effect Ithilien Woods and Hills of Ithilien give hears every engagement of a Harad enemy, one put into play engaged too (`rr.engaged`).
  - Oliphaunt, Haradrim Archer (144056:A:1, 144058): "cannot have attachments or take more than 3 damage each round" is `cannotHaveAttachments` and `damageCap` 3. The Archer's Threat and Archery are X, X the number of players (card text; Hall of Beorn and DragnCards print Threat X, the playthrough at https://theroadlotr.wordpress.com/2018/12/07/journey-to-the-crossroads-playthrough/ noting 0), and its Forced effect assigns X damage among the engaged player's characters.
  - Additional attacks (144039:A:2, 144057:A:2): the enemy that makes an additional attack is dealt a new shadow card, its old ones discarded first (`faq.additional-attacks`); Haradrim Soldier's Shadow is its own record; Ravenous Spider's, and the lines copied from it, deal a new shadow card the same way.
  - Brace of Coneys chosen at Setup (144069:A:2, B:1): each seat that shuffled a copy into its deck earns one at the Resolution, two copies of the code among the seats included (`dealtCopies` numbers them); the enemies under The Black Gate are written to the log by code (`blackGate`).
  - Table (`docs/design-system-table.md`, Cards, Journey to the Cross-roads; `src/play/readouts/roads.ts`): the top bar's Gate dial, the enemies facedown under The Black Gate against the players + 3 at which the players lose (144027:A:2), warming toward it and warning at 2 and at 1 from it, never the accent, and the options that put an enemy under it arming the two-tap caution when it is one short; the Harad deck dial, the cards left under The Cross-roads, which sets its Threat and bars its travel (144030:A:2, 144068:B:1); the enemies under The Black Gate drawn facedown under it as one counted peek; and the Harad deck as the second slot's pile (`haradDeck`, `src/play/store/pile-ids.ts`), so a card revealed from it comes off that pile.
