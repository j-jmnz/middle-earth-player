# Against the Shadow

The data, rulings and readings of the Against the Shadow cycle, scenario by scenario. Product rules, the Domain and the Core round are in `docs/brief.md`; the authority order is in `.claude/rules/authority.md`.

The cycle of 2012 to 2013: the deluxe expansion Heirs of Númenor (`HoN`, three scenarios) and its six Adventure Packs The Steward's Fear (`TSF`), The Drúadan Forest (`TDF`), Encounter at Amon Dîn (`EaAD`), Assault on Osgiliath (`AoO`), The Blood of Gondor (`BoG`) and The Morgul Vale (`TMV`). Like Dwarrowdelf and The Hobbit it is `kind: "cycle"` (`data/campaigns/against-the-shadow.json`): no campaign card, no booklet, no boon or burden and no Campaign Mode, so every game is standalone. Nightmare is not built: the Nightmare decks 9019 to 9027 are cards, and their scenario blocks and ledger lines are later work. The cycle is data: `scripts/data/cycle-defs.ts` (its nine scenarios with the DragnCards decks `Q03.n` and `E03.n`), `scripts/data/campaign-defs.ts` (its seven products), `scripts/data/normalize/setup-against-the-shadow.ts` (the Setup sentences of the stage 1A cards the other readers do not read), and `scripts/data/against-the-shadow.test.ts` (its piles, counts and spans). The printed ledger is `data/printed/against-the-shadow.json`.

- **Data.** Each scenario's `setupSpan` is its encounter-set paragraph, `ats.setup-<scenario id>`, in the Heirs of Númenor rulebook (`hon`) or the pack's rulesheet (`tsf`, `tdf`, `eaad`, `aoo`, `bog`, `tmv`; `data/rules/index.json`). The piles are the DragnCards load lists' (Q03.1 to Q03.9, E03.1 to E03.9), checked against the stage 1A card's Setup; the departures are overrides in `data/overrides.json`, each with its reason and source, and are named in the scenario's file.
- **Rules the cycle owns.** Battle and Siege (`hon.battle`, `hon.siege`) and Archery (`hon.archery`), unique cards (`ats.unique-cards`), ally objectives (`ats.ally-objectives`), The Siege of Cair Andros (`ats.cair-andros-location-damage`, `ats.cair-andros-defense-carry-over`, `ats.cair-andros-remove-stage`, `ats.cair-andros-remove-stage-example`), Underworld X (`ats.underworld`, `ats.underworld-example`, `ats.setup-the-stewards-fear-underworld-deck`), Prowl X (`ats.prowl`, `ats.prowl-example`), Villagers X (`ats.villagers`, `ats.villagers-example`, `ats.villager-tokens-discarded`, `ats.setup-amon-din-objectives`), controlling locations (`ats.controlling-locations`, `ats.losing-control-of-locations`), hidden cards (`ats.hidden-cards`, `ats.hidden-cards-turn-faceup`) and Captain enemies (`ats.captain-enemies`, `ats.setup-the-morgul-vale-to-the-tower`). Battle and Siege stay in the Heirs of Númenor spans where the engine already reads them; the Hobbit has its own copies (`hob.battle`, `hob.siege`).
- **FAQ spans the cycle adds.** `faq.moving-resources-is-not-adding` (FAQ 1.25), `faq.two-active-locations` (FAQ 1.34), `faq.ranger-spikes-ambush`, `faq.a-good-harvest` and `faq.blood-of-gondor-hidden-cards`. The player card readings below use them.
- **Errata.** Ten of the eleven errata that Hall of Beorn flags are spans: A Watchful Peace (`faq.errata.05012`), Master of Lore (`faq.errata.05016`), Blocking Wargs (`faq.errata.05029`), The Power of Mordor (`faq.errata.05061`), Trained for War (`faq.errata.06036`), Counter-attack (`faq.errata.06106`), Caldara (`faq.errata.06107`), The Ambush (`faq.errata.06117`), The Cross-roads (`faq.errata.06123`) and Impenetrable Fog (`faq.errata.06157`). Silvan Refugee has no span: FAQ 1.9 does not list it, and only community sources name its Scout trait from a reprint. A card's printed line is read from its scan; where the scan prints the pre-errata text, the erratum span wins (`.claude/rules/authority.md`).
- **Shared encounter sets.** The first scenario that deals a set holds its readings; a later scenario reuses them. Streets of Gondor and Brigands: Peril in Pelargir, The Steward's Fear. Brooding Forest: Into Ithilien, The Drúadan Forest. Creatures of the Forest: Into Ithilien, The Morgul Vale. Southrons: Into Ithilien, The Siege of Cair Andros, Assault on Osgiliath. Mordor Elite: The Siege of Cair Andros, Assault on Osgiliath, The Morgul Vale. Ravaging Orcs: The Siege of Cair Andros, Encounter at Amon Dîn, The Blood of Gondor. A card keeps one record in every scenario that deals it, so a fix in one scenario's reading moves the others' saved games (Peril in Pelargir and The Steward's Fear deal the same two sets).
- **Mechanics the engine has not read before.** Underworld, Prowl, Villagers, hidden cards, Battleground locations and skipped stages, player-controlled locations and Captain enemies. Each is built by the scenario that first deals it (`docs/agents/agent-brief.md`, Scenario procedure); none is built by the data.
- **Readings.** Each scenario file lists what its cards, rulesheet and sources leave open or settle, with sources (`.claude/rules/authority.md`, Open points). A reading is `span-settled` (a rule span or the printed line settles it), `researched` (an online source settles it) or `recommended` (no source settles it); each file's Readings to confirm lists every `recommended` reading and every `researched` one that rests on forum threads alone. The research rows were written against source text, so the scenario's Read task confirms every line against the scan-read ledger. The one user ruling that touches the cycle: a Response is offered immediately after its condition resolves (`docs/brief.md`, Keywords implemented once).

## Player card readings

The player card readings use the same statuses. The keys are the research keys, `<code>:<field>`, written from the source text, so the Read task of the scenario that first deals the card confirms each against its scan.

- `05001:text` (span-settled). Lower the cost by 2, never below 0; applies only to a Weapon or Armor attachment played on Beregond. Not taken: Cost can go negative. Basis: rr.modifiers ("if a value is below 0, it is treated as 0; a card cannot have negative cost").
- `05004:text` (span-settled). Citadel Custodian counts Gondor allies of every player (card says "in play", not "you control"), minimum cost 0. Not taken: Count only allies you control. Basis: card text; rr.modifiers.
- `06032:text` (span-settled). Mirlonde counts herself (a hero you control with printed lore icon); threat cost cannot go below 0. Not taken: Exclude herself. Basis: card text ("each hero you control"); rr.modifiers.
- `06033:text` (span-settled). Forlong: a neutral ally belongs to no sphere, so the four spheres must be leadership, tactics, spirit, lore (record 06033:triggered:1 already reads it so). Not taken: Neutral counts as a sphere. Basis: faq.stand-and-fight; rr.neutral-cards.
- `06001:text` (researched). Hirluin's pool pays for Outlands allies of any sphere, with no resource match needed when paid only from his pool; mixed with another hero's pool, that hero's sphere must still match. Not taken: A match is still needed with Hirluin's own pool. Basis: FAQ 1.9 Radagast (SoM 59) entry: "Radagast's ability breaks the need for a resource match if the resources are coming only from his pool. (If combining his resources with resources from a hero's pool, the hero's sphere must match the sphere of the Creature being played.)". Sources: https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/lotr_faq_19.pdf (by analogy: no entry names Hirluin; official FAQ, same wording shape).
- 06002:text, 06004:text, 06006:text, 06008:text (span-settled). Each copy of an Outlands bonus (Warrior of Lossarnach +1 def, Knights of the Swan +1 atk, Ethir Swordsman +1 will, Anfalas Herdsman +1 hp) applies independently to every Outlands character you control, itself and Hirluin included; two copies give +2. Not taken: A copy does not buff itself or the bonus does not stack. Basis: rr.constant-ability ("each instance affects the game state independently"); rr.modifiers. Sources: https://ringsdb.com/card/06002 (a reviewer reaches the same count; not needed).
- `06010:text` (researched). A Good Harvest: any-sphere resources pay for cards of the named sphere; a 0-cost card still needs a resource match, so it cannot play a 0-cost card without a matching hero. Not taken: Free play of 0-cost cards. Basis: FAQ 1.9 Q&A: "Can I used A Good Harvest (AtS 10) to play a 0 cost card without a resource match? A: No. A Good Harvest only allows you to spend resources for the named sphere, a resource match is still needed to play cards with no resource cost." Also rr.resource-match (0 cost still needs a match). Sources: https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/lotr_faq_19.pdf.
- `06003:text` (span-settled). Gaining Strength: the discard of 2 is the cost ("to"), so the event cannot be played if the hero has fewer than 2; then add 3 to the same hero. Not taken: Play with fewer than 2. Basis: rr.cost ("to" separates cost from effect).
- 05003:text, 06135:text (researched). Errand-rider and Pelargir Ship Captain MOVE a resource: the total does not rise, it is not "adding"; the hero chosen to receive it may be any player's hero (Ship Captain: "another hero's"), and the source must be a hero you control. Not taken: Moving counts as adding. Basis: FAQ 1.9 (1.25): "Moving a resource ... does not count as 'adding' a resource because it did not take a new resource from the token bank ... Gaining a resource is a blanket term that includes collecting, adding and moving." The record for 06135 already picks the second hero from any player. Sources: https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/lotr_faq_19.pdf.
- `06039:text` (researched). Harbor Master triggers only on a card effect that ADDS resources (Envoy of Pelargir, Wealth of Gondor, Gaining Strength, Hidden Cache, Squire of the Citadel); it does NOT trigger on a move (Errand-rider, Pelargir Ship Captain) or on collecting in the resource phase. Not taken: Any gain triggers it. Basis: FAQ 1.9 (1.25), same entry as above. Sources: https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/lotr_faq_19.pdf.
- `05005:text` (recommended). Mutual Accord: the set of cards is fixed when the event resolves (cards entering later get nothing); the two clauses give the same result in either order (every card that was Gondor or Rohan ends with both traits); it affects every card in play of every player (heroes, allies, attachments, enemies, locations with the trait). Not taken: Cards entering later also gain the trait. Basis: FAQ 1.9 (1.55): a lasting effect from a player card is "calculated at the time that the ability is triggered, and ... not recalculated if the game state changes" (span faq.lasting-effects; its example is a value, so I extend it to the set of cards). Sources: https://ringsdb.com/card/05005 (reviews, no ruling), https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/lotr_faq_19.pdf; no source names a trait-granting effect or a later entrant, so the extension from 1.55 is mine.
- `05011:text` (recommended). Light the Beacons: the characters in play when it resolves get +2 defense and do not exhaust to defend until the end of the round; a character that enters later gets neither; a defender who would exhaust (Sentinel and ordinary defenders alike) stays ready. Not taken: Later entrants also covered. Basis: faq.lasting-effects (1.55), same extension as Mutual Accord. Sources: https://ringsdb.com/card/05011 (no ruling; only that it combos with Sentinel and lasts the round), https://hallofbeorn.com/Cards/Details/Light-the-Beacons (no rulings); none names a later entrant.
- `06113:text` (span-settled). Children of the Sea: +2 willpower until end of phase; if the ally is no longer in play at the end of the phase, nothing is shuffled ("if it is still in play"). Not taken: Shuffle even if it left play. Basis: card text. Sources: https://ringsdb.com/card/06113 (no ruling; confirms the condition).
- 05013:text, 06085:text (span-settled). Blood of Númenor and Gondorian Fire: the 1 spent resource is a cost, paid before the effect, so the bonus counts the resources left after paying (9 in pool gives +8); the bonus is fixed at the trigger and does not change when resources are spent later that phase. Not taken: Count the spent resource too, or recount live. Basis: rr.cost ("to" = cost then effect); faq.lasting-effects (1.55: not recalculated; the Nenya example is the same kind of value). Sources: https://ringsdb.com/card/06085 (a commenter says the same: "+8 with 9, locks in at trigger"; not needed).
- `06140:text` (span-settled). Lay of Nimrodel: the +1 willpower per resource is fixed when the event resolves. Not taken: Recount live. Basis: faq.lasting-effects (1.55, Nenya example is a willpower value fixed at trigger).
- `06136:text` (span-settled). Visionary Leadership: "Gondor characters" is every player's Gondor character, heroes and allies, including a card made Gondor by Mutual Accord; active only while the attached hero has at least 1 resource, rechecked live. Not taken: Only your characters. Basis: card text (no "you control"); rr.constant-ability; rr.modifiers. Sources: https://ringsdb.com/card/05005 (reviews pair it with Mutual Accord).
- `05002:text` (span-settled). Boromir (HoN): +1 attack for every Gondor ally of any player while he has 1+ resource in his pool (not "you control"). Not taken: Only allies you control. Basis: card text; rr.constant-ability.
- `06111:text` (recommended). The Hammer-stroke: Encounter Action (encounter phase window, rr.action); each enemy in play (staging area and engaged with any player) engages you; an enemy already engaged with you does not "engage" again, so engage triggers fire only for newly engaged enemies. Not taken: Already-engaged enemies re-engage and re-trigger. Basis: card text; FAQ 1.9 (1.20) enemy "remains engaged ... until ... a card effect returns it to the staging area, engages it to another player"; FAQ 1.49 (engaging vs being engaged). Sources: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/309724-the-hammer-stroke-engage-responses, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/145013-the-hammerstroke-engagement-question (community only; no designer reply; they agree already-engaged enemies are unaffected but do not settle enemies engaged with other players, which the record includes by the card's words "in play").
- `05012:errata` (span-settled). A Watchful Peace erratum: "Response: After a location worth no victory points leaves play as an explored location..." (the FAQ elides the rest; the source text already holds the full line). Not taken: Original wording. Basis: faq.errata.05012; FAQ 1.9 "Heirs of Númenor Expansion, A Watchful Peace HoN 12". Sources: https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/lotr_faq_19.pdf.
- `05016:errata` (span-settled). Master of Lore erratum: "Action: Exhaust Master of Lore to name a card type. Lower the cost for you to play the next [lore] card of that type by 1 until the end of the phase (to a minimum of 1)." (the PDF shows the lore icon garbled as a letter); "[lore] card" is any lore card of that type, not only an event. Not taken: Pre-erratum wording. Basis: faq.errata.05016; FAQ 1.9. Sources: https://hallofbeorn.com/Cards/Details/Master-of-Lore (cites FAQ 1.04, 02/27/2013).
- `06036:errata` (span-settled). Trained for War erratum: "Action: Until the end of the phase, if the current quest does not have siege, it gains battle." Keywords are lowercase in the erratum. The Play-only line stays (the erratum text is quoted from "Action" on, so I keep the printed first line). Not taken: Drop the play-only restriction. Basis: faq.errata.06036; FAQ 1.9 "Against the Shadow Expansion, Trained for War AtS 36". Sources: https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/lotr_faq_19.pdf.
- `06107:errata` (span-settled). Caldara erratum: "(Limit once per game.)" (added; the printed card lacked it), in FAQ 1.9 (10/16/2017). Not taken: Unlimited use. Basis: faq.errata.06107; FAQ 1.9. Sources: https://hallofbeorn.com/Cards/Details/Caldara (FAQ 1.09, 11/15/2017 note), https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/lotr_faq_19.pdf.
- `06107:text` (researched). Caldara: "each other hero" excludes Caldara; counts heroes with a printed spirit icon, an ally turned hero keeping its printed icon; Caldara is discarded as the cost before the count (no effect on it). Not taken: Count Caldara. Basis: card text; rr.printed; FAQ 1.9 Sword-thain entry: "If an ally with a printed sphere icon becomes a hero, that hero is also considered to have that printed sphere icon.". Sources: https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/lotr_faq_19.pdf.
- `06037:errata` (researched). Silvan Refugee (source flag errata=True): FAQ 1.9 lists NO Silvan Refugee erratum. Hall of Beorn shows no erratum and the same text; RingsDB says only that the card "has been errata'd" with no details, and a Hall of Beorn note that the card should have the Scout trait (the source data already lists Scout). Take the printed text: "Forced: After a character leaves play, discard Silvan Refugee from play." (Sorted: no wording change was found.). Not taken: A changed trigger. Basis: FAQ 1.9 (no entry); data/cards. Sources: https://hallofbeorn.com/Cards/Details/Silvan-Refugee, https://ringsdb.com/card/06037, https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/lotr_faq_19.pdf (read in full for "Silvan"/"Refugee": none).
- 06034:text, 06036:text, 06038:text, 06040:text, 06009:text, 06058:text, 06059:text, 06065:text (researched). "Printed resource icon" is the icon in the card's resource-icon space; a gained icon (Oin's, Ring of Barahir's Aragorn lore icon) does not count; an ally made a hero keeps its printed sphere icon. Not taken: A gained icon counts. Basis: rr.printed; FAQ 1.9: "When Oin (OtD 4) gains the tactics resource icon from his ability, does that count as the 'printed' tactics resource icon? A: No. The 'printed' resource icon always refers to the icon that appears in the Resource Icon space as identified on page 8 in the rulebook.". Sources: https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/lotr_faq_19.pdf.
- 05017:text, 06064:text, 06115:text (researched). Trap cards (Ranger Spikes, Ithilien Pit, Poisoned Stakes): an unattached trap attaches to the next enemy that can take an attachment, one that enters the staging area by any route including a reveal with ambush; Ranger Spikes then prevents the engagement checks (ambush engagement included); an enemy that cannot take attachments is skipped. Not taken: The trap waits only for a revealed enemy, or ambush still engages. Basis: FAQ 1.9: "If there is an unattached Ranger Spikes (HoN17) in the staging area when an enemy with the ambush keyword is revealed from the deck ... The Ranger Spikes will attach to that enemy, preventing the players from making the engagement checks for the ambush keyword.". Sources: https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/lotr_faq_19.pdf, https://ringsdb.com/card/05017 (commenters: any non-immune enemy, however it arrives; same direction).
- `06062:text` (recommended). Small Target: if the attacking enemy has several shadow cards, reveal them all and use the card only if none has a shadow effect. Not taken: Check one shadow card only. Basis: none official; one unsigned forum post: "You must resolve each shadow card dealt to the attacking enemy for that attack.". Sources: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/137443-small-target-with-multiple-shadow-cards (the one reply that sounds official has no named sender; the others disagree), https://ringsdb.com/card/06062.
- `06041:text` (recommended). White Tower Watchman: "belongs to the same sphere" holds when all your heroes share at least one sphere icon; a hero with no sphere (neutral) does not make it true. Not taken: Each hero has exactly one sphere and they equal. Basis: no span defines a hero "belongs to" sphere; faq.stand-and-fight says a neutral card belongs to no sphere. Sources: https://ringsdb.com/card/06041 (comments only discuss assigning undefended damage to an exhausted ally: allowed); no source covers dual-sphere or neutral heroes.
- 06059:text, 06087:text, 06109:text, 06142:text (recommended). The Record attachments (Book of Eldacar, Map of Earnil, Tome of Atanatar, Scroll of Isildur): "play ... as if it were in your hand" is a normal play, so pay the event's cost with a resource match; the attachment is discarded as the cost; the event goes to the bottom of the deck whether or not it resolved; only Action events qualify. Not taken: Play for free. Basis: card text; rr.resource-match. Sources: https://ringsdb.com/card/06059 (a reviewer notes only Action events qualify, a Response such as Foe-Hammer does not; nothing on cost); no official ruling found.
- `06035:text` (recommended). Mighty Prowess: the scan prints "Attach to a Tactics hero." (the "hero hero" is a source slip); "shares a Trait" is checked against the destroyed enemy's traits as it left play, and the other enemy must be in play. Not taken: Needs the destroyed enemy to still be in play. Basis: card text. Sources: none found; no source discusses it (lotrtcgdb/ringsdb pages carry no ruling).
