# The Ring-maker

The data, rulings and readings of the Ring-maker cycle, scenario by scenario. Product rules, the Domain and the Core round are in `docs/brief.md`; the authority order is in `.claude/rules/authority.md`.

The cycle of 2014: the deluxe expansion The Voice of Isengard (`VoI`, three scenarios) and its six Adventure Packs The Dunland Trap (`TDT`), The Three Trials (`TTT`), Trouble in Tharbad (`TiT`), The Nîn-in-Eilph (`NiE`), Celebrimbor's Secret (`CS`) and The Antlered Crown (`TAC`). Like Against the Shadow it is `kind: "cycle"` (`data/campaigns/the-ring-maker.json`): no campaign card, no booklet, no boon or burden and no Campaign Mode, so every game is standalone. Nightmare is not built: the Nightmare decks are cards, and their scenario blocks and ledger lines are later work. The cycle is data: `scripts/data/cycle-defs.ts` (its nine scenarios with the DragnCards decks `Q04.n` and `E04.n`), `scripts/data/campaign-defs.ts` (its seven products), `scripts/data/normalize/setup-the-ring-maker.ts` (the Setup sentences of the stage 1A cards the other readers do not read), and `scripts/data/the-ring-maker.test.ts` (its piles, counts and spans). The printed ledger is `data/printed/the-ring-maker.json`.

- **Data.** Each scenario's encounter sets are printed in the insert of The Voice of Isengard (`voi`) or the pack's rulesheet (`tdt`, `ttt`, `tit`, `nie`, `cs`, `tac`), as the span `rm.setup-<scenario id>` (`data/rules/index.json`). The piles are the DragnCards load lists' (Q04.1 to Q04.9, E04.1 to E04.9), checked against the stage 1A card's Setup. The DragnCards decks of the first scenario are labelled "Fords of Isen" (`deck.label`); the departures from the load lists are overrides in `data/overrides.json`, each with its reason and source, and are named in the scenario's file. The Voice of Isengard's own Hall of Beorn product name and the pack names are in `scripts/data/campaign-defs.ts`; DragnCards spells the pack "The Nin-in-Eilph", which `dragnPackAliases` maps to `NiE`.
- **Rules the cycle owns.** Time X (`rm.time-x`; the keyword of the Angmar Awakened insert is `mec108.time-x`, and the engine reads one keyword for both), Immune to player card effects (`rm.immune-to-player-card-effects`, `faq.immune-expanded`), "This ability does not stack" (`rm.does-not-stack`), Doomed X on player cards (`rm.doomed-x-player-cards`, `rr.doomed-x`), Searches X and the out-of-play deck (`rm.searches-x`, `rm.out-of-play-deck`, `rm.mugash-player-card-backs`), Hinder (`rm.hinder`), the Gríma objective-ally (`rm.grima-objective-ally`), Secrecy X (`rm.secrecy`, printed in three rulesheets), the current trial (`rm.current-trial`), advancing to parallel stages (`rm.parallel-stages`), the threat elimination level (`rm.threat-elimination-level`), attacks against Nalir (`rm.attacks-against-nalir`), Scour (`rm.scour`), The Orcs' Search (`rm.the-orcs-search`) and the Raven deck (`rm.raven-deck`).
- **FAQ spans the cycle adds.** `faq.saruman-doomed-lord-of-the-dead` and `faq.saruman-out-of-play-guarded` (the two answers of the FAQ about Saruman, VoI 3). The player card readings below use them.
- **Errata.** Blue Mountain Trader (`faq.errata.08006`) and Wingfoot (`faq.errata.08092`) are the two erratum spans the cycle holds. No card of a scenario of the cycle has an erratum. A card's printed line is read from its scan; where the scan prints the pre-errata text, the erratum span wins (`.claude/rules/authority.md`).
- **Shared encounter sets.** The first scenario that deals a set holds its readings; a later scenario reuses them. Dunland Warriors: The Fords of Isen, The Dunland Trap, The Antlered Crown. Dunland Raiders: The Fords of Isen, The Antlered Crown. Misty Mountain Orcs: To Catch an Orc, Trouble in Tharbad, Celebrimbor's Secret. Broken Lands: To Catch an Orc, Celebrimbor's Secret. Ancient Forest: Into Fangorn, The Three Trials. Weary Travelers: Into Fangorn, The Dunland Trap, The Nîn-in-Eilph (the insert of The Voice of Isengard spells it "Weary Travellers" once; the cards and the rulesheets spell it with one l). A card keeps one record in every scenario that deals it, so a fix in one scenario's reading moves the others' saved games.
- **Mechanics the engine has not read before.** Scour, Hinder, the out-of-play deck and Searches X, the threat elimination level, the "does not stack" clause, the Raven deck (a second deck of enemies beside the encounter deck, like the Orc deck of Angmar Awakened), the current trial, a random or chosen stage 2A (the quest deck of The Three Trials and The Nîn-in-Eilph) and cards placed underneath an objective. Each is built by the scenario that first deals it (`docs/agents/agent-brief.md`, Scenario procedure); none is built by the data.
- **Readings.** Each scenario file lists what its cards, rulesheet and sources leave open or settle, with sources (`.claude/rules/authority.md`, Open points). A reading is `span-settled` (a rule span or the printed line settles it), `researched` (an official online source settles it) or `recommended` (no official source settles it); each file's Readings to confirm lists every `recommended` reading and every one settled by community discussion alone. The research rows were written against source text, so the scenario's Read task confirms every line against the scan-read ledger.

## Player card readings

The player card readings use the same statuses. The keys are `<code>:<line>`, written from the scan-read ledger (`data/printed/player-cards.json`).

- `07001` to `07015`, `08003`, `08005`, `08007` (span-settled). A card with Doomed X raises every player's threat by X when it is played or put into play (`rm.doomed-x-player-cards`). Not taken: only the player of the card raises. Basis: `rm.doomed-x-player-cards`, `rr.doomed-x`.
- `07003:A:1` to `07003:A:3` (span-settled). Saruman under The Lord of the Dead (SoE 5): the effect of that card blanks his text box the moment he enters play, so his Doomed does not trigger. Out of play counts for the card guarding an objective: the objective is out of play too, so it cannot be claimed. Basis: `faq.saruman-doomed-lord-of-the-dead`, `faq.saruman-out-of-play-guarded`.
- `08005:A:1` (recommended). Close Call (Doomed X): the player chooses X when the Response resolves, up to the damage just dealt, and each player raises threat by that X. Not taken: X is fixed at the damage dealt. Basis: the card prints no value for X. Sources read: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/110715-close-call-value-of-x (players agree a partial cancel is allowed, "no official answer"), https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/96500-close-call (a designer answer about Beorn, none about X).
- `08003:A:1`, `08033:A:1`, `08061:A:1` (span-settled). The three events that return to the hand: the threat condition (20 or less) and "the first time you played a copy this round" are checked when the event resolves, as the text orders ("Then, if ... return this card to your hand instead of discarding it"). Basis: the printed line; `rm.secrecy` uses the same threshold for its own cost.
- Secrecy X (`08008:A:1`, `08026:A:1`, `08089:A:1`, `08034:A:2`) (span-settled). The cost is lower by X while the player's threat is 20 or below, only for a card played from the hand, and never below the printed cost. Basis: `rm.secrecy`.
- `08001:A:1`, `08112:A:2` (span-settled). Celeborn (2022 printing) and Galadriel (2022 printing) are the printings the scans show; Galadriel's scan prints "their threat" where the source text prints "his". Basis: scan 08112.A.
- Line numbers: Elven Mail (08029), Bow of the Galadhrim (08088) and Cloak of Lórien (08120) print the attach line and Restricted or the limit as one paragraph, so `A:1` is that paragraph and `A:2` is the stat line. Basis: the scans.
- `08006:errata`, `08092:errata` (span-settled). Blue Mountain Trader and Wingfoot read as their erratum spans say; the player card records already follow them. Basis: `faq.errata.08006`, `faq.errata.08092`.
