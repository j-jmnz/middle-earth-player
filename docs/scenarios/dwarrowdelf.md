# Dwarrowdelf

The rulings, readings and table pieces of Dwarrowdelf, scenario by scenario. Product rules, the Domain and the Core round are in `docs/brief.md`; the authority order is in `.claude/rules/authority.md`.

The second cycle (2012): the Khazad-dûm deluxe expansion (Into the Pit, The Seventh Level, Flight from Moria; pack `KD`) and the six Adventure Packs The Redhorn Gate, Road to Rivendell, The Watcher in the Water, The Long Dark, Foundations of Stone and Shadow and Flame (`TRG`, `RtR`, `WitW`, `TLD`, `FoS`, `SaF`). Like Shadows of Mirkwood it is `kind: "cycle"` (`data/campaigns/dwarrowdelf.json`): no campaign card, no booklet, no Campaign Mode, every game standalone on Easy, Normal and Nightmare (user decision, 2026-11-16, for the cycle's nine scenarios). It lists under Coming later until one scenario is playable (`src/campaign/playable.ts`). The cycle is data: `scripts/data/campaign-defs.ts` (its seven products and nine scenarios, with the DragnCards decks `Q02.n` and `E02.n`), `scripts/data/normalize/setup-cycles.ts` (one reader of Setup sentences for every cycle), and the folder of its id for authored effects and seed finders (`scripts/data/effects/authored-paths.ts`, `seed-finder-paths.ts`); `scripts/data/cycles.test.ts` holds what every cycle shares and `scripts/data/dwarrowdelf.test.ts` this one's piles, counts and Nightmare decks.

- **Data.** Each scenario file has no `campaignCard` and a `nightmare` block for decks 9010 to 9018 (`scripts/data/nightmare-defs.ts`). The rules inserts are pinned as rules docs with `dwd.*` spans: the Khazad-dûm insert (`kd`, MEC08) holds the three scenarios' encounter sets (`dwd.setup-<scenario id>`) and special rules, and each Adventure Pack's rulesheet (`trg`, `rtr`, `witw`, `tld`, `fos`, `saf`) its own; all have a text layer. The piles are the DragnCards load lists' (Q02.1 to Q02.9, E02.1 to E02.9), checked against the stage 1A card's printed Setup. Cave Torch (Into the Pit, The Long Dark, Foundations of Stone) and Book of Mazarbul are attached to a hero at Setup (`attachedChosen`, `data/overrides.json`, `scenarioPiles`); Arwen Undómiel is set aside until Setup puts her into play under the first player (`groups`); Foundations of Stone's own encounter set is its second encounter deck (`encounterDeck2`). The seven stage 2 cards of Flight from Moria (all titled Search for an Exit) and the four stage 4 cards of Foundations of Stone (Below the Mines) are dealt in no printed order, so the load list's order may differ from Hall of Beorn's within a stage number.
- **Nightmare removals.** A setup card's list names the quest cards it takes out of the quest deck too (Into the Pit's Goblin Patrol, Road to Rivendell's "Orc Outpost (quest stage 2)", Flight from Moria's Blocked by Shadow, a stage 2 card named by its side B title) and, for Foundations of Stone, cards of the set-aside encounter set: `nightmare.removed` carries them with the piles `quest` and `encounterDeck2` (`NIGHTMARE_PILES`). `questDeckCodes` (`src/catalog/scenario-data.ts`) applies the quest removals: a Nightmare quest card takes the place of the first stage the setup card removes, and one that removes none follows the stages. The Nightmare deck's cards with the Foundations of Stone set icon are in the `encounter` pile (the icon is on the picture, in no source).
- **Readings.** Each scenario adds a `###` subsection below for what its cards and rulesheet leave open, with its sources (`.claude/rules/authority.md`, Open points).
- Wrapped! (The Watcher in the Water, #76): Hall of Beorn gives it the code 04076, RingsDB's Háma of The Long Dark, so the build dropped it; its code is the six-digit 040076 (`data/overrides.json`, `productCodes`). Agent recommendation: no source gives another code.
- Encounter sets, The Long Dark and Foundations of Stone: the rulesheets list Hazards of the Pit for The Long Dark (Hall of Beorn and the DragnCards load list deal Deeps of Moria) and Deeps of Moria for Foundations of Stone (the lists deal Hazards of the Pit). The built piles follow the lists, which the printed Nightmare removals agree with (Stray Goblin and Abandoned Mine are Deeps of Moria's, Cave In is Hazards of the Pit's); agent recommendation, no official ruling found, not confirmed by the user.
- Printed over Hall of Beorn's text, read from the scans (`data/printed/dwarrowdelf.json`, `nightmare.json`): lines Hall of Beorn runs together are split; the Nightmare Shadow and Flame setup card names the Shadow and Flame scenario (Hall of Beorn: Foundations of Stone); Trapdoor Spider's Forced line and Chill of the Roots' Shadow line are on the cards and not in Hall of Beorn's text; Foundations of Stone's setup card prints "Mitril Lode" for Mithril Lode.

## Into the Pit

Dwarrowdelf part 1 (pack `KD`), standalone only, on Easy, Normal and Nightmare (deck 9010). Encounter sets Into the Pit, Twists and Turns, Hazards of the Pit and Goblins of the Deep (`dwd.setup-into-the-pit`); First Hall and Bridge of Khazad-dûm are set aside out of play and join the staging area through East-gate's and First Hall's Forced lines (`dwd.setup-into-the-pit-set-aside`). Six cards print the text of The Caves of Nibin-Dûm's (Cave Torch 03041 = 41029, Lightless Passage 03037 = 41033, Branching Paths 03036 = 41034, Crumbling Ruin 03049 = 41038, Watchful Eyes 03062 = 41040, Goblin Tunnels 03063 = 41032) and take its readings (Mirkwood Paths part 5 and The Dark of Mirkwood part 2 above); a record that holds its own card code (Cave Torch's Action) is written again for the new code.

- Goblin Patrol (03065:B:2), the Forced discard: it is a replacement effect (`rr.replacement-effects`). The enemy's reveal resolves first and the discard takes the place of the staging step only when the enemy would be added to the staging area, so a Goblin Follower's "engages the last player" resolves and the Follower is not discarded; only a Follower whose When Revealed is canceled is discarded. Enemies that Goblin Patrol's own search, Cave Torch's Forced or a Goblin Swordsman's Shadow put into the staging area are not "revealed from the encounter deck" and stay; a card Watchful Eyes reveals is, and an enemy it reveals is discarded. FFG's Caleb answer, relayed on https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/100928-goblin-patrol-question (the replacement reading), with https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/71122-khazad-dum-goblin-patrol-questions and https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/58639-khazad-d%C3%BBm-card-questions/page/2 for the added enemies and `dwd.revealing-enemies` for shadow enemies (researched; the official answer is only relayed in a forum thread).
- Goblin Patrol (03065:B:1): each player searches for 1 enemy in player order, and the first player names which player's choice is Patrol Leader (`rr.first-player`: the first player selects among eligible options); with one player it is that player's choice; with no Patrol Leader in the deck or discard pile the choices are free. Searching the deck and discard pile is one search for one card (https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/59985-khazad-d%C3%BBm-patrol-leader-goblin-patrol-questions) and shuffles the deck (agent recommendation for who takes Patrol Leader; no source found).
- Goblin Patrol (03065:B:3): "no enemies in play" counts the staging area and every engaged enemy; it is checked whenever no effect is resolving, so it cannot fire before the stage's own When Revealed has added the searched enemies. Placing 11 progress also advances (agent recommendation).
- Cave Torch (03041:A:1): the Setup's attach follows `rr.restricted` like any attach. A hero that already holds 2 Restricted attachments discards one of the three at once, the choice of the hero's controller (`docs/brief.md`, Keywords implemented once).
- Patrol Leader (03014:A:1): the Forced fires for each separate damage event dealt to it (the attack's combined damage once, each response or event separately; https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/59985-khazad-d%C3%BBm-patrol-leader-goblin-patrol-questions, community discussion, not an official ruling). With the encounter deck empty nothing is discarded and the damage is not canceled: the discard pile is shuffled back only when a card is revealed in the quest phase (`rr.running-out-of-cards`; agent recommendation). The Shadow (03014:A:2) cancels the damage dealt to the attacker for the whole attack and only damage dealt in it (https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/58639-khazad-d%C3%BBm-card-questions, community).
- East-gate (03016): `dwd.immune-to-card-effects` settles it: no card effect can target or change it, it stays the active location until explored and only questing places progress on it. So Cave In and Twisting Shadows leave its progress, Dreadful Gap's "return it to the staging area" does nothing, and Cave Torch cannot be aimed at it (it is not a Dark location anyway). Its A:2 stops engagement checks and optional engagement; an enemy that engages through its own text (Goblin Follower) still engages; combat goes on (https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/02/07/into-the-pit/). Explored, East-gate and Bridge of Khazad-dûm (Victory 1 and 2) go to the victory display, so Many Roads never reshuffles them.
- Dreadful Gap (03052) with East-gate active: the travel to Dreadful Gap cannot happen (travel needs no active location and East-gate stays), so Dreadful Gap stays in the staging area; X is every character in play, heroes and allies of every player, exhausted ones too (agent recommendation; https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/92279-question-about-east-gate says only that Dreadful Gap cannot move East-gate, no source says what the Gap does).
- Cave In (03048:A:1): it gains surge when the effect removed no progress token from the quest card and from the active location together; East-gate's tokens do not count (`dwd.immune-to-card-effects`; https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/66530-cave-in-vs-east-gate, community, unsettled there).
- Bridge of Khazad-dûm (03018:A:1): "cannot play cards" bars playing a card from a hand (events, allies, attachments, as a response too) for every player; putting a card into play by an ability, using an attached card's Action and a card already in play are not playing (https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/64726-bridge-of-khazad-dum, https://visionofthepalantir.com/2025/11/24/qotw-into-the-pit; community).
- Zigil Mineshaft (03038:A:1): any player may trigger it at any action window while it is in the staging area or the active location (`dwd.encounter-card-actions`; https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/99603-zigil-mineshaft-question, community); the threat raise is a cost each activation.
- Burning Low (03040:A:1): the cards that get the Threat are the enemies and locations in the staging area when it resolves; whether a location is Dark is read again while the effect lasts (`faq.lasting-effects`: an encounter card's lasting effect is recalculated). A player who controls a ready Cave Torch may exhaust it before the effect applies, which cancels the card's whole When Revealed (https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/02/07/into-the-pit/; the timing is an agent recommendation); the Torch's Forced fires as for any exhaust, and an enemy it adds is not in the staging area when the effect resolves.
- Fouled Well (03053:A:1): each player in player order chooses to discard one random card or not; it gains surge when any player did not discard one, a player with an empty hand included (the literal "all players did not discard" could mean none discarded; the forum players and a designer answer relayed in https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/291017-how-do-you-play-fouled-well and https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/62926-fouled-well read it as "not every player discarded"; community, designer answer secondhand).
- Sudden Pitfall (03051): the discarded character is a destroy for elimination (as Nazgûl of Dol Guldur's discard, above), so a seat with no hero left is eliminated; "cannot be canceled" bars every cancel of the When Revealed (https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/107415-sudden-pitfall-is-really-annoying; agent recommendation for the elimination). Its Shadow does nothing when the attack is undefended; Dark and Dreadful's Shadow deals its damage to the defending character and gives +2 Attack instead when there is none.
- Goblin Follower (03035): "the last player" is the player to the first player's right, and the only player in a solo game (`dwd.last-player`); the Shadow's +2 applies when the attacked player is the last player. Goblin Scout (03060): a player with threat 25 or higher cannot optionally engage it, but an engagement check at its cost still engages him (`rr.optional-engagement`; https://visionofthepalantir.com/2025/11/24/qotw-into-the-pit).
- Cave Torch (03041) leaves play: it is removed from the game, not discarded, whether by an effect or because its hero left play (`dwd.cave-torch`); no printed line says so, so the Setup record of Entering the Mines carries it. Cave Torch and Lightless Passage's "a Cave Torch" match the card of this box (the title is also The Dark of Mirkwood's 41029; the two never share a game).
- Easy mode: the Easy counts of `data/scenarios/into-the-pit.json` only (Crumbling Ruin, Sudden Pitfall, Watchful Eyes and Dreadful Gap are not dealt).
- Nightmare setup card (901001:A:2): each exhaust of the Cave Torch puts 1 damage token on the setup card (shown as the Torch dial of the top bar, `readouts/dwarrowdelf/pit.ts`, its limit 5 warming and a warning at 4, `docs/design-system-table.md`, Cards, the Into the Pit entry); at the 5th the Torch is removed from the game. The setup card's Forced, Patrol Sentry's Forced and the Torch's own Forced all hear the same exhaust: the first player orders them (`faq.simultaneous-effects`) and all fire, the removal not stopping the others (`faq.triggering-forced-effects`; agent recommendation). Without a Torch Lightless Passage cannot be traveled to, Burning Low cannot be canceled and Narrow Fissure takes progress (https://visionofthepalantir.com/2021/01/17/nightmare-into-the-pit/, an article by a player, not a ruling).
- Goblin Ambush (901002, stage 2 in place of Goblin Patrol: the build removes Goblin Patrol (`nightmare.removed`, pile `quest`) and `questDeckCodes` deals it from `nightmare.questCards` in its place; its quest points X come from an override and its record): its A side's When Revealed searches the deck and discard pile for 1 plus the number of players Orc enemies (Shadow Dweller is a Creature and cannot be chosen); the first player chooses them all; Patrol Sentry and Patrol Leader are among them unless a copy is not in the deck or discard pile, and other Orcs fill the places (https://visionofthepalantir.com/2021/01/17/nightmare-into-the-pit/). Its quest points are X, 1 plus the number of players. "After an enemy is defeated" is an enemy destroyed by damage, any enemy of any player in play (not a discard or a shuffle back: Put Off Pursuit does not count, same article; agent recommendation from `rr.destroy`); the only progress on it is that token.
- Patrol Sentry (901003:A:1): after a Cave Torch exhausts it attacks the player it is engaged with, an immediate attack with a shadow card (`faq.enemy-attacks-outside-combat`); a Sentry in the staging area does nothing. Shadow Dweller (901005:A:2): "while Cave Torch is in play" is any Cave Torch in play, ready or exhausted; it makes no engagement check, and a player may still engage it optionally. Goblin Lurker (901004): the engagement cost never drops below 0. Twisting Shadows (901006), Narrow Fissure (901007) and Shadow-fall (901008): "in play" counts the staging area and the active location; Shadow-fall's Dark trait counts for every Dark text; the first player chooses the non-Dark location it attaches to, and with none it is discarded (it has surge); Narrow Fissure's bar is read while the Torch is ready at the moment the progress would be placed, so a Torch exhausted to place progress does not stop it (https://visionofthepalantir.com/2021/01/17/nightmare-into-the-pit/; agent recommendation for the exhausted case).

## The Seventh Level

Dwarrowdelf part 2 (pack `KD`), standalone only, on Easy, Normal and Nightmare (deck 9011). Encounter sets The Seventh Level, Plundering Goblins and Goblins of the Deep (`dwd.setup-the-seventh-level`); no set-aside set. Four cards print the text of earlier ones and take their readings: Goblin Scout 03060, Goblin Swordsman 03061, Watchful Eyes 03062 and Goblin Tunnels 03063 are Into the Pit's records; Goblin Spearman (03031) prints Goblin Swordsman's two lines (03061:A:1 and A:2) and takes the same records; the quest's 1B When Revealed (03067:B:1) is Entering the Mines' 03064:B:1.

- Cave-troll (03020:A:1): each excess point of the combat damage its attack deals (damage beyond the remaining hit points of the character it damages, read before the damage lands as for Hill Troll, `rr.damage`) is its own choice: the defending player damages 1 other character he controls, hero or ally, any point on any character but the one the attack damaged; a point that kills a character does not stop the next, and with no other character the excess is lost. Damage a shadow effect deals is not combat damage from the attack. FFG's answer, quoted second hand on https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/59164-cave-troll-damage, is that the overflow is assigned "point by point" and continues after a kill (researched; the official answer is only relayed in a forum thread; "you" is the defending player, agent recommendation).
- Orc Horn Blower (03021:A:2), Search for the Chamber (03067:B:1): a card revealed by an effect is revealed in full (`rr.reveal`): its staging resolves, so a treachery resolves its When Revealed and is discarded, a location or enemy is added to the staging area, and Surge, Doomed and the Forced effects that hear a reveal (Goblin Archer, Goblin Tunnels) all apply (span-settled).
- Hidden Threat (03022:A:1): the enemies in the staging area are counted once, as the effect resolves, and each player raises his threat by that number; then the last player (`dwd.last-player`, the only player in a solo game) discards 1 attachment he controls, of his choice, whatever it is attached to. With no enemy in the staging area the raise is by 0 and the last player still discards: `rr.then` asks that the part before "then" resolve in full, and raising threat by 0 is a complete resolution, not an effect that cannot be carried out (recommended, not user-confirmed; no official ruling found, the forum threads https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/194422-a-couple-of-questions/ and https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/240298-terrible-to-behold-question only say that "then" needs its first part resolved and that an amount may be 0). (span-settled; Vision of the Palantir's article https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/02/10/the-seventh-level/ counts it as one, a fan reading that the card and the Rules Reference do not support).
- Book of Mazarbul (03024, set up by 03067:A:1): the first player attaches it to a hero of any player, free, with no exhaust (community consensus, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/255370-seventh-level-setup-and-book-of-mazarbul; "any hero" as in Entering the Mines, agent recommendation; the KD rulebook says the first player selects any hero for Cave Torch, same phrase). The Action is usable by any player while the Book is in play, in the staging area or attached; `dwd.book-of-mazarbul` says a hero can exhaust to claim it even when it is attached to another hero, which moves it (the 2019 forum thread https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/296876-claiming-objectives-book-of-marzabul says no; the booklet wins). Detached, or with its hero out of play, it returns to the staging area; stage 2B's When Revealed removes it from the game from wherever it is, and its hero is free of it (span-settled). The attached hero still commits to the quest, exhausted by nothing, and may still defend; it cannot declare attacks, ranged attacks included. Restricted counts as for any attachment.
- Chieftain of the Pit (03030): the +3 Attack lasts until the end of the round it is revealed. Its Shadow attacks again once this attack ends, against the same player, after the shadow cards already dealt are discarded and a new one is dealt (`faq.additional-attacks`; the record is `ImmediateAttack` after this attack, as 10135:A:2 and 41021:A:2). Goblin Spearman's and Goblin Swordsman's Shadow add the card to the staging area; an enemy dealt as a shadow card is not revealed (`dwd.revealing-enemies`), so Goblin Archer and Fate of Balin do not hear it.
- Goblin Archer (03032): A:1 is as Goblin Scout's bar; an engagement check at its cost engages it. A:2: characters with the ranged keyword of any player may be declared as attackers against it in the staging area during the player attacks of the combat phase, with the usual one attack per seat; characters without ranged cannot (agent recommendation; no source says who declares it; the Vision of the Palantir article only notes the pings). A:3: it hears every enemy revealed from the encounter deck while it is in play, in the staging area or engaged, itself and an enemy revealed by Fate of Balin or Beats and Echoes included, once per Archer; the first player chooses the character, hero or ally (agent recommendation that it hears itself; https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/240696-how-to-get-rid-of-those-pesky-goblin-archers says only that a shadow enemy is not revealed).
- Undisturbed Bones (03033): each player in player order counts the allies he controls once, then puts all that many damage on 1 ally of his choice; with no ally nothing happens. Its Shadow discards the defending ally from play (a discard, not a destroy); an undefended attack has no defending character (literal).
- Plundered Armoury (03034): A:1 gives every enemy in play, in the staging area or engaged, +1 Attack while the Armoury is in the staging area, not as the active location; two copies give +2 (literal). A:2: after it is explored and leaves play each player in order may attach 1 Weapon or Armour attachment from his hand to 1 character he controls, a hero or an ally the attachment may be attached to (`rr.attachment-cards`): the attachment is put into play, not played, so it costs nothing (a community reading, https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/02/10/the-seventh-level/; researched, no official source).
- Fate of Balin (03068:B:1, B:2, B:3) and Beats and Echoes (901110:A:1): X cards are revealed one at a time from the top of the encounter deck; an enemy is revealed as in the staging step (its When Revealed resolves, Surge and Doomed apply, the Forced effects of a reveal fire) and goes to the staging area; every other card is discarded unresolved, with no When Revealed, no Surge and no Doomed (the card carves out only "the other revealed cards", so the enemies resolve; agent recommendation, no source found: https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1448313/questions-about-the-seventh-level could not be read). The Forced on 2B fires at the end of the staging step of each round the stage is current, not in the round it is revealed (`faq.triggering-forced-effects`); an enemy dealt as a shadow card does not trigger it (`dwd.revealing-enemies`). When the deck runs out partway through X the discard pile, the cards just discarded included, is shuffled back and the revealing goes on (`rr.running-out-of-cards`; agent recommendation). X of Beats and Echoes is 2 plus the number of living players with no Goblin enemy engaged, counted when it resolves, before the reveals. 2B's last line is the win; in Nightmare Overseer Maurûl bars it.
- Nightmare setup card (901101): Setup A:2 takes Overseer Maurûl and Chamber of Records out of the encounter deck, where the Nightmare deck's pile puts them, and sets them aside. A:3 fires when stage 2B is revealed beside 2B's own When Revealed (`faq.simultaneous-effects`; neither changes the other): Chamber of Records becomes the active location without a Travel (the current active location returns to the staging area with its progress, as `MakeSetAsideActive` does), Overseer Maurûl joins the staging area, and every Balin's Doom in the encounter discard pile is shuffled into the encounter deck, which is shuffled whether or not one was there (agent recommendation for the empty case). B:11 and B:12 print the "does not stack" rule: Goblin Skirmisher's and Western Hall's first lines count once however many copies of their own title are in play, and go on while one copy remains; the two cards' lines and every other source of Attack or Threat stack with each other (printed on the setup card; literal).
- Overseer Maurûl (901102): he is not a Goblin, so no card adds resources to him, Orc Taskmaster does not protect him and he is not counted in X; X is 0 with no Goblin enemy in play. His Victory 5 puts him in the victory display when destroyed; "cannot win the game unless" is a bar on the stage's advance that falls when he is in the victory display (Bag End's `Restrict advance`; progress keeps going on 2B, `faq.progress-without-advancing`; as Amon Forn's bar above). Nightmare article: https://visionofthepalantir.com/2021/03/21/nightmare-the-seventh-level (a player's article, not a ruling).
- Chamber of Records (901103): immune to player card effects (the printed keyword); each Goblin enemy with 1 or more resources is immune to player events, read live (the flag `immuneToPlayerEvents` of the Raiders' Shattered Monument; other player cards still reach it); its Forced adds 2 resources to a Goblin enemy as it engages a player, by an engagement check, an optional engagement or an effect (literal).
- Orc Taskmaster (901104): its Forced hears damage dealt to any Goblin enemy in play with at least 1 resource when the damage is dealt: that Goblin loses 1 resource and all that damage is canceled before it lands, so a Goblin the damage would destroy lives. It fires for each separate damage event, as Patrol Leader does (an attack's combined damage once, each event of a card separately; https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/59985-khazad-d%C3%BBm-patrol-leader-goblin-patrol-questions, community). With 2 Taskmasters in play both Forced effects were triggered by the same damage, so both resolve, each removing a resource (`faq.triggering-forced-effects`, `faq.simultaneous-effects`; agent recommendation, no source). A Taskmaster protects Goblins only: not itself, not Overseer Maurûl, not a Cave-troll.
- Resources on Goblins (901105, 901106, 901107, 901108, 901109): "Goblin enemy in play" is the staging area and every player's engaged enemies. Goblin Skirmisher's Shadow deals X damage to the defending character, X the resources on the attacking enemy as the Shadow resolves (nothing on an undefended attack or with none). Pit Goblin's and Balin's Doom's Shadow add 1 resource to the attacking enemy if it is a Goblin; Deep-Mines' Shadow gives +1 Attack and, to a Goblin, deals it another shadow card for this attack. Deep-Mines' Forced (901107:A:1) hears a Goblin entering play, by a reveal, an effect or a Shadow that adds it to the staging area, while Deep-Mines is in play; the resource comes only if it is in the staging area when the Forced resolves, not as the active location. Western Hall's Travel adds its resource when it is traveled to.
- Table: Book of Mazarbul (created by 03067:A:1) is a held card drawn under its host in every zone the host can be in, and the Nightmare resource tokens on Goblins are the ordinary enemy counters; no tracker, zone or readout is created by a line here (`docs/design-system-table.md`, Cards, The Seventh Level).
- Easy mode: the Easy counts of `data/scenarios/the-seventh-level.json` only (Orc Horn Blower, Chieftain of the Pit and Watchful Eyes are not dealt; Cave-troll and Undisturbed Bones have fewer copies).

## Flight from Moria

Dwarrowdelf part 3 (pack `KD`), standalone only, on Easy, Normal and Nightmare (deck 9012). Encounter sets Flight from Moria, Hazards of the Pit, Deeps of Moria and Plundering Goblins (`dwd.setup-flight-from-moria`); the other sets' cards are Into the Pit's and The Seventh Level's records. The quest deck is the Mount Gundabad shape (`dwd.creating-the-quest-deck`, `dwd.bypassing-a-quest-card`, as `emce.gundabad-quest-deck` and `emce.bypassing`): the stage 2s are shuffled with side A up beneath stage 1, there is no stage 3, and a stage 2B shows only from the beginning of the staging step. Official sources read for the whole scenario: the FAQ (`faq.search-effects` search shuffles the deck, `faq.victory-display`, `faq.reveal-and-add`, `faq.cancel-encounter-effect` and `faq.cancel-encounter-card` reveal and cancel, `faq.immune-expanded` immune to player card effects; official), https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/02/16/flight-from-moria/ and https://visionofthepalantir.com/2021/05/15/nightmare-flight-from-moria (player articles, not rulings).

## The Redhorn Gate

Dwarrowdelf part 4 (pack `TRG`), standalone only, on Easy, Normal and Nightmare (deck 9013). Encounter sets The Redhorn Gate and The Misty Mountains, the latter from the Khazad-dûm box (`dwd.setup-the-redhorn-gate`); Snowstorm (5 copies) and Arwen Undómiel are set aside, and Caradhras starts in the staging area (`dwd.setup-the-redhorn-gate-set-aside`). The rulesheet's `dwd.ally-objective-arwen` and `dwd.caradhras` say what Arwen and Caradhras are. The quest deck is the plain three stages, in the Nightmare deck as well (the setup card removes no stage). Every Misty Mountains card is new to the app (Black Uruks, Mountain Warg, Bitter Wind, Knees of the Mountain, Turbulent Waters, Warg Lair): none was dealt by Into the Pit, The Seventh Level or Flight from Moria.

- Setup (04011:A:1) and Arwen (04014): Caradhras is placed by the pile; Snowstorm is set aside by the pile (Easy deals the same 5). - Ambush (04049, 04050, 04052, 901403 to 901405; `dwd.ambush`): a new printed keyword. When an enemy with it enters play, each player in turn, the first player first, makes an engagement check against that enemy alone (`rr.engagement-check`: threat at least its engagement cost); the first player to engage it ends the checks, and an enemy nobody engages stays in the staging area. The enemy has entered the staging area before the checks, so an attachment that says "enters the staging area" and attaches itself to it (Ranger Spikes) stops them (researched: the FAQ answer quoted at https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/79049-question-about-ambush%E2%80%A6). An engagement made this way is an engagement for every "after it engages" Forced line. Recommended: an enemy put into play already engaged with a player makes no ambush check (the checks look only at the staging area; the FFG forum split, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/193908-ambush-keyword-and-dunedain-hunter; no official ruling). Only a reveal from the encounter deck counts (not a search or a return). The round's first reveal is counted whether or not Goblin Gate was active then, as 09069:B:1's tally (Reading 11 of The Weather Hills), so an enemy revealed before it became active leaves no first enemy that round (researched, community: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/108999-few-questions-about-rules, the ability is not active until after an enemy is revealed). - Barren Hills (04045:A:1): while active the ambush keyword has no effect, printed or gained, so no ambush check is made and Goblin Gate's immediate attack cannot follow; the enemies stay in the staging area with their threat (literal; https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/02/24/road-to-rivendell/ reads it so). Nightmare's Treacherous Bluffs and the setup card's threat line need an engagement, so they do not fire either.

## The Long Dark

Dwarrowdelf part 7 (pack `TLD`), standalone only, on Easy, Normal and Nightmare (deck 9016). Encounter sets The Long Dark, Twists and Turns and Deeps of Moria (the rulesheet names Hazards of the Pit for the third, `dwd.setup-the-long-dark`; the load lists and the Nightmare removals deal Deeps of Moria, see Dwarrowdelf above); the Twists and Turns and Deeps of Moria cards already have records (Into the Pit, Flight from Moria). Cave Torch (03041) is Into the Pit's card with its records. Stages: Journey in the Black Pit (04086), Continuing Eastward (04087); every difficulty deals both (the Nightmare setup card removes and adds no quest card). Sources for every reading below: the rules spans cited, and the community pages listed with each; no FFG or Hall of Beorn ruling on The Long Dark was found, and the two Long Dark threads in the FFG forum archive (https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/63845-lost-in-the-long-dark, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/86489-long-dark) hold the pack's announcement and player opinions only.

- Cave Torch and Durin's Greaves, the Setup and the reveal (04086:A:1, 04088:A:1): "a hero of his choice" is any hero of any player the first player picks (`dwd.cave-torch-the-long-dark`: the first player selects any hero; the Into the Pit and Seventh Level readings for the Torch and the Book of Mazarbul); Durin's Greaves, printed with the same words, is read the same way (agent recommendation: a fan article reads "a hero he controls", https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/02/28/the-long-dark/, no ruling). The Torch is removed from the game if it would leave play (`dwd.cave-torch-the-long-dark`, as in Into the Pit); Durin's Greaves attached to a hero that leaves play is discarded to the encounter discard pile (`rr.attachment-cards`) and can be revealed again. Status: span-settled for the Torch, recommended for Greaves.
- Lost effects (`dwd.lost-effects`): a "Lost:" line is an ability only other card effects trigger, "trigger all Lost: effects in play" runs the Lost line of every faceup card in play (`rr.in-play-and-out-of-play`): locations in the staging area and the active location, enemies in the staging area and engaged, attachments that carry one (Lost and Helpless), and the Nightmare setup card (it "remains active throughout the game"; agent recommendation, a fan article treats its Lost as in play, https://visionofthepalantir.com/2021/10/19/nightmare-the-long-dark/). A shadow card is not in play (`rr.shadow-effects`), so Goblin Warlord's Shadow triggers the Lost effects of the other cards and the Warlord's own Lost only when a Warlord copy is in play. Each copy of a card is its own Lost effect and each triggers. The set is fixed when the trigger happens; the first player orders the effects (`rr.first-player`, `faq.simultaneous-effects`) and each resolves in full before the next (`rr.forced`): when more than one Lost effect fires, he is offered the next effect to resolve one at a time (a card in the choice is a card in play, listed with its owner or place); one effect, or copies of one card, need no choice and go oldest first. Nothing in play with a Lost effect: nothing happens. Status: span-settled except the setup card and the fixed set (recommended; community pages above, no ruling).
- Locate test (`dwd.locate-test`, `dwd.locate-test-example`): made by one player, the one the card names; he may discard 1 card from his hand to discard the top card of the encounter deck; a card with PASS printed in its text box passes the test, and then no ill effects trigger; any other card lets him try again, and the test is failed when he runs out of cards or will not discard more (he may fail it at once by discarding none, the example in the span). The cards go to their discard piles one at a time and the test's own discards stay in the encounter discard pile. PASS counts when it is printed in the card's own text box (the "PASS." line of the ledger: Abandoned Mine, Dwarven Forge, Silent Caverns, Twisting Passage, Foul Air, Gathering Ground, and the Nightmare Goblin Stalker and Trapdoor Spider), whatever the card's type or where it was dealt from. The test is not a quest test or a Fortitude test: no action window between tries. Status: span-settled. Empty encounter deck: the deck is refilled from the discard pile in the quest phase, the test's own discards included, so a card may be discarded twice (as the Fortitude test, `mec113.fortitude-tests`, `rr.running-out-of-cards`, `faq.last-encounter-card`); outside the quest phase it stays empty, no card can be discarded and the test is failed at once. Agent recommendation, no source found (the extended search for a ruling on a locate test with an empty deck found only the general reset rule; https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/287372-guarded-keyword-and-empty-encounter-deck/ is about Guarded). A Nightmare card that makes a test without naming the player (Dreadful Deep's Shadow) makes it by the defending player ("you", `rr.defending-player`; the fan article reads it the same way).
- Journey in the Black Pit (04086:B:1): X is the number of players less one, at least 1 (1 and 2 players: 1 location, 3 players: 2, 4 players: 3). Cards are discarded from the top until X locations are in the discard pile; those locations go to the staging area (not revealed: no When Revealed, no Travel, no Surge) and the other cards the effect discarded are shuffled back with the encounter deck, and nothing else in the discard pile is (the pile holds only what this effect discarded: the stage is revealed at the setup). Literal; source for the amounts https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/02/28/the-long-dark/. B:2: each location in play gets +1 Threat while this stage is the current stage (staging area and active location, Nightmare locations too); if the players quest unsuccessfully (total Willpower less than the staging area's Threat, the existing `questFailed`) the Lost effects trigger after the quest resolves (https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/02/28/the-long-dark/). Status: literal.
- Continuing Eastward (04087:B:1): "Then, trigger all Lost: effects in play" is read as part of the failure: a passed locate test triggers no ill effects (`dwd.locate-test`), so a passed test reveals nothing and triggers no Lost effect; a failed one reveals as many cards as there are players, each resolved in full (`rr.reveal`: staging, When Revealed and keywords, as 04063:B:1), and then triggers the Lost effects. Alternative: the Lost effects trigger whether or not the test is passed; one player-site transcription drops the reveal sentence and reads that way. Status: researched, community: https://lotrlcgblog.wordpress.com/2020/02/09/the-long-dark-quest/ (reads it as failure only, no ruling), https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/02/28/the-long-dark/. The first player makes the test and the reveals. B:2: as stage 1B. B:3: the stage defeated wins the game at once (as the last stage of the other scenarios of the cycle); stage 2 has no "+1 Threat".
- Twisting Passage (04092:A:1): a Forced before progress is placed on it: the first player makes a locate test each time any amount of progress would be placed on it (one test for a quest phase's progress, one for each Cave Torch Action, one for each other effect; https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/02/28/the-long-dark/). A failed test places no progress on it and triggers the Lost effects; the progress is not placed anywhere else, the quest included (the progress "that would be placed on the current quest" is placed on the active location instead, `rr.active-location`, and none is placed on it). A passed test places it all, and what does not fit on the location goes on to the current quest in any phase (the quest phase, a Cave Torch Action, any card effect: `rr.active-location`, as the overflow of an explored active location does). Agent recommendation for the progress that is lost on a failed test, the quest's share included (alternative: the amount goes to the main quest); the test is made again each time, also while it is a staging location a card effect aims at. Status: recommended (Readings to confirm, at the end of this file).
- Gathering Ground (04095:A:1): it is attached to the location in the staging area with the highest sum of its current Threat and its remaining quest points (quest points less the progress on it, both as the cards stand with modifiers); on a tie the first player chooses (`rr.first-player`); with no location in the staging area it cannot attach and is discarded (`rr.attachment-cards`). It counts as a Condition attachment whose text is "Each enemy revealed from the encounter deck gains surge": it works while the host is in play, in the staging area or active, and an enemy that is not revealed (dealt as a shadow card, added by an effect: Cave Torch, Abandoned Mine, Goblin Sneak's shadow) gains nothing (`dwd.revealing-enemies`). Status: span-settled for the tie, no target and the revealing; recommended for "current" values and "remaining" (agent recommendation; the fan article gives only that the card does nothing with no location in play).
- Locate-test cards: Foul Air (04094:A:1) deals 2 damage to every character in play, the heroes and allies of every player, then the Lost effects; Vast and Intricate (04096:A:1) raises each player's threat by 7, removes every progress token from play (the current quest, the active location, the staging area's locations and any side quest, a player card's included) and then triggers the Lost effects; each only when the first player fails the test. Literal.
- Abandoned Mine (04089:A:1): the top 2 Goblin enemies are the two most recently discarded Goblin enemies of the encounter discard pile (a Goblin is an enemy with the trait, skipping other cards); they are returned, not revealed (no When Revealed, no Ambush, no Surge); fewer than two are returned if fewer are there ("if able"). Dwarven Forge (04090:A:1): each player in player order chooses and discards 1 card from his hand, none with an empty hand. Silent Caverns (04091:A:1): every character in play, of every player, is exhausted. Goblin Warlord (04099:A:1): each player chooses and discards 1 ally he controls from play (a discard, not a destroy), none with no ally. Status: literal.
- Fatigue (04093:A:1, A:2): each player in order exhausts one ready character he controls ("if able"); once all have, Fatigue gains surge if any player controls no unexhausted character (any player, not every player). The Shadow: the defending player exhausts one character he controls, chosen after the defender was declared (the defender is already exhausted unless it was a Sentinel). Literal.
- Cave Spider (04097:A:1, A:2): the first player draws 1 card and then chooses and discards 4 cards from his hand (all of it with fewer); "Then" means the discard resolves only if the draw did (`rr.then`: with an empty deck, or a Sentinel of the Deep's bar on the draw, nothing is discarded); the Forced fires for each engagement of the Spider with a player, by an engagement check, a card effect or an optional engagement, and that player discards 1 card. Literal.

- Durin's Bane (04150:A:1, A:2): Regenerate 3 and Indestructible are keywords (no record). "Players cannot play attachments on Durin's Bane" is the flag `noPlayerAttachments` ("Cannot have player attachments"); encounter cards still attach to it (Counter-Spell, Fiery Sword, Many Thonged Whip). It stays in the staging area (`cannotLeaveStaging`, which only Dark Pit's discard overrides, below), adds its Threat 4 to the staging area (the rulesheet says it adds its threat and attacks), and is considered engaged with each player whose threat is 1 or greater (the Watcher's and Thaurdir's `engagedWithAll`, gated on the seat's threat, read live): each such player resolves one attack by it among his enemy attacks of the combat phase, in the order he chooses, and may attack it as an engaged enemy (a Ranged character of another player may join, nobody else pools). Status: span-settled (`dwd.durins-bane`: attacks in each player's normal attack resolution step, no shadow card for a player raised during combat). "Remaining hit points" is 27 minus damage, floored at 0; "has 0 hit points" (04139:B:2) is remaining 0, checked after each damage event and not only at the end of a phase, and the stage advances at once. Status: researched (official, FAQ 3.057); the check timing is recommended (the card says "immediately").

With one player none. A player at threat 0 is not engaged with Durin's Bane until his threat is raised (the Refresh phase raises it to 1, so the first round's combat has no regular attack by it: https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/03/10/shadow-and-flame/ says the same). Status: literal.
