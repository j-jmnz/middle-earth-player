# Into the Pit

Dwarrowdelf part 1 (pack `KD`), standalone only, on Easy, Normal and Nightmare (deck 9010). Encounter sets Into the Pit, Twists and Turns, Hazards of the Pit and Goblins of the Deep (`dwd.setup-into-the-pit`); First Hall and Bridge of Khazad-dûm are set aside out of play and join the staging area through East-gate's and First Hall's Forced lines (`dwd.setup-into-the-pit-set-aside`). Six cards print the text of The Caves of Nibin-Dûm's (Cave Torch 03041 = 41029, Lightless Passage 03037 = 41033, Branching Paths 03036 = 41034, Crumbling Ruin 03049 = 41038, Watchful Eyes 03062 = 41040, Goblin Tunnels 03063 = 41032) and take its readings (Mirkwood Paths part 5 and The Dark of Mirkwood part 2 above); a record that holds its own card code (Cave Torch's Action) is written again for the new code.

- Goblin Patrol (03065:B:2), the Forced discard: it is a replacement effect (`rr.replacement-effects`). The enemy's reveal resolves first and the discard takes the place of the staging step only when the enemy would be added to the staging area, so a Goblin Follower's "engages the last player" resolves and the Follower is not discarded; only a Follower whose When Revealed is canceled is discarded. Enemies that Goblin Patrol's own search, Cave Torch's Forced or a Goblin Swordsman's Shadow put into the staging area are not "revealed from the encounter deck" and stay; a card Watchful Eyes reveals is, and an enemy it reveals is discarded. FFG's Caleb answer, relayed on https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/100928-goblin-patrol-question (the replacement reading), with https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/71122-khazad-dum-goblin-patrol-questions and https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/58639-khazad-d%C3%BBm-card-questions/page/2 for the added enemies and `dwd.revealing-enemies` for shadow enemies (researched; the official answer is only relayed in a forum thread).
- Goblin Patrol (03065:B:1): each player searches for 1 enemy in player order, and the first player names which player's choice is Patrol Leader (`rr.first-player`: the first player selects among eligible options); with one player it is that player's choice; with no Patrol Leader in the deck or discard pile the choices are free. Searching the deck and discard pile is one search for one card (https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/59985-khazad-d%C3%BBm-patrol-leader-goblin-patrol-questions) and shuffles the deck (agent recommendation for who takes Patrol Leader; no source found).
- Goblin Patrol (03065:B:3): "no enemies in play" counts the staging area and every engaged enemy; it is checked whenever no effect is resolving, so it cannot fire before the stage's own When Revealed has added the searched enemies. Placing 11 progress also advances (agent recommendation).
- Cave Torch (03041:A:1): the Setup's attach follows `rr.restricted` like any attach. A hero that already holds 2 Restricted attachments discards one of the three at once, the choice of the hero's controller (`docs/brief.md`, Keywords implemented once).
- Patrol Leader (03014:A:1): the Forced fires for each separate damage event dealt to it (the attack's combined damage once, each response or event separately; https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/59985-khazad-d%C3%BBm-patrol-leader-goblin-patrol-questions, community discussion, not an official ruling). With the encounter deck empty nothing is discarded and the damage is not canceled: the discard pile is shuffled back only when a card is revealed in the quest phase (`rr.running-out-of-cards`; agent recommendation). The Shadow (03014:A:2) cancels the damage dealt to the attacker for the whole attack and only damage dealt in it (https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/58639-khazad-d%C3%BBm-card-questions, community).
- East-gate (03016): `dwd.immune-to-card-effects` settles it: no card effect can target or change it, it stays the active location until explored and only questing places progress on it. So Cave In and Twisting Shadows leave its progress, Dreadful Gap's "return it to the staging area" does nothing, and Cave Torch cannot be aimed at it (it is not a Dark location anyway). Its A:2 stops engagement checks and optional engagement; an enemy that engages through its own text (Goblin Follower) still engages; combat goes on (https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/02/07/into-the-pit/). Explored, East-gate and Bridge of Khazad-dûm (Victory 1 and 2) go to the victory display, so Many Roads never reshuffles them.
- Dreadful Gap (03052) with East-gate active: the travel to Dreadful Gap cannot happen (travel needs no active location and East-gate stays), so Dreadful Gap stays in the staging area; X is every character in play, heroes and allies of every player, exhausted ones too (agent recommendation; https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/92279-question-about-east-gate says only that Dreadful Gap cannot move East-gate, no source says what the Gap does).
- Cave In (03048:A:1): it gains surge when the effect removed no progress token from the quest card and from the active location together; East-gate's tokens do not count (`dwd.immune-to-card-effects`; https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/66530-cave-in-vs-east-gate, community, unsettled there).
- Bridge of Khazad-dûm (03018:A:1): "cannot play cards" bars playing a card from a hand (events, allies, attachments, as a response too) for every player; putting a card into play by an ability, using an attached card's Action and a card already in play are not playing (https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/64726-bridge-of-khazad-dum, https://visionofthepalantir.com/2025/11/24/qotw-into-the-pit; community).
- Zigil Mineshaft (03038:A:1): any player may trigger it at any action window while it is in the staging area or the active location (`dwd.encounter-card-actions`; https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/99603-zigil-mineshaft-question, community); the threat raise is a cost each activation.
- Burning Low (03040:A:1): the cards that get the Threat are the enemies and locations in the staging area when it resolves; whether a location is Dark is read again while the effect lasts (`faq.lasting-effects`: an encounter card's lasting effect is recalculated). A player who controls a ready Cave Torch may exhaust it before the effect applies, which cancels the card's whole When Revealed (https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/02/07/into-the-pit/; the timing is an agent recommendation); the Torch's Forced fires as for any exhaust, and an enemy it adds is not in the staging area when the effect resolves.
- Fouled Well (03053:A:1): each player in player order chooses to discard one random card or not; it gains surge when any player did not discard one, a player with an empty hand included (the literal "all players did not discard" could mean none discarded; the forum players and a designer answer relayed in https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/291017-how-do-you-play-fouled-well and https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/62926-fouled-well read it as "not every player discarded"; community, designer answer secondhand).
- Sudden Pitfall (03051): the discarded character is a destroy for elimination (as Nazgûl of Dol Guldur's discard, above), so a seat with no hero left is eliminated; "cannot be canceled" bars every cancel of the When Revealed (https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/107415-sudden-pitfall-is-really-annoying; agent recommendation for the elimination). Its Shadow does nothing when the attack is undefended; Dark and Dreadful's Shadow deals its damage to the defending character and gives +2 Attack instead when there is none.
- Goblin Follower (03035): "the last player" is the player to the first player's right, and the only player in a solo game (`dwd.last-player`); the Shadow's +2 applies when the attacked player is the last player. Goblin Scout (03060): a player with threat 25 or higher cannot optionally engage it, but an engagement check at its cost still engages him (`rr.optional-engagement`; https://visionofthepalantir.com/2025/11/24/qotw-into-the-pit).
- Cave Torch (03041) leaves play: it is removed from the game, not discarded, whether by an effect or because its hero left play (`dwd.cave-torch`); no printed line says so, so the Setup record of Entering the Mines carries it. Cave Torch and Lightless Passage's "a Cave Torch" match the card of this box (the title is also The Dark of Mirkwood's 41029; the two never share a game).
- Easy mode: the Easy counts of `data/scenarios/into-the-pit.json` only (Crumbling Ruin, Sudden Pitfall, Watchful Eyes and Dreadful Gap are not dealt).
- Nightmare setup card (901001:A:2): each exhaust of the Cave Torch puts 1 damage token on the setup card (shown as the Torch dial of the top bar, `readouts/dwarrowdelf/pit.ts`, its limit 5 warming and a warning at 4, `docs/design-system-table.md`, Cards, the Into the Pit entry); at the 5th the Torch is removed from the game. The setup card's Forced, Patrol Sentry's Forced and the Torch's own Forced all hear the same exhaust: the first player orders them (`faq.simultaneous-effects`) and all fire, the removal not stopping the others (`faq.triggering-forced-effects`; agent recommendation). Without a Torch Lightless Passage cannot be traveled to, Burning Low cannot be canceled and Narrow Fissure takes progress (https://visionofthepalantir.com/2021/01/17/nightmare-into-the-pit/, an article by a player, not a ruling).
- Goblin Ambush (901002, stage 2 in place of Goblin Patrol: the build removes Goblin Patrol (`nightmare.removed`, pile `quest`) and `questDeckCodes` deals it from `nightmare.questCards` in its place; its quest points X come from an override and its record): its A side's When Revealed searches the deck and discard pile for 1 plus the number of players Orc enemies (Shadow Dweller is a Creature and cannot be chosen); the first player chooses them all; Patrol Sentry and Patrol Leader are among them unless a copy is not in the deck or discard pile, and other Orcs fill the places (https://visionofthepalantir.com/2021/01/17/nightmare-into-the-pit/). Its quest points are X, 1 plus the number of players. "After an enemy is defeated" is an enemy destroyed by damage, any enemy of any player in play (not a discard or a shuffle back: Put Off Pursuit does not count, same article; agent recommendation from `rr.destroy`); the only progress on it is that token.
- Patrol Sentry (901003:A:1): after a Cave Torch exhausts it attacks the player it is engaged with, an immediate attack with a shadow card (`faq.enemy-attacks-outside-combat`); a Sentry in the staging area does nothing. Shadow Dweller (901005:A:2): "while Cave Torch is in play" is any Cave Torch in play, ready or exhausted; it makes no engagement check, and a player may still engage it optionally. Goblin Lurker (901004): the engagement cost never drops below 0. Twisting Shadows (901006), Narrow Fissure (901007) and Shadow-fall (901008): "in play" counts the staging area and the active location; Shadow-fall's Dark trait counts for every Dark text; the first player chooses the non-Dark location it attaches to, and with none it is discarded (it has surge); Narrow Fissure's bar is read while the Torch is ready at the moment the progress would be placed, so a Torch exhausted to place progress does not stop it (https://visionofthepalantir.com/2021/01/17/nightmare-into-the-pit/; agent recommendation for the exhausted case).
