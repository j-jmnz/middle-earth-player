# The Watcher in the Water

Dwarrowdelf part 6 (pack `WitW`), standalone only, on Easy, Normal and Nightmare (deck 9015). Encounter sets The Watcher in the Water and The Misty Mountains, the latter from the Khazad-dûm box (`dwd.setup-the-watcher-in-the-water`); the Misty Mountains cards already have records (The Redhorn Gate, The Seventh Level, Road to Rivendell). The Watcher (04072) and Doors of Durin (04065) are set aside, out of play, at Setup (`dwd.setup-the-watcher-in-the-water-set-aside`: the data deals them `setAside`, so 04063:A:1 is the deck shuffle alone, as 04011:A:1). The Nightmare setup card removes 4 Black Uruks, 3 Mountain Warg, 2 Warg Lair, 2 Makeshift Passage and the standard Watcher; the Nightmare Watcher (901502) is set aside in its place and no quest card changes, so every difficulty deals 04063, 04064 (`questDeckCodes`). The new cards are the scenario's own (040076, 04063 to 04075) and the Nightmare deck (901501 to 901508). Sources: [V1] https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/03/02/the-watcher-in-the-water, [V2] https://visionofthepalantir.com/2021/08/16/nightmare-the-watcher-in-the-water/, [Q] https://visionofthepalantir.com/2025/12/31/qotw-the-watcher-in-the-water, [F1] https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/198935-doors-of-durin-test, [F2] https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/66280-question-about-grasping-tentacle, [F3] https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/84291-tentacles-in-watcher-in-the-water, [F4] https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/254414-grasping-tentacle-questions, [F5] https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/64885-the-watcher-in-the-water-doors-of-durin-grasping-and-thrashing-tentacles, [F6] https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/64775-perilous-swamp, [F7] https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/196648-snowdrifts-and-spill-over-progress. - Tentacle cards. Tentacle is a printed trait of every Tentacle enemy and of the treacheries Wrapped!, Pulled Under and Rippling From Beneath, so "all Tentacle cards in the encounter discard pile" (04064:B:1) takes those too. A Tentacle that "attaches ... as a Tentacle attachment" (Grasping, Writhing and Choking Tentacle, Wrapped!) is an attachment while it lies there: not an enemy (it does not attack, cannot be attacked or damaged, does not count as "another Tentacle enemy in play" or as a Tentacle enemy destroyed) and a Tentacle attachment for Pulled Under and the Combat Action of Wrapped!; when its host leaves play it goes to the encounter discard pile. Status: researched, community ([F2], [F3], [V1] "counts as a Tentacle attachment"); no ruling.
- 04063:A:1 (Setup): the set aside is the data's; the record only shuffles. Status: span-settled (`dwd.setup-the-watcher-in-the-water-set-aside`).
- 04063:B:1: the first player reveals one card after another, each resolved in full (staging, When Revealed, keywords), until the total Threat in the staging area is at least X, X twice the number of players; the cards already in the staging area count and none is revealed when it is already X. A treachery adds no Threat, so it never ends the reveals (Wrapped! and Disturbed Waters "effectively have surge" there, [Q]). An empty deck is refilled from the discard pile (`rr.running-out-of-cards`, `faq.last-encounter-card`); with the deck and the discard pile empty it stops. Same effect as 142021:A:1 (`RevealUntilThreat`; that card names the first player, this one names no player). Status: span-settled for the reveal and the refill; recommended that the first player reveals (`rr.first-player`: decisions are his; no ruling).
- 04064:B:1: The Watcher (the set-aside card of that title: 04072, or 901502 on Nightmare) is added to the staging area, then Doors of Durin (set aside) becomes the active location without a Travel, the active location it replaces going to the staging area, then every Tentacle card in the encounter discard pile is shuffled into the deck. Status: literal; the Nightmare Watcher stands in ([V2]).
- 04064:B:2: with at least 3 victory points in the victory display (the Watcher and Doors of Durin are 3 each, so either alone) and the stage's 5 quest points, the game is won, and at once, in any phase, when the last of the two comes (so the stage with its 5 progress waits for the points, as 04013:B:3 waits for its 5 victory points: `Restrict advance` while fewer than 3; the final-stage rule wins). Status: researched, community ([F1] "you win immediately no matter what phase", [V1] "a player who already has both wins immediately"); the alternative is that the points are read only at the moment the 5th progress is placed. Agreed in the sources, no official ruling.
- Doors of Durin (04065:A:1): the progress that would be placed on it, by questing (it has no quest points, so the active location buffer takes none) or by a card effect ("place progress on the active location"), is placed on the main quest stage instead and counts as placed there (a flag, not a cap); it can never be explored (`dwd.doors-of-durin`). Status: span-settled (`dwd.doors-of-durin`, `rr.active-location`); "the current quest card" is the stage, a side quest in play does not change it (recommended).
- Doors of Durin (04065:A:2): any player may use the Action while the card is in play (`dwd.encounter-card-actions`), once per round per copy whether or not it matched (`rr.limits`: a used ability counts). Each player in turn, the first player first, chooses any number of cards (0 included) from his own hand and discards them; then the top card of the encounter deck is discarded, not revealed (nothing resolves); when its title's first letter equals the first letter of the title of any card discarded this way, Doors goes to the victory display ("your", the group's: `rr.discard-pile-and-victory-display`). The first letter is the title's, case aside ("The", "A": a card "A Burning Brand" counts as A, [F1], community); an accent counts as its plain letter (Éowyn as E), recommended, no source. With the encounter deck empty outside the quest phase no card is discarded and nothing matches (brief, Core round: it stays empty). A look at the top card before the Action (Henamarth Riversong) is allowed ([F5], [F1]). Status: researched (community, letters and look); recommended (order, hands, accents).
- Stair Falls (04066:A:1): the first player pays the travel cost with 2 ready characters he controls, heroes or allies, his choice; with fewer than 2 ready characters the players cannot travel there (`rr.travel`). Alternative: any characters in play. Status: recommended (the first player alone pays, as 03019:A:1 prints "he controls"; [V1] gives no ruling).
- 04066:A:2, 04067:A:2 (Shadow): 1 progress token is removed from the main quest stage, none when it has none (`RemoveProgressFromQuest`, as the Misty Mountain Goblins' Shadow of the Hunt for Gollum). Status: literal.
- Perilous Swamp (04067:A:1): 1 progress at most is placed on it each round, however many are wanted: the `progressCap` of Snowdrifts. The progress beyond the cap from questing goes to the current quest as the engine places progress when the active location holds no more (`placeOnActive`); the alternative is that it is lost while the Swamp is unexplored. Status: recommended; the forums split ([F6] both readings, the majority lost; [F7] lost), no official ruling.
- Makeshift Passage (04068:A:1): after the players travel there, 2 progress go on the main quest stage, not on the new active location (`AddStageProgress`). Status: literal.
- Stagnant Creek (04069:A:1): the top card is discarded (the deck is refilled in the quest phase as `rr.running-out-of-cards` says); only an enemy with the Tentacle trait is added to the staging area, without being revealed (no When Revealed, no Ambush), and only then does each player raise his threat by 5. Status: literal; [V1].
- Ill Purpose (04070:A:1): every enemy in the staging area engages the player with the highest threat, whatever its engagement cost (the first player chooses among tied players, `rr.first-player`); the standard Watcher is included (its restriction is to optional engagement, [V1]) and the Nightmare Watcher stays while Doors of Durin is in play (`rr.cannot`). Then each player raises his threat by the total Threat of the staging area as it stands after those engagements, one number for all. Status: span-settled; literal.
- 04070:A:2 (Shadow): +1 Attack, or +3 instead (not +4) when the attacker is a Tentacle (as 03063:A:2 with Tentacle for Goblin). Status: literal.
- The Watcher (04072:A:1): Regenerate 2, a new printed keyword: an enemy with it heals X damage from itself each round, right after the first player token passes in the Refresh phase and before player actions (`dwd.regenerate`), in the staging area or engaged. It heals no more than it has. Status: span-settled.
- The Watcher (04072:A:2): it cannot be optionally engaged while any other enemy with the Tentacle trait is in play, in the staging area or engaged with any player; a Tentacle that lies as an attachment is not an enemy. It can still be engaged by an engagement check (engagement cost 48), by Ill Purpose and by a player card effect ([V1]: the card names optional engagement only). Status: literal; researched (community) for the attached Tentacle.
- The Watcher (04072:A:3, 901502:A:2): at the end of the combat phase, with it in the staging area, each player in turn, the first player first, assigns all 3 damage to 1 character he controls (they cannot be split among characters), hero or ally, as direct damage (no defense); a player with no character skips it. Status: literal (`AssignDamage` with `single`).
- Grasping Tentacle (04073:A:1): after the attackers are declared against it, the top card of the encounter deck is discarded (a card with a Shadow line, printed, or an enemy with the Tentacle trait matches; a treachery with the Tentacle trait does not), and on a match it attaches to an attacking character the first player chooses (`rr.first-player`), the attack is over (no damage is dealt to it: it is no longer an enemy; the attackers stay exhausted) and the host's Attack and Defense are 0 while it lies there, with no way to discard it. Status: researched, community ([F2], [F4]: attackers first, the Forced, then the attack fizzles); the first player's choice span-settled (`rr.first-player`); no ruling.
- Thrashing Tentacle (04074:A:1): the same discard check; on a match the damage of the attack (the total Attack of the attackers less Thrashing Tentacle's Defense, which is 0 unless a card such as Banks of Sirannon raises it) is dealt to 1 character an attacking player controls, the first player choosing among the characters of the attacking players, ignoring that character's defense; Thrashing Tentacle takes none ([F5]: "You don't do any damage to it"). Status: researched, community (no damage to it); recommended for the total and the chooser.
- Striking Tentacle (04075:A:1): when it attacks (any attack, immediate attacks included), before the defender is declared, the same discard check; on a match no defender can be declared: the attack is considered undefended (the damage goes to a hero the defending player chooses) and its Shadow card is dealt as for any attack. Status: literal; [V1].
- Wrapped! (040076:A:1): the first player attaches it to one of his heroes that has no Wrapped! (Limit 1 per hero); with none eligible it is discarded with no effect (an attachment cannot enter play without a target, `rr.attachment-cards`; as Shadow of Fear). As a Tentacle attachment it holds the hero so: it cannot exhaust (so it cannot quest, attack or defend, and a cost that exhausts it fails) or ready, but a hero that is already committed keeps its Willpower for the round. At the end of the round the hero is discarded from play (not destroyed: no destroyed Responses; its attachments go with it, a player with no hero left is eliminated). The Combat Action is any player's (`dwd.encounter-card-actions`): he exhausts a ready hero he controls that has no Tentacle attachment of any kind and Wrapped! is discarded to the encounter discard pile. Status: span-settled (Shadow of Fear reading, `rr.blank`); researched, community for the committed hero ([V1]); recommended for the no-hero-eligible case.
- Nightmare setup card, side A (901501): A:1 ("You are playing in Nightmare mode") and B:1 to B:9 (the deck-building text) are inert. A:2: while Doors of Durin is in play (the staging area or active; not set aside, not in the victory display) The Watcher cannot be engaged at all (`rr.cannot`: no engagement check, no optional engagement, not by Ill Purpose or a card effect). A:3: when a Tentacle enemy is destroyed (not discarded, [V2]), in the staging area or engaged, the Watcher takes 1 damage if it is in play, whatever its zone; the Watcher's own destruction deals nothing. A:4: the players cannot win the game unless both Doors of Durin and The Watcher are in the victory display: the last stage, The Seething Lake, cannot be defeated until they are (`Restrict advance`, one record for each card; To the West-door is defeated as usual). On Nightmare the standard Watcher's code reads as the Nightmare Watcher in these records and in 04064:B:1 (`twinCode`, `src/rules/families/nightmare/guards.ts`). Status: literal; [V2].
- The Watcher, Nightmare (901502): A:1 in the staging area it takes no damage from player card effects (the damage of an attack by a character is not one, and only an engaged Watcher can be attacked); once engaged it can be damaged ([V2]). A:2 is 04072:A:3 (a copy). No Regenerate ([Q]). Status: researched, community ([V2], [Q]).
- Writhing Tentacle (901503:A:1): after it engages a player (by any engagement) the discard check; on a match it makes an immediate attack against that player, and after that attack resolves it attaches to the defending character if that character is still in play ("Attached character cannot commit to the quest": `cannotQuest`); an undefended attack or a destroyed defender leaves it engaged as an enemy. Status: literal for the attack; recommended for the undefended and destroyed cases (no ruling found).
- Choking Tentacle (901504:A:1): after its attack resolves the discard check; on a match it attaches to a character the defending player controls (not necessarily the defender; the first player chooses among them, `rr.first-player`), and each time the attached character readies it takes 1 damage (the Refresh phase and any ready effect). Status: literal; [V1]; the chooser span-settled (alternative: the defending player).
- Banks of Sirannon (901505:A:1): each Tentacle enemy in play, staging area or engaged, the Watcher included, gets +2 Defense for each copy of Banks in the staging area ([V2]: "Three copies stack"). A:2 (Shadow): 1 random card from the defending player's hand is discarded for each point of damage the attack dealt to the defender after defense (an undefended attack's damage counts); none with an empty hand. Status: literal; researched ([V2]).
- Hideous Depths (901506:A:1): while it is in play, after any effect makes a player look at cards of the encounter deck (a look at the top cards, a search of the deck), the deck is shuffled; a reveal or a discard is not a look. "After" is after the effect that looked has resolved in full: an effect that looks and then arranges, picks or discards the cards it saw acts on them before the shuffle, and the shuffle follows (`rr.forced`: a Forced effect "after" an effect resolves once that effect has). Status: span-settled (`rr.forced`) for the timing; literal; recommended that a search counts (it shuffles after anyway). A:2: the cost of traveling there is revealing the topmost Tentacle enemy of the encounter discard pile, which is staged like any reveal (`rr.reveal`); with none in the discard pile the players cannot travel there ([V2]). Status: literal.
- Pulled Under (901507:A:1): 3 damage to each character, any player's, hero or ally, with a Tentacle attachment on it; if no character was damaged (damage prevented or canceled counts as not damaged, and nothing to damage) it gains surge. A:2 (Shadow): the defending character with a Tentacle attachment is discarded (not destroyed, `rr.destroy`; the attack then deals no damage); an undefended attack has no defending character. Status: literal; recommended for prevented damage.
- Rippling From Beneath (901508:A:2): Surge is a keyword. The first player chooses X Tentacle enemies in the encounter discard pile (all of them when there are fewer) and they are shuffled into the deck; then the Watcher, if in play, is healed X damage (up to what it has). X is the number of players. Status: span-settled for the chooser (`rr.first-player`); recommended against random cards ([V2] "return X Tentacles"; no ruling).
- Table: no tracker or zone is created by a line here. The held cards are the Tentacle attachments (Wrapped!, Grasping, Writhing and Choking Tentacle), each drawn under its host in every zone the host can be in, Doors of Durin (the active location with no quest points, its Action offered to any seat) and a Watcher in the staging area. Perilous Swamp's cap of 1 is the cap readout of Snowdrifts. The first player's picks (Wrapped!, Stair Falls, Ill Purpose's tie, Grasping, Thrashing and Choking Tentacle, Rippling) are not owned, the sheet adds who chooses; each player's own picks (Wrapped!'s Action, the Doors hand discards, The Watcher's damage) lead with the player; a pick that spans seats names the owner on each row (a character of an attacking player other than the one who owns the enemy's engagement needs Ranged, so Grasping's and Thrashing's candidates are one seat's characters). Choking Tentacle's candidates are the characters of the player the Tentacle is engaged with (`controlledBy: source`), whoever chooses. Table step: Wrapped! and the three attaching Tentacles lie in zone `attached` with the printed type enemy and are drawn under their host (`src/play/scenarios/the-watcher-in-the-water-table.test.ts`), cited in `docs/design-system-table.md` (Cards, The Watcher in the Water).
