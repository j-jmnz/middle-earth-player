# Foundations of Stone

Dwarrowdelf part 8 (pack `FoS`), standalone only, on Easy, Normal and Nightmare (deck 9017). Encounter sets Foundations of Stone (set aside at Setup, `encounterDeck2`), Twists and Turns, Deeps of Moria and Goblins of the Deep (the rulesheet names Hazards of the Pit's pile for Deeps of Moria, see Dwarrowdelf above; `dwd.setup-foundations-of-stone`). Stages: The Dripping Walls (04111), The Water's Edge (04112), Washed Away! (04113), four Below the Mines (04114 Sheltered Rocks 11, 04115 The Endless Caves 17, 04116 The Shivering Bank 7, 04117 Old One Lair 5 quest points), Out of the Depths (04118); every difficulty deals all eight (the Nightmare setup card removes and adds no quest card). The Torch: `dwd.setup-foundations-of-stone-cave-torch` (the first player picks any hero; removed from the game if it would leave play). Sources: the rules spans cited; community pages listed with each reading (https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/03/04/foundations-of-stone/, https://visionofthepalantir.com/2021/12/21/nightmare-foundations-of-stone/, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/66692-foundation-of-stone-questions, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/91385-nameless-thing, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/266594-nameless-things-forest-snare-and-first-player); no FFG or Hall of Beorn ruling on this pack was found, so every community reading below is not user-confirmed.

- Nightmare piles (data): the scans of the Nightmare deck carry two set icons. Mithril Seeker (901702) and Giant Cave Centipede (901703) bear the Goblins of the Deep icon (5 copies, shuffled into the standard deck); Unspeakable Thing, Primeval Thing, Nest of Horrors, Shivering River and Chill of the Roots (901704 to 901708, 14 copies) bear the Foundations of Stone icon and are shuffled into the set-aside set (901701:B:13). The two Goblins of the Deep codes go to the `encounter` pile and the five Foundations of Stone codes to `encounterDeck2`. Read from the scans (`.cache/scans/901702.A.jpg` to `901708.A.jpg`); https://visionofthepalantir.com/2021/12/21/nightmare-foundations-of-stone/ agrees (Seeker and Centipede in for stages 1 and 2, the others swapped in at stage 3). Status: span-settled by the scans.
- Washed Away! (04113:B:1) discards: every Item, Armor, Weapon and Light card in play (attachments, the Cave Torch among them, which is removed from the game by `dwd.cave-torch`; an Artifact, Mount, Condition or Title that has none of those traits stays in Normal mode) and every encounter card in play: the staging area, the active location, enemies engaged with players, encounter cards attached to characters (Watchful Eyes). The player cards attached to a discarded enemy go to their owners' discard piles. Alternative: only the cards in the staging area. Taken because the card says "from play" and the answers read it this way (https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/03/04/foundations-of-stone/; https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/66692-foundation-of-stone-questions, a player answer that engaged enemies are discarded too). Status: researched, community only.
- Washed Away! the new deck: the enemy and treachery cards of the encounter discard pile (including the cards just discarded) are shuffled with the whole set-aside Foundations of Stone set (and, in Nightmare, its FoS-icon cards) as the new encounter deck; every other card of the old encounter deck and discard pile (locations, objectives, the rest) leaves the game. Literal (`rr.discard-pile-and-victory-display`, `rr.removed-from-game`); status: literal.
- Washed Away! the stage 4 draw: starting with the first player each player draws one of the four stage 4 cards at random, without replacement, and it becomes that player's stage; the rest leave the game. Each stage is revealed for its player as it is drawn (agent recommendation: the printed order is silent; one player gets one card and one area). With one player, the stage 4 area is the one staging area and the game goes on as a normal quest. Status: recommended, no source found.
- Creating a staging area (04114 to 04117:B:1): `dwd.creating-a-staging-area` and `mec109.multiple-staging-areas`: each player has an area and a stage of his own, phases resolve in player order, each player reveals 1 card per player sharing his staging area, "each player" of an encounter card means each player at the staging area it entered, and cards that say "the quest" mean the player's own stage. The Breaking of the Fellowship areas and the Escape from Mount Gram join are the same state (`ScenarioState.separate`, `Seat.staging`, `Seat.stage`, `Seat.joined`). The first player token keeps moving as usual; a player alone at his stage acts as first player for the cards that name the first player at that stage ("first player's deck", Deep Deep Dark, Primeval Thing): the first player of the staging area (the token holder when he is there, else the nearest player clockwise from the token). Alternative: the global token holder everywhere. Taken from https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/03/04/foundations-of-stone/ and https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/266594-nameless-things-forest-snare-and-first-player (each staging area has its own first player; a player's old-ruling answer, not found in the thread). Status: researched, community only.
- Stage 4 text (04114 to 04117:B:1): "Reveal 2 (4) cards" is the revealing player's own count, not per player; "Discard all resources from your heroes" is the stage's player; "Discard your hand" the same. 04116's discard comes before its reveal. Literal.
- Stage 4 Forced (04114 to 04117:B:2): the stage is completed when the Nth progress token is placed (N = its quest points); it does not advance by the ordinary rule. At the beginning of the next travel phase the player joins another player (`dwd.joining-another-player`): his encounter cards move to the joined player's area (enemies stay engaged, the active location he leaves is discarded), his stage is set aside, and he shares the other area and stage from then on; several players complete in the same phase, they join in player order clockwise, and a player with no area to join (alone, or the joined area is his only other one) makes every player continue to stage 5 together (`Seat.joined` and `advanceTogether`, `families/captured/join.ts`). Progress placed on a completed stage while it waits is not needed; the player still quests that round. Status: span-settled for the join, recommended for the wait (agent recommendation: no source says whether a completed stage keeps taking progress).
- Out of the Depths (04118:B:2): while committing, a player cannot commit more allies than the number of heroes he is also committing; each commit is checked as it is made (an ally needs a committed hero first), a player with no committed hero commits no ally. Literal; the enforcement point is agent recommendation.
- Out of the Depths (04118:B:3, "have won the game"), 04111:B:1 and 04118:B:1 ("1 card per player"): copies of the earlier stage lines (`rr.winning-the-game`). Status: literal.
- The Water's Edge (04112:B:1): each player who committed at least one character discards the top 2 cards of his own deck (an empty deck discards fewer); in the shared stage that is every committing player at the staging step. Alternative: each player, committed or not. Status: recommended (literal "after a player commits characters").
- Durin's Axe and Helm (04119, 04120): Surge, so each is followed by another reveal. A revealed objective goes to the staging area (`rr.objective-cards`); its Action lets any player exhaust one of his ready heroes to claim it and attach it to that hero (the pattern of Abandoned Tools and Book of Mazarbul; `dwd.encounter-card-actions`). The Dwarf clause (+1 Willpower; +2 hit points) reads the host's trait while attached. An objective attachment whose host leaves play is discarded to the encounter discard pile and can be revealed again (as Durin's Greaves, The Long Dark; agent recommendation, `rr.attachment-cards`). Their Weapon and Armor traits would make Washed Away discard them, but they are not in play before it. Status: literal for the Action, recommended for the leave-play.
- Drowned Treasury (04121:A:1) is printed with no Forced label; it is read as a Forced like Twisting Hollow's and Drowned Cave's (12102:A:2, 12104:A:2): when it is the active location at the end of the quest phase each player at its staging area discards one character he controls of his choice, a hero included (a discarded hero is a destroy for elimination, as Sudden Pitfall above). Alternative: only allies. Status: recommended (agent recommendation; the card's wording and the Grotto cards).
- Drowned Treasury (04121:A:2): after it leaves play as an explored location each player at its area, in player order, may draw 2 cards, or claim 1 objective in play: an objective in the staging area of that area (each objective once), attached to a hero the claimant chooses; he may do neither. The claim needs no exhaust (https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/03/04/foundations-of-stone/: "claim an objective without exhausting a hero"). Status: researched, community only.
- Mithril Lode (04122:A:2): while it is the active location it gains a Refresh Action, usable in the action windows of the refresh phase after the characters are ready (https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/68924-peace-and-thought-refresh-action, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/237905-forced-and-refresh-actions, community; the City of Corsairs' Refresh Action records use the same window). Any player may use it (`dwd.encounter-card-actions`): he exhausts a ready character he controls and places that character's current Willpower as progress on his current quest card, bypassing the active location; once each round in all (the limit is the location's, not the player's). Status: researched.
- Deep Deep Dark (04123:A:2): each Nameless enemy in play at the revealing staging area (staging and engaged) gets the top card of the first player's deck, one after another, "if able". Its Shadow (04123:A:3, 04124:A:2): the defending player discards his hand when the attacking enemy is Nameless. Literal. Doomed 1 and Surge are keywords.
- Lost and Alone (04124:A:1): each player at the staging area chooses a hero he controls and shuffles it into his deck: the hero leaves play (it is not destroyed; its attachments are discarded; its threat counts as it is; a player left with no hero in play is not eliminated, since `rr.player-elimination` names heroes killed); when its owner draws it, it is put into play at once (ready, no damage, no resources), however it was drawn. A hero in the deck at the end counts as not in play for scoring (agent recommendation: treated as dead for the scoring threat). The hero may be attached to a Nameless enemy as a card with printed cost 0 (https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/66692-foundation-of-stone-questions, player answers; https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/03/04/foundations-of-stone/ says it returns "fully healed"). Status: researched, community only; the scoring is recommended.
- Nameless Thing and Elder Nameless Thing (04125, 04126), Unspeakable Thing and Primeval Thing (901704, 901705): "attach" puts the player card face up on the enemy as an attachment with no title and no game text; its only active part is its printed cost (https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/266594-nameless-things-forest-snare-and-first-player: a FAQ entry the thread cites; https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/91385-nameless-thing). X is the total printed cost of all the cards attached to it, applied literally: a player attachment on it (Forest Snare) counts its cost, an attached card with no printed cost (a hero) or an X cost adds 0, and any attached card makes "if there are no cards attached" false, so X is 3 (4, 5, 6) only with nothing attached; X is the enemy's Attack and its hit points (the scans print X for both; https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/66692-foundation-of-stone-questions, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/266594-nameless-things-forest-snare-and-first-player); attached cards that cost 0 in all (with at least one attached) make X 0 and the enemy is discarded. The attached cards go to their owners' discard piles when it leaves play (https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/91385-nameless-thing). With an empty deck fewer cards are attached and the enemy still engages. The Forced fires after each engagement (and, for Unspeakable Thing, when it attacks). Victory 1 (3 for the Elder) is a keyword. Status: literal for X (the printed text), researched, community only for the attached card's form.
- Nest of Horrors (901706): while in the staging area its Forced hears the cards attached to a Nameless enemy from the top of a player's deck (one event for the cards attached together) and attaches one additional card from that player's deck; the additional card is not a new event (otherwise the deck would be emptied; a second Nest in the staging area hears the same event once and adds its own card). Its Travel: each player at the stage discards the top card of the encounter deck; each Nameless enemy discarded this way is revealed (its When Revealed resolves and it enters the staging area of the player who travelled). Status: recommended for the event count (https://visionofthepalantir.com/2021/12/21/nightmare-foundations-of-stone/ says an extra card "each time that enemy receives a card from a player's deck", no ruling).
- Shivering River (901707): in a staging area it adds its Threat (3) to the total in every other staging area, once per area; Travel is free; its Shadow gives +3 Attack instead of +1 when more than one player is at the defending player's staging area (https://visionofthepalantir.com/2021/12/21/nightmare-foundations-of-stone/). Literal.
- Giant Cave Centipede (901703): When Revealed: each player at the stage shuffles the card in his hand with the highest printed cost into his deck, the player picking among ties (an empty hand: nothing). Forced at the end of the combat phase: the engaged player chooses a different player's staging area and adds it there as a staging card (it leaves the engagement). Players who share a staging area send it to that shared area as a staging card; with one player, and no other area, it stays engaged (https://visionofthepalantir.com/2021/12/21/nightmare-foundations-of-stone/, "in stage 4 it moves to another player's staging area ... chosen by the engaged player"). Status: researched, community only for the shared area (agent recommendation).
- Mithril Seeker (901702): the Forced moves 1 resource from each of the defending player's heroes that has one; its +1 Attack is per resource on it; none are returned (https://visionofthepalantir.com/2021/12/21/nightmare-foundations-of-stone/). Literal.
- Chill of the Roots (901708): each player chooses, in player order: discard his hand, or raise his threat by the number of cards in it; it gains surge when no player discarded a card (a player with an empty hand discards none either way). Its Shadow attaches the top card of the defending player's deck to an attacking Nameless enemy. Literal.
- Moria Bats (04127): only characters with the Ranged keyword (printed or gained) can attack or defend against it; with none able the attack is undefended. +1 Attack for each other enemy engaged with the same player. Status: literal.
- Nightmare setup card (901701:A:2): the Forced "when stage 3b is revealed" resolves before 3B's When Revealed: every Mount and Artifact attachment in play is discarded, then each player discards allies of his choice, one at a time, until the total printed cost of the allies he controls is 6 or less (https://visionofthepalantir.com/2021/12/21/nightmare-foundations-of-stone/, which reads it per player). Alternative: after Washed Away. Status: recommended (the article gives no order; the Forced's "when" is read as before the reveal's effects, `rr.forced`).
- Table (no new zone or dial; `docs/design-system-table.md`, Cards, Foundations of Stone): the separate staging areas are drawn as Breaking of the Fellowship's and Escape from Mount Gram's are (`dwd.creating-a-staging-area`, 04113:B:1); a stage 4 reads its progress over its quest points and, in its own Forced line, that it joins another player at the beginning of the travel phase (04114:B:2 to 04117:B:2); the player cards a Nameless enemy holds lie under it, in the staging area and engaged, and Durin's Axe and Helm under their hero (04125:A:1, 04119:A:2, 04120:A:2); the commit window offers only sets that keep to Out of the Depths' ally limit (04118:B:2); the owner is named on each row of a pick that spans seats (the Torch's hero) and in the sentence of every player's own choice. Status: agent recommendation for the wording.
- Easy mode: the Easy counts of `data/scenarios/foundations-of-stone.json` only.
- Printed over Hall of Beorn's text: the setup card's "Mitril Lode" is Mithril Lode (see Dwarrowdelf above).
