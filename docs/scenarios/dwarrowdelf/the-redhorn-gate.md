# The Redhorn Gate

Dwarrowdelf part 4 (pack `TRG`), standalone only, on Easy, Normal and Nightmare (deck 9013). Encounter sets The Redhorn Gate and The Misty Mountains, the latter from the Khazad-dûm box (`dwd.setup-the-redhorn-gate`); Snowstorm (5 copies) and Arwen Undómiel are set aside, and Caradhras starts in the staging area (`dwd.setup-the-redhorn-gate-set-aside`). The rulesheet's `dwd.ally-objective-arwen` and `dwd.caradhras` say what Arwen and Caradhras are. The quest deck is the plain three stages, in the Nightmare deck as well (the setup card removes no stage). Every Misty Mountains card is new to the app (Black Uruks, Mountain Warg, Bitter Wind, Knees of the Mountain, Turbulent Waters, Warg Lair): none was dealt by Into the Pit, The Seventh Level or Flight from Moria.

- Setup (04011:A:1) and Arwen (04014): Caradhras is placed by the pile; Snowstorm is set aside by the pile (Easy deals the same 5). Setup puts Arwen into play, ready, under the first player's control; her constant takes the control with the first player token at the end of each round, as Calphon's (11028:A:1), and a first player's elimination passes the token at once (`faq.first-player-elimination`), so it does not make her leave play (span-settled, `dwd.ally-objective-arwen`). She is an ally for every card effect, so Snow-Giant's discard and Rocky Crags' damage may choose her, and her leaving play for any reason, a discard included, loses the game (`dwd.ally-objective-arwen`; a LoseGame on `leftPlay` as 11028:A:3). Her Response hears every exhaust (questing, attacking, defending or a cost) and its controller chooses the hero, any player's (literal). Other copies of her cannot enter play (span-settled).
- 1B (04011:B:1): the same effect as 03064:B:1 in other words, one card per player, each revealed in full (`rr.reveal`, span-settled).
- Snowdrifts (04012): B:1 shuffles 1 more Snowstorm than there are players from the set-aside copies (5 at most: a table of 4 uses all). B:2 fires once the stage's quest points are met, whatever the number of tokens placed beyond the 11th, as Grimbeorn's Quest (02035:B:1), before the stage flips: the active location is discarded with its progress, Caradhras becomes the active location without a Travel (`dwd.caradhras`), and the characters are still committed (the engine keeps the commitments through the stage's defeat while Snowdrifts is the stage, `heldCommitted`, and clears them once the quest's resolution is done), so its -1 Willpower applies at once, and with 3B's rule the characters at 0 are discarded (researched: community consensus, no designer answer, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/65253-clarifications-on-redhorn-gate, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/78634-several-questions-about-the-redhorn-gate, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/102375-the-redhorn-gate, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/62038-redhorn-questions/). Caradhras comes from the staging area, or back from the victory display when it was explored first (the designer's answer, relayed in the 2014 thread above, and the 2012 thread; researched). From the encounter deck or discard pile (after The Dimrill Stair reshuffled it) it is searched out and the deck shuffled, and only then: from the staging area or the victory display the deck keeps the order Fell Voices gave it (agent recommendation: no source; the effect names no zone and the card must be in play to be the active location). A Caradhras already active is not discarded (agent recommendation).
- The Mountains' Peaks (04013): B:1 shuffles every Snowstorm in the encounter discard pile into the deck (`ShuffleEncounterDiscard` by name). B:2 is a state check, never an event: while this stage is current, any character in play whose current Willpower is 0, printed or from modifiers and counters, is discarded from play (not destroyed) as soon as it is 0, in any phase, heroes included; Caradhras' -1 only reaches a questing character, so a Willpower-1 character is discarded when it commits and a Willpower-2 hero with Freezing Cold when the stage flips (researched: FFG forum answers, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/65253-clarifications-on-redhorn-gate, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/78634-several-questions-about-the-redhorn-gate, https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/02/18/the-redhorn-gate/; the thread's answers are community members'). A character that chooses not to pay for Fanuidhol keeps its Willpower and is not discarded. B:3 bars the advance while the victory display holds fewer than 5 victory points (progress goes on, `faq.progress-without-advancing`; the display counts every card in it, locations and enemies, `rr.victory`; the Nightmare setup card's rule is below). B:4 is a WinGame on the stage's defeat (as 03066:B:2).
- Caradhras (04015): A:1 gives -1 Willpower to every questing character while it is the active location (Arwen too). A:2 is `dwd.caradhras`: only a quest effect makes it the active location; the travel phase never offers it (it is a Mountain and a Snow location while it waits in the staging area, so it feeds Mountain Goblin and Icy Cavern).
- Fanuidhol (04016): while it is the active location, each committed hero's Willpower counts only if its controller spends 1 resource from that hero's own pool when the quest total is counted (the resolution of the quest, after the staging step and its action window, so Snowstorm, Avalanche! and the other effects of the staging step have run); a hero with no resource counts 0, and a player may keep a resource back and let the hero count 0. Allies are not affected. Agent recommendation for the moment: no ruling found, the community writes the card the same way (https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/02/18/the-redhorn-gate/ says a committed hero's Willpower counts only if a resource is spent from its pool; nothing about the moment of payment, searched on the FFG forum archive and BoardGameGeek without an answer); the alternative, paying as the hero commits, is a payment made before the staging step can change anything. The engine asks when the quest total is counted, after the staging step and the action window that follows it (Fanuidhol's Forced effect on `questTotalCounted`), in player order, once for each committed hero with a resource; the resource is spent, not discarded.
- Celebdil (04017): at the end of each round while active, removes up to 2 progress tokens from itself (all it has if fewer), as River Ninglor (02017:A:1); the same step removes none from a Celebdil in the staging area (literal).
- The Dimrill Stair (04018): the effect shuffles the locations in the encounter discard pile and in the victory display into the encounter deck (the active location and the staging area are not touched; the Stair is not in either pile when it resolves). With at least two reshuffled (total, from both), each player reduces his threat by 11 and every Freezing Cold in play, the copies attached to heroes only, goes to the encounter discard pile; the copies in the deck stay (researched: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/247861-dimrill-stair-clarification, a community answer; the discard, not removal from the game, is the author's correction at https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/02/18/the-redhorn-gate/). With a reshuffle of 0 or 1 locations the deck is still shuffled and the rest of the line does not apply (literal). A reshuffled Caradhras leaves the display and its 3 victory points (https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/183393-traveling-to-the-dimrill-stair-in-stage-3b).
- Rocky Crags (04019): a Travel cost, so every player must pay it or the players cannot travel (`rr.travel`): each player in player order deals 2 damage to 1 character he controls, hero or ally, his choice, even a character the 2 damage destroys; a player with no character cannot pay (a game with a player and no character is over). The Shadow (04019:A:2 = 04021:A:2): +1 Attack for each progress token on the active location, counted as the Shadow resolves, 0 with no active location (literal).
- Fell Voices (04020): the top 2 Snow cards of the encounter discard pile, of any type (Snow Warg, Caradhras, Avalanche!, Blinding Blizzard), go to the top of the encounter deck, not revealed; the first player chooses their order (the designer's answer, Caleb Grace, relayed at https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/72027-fell-voices-the-redhorn-gate, researched). "Less than 2 Snow treachery cards" counts the treacheries among the cards returned, so a Snow Warg returned with one treachery gives surge; the alternative, surge only when fewer than 2 Snow cards were returned, ignores the word "treachery". Recommended (no ruling; the Vision of the Palantir review, https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/02/18/the-redhorn-gate/, writes "if that doesn't return 2 Snow treacheries"; the Hall of Beorn page, https://hallofbeorn.com/LotR/Details/Fell-Voices-TRG, has no ruling). With surge the card revealed next is the one just returned (muemakan on the thread above, community).
- Fallen Stones (04021): the first player chooses either option, and an option that does nothing may still be chosen, so he may pick "removes all progress tokens from play" with none in play (researched: community, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/62038-redhorn-questions/; the Vision of the Palantir comment disputing it is the other view, https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/02/18/the-redhorn-gate/). "All progress tokens in play" are those on the current quest stage, the active location, the locations in the staging area, a side quest and Blinding Blizzard, not on a card out of play (literal). The second option reveals 2 cards in full and adds them to the staging area (`rr.reveal`).
- Snowstorm (04022): "each questing character" is an encounter card's lasting effect, recalculated as the game changes (`faq.lasting-effects`), so a character committed later in the phase (Avalanche! after it) gets the -1 too (span-settled; the same answer, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/192497-redhorn-gate-question, GrandSpleen). The Shadow's -1 Willpower and the discard at 0 last until the end of the phase on the characters defending this attack, recalculated, so a defender whose Willpower is 0 at any moment of the phase is discarded, even one whose Willpower falls to 0 later (agent recommendation for the later moment; the one-time reading of 900102's wording is the alternative; no source). An undefended attack has no defending character. 5 copies are set aside, so none is in the deck until 2B.
- Freezing Cold (04023): the first player attaches it to a hero he controls (not another player's), of his choice, one that already carries a copy included; the attachment gives -2 Willpower and `cannotCommit`; a second copy on the same hero discards the hero from play, with the attachments, when it attaches. A hero already committed when it attaches stays committed (the card removes no one, as GrandSpleen answers on https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/78634-several-questions-about-the-redhorn-gate; community). With no hero the first player controls, the card is discarded (agent recommendation). The attachment is a Condition (as Shadow of Fear, 03027:A:1) and `The Dimrill Stair` discards it. With 3B current, its -2 discards a hero whose Willpower is 2 or less (researched, the threads under The Mountains' Peaks).
- Avalanche! (04024): every ready character of every player, Arwen and allies included, exhausts; in the quest phase each one just exhausted is committed to the quest (it counts its Willpower, `rr.quest-phase`); one that cannot commit (Freezing Cold's) stays exhausted and uncommitted (an effect's `CommitToQuest` refuses a character that cannot quest only in a game that deals Freezing Cold, so earlier games' Hobbit Pony, Grappling Hook, Windfola and Late Adventurer replay as before; the general refusal waits for an engine version change); outside the quest phase nothing is committed (literal). Played in the staging step it commits characters that Snowstorm and Caradhras then lower (the Vision of the Palantir article, https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/02/18/the-redhorn-gate/). Under 3B a Willpower-1 character committed this way is discarded at once (researched, the threads under The Mountains' Peaks).
- Mountain Goblin, Mountain Troll (04025, 04026): +1 Attack for each Mountain location in the staging area, read live, the active location not counted (literal). Their Shadow (A:2) already has its records (04025:A:2, 04026:A:2), and Mountain Warg's copies them.
- Snow Warg (04027): A:1 stops allies from being declared as defenders while it attacks; A:2 deals 1 damage to the character declared as its defender, whatever the rest of the attack does, and nothing when the attack is undefended, there being no defending character (literal; researched: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/78634-several-questions-about-the-redhorn-gate, community; https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/02/18/the-redhorn-gate/).
- Black Uruks (03054): the first player chooses 1 attachment on any questing character, any player's, hero or ally, and discards it; with none nothing happens (recommended: the card names "a questing character", not one he controls; no ruling found, searched on the FFG forum archive and the official FAQ). Its Shadow deals 2 more shadow cards when the attack is undefended (literal).
- Mountain Warg (03055): A:1 is Wargs' line (01085:A:1, condition `shadowBlank`): it returns to the staging area after it attacks when a card dealt to it as a shadow card has no Shadow effect, a defended attack or not and only if it survived. A shadow card with no Shadow text counts, a shadow effect that was canceled does not (literal; https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/02/18/the-redhorn-gate/ reads it so). A:2 is 04025:A:2 in other words.
- Bitter Wind (03056): When Revealed, the first player discards 3 resources from each hero he controls (as many as the pool holds); the Shadow makes the defending player, the player attacked even when the attack is undefended and a sentinel defends (`rr.defending-player`), discard 2 from each of his heroes.
- Knees of the Mountain (03057): while in the staging area (not as the active location) it gives each enemy that engages a player, by an engagement check or by choice, +1 Attack until the end of the round; copies in the staging area stack, one Forced each (literal, as Forest Spider 01096:A:1 and Stray Goblin 18021:A:2).
- Turbulent Waters (03058): while active, no player may engage an enemy by choice; the engagement check still engages (literal).
- Warg Lair (03059): When Revealed searches the encounter deck and discard pile for a Mountain Warg and adds it to the staging area, not revealed (nothing resolves), then shuffles the deck (with none found the deck is still shuffled, as Fog Bank's 11018:A:1, literal). A:2 fires when it leaves play as an explored location (the `subjectExplored` condition of 02056:A:2, here on its own leaving): each player draws 1.
- Nightmare setup card (901301): A:2 sets Blinding Blizzard (2 copies, the deck's pile) aside, and its rule makes the cards of players in the victory display worth 0 for the 5 victory points of 3B (a Black Arrow from the player's deck, say) and for the final score, which sums the display (`rr.victory`; recommended: the card says "count their victory points" and "during this game", no ruling; the Vision of the Palantir article, https://visionofthepalantir.com/2021/11/16/nightmare-the-redhorn-gate, says nothing of it). A:3 fires when stage 2B is revealed beside 2B's own When Revealed (`faq.simultaneous-effects`): every set-aside Blinding Blizzard shuffled into the deck with the Snowstorm copies, once, and the deck shuffled. A:1 and B:1 to B:13 are the deck-building text, inert. The quest deck is the standard one. The setup card removes 1 Black Uruks, 3 Mountain Warg, 1 Knees of the Mountain, 2 Turbulent Waters, 2 Warg Lair, 1 The Dimrill Stair, 2 Fell Voices, 2 Fallen Stones and 3 Mountain Goblin, so the Nightmare game has none of the Dimrill Stair's and Warg Lair's text to read.
- Wolfpelt Goblin (901302): Surge is a keyword. X is the active location's quest points minus its progress (0 with no active location or when it is full): the engaged player deals X damage to 1 character he controls, any character, in one pool; then, if able, 2 progress come off the active location (all it has if fewer). With X = 0 the damage is a complete resolution, so `rr.then` lets the removal follow (recommended, as Hidden Threat's 0 under The Seventh Level; no ruling; the Vision of the Palantir article, https://visionofthepalantir.com/2021/11/16/nightmare-the-redhorn-gate, only restates the text).
- Silvertine Marauder (901303): the Attack bonus, +2 while it attacks and in its Shadow (+2 to the attacker), needs a defending character with a current Willpower of 0 or 1 (modifiers counted, Snowstorm's -1 included); an undefended attack has none, and gives no bonus (literal; the article, https://visionofthepalantir.com/2021/11/16/nightmare-the-redhorn-gate, reads it so).
- Snow-Giant (901304): A:1 attacks the first player in each combat phase from the staging area, as an engaged enemy would: a shadow card dealt to it for the attack and discarded after, defenders declared as usual, and it stays in the staging area; at the beginning of the combat phase like The Nameless Fear (901203:B:1), before the shadow cards are dealt to the engaged enemies (recommended: the engine has no "start of enemy attacks" step, as Flight from Moria says; no source). A:2: after it engages you, discard the ally you control with the highest current Willpower, your choice among those tied, Arwen included when you control her (which loses the game, `dwd.ally-objective-arwen`); with no ally nothing. Its Victory 1 counts for 3B. The article, https://visionofthepalantir.com/2021/11/16/nightmare-the-redhorn-gate, reads A:1 the same way.
- Icy Cavern (901305): X is the number of Snow-trait cards in the staging area, of any type, Icy Cavern, Caradhras, Fanuidhol, Celebdil, Snow Warg, Wolfpelt Goblin, Snow-Giant and a Blinding Blizzard in play included; its Threat is X as the staging step counts it. Travel: it must find a Freezing Cold in the encounter deck or discard pile, which it reveals in full (`rr.reveal`: the first player attaches it to a hero), then shuffles the deck; with no copy to find the players cannot travel here (`rr.travel`, literal; the article names this cost, https://visionofthepalantir.com/2021/11/16/nightmare-the-redhorn-gate). The Shadow gives the defender -1 Willpower for the attack (a duration the card does not print; as the other Shadows of Defense, 02042 and 906203: agent recommendation) and deals the attacker another shadow card; Frozen Silverlode's Shadow is the same record (copyLine).
- Frozen Silverlode (901306): while in the staging area no character gains Willpower from a player card effect: an event, an attachment's bonus, a hero's ability or a constant bonus (Celebrían's Stone), whatever the printed Willpower; reductions still apply and the printed Willpower is not touched (researched: the Vision of the Palantir article, https://visionofthepalantir.com/2021/11/16/nightmare-the-redhorn-gate, names the constant case; a player's article, not a ruling).
- Blinding Blizzard (901307): A:1 is Escape from Khazad-dûm's 03066:B:1 (`noCollection` while in play, here in the staging area). When Revealed puts it in the staging area, where it stays; from then on every progress that would be placed on the current quest stage or on a Snow location, in play or in the staging area, from questing or an effect, is placed on it instead (`progressReplaced`, as 10152:B:2); when it holds 5 it is discarded as the 5th is placed, and a placement larger than what it can still take places the rest as the progress would have gone without it (agent recommendation: the card ends the replacement itself when it is discarded; no source). With two copies in the staging area the one that came first takes the progress and the other what is left (agent recommendation; a placement is not a moment the engine can ask the first player in, so the choice between copies is not offered). The progress on it is counted by Fallen Stones' removal (see above).
- Caradhras the Cruel (901308): every Snow card in the encounter discard pile, of any type, goes on top of the encounter deck shuffled among themselves (random order), then the top card is revealed (the top 2 at 3 or 4 players), in full: the revealed card is probably one of the Snow cards just returned (literal; the article, https://visionofthepalantir.com/2021/11/16/nightmare-the-redhorn-gate, reads it so). The treachery itself is not a Snow card and is discarded after it resolves.
- Table: Arwen is an ally in her controller's play area, her control passing with the first player token (`dwd.ally-objective-arwen`); the progress on Blinding Blizzard is the ordinary progress counter of a card in the staging area, with its limit of 5 (901307:A:2). Fanuidhol's payment is a choice at the quest total, one for each committed hero with a resource, asked of its controller and naming the hero and its pool (a resource spent from that pool; `docs/design-system-table.md`). The progress on Blinding Blizzard reads as "n / 5 progress" in its preview. No new zone or tracker; a held Freezing Cold is drawn under its host hero.
- Easy mode: the Easy counts of `data/scenarios/the-redhorn-gate.json` only (Avalanche! and Mountain Troll are not dealt, the other cards have fewer copies).
