# The Long Dark

Dwarrowdelf part 7 (pack `TLD`), standalone only, on Easy, Normal and Nightmare (deck 9016). Encounter sets The Long Dark, Twists and Turns and Deeps of Moria (the rulesheet names Hazards of the Pit for the third, `dwd.setup-the-long-dark`; the load lists and the Nightmare removals deal Deeps of Moria, see Dwarrowdelf above); the Twists and Turns and Deeps of Moria cards already have records (Into the Pit, Flight from Moria). Cave Torch (03041) is Into the Pit's card with its records. Stages: Journey in the Black Pit (04086), Continuing Eastward (04087); every difficulty deals both (the Nightmare setup card removes and adds no quest card). Sources for every reading below: the rules spans cited, and the community pages listed with each; no FFG or Hall of Beorn ruling on The Long Dark was found, and the two Long Dark threads in the FFG forum archive (https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/63845-lost-in-the-long-dark, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/86489-long-dark) hold the pack's announcement and player opinions only.

- Cave Torch and Durin's Greaves, the Setup and the reveal (04086:A:1, 04088:A:1): "a hero of his choice" is any hero of any player the first player picks (`dwd.cave-torch-the-long-dark`: the first player selects any hero; the Into the Pit and Seventh Level readings for the Torch and the Book of Mazarbul); Durin's Greaves, printed with the same words, is read the same way (agent recommendation: a fan article reads "a hero he controls", https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/02/28/the-long-dark/, no ruling). The Torch is removed from the game if it would leave play (`dwd.cave-torch-the-long-dark`, as in Into the Pit); Durin's Greaves attached to a hero that leaves play is discarded to the encounter discard pile (`rr.attachment-cards`) and can be revealed again. Status: span-settled for the Torch, recommended for Greaves.
- Lost effects (`dwd.lost-effects`): a "Lost:" line is an ability only other card effects trigger, "trigger all Lost: effects in play" runs the Lost line of every faceup card in play (`rr.in-play-and-out-of-play`): locations in the staging area and the active location, enemies in the staging area and engaged, attachments that carry one (Lost and Helpless), and the Nightmare setup card (it "remains active throughout the game"; agent recommendation, a fan article treats its Lost as in play, https://visionofthepalantir.com/2021/10/19/nightmare-the-long-dark/). A shadow card is not in play (`rr.shadow-effects`), so Goblin Warlord's Shadow triggers the Lost effects of the other cards and the Warlord's own Lost only when a Warlord copy is in play. Each copy of a card is its own Lost effect and each triggers. The set is fixed when the trigger happens; the first player orders the effects (`rr.first-player`, `faq.simultaneous-effects`) and each resolves in full before the next (`rr.forced`): when more than one Lost effect fires, he is offered the next effect to resolve one at a time (a card in the choice is a card in play, listed with its owner or place); one effect, or copies of one card, need no choice and go oldest first. Nothing in play with a Lost effect: nothing happens. Status: span-settled except the setup card and the fixed set (recommended; community pages above, no ruling).
- Locate test (`dwd.locate-test`, `dwd.locate-test-example`): made by one player, the one the card names; he may discard 1 card from his hand to discard the top card of the encounter deck; a card with PASS printed in its text box passes the test, and then no ill effects trigger; any other card lets him try again, and the test is failed when he runs out of cards or will not discard more (he may fail it at once by discarding none, the example in the span). The cards go to their discard piles one at a time and the test's own discards stay in the encounter discard pile. PASS counts when it is printed in the card's own text box (the "PASS." line of the ledger: Abandoned Mine, Dwarven Forge, Silent Caverns, Twisting Passage, Foul Air, Gathering Ground, and the Nightmare Goblin Stalker and Trapdoor Spider), whatever the card's type or where it was dealt from. The test is not a quest test or a Fortitude test: no action window between tries. Status: span-settled. Empty encounter deck: the deck is refilled from the discard pile in the quest phase, the test's own discards included, so a card may be discarded twice (as the Fortitude test, `mec113.fortitude-tests`, `rr.running-out-of-cards`, `faq.last-encounter-card`); outside the quest phase it stays empty, no card can be discarded and the test is failed at once. Agent recommendation, no source found (the extended search for a ruling on a locate test with an empty deck found only the general reset rule; https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/287372-guarded-keyword-and-empty-encounter-deck/ is about Guarded). A Nightmare card that makes a test without naming the player (Dreadful Deep's Shadow) makes it by the defending player ("you", `rr.defending-player`; the fan article reads it the same way).
- Journey in the Black Pit (04086:B:1): X is the number of players less one, at least 1 (1 and 2 players: 1 location, 3 players: 2, 4 players: 3). Cards are discarded from the top until X locations are in the discard pile; those locations go to the staging area (not revealed: no When Revealed, no Travel, no Surge) and the other cards the effect discarded are shuffled back with the encounter deck, and nothing else in the discard pile is (the pile holds only what this effect discarded: the stage is revealed at the setup). Literal; source for the amounts https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/02/28/the-long-dark/. B:2: each location in play gets +1 Threat while this stage is the current stage (staging area and active location, Nightmare locations too); if the players quest unsuccessfully (total Willpower less than the staging area's Threat, the existing `questFailed`) the Lost effects trigger after the quest resolves (https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/02/28/the-long-dark/). Status: literal.
- Continuing Eastward (04087:B:1): "Then, trigger all Lost: effects in play" is read as part of the failure: a passed locate test triggers no ill effects (`dwd.locate-test`), so a passed test reveals nothing and triggers no Lost effect; a failed one reveals as many cards as there are players, each resolved in full (`rr.reveal`: staging, When Revealed and keywords, as 04063:B:1), and then triggers the Lost effects. Alternative: the Lost effects trigger whether or not the test is passed; one player-site transcription drops the reveal sentence and reads that way. Status: researched, community: https://lotrlcgblog.wordpress.com/2020/02/09/the-long-dark-quest/ (reads it as failure only, no ruling), https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/02/28/the-long-dark/. The first player makes the test and the reveals. B:2: as stage 1B. B:3: the stage defeated wins the game at once (as the last stage of the other scenarios of the cycle); stage 2 has no "+1 Threat".
- Twisting Passage (04092:A:1): a Forced before progress is placed on it: the first player makes a locate test each time any amount of progress would be placed on it (one test for a quest phase's progress, one for each Cave Torch Action, one for each other effect; https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/02/28/the-long-dark/). A failed test places no progress on it and triggers the Lost effects; the progress is not placed anywhere else, the quest included (the progress "that would be placed on the current quest" is placed on the active location instead, `rr.active-location`, and none is placed on it). A passed test places it all, and what does not fit on the location goes on to the current quest in any phase (the quest phase, a Cave Torch Action, any card effect: `rr.active-location`, as the overflow of an explored active location does). Agent recommendation for the progress that is lost on a failed test, the quest's share included (alternative: the amount goes to the main quest); the test is made again each time, also while it is a staging location a card effect aims at. Status: recommended (Readings to confirm, at the end of this file).
- Gathering Ground (04095:A:1): it is attached to the location in the staging area with the highest sum of its current Threat and its remaining quest points (quest points less the progress on it, both as the cards stand with modifiers); on a tie the first player chooses (`rr.first-player`); with no location in the staging area it cannot attach and is discarded (`rr.attachment-cards`). It counts as a Condition attachment whose text is "Each enemy revealed from the encounter deck gains surge": it works while the host is in play, in the staging area or active, and an enemy that is not revealed (dealt as a shadow card, added by an effect: Cave Torch, Abandoned Mine, Goblin Sneak's shadow) gains nothing (`dwd.revealing-enemies`). Status: span-settled for the tie, no target and the revealing; recommended for "current" values and "remaining" (agent recommendation; the fan article gives only that the card does nothing with no location in play).
- Locate-test cards: Foul Air (04094:A:1) deals 2 damage to every character in play, the heroes and allies of every player, then the Lost effects; Vast and Intricate (04096:A:1) raises each player's threat by 7, removes every progress token from play (the current quest, the active location, the staging area's locations and any side quest, a player card's included) and then triggers the Lost effects; each only when the first player fails the test. Literal.
- Abandoned Mine (04089:A:1): the top 2 Goblin enemies are the two most recently discarded Goblin enemies of the encounter discard pile (a Goblin is an enemy with the trait, skipping other cards); they are returned, not revealed (no When Revealed, no Ambush, no Surge); fewer than two are returned if fewer are there ("if able"). Dwarven Forge (04090:A:1): each player in player order chooses and discards 1 card from his hand, none with an empty hand. Silent Caverns (04091:A:1): every character in play, of every player, is exhausted. Goblin Warlord (04099:A:1): each player chooses and discards 1 ally he controls from play (a discard, not a destroy), none with no ally. Status: literal.
- Fatigue (04093:A:1, A:2): each player in order exhausts one ready character he controls ("if able"); once all have, Fatigue gains surge if any player controls no unexhausted character (any player, not every player). The Shadow: the defending player exhausts one character he controls, chosen after the defender was declared (the defender is already exhausted unless it was a Sentinel). Literal.
- Cave Spider (04097:A:1, A:2): the first player draws 1 card and then chooses and discards 4 cards from his hand (all of it with fewer); "Then" means the discard resolves only if the draw did (`rr.then`: with an empty deck, or a Sentinel of the Deep's bar on the draw, nothing is discarded); the Forced fires for each engagement of the Spider with a player, by an engagement check, a card effect or an optional engagement, and that player discards 1 card. Literal.
- Goblin Sneak (04098:A:1, A:2): the Forced fires after each engagement: the top card of the encounter deck is discarded (not revealed: no When Revealed, no Surge) and, if it is a treachery, the Sneak engages the next player, the player to the left of the one it is engaged with (`rr.next-player`), which is a new engagement and fires the Forced again; with one player there is no next player and it stays (`faq.next-player`); with the deck empty outside the quest phase nothing is discarded and nothing happens. The Shadow adds the Sneak, the shadow card, to the staging area (not revealed; as Goblin Swordsman, `03061:A:2`). Status: span-settled for the next player, literal for the repeat.
- Rock Adder (04100:A:1, A:2): "cannot be attacked unless it has dealt at least 1 damage this round" bars every attack on it (a player's attack, Ranged, an event that attacks) until it deals damage; damage dealt is damage placed on a character after prevention (`rr.damage`: determine, assign, place; damage canceled or reduced to 0 is not dealt), whoever's character, during any phase of the round, noted for the round; it can still take damage from effects (https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/02/28/the-long-dark/). So an Adder that hurts a character in the combat phase's enemy attacks can be attacked in the same phase's player attacks. The Shadow: the defending player chooses and discards 1 character he controls from play (a discard, not a destroy) when this attack is undefended, before the damage is assigned. Status: span-settled for "dealt", literal for the rest.
- Nightmare setup card (901601:A:2, A:3): Durin's Greaves gains surge each time it is revealed; the Lost effect makes the first player choose and discard 1 ally in play, any player's (the first player chooses among all allies, https://visionofthepalantir.com/2021/10/19/nightmare-the-long-dark/); with no ally in play it does nothing. Dreadful Deep may choose it. Status: literal.
- Goblin Stalker (901602): "When Goblin Stalker attacks" is each attack the Stalker makes, in the combat phase or outside it (an immediate attack, an additional attack): after the shadow card the attack deals it, one more is dealt, two in all, each resolved in the shadow step. A:2: each shadow card dealt to it with PASS printed in its text box (a location with PASS, a Foul Air, a Gathering Ground, another Stalker or a Trapdoor Spider) gives it +2 Attack until the end of the phase, one +2 for each such card (they stack); the printed PASS is read when the card is dealt, so the +2 shows before defenders are declared (agent recommendation; the fan article only says "if any shadow card has PASS, the Stalker will hit for +2", no timing). Status: recommended.
- Trapdoor Spider (901603): A:1 discards from play any character it damages (a discard, not a destroy: no destroyed Responses, and a player with no hero left is eliminated, as Wrapped!'s discard of a hero); it applies once the damage is placed and only when damage is placed on the character. A:2 Lost: it engages the last player (the first player in a one-player game, `dwd.last-player`) and makes an immediate attack (`faq.enemy-attacks-outside-combat`: dealt a shadow card), whether it was in the staging area or engaged with someone else; the attack follows only when it did engage the last player, and with the engagement refused (a card that bars engaging it) it makes none (`recommended`, no source found). Status: literal for the engagement, recommended for the refusal.
- Sentinel of the Deep (901604): "cannot draw cards from card effects" bars draws by every card effect, player and encounter cards alike (Cave Spider's draw, Gandalf, Daeron's Runestone), not the draw phase (`rr.effects`: a framework effect is not a card effect); it applies to the player the Sentinel is engaged with, and only while it is engaged. The Shadow: the defending player chooses 1 Lost effect among the cards in play and triggers it (a shadow card is not in play); with none, nothing. Eastern Mines' Shadow (901605:A:2) reads the same way. Status: span-settled for the draw, literal for the choice.
- Eastern Mines (901605:A:1): the players travel to it and the first player then makes the locate test, with the Mines already the active location (the printed text returns it "to the staging area" on failure, so it had left it; `rr.travel`, `rr.step-4-2`); a failed test returns it to the staging area, where the responses to the travel have been offered, and triggers the Lost effects; the players have used their travel for the round (`rr.step-4-2`: one location, one opportunity). A passed test leaves it active. Status: literal (user ruling: the test follows the travel; the record's Travel effect is `Travel` itself, then the test, then `ReturnToStaging` on a failure).
- Bottomless Chasm (901606:A:1, A:2): for each token of progress actually placed on it (by questing as the active location, by a Cave Torch Action, by an effect, the placement that explores it included: its record hears `progressPlacedOnSelf` even once it has left play; progress that goes on to the quest is not counted) one card is discarded from a player's hand, chosen by the players: the first player decides whose hand and which card (`rr.first-player`, "the players get to select who discards what card", https://visionofthepalantir.com/2021/10/19/nightmare-the-long-dark/, community); with no card in any hand nothing is discarded. The Shadow: when this attack destroys a character, the defending player discards his whole hand. Status: recommended for who chooses (the first player decides, `rr.first-player`), literal for the rest.
- Dreadful Deep (901607:A:1): starting with the first player each player in turn chooses a Lost effect that no earlier player chose in this resolution and triggers it before the next player chooses; two copies of one card are two different effects; a player with no Lost effect left to choose does nothing (`rr.effects`: as much as able). The fan article finds the case of more players than Lost effects unclear (https://visionofthepalantir.com/2021/10/19/nightmare-the-long-dark/). Status: recommended. A:2 Shadow: the defending player makes a locate test; when it fails the Lost effects trigger at once and the attacking enemy returns to the staging area after the attack (the words "after this attack" belong to the return; the attack goes on, an enemy in the staging area makes no further attack); agent recommendation, the card gives no order.
- Lost and Helpless (901608): Surge is a keyword. When Revealed the first player attaches it to a hero of any player, chosen by the first player (as Wrapped!, `rr.first-player`), with no hero it is discarded (`rr.attachment-cards`); the Lost effect discards the attached hero from play (a discard, not a destroy; a player's last hero eliminates him). The Shadow: if the defending player has no card in his hand the defending character is discarded from play (`rr.defending-player`: "you"), a discard. Status: recommended for who chooses the hero.
- Table: no tracker or zone is created by a line here (the Table step, `docs/design-system-table.md`, Cards, The Long Dark; `src/play/scenarios/the-long-dark-table.test.ts`). The held cards are Durin's Greaves (04088:A:1) and Lost and Helpless (901608:A:2), each drawn under its hero in every zone the hero can be in, Gathering Ground (04095:A:1) under its location in the staging area or as the active location, the Torch (04086:A:1) as in Into the Pit and the Nightmare setup card in the Nightmare zone. The locate test (04087:B:1) is a choice: discard a hand card or stop; its sheet lists the encounter cards discarded so far with whether each prints PASS and its banner shows each try; a Lost choice (the Lost order of the first player, Sentinel, Eastern Mines, Dreadful Deep) lists the cards in play that hold a Lost line, each row its card.
- Easy mode: the Easy counts of `data/scenarios/the-long-dark.json`; the Easy deck has no Gathering Ground and no Goblin Warlord.
