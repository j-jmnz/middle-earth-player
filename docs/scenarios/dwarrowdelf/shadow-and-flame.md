# Shadow and Flame

Dwarrowdelf part 9 (pack `SaF`), the last, standalone only, on Easy, Normal and Nightmare (deck 9018). Encounter sets Shadow and Flame, Goblins of the Deep and Deeps of Moria (`dwd.setup-shadow-and-flame`). Stages: Nearing the Gate (04138, 9 quest points), The Rear Guard (04139, 16), Last Lord of Moria (04140, 1); every difficulty deals all three (the Nightmare setup card removes no quest card). Setup (04138:A:1) puts Durin's Bane into the staging area and Dark Pit aside (the data's `staging` and `setAside` piles). Sources: the rules spans cited, the official FAQ answers in the Semi-official Errata and FAQ 1.10 (https://alongextendedparty.com/semi-official-errata-faq-1-10/, entries 3.026 and 3.057, FFG rules answers of 2012 and 2013, not yet `faq.*` spans), and the player articles https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/03/10/shadow-and-flame/ and https://visionofthepalantir.com/2022/01/06/nightmare-shadow-and-flame/ (community; the Nightmare article describes older card text for Fiery Depths and Servant of Flame, the scans win).

- Servant of Flame (901807): Hall of Beorn prints Defense 32, the scan Defense 2 (override in `data/overrides.json`); Durin's Bane's stats (Engagement 1, Threat 4, Attack 6, Defense 3, 27 hit points) are as scanned.
- Durin's Bane (04150:A:1, A:2): Regenerate 3 and Indestructible are keywords (no record). "Players cannot play attachments on Durin's Bane" is the flag `noPlayerAttachments` ("Cannot have player attachments"); encounter cards still attach to it (Counter-Spell, Fiery Sword, Many Thonged Whip). It stays in the staging area (`cannotLeaveStaging`, which only Dark Pit's discard overrides, below), adds its Threat 4 to the staging area (the rulesheet says it adds its threat and attacks), and is considered engaged with each player whose threat is 1 or greater (the Watcher's and Thaurdir's `engagedWithAll`, gated on the seat's threat, read live): each such player resolves one attack by it among his enemy attacks of the combat phase, in the order he chooses, and may attack it as an engaged enemy (a Ranged character of another player may join, nobody else pools). A player whose threat reaches 1 during the combat phase before his attacks are done is attacked too, with no shadow card; one whose threat is 0 is not attacked by it at all, and no engagement ever happens. Each attack is dealt a new shadow card, discarded after the attack, so it never holds the benefit of two (`faq.additional-attacks`, `faq.enemy-attacks-outside-combat`). Status: span-settled (`dwd.durins-bane`: attacks in each player's normal attack resolution step, no shadow card for a player raised during combat). The rulesheet says the cards "should be dealt at the beginning of combat"; the engine deals each at the start of that attack, as the Watcher and Thaurdir are (recommended: equivalent unless a later step reads the deck, no source separates them), except that a seat whose threat was below 1 when the combat phase began gets none.
- Damage on Durin's Bane (04150, 04139:B:2, 04141): damage can exceed its 27 hit points (FAQ 3.057, https://alongextendedparty.com/semi-official-errata-faq-1-10/: "You can indeed place more than 27 damage on Durin's Bane", so Regenerate can leave it at 0 remaining hit points). "Remaining hit points" is 27 minus damage, floored at 0; "has 0 hit points" (04139:B:2) is remaining 0, checked after each damage event and not only at the end of a phase, and the stage advances at once. Status: researched (official, FAQ 3.057); the check timing is recommended (the card says "immediately").
- Nearing the Gate (04138:B:1): each player's threat becomes 0 (not lowered by a number); then X = players minus 1 cards are revealed from the encounter deck one at a time and each is staged as in the staging step (a treachery resolves and is discarded, an enemy or location is added to the staging area). With one player none. A player at threat 0 is not engaged with Durin's Bane until his threat is raised (the Refresh phase raises it to 1, so the first round's combat has no regular attack by it: https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/03/10/shadow-and-flame/ says the same). Status: literal.
- The Rear Guard (04139:B:1): "if at least 1 hero committed to the quest this round" counts a hero that was committed when the commit step ended, even if a card (Inner Flame) removed it from the quest later in the phase; at the end of the quest phase, after the quest is resolved, 4 progress are placed on the stage, bypassing the active location, and the stage advances if that fills it. Status: recommended (agent recommendation; the card says "committed", the player article says "committed ... last round"; alternative: a hero still committed at the end of the phase).
- Last Lord of Moria (04140:B:1, B:2): the immediate attack on the first player is made whatever the first player's threat (the card names him, and the threat gate is a line of Durin's Bane's regular attacks; https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/03/10/shadow-and-flame/ reads Leaping Flame's attack as "regardless of his threat"), with a shadow card dealt and discarded after it. Dark Pit is added to the staging area from the set-aside zone. "The players cannot win the game while Durin's Bane is in play" is the table flag of Orc War Party (09017) and Amon Forn; the stage's 1 quest point can be filled meanwhile and progress beyond it stays (`faq.progress-without-advancing`). If Durin's Bane leaves play by Dark Pit's effect the players win at once, with no progress needed (https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/03/10/shadow-and-flame/). Status: literal for the attack; the win is literal.
- Dark Pit (04141:A:1): while it is the active location it gives each player a Refresh Action (a location's gained Action: any player, in the Refresh phase, at the Refresh Action window, once the Regenerate heal of the same step has run: "Regenerate ... occurs before player actions", `dwd.regenerate`): exhaust X characters he controls (X from 0 to 3, a ready character each), discard the top X cards of his own deck (fewer if the deck holds fewer; X may be 0); if the combined printed cost of the discarded cards (no printed cost, an X cost and a hero count 0) is higher than the remaining hit points of Durin's Bane, it is discarded from play. "All discarded cards have a higher combined printed cost" is the total of the discarded cards against the remaining hit points, not each card. The discard overrides "cannot leave the staging area": FAQ 3.026 (https://alongextendedparty.com/semi-official-errata-faq-1-10/): "That card will receive an erratum ... its ability supersedes the game text on Durin's Bane"; the printed 04140:B:2 says the same. Status: researched (official, FAQ 3.026) for the override; the total reading is recommended (agent recommendation; the Palantir article reads it as "the combined cost of discarded cards exceeds his remaining hitpoints").
- Second Hall (04142:A:1): while active, a gained Forced: after any character (a hero or ally of any player) leaves play, one card is revealed from the encounter deck and added to the staging area. Status: literal. Second Deep (04143:A:1): while Durin's Bane has no damage (0 damage tokens) no progress can be placed on Second Deep by anything; the quest progress it would take is lost. Status: literal.
- Fires in the Deep (04144): each player discards 1 ally he controls if able (his choice); if no player discarded one, Fires in the Deep gains surge (a single check for the whole effect, as Chance Encounter 03045's). The Shadow: the defending player discards 1 ally he controls if able. Status: literal.
- Counter-Spell (04145:A:1): attached to Durin's Bane as a Condition attachment (a treachery attaching itself, not a played attachment, so `noPlayerAttachments` does not stop it) with the Forced "When a player plays an event, discard the top card of the encounter deck. If that card is a treachery card, cancel the effects of the event, discard the event player's hand, and discard Counter-spell from play." The event is still played and its cost paid and goes to its owner's discard pile; its effects are canceled (the Fire-filled Trench, 145048, cancels the effects of an event the same way); the hand discard is the event player's and follows the cancel; an enemy or location discarded leaves Counter-Spell in place and the event resolves; each Counter-Spell on Durin's Bane (up to 3) triggers on its own for each event. Status: researched, community (https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/03/10/shadow-and-flame/ reads it this way and the card says so).
- Inner Flame (04146:A:1): Durin's Bane gets +3 Attack until the end of the round (copies stack); the first player may remove 1 hero he controls that is committed to the quest from the quest (the hero stays exhausted, no refund) to cancel this effect; the cancel is the first player's choice at reveal time and cancels only the +3. Inner Shadow (04147): Surge (keyword), then heal 5 damage from Durin's Bane, if able; the same removal cancels only the heal, not the surge (https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/03/10/shadow-and-flame/). The shadows of Inner Flame, Leaping Flame (04146:A:2, 04149:A:2) and Whip Lash (04148:A:2) work only when the attacking enemy is Durin's Bane and do nothing for any other attacker. Status: literal for the card; the cancel scope (the +3 or the heal only, never the surge) is researched, community (the Palantir article above).
- Leaping Flame (04149:A:1): Durin's Bane makes an immediate attack against the first player (a shadow card dealt and resolved for it, discarded after, `faq.enemy-attacks-outside-combat`), whatever his threat. Status: literal; the threat reading is researched, community (the Palantir article above).
- Whip Lash (04148): each player must discard 1 attachment he controls if able (any attachment he controls on any card, his choice); the Shadow discards all attachments the defending player controls. Status: literal.
- Ranging Goblin (04151): When Revealed: each player chooses a hero he controls and deals it 1 damage. Forced: after a character (any player's) leaves play, shuffle Ranging Goblin and the top card of the encounter discard pile into the encounter deck (the Goblin alone when the discard pile is empty); each copy in play triggers for each character. Status: literal.
- Fiery Sword and Many Thonged Whip (04152, 04153): attached to Durin's Bane as Weapon attachments (a treachery attaching itself) with the quoted text; Fiery Sword's +3 Attack stacks with the other; the Whip's Forced fires for every attack Durin's Bane makes, immediate ones included, and the defending player discards 1 card at random from his hand. Status: literal.
- Fiery Sword's and the Whip's Flame trait, Counter-Spell's Shadow trait: they are what Servant of Flame (901807:A:2, a Shadow effect) searches for. It searches the encounter deck (never the Balrog deck) for a treachery with the Shadow or Flame trait, the defending player choosing among those found, reveals it (When Revealed resolves, the treachery is discarded; Counter-Spell and the weapons attach) and shuffles the deck; with none found it only shuffles. Status: recommended (agent recommendation: the card does not say who chooses; the search is the defending player's as he is the one a Shadow effect works against).
- Servant of Flame (901807:A:1): "Forced: While Durin's Bane has no damage" is a constant effect (as Gladden Fields' "Forced: While ...", 01114): +2 Attack and +2 Defense while it has 0 damage tokens. Status: literal (precedent).
- Nightmare, Balrog deck (901801:A:2 to A:4): the 10 encounter cards with the Attack trait (901802 to 901806, two copies each; they carry no When Revealed and are never revealed) are moved out of the encounter deck at Setup into a separate Balrog deck, shuffled. When Durin's Bane would be dealt a shadow card the card comes from the Balrog deck and is discarded to the Balrog discard pile after the attack; at the beginning of each combat phase, and whenever a card is to be dealt from an empty Balrog deck, the Balrog discard pile is shuffled back into the deck. Every other enemy and every additional shadow card for another purpose uses the encounter deck. Beginning of the combat phase: the reshuffle happens before any shadow card of that phase is dealt. Status: literal; the order against other beginning-of-combat effects is recommended (agent recommendation; the Palantir Nightmare article, https://visionofthepalantir.com/2022/01/06/nightmare-shadow-and-flame/, says "reshuffled at least once each turn" and names no order). Inert on the setup card: A:1 and B:1 to B:12 (the removals and the flip are the data's, B:12 is the Play Tip).
- Terrible Strength (901802): for each excess point of combat damage (beyond the remaining hit points of the character this attack damaged, a hero taking undefended damage included) the defending player deals 1 damage to a character he controls other than the one damaged (he chooses for each point and may choose the same one again; a character that cannot be damaged takes none), and every character so damaged is exhausted. Status: recommended (agent recommendation; the Nightmare article leaves "several characters?" open, no official source).
- The Dark Fire (901803): heals 1 damage from Durin's Bane for each point of damage the attack dealt (damage actually placed, after shields and cancels, undefended damage included). Blazing Grip (901804): the whole shadow effect cannot be canceled (A Foe Beyond's and Sudden Pitfall's reading, Flight from Moria above): discard all cards attached to the defending character and deal it 1 damage; nothing happens when the attack is undefended. Status: recommended (precedent above).
- Morgoth's Bidding (901805): if the attack destroys a character (a hero killed by undefended damage included), Durin's Bane makes an additional attack against the defending player after this one (`faq.additional-attacks`: the earlier shadow cards are discarded first and a new one dealt), whatever his threat. Flame of Udûn (901806): Durin's Bane is dealt 3 additional shadow cards from the encounter deck, which resolve with the attack (fewer if the deck holds fewer, `rr.step-6-2`), discarded to the encounter discard pile. Status: literal.
- Captain of Mordor (901808): -20 engagement cost for each enemy in play (floored at 0, `rr.modifiers`), in the staging area or engaged; its Shadow: if the attack destroys a character the attacking enemy cannot take damage until the end of the round. Status: literal.
- Fiery Depths (901809): while in the staging area no progress can be placed on it (a flag read where progress is placed; explored by travel only). Travel: each player must move 1 damage from Durin's Bane to a hero to travel here: the cost is unpayable unless Durin's Bane holds at least as many damage as there are players, and each player chooses any hero in play (the card says "a hero", not "a hero he controls"); a move is not damage dealt (no shield, cancel or "deals damage" trigger; a hero that reaches its hit points is destroyed). Status: recommended (agent recommendation; the Nightmare article's older text had a threat cost, so no source confirms the hero's owner).
- Crumbling Stairs (901810): while it is in the staging area each event card costs 1 additional matching resource to play from hand (the costs family: The West Bank's line for events, 02019, with the source's zone being the staging area); a neutral event is paid with a resource of the hero that pays its cost. The Shadow: if the attack destroys a character, the defending player discards all event cards in his hand. Status: literal.
- Table (docs/design-system-table.md, Cards, Shadow and Flame): no new dial; the Nightmare setup card creates a Balrog deck and a Balrog discard pile, drawn as readouts with their counts as the second slot's pair (901801:A:2 to A:4: no card line puts them anywhere else); Counter-Spell, Fiery Sword and Many Thonged Whip are drawn under Durin's Bane in the staging area (held cards under their host, as the Nameless enemies' attachments; Durin's Bane never leaves the staging area); the seats Durin's Bane is considered engaged with (threat 1 or more, 04150:A:2) are said in its preview; the Dark Pit Action and Fiery Depths' travel cost lead with the player, and each hero row of the latter names its owner (the pick spans seats). Status: agent recommendation for the wording, no source.
- Easy mode: the Easy counts of `data/scenarios/shadow-and-flame.json` only (Fires in the Deep is not dealt).
- Out of Sight (04081, "Enemies engaged with you cannot attack you this phase") and Hobbit-sense (06065, "Enemies engaged with you do not attack this round") stop the attacks of Durin's Bane (04150) against the player it is considered engaged with: it is not immune to player card effects, and it is engaged with a player for a card that reads "engaged with" (`recommended`; docs/brief.md, Considered to be engaged; FAQ 1.50's Feint example is the same case for a pick).
