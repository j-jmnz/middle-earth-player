# Road to Rivendell

Dwarrowdelf part 5 (pack `RtR`), standalone only, on Easy, Normal and Nightmare (deck 9014). Encounter sets Road to Rivendell, The Misty Mountains and Plundering Goblins, the last two from the Khazad-dûm box (`dwd.setup-road-to-rivendell`); Arwen Undómiel (04041) is set aside until Setup puts her into play (`dwd.ally-objective-arwen-road-to-rivendell`). The Nightmare setup card removes the quest card Orc Outpost (stage 2) and Ambush in the Night (901402) takes its place, so Nightmare deals 04038, 901402, 04040 (`questDeckCodes`). The Misty Mountains and Plundering Goblins cards already have records (The Redhorn Gate, The Seventh Level); the new cards are the Road to Rivendell set and the Nightmare deck:

- Ambush (04049, 04050, 04052, 901403 to 901405; `dwd.ambush`): a new printed keyword. When an enemy with it enters play, each player in turn, the first player first, makes an engagement check against that enemy alone (`rr.engagement-check`: threat at least its engagement cost); the first player to engage it ends the checks, and an enemy nobody engages stays in the staging area. The enemy has entered the staging area before the checks, so an attachment that says "enters the staging area" and attaches itself to it (Ranger Spikes) stops them (researched: the FAQ answer quoted at https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/79049-question-about-ambush%E2%80%A6). It fires for an enemy revealed in the staging step, at Setup (04038:A:1; https://visionofthepalantir.com/2021/08/12/nightmare-road-to-rivendell/ says so for the Nightmare setup), added to the staging area by a search, or returned from the discard pile (Orc Ambush); it does not fire for an enemy that is already in play and moves to the staging area (a Shadow's return, Tumultuous Assault). An engagement made this way is an engagement for every "after it engages" Forced line. Recommended: an enemy put into play already engaged with a player makes no ambush check (the checks look only at the staging area; the FFG forum split, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/193908-ambush-keyword-and-dunedain-hunter; no official ruling). Several enemies that enter together (Orc Ambush's return, 901402's discards) resolve one after the other, in the order they enter (agent recommendation). An enemy that engages does not add its threat to the staging area (`rr.engaged`).
- Goblin Gate (04043:A:1): while active, the first enemy revealed from the encounter deck each round has ambush, added to any it prints, and if it engages a player, by the ambush checks or any other engagement, it makes an immediate attack (a shadow card dealt and resolved, `rr.defending-player`), and attacks again in the combat phase; the defender is chosen as in any attack (researched, community with a designer quote relayed: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/62435-ambush-question). Only a reveal from the encounter deck counts (not a search or a return). The round's first reveal is counted whether or not Goblin Gate was active then, as 09069:B:1's tally (Reading 11 of The Weather Hills), so an enemy revealed before it became active leaves no first enemy that round (researched, community: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/108999-few-questions-about-rules, the ability is not active until after an enemy is revealed). The ambush lasts until the end of the round (recommended: the card speaks of each round; no ruling).
- Barren Hills (04045:A:1): while active the ambush keyword has no effect, printed or gained, so no ambush check is made and Goblin Gate's immediate attack cannot follow; the enemies stay in the staging area with their threat (literal; https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/02/24/road-to-rivendell/ reads it so). Nightmare's Treacherous Bluffs and the setup card's threat line need an engagement, so they do not fire either.
- Orc Outpost (04039:B:1): searches the encounter deck and discard pile (not the victory display) for Goblin Gate, adds it to the staging area without revealing it (nothing resolves, as Warg Lair 03059:A:1) and does not shuffle (the card prints no shuffle); then, if there is no active location, Goblin Gate becomes the active location. Goblin Gate already in the staging area is not found and is made active the same way; one in the victory display, or not in the deck or discard pile, is not found (literal; https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/02/24/road-to-rivendell/: it adds nothing if it is not in the deck or discard pile).
- Approaching Rivendell (04040): B:1 reveals 1 card per player in full, the same effect as 04011:B:1 (a copy). B:2: no heal effect takes damage off any character, Arwen's included; damage prevented, canceled, redirected or taken as threat is not healing (`ModifyStat cannotBeHealed`, as 09055:A:2; https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/02/24/road-to-rivendell/). B:3: defeating the stage wins the game: the record is empty and the engine's final-stage rule wins it, as 04013:B:4's does.
- Arwen Undómiel (04041): the same three lines as 04014 (the Redhorn Gate's Arwen): the first player's control follows the first player token and is no loss when a first player is eliminated (`dwd.ally-objective-arwen-road-to-rivendell`, `faq.first-player-elimination`); after she exhausts, a hero of any player, the controller's choice, gets 1 resource, a Response the controller may decline; her leaving play for any reason loses the game. The records are the copies of 04014:A:1 to A:3. Span-settled for control and loss, literal for "choose a hero" (the same reading as The Redhorn Gate).
- Ruined Road (04042:A:1): the Response is the first player's: he chooses between 2 progress on Ruined Road or readying 1 hero he controls, and must take one; an option that does nothing, readying with no exhausted hero, may still be chosen (recommended, as Fallen Stones in The Redhorn Gate; the Vision of the Palantir article, https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/02/24/road-to-rivendell/, writes it "you may" and no ruling was found). Shadow (04042:A:2, 04045:A:2, 04047:A:2, 04051:A:3): the attacker returns to the staging area after it attacks, only if it survived (as the Wargs' 01085:A:1); no ambush check follows.
- Pathless Country (04044): A:1 is a Forced after progress is placed on it, one removal for each placement however many tokens, from questing, travel or an effect; a placement that reaches its quest points explores it first (`rr.explored`) and nothing is removed (literal: the `progressPlaced` trigger fires only for a card still in play). A:2 deals 1 damage to each ally in play, every player's, Arwen included.
- Sleeping Sentry (04046): A:1 deals 1 damage to each exhausted character of every player, Arwen and allies included, and then exhausts every ready character, so none of those is damaged; Arwen's Response hears her exhausting (`rr.exhausted`, literal). The Shadow discards every exhausted character the defending player controls, heroes included, and the defender itself, which exhausted when declared (`rr.defending-player`: the player attacked also when the attack is undefended); the printed text is not a one-time check (literal; https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/02/24/road-to-rivendell/).
- Followed by Night (04047:A:1): the first player chooses an option, and an option that does nothing may still be chosen: 1 damage to every ally in play, every player's, and surge; or every enemy engaged with a player makes an immediate attack, each one dealing and resolving its own shadow card, in the order of 900208:A:1's `ImmediateAttackEach`. Surge is given by the first option alone (recommended: no ruling found, the 2012 FFG forum thread on it recalls none; same reading as Fallen Stones).
- Orc Ambush (04048:A:2): Surge is a keyword. Every Orc enemy in the staging area engages the first player, whatever its engagement cost, each engagement an `engages` event for the Forced lines (Goblin Taskmaster, Orc Raiders, Wild Bear), in the order the first player chooses (agent recommendation). With no Orc in the staging area, every Orc enemy in the encounter discard pile returns to the staging area, and each, entering play, makes its ambush checks (literal; https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/02/24/road-to-rivendell/). They do not engage the first player this time. Orc is the Trait, Goblin Orcs included.
- Goblin Taskmaster (04049:A:2): after it engages, the engaged player deals 2 damage to 1 character he controls, hero or ally, his choice, even a character the damage destroys (literal; as Rocky Crags' payment in The Redhorn Gate).
- Orc Raiders (04050:A:2): the engaged player chooses and discards 2 attachments he controls, on any card (his characters, an enemy, a location), 1 when he controls just one, none when none (`if able`; https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/02/24/road-to-rivendell/, literal).
- Crebain (04051): Surge is a keyword. While in the staging area no encounter card effect can be canceled: When Revealed, Forced and Shadow effects of an encounter card, as 18038:A:1 and 41010:A:1 with the condition in the staging area (`encounterUncancelable`; literal).
- Wild Bear (04052:A:2): after it engages a player, whenever, it makes an immediate attack; with Goblin Gate's ambush and Followed by Night it can attack more than once a round (literal; https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/02/24/road-to-rivendell/).
- Nightmare setup card (901401): A:1 and B:1 to B:15 are the deck-building text (inert). A:2 fires when any enemy with the ambush keyword, printed or gained, engages a player during the quest phase, by an ambush check or a player's choice (https://visionofthepalantir.com/2021/08/12/nightmare-road-to-rivendell/ names the optional engagement): it contributes its Threat to the staging area's total until the end of the phase, while it stays in play (`engagedThreat`, as 12075:A:1; recommended for the end: the card says "until the end of the phase").
- Ambush in the Night (901402): A:1 is the 2A side's When Revealed: it searches the encounter deck, the discard pile and the victory display for Goblin Gate and adds it to the staging area without revealing it, if able; shuffles the discard pile into the deck; discards from the top until X enemies are discarded, X the number of players, or the deck is out (a discarded treachery or location is not reshuffled, https://visionofthepalantir.com/2021/08/12/nightmare-road-to-rivendell/); then reveals each enemy it discarded, in the order discarded, in full: keywords, When Revealed and ambush resolve and the enemy is added to the staging area (`rr.reveal`; the card says "Reveal", not "Add"). Goblin Gate is not made the active location. B:1 fires as the stage flips: the first player loses control of Arwen and attaches her to Goblin Gate as a guarded objective, whichever zone Goblin Gate is in; she stays in play with her damage, tokens and attachments, can be damaged and her leaving play loses the game; with no Goblin Gate in play she stays with the first player (recommended; it is always found unless the earlier search found nothing). While she is attached the first player cannot gain control of her, and the stage's last line stops progress; when Goblin Gate leaves play, explored or otherwise, she is no longer guarded and her constant gives control to the first player (`rr.guarded`, `dwd.ally-objective-arwen-road-to-rivendell`; `GuardReturnedBy` of Sneaking Off 19044:A:1). B:2: Goblin Gate's quest points are its printed ones plus 1 for each enemy engaged with a player, read live; a location whose progress then equals its quest points is explored at once (`rr.explored`, literal). B:3: while no player controls Arwen, no progress is placed on the stage, whether from questing or an effect: the quest total is not placed, nor kept (as 12039:B:3; literal).
- Uruk Elf-stalker (901403:A:2): after it attacks and destroys a character, by the defender's damage or by damage assigned to a hero, it deals 1 damage to Arwen, whoever controls her or whatever guards her (https://visionofthepalantir.com/2021/08/12/nightmare-road-to-rivendell/, literal). If it destroyed Arwen the game is already lost.
- Goblin Trapper (901404): A:2 makes the engaged player exhaust a ready character he controls, his choice, hero or ally; with none ready nothing happens (https://visionofthepalantir.com/2021/08/12/nightmare-road-to-rivendell/). The Shadow adds it to the staging area (the Spearman's 03031:A:2, a copy); it does not make an ambush check, being in play already.
- Raiding Party (901405:A:2): each player in order, the first player first, discards the top card of the encounter deck (the deck is refilled from the discard pile as `rr.running-out-of-cards` says); a player whose card is an enemy with ambush, printed or gained, makes an engagement check against that enemy alone, no other player does (researched, community: https://visionofthepalantir.com/2021/08/12/nightmare-road-to-rivendell/). Recommended for where the enemy is: it is in the discard pile; if the check engages it, it leaves the discard pile and enters that player's play area, engaged (and its Forced line fires, a second Raiding Party included), and if not it stays in the discard pile; the alternative, adding it to the staging area first, is not what the card says. No ruling.
- Vicious Trackers (901406): Surge is a keyword. While it is engaged with a player, that player counts +20 threat in every engagement check, an ambush check, the encounter phase's, an effect's (Tumultuous Assault), and not in a player's optional engagement (researched, community: https://visionofthepalantir.com/2021/08/12/nightmare-road-to-rivendell/; the optional-engagement half is a literal reading of "engagement checks only", `rr.engagement-check`). The two copies add.
- Treacherous Bluffs (901407): A:1 fires on any engagement of an enemy with the ambush keyword, printed or gained, by a check or a player's choice, in any phase, while Bluffs is in the staging area or active: +2 Threat until the end of the phase, as many times as it happens, and for each copy (https://visionofthepalantir.com/2021/08/12/nightmare-road-to-rivendell/). The Shadow: the damage dealt by the attack beyond the remaining hit points of the defending character, the excess, must be assigned to Arwen, whoever controls her or guards her; an undefended attack has no excess (`rr.damage`, literal; as 19080:A:3, 11055:A:2).
- Concealed Woods (901408): A:1 is Goblin Gate's tally on an enemy with ambush: the round's first enemy that has the ambush keyword, printed or gained, revealed from the encounter deck gains surge, counted from the round's start whether or not Concealed Woods was active (Reading 11 of The Weather Hills; recommended, as Goblin Gate above). A:2 reads the attacked player when the Shadow resolves: +2 Attack if the attacking enemy is engaged with the first player (literal).
- Tumultuous Assault (901409:A:1): every engaged enemy of any player with the ambush keyword goes to the staging area, which is no entering of play and so no ambush check; then the players make engagement checks against each enemy in the staging area, the enemies already there too, outside the encounter phase, pass after pass until a pass engages none, the first player choosing among a tie for the highest engagement cost, as `MakeEngagementChecks` (12122:A:1). An enemy engaging again fires its Forced lines again (researched, community, one article: https://visionofthepalantir.com/2021/08/12/nightmare-road-to-rivendell/; the alternative, one check for each player as the encounter phase does, is not what "each enemy" says). It gains surge when no enemy with the ambush keyword is in play at all, in the staging area or engaged, when the effect resolves (literal; the article).
- Table: Arwen is an ally in her controller's play area, her control passing with the first player token; under 901402 she is drawn under Goblin Gate in its zone (the guarded pair in the staging area, a peek under the active location), a held card with no controller (`rr.guarded`), and Goblin Gate's quest points read live with the enemies engaged (`docs/design-system-table.md`, Cards; `src/play/scenarios/road-to-rivendell-table.test.ts`). No new zone or tracker; an ambush check needs no choice (an engagement check is a comparison), a tied choice among enemies is the first player's (Tumultuous Assault, Orc Ambush's order).
- Easy mode: the Easy counts of `data/scenarios/road-to-rivendell.json` only (Sleeping Sentry, Orc Ambush and card 03030 are not dealt, other cards have fewer copies).
