# Flight from Moria

Dwarrowdelf part 3 (pack `KD`), standalone only, on Easy, Normal and Nightmare (deck 9012). Encounter sets Flight from Moria, Hazards of the Pit, Deeps of Moria and Plundering Goblins (`dwd.setup-flight-from-moria`); the other sets' cards are Into the Pit's and The Seventh Level's records. The quest deck is the Mount Gundabad shape (`dwd.creating-the-quest-deck`, `dwd.bypassing-a-quest-card`, as `emce.gundabad-quest-deck` and `emce.bypassing`): the stage 2s are shuffled with side A up beneath stage 1, there is no stage 3, and a stage 2B shows only from the beginning of the staging step. Official sources read for the whole scenario: the FAQ (`faq.search-effects` search shuffles the deck, `faq.victory-display`, `faq.reveal-and-add`, `faq.cancel-encounter-effect` and `faq.cancel-encounter-card` reveal and cancel, `faq.immune-expanded` immune to player card effects; official), https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/02/16/flight-from-moria/ and https://visionofthepalantir.com/2021/05/15/nightmare-flight-from-moria (player articles, not rulings).

- Stage 1 (03069): Setup A:1 prepares the quest deck (`ShuffleStages`, the Nightmare codes in the same list), puts The Nameless Fear in the staging area (the scenario's pile does), and shuffles 1 copy of A Foe Beyond per player back in: the other copies are set aside by the pile, one `ShuffleSetAsideIntoEncounterDeck` per player, and with fewer copies than players (Easy deals 1) as many as there are go in (`rr.effects`, span-settled). 1B prints no quest points: its When Revealed reveals 1 card per player into the staging area (`ForEachPlayer` of `RevealEncounter`, as 03064:B:1), then adds stage 1 to the victory display and the quest advances to the top stage 2, revealed side A. The Nameless Fear's X is 0 during those reveals (the display is empty until "Then, add"), so a Dismay and Fear or A Foe Beyond revealed there does nothing (literal). Stage 1 is the only time the players are "on stage 1" (New Devilry surges there). Researched: stage 1 goes to the victory display and the first round is played on a random stage 2 (https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/02/16/flight-from-moria/, a player article; `dwd.bypassing-a-quest-card`: a completed stage is added to the victory display or wins the game).
- Flip at the staging step (every 2A, 03070 to 03076 A:1, 901202 to 901204 A:1): a stage 2 arrives side A and only its side A text works; it shows side B at the beginning of the staging step of the quest phase, after the characters are committed, and its When Revealed resolves then, before the first card is revealed (`dwd.creating-the-quest-deck`: "the current quest card is revealed only at the beginning of the staging step"; span-settled). The first stage 2 is flipped at the first round's staging step; a stage that arrives by defeat or bypass waits for the next round's. Mount Gundabad flips at the beginning of the quest phase and none of its stages has a side B When Revealed, so the flip here is its own timing and runs the B side's When Revealed.
- Bypass (the nine "Players may bypass this quest card at the end of the combat phase" lines): the first player answers for the group; the stage loses its progress, goes to the bottom of the quest deck side A up, and the top stage 2A is revealed; it is not defeated, so no "when this stage is defeated" Forced hears it (`dwd.bypassing-a-quest-card`, span-settled). Offered only while the quest deck holds another stage (Mount Gundabad reading 4, `rr.effects`; recommended, no source). Hasty Council and the two winning stages' defeat are the only ways out that are not a bypass.
- Stage 2s that go to the victory display when defeated are the ones that print Victory (03070 to 03074, 901203, 901204); Blocked by Shadow and Escape from Darkness print none and win the game (03075:B:5, 03076:B:4, 901202:B:3: `WinGame` when the stage is defeated, the quest deck's other stages left as they are; literal).
- The Nameless Fear (03025): "Immune to player card effects" is the printed text and `faq.immune-expanded` says what it means: player cards cannot target or change it, framework effects (progress, an attack by a character, an engagement check) and encounter and quest card effects still do. The Khazad-dûm insert paraphrases it as "immune to card effects" (`dwd.the-nameless-fear`); the card wins (`.claude/rules/authority.md`), and Nightmare's Blocked by Flame needs a quest card to change it (literal, researched: `faq.immune-expanded`). "Cannot engage or be engaged" is `cannotBeEngaged`; its engagement cost 50 and the stats X, X, X with 27 hit points are as scanned. X is the total of the Victory printed on every card in the victory display (stages, enemies, locations, An Evil Fortune), read live (`dwd.the-nameless-fear`: recalculated whenever points are added or removed; `faq.victory-display`: cards in the victory display can be referenced; https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/62047-flight-from-moria says encounter cards with Victory count, community; span-settled). Orc Drummer's and Burning Low's +Threat apply to it as to any enemy.
- Search for an Exit, Heading Down and Heading Up (03070, 03071): each Forced fires at the end of any quest phase in which that stage shows side B and the other stage is in the victory display; it moves the other stage from the victory display into the quest deck, side A up, and shuffles the deck (X falls by its Victory). A stage defeated in that quest phase has left play before the phase ends and hears nothing (literal; `faq.victory-display`). 03071:B has the bypass line on the scan though Hall of Beorn's text lacks it (the ledger reads the card).
- Hasty Council (03074): its When Revealed, at the flip, shuffles every A Foe Beyond of the encounter discard pile into the encounter deck (the deck is shuffled), looks at the top 2 stages of the quest deck (as many as there are; the deck always holds at least Blocked by Shadow's or Escape from Darkness's stage while it is current), and the first player chooses 1 (`rr.first-player`): it becomes the current quest and is flipped at once, so its When Revealed resolves inside this effect; the other goes to the bottom of the quest deck, side A up. Then Hasty Council, which has no quest points, goes to the victory display: so its 2 points are not in the display while the chosen stage's When Revealed resolves (recommended: "Then" orders the effect, the reveal of the chosen card is part of "become the active quest"; alternative, the display first, which would raise The Nameless Fear's X for an A Wrong Turn reveal; no source, the Vision of the Palantir article and the insert only say the card is the exception to the staging-step flip). The replaced Hasty Council is added to the display, not defeated (no advance, no defeat Forced).
- New Devilry (03026): on any stage but stage 1 the current stage, with its progress removed, is shuffled into the quest deck (side A, not bypassed: Swarming Goblins and a bypass cost do not hear it) and "a new quest card" is revealed: the top stage 2 of the shuffled deck, which can be the stage just shuffled. It is revealed side A and flipped at the next staging step, since the insert names only effects that "reveal and flip" as the exception (`dwd.creating-the-quest-deck`; span-settled). On stage 1 it gains surge. A New Devilry that is canceled leaves the stage in place. With an empty quest deck the stage is put back and revealed again (shuffled into a deck of one; recommended, agent recommendation, no source; not reachable in this scenario, where the deck holds a stage while a stage that can be current is). The Shadow raises the attacked player's threat by The Nameless Fear's current Threat when the attack is undefended (literal).
- A Foe Beyond (03028): the last player (`dwd.last-player`) chooses a hero he controls and it takes damage equal to The Nameless Fear's current Attack, read as the effect resolves (0 means none: "an amount read may come to 0"). "This effect cannot be canceled" is the whole When Revealed, like Sudden Pitfall's (`uncancelable`, `faq.cancel-encounter-effect`: a canceled effect never triggers): no cancel of the When Revealed, and no ability that cancels the damage it deals; redirecting or absorbing that damage (Dori, a character taking it) is not a cancel and still works (recommended; no official source; the Nightmare article says redirection still works and a reader doubts it). The Shadow deals the same damage to the defending character, nothing when undefended. Pursued by Shadow resolves the When Revealed of each copy it discards (below) as if revealed there, and the same reading applies.
- Shadow of Fear (03027): the first player attaches it to one of his own heroes without a Shadow of Fear (Limit 1 per hero); with none eligible it is discarded with no effect (an attachment cannot enter play without a target, `rr.attachment-cards`; recommended). As a Condition attachment the hero cannot exhaust (so it cannot quest, attack or defend, and any cost that exhausts it fails) and cannot ready (an already exhausted hero stays exhausted), and its printed text box is blank: abilities, keywords and traits (`rr.blank`, span-settled). The Action is any player's (`dwd.encounter-card-actions`): 3 resources from the attached hero's pool, whoever's hero it is, discard the card (as the Adaptations of 41044 to 41047). Shape of 10080 No Rest (`CountsAs`, `AttachesTo` `limitPerHost`).
- Abandoned Tools (03029, 03073, 03076): Guarded applies when it is revealed from the encounter deck (`rr.guarded`); an effect that adds it to the staging area (Narrow Paths) does not reveal it, so it comes unguarded (`rr.guarded`, `faq` 1.22 "revealed" needs the word reveal; https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/106459-kahzad-dum-narrow-paths, community; span-settled). The Action (03029:A:2) exhausts a ready hero of any player to claim it only while it lies in the staging area with no encounter card attached, then it attaches to that hero; the exhaust is a cost, so a hero that cannot exhaust (Shadow of Fear) is not offered and with no other hero the Action is not offered at all (the pick filter `canExhaust`; literal); unlike Book of Mazarbul, the insert does not let a hero take it from another hero, so a held Tools is taken only after it returns to the staging area (literal; the alternative is the Book's rule, `dwd.book-of-mazarbul`, which is that card's own). Restricted counts as for any attachment.
- Narrow Paths (03073): B:1 each player chooses 1 questing character he controls; every other questing character stays committed, exhausted and questing but does not count its Willpower (the quest total and effects that sum committed Willpower) until the end of the round (the table holds `ignoresWillpower` to the end of the round and each chosen character `countsWillpower`, so a character that commits later, or was not questing when the choice was made, adds nothing; recommended, no source): "turn" is not a rules term and only this card uses it, and every Willpower count it can change falls in this quest phase, so round or phase play the same except for Dismay and Fear's count (recommended, no source: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/106459-kahzad-dum-narrow-paths only says the others add nothing). B:2 "the 1st progress token": the Forced fires when the stage goes from no progress to some progress, once for the placement, so a Narrow Paths that was bypassed (it loses its progress) fires again on a later first progress; the search of the encounter deck and discard pile finds the Tools only there (nothing when it is attached or in the staging area) and the deck is shuffled afterwards (`faq.search-effects`, researched; recommended for the repeat: the alternative is once a game, which the card does not say).
- Blocked by Shadow (03075): B:1 the first player chooses one: each player in player order discards the top card of the encounter deck and a treachery discarded eliminates the player who discarded it (before the next player discards; a discarded card is not revealed, so no When Revealed; the deck runs out and resets as `rr.running-out-of-cards` says, B:1 being in the quest phase), or the stage goes to the bottom of the quest deck, side A up, with its progress removed, and the next stage is revealed side A (not flipped: the card says "reveals", `dwd.creating-the-quest-deck`; not a bypass). The second choice is not offered with an empty quest deck (`rr.the-word-choose`, no valid target; recommended). 9 quest points; defeating it wins.
- Escape from Darkness (03076): B:1 while it is the current quest showing side B, Abandoned Tools has a Refresh Action: exhaust the attached hero to put 1 progress token on it (the Action is a record on the stage, not on the Tools: a record cannot claim another card's line, and a quest card's Action is any player's, `rr.action`, so whoever controls the hero uses it; the Tools must be attached to a hero, who must be ready and able to exhaust, the exhaust being a cost, and the stage's progress is placed only once it is paid; a Refresh Action is an action in the refresh phase's windows; recommended for the record's place, agent recommendation). B:3 no progress can be placed on it by questing or any card, only by the Tools: the Action is the exception (`noQuestProgress` and `AddStageProgress`, as Explore the Island 11025:B:1). 4 quest points, so 4 uses; faster only with a readying effect (Palantir article). Defeating it wins.
- Great Cave-troll (03042): `dwd.immune-to-ranged-damage` is the reading: characters that attack it by the ranged keyword deal no damage, a ranged character that attacks it another way does (span-settled); the printed "No attachments can be played" is the Nazgûl of Dol Guldur's 01102:A:1 text and its record `cannotHaveAttachments` (copy). Victory 3 raises X when it is defeated, not when it is discarded.
- Orc Drummer (03043): each enemy in play, in the staging area or engaged, gets +X Threat while the Drummer is in the staging area, X the players; The Nameless Fear is an enemy and gets it (literal). The Drummer itself too.
- Stray Goblin (03044), The Mountains' Roots (03047): the scans print the X stats (Stray Goblin Threat and Attack, The Mountains' Roots Threat and quest points); X is the number of players, and the Shadow's +X Attack the same (`FAQ 1.40`, X is 0 unless the card says; literal). Massing in the Deep (03046): Doomed 1 is a keyword; "reveal X additional cards and add them to the staging area" reveals them one after another, each resolved in full (`rr.reveal`, `faq.reveal-and-add`; copy of 01112:A:1's shape); its Shadow is the same +X.
- Chance Encounter (03045): the topmost enemy of the encounter discard pile is put into play engaged with the first player, not revealed (no When Revealed, as 142089:A:2); with no enemy in the discard pile Chance Encounter gains surge, (literal). The Shadow's +3 applies when the attacked player is the first player.
- Nightmare setup card (901201): A:2 a bypassed stage is shuffled into the quest deck, which is shuffled, and then the top stage is revealed, so the bypassed stage can come straight back (https://visionofthepalantir.com/2021/05/15/nightmare-flight-from-moria says it "can bring a card back quickly"; researched, a player article); a bypass is offered only with another stage in the quest deck. A:3 while Escape from Darkness is the current quest, the Tools' Action can be used once a round for the group (the rule is the setup card's, nothing else changes). B:1 to B:14 are the deck builder: `questDeckCodes` deals the stages as 03070 to 03074, 901202, 03076, 901203, 901204 (Blocked by Shadow's place goes to Pursued by Shadow, the other two follow; checked), and the lines are inert in `data/printed/nightmare.json`. 901201:A:1 is inert.
- Pursued by Shadow (901202): its Forced at the end of the round shuffles the encounter discard pile into the encounter deck, discards the top 5 cards one after another, and then resolves the When Revealed of each A Foe Beyond among them in the order discarded (literal; the Nightmare article: "any A Foe Beyond discarded this way is resolved"; they stay in the discard pile); no more than 1 progress a round (`progressCap`, the quest and any card together, the Nightmare article says at least five rounds).
- Blocked by Flame (901203): The Nameless Fear attacks each player in turn order from the staging area during the combat phase, each attack with a new shadow card that is discarded after it (`faq.additional-attacks`), at the beginning of the combat phase, before shadow cards are dealt to the engaged enemies and before they attack, one attack a player, each dealt its own shadow card and the earlier one discarded before the next is dealt (`recommended`: the card says only "during the combat phase" and no step; no official ruling or forum answer exists: the FAQ and Rules Reference say nothing of it, and the two player articles, https://visionofthepalantir.com/2021/05/15/nightmare-flight-from-moria ("will continue to attack each player in turn order from the staging area with a shadow card") and the Hall of Beorn card page https://www.hallofbeorn.com/LotR/Details/Search-for-an-Exit-Blocked-by-Flame-FfMN, name no step either. The alternative, the start of the enemy attack step, needs a trigger point the engine has not got and moves no outcome the card states; Durin's Bane, `dwd.durins-bane`, resolves in each player's normal enemy attack step, the other reading). Every character, not only ranged ones, may be declared as an attacker against it in the staging area (`attackableByAny`; `faq.immune-expanded`: an attack by a character is not a player card effect); each player attacks with his own characters in his turn and the damage on it adds up. With 10 or more damage on it all its damage is removed (not just 10: the card says "all damage", the Nightmare article's "leftover damage stays" is wrong against the card) and the stage advances, victory 1 into the display; checked after each damage event, not only at the combat phase's end (recommended). No progress can be placed on it by questing (no quest points).
- Ghâsh (901204): at the flip, 1 damage to each questing character; 1 quest point.
- Swarming Goblins (901205): each copy engaged with a player gives its own Forced; when a stage is bypassed, as it happens and before the next stage is revealed, each engaged copy searches the encounter discard pile (not the deck, so nothing is shuffled) for a copy of Goblin Spearman and puts it into play engaged with its player; with none in the discard pile nothing (recommended for the timing, the Nightmare article for the rest). Two engaged copies fire twice. A stage shuffled away by New Devilry or Blocked by Shadow is not bypassed.
- Coal-Black Orc (901206): Threat and Attack are X, X the Victory in the display, read live like The Nameless Fear's; its Shadow +X Attack the same.
- Shadowed Corridor (901207): "as an additional cost to bypass" is paid when the first player chooses to bypass: each player raises his threat by 2 for each copy in play (in the staging area or active), the copies stack (the Nightmare article), and a raise that eliminates a player still happens (the bypass is optional); recommended for the elimination. The Shadow: if the defending character is a hero all the resources in its pool are discarded and the attacking enemy is dealt another shadow card for each (as Deep-Mines' 901107:A:2, `DealShadow` `additional`), the new cards resolving in this shadow step; an ally or an undefended attack does nothing.
- Dismay and Fear (901208): the first player chooses characters from the quest, any player's, one at a time (`rr.first-player`: an effect with several eligible targets), each removed with its current Willpower counted, until the total is equal to or higher than The Nameless Fear's current Threat, or no questing character is left (the character whose Willpower reaches the Threat is removed too); with Threat 0 none is removed; removed characters stay exhausted (recommended: the card names no chooser, the alternative is each player in turn removing his own). The Shadow discards each attachment the defending player controls whose printed cost is less than The Nameless Fear's Threat: a printed cost of 0 counts, an attachment with no cost (an objective, Shadow of Fear) does not (recommended, `DiscardCostlier`'s reading of cost).
- An Evil Fortune (901209): Surge and Doomed 1 are keywords; the When Revealed adds it to the victory display in place of the encounter discard pile, where its printed Victory 1 raises X; as a shadow card it goes there after an attack that destroyed a character, else it is discarded as usual (literal). Cancelling the When Revealed leaves it in the discard pile.
- Table: no tracker or readout is created by a line here, and the one zone, the quest deck (03069:A:1 `ShuffleStages`, "Prepare the quest deck"), is told in words as Mount Gundabad's is: the bypass choice says how many stages wait in it, the shuffle of the Nightmare setup card (901201:A:2) in place of the bottom of the deck and the threat Shadowed Corridor makes each player pay first (901207:A:1), and Blocked by Shadow's choice says its two branches. The victory display total that sets The Nameless Fear's Threat, Attack and Defense (03025:A:3) is the victory pile and the enemy's own live stats. The held cards are Abandoned Tools (03029:A:2, 03073:B:2, 03076:B:1) and Shadow of Fear (03027:A:1): each is drawn under its host in every zone the host can be in, and the Tools' Guard lies over it in the staging area. The picks that span seats (Dismay and Fear, Escape from Darkness's hero, the Tools' claim) name the owner on each row (`docs/design-system-table.md`, Cards, Flight from Moria).
- Easy mode: the Easy counts of `data/scenarios/flight-from-moria.json` only (Great Cave-troll, Orc Drummer, Massing in the Deep, Crumbling Ruin, Sudden Pitfall and Dreadful Gap are not dealt; A Foe Beyond has 1 copy).
