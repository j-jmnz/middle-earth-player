# The Seventh Level

Dwarrowdelf part 2 (pack `KD`), standalone only, on Easy, Normal and Nightmare (deck 9011). Encounter sets The Seventh Level, Plundering Goblins and Goblins of the Deep (`dwd.setup-the-seventh-level`); no set-aside set. Four cards print the text of earlier ones and take their readings: Goblin Scout 03060, Goblin Swordsman 03061, Watchful Eyes 03062 and Goblin Tunnels 03063 are Into the Pit's records; Goblin Spearman (03031) prints Goblin Swordsman's two lines (03061:A:1 and A:2) and takes the same records; the quest's 1B When Revealed (03067:B:1) is Entering the Mines' 03064:B:1.

- Cave-troll (03020:A:1): each excess point of the combat damage its attack deals (damage beyond the remaining hit points of the character it damages, read before the damage lands as for Hill Troll, `rr.damage`) is its own choice: the defending player damages 1 other character he controls, hero or ally, any point on any character but the one the attack damaged; a point that kills a character does not stop the next, and with no other character the excess is lost. Damage a shadow effect deals is not combat damage from the attack. FFG's answer, quoted second hand on https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/59164-cave-troll-damage, is that the overflow is assigned "point by point" and continues after a kill (researched; the official answer is only relayed in a forum thread; "you" is the defending player, agent recommendation).
- Orc Horn Blower (03021:A:2), Search for the Chamber (03067:B:1): a card revealed by an effect is revealed in full (`rr.reveal`): its staging resolves, so a treachery resolves its When Revealed and is discarded, a location or enemy is added to the staging area, and Surge, Doomed and the Forced effects that hear a reveal (Goblin Archer, Goblin Tunnels) all apply (span-settled).
- Hidden Threat (03022:A:1): the enemies in the staging area are counted once, as the effect resolves, and each player raises his threat by that number; then the last player (`dwd.last-player`, the only player in a solo game) discards 1 attachment he controls, of his choice, whatever it is attached to. With no enemy in the staging area the raise is by 0 and the last player still discards: `rr.then` asks that the part before "then" resolve in full, and raising threat by 0 is a complete resolution, not an effect that cannot be carried out (recommended; no official ruling found, the forum threads https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/194422-a-couple-of-questions/ and https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/240298-terrible-to-behold-question only say that "then" needs its first part resolved and that an amount may be 0). (span-settled; Vision of the Palantir's article https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/02/10/the-seventh-level/ counts it as one, a fan reading that the card and the Rules Reference do not support).
- Book of Mazarbul (03024, set up by 03067:A:1): the first player attaches it to a hero of any player, free, with no exhaust (community consensus, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/255370-seventh-level-setup-and-book-of-mazarbul; "any hero" as in Entering the Mines, agent recommendation; the KD rulebook says the first player selects any hero for Cave Torch, same phrase). The Action is usable by any player while the Book is in play, in the staging area or attached; `dwd.book-of-mazarbul` says a hero can exhaust to claim it even when it is attached to another hero, which moves it (the 2019 forum thread https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/296876-claiming-objectives-book-of-marzabul says no; the booklet wins). Detached, or with its hero out of play, it returns to the staging area; stage 2B's When Revealed removes it from the game from wherever it is, and its hero is free of it (span-settled). The attached hero still commits to the quest, exhausted by nothing, and may still defend; it cannot declare attacks, ranged attacks included. Restricted counts as for any attachment.
- Chieftain of the Pit (03030): the +3 Attack lasts until the end of the round it is revealed. Its Shadow attacks again once this attack ends, against the same player, after the shadow cards already dealt are discarded and a new one is dealt (`faq.additional-attacks`; the record is `ImmediateAttack` after this attack, as 10135:A:2 and 41021:A:2). Goblin Spearman's and Goblin Swordsman's Shadow add the card to the staging area; an enemy dealt as a shadow card is not revealed (`dwd.revealing-enemies`), so Goblin Archer and Fate of Balin do not hear it.
- Goblin Archer (03032): A:1 is as Goblin Scout's bar; an engagement check at its cost engages it. A:2: characters with the ranged keyword of any player may be declared as attackers against it in the staging area during the player attacks of the combat phase, with the usual one attack per seat; characters without ranged cannot (agent recommendation; no source says who declares it; the Vision of the Palantir article only notes the pings). A:3: it hears every enemy revealed from the encounter deck while it is in play, in the staging area or engaged, itself and an enemy revealed by Fate of Balin or Beats and Echoes included, once per Archer; the first player chooses the character, hero or ally (agent recommendation that it hears itself; https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/240696-how-to-get-rid-of-those-pesky-goblin-archers says only that a shadow enemy is not revealed).
- Undisturbed Bones (03033): each player in player order counts the allies he controls once, then puts all that many damage on 1 ally of his choice; with no ally nothing happens. Its Shadow discards the defending ally from play (a discard, not a destroy); an undefended attack has no defending character (literal).
- Plundered Armoury (03034): A:1 gives every enemy in play, in the staging area or engaged, +1 Attack while the Armoury is in the staging area, not as the active location; two copies give +2 (literal). A:2: after it is explored and leaves play each player in order may attach 1 Weapon or Armour attachment from his hand to 1 character he controls, a hero or an ally the attachment may be attached to (`rr.attachment-cards`): the attachment is put into play, not played, so it costs nothing (a community reading, https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/02/10/the-seventh-level/; researched, no official source).
- Fate of Balin (03068:B:1, B:2, B:3) and Beats and Echoes (901110:A:1): X cards are revealed one at a time from the top of the encounter deck; an enemy is revealed as in the staging step (its When Revealed resolves, Surge and Doomed apply, the Forced effects of a reveal fire) and goes to the staging area; every other card is discarded unresolved, with no When Revealed, no Surge and no Doomed (the card carves out only "the other revealed cards", so the enemies resolve; agent recommendation, no source found: https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1448313/questions-about-the-seventh-level could not be read). The Forced on 2B fires at the end of the staging step of each round the stage is current, not in the round it is revealed (`faq.triggering-forced-effects`); an enemy dealt as a shadow card does not trigger it (`dwd.revealing-enemies`). When the deck runs out partway through X the discard pile, the cards just discarded included, is shuffled back and the revealing goes on (`rr.running-out-of-cards`; agent recommendation). X of Beats and Echoes is 2 plus the number of living players with no Goblin enemy engaged, counted when it resolves, before the reveals. 2B's last line is the win; in Nightmare Overseer Maurûl bars it.
- Nightmare setup card (901101): Setup A:2 takes Overseer Maurûl and Chamber of Records out of the encounter deck, where the Nightmare deck's pile puts them, and sets them aside. A:3 fires when stage 2B is revealed beside 2B's own When Revealed (`faq.simultaneous-effects`; neither changes the other): Chamber of Records becomes the active location without a Travel (the current active location returns to the staging area with its progress, as `MakeSetAsideActive` does), Overseer Maurûl joins the staging area, and every Balin's Doom in the encounter discard pile is shuffled into the encounter deck, which is shuffled whether or not one was there (agent recommendation for the empty case). B:11 and B:12 print the "does not stack" rule: Goblin Skirmisher's and Western Hall's first lines count once however many copies of their own title are in play, and go on while one copy remains; the two cards' lines and every other source of Attack or Threat stack with each other (printed on the setup card; literal).
- Overseer Maurûl (901102): he is not a Goblin, so no card adds resources to him, Orc Taskmaster does not protect him and he is not counted in X; X is 0 with no Goblin enemy in play. His Victory 5 puts him in the victory display when destroyed; "cannot win the game unless" is a bar on the stage's advance that falls when he is in the victory display (Bag End's `Restrict advance`; progress keeps going on 2B, `faq.progress-without-advancing`; as Amon Forn's bar above). Nightmare article: https://visionofthepalantir.com/2021/03/21/nightmare-the-seventh-level (a player's article, not a ruling).
- Chamber of Records (901103): immune to player card effects (the printed keyword); each Goblin enemy with 1 or more resources is immune to player events, read live (the flag `immuneToPlayerEvents` of the Raiders' Shattered Monument; other player cards still reach it); its Forced adds 2 resources to a Goblin enemy as it engages a player, by an engagement check, an optional engagement or an effect (literal).
- Orc Taskmaster (901104): its Forced hears damage dealt to any Goblin enemy in play with at least 1 resource when the damage is dealt: that Goblin loses 1 resource and all that damage is canceled before it lands, so a Goblin the damage would destroy lives. It fires for each separate damage event, as Patrol Leader does (an attack's combined damage once, each event of a card separately; https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/59985-khazad-d%C3%BBm-patrol-leader-goblin-patrol-questions, community). With 2 Taskmasters in play both Forced effects were triggered by the same damage, so both resolve, each removing a resource (`faq.triggering-forced-effects`, `faq.simultaneous-effects`; agent recommendation, no source). A Taskmaster protects Goblins only: not itself, not Overseer Maurûl, not a Cave-troll.
- Resources on Goblins (901105, 901106, 901107, 901108, 901109): "Goblin enemy in play" is the staging area and every player's engaged enemies. Goblin Skirmisher's Shadow deals X damage to the defending character, X the resources on the attacking enemy as the Shadow resolves (nothing on an undefended attack or with none). Pit Goblin's and Balin's Doom's Shadow add 1 resource to the attacking enemy if it is a Goblin; Deep-Mines' Shadow gives +1 Attack and, to a Goblin, deals it another shadow card for this attack. Deep-Mines' Forced (901107:A:1) hears a Goblin entering play, by a reveal, an effect or a Shadow that adds it to the staging area, while Deep-Mines is in play; the resource comes only if it is in the staging area when the Forced resolves, not as the active location. Western Hall's Travel adds its resource when it is traveled to.
- Table: Book of Mazarbul (created by 03067:A:1) is a held card drawn under its host in every zone the host can be in, and the Nightmare resource tokens on Goblins are the ordinary enemy counters; no tracker, zone or readout is created by a line here (`docs/design-system-table.md`, Cards, The Seventh Level).
- Easy mode: the Easy counts of `data/scenarios/the-seventh-level.json` only (Orc Horn Blower, Chieftain of the Pit and Watchful Eyes are not dealt; Cave-troll and Undisturbed Bones have fewer copies).
