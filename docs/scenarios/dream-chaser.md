# Dream-chaser

The rulings, readings and table pieces of Dream-chaser, scenario by scenario. Product rules, the Domain and the Core round are in `docs/brief.md`; the authority order is in `.claude/rules/authority.md`.

The Dream-chaser campaign is nine scenarios played in order: The Grey Havens (Voyage Across Belegaer, The Fate of Númenor, Raid on the Grey Havens), the six Adventure Packs (Flight of the Stormcaller, The Thing in the Depths, Temple of the Deceived, The Drowned Ruins, A Storm on Cobas Haven, The City of Corsairs) and, for campaign mode, the Dream-chaser Campaign Expansion (MEC111, rules doc `dcce`, RingsDB `DCCE`), which reprints the nine with their campaign cards, 38 boons and burdens and the "Campaign Mode only" and "Upgraded" sides. The campaign is registered (`data/campaigns/dream-chaser.json`: `saga` false, `xp` true, `bookletId` `dream-chaser`, each scenario's page of the book) and its nine scenarios are one box on the Scenarios tab, `dream-chaser` (user decision, 2026-10-30). Which of its scenarios are playable is `src/campaign/playable.ts`; a scenario that is not is coming soon: the path shows it as Coming soon, wherever it stands, with no Begin, no briefing and no deal (a briefing by address says "{scenario} is coming soon."; `comingSoonProblems`, `src/campaign/briefing.ts`), the campaign list's next stop reads "{scenario} is coming soon" and opens the path, and the log keeps what was won.

- **XP** (`dcce.gaining-xp`). The campaign card's Resolution awards XP from the conditions on its back ("1 XP for each surviving player", "2 XP if Thunderstruck is in the victory display", "-1 XP if Ominous Fate is in the victory display"). Each line is a `GainXp` record (`src/catalog/families/dreamchaser.ts`: an amount, once or for each surviving player, card in the victory display or token on a card, a maximum, and the record's condition); the Resolution adds them up, and the log keeps the scenario's total, never below 0 (`xpGained`). The Resolution runs on a win only, and a scenario already on the log is not awarded again. XP is the players' together: one number for the campaign, not one per seat. The scenario score still follows `ltp.scoring`.
- **Spending** (`dcce.scalable-upgradable`). One XP marks one box beside a card in the log; the players can spend only before or after a scenario, so the app refuses it while a scenario is open. The sheet's rows are `XP_ROWS` (`src/campaign/xp.ts`, MEC111 p31): the scalable cards (Círdan's Blessing, Command and Galdor's Counsel in each sphere, Come Together, Starla) and the upgradable ones (the Modification boons, the ships, Black Key as a boon and as a burden, Gavin, Send for Help in each sphere and the rest), each with the sheet's box count; an upgradable card's count is the XP cost printed on its Upgraded side. A scalable card's level is its marked boxes and is the X its text reads; an upgradable card is upgraded once every box is marked and is dealt on its Upgraded side for the rest of the campaign (a burden's Upgraded side is the weaker one). MEC111 lists The Havens Burn and the Dream-chaser location as upgradable, but no text for their Upgraded side is in any source: their rows stay on the sheet, dimmed with the reason "Upgraded side not in the sources", take no XP, and are never dealt on that side (`NO_UPGRADED_SIDE`). The Spend XP screen lets the players pick boxes and then mark them: no control types a number, and the app never chooses where XP goes. The unspent XP is the XP gained less the boxes marked, so it cannot drift. A mark is final once written, and the screen says so beside Mark; a pick before Mark can be taken back. The path says XP is spent after a scenario while one is open, and a Dream-chaser briefing with XP unspent reminds the players to spend it before the deal.
- **Gavin** (11209's Resolution, 11215's Setup). "The players may spend 4 XP on Gavin to record that 'Gavin returned'": Gavin's four boxes are spent together, as one pick of four XP, and only once A Storm on Cobas Haven is won (until then his row says "After A Storm on Cobas Haven"); the players still choose whether to. "Gavin returned" is those four boxes, read by `gavinReturned`. The log shows him with the other upgradable cards, not on a row of his own. Where a Setup or an Action puts Gavin into play "under any player's control", the first player names that player (user ruling, 2026-11-05).
- **Send for Help** (11211 to 11214, `dcce.campaign-setup-a-storm-on-cobas-haven`). Gavin's Planning Action shuffles himself and one random set-aside Send for Help into the encounter deck (`ShuffleRandomSetAside`); each is removed from the game once it resolves, as a When Revealed or as a Shadow. The Upgraded clauses are not recorded: no source prints an Upgraded side. Tactics takes a hero out of the collection (`TakeHeroFromCollection`: the app tracks no collection, so every hero card the game knows whose title is not in play, as Helm of Secrecy) and Lore takes Gandalf (01073, `TakeCardFromCollection`, loaded with the world by `loadWorld`); `PutIntoPlay` puts either into play under a player the first player names (`rr.first-player`: an encounter effect that targets a single player or card has the first player select, so the first player also names the player for Leadership and chooses the hero and Gandalf; `ActAs` `firstPlayer`), `ignoreUnique` for Gandalf, and `then: removeFromGame` for the hero (out of the game at the end of the round or as soon as it leaves play by any route, defeated, returned to a hand or deck, or discarded). Spirit adds a phase (`AddPhaseInstance`): the state holds `extraPhases`, and when the phase in progress ends `withMode` runs the chosen phase in full (Resource, Planning, Combat or Refresh, with its action windows) before the round goes on to the phase it was heading for; one asked for during an additional instance runs after it. Each instance ends like any phase, so what is set for the end of the phase fires at the end of each one. A phase limit (once per phase) is counted per phase name and round, so it is not given back by an additional instance. An additional Refresh phase raises each player's threat and passes the first player token again (user ruling, 2026-11-05).
- **A Storm on Cobas Haven on the table** (12134 to 12137, 12121, 12129, 12125, 11213). A tower freed of encounters (`ControlWhenFree`) stays in the staging area in the controlled rank of the fold, and its preview says "Free of encounters: the players control it"; an objective with a guard says "Guarded by <Ship enemy>" and the guard lies over it as for Escape from Dol Guldur's objectives. The Ship enemy Belfalas Islet holds out of play is not drawn, and the Islet's preview names it ("<enemy> is held underneath, out of play, while this is the active location"). Corsair Skirmisher taken as a Ship objective (`12125~ship`) wears the enemy's art, stands in its controller's ship row, and its preview text gives its Willpower, Attack, Defense and hit points. Taking on Water peeks under its ship as any attachment does. A hero from Send for Help (Tactics) is an ordinary hero until the end of the round. An additional phase Send for Help (Spirit) adds is named by the phase rail as the next phase while it is owed, and the round's own next phase after the last one runs. Send for Help (Leadership)'s two ways (draw the card, or play it at no cost) and (Spirit)'s four phases are choices with their own words (`src/play/words`); the pick of the top 10 cards of the deck says so.
- **From Sea to Land** (`dcce.from-sea-to-land`, `dcce.setup-the-city-of-corsairs`; The City of Corsairs, 12147 to 12150; the sea family, `src/rules/families/sea/swap.ts`). The scenario's piles deal the first encounter deck (Coast of Umbar, Corsair Raiders, Stormy Weather, Umbar Fleet and Voyage Across Belegaer), the Corsair deck, the fleet and The City of Corsairs set (`encounterDeck2`) set aside as the inactive second encounter deck; stage 1A prepares the Corsair deck and the fleet. Stage 1B takes no progress off-course and advances at 15 progress (its quest points) or when the Stormcaller is destroyed (`enemyDestroyed`, `AdvanceStage`). Stage 2 has no quest points and resolves A then B as it is revealed. 2A: each player in turn order assigns damage among the non-Ship characters they control equal to the damage on the Ship objectives they control (`AssignDamage` for each player, the amount `damageOn` read before the ships leave), then `DiscardAllInPlay` discards every Ship enemy, Ship objective and Ocean location in play (Southern Belfalas among them). That is a discard, not a destruction (`rr.destroy`, `rr.victory`): the Stormcaller does not go to the victory display, and the Dream-chaser's "if destroyed, the players lose" does not hear it. 2B: `SetAsideEncounterDeck` sets the encounter deck and its discard pile aside, facedown and inactive, except the copies of Watch Tower and Battle-hardened, which the stage's search "shuffles into the second encounter deck" (`carry`, codes 12156 and 11067: a copy of Watch Tower in play stays in play); `ShuffleSetAsideSet` brings the second deck in and `EndCorsairDeck` shuffles the Corsair deck and discard pile into it, so the Corsair deck is gone and a non-ship enemy is discarded to the encounter discard pile again; the quest advances to stage 3. The record runs the card's sentences in the order that leaves one zone to build (set aside, then merge); the result is the card's. Stage 3's When Revealed sets The Shattered Monument and Captain Sahír found in the encounter deck aside (`SetAsideFromDeck`, for 12159 and its Campaign Mode printing 110129), each player's search adds an enemy and a location to the staging area, and each Raider enemy gets a resource unless the Stormcaller is in the victory display. Stage 4 adds the Monument to the staging area, puts Sahír into play engaged with the first player, each player's search adds an enemy, shuffles, and gives each Raider (Sahír too) a resource; its back makes Sahír indestructible while any enemy has resources (his own count), adds the resources of a hero or enemy destroyed to him, and wins the game when he is destroyed. Damage he took while indestructible stays on him: once no enemy has a resource (The Black Sword removed by the Refresh Action, every resource discarded or moved to him and discarded) the sweep destroys him and announces it as an enemy destroyed, which wins the game.
- **The City of Corsairs' campaign Setup** (11215, `dcce.campaign-setup-the-city-of-corsairs`, `dcce.campaign-mode`). "Flip the Stormcaller to its 'Campaign Mode only' side" does nothing in this scenario: its Stormcaller (12151) has no such side (only 110042, which the scenario does not deal, has one), so the campaign game deals 12151 side A. "Attach Black Key (burden) to it" attaches Black Key to 12151 all the same: the Setup's explicit instruction beats 12151's "Cannot have attachments" (user ruling, 2026-11-05).
- **What the game reads.** `StartScenario.facts` carries `levels` (the code of each scalable card with a box marked and its level), `upgraded` (the codes of the upgraded cards) and `modeCard` (taken from the deal's Hard/Expert choice). A card in `upgraded` is dealt on its Upgraded side, side B, wherever a campaign game deals it: a pool card shuffled into a deck or the encounter deck, a card the checklist or the campaign card places (`dealtFace`, `src/rules/families/dreamchaser/facts.ts`, run by every spawn). A card dealt facedown (set aside) stays facedown. A scalable card's level is `levelOfCard` in the same file: the X its records read. A standalone game reads no log, so it deals the original sides.
- **Hard/Expert Mode card** (11217, `dcce.hard-expert`). The campaign setup offers None, Hard or Expert as the card each briefing starts on; each briefing may change it. The deal puts the chosen side next to the quest deck at the end of setup (zone `modeCard`, its own, so nothing that reads the Nightmare setup card reads it; in play all game) and appends the card's Resolution to the campaign card's: Hard gains 1 XP, Expert 2. The log keeps the card each won scenario was played with (`modes`). Its other lines are records of the dreamchaser family: threat cannot be reduced by more than 1 each round (Hard) or at all (Expert), by any effect; after a player draws, that player raises their threat by the number of cards; and (Expert) after a character is healed or readied by a player card effect, its controller raises their threat by 1. Nightmare is not built for Dream-chaser.
- **Na'asiyah** (SoCH hero 12109, `dcce.naasiyah`): "When playing the Dream-chaser campaign, the Na'asiyah hero cannot be used in scenarios 1 through 7." A campaign briefing refuses her in the first seven scenarios, naming the card and the rule; a standalone game of those scenarios is not bound by it.
- **Reprints.** A scenario's `reprints` maps a card it deals to the Campaign Expansion's reprint (110NNN, with its "Campaign Mode only" or "Upgraded" side). A campaign game deals the reprint (`campaignScenario`, `src/catalog/scenario-data.ts`); a standalone game deals the original.
- **Campaign Pool.** The pool, the checklist and the carry-over are the Learn to Play's (`ltp.campaign-pool`); a scalable card is one code for each sphere, so each is one entry, and the checklist deals it like any pool card: a player-backed card into its seat's deck, an encounter-backed one into the encounter deck, the cards the campaign card names as it says (`src/campaign/dream-chaser-deal.test.ts`). The campaign card says how each pool card is used. The Dream-chaser rulebook's "Campaign Setup" paragraph for each scenario (`dcce.campaign-setup-<id>`) lists the extra cards a campaign game needs.
- **Readings checked online** (A Storm on Cobas Haven, The City of Corsairs, The Drowned Ruins, The Thing in the Depths). No official ruling or FAQ entry names any of these cards; Hall of Beorn and RingsDB print the text only. Confirmed: Coast of Anfalas' travel cost takes any player's Ship (`rr.travel`, `rr.cost`); the Black Key stays on Captain Sahír when he flips and gives no bonus on the enemy side (`rr.attachment-cards`: play restrictions are checked only as an attachment enters play); the Corsair Skirmisher taken as a ship gets no Boarding (`dcce.boarding`); Gavin ends in the encounter deck or discard pile in the upgraded games (the printed text); Ship objectives count as allies for the Skirmisher's discard (`dcce.ship-objectives`). Overturned and rebuilt: the first player, not the revealing player, makes the Send for Help choices (`rr.first-player`); "exhaust 3 characters" in Taking on Water's Action is its cost and the discard its effect (`rr.cost`: "to" marks a cost; the Action is offered only with 3 ready characters, and the characters are chosen, so the cost is the first instructions behind that condition), who may trigger it staying the ship's controller; an upgraded game turns The Shattered Monument up on its Upgraded side (`dcce.scalable-upgradable`: the Upgraded side is used for the rest of the campaign). Agent recommendation (no ruling found): a tower the players control stays theirs when it is guarded again (it counts for 11209:B:7); Ramming Speed! (12131) reads only the Ship enemies engaged with the revealing player; the Skirmisher taken as a ship keeps its immunity; Spyglass from outside the game (11203:A:5, https://ringsdb.com/card/11203 and https://ringsdb.com/card/11180 print the text and no source); The Thing in the Depths without Sahír and Na'asiyah.
- **Booklet.** The Dream-chaser campaign rulebook (MEC111) is the fifth bundled book; the path, the log and the briefing open it on the scenario's setup page.
- **Ships** (`dcce.ships`, `dcce.ship-enemies`, `dcce.ship-objectives`; the `sea` family, `src/rules/families/sea/ships.ts`). A ship-enemy is an enemy and a ship-objective an objective ally that fights, for every purpose (the catalog types them `ship-enemy` and `ship-objective`, as their scans print; the engine's `PrintedCard.ship` marks them and its card type stays enemy or objective; a card with the Ship trait whose scan prints ENEMY, such as Nightmare Sahír's Pursuer and Patrol Ship, is no ship). A ship-enemy's attack is defended only by a ship-objective, and left undefended its damage goes to a ship-objective its player controls, never a hero (a player who controls no ship-objective, which no rule covers since a destroyed ship eliminates its controller, takes it on a hero as for any attack, so it never stalls). A ship-objective attacks only ship-enemies and defends only against a ship-enemy's attack. A ship-enemy is attacked by ship-objectives and by every other character. The defender and attacker pickers, the legal-intent check and its refusal read the mark. A ship's stats are the printed ones: Hall of Beorn has no stat fields for a ship, so each ship's stats (and a burden enemy's missing engagement cost) are overrides in `data/overrides.json` with the scan named, and the ledger's `notes` hold the reading (`scripts/data/sea-data.test.ts` compares the two).
- **Keywords of the box.** Sailing, Boarding X, Uncharted, Aflame X and Grapple are parsed (`parseKeywordTokens`); Exploration is a quest card's line (The Island Map and Exploration, below). For the box cards the keyword sentences that open a line of the ledger are keywords the card has (Hall of Beorn lists Immune to player card effects as text only: it is added to every card whose first ledger line opens with it, the ships and the other cards of the box that print it), and the ledger's Sailing success symbol count of an encounter card is its `sailingSuccess`. Archery X (cumulative, to characters of any player, ships included, dealt as the combat phase begins), Immune to player card effects and Indestructible are the engine's own keywords: Archery gained by a card ("gains archery 4") is the stat flag `archery`; Boarding gained or lost is the flag `boarding`.
- **Preparing the fleet** (`dcce.prepare-your-fleet`). The verb `PrepareFleet`, in the quest card's Setup: each player in player order chooses one of the six ship-objectives set aside (a choice of cards the seat answers), the last player who has not got the Dream-chaser is left with it as the only choice, and a lone player takes the Dream-chaser first and then one other; the ships not used are removed from the game, and the Heading card is attached to the Dream-chaser on-course (Sun).
- **Heading** (`dcce.heading`). One heading for all players: Sun (the only on-course setting), Cloud, Rain, Storm (the worst). `ShiftHeading` moves it one step off-course (toward Storm) or on-course (toward Sun) and never past an end. The condition `heading` reads on-course, off-course or the worst setting, false in a game with no Heading card. The Heading card is a dial in the top bar of the table.
- **Sailing and Sailing tests** (`dcce.sailing`, `dcce.sailing-tests`). At the beginning of each quest phase, before characters commit to the quest, a main quest with the Sailing keyword makes the first player perform a Sailing test: an Action window (when some seat has an Action), then the test, then an Action window again; no Action is used during the test, though a Response may be. The test runs in the rule's order: the heading shifts off-course as the test opens (before the commit, so the table shows it); the first player commits characters by a Commit intent (a Pass commits none) and they are exhausted; as many encounter cards are looked at (a deck too short is first shuffled with its discard pile; a character counts as one, a ship as the flag `sailingExtra` adds, and `sailsAnyTest` lets a ship be committed whoever controls it, as the Dream-chaser's records say); for each Sailing success symbol among the cards looked at the first player may shift on-course (a choice "shift" or "keep", asked once for each symbol while the heading is off-course); the cards looked at are then discarded, after the last symbol's answer or, when no symbol is left to answer (none found, or the heading is back on Sun), after the first player's Done. A Pass at the commit looks at nothing. The test is the state's `sea.sailing`, which the table reads.
- **The Corsair deck** (`dcce.corsair-deck`). A scenario deals its non-ship enemies as the `corsairDeck` pile; `PrepareCorsairDeck` also takes any non-ship enemy left in the encounter deck and shuffles the deck. A non-ship enemy that would go to the encounter discard pile goes to the Corsair discard pile, and the Corsair deck refills from it the moment it runs out. `RevealCorsair` reveals cards one at a time into play engaged with a player, each an enemy entering play. `EndCorsairDeck` (`dcce.from-sea-to-land`) shuffles the Corsair deck and its discard pile into the encounter deck and ends the deck: a non-ship enemy is discarded to the encounter discard pile from then on.
- **Boarding X** (`dcce.boarding`). When a ship-enemy engages a player from the staging area (an engagement check, an optional engagement, or an effect that engages it there) the top X Corsair cards are revealed into play engaged with that player. An engagement from another player's play area or from a deck or discard pile does nothing.
- **The Stormcaller Elite** (`dcce.stormcaller-elite`). Its cards (the encounter set of that name) have no encounter back, so a move to the encounter deck or discard pile goes to the victory display for an enemy card that leaves play and out of the game for an objective ally card that leaves play; a card that is out of play never moves there at all.
- **Faces** (`dcce.scalable-upgradable`, `dcce.campaign-mode`; `recordsOfFace`, `src/rules/families/dreamchaser/faces.ts`). A two-sided card shows one side, and only the abilities printed on that side work: a card dealt on its Upgraded side, side B, uses the records of its B lines, and a record with lines on both sides works on both. Quest stages, quest decks, campaign cards and Nightmare cards read their sides through their own zones and are not filtered. The ships' Upgraded stats are built into the catalog from the scan (`upgraded` in `src/catalog/schema.ts`) and read for a card dealt upgraded.
- **Quest order** (`dcce.heading`, `rr.quest-advancement`; `src/rules/families/quest/order.ts`). The Setup verb `ShuffleStages` puts the stage 2 cards in a random order; `LookAtStages` advances the quest to the top stage of the deck, or, with the Heading on-course (Sun), to one of the top two that the first player looks at and chooses, the other going to the bottom. A stage that holds no `LookAtStages` ends the quest. A quest that holds a `look` never branches.
- **The City of Corsairs' campaign card, side B** (11215, `dcce.campaign-setup-the-city-of-corsairs`, `dcce.from-sea-to-land`, `dcce.campaign-mode`). The Setup is the Storm on Cobas Haven's with The Black Sword also set aside: Modification boons fitted to the ships, Calaerion under the player with the Dream-chaser, and one player may take a copy of Círdan's Blessing that is not in the Campaign Pool. "If 'Gavin returned', put him into play under any player's control" reads the condition `upgraded` (the Campaign Log lists his four boxes marked, `CampaignFacts.upgraded`): without it Gavin stays set aside; with it the first player names the seat that takes him (a lone player takes him unasked). "Flip this card over" is the card passing from its Setup to its side B: a campaign card keeps every record, and the card with Forced and Action records stays beside the quest deck, so side B stands from the start. Its Forced effect on stage 2B (`stageRevealed`, the subject Fate of the Dream-chaser) resolves above the stage's When Revealed, while the first deck is still the encounter deck: `CarryToSecondDeck` marks each boon and each burden without the Ship trait in the encounter deck or its discard pile (a discard-pile card moves to the deck), and the stage's `SetAsideEncounterDeck` keeps the marked cards in the deck as it sets the rest aside, the way it keeps Watch Tower and Battle-hardened, so they end in the second deck (Ominous Fate does; Thunderstruck, a Ship, and Crowley, who is in the Corsair deck the stage shuffles in, follow the stage). The Forced effect when Captain Sahír enters play (`encounterEnteredPlay`) attaches Black Key (burden) and The Black Sword to him; Black Key "when it leaves play, remove it from the game", and it was attached to the Stormcaller, which stage 2A discards, so it is gone and only the Sword attaches (the Key attaches if a copy is still set aside; user ruling, 2026-11-05: the Key does not reach Sahír). The Refresh Action (any player, group limit once per round) exhausts a ready hero to move 1 resource from The Black Sword to that hero's pool, and removes the Sword from the game once it has none. The Resolution "The players win the campaign!" is the end of the path: the scenario is the campaign's last, and winning it leaves no scenario unwon (`nextStep` is `finished`, "Campaign complete"); it writes nothing else. The XP list adds 1 XP for each Corsair Warship, Shattered Monument (either printing), Crowley and Stormcaller in the victory display (each copy counts, so two Corsair Warships give 2 XP; user ruling, 2026-11-05), with the lines the templates read (1 per surviving player, 2 for Thunderstruck, -1 for Ominous Fate).
- **The Black Sword and Captain Sahír** (11216, 12158; the sea family, `src/rules/families/sea/attacks.ts`). The Sword enters play, attached, with 3 resources (2 on its Upgraded side; the `attached` event it hears), gives Captain Sahír +2 Attack (side A) and indestructible, and "after Captain Sahír attacks for the first time each round" (`attackResolved`, the Forced limit once per round) he makes an attack against each other player in turn order (`AttackOtherPlayers`: one immediate attack for each player he is not engaged with, from the first player on, each dealt a shadow card of its own, queued so each resolves before the next). Sahír himself engages the first player and pays a resource for each point of damage (the lines of his objective side 11076); for every 5 resources on him (the amount `every`: the value read divided by 5, rounded down) he makes an additional attack against the engaged player after his attack in the combat phase (`ImmediateAttack`, once a round).
- **The Raiders of the City of Corsairs** (The City of Corsairs set, 12153 to 12165, 110129). The Shattered Monument (12159, and 110129 in a campaign game, whose Upgraded side drops the immunity line; an upgraded game turns it up on that side when it is revealed or added to the staging area, `turnedUp`, `dcce.scalable-upgradable`) makes each Raider enemy with a resource immune to player events (the flag `immuneToPlayerEvents`: a card holding it is not offered to a Pick whose source is a player event, `families/sea/immune.ts`; other player cards still reach it) and gives each Raider enemy a resource at the beginning of the resource phase. Umbar Slaver gets +1 Attack and +1 Threat for each resource and, after its engaged player spends resources, takes 1 (the trigger `resourcesSpent`, announced by `noteSpent` for every payment of a card, an ability or a cancel only in a game whose cards hear it). Corsair Waters allows no more than 1 ally to enter play each round (`LimitEntries`: played or put into play, an ally that entered and left counts; `ScenarioState.entries` is kept by the sea family's settle sweep in a game that reads it and `playBlocked` refuses the second). Streets of Umbar in the staging area, while the players are faced with the option to travel, charges 2 resources on a Raider of the first player's choice for declining or for traveling to a location with another title (`TravelOrAddResources`, `families/sea/streets.ts`, through the travel hook `cost`: the charge resolves in a frame of its own for Streets of Umbar, over and so before the destination's own Travel cost, and the travel sheet's "Do not travel" and each other location say "Tap again: 2 resources go on a Raider enemy (Streets of Umbar)" before it sends); adding the 2 resources to a Raider meets the "must", after which the players may decline travel or travel elsewhere; with no Raider in play they must travel to a Streets of Umbar (user ruling, 2026-11-05). Southern Belfalas' Travel reveals the top Corsair for a player the first player names (`RevealCorsair` `who: "anyPlayer"`); the cost is unpayable with both Corsair piles empty. Umbar Patrol returns an engaged enemy of each player to the staging area and bars optional engagement for the rest of the round with a flag the table holds (`cannotBeOptionallyEngaged` on `table`, so enemies that enter play later that round are barred too; user ruling, 2026-11-05). Foes of Ecthelion and Fury and Malice place resources on Raiders (Fury and Malice's damage is each player's, equal to the resources on the Raiders engaged with them; with no Raider engaged nothing is assigned and it gains surge); Serpent's Tail deals a shadow card to each Ship enemy in play (Eye of the Beast and Serpent's Tail readings) and, off-course, takes 10 from each Ship enemy's engagement cost until the end of the round; Slave Ship deals a non-Ship character 1 damage for each point of damage its attack deals to a Ship objective; the Stormcaller's end-of-round Forced effect lets its engaged player return it to the staging area or shift the heading off-course.
- **Campaign Setup of a ship** (`dcce.ship-objectives`, `dcce.encounter-cards`, `rr.campaign-setup-checklist`; `src/rules/families/sea/campaign.ts`). After the fleet is prepared, each player fits a Modification boon to each of their ship-objectives (`FitShips`) and may deal 2 damage to a ship for 1 resource (`TradeShipDamage`); both are choices of cards, one decision at a time. The cards the Campaign Setup names are shuffled into the Corsair deck (`ShuffleIntoCorsairDeck`) or searched for (`SearchShips`).
- **Sea verbs of the cards.** `DamageShip`, `PlunderHand`, `NameCard` and `DiscardFromAnyDeck` (name a card, discard from any deck, X is 1 when the named card was discarded), `Board` (a card boards at once) and the flags `cannotBeAttacked`, `cannotAttackShips`, `sailingLook` (the test looks at one more card), `sailingCancel` (one fewer symbol counts), `excessToShip`, `boardingResource`, `engagedThreat` (engaged cards add their threat to the staging area), `damageCap`, `gainsVictory`, `threatReductionCap` and the trigger `cardsDrawn`. Each is cited by the line of the card that creates it. A Shadow effect that reveals an encounter card runs in a frame.

With Cursed Temple active Temple Guardian's engagement cost is 0, so it engages every player at the engagement checks; that is how the players draw it out. Profane Dead attacks when the players travel to the right (a column to the right of the one they left, whatever the distance); Island Watcher in the staging area engages the player with the highest threat at the beginning of the encounter phase while a Ruins location is active.
- **Temple of the Deceived's campaign card** (11197, `dcce.campaign-setup-temple-of-the-deceived`). The Setup sets Spyglass, Black Key (boon), Starla and both Recurring Nightmares aside, shuffles Revenant into the encounter deck, shows Temple Guardian's Campaign Mode only side (`FlipSetAside` finds the card in the staging area), and after the quest card's Setup attaches Black Key to Captain Sahír, shuffles Starla and the Recurring Nightmares and lays one facedown under each Temple location as a secret (`PlaceSecrets`), offers each character a 1-damage-for-1-threat trade, and offers each player in turn to raise their threat by 4 to attach Spyglass to a hero (`RaiseThreatToAttach`: not offered to a player whose threat would reach 50; the first yes ends the offers and that player chooses the hero, any player's). The card's Travel Action flips the secret under the active location (`FlipSecret`, any player's, only in the Travel phase's action window): Starla comes into play ready under the player, a Recurring Nightmare engages them. The campaign card's Actions are any player's (it belongs to no player, as an objective does). The Resolution adds Starla to the pool if a player controls her, and each Recurring Nightmare that is no secret, for a player the first player chooses (`ChooseCards` with `to: player`, one option for each player, named by a hero; the entry is the seat's, `afterHand`, and the next scenarios deal it into that deck after the starting hand and the mulligans, `StartSeat.afterMulligan` with the prefix `deck:`). The XP list: 1 for each surviving player; 1 for each resource on the card (at most 3); 1 for each Temple Halls that was flipped over; 1 if 4 or fewer of the map's double-sided locations are flipped over to their location side (a short route, e.g. through Winding Caverns; "Uncharted locations faceup" is read as the flipped ones, user decision 2026-11-01); 1 if Temple Guardian (either printing) is in the victory display; 1 if each surviving player's threat is 40 or less; -1 if Ominous Fate is in the victory display.
- **Recurring Nightmare and Starla.** Recurring Nightmare's Defense is X, the number of players; when it is destroyed it is shuffled into a player's deck, the first player choosing whose with more than one player (an enemy that leaves play is destroyed: the engine announces no other leaving of one); when an enemy is drawn into a hand it is announced to itself (`enteredHand`, from the draw steps) and its Forced effect adds it to the staging area. Starla's Willpower, Attack and Defense are half her level, rounded up; she is played when she is discarded from a hand or a deck, and places 1 progress on a location when she exhausts.
- **Open (Temple of the Deceived).** "Up to 3 locations away" (Winding Caverns) is read as 3 orthogonal steps over any cards of the grid, the rule text and the card saying no more. The first player chooses the player Recurring Nightmare is shuffled into and the player it goes to at the Resolution.

## Umbar Raider

- **Umbar Raider** (11064:A:2). Its Forced effect moves 1 resource from one of the engaged player's heroes only when a hero has one; with none it places nothing on the Raider.

## Eye of the Beast and Serpent's Tail readings

- **Eye of the Beast** (12050:A:3). Each Eye engaged with a player is a separate instance of its constant ability, so The Thing in the Depths attacks that player once for each such Eye in the combat phase, and each attack is dealt a new shadow card (`rr.constant-ability`, `faq.additional-attacks`). `researched`: the FAQ's answer on Caught in a Web (ALeP 2.15) applies the same principle (https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/lotr_faq_19.pdf, https://alongextendedparty.com/semi-official-errata-faq-1-10/).
- **Serpent's Tail** (12157:A:1). A Ship enemy in the staging area is in play (`rr.in-play-and-out-of-play`), so it is dealt a shadow card like an engaged one (`DealShadowTo` with `inPlay`; user decision, 2026-11-23). The -10 follows only when the first part found a Ship enemy in play: with none, nothing is dealt and no Ship enemy that enters play later that round gets the -10 (`rr.then`; Caleb Grace, ALeP 3.119, https://alongextendedparty.com/semi-official-errata-faq-1-10/). `researched`.

## The Drowned Ruins Then readings

- **Drowned Cave** (12104:A:2). "Each player must discard the top 10 cards of his deck. Then, you may return Drowned Cave to the staging area": a deck of fewer than 10 cards cannot discard in full, so the card stays the active location (`rr.then`; the `thenResolves` condition). Recommended: no ruling covers a short discard before "Then"; `rr.effects` reads "as much as they are able" for the discard itself, which still happens.
- **Sunken Temple** (12103:A:2). "Discard 1 resource from each objective-ally and each hero's resource pool. Then, you may flip Sunken Temple": a pool with no resource cannot discard 1, so the discard is not in full and Sunken Temple is not offered for flipping (`rr.then`). Recommended on the same ground.
