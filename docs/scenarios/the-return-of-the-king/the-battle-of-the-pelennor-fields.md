# The Battle of the Pelennor Fields

Scenario 15 (`mec113.minas-tirith`, `mec113.assault`, `mec113.setting-up-the-game`, `mec113.saga-staging-rules`, `mec113.immune-to-player-card-effects`, `mec113.peril`, `mec113.archery`, `rr.active-location`, `faq.lasting-effects`; records `src/effects/authored/return-of-the-king/the-battle-of-the-pelennor-fields.ts` and `pelennor/{shared,stages}.ts`, campaign card 145078 in `the-return-of-the-king.ts`; the family `pelennor` in `src/catalog/families/pelennor.ts` and `src/rules/families/pelennor/`). The quest card's piles set Minas Tirith, Grond, The Witch-king (Captain side), each Wraith on Wings and the two Esquires aside, and Pelennor Field is the active location (no record places them); the campaign card's Setup (145078:A:1) deals the log's Harad enemies, Aragorn with Andúril and the Banner of Elendil, The Corsair Fleet and the burden sets. Every reading below is applied as written; the ones marked agent recommendation (no ruling found). Sources: [V] https://visionofthepalantir.com/2024/06/12/the-battle-of-the-pelennor-fields/ (review with a playthrough), [F] https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/228696-flame-of-the-west (player answers, none official), [H] https://hallofbeorn.com/LotR/Details/The-Battle-of-the-Pelennor-Fields-Campaign-TRotK and https://hallofbeorn.com/LotR/Details/Retreat-from-Osgiliath-TRotK (card text, no rulings), [E] the FFG FAQ v1.9 (no entry for this box); searches for the Fleet at 0 resources, Southron Champion's timing and Andúril's source found no ruling.

- **Readings.**
  - 145078:A:1 (Harad enemies). Each card recorded in `notes.blackGate` with the Harad trait is set aside, one copy for each recorded; with no log nothing is set aside and 145078:B:1 adds nothing. Alternative: every logged enemy (the log holds only Harad enemies, so they agree). Source: the line, [V]. Status: literal.
  - 145078:A:1 (Aragorn). The first player attaches Andúril and Banner of Elendil to Aragorn and sets him aside: he and they leave play at Setup, before round 1, which is not "leaves play" (his loss does not fire; `rr.in-play-and-out-of-play`, `mec113.setting-up-the-game`). Alternative: the players lose at once. Source: [V]. Status: researched.
  - 145078:A:1 (Andúril and the Banner). They are fresh copies from the supply, not the copies a player holds in a deck, hand or pool: the line names no source, the Banner is the box's own card, and the Setup runs before the players' decks are drawn. A player's own Andúril, found by the Siege's search, stays where it is; Unique stops a second copy from entering play while the first is. Alternative: the players' own copies (taken from deck, hand or pool). Source: [H], [V] (neither says where they come from; no ruling found). Status: agent recommendation (no ruling found).
  - 145078:A:1 (rest). "Remove each burden ..." is the five burden sets of the earlier boxes (as 145070:A:1); no hero-change waiver and no Andúril search are printed on this card. Status: literal.
  - 145072:A:1 (The Corsair Fleet). The Gondor side joins the staging area at Setup with X resources, X the number the Siege recorded (`corsairFleet`; 0 with none). The Forced at the beginning of each quest phase removes 1, then the first player takes control of Aragorn (out of play to his hero zone with his attachments, resource pool empty) and the Fleet goes to the victory display. A skipped quest phase (stage 1) runs no removal. Source: the line, [V]. Status: literal.
  - 145072:A:1 (no resource to remove). With 0 resources the "remove 1 resource" cannot resolve; the check "if there are no resources here" runs anyway, so Aragorn returns at the first quest phase (stage 2). The strict reading of `rr.then` (the text before "then" must resolve in full) would leave Aragorn out of play, and stage 5B, which cannot be defeated without him, unwinnable after an empty log or a standalone game; a real Siege records at least 1, since the Fleet gets a resource each round. Alternative: `rr.then` skips the return. Source: `rr.then`, [H], [V] (no ruling). Status: agent recommendation (no ruling found).
  - 145073:A:1 (Esquires). The first player decides for the table: each Esquire is optional, each goes to a hero of any seat, the two to different heroes (Rohan's first, then Gondor's onto a hero without the Rohan one); an Esquire still on a hero when the scenario is won is earned for that hero's controller (145078:B:2). The Esquires' own "Setup: Attach to a hero" is for the pool copy and does not run for the set-aside copy. Alternative: each player attaches one to a hero they control. Source: [V] says only "attach to two different heroes". Status: agent recommendation (no ruling found).
  - 145073:A:1 (Pelennor Field). The `activeLocation` pile deals one copy as the active location, the same as a search and a shuffle (the encounter deck is shuffled at Setup). Status: literal.
  - 145073:B:1 and B:2 (stage 1). The quest phase and the combat phase are skipped, so no beginning-of-quest-phase Forced effect (the Fleet, Grond, a Wraith on Wings, The Witch-king) and no combat runs; the travel, encounter and refresh phases still happen. At the end of the planning phase 1 progress is placed here, so stage 2 appears at the end of round 2's planning phase. Alternative: the Forced effects of the skipped phase still fire. Source: `rr.quest-advancement`, [V] (silent). Status: agent recommendation (no ruling found).
  - 145043 (Minas Tirith; `mec113.minas-tirith`). It is not in play before stage 3A. Damage to it while it is out of play does nothing, and an "either ... or" option that needs it is not offered, so only the option that can be done is (`rr.effects`: "as fully as possible"). It has 30 hit points plus 10 for each player; at the end of the round 1 damage for each enemy in the staging area is dealt to it (the count is taken when the Forced resolves), and then, with no hit points remaining, the players lose. Source: `mec113.minas-tirith`, the line. Status: span-settled.
  - 145046:A:1 (City Wall, overflow). While it is the active location and Minas Tirith is in play, damage that would go on Minas Tirith goes on the Wall until it holds damage equal to its quest points (4); damage beyond that goes on Minas Tirith. With Minas Tirith out of play nothing is redirected. Alternative: the whole packet goes on the Wall, however large. Source: [V] ("damage meant for Minas Tirith goes to City Wall first"), [F] (no damage goes to the Wall with Minas Tirith out of play); neither settles the overflow; by analogy to `rr.active-location` (progress goes on the active location first, up to its quest points, the rest on the quest). Status: agent recommendation (no ruling found).
  - 145046:A:2. As soon as the Wall holds damage equal to its quest points it is discarded and each player raises their threat by 3, once. Source: the line. Status: literal.
  - 145075:B:1 (Assault; `mec113.assault`). While stage 3B is the current stage, no progress is placed on it by questing or by a player card effect (progress on the active location is normal); a failed quest raises no player's threat and deals one group damage to Minas Tirith, the total Threat in the staging area less the willpower committed (through City Wall; nothing with Minas Tirith out of play). B:2: 4 quest points for each player. B:3: the players cannot travel to a Pelennor location. B:4: when an enemy is destroyed, progress equal to its total Threat as it stood in play (a Wraith on Wings with its resources) is placed on the active location first, up to its quest points, the rest on the stage. Source: `mec113.assault`, the lines. Status: span-settled.
  - 145055:A:2 (Mordor Orc). While Minas Tirith is in play the damage the Orc deals to an undefended player goes to Minas Tirith instead of a hero (through City Wall); the defending player still decides whether the attack is undefended. Alternative: damage to both. Source: [F] (one player answer: "no damage goes to a hero"). Status: confirmed (community-agreed, none official: https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3522851/battle-of-the-pellenor-fields-questions, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/228696-flame-of-the-west).
  - 145055:A:3 (Shadow). With Minas Tirith in play this card engages the defending player and is dealt a shadow card of its own; it stays engaged. With none the shadow does nothing and the card is discarded as any shadow card. Status: agent recommendation (no ruling found).
  - 145052:A:1 (Southron Champion). After it is dealt a shadow card with no shadow effect, the chooser (the defending player; the first player for an attack from the staging area) either deals 3 damage to Minas Tirith (not offered while it is out of play) or the Champion makes an additional attack after this one (a new shadow card is dealt, `faq.additional-attacks`); each blank shadow card triggers it. The choice waits for the end of the attack, where the engine reads a blank shadow card that was discarded (`attackResolved` with `shadowBlank`, as Journey up the Anduin's 18022:A:1 does), not when the card is revealed; the 3 damage and the extra attack are the same either way, and only the moment of the damage (mid-attack) differs. Alternative: the choice at the moment the blank card is revealed. Source: [V] says only "after the Champion is dealt a shadow card with no shadow effect"; no ruling found. Status: agent recommendation (no ruling found).
  - 145074:A:1 (Retreat from Osgiliath). Each player, the first player first, adds 1 set aside Wraith on Wings (if one is left) and reveals an encounter card; then, the first player first, discards from the top of their deck until an ally that is not in play is discarded (a unique ally already in play is skipped) and puts it into the staging area; with none the deck is emptied and nothing is added. The owner stays the owner. Source: [H] (card text), [V]. Status: agent recommendation (no ruling found; no ruling on "not in play" or an empty deck; the siege's `DiscardUntil`).
  - 145074:B:1 to B:3 (allies in the staging area). X, the quest points, is their total printed cost. Their text box is blank (as 145061 does: printed text only; their Traits stay, which nothing in the box reads), they are immune to player card effects, controlled by no player and add no Threat. At the end of the round damage equal to the enemies in the staging area is assigned among them, one at a time, the first player assigning; a destroyed ally goes to its owner's discard pile. Alternative: the Traits go too. Source: [V] reads "blank" as including Traits. Status: agent recommendation (no ruling found).
  - 145075:A:1 (3A). Each player takes control of the allies they own in the staging area and exhausts them; the first player adds Minas Tirith, Grond and The Witch-king (Captain); each other player searches the encounter deck and discard pile for a different enemy and adds it; the deck is shuffled. One player: no search. Status: literal.
  - 145044 (Grond). Threat X, X the resources on it (the card prints it; the data holds none, so the record adds it). Forced at the beginning of each quest phase: 1 resource, then X damage to Minas Tirith (through City Wall). Travel: The Witch-king, if in play, makes an immediate attack against each player in turn order, from the staging area, a shadow card dealt; if he is not in play nobody attacks and the travel still happens ([F]: "he just won't attack"). Status: literal for the damage; the travel researched ([F], not official).
  - 145062 (Wraith on Wings). +1 Threat and -1 engagement cost for each resource, up to 5 (a limit on the tokens, not a way to lose), placed at the beginning of each quest phase on every copy in play (staging or engaged). Status: literal.
  - 145079 (The Witch-king, Captain). Cannot be optionally engaged or take damage. Forced at the beginning of each quest phase: cards are discarded from the encounter deck until an enemy is, and it is added to the staging area, not revealed (no When Revealed, no Surge); with none left the deck is emptied and nothing is added. Status: agent recommendation (no ruling found) for the empty deck (the siege's precedent).
  - 145076:A:1 (Rohan has Come!). The Witch-king is set aside, out of play, wherever he is in play; each player searches the top 10 cards of their deck for an ally that matches the sphere of a hero they control (a neutral ally matches none, nor does a Fellowship hero unless it gained an icon) and puts it into play under their control, then shuffles. Status: literal.
  - 145076:B:1. Only the progress that questing successfully would place on the stage (the active location took its share first) is dealt as damage among the enemies in play, the first player assigning; progress from a player card effect is placed on the stage as usual. B:2: at the end of the quest phase the players advance to stage 5A. Source: the line, `rr.first-player` (no ruling on the assigner). Status: literal for the progress; agent recommendation (no ruling found) for who assigns.
  - 145077 (Fighting in the Fields). A:1: the first player adds The Witch-king (Sorcerer), searches the encounter deck and discard pile for a Fell Beast and attaches it to him if able, and the deck is shuffled; each other player reveals one encounter card (a normal reveal). B:1: the Witch-king and the shadow cards dealt to him are immune to player card effects. B:2: when an enemy attacks and destroys a character, 1 damage to Minas Tirith (through the Wall). B:3: the stage cannot be defeated unless Aragorn is in play (the hero zone; a set-aside Aragorn is not) and the Witch-king is in the victory display; progress piles up meanwhile (`faq.progress-without-advancing`); defeating it wins the game. Status: literal; Aragorn "in play" agent recommendation (no ruling found; [V]: "a hard time"; no ruling).
  - 145078:B:1 (the Harad enemies). The Forced fires once stage 5A has resolved and the card is on 5B (`afterStageRevealed`), so the Witch-king, the Fell Beast search and the other players' reveals come first; each set aside Harad enemy is added to the staging area, not revealed. Source: the line. Status: literal.
  - 145080 (The Witch-king, Sorcerer). Only the engaged player can declare attackers against him (no other seat's characters, ranged or not); Forced at the beginning of the encounter phase: he engages the first player, then makes an immediate attack if he engaged (`faq.next-player`). Source: [V] reads it the same and asks for dissent. Status: agent recommendation (no ruling found).
  - 145050 (The Black Serpent). "Cannot be attacked by more than 1 character at a time": a player attack against it is declared with one attacker (ranged characters of other seats take turns, one for each attack). When Revealed: it makes an immediate attack against the first player, from the staging area. Source: [V] (an interpretation). Status: agent recommendation (no ruling found).
  - 145051 (War Mûmak). "Cannot take more than 3 damage each round": the round's damage ledger (`damageCap`). Forced when it is destroyed: each player lowers their threat by 4. Status: literal.
  - 145053 (Morgul Captain). When Revealed: the topmost (last discarded) Orc enemy in the encounter discard pile enters play engaged with the revealing player (put into play, not revealed); none: nothing. Shadow: if the attack destroys a character, the attacking enemy returns to the staging area. Status: literal.
  - 145054, 145059, 145056, 145060, 145061 (the either/or treacheries). With Minas Tirith out of play only the option that can be done is offered (`mec113.minas-tirith`). 145054: the revealing player's hero (Peril: alone). 145059: "each exhausted character" is every exhausted character of every player; the other option is 5 damage to Minas Tirith. 145056:A:1: each player, the first player first, searches the deck and discard pile for one enemy (different not required) and puts it into play engaged with them. 145060: the revealing player discards an ally they control (their pick) and, until the end of the phase, the staging area's Threat is raised by 1 for each ally in their discard pile, read at every point of the phase (an encounter card's lasting effect, `faq.lasting-effects`; `RaiseStagingThreat` with `live`), with Doomed 1 resolving first. 145061: each player, the first player first, deals 1 damage to a hero they control; each damaged character's printed text box is blank until the end of the round, now or later (`faq.lasting-effects`). Status: literal for the text, span-settled for the options.
  - 145057 (Fell Beast, Surge). When Revealed: it attaches to a Nazgûl enemy in play (staging or engaged), Limit 1 per enemy; with none it does nothing. It counts as a Mount: the attached enemy gets +1 Threat, +1 Attack, +1 Defense and -10 engagement cost, and at the end of the round it returns to the staging area. Status: literal.
  - 145058 (Black Dart, Peril). When Revealed: the revealing player removes a hero they control from the quest (a hero not committed stays as it is) and attaches it to that hero, Limit 1 per hero; it counts as a Condition that deals 1 damage to its hero at the end of the round. Shadow: if the attack damages a hero (the defender, or the hero an undefended attack landed on), it attaches to that hero. Status: literal.
  - 145048 (Fire-filled Trench). Forced when a player plays an event that would cancel the effects of a treachery just revealed: that event's own effects are canceled instead (the event is played and paid for, and goes to the discard pile), then the Trench is discarded. Active while in play anywhere. Alternative: the event cannot be played. Source: the line (no ruling found). Status: agent recommendation (no ruling found).
  - 145049 (Siege Tower). In the staging area it has Archery 3 (a location's archery counts there, one holder for each Tower, additive). Forced after it becomes the active location: the discard pile is shuffled into the deck and cards are discarded until an enemy is; that enemy is added to the staging area; with none, nothing is. Source: `mec113.archery`, the siege's precedent. Status: agent recommendation (no ruling found) for the stacking and the empty deck.
  - 145047 (Pelennor Field). While at least 1 enemy is in the staging area, no progress can be placed on locations in the staging area (the active location is not). Shadow: +1 Attack, +2 with the Harad trait. Status: literal.
  - 145045 (Gate of Gondor). While it is the active location each enemy in play (staging or engaged) gets -30 engagement cost. Forced when it is explored, as the active location or in the staging area, once: each engaged enemy, every player's, returns to the staging area. Status: literal.
  - 145014, 145015 (Esquires). +1 Attack while attacking with another Rohan character, +1 Willpower while committed to the quest with another Gondor character: all the attackers of a joint attack count. Status: literal.
  - Campaign card 145078. Setup as above; Resolution (145078:B:2): each player earns a Wraith on Wings, and an Esquire on a hero is earned for that hero's controller. Source: the card. Status: literal.
- **Table pieces** (`src/play/readouts/pelennor.ts`, `src/play/words/scenarios/pelennor.ts`, `src/play/words/asks/the-battle-of-the-pelennor-fields.ts`, test `src/play/scenarios/the-battle-of-the-pelennor-fields-table.test.ts`; `docs/design-system-table.md`, Top bar). Then The Corsair Fleet's resources (145072:A:1; `pelennor-fleet`, "Fleet"), with no limit so it never warms, its words saying Aragorn returns with Andúril and the Banner when none are left (two-handed they name the first player's seat, "Player 2, the first player, takes control") and that Fighting in the Fields, stage 5B, cannot be defeated without him; Grond's resources, X (145044:A:2, 145044:A:3; `pelennor-grond`, "Grond"); and on stage 2B the total cost of the allies in the staging area, X (145074:B:1; `pelennor-allies`, "Allies"). The quest result line says on stage 3B that Assault places no progress on the stage (the active location takes it), that a failed quest deals the shortfall to Minas Tirith, no threat raised, warning when it would destroy it (and no elimination warning), and that each enemy destroyed places its Threat as progress, the active location first, and on stage 4B that the progress is dealt as damage among the enemies in play (`pelennorQuestWords`). The allies in the staging area are cards in the staging area, drawn there as any card is; an Esquire is an attachment and hangs under its hero in the heroes' zone like any attachment (`entriesOf`), so no piece is added for either; no record holds a seat's held dial. The seat that holds Aragorn wears a mark ("Aragorn", "P2 · Aragorn" two-handed, spoken "Aragorn passes with the first player token"), merged by `seatMarks` with the Fear mark of the Grey Company; a staged ally on stage 2B says in its preview whose ally it is ("Player 2's ally"), that no one controls it, that it is immune to player card effects with a blank text box, and that its owner takes control at stage 3, and each row of Retreat from Osgiliath's damage names the ally's owner two-handed (`pelennor-preview.ts`, `stagedAllyLabel`). The choices say the number, the side and the player: two-handed they lead with "Player N" (`owned`), solo with no lead; the picks that span seats (the Rammas is Breached's search, Retreat from Osgiliath's damage among every player's staged allies, The White City Besieged's search, Rohan has Come!'s damage among all enemies) do the same. The Esquire questions say that an Esquire still attached when the scenario is won is earned for that hero's controller, each Esquire row says its hero gains the trait and the attack or willpower bonus while with another character of it, and each row that deals damage to Minas Tirith reads "Deal 3 damage to Minas Tirith (31 damage of its 40 hit points)", adding that City Wall takes it first while it is the active location, and arms "Tap again: Minas Tirith is destroyed and the players lose the game" when the damage reaches the hit points left plus the room of the active City Wall (the amount `minasTirithLeft`: a Wall in the staging area shields nothing, and damage beyond the Wall's room counts). Grond's Travel pick says to tap The Witch-king.
