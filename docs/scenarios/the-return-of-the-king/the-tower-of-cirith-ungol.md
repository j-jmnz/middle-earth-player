# The Tower of Cirith Ungol

Scenario 16 (`mec113.setup-the-tower-of-cirith-ungol`, `mec113.setting-up-the-game`, `mec113.the-one-ring`, `mec113.multiplayer-the-one-ring`, `mec113.saga-staging-rules`, `mec113.immune-to-player-card-effects`, `mec113.archery`, `rr.guarded`, `rr.objective-cards`, `rr.lasting-effects`, `faq.lasting-effects`, `faq.additional-attacks`, `faq.progress-without-advancing`; records `src/effects/authored/return-of-the-king/the-tower-of-cirith-ungol.ts` and `cirith-ungol/{shared,stages}.ts`, campaign card 146069 in `the-return-of-the-king.ts`; the family `cirith-ungol` in `src/catalog/families/cirith-ungol.ts` and `src/rules/families/cirith-ungol/`, with verbs in the campaign, south and grey-company families). The quest card's piles set Shagrat aside and put Topmost Chamber and The Two Watchers (location side) in the staging area; the saga start gives the first player's Ring-bearer, Frodo, with The One Ring. Sources: [V] https://visionofthepalantir.com/2025/01/07/the-tower-of-cirith-ungol/, [Q] https://visionofthepalantir.com/2025/01/27/qotw-the-tower-of-cirith-ungol-results, [H] https://hallofbeorn.com/LotR/Details/The-Tower-of-Cirith-Ungol-Campaign-TRotK, [T] https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/262583-the-tower-of-cirith-ungol-stage-3b-campaign. No official FAQ entry names this box. A standalone game has no campaign card in this engine, so it has no A Heavy Burden, no boon set aside and no Forced at stage 3A (the Setup, Forced and Resolution of 146069 are Campaign Mode only).

- **Readings.** Each: line, reading, alternative, source, status.
  - 146069:A:1 (A Heavy Burden). It comes from the supply onto the hero that Last of the Company is attached to, after the quest card's Setup chose that hero. Alternative: onto the Ring-bearer. Source: [V] ("the same hero as Last of the Company"), [H]. Status: literal.
  - 146069:A:1 (Item boons). Each player in turn, the first player first, chooses an Item boon (a pool card with `campaignKind` boon and the trait Item, never Andúril) from the whole campaign pool and sets it aside, out of play: it is left out of the decks and stays in the pool, and it is the chooser's own, so it returns to their hand. A boon another seat's pool entry holds may be taken (the chooser's own seat's copy first); a player with none left chooses nothing. Alternative: only the boons assigned to the player. Source: the card says "from the campaign pool", [V] ("not just their own items"). Status: researched.
  - 146069:A:1 (rest). Waives the +1 for changing heroes; the burdens of Helm's Deep, The Road to Isengard and The Passing of the Grey Company leave the encounter deck only, so the Grey Company's Overcome by Fear is no longer dealt on a dial while the pool keeps it; the Wraith on Wings is no burden and stays. Status: literal.
  - 146069:B:1 (3A, A Heavy Burden). Alternative: before the When Revealed, while two heroes are Ring-bearers, or a second copy. Source: [V], [H]; no ruling orders a campaign card's Forced against a stage's When Revealed (searched: FFG forum archive, community quotes of an FFG answer that the new stage's effects follow the advance). Status: confirmed (https://visionofthepalantir.com/2025/01/07/the-tower-of-cirith-ungol/: "you attach A Heavy Burden to the Ring-bearer instead of the hero with Last of the Company").
  - 146069:B:1 (boons on Shagrat). Alternative: they go to hand at once. Source: [V], [T] (a Shagrat already defeated: "none of the boons are removed from the campaign pool"). Status: confirmed for Shagrat out of play (the boons stay set aside and none is lost: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/262583-the-tower-of-cirith-ungol-stage-3b-campaign, one community answer, consistent with the card).
  - 146069:B:1 (return). Each guarded boon goes to its owner's hand when Shagrat leaves play, defeated or not: the guarded rule counts an enemy "defeated or leaving play by a card effect" as dealt with, and the Resolution loses only boons still guarded by a Shagrat in play. Alternative: only when he is defeated (the card says "defeated"; the reading widens it to any leaving play, as the guarded rule does). Source: `rr.guarded`, the card's Resolution. Status: agent recommendation (no ruling found: the card and https://visionofthepalantir.com/2025/01/07/the-tower-of-cirith-ungol/ say "defeated" only; the return when Shagrat leaves play any other way rests on `rr.guarded`).
  - 146069:B:2 (Resolution). With Shagrat in play, the boons attached to him leave the pool ("The players have lost those boons"); with him defeated, or out of play before stage 3A, nothing is removed. Source: the card, [V], [T]. Status: literal.
  - 146066:A:1 (the Ring-bearer under Topmost Chamber). The Ring-bearer (Frodo, with its attachments and tokens) lies faceup under Topmost Chamber as a guarded objective: the first player loses control of it, nobody controls it, it stays in play and takes no archery or other damage while nobody controls it (`guardedHero`), and The One Ring is moved off it first.   - 146014:A:1 (Fellowship sphere).   - 146014:A:2 (action). Exhaust The One Ring (ready) and raise your threat by 1 to choose any enemy in the staging area or engaged: until the end of the phase it gets -X Threat, X the Willpower of the hero carrying Last of the Company now, recalculated as it changes (an encounter card's lasting effect, `faq.lasting-effects`: Last of the Company is an objective with an encounter-card back).   - 146017:A:1 (Bloodied Courtyard). Status: literal.
  - 146018:A:1. While active each enemy in the staging area gets +10 engagement cost. Status: literal.
  - 146019:A:1, 146067:B:1.   - 146020 (Shagrat). Status: literal.
  - 146021 (Gorbag). Forced: after he engages you he makes an immediate attack. Shadow: the attacking enemy makes an additional attack against you after this one, dealt a new shadow card, the old ones discarded first (`faq.additional-attacks`); written as its own record. Status: span-settled.
  - 146022 (Wounded Uruk). It enters play with 2 damage (staged, engaged or revealed); after it engages you it gets +2 Attack until the end of the round; its shadow gives the attacker +1 Attack for each damage on it, recalculated while the attack lasts (`faq.lasting-effects`). Status: span-settled.
  - 146023 (Orcish Howls). Shadow: with another enemy engaged with the defending player the attack is resolved against that enemy chosen by the defending player, which takes the damage after its Defense and no player takes any; with none nothing happens. Status: span-settled; the shadow agent recommendation (no ruling found; precedent 06062:A:1, `RedirectAttack`; no ruling).
  - 146058, 146059, 146060 (the Orcs). Orc of Mordor exhausts a character you control; Snaga Archer deals 2 damage to one character you control (its shadow +1 Attack, +2 when the defending character is damaged); Uruk Soldier raises the engaged player's threat by 2. Status: literal.

  - 146063 (Evil Vigilance).   - 146066:B:1 to B:3 (stage 1B).
