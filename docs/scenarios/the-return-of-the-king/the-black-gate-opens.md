# The Black Gate Opens

Scenario 17 (`mec113.dire`, `mec113.saga-staging-rules`, `mec113.immune-to-player-card-effects`, `mec113.peril`, `mec113.campaign-mode`, `mec113.multiplayer-aragorn`, `rr.in-player-order`, `rr.first-player`, `rr.cancel`, `rr.search`, `rr.defending-player`, `rr.cannot`, `rr.quest-advancement`, `faq.lasting-effects`, `faq.additional-attacks`, `faq.progress-without-advancing`; records `src/effects/authored/return-of-the-king/the-black-gate-opens.ts` and `black-gate/{shared,stages}.ts`, campaign card 146074 in `the-return-of-the-king.ts`; the family `black-gate` in `src/catalog/families/black-gate.ts` and `src/rules/families/black-gate/`, with verbs in the encounter, south, nightmare and campaign families). Sources: VotP is https://visionofthepalantir.com/2025/05/31/the-black-gate-opens/ and https://visionofthepalantir.com/2025/09/15/mount-doom/; FAQ 1.50 is quoted in https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/227446-put-into-play-engaged-equals-engaged.

- **The end (user decision, 2026-11-12).** The scenario cannot be won: stage 2B cannot be defeated (146073:B:3) and the campaign card has no victory condition. It ends when the last player is eliminated (threat 99 under Dire, or no hero left), and in Campaign Mode that end runs the campaign card's Resolution (146074:B:1) and unlocks Mount Doom. The flag `endsInDefeat` comes from the campaign card (a constant record, `146074:constant:1`; `endsInDefeat` and `eliminateSeat` in `src/rules/core/eliminate.ts`): the last living seat that falls is left as it stood, the scenario is cleared and the Resolution reads the state, the cards in play, the first player, the discard piles and the victory display included. A standalone game has no campaign card, so the same elimination is a loss; every other loss (Aragorn leaving play) writes nothing. The table says "The end", not "Victory", on the rail and the outcome sheet, the Resolution screen says "The end" and "Every player fell. The scenario ends here, and the campaign goes on", and the run list says "Ended · Every player fell" (`endedInDefeat`, `ENDED_BY_DEFEAT`; the run keeps the result won so the campaign goes on, with that cause). The path says "Ended" on the part's stop (`PathNode.ended`, from the campaign card's flag), the quest readout and the Black Gate's dial say on stage 2B that the stage cannot be defeated and what ends the scenario, and the Resolution's sentence records "the end", never the win. Every seat scores as eliminated at this end (50 and the threat cost of every hero, `scoreParts`): the last seat is left as it stood for the Resolution's reading, but every player fell. Source: VotP ("This scenario cannot be won"; the Black Gate's count is the result; Mount Doom starts with as many resources as were placed at the Black Gate). Status: user decision.
- **Dire (`mec113.dire`).** While the main quest has the keyword (read from the current quest card, from the first reveal of 146072 on): each player's elimination level is 99 (`eliminateEmpties`; the scoring rule's 50 for an eliminated seat is unchanged), each player's threat cannot be reduced by more than 1 each round by non-boon player card effects (`lowerThreatByEffect`, the cap of `playerThreatReductionCap`; a boon is a player card with `campaignKind` boon), and Valour reads 80 instead of 40: a condition carries `valour: true` where the printed text is Valour (the threshold and the "not Valour" complements of Shining Shield, Valiant Sword and Soldier of Gondor; Pillars of the Kings keeps its own 40). `state.dire` is kept by the settle loop (`syncDire`). Status: span-settled.
- **Readings.** Each: line, reading, alternative, source, status.
  - 146072:B:1 (the row). Each player in turn, the first player first, places the top card of their deck faceup until there are 4 in all, the round continuing round the table (a solo game places 4, two players 2 each); a player with an empty deck is skipped. Source: `rr.in-player-order`. Status: span-settled.
  - 146072:B:1 (pairing). Player cards are taken from the highest printed cost down (X and no cost count 0); each takes the highest-Threat encounter card left (a card with no Threat is 0; a location or enemy uses its current Threat). A tie for either is decided by the first player. Alternative: reveal order. Source: the card, `rr.first-player`; no ruling on ties found (Hall of Beorn, VotP, the FFG forum archive). Status: agent recommendation (no ruling found).
  - 146072:B:1 (the choice). Per pair, one at a time, the first player chooses: discard both, or reveal the encounter card (a "you" on it is the first player; Peril, Surge and Doomed as for a card revealed in the staging step) and play the player card at no cost, its owner playing it and choosing hosts and targets. A player card that cannot be played at no cost (an event with nothing to do, an attachment with no host, a unique ally already in play, a side quest) is discarded with its pair without asking, the option "reveal and play" being offered only while the card can be played. Alternative: the reveal is always offered, and the encounter card is revealed for nothing before the pair is discarded when the card cannot be played. Source: the card ("to play the player card"); VotP ("As a group, the players must now decide for each pair" and, of a conditional event with no effect, "don't bother and discard it"); searched Hall of Beorn, BoardGameGeek, the lotrlcg subreddit and the FFG forum archive: no ruling on an unplayable card. Status: confirmed (the card: "the first player must choose"; https://visionofthepalantir.com/2025/05/31/the-black-gate-opens/ has the players decide for each pair).
  - 146072:B:1 (timing). It resolves during Setup after the opening hands, the mulligans and the Andúril search, reading the decks as they are then; with fewer than 4 cards in all, the encounter cards left unpaired go back on top of the deck in their order. Status: agent recommendation (no ruling found; the card is silent).
  - 146073:A:1 (stage 2A). Each player in turn, the first player first, searches the encounter deck and discard pile for an Orc, Troll or Nazgûl enemy with a title no other player took and adds it to the staging area (nothing when there is none); Gwaihir, set aside by the Setup, is shuffled into the encounter deck. Status: literal.
  - 146073:B:1 (reveals). Only the first player's reveal is replaced: the first player reveals one encounter card for each resource on The Black Gate, read when the staging step begins (after the Black Gate's Forced effect placed one), all before the next player, who each reveal their one card as usual. Alternative: the whole staging step reveals only that many cards, all by the first player. Source: the card ("the first player does not reveal an encounter card as normal"), `mec113.saga-staging-rules`; VotP is solo. Status: literal for the card, agent recommendation (no ruling found) for more than one player.
  - 146073:B:2 (Forced). After the players quest unsuccessfully the active location goes to the encounter discard pile unexplored: no victory points, no progress carried (the verb `Discard` reaches `activeLocation`); nothing when there is none. Status: literal.
  - 146073:B:3 and 146072 (0 quest points). The stage cannot be defeated (`Restrict advance`; progress still goes on it, `faq.progress-without-advancing`). Stage 1B, with 0 quest points, advances when it next takes progress, the first successful quest, as the engine reads any 0-point stage (`rr.quest-advancement`); no location can be active before it. Alternative: advances at once when revealed. Source: the FFG FAQ and forum archive (stages with 0 or "-" points vary by cycle). Status: agent recommendation (no ruling found).
  - 146024:A:1 to A:3 (The Black Gate). The players cannot travel here (a travel bar on itself, absolute), so stage 1B never has an active location; it is immune to player card effects (an override: the scan prints it, Hall of Beorn lists no keyword). Forced at the beginning of the quest phase: a resource on it, then each player in player order raises their threat by 1. It has +1 Threat for each resource on it. Status: literal.
  - 146025:A:2, A:3 (Towers of the Teeth). Its Threat X, shown on the card as X, is the number of characters controlled by the player who controls the most, read live; its 8 quest points stay printed. While it is the active location no player can play allies (any player, absolute); allies put into play by an effect are not played. Status: literal.
  - 146026:A:1 (Beleaguered Hills). While it is the active location the total Threat in the staging area is lowered by the current Threat of every engaged enemy (any player's), read live and never below 0 (the amount `totalOf`). Alternative: the printed Threat, or the enemies engaged with the first player only. Source: the card, `faq.lasting-effects`. Status: literal.
  - 146026:A:2. After it becomes the active location each player in turn, the first player first, chooses a non-unique enemy in the staging area and engages it (no engagement check, no cost); with fewer enemies than players the rest engage none. Alternative: the first player chooses for everyone. Source: the card ("each player engages"); `rr.first-player` covers only a target chosen for one player. Status: agent recommendation (no ruling found).
  - 146027:A:1 (Waste of Dagorlad). While it is the active location every ally in play has a blank text box (Traits kept). Status: literal.
  - 146028:A:1 (Slopes of Cirith Gorgor). Forced when it leaves play, explored or discarded: the first player shuffles the encounter discard pile into the deck and discards from the top until an enemy is discarded; that enemy comes into play engaged with the first player (not revealed); nothing when no enemy is left. Status: literal.
  - 146029:A:1, A:2 (Reeking Moat). Forced after it becomes the active location: the first player chooses a non-unique, non-Nazgûl enemy in the staging area and places it facedown under it, out of play; when it leaves play every enemy under it comes into play engaged with the first player (`ReturnUnderToStaging to: "engaged"`). Source: `rr.first-player`, the card. Status: span-settled for the chooser, literal otherwise.
  - 146030:A:1, A:2 (Wall of Morannon). While it is the active location each enemy and each location in the staging area is immune to player card effects; the Forced effect is Shadow of Mordor's (a copy of 144031:A:2). Status: literal.
  - 146031:A:1, A:2 (The Mouth of Sauron). Immune (an override) and cannot leave the staging area, so it is never engaged. Forced: after a player cancels a When Revealed effect it makes an immediate attack against that player, dealt a shadow card, with Sentinel allowed to defend (`faq.enemy-attacks-outside-combat`); none when it is not in the staging area. The cancel names its player (`whenRevealedCanceled` carries the seat; `AttackFromStaging who: "eventSeat"`). Status: literal.
  - 146032:A:2 (Nazgûl of Mordor). Forced after it attacks: the defending player chooses to raise their threat by 4 or return it to the staging area. Source: `rr.defending-player`. Status: span-settled.
  - 146033:A:2 (Gorgoroth Hill-troll). Forced after it attacks and destroys a character, an undefended hero included: the defending player discards a random card from their hand and raises their threat by its printed cost (X or no cost counts 0; an empty hand raises 0). Source: `rr.defending-player`. Status: span-settled for who; agent recommendation (no ruling found) for X and no cost.
  - 146034:A:2, 146035:A:2, 146039:A:2 ("you"). The revealing player (saga staging, Peril: resolved alone): Easterling Bowman deals 1 damage to a questing character they control, Warrior of Rhûn removes one from the quest, and Rage Filled Him has The Mouth of Sauron attack them and raises their threat by 4 if the attack destroys a character (`AttackFromStaging raiseThreatIfDestroys`). Source: `mec113.saga-staging-rules`, `mec113.peril`. Status: span-settled.
  - 146034:A:3 (Easterling Bowman shadow). After this attack the attacking enemy engages the next player and then attacks again, dealt a new shadow card with the old ones discarded first; none with one player. Source: `faq.next-player`, `faq.additional-attacks`. Status: span-settled.
  - 146035:A:3 (Warrior of Rhûn shadow). The defending character cannot ready until the end of the round, absolute (`cannotReady`, also against card effects that ready it). Source: `rr.cannot`. Status: span-settled.
  - 146036:A:2 (Hell-hawk). The revealing player chooses: search the encounter deck, discard pile and victory display for a Nazgûl enemy and add it to the staging area, or attach Hell-hawk to a Nazgûl enemy in play (staging or engaged) with no Hell-hawk or Fell Beast on it. An option with no card is not offered. It counts as a Mount attachment (+2 Threat, Attack and Defense, immune to player card effects). Alternative: the first player chooses. Source: the card, `mec113.saga-staging-rules` ("you"). Status: agent recommendation (no ruling found).
  - 146037:A:2 (The Hour of Doom). Doomed 1 for each player; in player order each player chooses to raise their threat by 1 for each character they control, or to discard the highest cost ally they control (the player chooses among ties; not offered without an ally). Status: literal.
  - 146038:A:1, A:2 (Jaws of Steel). X is the resources on The Black Gate now (0 when it is not in play); each player assigns X damage among their own characters; the shadow gives the attacker +1 Attack for each resource, counted when it resolves. Status: literal.
  - 146039:A:3, 146040, 146041. Rage Filled Him's shadow is +1 Attack, or +3 instead when the attacker is The Mouth of Sauron (not both). Onslaught of Mordor: each player in turn deals 1 damage to a questing character they control; until the end of the phase each damaged character gets -1 Willpower and cannot be healed, recalculated while it lasts (`faq.lasting-effects`); its shadow has the first player choose a non-unique enemy in the staging area, which engages the defending player and is dealt a shadow card (agent recommendation, no ruling found: who chooses; the first player choosing the target by `rr.first-player` is the span-based alternative). Gwaihir: the first player chooses to take control of him or to spend 1 Fellowship resource (only Aragorn's pool holds one, as the Grey Company's reading) and discard him to discard a non-unique enemy in the staging area, each only while it can be done; his shadow makes the attack deal no damage. Status: span-settled for Onslaught, literal for Gwaihir and the shadows.
  - 146028:A:1, 146029:A:2 and every "After X engages you" (put into play engaged). An enemy put into play engaged with a player engages that player, so the Orcs of Mordor's Forced effects are heard. Source: FAQ 1.50 "Considered to be engaged vs actual engagement", quoted secondhand (one dissent). Status: confirmed (FAQ 1.50 "Considered to be engaged vs actual engagement" as the forum thread quotes it, secondhand and with a few dissenters: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/227446-put-into-play-engaged-equals-engaged).
  - 146074:B:1 (Resolution). The tokens on The Black Gate are recorded for Mount Doom, a Wraith on Wings leaves the pool for each copy in the victory display, Gwaihir is earned for the players when the first player controls him in the final state, and a hero in its controller's discard pile is not on the fallen list. Status: literal.
- **Table pieces** (`src/play/readouts/black-gate.ts`, `src/play/readouts/threat-level.ts`, `src/play/words/scenarios/black-gate.ts`, `src/play/words/asks/the-black-gate-opens.ts`; `docs/design-system-table.md`, Top bar and Cards). Each seat's threat dial fills and warns toward 99 while the main quest has Dire (146072:B, `mec113.dire`), and the threat panel says "of 99". The enemies under Reeking Moat are a dial counting them (`moat-under`, "Moat"), naming them and saying they engage the first player when it leaves play (146029:A:1, A:2). The Embassy's row is a dial while Setup decides it (`embassy-pairs`, "Embassy": the pairs left, each as the owner's player card with its encounter card, "Player 1's Gandalf with Hill-troll"); the pair being decided is named in the choice's sentence (`{pair}` in the asks file), and the row's cards are not drawn on the canvas. The seat that controls Gwaihir wears a "Gwaihir" mark ("P1 · Gwaihir" two-handed) saying he is earned only if the first player controls him when the scenario ends (146041:A:2, 146074:B:1). No dial fills toward a goal. Every Pick and Choose is worded by the asks file, leading with the player two-handed, and the rows that can lose are armed with "Tap again": Aragorn destroyed by the damage ("the players lose the game", `mec113.multiplayer-aragorn`) and a raise of threat to 99, which says that the last player eliminated ends the scenario and that the resources on The Black Gate go to Mount Doom (a standalone game: that the game is lost). Words for the end: the rail and sheet "The end", the Resolution "The end", the run list "Ended".
