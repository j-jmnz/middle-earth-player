# Mount Doom

Scenario 18, the saga's last (`mec113.fortitude-tests`, `mec113.player-actions-during-fortitude-tests`, `mec113.dire`, `mec113.the-one-ring`, `mec113.setting-up-the-game`, `mec113.saga-staging-rules`, `mec113.setup-mount-doom`, `rr.then`, `rr.active-location`, `rr.travel`, `rr.first-player`, `rr.in-player-order`, `rr.the-word-choose`, `rr.running-out-of-cards`, `rr.winning-the-game`, `faq.lasting-effects`, `faq.additional-attacks`, `faq.progress-without-advancing`; records `src/effects/authored/return-of-the-king/mount-doom.ts` and `mount-doom/{shared,locations,enemies,stages}.ts`, campaign card 146079 in `the-return-of-the-king.ts`; the family `mount-doom` in `src/catalog/families/mount-doom.ts` and `src/rules/families/mount-doom/`, with verbs in the encounter and campaign families). Sources: VotP is https://visionofthepalantir.com/2025/09/15/mount-doom/, [BGG1] is https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3556685/mount-doom-questions, [BGG2] is https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3591157/lotr-saga-questions, [FFG] is https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/287588-mount-doom-strategy-advice/page/2/ and [ENG] is https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/112007-engages-the-first-player. The BoardGameGeek pages are read through `api.geekdo.com`. No official ruling about the scenario exists: the FFG FAQ has no Fortitude entry, and the answers found are a long-standing community rules answerer's.

- **The end.** The players win when stage 4B's Fortitude test is passed after Mount Doom is explored (`WinGame`: the campaign card's Resolution runs and the saga is over); they lose the game when it is failed (`LoseGame`, "The Ring-bearer claimed The One Ring": nothing is written), at threat 99 under Dire, when The One Ring leaves play, and when the campaign card's last token comes off (146079:A:2). A loss writes nothing in any case. The Resolution (146079:B:1 to B:3) asks the first player to destroy The One Ring (the players win the campaign, "The One Ring is destroyed. The players win the campaign.") or keep it (they lose the campaign, "The One Ring is kept. The players lose the campaign."): the Resolution screen words each option above Confirm (the keep option in the danger tone), and the path and the Resolution word the end from the log. After the Ring is kept the settled Resolution is the scene of an end, calm, and says once, over Confirm, "Confirming records the end: The One Ring is kept and the players lose the campaign." Either way the campaign is finished and the path offers no next part. Source: the card. Status: literal.
- **The Fortitude test (`mec113.fortitude-tests`).** A frame like the Hide test's (`FortitudeTest`, window `fortitudeCommit`, intent `CommitFortitude`; `src/rules/families/mount-doom/fortitude.ts`): the tester exhausts any number of ready heroes to commit them (heroes only; none of them is asked when no hero can commit), then discards the top X cards of the encounter deck, X the tens digit of their own threat dial (0 below 10), not revealed; the Sauron's Eye icons the cards print (the catalog's `keywords.eyeIcons`, read from the ledger) and the icons a card adds are counted; an action window follows the count; then the Willpower of the committed heroes still in play is compared: strictly greater passes, equal or less fails (so X of 0 with no hero committed fails, with a hero it passes). A failed test is heard (`fortitudeFailed`, `lastFortitudeFailed`, the round's `fortitudeFailures`) before the card that made it goes on. Several players' tests inside one effect are played in player order, each test with its consequence before the next (not forced by the card; [BGG2] says the order is free). Source: `mec113.fortitude-tests`, `mec113.player-actions-during-fortitude-tests`. Status: span-settled; researched for "no window before the commit" ([FFG], [BGG1] for a tie failing in VotP).
- **A reshuffle in a test (`mec113.fortitude-tests`).** When the encounter deck is empty during a test the discard pile, the test's own discards included, is shuffled into it, so a card may be discarded twice in one test and its icons are counted at each discard. The deck emptied by the test's last discard is refilled at once, so the reveal a failed test makes (Orc Captain) has a card. Alternative: the test's discards stay out of the shuffle, or count once. Source: the span says only to shuffle the discard pile into the deck; no ruling found (VotP, BGG, the FFG forum archive, the card pages). Status: confirmed for the reshuffle (`mec113.fortitude-tests`); agent recommendation (no ruling found) for counting a card again.
- **Gollum (146049:A:2, A:3).** The unprefixed line is a passive: he engages the first player when he is added to the staging area, when the first player token passes (the same as 144075) and at once whenever an effect returns him to the staging area (`returnedToStaging`; Orc Captain's shadow). Alternative: he engages only on being added and on the token pass, and a returned Gollum stays in the staging area. Source: [ENG] ("unprefixed text is a passive ability; the enemy follows the first player token"; community), `mec112.gollum-smeagol` ("already engaged"). Status: researched. The Forced of A:3 is heard once every card of the test is discarded and before the icons are counted: he comes into play engaged with the first player (an engaged-by-effect enemy, so engagement Forced effects are heard), then the first player, who chooses as the controller of an encounter effect, chooses an encounter card discarded for the test (Gollum himself is no longer in the discard pile) and its icons are not counted. The asterisk is emphasis only. Alternative: the failing or testing player chooses. Source: `rr.the-word-choose`, `rr.first-player`, [BGG1] ("the asterisk is just emphasis"). Status: span-settled for the chooser; confirmed for the lasting engagement with the first player (https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/112007-engages-the-first-player, https://visionofthepalantir.com/2025/09/15/mount-doom/); agent recommendation (no ruling found) for the timing before the icon count. Gollum's Surge and Immune to non-Fellowship are as in 144075.
- **Failure effects inside the test.** The Ringwraith of Sauron in the staging area engages the player who fails and attacks mid-resolution of the treachery, before Foul Fumes' damage and blanking (146050:A:2, 146056:A:1, 146055:A:2, 146051:A:1, Heavy and Tired's Forced). Source: [BGG2]. Status: agent recommendation (no ruling found; https://visionofthepalantir.com/2025/09/15/mount-doom/ gives the Ringwraith's Forced but not its order against the treachery's text).
- **Round memory (146046:A:1, 146055:A:3, 146056:A:2).** "If a player has failed a Fortitude test this round" (Plain of Gorgoroth, any player, read live each time the staging Threat is totalled) and "if you failed" (the shadows, the defending player): the round's failures, pruned when the round ends. Status: literal.
- **Added icons (146047:A:1, 146055:A:2).** Brake of Thorns in the staging area adds one icon to each test, once for the test and not for each card, not while it is the active location. The Lidless Eye adds two to its own test when The One Ring is exhausted, checked as the test is made. Status: literal.
- **Brake of Thorns (146047:A:2).** Its Forced fires at Setup: stage 1A makes it the active location, so each player assigns X damage (X their own tens digit) at the start of the game (`announceSetupActive`). Source: [BGG1] ("after it becomes active ... stage 1a reads make it active. So yes"). Status: researched. Data: the Setup makes one copy of Brake of Thorns the active location, so the set's other copies are dealt to the encounter deck like any location the players search for. The scenario data lists the four searched locations as `searched` copies that stay in the encounter deck. Status: confirmed that the Forced fires at Setup (the card and https://visionofthepalantir.com/2025/09/15/mount-doom/: making it active counts as becoming active); agent recommendation (no ruling found) for the copy made active (a data reading: the stage's pile list names one active copy and no count for the rest).
- **Heavy and Tired (146057:A:1).** It attaches to the hero with the most attachments (the first player chooses among ties) and exhausts it; it counts as a Condition attachment; the attached hero cannot commit to Fortitude tests; Forced after its controller fails a test: exhaust the hero and discard each non-objective attachment on it, Heavy and Tired itself included. Source: [BGG1] ("Yes, you would discard Heavy and Tired as well"). Status: confirmed for the host (the card and https://visionofthepalantir.com/2025/09/15/mount-doom/); agent recommendation (no ruling found) for ties.
- **Foul Fumes (146056:A:1).** Each player makes a test; each who fails deals 1 damage to each character they control; the blank text box is live (a healed character is no longer blank, one damaged later is, until the end of the round). Source: `faq.lasting-effects`. Status: span-settled.
- **Mount Doom (146043).** Immune to player card effects (an override: the scan prints it, Hall of Beorn lists no keyword), Threat X the tens digit of the first player's threat read live, no travel here unless stage 3B holds 20 progress, Travel: each player in player order makes a test and each who fails raises their threat by 9. Status: literal.
- **The Tower of Barad-dur (146042:A:1, A:2).** While it is in the staging area no player raises threat at the end of the refresh phase (a table flag, `noRefreshThreat`, read by step 7.3); Forced at the end of the round each player raises threat by 1 for each non-Fellowship character they control (heroes and allies; the Ring-bearer is Fellowship; The One Ring is no character). Source: VotP ("each non-fellowship character"), `rr.step-7-3`. Status: literal.
- **The locations.** Orc-hold (146044): while in the staging area no progress is placed on the other locations there; Forced after it becomes active: the discard pile is shuffled in, cards are discarded until an Orc, which comes into play engaged with the first player. Mordor Road (146045): +1 Threat for each ally of the player with the most; Forced after it becomes active: each player discards the top card and each enemy is added to the staging area. Plain of Gorgoroth (146046:A:2): the first player makes the test, a failure raises each player's threat by 4, and the players travel either way. Old Water-course (146048): while active it has the Response "When it is explored, reduce each player's threat by 5" (an encounter location's effect: Dire's cap on reduction counts player card effects only, `mec113.dire`); Travel: each player exhausts a hero, and a player with none ready cannot pay, so the players cannot travel. Status: literal; span-settled for the cap.
- **The enemies and treacheries.** Orc Captain (146051:A:1): after it engages you, make a test; a failure reveals the top card (you reveal). Big Uruk (146052): after it engages you, raise your threat by 4 or it attacks you at once; the shadow is +1 Attack for each ally you control. Little Snuffler (146053): raise threat by 1 for each ally or discard an ally (not offered with no ally); the shadow exhausts a character you control. 'Wicked Master!' (146054:A:1): Gollum attacks the first player at once, then, if he is not in play, he is searched for and comes into play engaged with the first player (a Gollum fetched this way does not attack: the order of the lines; alternative: he attacks too; no ruling found in VotP, BGG or the FFG archive; status: agent recommendation (no ruling found)); the shadow gives Gollum an additional attack, dealt a new shadow card (`faq.additional-attacks`). Heavy and Tired, Foul Fumes and The Lidless Eye are above. Status: literal unless named.
- **The first treachery each quest phase (146075:B:2, 146077:B:1).** The first treachery revealed in each quest phase (any seat's, a Surge or Peril reveal included) is immune to player card effects and gains surge until it leaves play, the marker resetting each quest phase; the one marker serves stages 1B and 3B. A treachery revealed outside the quest phase is not marked. Alternative: the first each round. Source: no ruling found (VotP, the FFG archive, BGG). Status: confirmed (the card prints "each quest phase" on both stages; https://visionofthepalantir.com/2025/09/15/mount-doom/ paraphrases it as each round).
- **Stage 1 (146075).** Setup: Mount Doom and the Tower of Barad-dur are in the staging area, Brake of Thorns is the active location, each player in player order searches for a different location and adds it to the staging area, and the deck is shuffled. Side B has 4 quest points and +4 for each player. Status: literal.
- **Stage 2 (146076).** 2A: Mordor Road is searched for in the deck, the discard pile and the victory display and added to the staging area, the discard pile is shuffled into the deck, and the players travel there, becoming active with its Forced only when no location is active (`rr.active-location`; `TravelTo unlessActive`). 2B has no quest points and cannot be defeated by progress; its Forced fires after progress is placed on the stage itself from a successful quest (not when the active location took it): each player in player order makes a test, a pass places a resource on the stage, a failure discards until an enemy is discarded and puts it into play engaged with that player (nothing with no enemy in the deck); at the end of the quest phase the quest advances to 3A when the resources equal or exceed the players. Status: literal; span-settled for 2A (`rr.active-location`, `rr.travel`).
- **Stage 3 (146077).** 3A: the discard pile is shuffled in and cards are discarded until one location for each player is discarded, each added to the staging area. 3B has 20 quest points: at the beginning of the quest phase the first player makes a test and on a failure no more than 5 progress can be placed on the stage this round (`questProgressCap`; the active location takes progress first); it cannot be defeated unless Mount Doom is the active location (`Restrict advance`; progress still goes on, `faq.progress-without-advancing`). Status: literal.
- **Stage 4 (146078).** 4A: one enemy for each player is discarded the same way and added to the staging area. 4B has no quest points and cannot be defeated by progress, allies cannot enter play (`LimitEntries`) and when revealed effects cannot be canceled; when Mount Doom is explored the first player makes a test discarding 1 extra card for each player in the game, counted with the X cards before the icons: a failure loses the game, a pass wins it. Alternative: one extra card for each player who tests (only one tests: the same count only alone). Status: confirmed (the card prints "for each player"; https://visionofthepalantir.com/2025/09/15/mount-doom/).
- **The campaign card, Setup (146079:A:1).** No +1 threat for changing heroes, X resource tokens are placed on the card (X the tokens recorded for The Black Gate in the log, 0 with none recorded), and the burdens of Helm's Deep, The Road to Isengard and The Passing of the Grey Company are left out of the encounter deck. The card stays in the game in the campaign zone. Source: VotP. Status: literal.
- **The campaign card, Forced (146079:A:2).** At the end of each round one token comes off, and then, if none is left, the players lose the game: X tokens last X rounds (the end of round X loses). With no token on the card (X is 0) there is nothing to remove, so "then" does not resolve and the timer never loses (`rr.then`: the part before "then" must resolve in full). Alternative: a check of the card's state that loses at the first end of round. Source: `rr.then`. Status: span-settled.
- **Dire, the Ring and the elimination level.** All four stages have Dire (`mec113.dire`): each player's elimination level is 99, non-boon player card reductions of threat are capped at 1 each round and Valour reads 80 (no card here reads it). Under Dire each threat dial fills toward 99. If The One Ring leaves play the players lose the game (`mec113.the-one-ring`). Status: span-settled.
- **The auto-player.** The goldens commit one hero to each test and quest with the others, because a test exhausts the heroes it commits and a quest of exhausted heroes has no Willpower; a choice takes its last option (the discard of a questing character, which would take the Ring-bearer, is a first option).
- **Table pieces** (`src/play/readouts/mount-doom.ts`, `src/play/readouts/fortitude.ts`, `src/play/words/scenarios/mount-doom.ts`, `src/play/words/asks/mount-doom.ts`; `docs/design-system-table.md`, Cards, Mount Doom, says how each is drawn and worded). The timer's dial is drawn only while a token is on the campaign card, since the timer runs from what Setup placed and the last token off is the loss (146079:A:1, A:2). The Fortitude sheets say what passing and failing do for the card that made the test and for each effect in play that hears a failure (Ringwraith of Sauron, Heavy and Tired), and arm their confirm with the failure when the test cannot pass (146043:A:3, 146050:A:2, 146057:A:1, 146076:B:2). Each seat-spanning pick leads with its player in the asks.
