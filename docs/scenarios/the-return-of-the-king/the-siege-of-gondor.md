# The Siege of Gondor

Scenario 14 (`mec113.saga-staging-rules`, `mec113.peril`, `mec113.archery`, `mec113.army-of-the-dead`, `mec113.multiplayer-aragorn`; records `src/effects/authored/return-of-the-king/the-siege-of-gondor.ts`, campaign card 145070 in `the-return-of-the-king.ts`; the heal cap in `src/rules/families/dreamchaser/heal-cap.ts`, the `highestCost` pick filter in `families/costs/pick.ts`). Great Corsair Ship and The Corsair Fleet are placed in the staging area by the scenario's staging pile (no record places them); the Army of the Dead objective ally is in play only when the campaign pool holds it, under the first player. Every reading below is applied as written; the ones marked agent recommendation (no ruling found) are not user-confirmed. Sources: [A] https://visionofthepalantir.com/2024/02/05/the-siege-of-gondor/, [B] https://theroadlotr.wordpress.com/2019/04/23/the-siege-of-gondor-playthrough/, [C] https://hallofbeorn.com/LotR/Details/The-Siege-of-Gondor-Campaign-TRotK (setup and resolution text, no rulings), [D] https://hallofgondor.com/rulings/ (nothing on these cards), [E] the FFG FAQ v1.9 (no entry for this box); searches of BoardGameGeek, the lotrlcg subreddit and the Hall of Beorn card pages for Fell Folk of Umbar, Raiding the Coasts and The Corsair Fleet found no ruling.

- **Readings.**
  - 145069:B:2 (stage 1B). Progress goes on past 20 while Great Corsair Ship is in play and the stage is not defeated then (`Restrict advance`); the moment the Ship leaves play (explored, so in the victory display) with 20 or more progress on the stage, the stage is defeated and the players win (advancing past the only stage is the win, `rr.winning-the-game`; no `WinGame` record). Alternative: the stage is checked only when more progress is placed after the Ship is gone. Source: `faq.progress-without-advancing`, `rr.quest-advancement` ("as soon as"), [B] (plays 23 of 20 with the Ship active and wins on exploring it), [A]. Status: span-settled.
  - 145069:B:1. Ship locations are immune to player card effects only in the staging area (the Ship location that becomes active is not), and one that enters the staging area later is immune too (a quest card's constant). Source: the card, `rr.lasting-effects`. Status: literal.
  - 145069:A:1 (Setup). Each player, the first player first, searches the encounter deck, not its discard pile (`rr.search`), for a Ship location of any title and adds it to the staging area; nothing found adds nothing; the deck is then shuffled. Alternative: also the discard pile, or different titles. Source: the line, 145064:A:1 (the deck-only form). Status: literal.
  - 145071:A:1 (The Corsair Fleet). At the beginning of the staging step it gets 1 resource, then (`rr.then`) each player in turn, the first player first, chooses either to reveal an encounter card (alone, in a staging of their own: `mec113.saga-staging-rules`, `mec113.peril`) or to assign X damage, X the resources on the Fleet after that one, among the characters they control (the Army of the Dead and Aragorn included). An option that cannot change the game is not offered (`rr.effects`): a player with no character has only the reveal. Each player chooses and resolves in turn (143067:A:1 and 143063:A:2 do). Alternative: all choose first, or the first player assigns for everyone. Source: `rr.then`, `rr.effects`, `mec113.saga-staging-rules`, [A], [B]. Status: span-settled for the option rule and X; the sequential order is agent recommendation (no ruling found; [A], [B] and [D] do not speak to it).
  - 145030:A:1. Great Corsair Ship's Threat X is the resources on The Corsair Fleet, read live (it grows each round while the Ship is in the staging area); 145042:A:2 reads the same number. The data prints no Threat for it, so the record adds X to 0. Source: the scan, [A]. Status: literal.
  - 145030:A:2. The players cannot travel to Great Corsair Ship unless 3 or more Ship locations (location type, trait Ship: the Umbar War Galley and Corsair Dromon) are in the victory display, any owner. Source: the line, 12092:B:3 and 18068:B:1 (the same form). Status: literal.
  - 145030:A:3 (Travel). Each player in turn, the first player first, searches the encounter deck and discard pile for a Corsair enemy and adds it to the staging area; "one of those enemies must be Corsair Captain, if able": the first search must take a Captain while one is in the deck or discard pile and the later searches are free (`SearchMove` `require` binds until the effect has taken the code once). Alternative: every search must take a Captain while any remain, or only the last. Source: the line; [A] confirms only the "if able" wording. Status: agent recommendation (no ruling found; no source speaks to two copies).
  - 145031:A:2 and 145032:A:2 (Travel). The discard pile is shuffled into the encounter deck and cards are discarded one at a time until a Corsair enemy is discarded, which is added to the staging area; with no Corsair enemy left the whole deck is discarded, nothing is added and the travel still happens (the deck is reset at the next quest phase, `rr.running-out-of-cards`). Source: the lines, 19018:A:2 and the-ghost-of-framsburg 191, [B] (the deck ran out and was reset). Status: span-settled; the edge with no Corsair left is agent recommendation (no ruling found; agent recommendation).
  - 145031:A:1 and 145032:A:1. While the Galley or the Dromon is the active location every Corsair enemy in play (staging or engaged, one that arrives later too) gets -10 engagement cost or gains Archery 1, which deals damage at the beginning of each combat phase for each such enemy, the staging area's included (`mec113.archery`). Source: the lines, 12073:A:1, 144039:A:1. Status: literal.
  - 145033:A:1 (Port of Pelargir). While it is in the staging area no more than 1 damage can be healed from each character each round, by every effect together, counted from the start of the round (a heal made before the Port arrived counts) and cleared as the round ends; a heal of 2 removes 1. Alternative: the cap is per effect. Source: the line ("each round"), [A] ("EVEN when in the staging area"). Status: researched (the literal reading, [A] agrees; no ruling on the earlier-heal edge). The stat is `healCap` on the table with the ledger `roundHealed`, read by `Heal`, `HealAmong` and `RemoveDamageFrom` on characters only.
  - 145033:A:2 (Shadow). If the attack deals damage to a character (the defender, or the hero an undefended attack landed on) that character cannot ready until the end of the round (`cannotReady`, `rr.cannot`; not the refresh's skip). Source: the line, 18052:A:1, 18072:B:2. Status: literal.
  - 145034:A:1 (Pelargir Quays). At the end of the round, in the staging area or as the active location, a damage token is placed on it; then (`rr.then`) with 4 or more it is discarded and 4 damage is assigned among the characters in play, any player's, one point at a time, the first player deciding (`rr.first-player`). Alternative: each player assigns among their own. Source: the line, [A] ("it does not have to be characters you control"). Status: researched.
  - 145035:A:2 (Corsair Captain). With a Ship as the active location it gets -30 engagement cost and its gained Forced "When Corsair Captain engages you, it makes an immediate attack" is heard only while a Ship is active as it engages, after it is in that player's play area, for an optional engagement and an engagement check alike; "Cannot be optionally engaged" (145035:A:1) stands. Source: 19128:A:1 and 145022:A:2 (the same timing), [B] (the blog engaged it optionally, which the line forbids; the line wins). Status: agent recommendation (no ruling found; as in The Passing of the Grey Company).
  - 145036:A:1. +2 Attack and +2 Defense while a Ship is the active location, checked live; its Shadow is "+1 Attack, +2 with a Ship active" ([A] misreads it; the scan read stands). Status: literal.
  - 145037:A:2 and 145038:A:1 (engages you). Heard after the enemy is in the engaged player's play area; "you" is that player, who chooses the character, a ready one to exhaust or the one to take 1 damage (2 with a Ship as the active location, checked as the Forced resolves). Source: the same timing as 145035:A:2. Status: agent recommendation (no ruling found) for the timing, literal for the numbers.
  - 145037:A:3 (Shadow). With a Ship as the active location the attacker returns to the staging area after this attack. Status: literal.
  - 145038:A:2 (Shadow). The defending character gets -1 Defense for each damage on it, read as the shadow resolves, for this attack only; an undefended attack has no defender and nothing happens. Source: the line, 12080:A:2. Status: literal.
  - 145039:A:2 (Fell Folk of Umbar, Peril). The revealing player alone chooses (`mec113.peril`, [A]): either to return each Corsair enemy engaged with them to the staging area, or to discard cards from the top of the encounter deck until a Corsair enemy is discarded and put it into play engaged with them. The first option is offered only with a Corsair enemy engaged with them; the second whenever the encounter deck or its discard pile holds a card, since "an effect that has the potential to change the game state must be selected" (`rr.effects`) and discarding does; if the deck runs out before a Corsair enemy comes up the discard pile is shuffled back in and the discarding goes on (`faq.last-encounter-card`, `rr.running-out-of-cards`), and with none left in either pile it ends there; with neither option available the card does nothing. Alternative: the discard offered only while a Corsair enemy is in the deck or discard pile. Source: `rr.effects`, `faq.last-encounter-card`, [A] ("your choice has to change the board state"). Status: span-settled; ending with no Corsair left is agent recommendation (no ruling found; agent recommendation).
  - 145040:A:2 (Black Sails, Doomed 1). Doomed resolves first and hits every player (`mec113.saga-staging-rules`). Each Ship location in the staging area gets +1 Threat until the end of the phase, and one that arrives later this phase gets it too (an encounter card's lasting effect applies to every card that meets it: `rr.lasting-effects`, `faq.lasting-effects`; the `stagingCards` holder class with the trait Ship). Then, with no non-unique Ship location in the staging area (Great Corsair Ship is unique), the revealing player searches the encounter deck and discard pile for a Ship location and adds it to the staging area; the deck is shuffled either way. Source: `rr.lasting-effects`, [B]. Status: span-settled for the +1; the revealing player as searcher is agent recommendation (no ruling found; no span says who searches, and the default is the actor).
  - 145041:A:1 (Raiding the Coasts). Each player in turn, the first player first, chooses to add 3 to the staging area's total Threat until the end of the phase (each who chooses it adds 3) or to discard the highest cost attachment they control, wherever it is attached (printed cost, an attachment with no printed cost counting as 0; the player picks among equal costs). With no attachment the second option is not offered. Alternative: only attachments on that player's own cards. Source: the line, `rr.effects`, [A] (the player picks a tie). Status: researched for the option rule and the tie; agent recommendation (no ruling found; agent recommendation) for a printed cost of none counting as 0 and for attachments controlled but attached elsewhere.
  - 145041:A:2 (Shadow). +1 Attack; if the attack destroys a character, the defending player discards an attachment they control, their choice; with none, nothing. Status: literal.
  - 145042:A:2 (Driven to the Brink, Peril). The revealing player alone chooses either to end the quest phase at once without resolving the quest (no progress, no failure threat; the cards the other players had yet to reveal are not revealed; play goes on to the travel phase) or to assign X damage among the questing characters they control; the damage option is not offered with no questing character they control or with X 0. Alternative: all players choose, or any player's characters. Source: the line, [A] ("your questing characters"), `rr.effects`. Status: span-settled for the revealer and the option rule, literal for the rest.
  - 145042:A:3 (Shadow). The defending player exhausts a ready character they control (the line of 19158:A:2). Status: literal.
  - 145068 (Army of the Dead, objective ally). A:1: immune to player card effects, and the first player controls it, passing with the first player token like Aragorn (`mec113.multiplayer-aragorn`; 144076:A:1's constant). A:2: the campaign Setup puts it into play when it is in the campaign pool, under the first player (01135:campaignSetup:1's verb; no standalone game deals it). A:3: when it leaves play it is removed from the game, not discarded; the Resolution (145070:B:1) removes it from the pool whether it survived or not. It can take the Fleet's and Haradrim Pirate's damage (a character; immune to player card effects only). Source: `mec113.army-of-the-dead`, `mec113.immune-to-player-card-effects`, [A], [B]. Status: literal.
  - Campaign card 145070. Setup: the first player may raise each player's threat by 1 to search their deck for Andúril, and the burdens of the five earlier burden sets are left out of the encounter deck; there is no waiver for changing heroes, so each hero changed since the earlier part adds 1 to the starting threat (`rr.campaign-setup-checklist`). Resolution: the resources on The Corsair Fleet are written in the campaign log (`corsairFleet`) and the Army of the Dead boon leaves the pool. Source: the card, [C]. Status: literal.
- **Table pieces** (`src/play/readouts/siege.ts`, `src/play/words/scenarios/siege.ts`, `src/play/words/asks/the-siege-of-gondor.ts`, test `src/play/scenarios/the-siege-of-gondor-table.test.ts`; `docs/design-system-table.md`, Top bar). The dials that can warn come first: each Pelargir Quays has a dial of its damage tokens against the 4 at which it is discarded (`siege-quays`, `siege-quays-2`, "Quays", 145034:A:1; spoken as "the active location" or "in the staging area" for each copy), which warms one token short of the limit and says it then deals 4 damage among the characters; while the Port of Pelargir is in the staging area a dial of the most damage healed from one character this round against the 1 it allows (`siege-port`, "Healed", 145033:A:1), which warns once a character has used it and names the characters at the cap (the cap is a rule over every character, so no mark is drawn on each). Then The Corsair Fleet's resources are a dial (`siege-fleet`, "Fleet": X, 145071:A:1) with no limit, so it never warms, and its words say that the number is written in the campaign log on a win; and the Ship locations in the victory display are a goal dial against the 3 the record asks for, counted with the record's own pick (`siege-ships`, "Ships", 145030:A:2: it fills in the accent, never warms, and is gone once Great Corsair Ship is the active location or has left play). Stage 1B's progress has no dial: while Great Corsair Ship is in play the quest meter says "N progress if nothing changes: held while Great Corsair Ship is in play" when the progress would reach the stage's quest points (145069:B:2). The seat that controls the Army of the Dead wears a mark on its threat dial ("Army", "P2 · Army" two-handed: the held dial, 145068:A:1) that follows the first player token; a seat holding Overcome by Fear as well wears one mark ("P1 · Fear · Army") that joins their words. The Army is a card in its seat's play area under its control and no record holds a card under another, so no zone draws a held card beside a host. The picks that span seats (145034:A:1, the Fleet's and Driven to the Brink's damage, Raiding the Coasts' discard) lead two-handed with the chooser, "Player N" (`owned`), solo with no lead, and their rows are cards on the table in their own seat's area; the Fleet's choice reads "reveal 1 encounter card", and Raiding the Coasts' discard row says whether the highest cost is one attachment or a tie the player settles (an asks entry names only its record's own card, so it cannot name the attachment).
