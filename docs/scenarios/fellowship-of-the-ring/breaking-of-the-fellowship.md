# Breaking of the Fellowship, stage 2B

Stage 2A removes the Ring-bearer and its attachments from the game, with The One Ring's text blank while that resolves: the cards go to the removed zone without being destroyed or discarded, so The One Ring's loss line has nothing to fire. A seat left with no hero is eliminated. Each enemy's engagement cost is 0 during that round's encounter phase, and that round has no travel phase (`engagementCostZero`, `skipTravel`). Stage 1B skips the encounter phase (`skipEncounter`, 142061:B:1); the combat phase still follows.

At the end of the refresh phase on stage 2B, Seat of Seeing and Frodo's Choice are shuffled into the encounter discard pile, which goes under the encounter deck. Each seat, starting with the first, creates its own staging area and advances to a different stage 3A of its choice (142063 to 142066; a true solo player chooses among all four), then the seats move the shared encounter cards to their own areas one at a time in player order, each seat choosing its own card (the last card is taken without asking). Stage 2B is then discarded to the set-aside pile as a defeated stage is, and each seat hears it for its own stage 3A, in player order, as one occurrence: a stage 3A reveals one encounter card for its seat when the total Threat in that seat's staging area is under 4. Phases still resolve in seat order, starting with the first seat.

The state: `ScenarioState.separate` is true from the first `StartSeparateStage`, `Seat.staging` lists that seat's encounter cards and `Seat.stage` is its quest stage; `ScenarioState.questStage` is then null. Every card in an area stays in the zone `staging`, and `ScenarioState.staging` keeps listing every staging card of every area (plus any in no area), so code that does not care which area reads it as before. The engine asks per seat through `viewOf(state, seat)` (`src/rules/families/breaking/areas.ts`): the state with that seat's area as `staging` and its stage as `questStage`. An instruction runs in the view of the seat that acts, so "the staging area", "this stage" and "the quest" are that seat's; a pick's `stagingArea` says `own` (the default), `shared` or `any` ("in any staging area"). A card that enters "the staging area" goes to the acting or revealing seat's area; an enemy that returns from being engaged goes to the area of the seat it was engaged with.

After a seat commits to its own stage, it reveals its encounter card into its own area, then compares its own committed willpower with the threat in its own staging area. Progress goes on its stage (or on the active location first), or its own threat rises by the difference. Another seat does not raise threat for that failure. Each seat's quest resolves in player order; "the players quested successfully" for a delayed effect is true when every living seat did.

Engagement checks and optional engagement use only that seat's staging area. "At this stage" does not reach another seat's cards. Archery is totaled per stage (the seat's staging area, the enemies engaged with it and its stage card), each stage in player order with its own window, and only that seat assigns it, to its own characters. Cards that say "the staging area" or "the quest" mean that seat's. Seats may still affect each other through ordinary card effects that do not use those phrases.

If a seat is eliminated while stages are separate, that stage and the encounter cards at it are discarded: the cards in its area, and the enemies that were engaged with it (they return to its area first), go to the encounter discard pile, and its stage to the set-aside pile.

Each seat has an active location of its own at its stage (agent recommendation, no ruling found: `rr.active-location` allows one active location at a time and `mec109.multiple-staging-areas` gives each player a staging area and a stage of their own; no card states it, and the forum threads https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/124254-doomed-cards-in-separate-staging-areas and https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/125717-breaking-of-the-fellowship-quest-card-2b discuss other interactions). The active location buffers the quest (`rr.active-location`), and "the quest only applies to your quest stage" (`mec109.multiple-staging-areas`); 142079 prints "a player at this staging area ... travel here" and 142075 "While The Argonath is the active location". `Seat.active` is that seat's active location (the card keeps the zone `activeLocation`); `ScenarioState.activeLocation` and `secondActiveLocation` mirror seat 0's and seat 1's, so code outside a seat's view still sees them, and in `viewOf(state, seat)` `activeLocation` is that seat's and `secondActiveLocation` is null. In the travel phase each living seat, in player order, has its own opportunity (`ScenarioState.travelDone`, `rr.travel`, `rr.step-4-2`): it may travel to a location of its own staging area, even when another seat has an active location, and only a seat with none can; the phase's opportunity ends with the last seat's. A seat's questing progress goes on its own active location first. The Travel effects of 142073, 142078 and 142079 resolve for the seat that travels. Before stage 2B the active location is shared; one still active then goes back to the staging area as the areas are created, and a location a seat's area holds when that seat is eliminated is discarded with it.

Stage 3 (142063 to 142066). Each 3B holds `progressDiscards` while Frodo's Choice is not in play: progress that would be placed on it discards that many cards from the top of the encounter deck instead, once the progress step is over (`ScenarioState.progressOwed`, `payOwed`). Frodo's Choice (142068:A:1) is revealed by the seat that discarded it instead of being discarded (`RevealIfDiscarded`), so the discards are how a seat reaches it; its Forced effect on that seat's 3B advances that seat to stage 4A. A stage card is controlled by its seat while stages are separate, which is what makes "at this stage" and "after you quest" about that seat. Stage 4 has one card (142067): the first seat to advance takes it, a seat that advances after finds none and stays on its 3B, and defeating 4B wins the game for all (`rr.winning-the-game`). 4A adds Parth Galen to the revealing seat's area and ends the quest phase for the table when it resolves during the staging step (`EndQuestPhase`: no more cards are revealed and no quest is resolved); 4B reveals one more card for each player in the game (`revealMore`), all by the seat on 4B once stages are separate ("the quest" is each seat's own). The 4B Response (a limit of once a round for each player, at any stage) is offered to every seat but, while a Peril card's staging resolves, only to the revealing seat (`mec109.peril`).

Frodo's Choice (142068:A:2) takes the first player token for its revealer, who cannot lose it: the token does not pass in the refresh phase (`firstPlayerLocked`; `rr.first-player`). Its clauses "take control of the Ring-bearer (and each card attached to it)" and "attach to the Ring-bearer" find none, since stage 2A removed the Ring-bearer from the game (`rr.removed-from-game`); the card stays in the revealer's staging area and so stays in play, which ends 3B's discarding. The token and the empty Ring-bearer clauses are confirmed by the rules spans (`rr.effects`, `rr.removed-from-game`; no online ruling names the card); where the card sits when it cannot attach is an agent recommendation (no ruling found).

"At this stage" (142081:A:1, 142082:A:1, 142070:A:2): the staging area of the seat, the enemies engaged with it and the characters it controls; before stage 2B, every card in play. Archery X on 142070 is the players at its stage (one once stages are separate); 142078 and 142082 add to the archery total of the stage that holds them. 142077 raises the threat of the seat whose area holds it at the end of the travel phase, then moves to the next living seat's area. 142080 and its Shadow move an enemy to the first player's staging area. The Shadows of 142079, 142082 and 142081 compare the defender's printed hit points with the attacking enemy's. 142069 bars allies with fewer printed hit points from defending against it; 142071 gets +2 Attack against a defender with 2 or fewer. The Argonath has Threat X for the players in the game, cannot be traveled to while Sarn Gebir is in play, and skips the combat phase while it is the active location (`skipCombat`). 142073 keeps the players from winning while it is in play, as a card that holds a stage back does (`Restrict advance`). 142074 (Seat of Seeing), once explored, removes a burden card in play, in the encounter deck or in its discard pile from the game and, in Campaign Mode, from the campaign pool.

Fallen Into Evil (142083) attaches to a non-Fellowship hero, the first player choosing among all of them, and the hero becomes an enemy card in the first player's staging area with Threat equal to its Willpower and engagement cost equal to its threat cost; it keeps its attack, defense and hit points and its damage, and its game text is blank while it is an enemy. In the engine it is the same card with the code `<code>~enemy` (`FALLEN_SUFFIX`, `src/rules/core/printed.ts`), which a table draws with the hero's art. A seat left with no hero is eliminated. When Fallen Into Evil leaves play, discarded by a player card too, the hero returns among its owner's heroes; one defeated as an enemy goes to its owner's discard pile. The campaign card's Resolution logs the hero Fallen Into Evil still holds as fallen, and a hero that is no longer among its player's heroes is not offered as a captive.
