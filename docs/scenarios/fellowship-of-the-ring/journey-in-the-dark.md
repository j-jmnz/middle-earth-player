# Journey in the Dark, the Doom countdown and the Balrog

The campaign card (142039) is not in the game: it has a Setup (the burdens, in the campaign record) and a Resolution that reads the final state (below). There is no life number and no burden deck. Damage tokens on Doom, Doom, Doom and resource tokens on the current stage are drawn on their cards; the stage's tokens are also on the top bar (`campaignTokens`, `src/play/readouts/widgets.ts`).

- Stage 1A Setup (142040:A:1): the Balrog, the Great Bridge and Chamber of Mazarbul are set aside by the scenario's piles; Doom, Doom, Doom joins the staging area (an objective: no Threat) and the Setup record places 10 damage tokens on it. "Each player adds 1 different location to the staging area" is a search of the encounter deck, in player order, for a location whose title no earlier search of the Setup found, then the deck is shuffled. The scenario data keeps every copy of the four locations in the encounter deck (`searched`, `data/scenarios/journey-in-the-dark.json`), so a one-player game finds one, and the locations nobody found stay in the deck.
- Doom, Doom, Doom (142043:A:1, A:2): damage on it is a count of its own (tokens, not dealt damage: nothing answers or cancels them). One comes off after each optional engagement (142040:B:1), for each player when stage 3A is revealed, when a Moria Orc's player chooses it, when a Fool of a Took! chooses it, and at the end of every quest phase. At the end of the quest phase the Balrog joins the staging area when none is left, and then, in the card's printed order, attacks if it is in play: it attacks the first player the round it arrives. A token already gone (an earlier effect took the last one) does not bring the Balrog before the end of the quest phase.
- The Balrog (142044:A:1, A:2) stays in the staging area and is considered engaged with the first player (`engagedWithFirst`, `src/rules/families/south/watcher.ts`, as the Watcher in the Water is with every player): it adds no Threat to the staging area, no player engages it (optionally or by an engagement check), it attacks the first player in the combat phase with a shadow card dealt for the attack, and only the first player declares attackers against it (a ranged character of another player may join). "In the staging area" still holds for text that counts enemies there (142041:B:1). An enemy that attacks from the staging area, flagged or not (the Balrog, the Watcher in the Water, Uruk from Mordor, a Storm of Howls attacker), makes the additional attack its shadow card grants ("Attacking enemy makes an additional attack after this one", 142037:A:2, 142047:A:2) against the same player, dealt a shadow card of its own, and a shadow or card line that says "after this attack" runs for it. It is immune to player card effects and so are the shadow cards dealt to it: no Response cancels their effects. It is Indestructible until The Great Bridge takes its keywords away; damage it took stays on it, and it is destroyed as soon as that damage reaches its 25 hit points. A destroyed Balrog goes to the victory display, so "in play" is false for the Resolution; a Balrog never added is not in play either.
- The Great Bridge (142050:A:2): explored, it offers its Response to any player, only while the Balrog is in play (no hero is given up for nothing). The player picks any hero in play; the hero is discarded and the Balrog takes damage equal to that hero's threat cost (an encounter card's damage: the Balrog's immunity does not stop it); the Balrog loses all keywords while it stays in play (`losesKeywords`).
- Stage 2B (142041:B:1 to B:3): at the beginning of the quest phase with no enemy in the staging area the first player reveals one more card in the staging step. One resource token goes on the stage after each enemy is defeated (destroyed by damage). The stage is held back, whatever its progress, until it has 3 tokens for each player (`tokens` with `perPlayer`) and Chamber of Mazarbul is gone (142051:A:2); progress still accumulates (`faq.progress-without-advancing`), and the stage advances at the next quest resolution that finds the gates open. Chamber's Travel (142051:A:3) has each player find a different enemy in the encounter deck or discard pile, the first search taking Orc-chieftain while it is there.
- Stage 3 (142042): 3A adds the Great Bridge to the staging area, then each player in turn reveals a card and removes a damage token from Doom, Doom, Doom. 3B takes at most 5 progress a round (`questProgressCap`; the active location takes its share first) and cannot be defeated while the Great Bridge is in play; defeating it wins the game (`rr.winning-the-game`, nothing to run).
- Mines of Moria (142052:A:1): in the staging area, progress from questing and from card effects goes on it before the current quest, after the active location (`progressFirstRequired`). Its Shadow and Many-pillared Hall's read the defending player's threat (35 or more); "non-objective attachment" leaves out the burden objectives that count as attachments.
- Orc-chieftain (142045): allies cannot defend against its attacks; it engages the first player at the beginning of every encounter phase, from the staging area or from another player. Uruk from Mordor (142047:A:1) attacks the first player as it is revealed, from the staging area; like any enemy that attacks from the staging area it is dealt a shadow card of its own.
- Deep Fissure (142058:A:2): a character the revealing player has committed leaves the quest, the top card of the encounter deck is discarded, and the character is discarded when its printed Willpower is below that card's printed Threat (a treachery has none).
- The burdens (142039:A:2): Overcome by Grief (142087) and Grievous Wound (142088) are dealt to the staging area. After a character is destroyed, Overcome by Grief attaches to a hero the first player chooses; after a hero takes damage and does not die of it, Grievous Wound attaches to that hero. Each attaches while it is in the staging area only, counts as a Condition attachment (not a non-objective one), and is discarded with its hero like any attachment (`rr.attachment-cards`). Overcome by Grief reads "a character you control" as one controlled by the player who controls its hero. Shadow of Fear and Pursued by the Enemy are encounter cards (the latter's attacks are made by each enemy engaged with a player, the Balrog in the staging area included: it is considered engaged with the first player, `rr.engaged`).
- The Resolution (142039:B:1) reads the final state: with the Balrog in play, all four burdens are earned; otherwise the first player chooses one burden treachery and one burden objective. A loss writes nothing.
