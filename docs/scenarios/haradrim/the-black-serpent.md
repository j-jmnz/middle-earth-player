# The Black Serpent

Haradrim part 7 (The Black Serpent, `TBS`), standalone only, on Easy and Normal (Q07.7, E07.7). Encounter sets The Black Serpent and Harad Soldiers (`hrd.setup-the-black-serpent`). The scenario adds Guarded (X) with a list of types (`hrd.guarded-x`, `hrd.guarded-x-example`), Valour (`bts.valour`) and the encounter side quest Don't Let Them Escape! (17104).

- **Setup.** Stage 1A prints "Set each Harad objective aside, out of play. Add The Black Serpent to the staging area. Shuffle the encounter deck. The first player randomly selects 1 set aside Harad objective, reveals it, and adds it to the staging area." (`17106:A:1`). The reader gives an aside of the three Harad objectives (Banner of the Serpent 17091, The Serpent's Garb 17092, Summons from Mordor 17093), a staging clause and the step `revealRandomAsideObjective`.
- **Piles.** The staging pile holds The Black Serpent (17094); the set-aside pile holds the three Harad objectives. No override.
- **Side quest.** Don't Let Them Escape! (The Black Serpent) is in the encounter pile at both difficulties.
- **Guarded (X) (what the engine needs).** Ered Mithrin's Guarded (X) (`emce.guarded-x`, user ruling: with no matching card the objective stays unguarded) and The Wilds of Rhovanion's (`wor.guarded-x`) exist. The cycle prints three forms: Guarded (enemy or location), Guarded (enemy) and Guarded (location); the guard is the first discarded card of a listed type. Each objective has an Action that exhausts it.
- **Errata.** None.

## Data

- Stage 1B, 2B and 3B require the first player to claim any unattached Harad objective and attach it to a hero, and cannot be defeated until each Harad objective in play is attached to a hero. Stage 3A attaches a random objective to The Black Serpent.
- The Black Serpent (17094) cannot leave the staging area or take damage; X is the stage number.

## Readings

The statuses are those of the Readings bullet of `docs/scenarios/haradrim.md`. Each key is the scan-read ledger line (`data/printed/haradrim.json`), `<code>:<side>:<n>`. Sources read: `hrd.guarded-x`, `hrd.guarded-x-example`, https://lotr-lcg-quest-companion.gamersdungeon.net/haradrim-cycle-quest-the-black-serpent.html (a fan site that restates the empty-deck rule), https://visionofthepalantir.com/2019/12/03/the-black-serpent/.

- Guarded (X) (span-settled). On reveal, discard cards from the encounter deck until one of a listed type is discarded; add it to the staging area and attach the objective to it; after that it works as the Guarded keyword. Basis: `hrd.guarded-x`, `hrd.guarded-x-example`.
- Several types (span-settled). "Guarded (enemy or location)" takes the first discarded card of either type; "Guarded (enemy)" and "Guarded (location)" take one of that type. Basis: `hrd.guarded-x-example`.
- No match (user ruling, `docs/scenarios/ered-mithrin.md`). An empty encounter deck while resolving the keyword: the discard pile is shuffled in and the discard continues; if no card of the type exists the objective is unguarded. Basis: that ruling.
- Capital letters (span-settled). Hall of Beorn's text of Summons from Mordor reads "GUarded (location)"; the scan prints "Guarded (location)", as the ledger has it. The Action lines of 17091 to 17093 read the same on the scans as in the ledger. Basis: the scans (`17093:A:1`).
- Attached objectives (span-settled). The Action of each Harad objective is usable by the player who controls it once a hero holds it; while attached to an encounter card the passive lines apply. Basis: the printed lines.
- Engine (read task). `GuardObjective` with `types` (`src/catalog/families/objectives.ts`) already is Guarded (X) for an objective: discard until a listed type, add it to staging, attach it as the guard, no match leaves the objective unguarded. No new design; the three forms are `types` of `["enemy","location"]`, `["enemy"]`, `["location"]`.
- Attached objective passives (span-settled). "While attached to an encounter card" (17091:A:2, 17092:A:2, 17093:A:2) holds while the objective guards or hangs on any encounter card, in staging or elsewhere; 17092:A:2 gives +2 Threat and immunity to player card effects to every attached encounter card (a location too). The Actions are usable by the player who controls the objective (a hero holds it), not while it is guarded (`rr.guarded`). Basis: printed lines, `rr.guarded`, https://visionofthepalantir.com/2019/12/03/the-black-serpent/.
- 17092:A:3 (recommended). "A Harad enemy in the staging area" is one chosen enemy; its Threat cannot go below 0. No source says more.
- 17093:A:2 (researched). While attached, no player's threat is reduced by any effect, the Easy-mode and Fastred reductions included; raising is allowed. 17093:A:3 adds the resource to the pool of the hero it is attached to.
- The Black Serpent (span-settled `hrd.stage-number`). X is the stage number of the main quest. It cannot leave staging (no travel, engage, return) and takes no damage; 17108:B:2 removes only that first sentence. 17094:A:3: at the end of the encounter phase the first player chooses (recommended: the card names the target, not the chooser) to remove X progress (legal only when the main quest holds progress) or the Serpent attacks the first player from staging, dealt a shadow card. 17107:B:2: considered engaged with each player controlling a Harad objective (researched, visionofthepalantir link above).
- Who chooses (recommended, no source). 17096:A:1 and 17097:A:1: the player who played the ally or event. Those effects are "gains" Forced effects of a location in staging: the event is still played, only its effects are cancelled (`faq.cancel-encounter-effect` by analogy).
- Shadows and treacheries (recommended, the research rows). 17101:A:3 detaches the objective from its hero, guards the attacker with it, and the additional attack is dealt a shadow card; 17102:A:1 option two needs a Harad objective on a hero, and the revealed objective resolves its Guarded; 17102:A:2 detaches likewise. 17100:A:3 returns the survivor to staging after the attack. 17100:A:2 caps further progress only; progress above the value stays.
- Stages (span-settled, printed). A set-aside objective is out of play and does not stop stage 1B, 2B or 3B; "each Harad objective the players control" (17098) counts objectives on heroes only. 17108:A:1 attaches a random set-aside objective to The Black Serpent without revealing it (recommended: no Guarded). 17107:A:1 with no set-aside objective left does nothing (recommended).
- Held objectives that lose their hero (recommended, rr.objective-cards: "Objective cards remain in play unless the game instructs the players to remove them"). The Harad objectives print no line for it, so a claimed one whose hero leaves play, or that a card discards, returns to the staging area (`returnsToStagingArea`) and a stage claims it again. No source says more.
- Claiming (span-settled, `rr.guarded`). The stages' claim line (17106:B:1, 17107:B:1, 17108:B:1) claims a Harad objective in the staging area with no encounter card attached, whoever has it there: the first player chooses the hero. An unguarded objective that Guarded (X) found no card for is claimed at once. One that hangs on The Black Serpent (stage 3A) is attached, so it is not claimed until the Serpent is defeated.
- 17107:B:2 engine (researched, visionofthepalantir link above). The Serpent is engaged with each player who controls a Harad objective, read live (`engagedWithClaimers`): it attacks each of them in the combat phase, dealt a shadow card, and only they declare attackers against it; it adds no Threat to the staging area while a player controls a Harad objective (rr.engaged). The attack from its Forced effect (17094:A:3) is unchanged.
- 17101:A:3 and 17102:A:2 (recommended). The attached Harad objective is detached from its hero and the enemy or location becomes its guard; an attacking enemy stays engaged with the defending player while it guards the objective.
- 17102:A:1 (recommended). The first player chooses: his choice of option is the card's "either...or". The detached objective is faceup and unclaimed when it is revealed, so its Guarded (X) resolves.
- 17104:A:1 (span-settled, `hrd.side-quests-in-play`, `rr.in-play-and-out-of-play`). "While any side quest is in the staging area, it functions like a quest card": the Forced effect is active while the side quest is in play, in the staging area, whether or not it is the current quest.
- Actions of 17091 to 17093 (span-settled, printed lines, `rr.action`). An objective's Action is any player's in the rules, but "your threat" and "engaged with you" name the player who controls the objective: only that player (the controller of the hero that holds it) uses it, and only while a hero holds it. 17091:A:3 needs a Harad enemy engaged with that player and 17092:A:3 a Harad enemy in the staging area, so the cost is never paid for nothing (`rr.initiating-abilities-playing-cards`, agent recommendation).
- 17103:A:1 option two (span-settled, `mec108.side-quests-in-play`). "Progress cannot be placed on quest cards" bars the main quest and every side quest: the table holds `noQuestProgress` (the current quest) and `noMainQuestProgress` (the main quest while a side quest is current) until the end of the round.
- 17100:A:2 (printed line). The cap is the quest points printed on the main quest stage (`stageCappedPrinted`), also while a side quest is the current quest; progress above it already there stays.
- 17094 (span-settled, `hrd.stage-number`). X is the stage number of the main quest card, also while a side quest is the current quest.
- 17096:A:1 and 17097:A:1 (printed lines). The ally of 17096 is heard only when played from a hand (a card put into play by an effect is not played). 17097 hears the event when it is played, before its effects resolve, so the cancel option works (`faq.cancel-encounter-effect` by analogy).
- 17096:A:2 (printed line). The first player searches (the Travel effect names him, not the traveller).
