# The Dungeons of Cirith Gurat

Haradrim part 8 (The Dungeons of Cirith Gurat, `DoCG`), standalone only, on Easy and Normal (Q07.8, E07.8). Encounter sets The Dungeons of Cirith Gurat, Mordor Orcs and Harad Territory (`hrd.setup-the-dungeons-of-cirith-gurat`). The scenario adds The Captives of Cirith Gurat (`hrd.captives-of-cirith-gurat`), Valour (`bts.valour`), Abaan and the encounter side quest Captured! (17131).

- **Setup.** Stage 1A prints "Set Uruk Chieftain aside, out of play. Add The Captives of Cirith Gurat and 1 copy of Dungeon Cell to the staging area. Attach Abaan to Dungeon Cell. Each player searches the encounter deck for a different Orc enemy and adds it to the staging area. Shuffle the encounter deck. Starting with the first player, each player discards cards from the top of his deck until an ally that is not in play is discarded and attaches that ally to an unattached enemy in the staging area." (`17132:A:1`). The reader gives an aside, a staging clause, the shared attach step, the search of an Orc enemy for each player, a shuffle and the step `captureAllyFromDeck`.
- **Piles.** The staging pile holds The Captives of Cirith Gurat (17119) and Dungeon Cell (17125); the set-aside pile holds Uruk Chieftain (16061) and Abaan (17120), whom the Setup attaches. DragnCards stages Abaan and four Orc enemies (Jailer of Cirith Gurat 17121, Prison Guard 17122, Uruk of Mordor 16062, Uruk Warg-rider 16063); five overrides put Abaan aside and the four enemies in the encounter pile, because the players find them there.
- **Side quest.** Captured! (The Dungeons of Cirith Gurat) is in the encounter pile at both difficulties.
- **Captured characters (what the engine needs).** While a character is attached to an encounter card, it is a guarded objective with a blank text box: it loses its card type and its player card status; when it is rescued it gets both back. A card in a player's deck is discarded, found and attached as an ally from the deck (`captureAllyFromDeck`); a hero is attached by stage 3A as a captive. The Captives objective discards tokens and attachments from a captured character, returns an objective free of encounters to its owner, exhausted. Abaan: the first player gains control while he is free of encounters; the players lose if he leaves play.
- **Errata.** None.

## Data

- Stage 1B makes enemies and locations that guard an objective immune to player card effects and reveals an encounter card after characters are rescued; stage 2B needs 2 resources and no engaged enemy; stage 3A adds Uruk Chieftain and attaches a hero to an enemy as a captive.
- Prison Guard and Dungeon Cell hold captured characters (Dungeon Cell: +X Threat, X the printed Willpower of the attached objective).

## Readings

The statuses are those of the Readings bullet of `docs/scenarios/haradrim.md`. Each key is the scan-read ledger line (`data/printed/haradrim.json`), `<code>:<side>:<n>`. Sources read: `hrd.captives-of-cirith-gurat`, https://visionofthepalantir.com/2019/11/10/the-dungeons-of-cirith-gurat/ and https://lotr-lcg-quest-companion.gamersdungeon.net/haradrim-cycle-quest-the-dungeons-of-cirith-gurat.html (fan sources, no official ruling on the side quest). User rulings that touch it: the Uruk-hai Captives (`docs/scenarios/the-two-towers/the-uruk-hai.md`), Lost in Mirkwood (`docs/scenarios/ered-mithrin/lost-in-mirkwood.md`).

- Captured characters (span-settled). `hrd.captives-of-cirith-gurat`: a character attached to an encounter card is a guarded objective with a blank text box and loses card type and player card status; rescued, it regains both. Basis: that span.
- Captured! (`17131:A:1`) (recommended). The ally attached to the side quest is the one the player loses control of; when the quest is defeated it returns to its owner's control, exhausted, as a rescued objective, and the threat reduction uses the printed cost. When the side quest leaves play in any other way, each ally returns the same way, with no threat reduction, so none stays under a card that is out of play. Not taken: the ally is discarded. Basis: `hrd.captives-of-cirith-gurat`. Sources: the review above states the same.
- Not eliminated (user ruling, `docs/scenarios/ered-mithrin/lost-in-mirkwood.md`). A player is not eliminated while an enemy holds their only hero; a guard that leaves play returns the hero, exhausted. Basis: that ruling, applied to stage 3A.
- Abaan (`17120:A:1`) (span-settled). "While Abaan is free of encounters, the first player gains control of Abaan"; attached to Dungeon Cell he is guarded by it. Basis: the printed line.
- Captured ally's owner (span-settled). The captured ally keeps its owner. Basis: `hrd.captives-of-cirith-gurat`.
- Dungeon Warg (`17123:A:2`) (researched). "Makes an immediate attack against the player who controls the most allies": an attack outside the combat phase (`rr.attacks-outside-the-combat-phase`, `faq.enemy-attacks-outside-combat`): a shadow card is dealt, the enemy does not engage and stays in the staging area. Players tied on allies (all at 0 too): the first player chooses (recommended, as `EngageSeatWithMost` does). Not taken: it engages the player. Sources: https://visionofthepalantir.com/2019/11/10/the-dungeons-of-cirith-gurat/. Scan read: the ledger line says "immediate attack" and "the most allies".
- Gate of Iron (`17124:A:2`) (span-settled, `rr.then` and the encounter deck rule). "Discard the top card of the encounter deck. Then, ...": an empty encounter deck is refilled by the ordinary rule (the discard pile is shuffled in) and the top card is discarded; the first player's cost choice reads that card's Threat (a card with no printed Threat counts 0, so any card in hand with cost 1 or more pays). Only when nothing can be discarded (deck and discard pile both empty) does the part after "Then" not resolve. Not taken: no discard forces the 3 threat.
- Dungeon Cell (`17125:A:3`) (researched). The Forced line holds while Dungeon Cell is in the staging area and nothing is attached to it: the destroyed ally goes from the discard pile (or from play) to Dungeon Cell and keeps its owner. Scan read: the quoted text says "if there is no objective attached to Dungeon Cell".
- Uruk Guardroom (`17126:A:1`) (recommended). Two copies in the staging area add (cost +2); separate constant effects add. Scan read: "increase the cost to play each ally by 1".
- Mines of Cirith Gurat (`17127:A:1`) (recommended). "A player card would reduce a player's threat by any amount" caps the reduction at 1 for player cards only; encounter-card reductions (Captured!) are not touched.
- Taken Prisoner (`17129:A:1`) (recommended). "Unattached" means no attachment of any kind (a player attachment, a Mordor Warg, a captive); with no ally or no target the card gains doomed 2. No official answer (https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/270882-cirith-gurat-and-unattached-enemy).
- Setup ally (`17132:A:1`) (recommended; no source settles it). "An ally that is not in play": the discard skips an ally only when a unique ally of its title is in play (a non-unique ally is a card not in play); the card in the deck is never itself in play, and only uniqueness makes its title "in play". The ally attached at setup enters play only when rescued: no "enters play" Response fires on a capture or a rescue (https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/268859-an-ally-that-is-not-in-play, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/270785-cirith-gurat-and-captured-allies-with-enters-play-effects: no official answer, community agrees).
- Sneak Past the Guards (`17133:B:2`) (recommended). "No enemies were engaged this round": no enemy engaged a player (no engagement happened) during this round; an enemy still engaged from an earlier round does not count. "Then, place 1 resource" waits for the reveal (`rr.then`): with no encounter card to reveal no resource is placed.
- Fight Your Way Out (`17134:A:1`) (recommended). Uruk Chieftain "cannot have player card attachments" means Attachment cards. A captive hero is attached by the Captives rule as an objective, not as an Attachment card, so the Chieftain is a legal target (the card, `hrd.guarded-heroes` and the forum's argument that the Chieftain could never capture anyone favour this). No official answer: the forum thread names none and its posters disagree. Source read: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/270882-cirith-gurat-and-unattached-enemy. By `rr.then` (span-settled) the engagement checks happen only when each player attached a hero; if any player could not, they do not. The engagement cost of 0 lasts only during those checks. The hero's tokens and attachments are discarded before the attach, also when it cannot be attached.
- Fight Your Way Out (`17134:B:1`) (recommended). An empty encounter deck is reshuffled from the discard pile as the ordinary rule says.
- Captured characters, tokens (span-settled). The Captives line discards tokens and attachments; the Uruk-hai ruling (a Captive keeps them) is for that quest and does not apply here.
- Prison Guard (`17122:A:2`) (recommended). The Forced attaches the ally only while it is still in play: an ally that the attack destroyed lies in its owner's discard pile and is not attached. Basis: "damages an ally"; an ally destroyed was damaged and then left play (`rr.destroy`).
- Captured! (`17131:A:2`) (recommended). The side quest rescues the allies it holds itself when it is defeated, after each player has lowered his threat by the printed cost of his own ally (a Captives rescue on the quest leaving the staging area would free them first and the cost would read 0). A seat that left the game gets its ally in the discard pile.
- Torture Room (`17128:A:1`) (recommended). "Each other copy of that ally" is every other card in play with the title, controlled, held or in the staging area, discarded to its owner's discard pile.

## Table

What the table shows of the pieces this scenario's records create (`docs/agents/agent-brief.md`, Scenario procedure, The Table step). The engine decides each fact; no control types damage, resources, threat, progress or life. The test is `src/play/scenarios/the-dungeons-of-cirith-gurat-table.test.ts`; the sentences are in `src/play/words/scenarios/haradrim/captured-table.ts`, the readouts in `src/play/readouts/haradrim/captured.ts`. Held heroes reuse the guarded-hero display of The Long Arm of Mordor (`src/play/readouts/haradrim/staged.ts`).

- **A captured hero** (`17134:A:1`, `17119:A:1`). Drawn faceup under the enemy that holds it, which wears "1 held"; its preview says who guards it and that it returns, exhausted; its seat's dial says "is guarded by".
- **A captured ally** (`17121:A:1`, `17122:A:2`, `17129:A:1`, `17131:A:1`, `17132:A:1`). Drawn faceup under the enemy, location or side quest that holds it, in every zone the host can be in: the staging area, the engagement area and, for a location, the active location. Its preview names its owner ("Player 1's ally"), its holder and that it returns, exhausted; the host's preview lists what it holds; its owner's dial says "is captured by". Role label: "Captured ally".
- **Abaan** (`17120:A:1`, `17132:A:1`). Drawn under Dungeon Cell the same way, with no owner; his preview says who holds him, that the first player gains control of him when the holder leaves play, and that the players lose if he leaves play. Free of encounters he lies in the first player's play area, as any card that player controls.
- **The strip of a held character.** A hero, ally or Abaan an encounter card holds is drawn under it as a strip 2.6 peeks tall (an attachment keeps one peek), counted in the row's depth, with its name at the full names size and, two-handed, its owner's chip ("P1"); Abaan has no chip. The same strip serves every guarded hero (The Long Arm of Mordor, Lost in Mirkwood). The test is `the-dungeons-of-cirith-gurat-table.test.ts`.
- **Steps.** While The Captives of Cirith Gurat is in play (the table flag `announcesCaptives`) a capture, a rescue and the freeing of Abaan are each a `captive` step: the banner and the log say "Guard of the Citadel is captured by Jailer of Cirith Gurat", "... is rescued: Player 1 takes control of it, exhausted" and "Abaan is free: Player 1 takes control of him". No other game has the step.
- **Dials.** A seat that lost only an ally wears the ally mark and its dial says "Quickbeam is captured by Jailer of Cirith Gurat" in words under the label; the seat that controls Abaan reads "P1 · Abaan", as Derufin's does. The loss when Abaan leaves play says "Abaan was destroyed. If Abaan leaves play, the players lose". The screens say "Every player is eliminated" for the engine's "No seats remain".
- **Captured!** (`17131:A:1`). An encounter side quest in the staging area (the side quest rules); the allies it holds are drawn under it.
- **Choice words** (`src/play/words/asks/the-dungeons-of-cirith-gurat.ts`). Each pick that captures a character carries a "Tap again" warning that names the character and says it loses its tokens and attachments; a pick of a holder names the character and its owner; the Gate of Iron's choice names the discarded card and its Threat, and its threat raise names each player it would eliminate; the search for an Orc enemy shows each enemy's Threat and engagement cost.
