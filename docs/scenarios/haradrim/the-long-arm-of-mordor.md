# The Long Arm of Mordor

Haradrim part 3 (The Sands of Harad, `TSoH`), standalone only, on Easy and Normal (Q07.3, E07.3). Encounter sets The Long Arm of Mordor, Mordor Orcs and Jungle Forest (`hrd.setup-the-long-arm-of-mordor`). The scenario adds the objective-hero card type (`hrd.objective-heroes`, `hrd.objective-hero-elimination`), heroes in the staging area (`hrd.heroes-in-the-staging-area`), guarded heroes (`hrd.guarded-heroes`), the stage number (`hrd.stage-number`) and the encounter side quest Uruks of the Eye (16060).

- **Setup.** Stage 1A prints "Set Uruk Chieftain aside, out of play. Each player adds 1 copy of Sauron's Enforcers to the staging area. Move each hero to the staging area. Each player takes control of an objective-hero and adds 2 resources to its resource pool. Remove each remaining objective-hero from the game. For the remainder of the game, heroes in the staging area are in play but under no player's control, immune to player card effects, and their text boxes are considered to be blank." (`16071:A:1`). The reader gives an aside, a per-player stage, the steps `moveHeroesToStaging`, `takeObjectiveHero` (count of resources 2), `removeFromGame` and `heroesInStagingAreaBlank`.
- **Piles.** The set-aside pile holds the four objective heroes (16031 to 16034) and Uruk Chieftain (16061). The objective heroes had no encounter set and were not dealt; the card normaliser now deals the `Objective_Hero` type of a built product (`scripts/data/normalize/product-cards.ts`). DragnCards sets the four Sauron's Enforcers (16035) aside; an override stages them, one for each player, as the Setup says.
- **Objective heroes (what the engine needs).** Each player takes control of one chosen in turn order from the four; the others leave the game. An objective-hero is a neutral hero with no threat cost that does not count toward the limit of 3; it collects 1 resource in the resource phase. "If X leaves play, you are eliminated from the game" cannot be modified. Heroes in the staging area are under no player's control, immune to player card effects and blank; a player takes control of one by moving it from the staging area. Stage 3A attaches each hero in the staging area to a different Orc enemy as a guarded hero.
- **Side quest.** Uruks of the Eye (Mordor Orcs) is in the encounter pile at both difficulties.
- **Errata.** None.

## Data

- Stage 1B and 2B deal damage of undefended attacks to a hero in the staging area; the first card each player plays each phase needs no resource match. Stage 1B advances on a successful quest; stage 2B returns control of a hero to each player when defeated.
- Stage 4A adds Uruk Chieftain to the staging area; it cannot take non-combat damage and the stage cannot be defeated while it is in play.
- Sauron's Enforcers, Southron Boma and Overrun by Orcs read X as the stage number of the main quest.

### Heroes in the staging area, objective-heroes and guarded heroes

- Heroes in the staging area (span-settled). A hero there is in play under no player's control; if its hit points reach 0 it is destroyed and goes to its owner's discard pile ("owner" is the player who chose it as a starting hero). It collects no resource, exhausts, readies or defends, adds no Threat or Willpower to the staging area or the quest, and its text box is blank for the rest of the game; its sphere, stats and printed threat cost stay. Basis: `hrd.heroes-in-the-staging-area`, `rr.blank`. Engine: the Captives of The Uruk-hai held by no card (src/rules/families/haradrim/staged.ts); the haradrim settle sweep keeps the blank and immune marks on each.
- Objective-heroes (span-settled). The four are dealt aside; each player takes one in turn order from the first player, with 2 resources on it, and the rest leave the game. An objective-hero is a neutral hero with no threat cost that functions as a hero, so it holds the flag `heroType` for as long as it is in play (it is no hero by printed type) and pays a neutral card with its own resources, and no card of a sphere. Basis: `hrd.objective-heroes`. Data: the stats are overrides read from the scans (data/overrides.json).
- 16031:A:2, 16032:A:2, 16033:A:3, 16034:A:3 (span-settled). "If X leaves play, you are eliminated from the game": any way the objective-hero leaves play (destroyed, discarded, removed); the seat that controlled it is eliminated, the other players continue, and all eliminated is a loss. The text cannot be modified or blanked. VOP says "the players lose the game"; the card says "you are eliminated" and wins. Basis: `hrd.objective-hero-elimination`, `rr.player-elimination`.
- Elimination while an enemy holds a player's only other hero (conflict, settled by the user rulings of Lost in Mirkwood and The Uruk-hai). A player is not eliminated while an enemy holds their only hero: a hero in the staging area or a guarded hero keeps its owner in the game (`heldHero`, core/eliminate.ts). Source that disagrees: a forum quote of a FAQ answer on Escape from Dol Guldur, "the player is eliminated" (FFG2), not in `data/rules/`. The ruling applies.
- 16071:A:1 (staged heroes' attachments, recommended). "Move each hero to the staging area" leaves the attachments attached: they stay in play with the hero and keep their own text (blank applies to the hero's text box only), the tokens stay, and the hero returns with them. An attachment another player controls changes control with the hero, to the hero's owner (`rr.ownership-and-control`), however the hero returns: a stage, a guard that leaves play or the Uruk-hai's rescue (`releaseCaptive`, `src/rules/families/captives/index.ts`). This follows the user ruling that captives keep tokens and attachments (docs/scenarios/the-two-towers/the-uruk-hai.md); it differs from Lost in Mirkwood, whose card text discards them. The One Ring on a staged Ring-bearer: its "first player gains control" is a player card effect on an immune hero, so it does not apply while the hero is staged. Not taken: the attachments are discarded. Sources read: VOP and FFG1 (Setup effects only); no official ruling and no source for the One Ring case, so the status stays recommended. Basis: `rr.attachment-cards`, `faq.immune-expanded`.
- 16071:A:1 (order, span-settled). Chieftain aside; one Enforcers copy for each player (the pile); each hero (every hero of every seat) moves to the staging area; each player takes an objective-hero in turn order and puts 2 resources on it; the rest leave the game (`hrd.removed-from-the-game` is for sets, so the heroes are removed). Starting threat stays as `rr.setup` step 2 set it, the staged heroes' costs counting; hero setup effects (Éowyn, The One Ring) resolve at step 2, before this Setup, and stay (FFG1, a single forum answer, not official).
- 16035:A:1 and 16071:A:1 (the unused Enforcers copies, conflict settled by the user ruling). The Setup line says only "Each player adds 1 copy"; the pile stages one for each seat (`data/overrides.json`, 16035) and the copies it does not stage stay in the encounter deck, as for every per-player staging card. No record stages them. DragnCards sets all four aside; the Easy quantity is 0.

### Stage 1 and 2

- 16071:B:1, 16072:B:1 (span-settled). Damage of an undefended attack goes to a hero in the staging area, chosen by the attacked player; any seat's staged hero; the whole attack strength, no Defense subtracted; with no hero in the staging area (or none that can take it) to a hero the player controls (`rr.step-6-4-3`). A staged hero is a hero, so it is not immune to attack damage. Stage 3B has no such line. Basis: VOP ("undefended attacks must be assigned to your captured heroes"). Engine: flag `undefendedToStaged`, hook `combat.undefended`.
- 16071:B:2, 16072:B:2 (recommended). "Does not require a resource match": the cost is paid, from any hero's pool the player controls (an objective-hero included); it applies to the first card each player plays in each phase (events, attachments, allies and player side quests; an effect that puts a card into play is not "playing"); a neutral card or a 0-cost card uses the slot too; the count resets at each phase. Not taken: the card is free. Sources read: VOP, forum threads; no source on it, and the printed text speaks of the match only.
- 16071:B:3 (recommended). Forced after progress is placed on stage 1 from questing successfully (progress the active location absorbs does not count, `faq.legolas-active-location`): each player in turn order chooses a hero he owns in the staging area, takes control of it and exhausts it (a player who owns none picks none); the quest advances to 2A at once, whatever the quest points (the scan prints none, so the stage has no quest points). Not taken: any successful quest triggers it. VOP and Hall of Beorn give card text only.
- 16072:B:3 (span-settled). When stage 2 is defeated (progress equal to 5; `faq.progress-without-advancing` lets progress go beyond) each player takes control of one more hero he owns in the staging area and exhausts it; a hero destroyed in the staging area cannot be chosen. Basis: `hrd.heroes-in-the-staging-area`.

### Stage 3 and 4

- 16073:A:1 (search, span-settled). Each player in turn order searches the encounter deck and the discard pile for an Orc enemy (any Orc not in play, a second copy of a title one player found included: "different" belongs to the attach sentence only, literal; an optional find, `faq.search-effects`) and adds it to the staging area with no When Revealed; the encounter deck is shuffled, the discard pile is not. Basis: `faq.reveal-and-add`.
- 16073:A:1 (attach, recommended). Each hero in the staging area attaches to a different Orc enemy in the staging area (any Orc there); the first player chooses the pairing; each pair is guarded; with fewer Orcs than heroes the extra heroes stay in the staging area unattached (blank, still staged, not guarding). A guarded hero keeps its tokens and attachments. Not taken: the extra heroes are discarded. Basis: `hrd.guarded-heroes`, `rr.guarded`; Hall of Beorn's Free the Captives and VOP give no word on the pairing or a shortage.
- 16073:B:1 (span-settled). While an enemy guards a hero it gets +1 Threat, +1 Attack, +1 Defense and is immune to player card effects (not to attacks, to the stage's progress or to encounter effects). Basis: `faq.immune-expanded`.
- 16073:B:2 (conflict, settled by the user ruling for Lost in Mirkwood). The printed Forced fires when the guard is defeated; the ruling applies to every guard that leaves play in any way (defeated, discarded, a card effect): the hero returns to its owner's control, exhausted, with its attachments (`ReleaseGuardedHero` on the guard's `leftPlay`, the form of 18074:B:2). Source that disagrees: the card text names only "defeated", and `rr.attachment-cards` would discard a hero with its host.
- 16073:B:3 (span-settled). The stage cannot be defeated while an enemy guards a hero; progress still goes on the stage beyond the quest points (`faq.progress-without-advancing`); an unattached extra hero does not block. VOP: "once your hero is free you only need 10 progress".
- 16074:A:1 (span-settled). Uruk Chieftain from the set-aside pile goes to the staging area with no When Revealed; the encounter discard pile is shuffled into the encounter deck; then 1 encounter card is revealed for each player still in the game (eliminated players do not count). Basis: `rr.player-elimination`, `faq.reveal-and-add`.
- 16074:B:1, 16061:A:2 (span-settled). Chieftain takes no non-combat damage (only damage from an attack made by characters is combat damage; Yazan's damage does nothing to it, and the Enforcers' damage does not target enemies); while the player it is engaged with is engaged with another Orc enemy (any trait Orc, unique or not) it takes no damage from any source; in the staging area it has no engaged player and can take combat damage. Basis: `rr.step-6-8-3`, the printed lines.
- 16074:B:2 (span-settled). Each non-unique Orc enemy gets -10 engagement cost (floor 0; stacks with Uruks of the Eye's -5; guards and Orcs with a Warg included).
- 16074:B:3 (span-settled). After Chieftain engages a player (by 16061:A:3 only), it attacks at once, outside the combat phase: it is dealt a shadow card, then the 4 steps of the combat phase attack follow with an action window after each; the shadow card is discarded after (`faq.enemy-attacks-outside-combat`); it attacks again in the combat phase.
- 16074:B:4 (span-settled). The stage cannot be defeated while Chieftain is in play (progress may go on); when it is defeated and the stage is defeated, the players win; defeating the stage with no Chieftain in play ends the game as a win.
- 16061:A:1 (span-settled). Chieftain cannot have player card attachments (an encounter Mordor Warg can attach) and cannot be engaged by an engagement check or an optional engagement; only 16061:A:3 and the stage 4B Forced engage it.
- 16061:A:3 (recommended). At the end of the encounter phase Chieftain engages the player engaged with the most non-unique Orc enemies; a tie: the first player chooses among the tied players; at a highest count of 0 the tied players are all players and it still engages one of them (the first player chooses); already engaged with the chosen player, nothing changes and 16074:B:3 does not fire; another player's, it moves to the chosen player and fires 16074:B:3. Not taken: nobody is engaged when the most is 0, or the same player counts again. Hall of Beorn and VOP give card text only. Engine: `EngageSeatWithMost`.

### Encounter cards

- 16031:A:1 (span-settled). Kahliel's optional Response at the start of the planning phase adds 1 resource to the pool of one hero in play that is not immune (any player's hero, Kahliel included); a hero in the staging area or a guarded hero is immune to player card effects, so it cannot be chosen (`faq.immune-expanded`). The Captive-resource ruling for The Uruk-hai does not carry over: those heroes are not immune.
- 16032:A:1 (span-settled). Firyal's optional Response after she commits to the quest (once for each commit): the player looks at the top encounter card, then may discard it; an empty deck: nothing.
- 16033:A:1, 16034:A:1 (span-settled). Ranged and Sentinel are real keywords for the objective-hero only while a player controls it; a hero in the staging area has a blank text box (`rr.blank`).
- 16033:A:2 (recommended). Yazan's optional Response after he is declared as an attacker (a ranged attacker too): 1 damage to a non-unique enemy in play (staging area or engaged, any player's), once per phase (`rr.limits`). An enemy that guards a hero is immune to player card effects, and Uruk Chieftain is unique, so neither can be chosen. An objective-hero is a hero and so a player card. Not taken: Yazan is not a player card, so a guarding enemy can be chosen. VOP says an objective-hero counts as a normal hero for practically all purposes; no source states that its effects are player card effects.
- 16034:A:2 (span-settled). Jubayr's optional Response after he is declared as a defender, before the shadow cards are revealed (the Response comes in the action window after `rr.step-6-4-1`; the reveal is 6.4.2): the enemy is any non-unique enemy with a facedown shadow card that is not immune (a guarding enemy is immune); once per phase.
- 16035:A:1, 16036:A:1, 16039:A:1, 16039:A:2 (span-settled). X is the stage number of the main quest, the number in the upper left of the active main quest card (1 to 4; a current side quest changes nothing): Sauron's Enforcers' Threat, Attack and Defense, Southron Boma's Threat, and Overrun by Orcs' X (+2 times the stage number of total Threat in the staging area until the end of the phase, counted at the quest phase if revealed in it, lapsing at the phase's end otherwise, two copies stacking; Shadow: the defending player assigns the stage number of damage among characters he controls, never a staged hero). Basis: `hrd.stage-number`, `faq.side-quest-current-quest-text`.
- 16035:A:2 (recommended). Each Enforcers copy in play has its own Forced effect at the end of the round; the first player assigns the X damage among all characters in play, any number on each: heroes in the staging area and guarded heroes (damage is no player card effect), allies, objective-heroes, any player's. A staged hero at 0 hit points is destroyed and goes to its owner's discard pile; a destroyed objective-hero eliminates its controller. Not taken: each player assigns the damage to the characters he controls, or staged heroes excluded. VOP says only "will assign you damage each turn equal to the stage number"; no source says who splits the damage; the first player has the final say on simultaneous effects.
- 16036:A:2 (recommended). Travel: each player pays 1 resource from the pool of a hero they control (an objective-hero included; no resource match, since the stage text applies to cards played, not to a travel cost); if any player cannot pay, the players cannot travel here; an eliminated player does not pay. Not taken: the first player pays. No source settles which pool or who pays; the line says "each player".
- 16036:A:3 (span-settled). Shadow: if the defending character is an objective-hero (defended attacks only; an undefended attack has no defender) it is readied after it exhausted to defend, and all damage from the attack is cancelled (the enemy deals none). Not for staged heroes. VOP: "Southron Boma will ready that hero".
- 16037:A:1 (span-settled). Response (optional, first player): after the players travel here the first player discards the top card of his deck; an ally enters play under his control, exhausted (no cost, not played); a non-ally: nothing more; an empty deck: nothing.
- 16037:A:2, 16057:A:1, 16058:A:1 (span-settled). Discarding the top card of the encounter deck is no reveal: an enemy (Village Hut, Forest Clearing) or a location (Dense Jungle) is added to the staging area with no When Revealed or Surge; the Forced effect of an explored location resolves before the location leaves play; an empty encounter deck: nothing (`faq.reveal-and-add`, `faq.last-encounter-card`).
- 16038:A:1 (recommended). Each player chooses in turn order, one at a time: discard their highest-cost ally (a tie: the player's choice, `rr.the-word-choose`) or reveal the top encounter card (staged as `faq.reveal-and-add`); a player with no ally must reveal; an ally with no cost counts as the lowest. Not taken: the first player decides for all. No source on ties or on a player with no ally.
- 16038:A:2 (span-settled). Shadow: +1 Attack; if the attack destroys a character (the defender, or a hero an undefended attack lands on, a staged hero too), the defending player reveals the top encounter card. Precedent: Thaurdir in docs/scenarios/angmar-awakened/deadmens-dike.md, and `rr.step-6-4-3`.
- 16056:A:1 (span-settled). While Jungle Trail is active, each location in the staging area has -1 Threat (the card itself is not in the staging area); Threat cannot go below 0; two effects stack. Basis: `rr.active-location`, `rr.modifiers`.
- 16057:A:2 (span-settled). Shadow: the defending player discards an attachment he controls, his choice (The One Ring included); none: nothing. Precedent: Umbar Street in docs/scenarios/haradrim/escape-from-umbar.md.
- 16058:A:2 (span-settled). Shadow: the defending player discards a random card from his hand; an empty hand: nothing.
- 16059:A:2 (recommended). Overgrown attaches to the location in the staging area with the highest current Threat (modifiers such as Jungle Trail included, `rr.modifiers`) that has no Overgrown; a tie: the first player chooses; with no eligible location it is discarded without effect. The attachment stays on a location that becomes active (it is immune to player card effects: `faq.legolas-immune-location`). Not taken: printed Threat only, or the card stays in play. VOP: "increases the threat of the location that already had the highest in the staging area and makes it immune"; it names no tie rule or empty case.
- 16060:A:2, 16060:A:3 (span-settled). Uruks of the Eye is an encounter side quest; revealed, it goes to the staging area (`hrd.encounter-side-quests`) and its constant text is active while it is there, current quest or not (`hrd.side-quests-in-play`, `faq.side-quest-current-quest-text`). Orc enemies in the staging area (guards, Uruk Chieftain, Mordor Warg hosts) get -5 engagement cost (floor 0); an Orc engaged with a player gets +1 Threat. Surge as printed. Quest points 5, victory 10 (override from the scan).
- 16062:A:1 (span-settled). The ally's current Willpower is compared with the current, modified Threat of Uruk of Mordor (+1 guarding, +1 engaged with Uruks of the Eye); an ally with less cannot be declared a defender against it; heroes are never barred. Engine: flag `defendersBelowThreat`, hook `combat.defense`.
- 16062:A:2, 16065:A:2 (span-settled). Shadow: +1 Attack, or +2 instead when the attacker's current Threat is greater than the defending character's Willpower; an undefended attack has no defending character, so +1 and no additional attack (the new attack of 16065:A:2 is dealt a new shadow card, `faq.additional-attacks`). Precedent: Cornered Orc in docs/scenarios/angmar-awakened/the-weather-hills.md, and docs/scenarios/angmar-awakened/across-the-ettenmoors.md. Engine: condition `threatAboveWillpower`.
- 16063:A:1 (recommended). +X Attack while it attacks a character whose Willpower is below its Threat, X the difference, read once the defender is declared; an undefended attack has no defending character, so +0 (the hero that takes the damage afterward is not "attacked"). Not taken: the bonus applies to the hero that takes the damage. No source (VOP, Hall of Beorn, forums) on this card; the attack strength is fixed when the defender is declared, before undefended damage is assigned. Engine: amount `willpowerGap`.
- 16064:A:1 (recommended). When Revealed: the first player chooses an Orc enemy in play (staging area or engaged, not already carrying a Mordor Warg; a guard and Uruk Chieftain included) and attaches Mordor Warg; that enemy returns to the staging area (a guard stays guarding its hero: returning is not leaving play; engaged enemies lose the engagement); with no valid Orc, the Warg is discarded. The Warg lies on its host as an attachment that is an enemy (`AttachEnemyToPicked`, the Wraith of Carn Dûm's mechanism): a Mount attachment with +2 Threat, Attack, Defense and hit points, and when its host leaves play in any way the engine returns it to the staging area as an enemy. Not taken: the card stays in play unattached. Hall of Beorn's Mordor Warg and VOP give no ruling; no official answer on a Warg with no Orc.
- 16065:A:1 (span-settled). Each ally in play (any player's) gets -1 Willpower, -1 Attack and -1 Defense until the end of the round; stats stop at 0; an ally that enters play later that round gets it too (`faq.lasting-effects`: a lasting effect of an encounter card is recalculated when the game state changes; `rr.lasting-effects`, encounter or quest card).
