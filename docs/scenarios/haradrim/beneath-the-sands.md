# Beneath the Sands

Haradrim part 6 (Beneath the Sands, `BtS`), standalone only, on Easy and Normal (Q07.6, E07.6). Encounter sets Beneath the Sands, Desert Creatures and Harad Territory (`hrd.setup-beneath-the-sands`). The scenario adds the double-sided Search objective, Off Track and On Track (`hrd.search-objective`), the Track X keyword (`hrd.track`, `hrd.track-example`), Valour (`bts.valour`) and the encounter side quest Lost Underground (17075).

- **Setup.** Stage 1A prints "Set Brood Mother aside, out of play. Add the Search objective to the staging area (Off Track face up)." (`17076:A:1`). The reader gives an aside and a staging clause naming Off Track.
- **Piles.** The set-aside pile holds Brood Mother (17065). The staging pile holds the Search objective as one placement, Off Track (17079) faceup with On Track (17080) as its `back`. DragnCards files the one card with On Track as side A; two `dragnCardCodes` rows (the OCTGN guid finds side A, an override names side B) and a rule in `scripts/data/normalize/scenarios.ts` (a two-sided card the Setup stages is dealt on the side the Setup names) give Off Track as the faceup side.
- **Track X (what the engine needs).** On a location with Track X: after the players travel there while Off Track, discard X cards from the encounter deck (shuffle the discard pile in when it runs out); if one has "On Track" in its lower right corner, flip the Search objective to On Track and discard its resources. While On Track is in play many cards read it. While Lost Underground is in play the objective cannot flip and Track does not resolve at all (no card is discarded).
- **Side quest.** Lost Underground (Beneath the Sands) is in the encounter pile at both difficulties; while it is in play the objective cannot flip.
- **Errata.** None. Off Track prints "place 1 resource here" where Hall of Beorn says "progress"; the ledger follows the scan (misprints keep the source line).

## Data

- Track is in the Hall of Beorn keyword tokens ("Track 2", "Track 3", "Track 4"); the build lists them among the unknown keyword tokens and `data/cards` holds no `keywords` for them. The ledger lines "Track X." are owned by a record of the haradrim family, not by the keyword reader.
- The On Track marker ("On Track" in the lower right corner, `hrd.track`) is on no data field. Read from the scans: Hive Guardian 17066, Spider Broodling 17067, Pitch Black Tunnel 17069, Nest of Vermin 17070 and Blocked Passage 17072 print it; Spider Burrow, Cobwebbed Cavern, Forced Off Track, Grabbed by Spiders, Lost Underground and the Desert Creatures and Harad Territory cards print none.
- Stage 1B discards cards until X locations are discarded and stages them; stage 2A flips Off Track and stages X enemies; stage 3A adds Brood Mother.
- Quest 17078 is "The Spiders' Hive", as printed on the card and spelled by Hall of Beorn. DragnCards spells it "The Spider's Hive"; no source of `data/cards` reads that spelling.

## Table

What the table shows of the pieces this scenario's records create (`docs/agents/agent-brief.md`, Scenario procedure, The Table step). The engine decides each number; no control types damage, resources, threat, progress or life. The test is `src/play/scenarios/beneath-the-sands-table.test.ts`; the choice sentences are in `src/play/words/asks/beneath-the-sands.ts`; the screen moments are in `test/scenarios/beneath-the-sands/screens.ts`. No tracker, dial or held card is new, so the scenario adds no widget and no readout file.

- **The Search objective** (`17076:A:1`, `17079:A:1`, `17080:A:1`, `hrd.track`). One card in the staging area. A flip changes the code the card holds (`FlipSearchObjective`), so the table shows the face by the card's own picture and name, Off Track or On Track, with no side marker. Its resources (Off Track's Forced effect places them, the flip to On Track discards them) are the card's resource tokens, which every card already draws. No Track dial: the Track values are printed on the locations.
- **Grabbed by Spiders** (`17074:A:1`). It attaches to the main quest card; the table draws every card attached to a quest stage as one counted peek below the stage card (`placeCounted` in `src/play/layout/layout.ts`), whether or not the table stacks what lies under a card. The peek is no taller than half of what a staging card is taller than the sideways stage card by, and a tap on it opens the attachment. Other scenarios' attachments on a quest stage (a Condition on the main quest) are drawn the same way.
- **Brood Mother** (`17076:A:1`, `17068:A:3`, `17078:A:1`). She starts set aside and returns there when Spider Burrow leaves play explored. The set-aside panel lists her (`scripts/checks/set-aside-sites.ts` names the verb that sets her aside); no zone is added for her.
- **Setup records that create nothing new.** The One Ring's Setup (`141085:setup:1`) attaches a card to a hero, which the table already draws.
- **The owner on each row of a pick that spans seats.** The picks are the defending player's own (Cobwebbed Cavern, Blocked Passage, Grabbed by Spiders) or the first player's (Forced Off Track, Off Track); the asks entries name the card and the number; the shadow picks say "you control".
- **Track and the flips** (`hrd.track`, `hrd.search-objective`). The engine pushes a `track` step when Track resolves (the location, the discarded cards, those that print On Track, whether the objective flipped) and a `searchFlip` step for each flip that happens. The banner tells them as a beat with the discarded cards in the look strip, as Discover's (`words/scenarios/haradrim/track.ts`). The card's picture changes with no turn: the table's motion keys on a slot's code, which Day and Night, the Grotto, Gundabad's stages and the Uncharted deck change in place too.
- **The choices' words** (`17073:A:1`, `17079:A:1`). Forced Off Track's flip row says what the flip does now (it flips from On Track, or changes nothing when Off Track is up or Lost Underground blocks it) and asks for a second tap naming Grabbed by Spiders' discard and stage 3's progress loss. Off Track's rows name the location that returns and the raise (the resources it counts), and warn when the raise takes the first player, who chooses, to 50; another player's threat is not read by a caution.

## Readings

The statuses are those of the Readings bullet of `docs/scenarios/haradrim.md`. Each key is the scan-read ledger line (`data/printed/haradrim.json`), `<code>:<side>:<n>`. Sources read: `hrd.search-objective`, `hrd.track`, https://lotr-lcg-quest-companion.gamersdungeon.net/haradrim-cycle-quest-beneath-the-sands.html, https://secondhandtook.wordpress.com/2017/05/30/first-impressions-beneath-the-sands/ (player reviews; no official ruling on the travel step).

- Track (span-settled). The keyword resolves after the players travel, only while Off Track; X is the active location's Track value; the encounter discard pile is shuffled into the deck when the deck is empty and the discard continues. Basis: `hrd.track`.
- Off Track and progress (span-settled). While Off Track is in play, "Progress cannot be placed on the main quest." (17079:A:2); On Track lets them. Basis: `hrd.search-objective`.
- Lost Underground (`17075:A:1`) (span-settled). "Flip the Search objective to Off Track" on reveal; the objective cannot flip while the side quest is in play; Track is not resolved. Basis: the printed line.
- Track and the quest phase (span-settled). The Forced effect of On Track at the end of the quest phase searches the top 3 cards; the Track keyword is not resolved while On Track. Not taken: Track resolves in both states. Basis: `hrd.track` ("if they are off track"). Sources: the review above states the same; no official ruling.
- Flip resources (span-settled). Flipping from Off Track to On Track discards all resources on the objective. Basis: `hrd.track`.
- Valour (span-settled). `bts.valour`.

Scan-read (span-settled):

- On Track marker. The five codes above; the family holds them as a list, cited to the scans. Basis: the scans of 17066 to 17075 and 16045 to 16055.
- Pitch Black Tunnel (`17069:A:1`, `17069:A:2`) (span-settled). The scan prints Threat X in the threat box and "Track 4." in the text; "X is the number of characters controlled by the first player" gives the Threat, not the Track value. Sources: the scan, https://hallofbeorn.com/LotR/Details/Pitch-Black-Tunnel-BtS.

Researched or recommended (no FFG or designer ruling and no agreed forum answer exists; play reports read: https://visionofthepalantir.com/2020/07/28/beneath-the-sands/, https://visionofthepalantir.com/2025/08/27/qotw-beneath-the-sands, https://cardtalk2018.com/category/scenario/beneath-the-sands/, https://masteroflore.wordpress.com/2018/06/26/quest-profile-081-beneath-the-sands/, https://hallofbeorn.com/LotR/Details/Pitch-Black-Tunnel-BtS):

- `17065:A:2` (recommended). Brood Mother moves to the first player's play area at once when it enters the staging area, by any add. Not taken: it waits for the engagement step.
- `17065:A:3` (recommended). Search the deck and the discard pile for one Spider Broodling; none found adds nothing; the deck is shuffled in every case; the discard pile is not shuffled in.
- `17067:A:2` (span-settled). If On Track is in play when the shadow resolves, Broodling goes into play engaged with the defending player and is dealt a shadow card (printed). It has not attacked this round and is still able to, so it is eligible and attacks later in the same combat phase (`rr.step-6-4a`). Not taken: it makes no attack that phase (no source says so; an FFG designer's answer relayed on the FFG forum says an enemy that engages during the enemy attacks attacks: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/93529-do-new-enemies-engaging-during-combat-also-attack; the FAQ, the Rules Reference and Hall of Beorn's rulings (https://hallofbeorn.com/LotR/Details/Spider-Broodling-BtS, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/77099-immediate-attacks-and-the-combat-phase) say nothing against it).
- `17068:A:2` (recommended). The flip happens only if Off Track; Brood Mother is added in every case, also when Lost Underground blocks the flip.
- `17068:A:3` (recommended). Brood Mother is set aside from any zone unless the main quest is stage 3B.
- `17070:A:2` (recommended). The discard pile is shuffled in even if the deck is not empty; no Creature found when the deck is empty stops the search and adds nothing.
- `17073:A:1` (recommended). The first player chooses; the flip option is legal when already Off Track and then does nothing.
- `17074:A:2` (recommended). A second copy is not attached and is discarded (Limit 1 per quest); the Forced effect runs after a flip to Off Track (not after a flip to On Track): each player discards an ally of their choice, then the card is discarded. The card is discarded only when each player had an ally to discard (`rr.then`: the "then" part resolves only when the part before it resolved in full); if a player controls no ally it stays attached and fires again at the next flip to Off Track. No ruling covers the no-ally case; agent recommendation, no source.
- `17075:A:1`, `17075:A:2` (recommended for the response). While Lost Underground is in play the Track keyword does not resolve at all (no card is discarded), per its printed "(Do not resolve the Track keyword.)". The reveal flip is allowed; while Lost Underground is in play the objective cannot flip in either direction. The Response resolves while it is still in play and is not blocked by its own text; On Track then ignores its Forced effects until the end of the phase, and that includes the end of the phase itself: On Track's "At the end of the quest phase" Forced (`17080:A:1`) does not fire in the phase Lost Underground was defeated (`rr.lasting-effects` does not give the window; "until the end of the phase" holds through the phase's end step). The engine keeps the lasting effects that end with a phase or round while it collects that moment's Forced effects (`lapsedModifiers`). Source: cardtalk2018 (a play report).
- `17076:B:1` (recommended). The deck running out stops the discard (no shuffle: only Track shuffles). `17077:A:1`: the discard pile is shuffled in first, then the discard stops if the deck runs out.
- `17078:A:1` (recommended). The first player adds Brood Mother, then each other player reveals one encounter card, then the flip to Off Track.
- `17079:A:1` (recommended). With no location in the encounter discard pile only the threat option is legal.
- `17080:A:1` (recommended). The first card with the marker among the top 3 is added to the staging area (it is not revealed); the rest are discarded; none found flips the objective to Off Track; fewer than 3 cards are looked at without a shuffle.
- `17080:A:2` (span-settled). Unsuccessful means threat higher than willpower; a tie is not (`rr.step-3-4`).
- Other lines read as printed (span-settled): 17065:A:1 (the text returns when the stage falls below 5 progress), 17066, 17067:A:1, 17071, 17072, 17073:A:2 (`faq.additional-attacks`), 17077:B:1, 17078:B:1 to B:3, 17079:A:2 ("Progress cannot be placed on the main quest." reads "the main quest" as the main quest card only: a side quest that is the current quest still takes progress).
