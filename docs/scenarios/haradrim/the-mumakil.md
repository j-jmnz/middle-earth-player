# The Mûmakil

Haradrim part 4 (The Mûmakil, `M`), standalone only, on Easy and Normal (Q07.4, E07.4). Encounter sets The Mûmakil and Jungle Forest (`hrd.setup-the-mumakil`; Jungle Forest is in The Sands of Harad and shared with The Long Arm of Mordor). The scenario adds the Capture objectives and the encounter side quest Guardians of the Jungle (17025).

- **Setup.** Stage 1A prints "Set each copy of Wild Mûmak and each Capture objective aside, out of play. Each player adds 1 different location to the staging area. Shuffle the encounter deck." (`17026:A:1`). The reader gives one aside clause naming the four Wild Mûmak (17015) and the four Capture objectives (Horse-hair Lasso 17011, Poisoned Spear 17012, Pit Trap 17013, Noose of Vines 17014; trait Capture) and `eachPlayerAddsLocation`.
- **Piles.** The set-aside pile holds Wild Mûmak and the four Capture objectives. DragnCards sets aside one copy each of Mûmak Trail (17020), Jungle Trail (16056), Forest Clearing (16057) and Dense Jungle (16058); four overrides stage them as locations the Setup finds in the encounter deck.
- **Stage 2A name.** DragnCards spells it "Capture the Mumakil", Hall of Beorn and the scan "Capture the Oliphaunts" (`17027`); the collector number maps the row (`dragnCardCodes`).
- **Explored note.** Territorial Ape's shadow (`17017:A:2`) reads "a location was explored this round": the Setup record sets the table flag `noteExplored` (`ModifyStat`, until the end of the game) and `destroyCard` then notes each explored location, active or staged, as `exploredThisRound` until the end of the round (`src/rules/families/haradrim/explored.ts`); a game without the flag notes nothing.
- **Side quest.** Guardians of the Jungle (The Mûmakil) is in the encounter pile at both difficulties.
- **Capture objectives (what the engine needs).** Stage 2A attaches one random set-aside Capture objective for each player to a Wild Mûmak in play, in player order; each is an objective attached to the Mûmak (it lies attached, controlled by nobody); each Capture has its own Response that adds it and the Mûmak to the victory display; stage 2B cannot be defeated while a Capture objective is in play. Wild Mûmak is shuffled into the encounter deck by stage 1B each time the active location is explored.
- **Errata.** Dwarf Pipe (17007) has an erratum in Hall of Beorn's data; the FAQ lists none, so no span exists. The ledger line follows the scan.

## Data

- Wild Mûmak has Indestructible, is immune to player card effects and takes no more than 3 damage a round.
- Stage 1B cannot be advanced unless the number of Wild Mûmak in play equals the number of players.

## Readings

The statuses are those of the Readings bullet of `docs/scenarios/haradrim.md`. Each key is the scan-read ledger line (`data/printed/haradrim.json`), `<code>:<side>:<n>`. Sources read: the Mûmakil rulesheet (`hrd.setup-the-mumakil`, no rule of its own beyond the Setup), the Sands of Harad insert, ALeP = https://alongextendedparty.com/semi-official-errata-faq-1-10/ (developer answers, each item with its source), VotP = https://visionofthepalantir.com/2019/03/07/the-mumakil/ (a play article, no rules), Forum = https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/227249-the-m%C3%BBmakil/page/5 (members only). A line not listed here is span-settled (its basis is the printed line and the rule span it names).

- Stage 1B advance gate (`17026:B:2`) (span-settled, conflict with a ruling). Taken: the printed "equal to": the players cannot advance unless the number of Wild Mûmak in play is exactly the number of players; progress still lands. A Wild Mûmak in the staging area or engaged counts; one set aside, in the deck, the discard pile or the victory display does not (`rr.in-play-and-out-of-play`, `rr.cannot`). Not taken: "equal to or greater than", which ALeP item 3.398 (22 Jan 2026, ruling of 25 Feb 2023, Caleb Grace, paraphrased) gives as the intent; the printed card wins over a ruling (`.claude/rules/authority.md`, Open points). The Forum (Seastan) also thought "equal or more" the intent, no staff answer.
- Different location (`17026:A:1`) (span-settled for "title", recommended for the pick). Each player in player order picks 1 location from the encounter deck; "different" is a title; found cards are not revealed; the deck is shuffled after (user ruling 7, `docs/scenarios/ered-mithrin/roam-across-rhovanion.md`; `docs/scenarios/haradrim/escape-from-umbar.md`, reading 33). Not taken: a random location. Why recommended: VotP says each player "adds one different location" and advises a player to start with Mûmak Trail; no official answer on who picks.
- Attaching a Capture (`17027:A:1`) (recommended). Each player in player order attaches the next random set-aside Capture objective to a Wild Mûmak in play of his choice; two Captures may share a Mûmak; a Mûmak may have none (`rr.attachment-cards`: no attachment limit). Not taken: one Capture on each Mûmak. Why recommended: Forum (donkler) asked and got no answer; ALeP has no entry; https://www.hallofbeorn.com/LotR/Details/Capture-the-Oliphaunts-TMk prints text only; VotP describes one each at the exact-equal count and states no rule.
- Capture objectives (`17011` to `17014`) (span-settled unless listed). Each is an objective attached to a Wild Mûmak; its Response is optional and is offered to the player the Mûmak is engaged with (recommended, agent recommendation: an objective no player controls has no controller to trigger it, `rr.response` does not say who is offered it, and the effect names the engaged player as the one who acts, so the engine offers it to that player; the Spear keeps `answeredBy: eventSeat`). A Mûmak in the staging area has no engaged player, so the Response is not offered and does nothing. The Capture and the Mûmak go to the victory display together (Victory 1 and 3); the Mûmak's remaining hit points are read before the Capture leaves play, and a second Capture on the Mûmak goes to the encounter discard pile with it. The Capture and the Mûmak go to the victory display (Victory 1 and 3).
- Horse-hair Lasso (`17011:A:1`) (researched). The discarded card's printed cost counts, an X or no cost as 0 (ALeP 3.062, 3.070; `docs/scenarios/angmar-awakened/the-dread-realm.md`, reading 7); an empty deck discards nothing and adds nothing (ALeP 3.037); damage may pass 9 over several rounds because the Mûmak is Indestructible (ALeP 3.056), so the remaining hit points may reach 0. Damage the cap removes is not dealt, so it does not trigger. Not taken: an X cost as its X value.
- Poisoned Spear (`17012:A:1`) (recommended). An empty encounter deck is refilled from the encounter discard pile, then the top card is discarded; Threat is the printed Threat (treachery, objective and side quest count 0). Not taken: an empty deck discards nothing. Why recommended: ALeP 3.083 covers a reveal in the quest phase, not a discard; no ruling on a discard.
- Noose of Vines (`17014:A:1`) (recommended). The defender's remaining hit points are read after the attack resolves; a defender the attack destroyed no longer qualifies. Not taken: a destroyed defender counts as 0 or less. Why recommended: ALeP 3.010 (the attack resolves at the end of its last step) gives the timing only; no source names a destroyed defender.
- A second Capture on a Mûmak that leaves play (recommended). The Capture leaves play with its host and goes to the encounter discard pile, so it no longer blocks stage 2B. Not taken: it stays in play. Why recommended: `rr.attachment-cards` and `rr.objective-cards` name no pile for an objective; no ruling.
- Wild Mûmak end of round (`17015:A:3`) (researched). The engaged player chooses; an immediate attack follows every attack step with its action windows, the Mûmak is dealt a new shadow card (ALeP 3.196, 3.380, 3.102) and stays engaged. Not taken: no shadow card.
- Harad Tiger additional attack (`17016:A:3`) (researched). The additional attack deals a new shadow card and discards the old one (ALeP 3.149, 3.385).
- Strangling Python (`17018:A:2`) (recommended). After it deals 1 or more damage to a character it attaches to that character as a Condition and is no longer an enemy; the host cannot ready; a character the damage destroys cannot take it, and the Python stays engaged. Not taken: the Python stays an enemy. Why recommended: ALeP 3.182 only; no entry on an enemy that attaches itself.
- Giant Centipede (`17019:A:1`) (researched). The character's printed text box is blank until the end of the round; text gained from another card is not blanked (`rr.blank`; ALeP 3.184). Not taken: gained text blanked too.
- Mûmak Trail (`17020:A:1`) (span-settled by `rr.response`, user decision 2026-11-26). Its gained Response ("When") resolves before stage 1B's Forced ("After") for the same exploration, so the Trail finds only a Mûmak already in the encounter deck or discard pile; the deck is shuffled even when none is found. The engine resolves every Forced effect before every Response, so the record sets `whenFirst: true` (`src/catalog/effect-schema.ts`): the Response is offered before the Forced effects of its event (`src/rules/windows/events.ts`); no other record sets it. Not taken: the Forced first, so the Trail finds the Mûmak 1B just shuffled in. VotP and its author's comment of 28 Sep 2021 (https://visionofthepalantir.com/2020/03/27/the-mumakil/) say both orders; ALeP 3.293 and 3.308 order Forced before Response for the same trigger only.
- Stampeding Oliphaunt (`17021:A:2`) (researched for the attack, recommended for "no attacks"). The first player chooses a Wild Mûmak in the staging area; it attacks the first player from the staging area, is dealt a new shadow card and stays in the staging area (ALeP 3.277, 3.196, 3.380). "If no attacks are made" holds when no Mûmak is in the staging area; a declared attack counts as made even if its damage is cancelled. Not taken: the Trail is also found after a cancelled attack. Why recommended: https://visionofthepalantir.com/2025/08/11/qotw-the-mumakil gives the no-Mûmak case; no answer on a cancelled attack.
- The Savage South (`17022:A:1`) (recommended). Each player discards 5 cards, then discards each card he controls (a hero too) that shares a title with one of the 5 he just discarded from his own deck. Not taken: the titles of every player's cards. Why recommended: ALeP 3.182 gives "controls" only; no source says whether a hero is a card he controls here.
- Terrible Fever (`17023:A:1`) (recommended). The first player chooses a hero committed to the quest, of any player; it is removed from the quest and the Fever attaches to it; with no committed hero the Fever goes to the encounter discard pile. Not taken: any hero in play. Why recommended: no entry on "remove a hero from the quest"; ALeP 3.266 for the chooser.
- Biting Insects (`17024`) (researched). A player cannot assign more than a character's remaining hit points while a hit point remains (ALeP 3.132); X is the printed cost, an X or no cost counts 0; an empty hand deals none.
- Guardians of the Jungle (`17025:A:2`) (researched). The Forced fires each time the first player chooses it as the current quest (it is current only until the end of the phase); it does not fire when revealed (`hrd.current-quest`; https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/312011-the-road-the-fortress-of-nurn). Its +1 Threat applies while it is in the staging area, to every location including the active one (`hrd.side-quests-in-play`).
- Side quest (span-settled). `hrd.side-quests-in-play`.
