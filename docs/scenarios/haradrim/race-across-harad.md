# Race Across Harad

Haradrim part 5 (Race Across Harad, `RAH`), standalone only, on Easy and Normal (Q07.5, E07.5). Encounter sets Race Across Harad, Desert Sands and Mordor Orcs (`hrd.setup-race-across-harad`). The scenario adds the Orcs' area with a second quest deck (`hrd.prepare-the-orcs-area`, `hrd.orcs-area`, `hrd.orcs-area-different-stage`, `hrd.orcs-area-same-stage`), the Tamed Mûmak objectives and Doomed X on player cards (`hrd.doomed-x-player-cards`).

- **Setup.** Stage 1A prints "Set River Harnen and 1 copy of Uruk Warg-rider aside, out of play. Each player takes control of a Tamed Mûmak. Remove each remaining Tamed Mûmak from the game. Create the Orcs' area with a second quest deck consisting of stage 1C, 2C, 3C, and 4C. Place Uruk Chieftain in the Orcs' area." (`17047:A:1`, read from the scan: Hall of Beorn's text says "Ford of Harnen" and puts Uruk Chieftain in the quest deck sentence). The reader gives an aside, the step `takeControl` for the players, `removeFromGame` and the step `createOrcsArea` (the four C stages, then Uruk Chieftain).
- **Piles.** The set-aside pile holds River Harnen (17039), Uruk Warg-rider (16063), the four Tamed Mûmak (17038), Uruk Chieftain (16061) and the Orcs' quest deck, stages 1C to 4C (17051 to 17054). The quest pile holds the players' stages 1A to 4A (17047 to 17050). DragnCards' second quest deck (`sharedQuestDeck2`) maps to the set-aside pile (`groups` in `scripts/data/cycle-defs.ts`), and its rows, which carry the guid of the stage of the same title, are named by collector number (`dragnCardCodes`). A quest card in the set-aside pile is not a stage of the players' deck (`scripts/data/normalize/scenarios.ts`). DragnCards also sets aside one copy of five locations (Expanse of Harad, Blistering Erg, Harad Road, Towering Dunes, Desert Oasis); five overrides put them in the encounter pile, because stage 1B searches the encounter deck for locations.
- **The Orcs' area (what the engine needs).** A second quest deck of four stages whose C and D sides are in play; a play area for cards (enemies, locations) beside the staging area. The Orcs' stage gets progress at the end of each quest phase; it advances through its C and D sides. While the Orcs and the players are at different stages, the cards in the Orcs' area are immune to player card effects, cannot leave it and are not in the staging area; at the same stage they are. A table widget for the area is the scenario author's.
- **How the engine holds it.** The area is the zone `orcsArea` and the optional state field `orcs` (the haradrim family, `src/rules/families/haradrim/orcs-*.ts`): the Orcs' current stage, its number, the stages left in its deck and the members. It is not the Stormcaller's area and sets no `state.sea`. A settle hook stands the members in the area zone while the players' main quest stage number differs from the Orcs' and in the staging area when it matches. The progress, the C then D advance and the loss of a defeated stage are in `orcs-stage.ts`; a stage that loses when defeated carries a record that hears `orcsStageDefeated`, which the engine pushes itself.
- **Tamed Mûmak.** Each player takes control of one (objective ally); "If Tamed Mûmak leaves play, the controlling player is eliminated" (`17038:A:3`).
- **Errata.** None.

## Data

- Stages 2B to 4B cap the progress placed each round at 5 (10 with every Tamed Mûmak committed). Stage 4B wins the game; stage 2D, 3D and 4D lose it when defeated at the wrong stage or by the Orcs.
- Racing Warg attaches to an Orc enemy; Uruk Pursuer, Pursuers from Mordor and Harad Road are added to the Orcs' area.

### Setup and Tamed Mûmak

Sources read for the setup and tamed mûmak readings: https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/07/09/race-across-harad/, https://visionofthepalantir.com/2025/08/20/qotw-race-across-harad, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/243779-habemus-race-across-harad-spoilers/page/4, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/253804-race-across-harad-orcs-area-quest-timing-issue-twice-in-some-rounds, https://alongextendedparty.com/semi-official-errata-faq-1-10/ and https://hallofbeorn.com/LotR/Details/Racing-North-C-RaH; none rules on the readings below.

- `17047:A:1` (span-settled). Setup runs as printed: River Harnen and 1 Uruk Warg-rider go aside; each player takes control of one Tamed Mûmak (one per seat), the other Mûmakil leave the game; stages 1C to 4C form the Orcs' quest deck (1C on top, played as 1D); Uruk Chieftain goes in the Orcs' area (the ledger line, not Hall of Beorn's sentence). Not taken: Hall of Beorn's order. Basis: printed line, `hrd.prepare-the-orcs-area`, `hrd.orcs-area`. Sources: VOP.
- `17047:A:1 / 17038:A:1` (span-settled). The Tamed Mûmak is an objective-ally: an ally for every "each ally" and "character" effect (it can be committed, exhausted, chosen as a defender), and a character its controller controls. Not taken: A pure objective that is no ally. Basis: `ats.ally-objectives`, `dwd.ally-objective-arwen` ("use as any ally he controls"), data type objective with Willpower 2, Attack 5, Defense 2, hit points 8. Sources: VOP (the Mûmak defends and quests).
- `17038:A:1` (span-settled). Immune to player card effects: no player card can target, heal, move or change it; encounter effects still reach it. So Heat Exhaustion, Desert Oasis (exhaust, and its heal of 4 may go to it), Blistering Erg, Sandstorm (damage) and Fear of Mordor (-1 each ally) may all pick or hit it; only Desert Oasis can heal it. Not taken: Encounter effects may not pick it. Basis: `hrd.immune-to-player-card-effects`, `faq.immune-expanded`, `rr.immune`. Sources: VOP, QOTW (Desert Oasis is the only heal).
- `17038:A:2` (span-settled). All damage of an undefended attack against its controller goes to the Mûmak, even past its 8 hit points (it is then destroyed); the player assigns none to a hero. Not taken: The controller may split it. Basis: printed line ("must be assigned"); engine precedent `undefendedToStaged`. Sources: VOP (it takes all undefended attacks).
- `17038:A:2` (span-settled). Only undefended attacks are redirected: Archery, direct damage, shadow damage and a defended attack's damage are not; an attack the Mûmak itself defends against is a defended attack. Not taken: All damage. Basis: printed line ("undefended attacks").
- `17038:A:3` (span-settled). Any way the Mûmak leaves play (destroyed, discarded, removed) eliminates its controller; the other players go on and reveal one card fewer; all players eliminated is a loss. An eliminated player's Mûmak leaves with them and is not counted for "each Tamed Mûmak in play". An enemy holding a hero does not change this. Not taken: The players lose when one Mûmak leaves. Basis: `rr.player-elimination`, `hrd.objective-hero-elimination`, docs/scenarios/haradrim/the-long-arm-of-mordor.md (16031:A:2 row); ALEP 3.276 (the players lose only when the text says so; this card says "controlling player is eliminated"). Sources: ALEP.
- `17038:A:3` (recommended). An attack that destroys the Mûmak triggers both elimination and any "destroys a character" progress (Pursuers from Mordor's shadow, stage 4B's Forced): the Orcs' stage still gets its progress; the first player orders the two. Not taken: No progress once the player is eliminated. Basis: `rr.first-player` (simultaneous triggers) for the order; no source for the progress after elimination. No source settles it: VOP, QOTW, F4, ALEP: none treats a destroyed Mûmak and the progress trigger. The trigger "after an enemy attacks and destroys a character" is met before the elimination resolves..
- `16062:A:1, 16063:A:1, 16065:A:1 (with a Mûmak)` (span-settled). Uruk of Mordor's bar uses the Mûmak's current Willpower (2, less with Fear of Mordor): a Mûmak below the Uruk's Threat cannot defend against it; Warg-rider's +X reads a defended attack only (an undefended attack has no defending character, so +0). Not taken: Warg-rider adds X when the Mûmak takes the undefended damage. Basis: docs/scenarios/haradrim/the-long-arm-of-mordor.md (16062:A:1 span-settled, 16063:A:1 recommended).
