# Desert Crossing

Haradrim part 2 (The Sands of Harad, `TSoH`), standalone only, on Easy and Normal (Q07.2, E07.2). Encounter sets Desert Crossing, Desert Creatures and Desert Sands (`hrd.setup-desert-crossing`). The scenario adds the heat tracker and the temperature (`hrd.heat-tracker`) and the encounter side quests Seek Shade (16023), Find Water (16048) and Craft Remedy (16053).

- **Setup.** Stage 1A prints "Each player searches the encounter deck for a copy of Burnt Sands and adds it to the staging area. Shuffle the encounter deck. Place the heat tracker next to the quest deck and set the temperature to 10." (`16068:A:1`). The Setup reader gives a per-player staging clause (one copy of Burnt Sands, 16025, for each player, of the four in the set) and the step `setTemperature` with count 10.
- **Piles.** The staging pile holds Burnt Sands. No override.
- **Side quests.** Seek Shade, Find Water and Craft Remedy are in the encounter pile, one copy each, at both difficulties.
- **Heat tracker (what the engine needs).** A counter next to the quest deck, shown as a threat tracker: the temperature, set to 10 at Setup, increased by quest stages and cards, reduced by Seek Shade, with the players lost at 60 or more (`16068:B:3`, `16069:B:2`, `16070:B:3`). Cards read its tens digit (X). It is not a time counter: the Ring-maker's time code is not used.
- **Errata.** None.

## Data

- The three stages each increase the temperature by 4 at the end of the round; stage 2B and 3B deal or add its tens digit as X. Stage 1B: each ally entering play is exhausted or takes 1 damage.
- Were-worm, Scorched Hamada and Unbearable Heat read X as the tens digit; Burnt Sands gets +2 Threat at 40 or more.

- **The temperature** (`16068:A:1`, `16068:B:3`, `16069:B:2`, `16070:B:3`, the `temperature` table modifier). A top-bar dial "Heat" with the value and the limit 60 (`heatDials`, the form of the pursuit dial: `warms`, `limit`). It warms near the limit at 48 and is critical from 54, where its warning says how far the limit is; its note says the tens digit the cards read as X. It shows from Setup (the tracker is set) and in no other scenario. The value is read from the record's own state (`temperatureOf`).
- **Craft Remedy** (`16053:A:1`, `blankText`). No mark showed a blanked character, so a damaged character whose text box is blank wears the word pill "Blank" (the pill of a determined character, `DeterminedMarkView`; a round "B" where the word does not fit), and its preview says the text box is blank while it has damage (`blankMark`).
- **Find Water** (`16048:A:1`, `cannotReadyByPlayerCard`). No mark showed a bar on readying by player card effects. The bar reaches every character, so a pill on each would crowd the table: the preview of each character says that player card effects cannot ready it (`noReadyLine`); the side quest's own printed line is on its card.
- **Setup records that create nothing new.** The campaign card's Setup (`141085:setup:1`, the first player claims The One Ring) adds a card attached to a hero, which the table already draws. Stage 1A's Setup (`16068:setup:1`) sets the temperature (the dial); the Burnt Sands it adds go to the staging area, which the existing zones draw.
- **The owner on each row of a pick that spans seats.** The picks are each player's own (Unbearable Heat, Sandstorm, Heat Exhaustion, Find Water, Desert Oasis, Beasts of Legend) or the controller's (stage 1B's ally); the asks entries name the card, the number and the player (`src/play/words/asks/desert-crossing.ts`).

### Temperature and stages

- Setup (`16068:A:1`) (span-settled; `hrd.heat-tracker`, `hrd.setup-desert-crossing`). Each player adds one Burnt Sands from the encounter deck to the staging area, the deck is shuffled, the tracker is set to 10.
- Ally enters play (`16068:B:1`) (recommended). The ally's controller chooses: exhaust it, or deal 1 damage to it; it fires for each ally of any player that enters play while stage 1B is current. Not taken: the first player chooses. Why recommended: the card has no "you" and `rr.forced` only orders abilities; no source names the chooser; the controller bears the cost.
- End of round (`16068:B:2`, `16069:B:1`, `16070:B:2`) (span-settled; `faq.triggering-forced-effects`). The temperature rises by 4; a stage reached in the same end of round does not fire its Forced that round.
- Loss (`16068:B:3`, `16069:B:2`, `16070:B:3`) (span-settled; `hrd.heat-tracker`, "reaches 60"). The players lose as soon as the temperature is 60 or higher, at the moment it reaches 60 (a +2 or +4 effect, a Burnt Sands Forced); Seek Shade cannot undo it afterwards. Not taken: a check only at the end of the round. The engine queues `temperatureChanged` on every change and each stage's Forced answers it.
- A stage's own When Revealed (`16069:A:1`, `16070:A:1`) (recommended). A stage that raises the temperature to 60 or higher in its own When Revealed loses the game as soon as the value is reached. Not taken: no loss until the next round. Why recommended: the sources do not treat a stage that raises the temperature to 60 itself; the B-side line is constant.
- Under the Merciless Sun (`16069:A:1`) (span-settled). +2, then the encounter discard pile is shuffled into the deck (even with cards in it) and one encounter card per player is revealed, one at a time, as normal reveals (Surge and When Revealed apply).
- Under the Merciless Sun damage (`16069:B:1`) (span-settled). The +4 comes first, then each player assigns X damage among characters he controls, X the tens digit read after the rise ("Then"); a +4 that reaches 60 loses before the damage.
- Beasts of Legend (`16070:A:1`) (span-settled). +2; each player in turn finds a Were-worm in the encounter deck, discard pile or victory display and adds it to the staging area (a found card leaves its pile; not revealed, no shadow card); a player with none left adds none; the deck is shuffled. Not taken: counting a Were-worm already in play (`rr.in-play-and-out-of-play`).
- Beasts of Legend threat (`16070:B:1`) (span-settled). In the quest phase X (tens digit, read live) is added to the total Threat in the staging area, not to one card.
- Beasts of Legend win (`16070:B:3`) (span-settled; `rr.cannot`). While at least 1 Were-worm is in play (staging or engaged) the stage cannot be defeated; progress is still placed, and once the last Were-worm leaves play the stage is defeated at once (the reading of `docs/scenarios/shadows-of-mirkwood/return-to-mirkwood.md`). Defeating it wins the game.
- Order of end-of-round Forced effects (span-settled; `rr.forced`). The first player chooses the order; Seek Shade reads the temperature when it resolves.

### Temperature at 0

- Seek Shade (`16023:A:3`) (recommended). The temperature falls by 10 with a floor of 0; at 0 to 9 it becomes 0, never negative; X is then 0. Not taken: a negative temperature. Basis: `rr.modifiers` ("if a value is below 0, it is treated as 0"), `rr.threat-threat-dial`, `hrd.heat-tracker` (a threat tracker). Why recommended: no source rules on the heat tracker at 0; the spans cover a card value and a threat dial, not this tracker.

### Encounter cards

- Mirage (`16022:A:1`) (recommended). The active location is discarded (a discard, not an explore); the whole discard pile (that location included) is shuffled into the deck; cards are discarded from the top until a location is; that location becomes the active location, so its "after it becomes the active location" Forced fires (a copy of the same location too); no Travel effect resolves; with no active location the first step does nothing; with no location in the deck nothing becomes active. Not taken: the new location's Forced does not fire. Why recommended: https://visionofthepalantir.com/2019/08/17/desert-crossing/ says the Forced fires again and that it triggers no "explored" effect, but it is one fan blog and the spans name no non-travel activation; the "no location found" case has no source.
- Seek Shade (`16023:A:2`) (span-settled; `hrd.side-quests`, `faq.side-quest-current-quest-text`). Forced in the staging area: at the end of the round X progress is removed from the main quest (the quest deck's top card, never the active location or a side quest); X = 0 below 10.
- Carrion Bird (`16024:A:1`, `16024:A:2`) (span-settled). Engagement cost 40 less 1 for each character in play with damage (heroes and allies of every player), live, never below 0; after it engages you, you discard one damaged character you control of your choice, a hero included; none damaged: no effect.
- Burnt Sands (`16025:A:1`, `16025:A:2`) (span-settled). +2 Threat while the temperature is 40 or higher, live, only in the staging area; after it becomes the active location by travel or by Mirage the temperature rises by 2.
- Scorched Hamada (`16026:A:1`, `16026:A:2`) (recommended). X is the tens digit read when the Forced resolves (the printed Threat X is a constant ModifyStat); X damage is assigned among characters in play (any player's), all of it assigned; the first player decides when the players disagree. Not taken: each player assigns X among his own. Why recommended: https://visionofthepalantir.com/2019/08/17/desert-crossing/ says the damage "can be spread across the group"; no source names who decides; `hrd.archery` is an analogy only.
- Scorched Hamada shadow (`16026:A:3`) (span-settled). The defending player raises his threat by X, read when the shadow resolves.
- Parched Wadi (`16027:A:1`, `16027:A:2`) (span-settled; `hrd.guarded-x`). While in the staging area each Creature enemy in play (staging or engaged, any player) gets +1 Threat and +1 Attack; two copies stack. After it becomes the active location a Creature enemy is found as Guarded does and added to the staging area (not revealed: no When Revealed, no Surge).
- Parched Wadi with no Creature in the deck (`16027:A:2`) (recommended). The whole deck is discarded, nothing is added, and the discard pile is not shuffled in again. Not taken: shuffling again and continuing. Why recommended: no source covers a deck with no Creature; a second shuffle would loop.
- Were-worm (`16028:A:1`, `16028:A:2`, `16028:A:3`) (span-settled). Threat, Attack and Defense are each X, the tens digit read live (printed X, held as no value in the data, so constant ModifyStat records give them); engagement cost 22; after it engages you by any engagement it makes one immediate attack with no shadow card, and it also attacks in the combat phase. Shadow: +1 Attack to the attacking enemy, +2 instead (not +3) at 40 or higher.
- Unbearable Heat (`16029:A:1`, `16029:A:2`) (span-settled). Each player assigns X damage among characters he controls, X read when it resolves; shadow: +X Attack, X read when the shadow resolves.
- Scorching Sun (`16030:A:2`, `16030:A:3`) (span-settled). +4 when revealed, then Surge; 60 or higher loses at once. Shadow: the defending player chooses +2 temperature, or the attacking enemy returns to the staging area after this attack.
- Find Water (`16048:A:1`, `16048:A:2`) (span-settled; `rr.cannot`, `faq.side-quest-current-quest-text`, `hrd.side-quests-in-play`). Active while in the staging area, current quest or not: a player card effect cannot ready a character; the refresh phase and encounter card effects still ready. When defeated each player readies 1 hero he controls (an encounter card effect, so its own line does not stop it).
- Towering Dunes (`16049:A:1`, `16049:A:2`) (span-settled). While in the staging area no progress is placed on any other staging location, from any source (a second Towering Dunes is exempt); after it becomes the active location (by Mirage too) the top card of the encounter deck is revealed.
- Desert Oasis (`16050:A:1`) (span-settled; the division is recommended). The printed line says "heal 4 damage", with no "up to": while active it gains the Response that, when explored, heals 4 damage in total, so the players cannot stop while a damaged character remains and less than 4 is healed (`HealAmong` with `exact`). The 4 is divided among characters in play (any player's); the first player decides when the players disagree. Not taken: 4 on one character only. Why recommended for the division: https://visionofthepalantir.com/2019/08/17/desert-crossing/ says it "heals 4 damage from characters in play"; no source says how 4 is divided or who decides.
- Desert Oasis exhaust (`16050:A:2`) (span-settled). Each player exhausts one ready character he controls; none ready: nothing.
- Sandstorm (`16051:A:1`) (span-settled). Each player chooses: discard his hand, or assign X damage among his characters, X the cards in his hand when he chooses (0 cards: either costs nothing).
- Sandstorm shadow (`16051:A:2`) (recommended). The defending player chooses: discard his highest-cost attachment (a tie: his choice), or assign X damage among his characters, X the attachments he controls. Why recommended: no source on ties, on a cost of "-" or on The One Ring; the player's own choice needs no extra rule.
- Heat Exhaustion (`16052:A:1`) (recommended). Each player exhausts a ready character he controls and deals 1 damage to that same character; none ready: no effect, no damage. Not taken: an exhausted character may be chosen and still take the damage. Why recommended: https://visionofthepalantir.com/2019/08/17/desert-crossing/ says "if no characters are ready, the treachery has no effect"; `rr.exhausted` names no damage case.
- Heat Exhaustion shadow (`16052:A:2`) (span-settled; `rr.exhausted`). The defending player exhausts a ready character he controls; none: nothing.
- Craft Remedy (`16053:A:1`, `16053:A:2`) (span-settled; `rr.blank`, `faq.lasting-effects`). Each damaged character (any player's, heroes and allies) has its printed text box blank, Traits kept, read live: healed, it is no longer blank. When defeated, 1 damage is healed from each character in play.
- Sand Viper (`16054:A:1`) (span-settled; `rr.cannot`). No player can declare a defender against it, for every attack it makes; the attacked player takes the damage on a hero of his choice.
- Sand Viper engages (`16054:A:2`) (recommended). After the players travel (not after Mirage) it engages the first player from the staging area; engaged with another player it moves to the first player; no immediate attack. Not taken: it only engages from the staging area. Why recommended: https://visionofthepalantir.com/2019/08/17/desert-crossing/ says only "when players travel to a new location, it engages the first player"; no source covers a Sand Viper engaged with another player.
- Giant Scorpion (`16055:A:1`) (recommended). After it deals damage to a character (an undefended attack included) that character cannot ready until the end of the round, which holds through the refresh phase ready step. Not taken: the effect ends before the refresh phase. Why recommended: no source says when "end of the round" falls against step 7.2; it is the only reading that gives the line an effect.
- Giant Scorpion shadow (`16055:A:2`) (span-settled). If this attack deals damage the defending character cannot ready until the end of the round; an undefended attack has no defending character: no effect.
