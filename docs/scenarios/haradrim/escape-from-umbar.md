# Escape from Umbar

Haradrim part 1 (The Sands of Harad, `TSoH`), standalone only, on Easy and Normal (Q07.1, E07.1). Encounter sets Escape from Umbar, Harad Soldiers and Harad Territory (`hrd.setup-escape-from-umbar`). The scenario adds the Archery X keyword (`hrd.archery`) and the encounter side quests Southron Champion (16040) and In Enemy Territory (16045) (`hrd.side-quests`, `hrd.encounter-side-quests`).

- **Setup.** Stage 1A prints "Add "Seize them!" to the staging area. Each player adds 1 different location to the staging area and puts 1 copy of Umbar Sentry into play engaged with him. Shuffle the encounter deck." (`16066:A:1`). The Setup reader gives it a staging clause, `eachPlayerAddsLocation` and the step `putCopyEngagedEachPlayer`. The card's title has straight quotes in the data and curly ones on the quest card; the reader names it with both.
- **Piles.** The staging pile holds "Seize Them!" (16014) and one copy of each of the five Umbar locations (16016 to 16020), which the Setup finds in the encounter deck (`searched`, so they are dealt with the encounter pile and the encounter count includes them). No override moves a pile.
- **Counts.** Hall of Beorn lists 2 Enemy Pursuit (16021), 5 Umbar Sentry (16015) and 1 Umbar Street (16017) at Easy; DragnCards loads 1, 4 and 2. Three `scenarioCounts` rows keep Hall of Beorn's Normal counts for the first two and the DragnCards Easy count of Umbar Street (staged copy only).
- **Side quests.** Southron Champion (Harad Soldiers) and In Enemy Territory (Harad Territory) are in the encounter pile, one copy each, at both difficulties. Their set was `null` in `data/cards`; the scenario files of Hall of Beorn give it, and the card normaliser reads it from there.
- **Errata.** None.

## Data

- Stage 2A has "When Revealed: End the current phase." (`16067:A:1`). Stage 1B and 2B cannot hold more than 15 progress tokens, and neither can be defeated while a player is engaged with an enemy.
- "Seize Them!" is lost to the Forced effect that removes progress from the main quest when an enemy destroys a character; the players lose when the main quest has no progress at the end of a round it has been in play for the whole of.
- Umbar Sentry and Southron Archer print Archery; Archery is a keyword the data does not hold, the records name it by the line.

## Table

What the table shows of the pieces this scenario's records create (`docs/agents/agent-brief.md`, Scenario procedure, The Table step). The engine decides each number; no control types damage, resources, threat, progress or life. The test is `src/play/scenarios/escape-from-umbar-table.test.ts`; the sentences are in `src/play/words/scenarios/haradrim/umbar-table.ts`, the readouts in `src/play/readouts/haradrim/umbar.ts`.

- **Enemy faceup under Narrow Alleyway** (`16016:A:1`, `PlaceUnderSelfFaceup`). The enemy is drawn under its host like an attachment, faceup, in every zone the host can be in (the active location, the staging area): `entriesOf` adds `underAlleyway` to the host's `attached` list (`src/play/store/selectors.ts`). The existing peek drawing (`placeUnder`) and the host's "N held" pill (`UnderMarkView`, as for the cards under The Havens Burn) cover the rest; a long press on the location lists the enemy as a full card with the line that says where it lies and when it returns. A new piece: the drawing is the existing one, only the list is new.
- **The Champion** (`16040:A:1`). The enemy that holds the resource token while Southron Champion is in the staging area wears the word pill "Champion" at the top of its card (the pill of the current quest and of a determined character, `DeterminedMarkView`), in staging and engaged; its preview and the reader say what it gets and that it takes no damage until the side quest holds 4 progress. The resource token itself is drawn by the existing token art.
- **Hand limit of 5** (`16045:A:2`, flag `handLimit`). A readout on each seat's threat dial, "Hand 4/5" (a seat mark, `umbarHands`, the form of The Road to Isengard's hand mark), with its note spoken after the dial. It warms (near) from one card short of the limit and at it, and is critical over it, where it says the player chooses the cards to discard. It shows only while In Enemy Territory holds the flag.
- **The 15-progress cap of stages 1B and 2B** (`16066:B:1`, `16067:B:1`, flag `stageCapped`). Covered by the existing quest strip: the stage's progress against its 15 quest points ("n / 15"), which the cap holds at 15; and by the existing stage hold line (`advanceHold`), which shows the printed line "cannot be defeated while any player is engaged with an enemy" (`16066:B:2`, `16067:B:3`) while a player is engaged. No new widget.
- **The "Seize Them!" loss** (`16014:A:2`). A pill on the card, "Lose at 0" (the ship-loss pill, `ShipLossView`), threat red when the main quest has no progress and has been in play the whole round (`stageInPlayWholeRound`), so the players see the loss coming; the main quest's progress is on its own strip. Its preview and the reader say the rule.
- **Setup records that create nothing new.** The campaign card's Setup (`141085:setup:1`, the first player claims The One Ring) adds a card attached to a hero, which the table already draws; Stranded in Umbar's Setup (`16066:setup:1`) adds the "Seize Them!" card and locations to the staging area and engages the Umbar Sentries, all drawn by the existing zones.
- **The owner on each row of a pick that spans seats.** The picks of this scenario are the first player's (Narrow Alleyway, Southron Soldier's Shadow) or each player's own (Injured Companion, Enfeebled, Southron Archer); the asks entries name the card, the number and the player (`src/play/words/asks/escape-from-umbar.ts`).

## Readings

The statuses are those of the Readings bullet of `docs/scenarios/haradrim.md`. Each key is the scan-read ledger line (`data/printed/haradrim.json`), `<code>:<side>:<n>`. Sources read: the Sands of Harad insert (`hrd.archery`, `hrd.side-quests`), https://warriorsofthewestblog.wordpress.com/2016/12/12/turn-by-turn-report-escape-from-umbar/ and https://visionofthepalantir.com/2019/09/08/escape-from-umbar/ (play reports, not rules authorities), https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/240425-a-few-escape-from-umbar-questions-and-one-from-return-to-mirkwood and https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/261306-put-into-play-engaged-vs-when-engaged (community discussion).

- Setup (`16066:A:1`) (span-settled). Each player puts 1 Umbar Sentry, the card printed on the quest card, into play engaged with him. The fan blog under "Achievements" at https://visionofthepalantir.com/2025/07/20/qotw-escape-from-umbar/ gives Southron Archer; it names no source and no FFG erratum exists (`data/rules/` lists none). The card wins. Setup runs after the opening hand and the mulligan (`rr.setup`). "1 different location" is a title, not a card, as in Roam Across Rhovanion (user ruling 7) (recommended).
- Archery X (`16015:A:1`, `16043:A:1`) (span-settled). The cumulative archery damage is dealt to characters in play at the beginning of each combat phase and may be split among the players; the first player decides a disagreement; [defense] does not block it. Basis: `hrd.archery`.
- Side quests (`16040:A:1`, `16045:A:1`) (span-settled). An encounter side quest revealed from the encounter deck goes to the staging area; its "When Revealed" effect cannot be canceled by player card effects; it functions like a quest card and is added to the victory display when defeated, with no advance to the next stage. Progress goes on it only while it is the current quest. Basis: `hrd.encounter-side-quests`, `hrd.side-quests-in-play`, `hrd.current-quest`, `faq.side-quest-current-quest-text`. The quest points (4 and 8) are read from the scans.
- "End the current phase" (`16067:A:1`) (recommended). Stage 2A is revealed when 1B is defeated at the quest resolution, so the phase ends once the whole "When Revealed" effect has finished (shuffle, each player discards until an enemy and engages it), and the game goes to the travel phase; "at the end of the phase" effects still occur. The existing verb `EndPhase` (angmar family) does it. Not taken: the phase finishes its current step first. Sources read, none rules on it: https://hallofbeorn.com/LotR/Details/Flee-the-City-TSoH, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/224887-the-sands-of-harad/page/6, /page/10 and /page/12, the two play reports; the FAQ and the Rules Reference say nothing.
- "Seize Them!" loss (`16014:A:2`) (recommended). "In play the entire round" counts from the round the main quest card entered play: stage 1A is in play from Setup, so the check applies in round 1; a stage revealed in the middle of a round (the advance to 2A) is not in play the entire round, so no check in that round. Not taken: the check runs in the round the stage is revealed (a new stage has 0 progress, so every advance would lose the game). Basis: the printed line; https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/224887-the-sands-of-harad/page/12 (a player lost in round 1 with no progress) and https://visionofthepalantir.com/2019/09/08/escape-from-umbar/ ("the check only happens at the end of the round"). No source covers a mid-round advance. The haradrim family notes the round the stage is first seen (`stageEntered`, condition `stageInPlayWholeRound`).
- "Seize Them!" damage (`16014:A:1`) (span-settled). X is the attacking enemy's current Threat with every modifier (Southron Captain, the Champion's +2); the progress comes off the main quest only (never a side quest or the active location) and the main quest holds no fewer than 0. An archery hit is no enemy attack. Basis: `rr.modifiers`, `hrd.side-quests`.
- Umbar Sentry (`16015:A:2`) (recommended). The player the Sentry attacks makes the choice: remove 1 progress, or +2 Attack for this attack. Not taken: the first player chooses. No source names the chooser; `rr.first-player` gives the first player the final say only for a target among several, which this is not. The thread at https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/240425-a-few-escape-from-umbar-questions-and-one-from-return-to-mirkwood has no post on it.
- Either/or with one option (`16015:A:2`, `16021:A:1`, `16042:A:1`, `16043:A:2`) (recommended). With one option that can be done (no progress to remove, no ally to discard), that option is forced. Not taken: the player may choose the impossible option and do nothing. Source: one unchallenged member answer in the thread above (community only).
- Narrow Alleyway (`16016:A:1`) (span-settled). The Response is optional (`rr.response`); the first player picks (`rr.first-player`) a non-unique enemy engaged with any player, a Southron Champion included (the Alleyway is an encounter card, so "Immune to player card effects" does not bar it; `faq.immune-expanded`); the enemy goes faceup under the location and is out of play with its attachments, so it is not engaged and does not count for "a player is engaged with an enemy" (`rr.in-play-and-out-of-play`, `faq.saruman-out-of-play-guarded`). The core verb `PlaceUnderSelf` leaves the card facedown, so the haradrim verb `PlaceUnderSelfFaceup` turns it up: a facedown card under a location is hidden from the table, and every player reads this one.
- Narrow Alleyway, the Champion under it (`16016:A:1`) (recommended). The Champion keeps its name while out of play; its +2 Threat, Attack and Defense and its gained text apply only while it is in play and return with it; the stage's "cannot be defeated while the Champion is in play" lifts. The Champion is the enemy that holds the resource token the side quest placed, and a card that leaves play loses its tokens, so the engine reads the Champion as gone after it returns from under the Alleyway: its stat bonuses return, the stage locks do not. Not taken: the bonuses stay on while it is under the location. Basis: `rr.in-play-and-out-of-play`; https://visionofthepalantir.com/2025/07/20/qotw-escape-from-umbar/ (opinion, no ruling).
- Narrow Alleyway leaves play (`16016:A:2`) (span-settled). The enemy under it is put into play engaged with the first player, as "put into play engaged", which triggers "engages you" effects. A forum post says "back in the staging area"; the printed line wins.
- Engages you, put into play engaged (`16042:A:1`, `16043:A:2`) (recommended). "After Southron Soldier engages you" triggers however the Soldier comes to the player, a put into play engaged included (Flee the City, Nowhere to Hide, the Alleyway's return, a Shadow effect). Not taken: a Soldier put into play engaged does not trigger. Sources: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/261306-put-into-play-engaged-vs-when-engaged (members agree; no staff) and the thread above. Community only.
- Umbar Street (`16017:A:1`) (span-settled). While active, each enemy, staged and engaged, has its engagement cost lowered by 1 for each progress token on the main quest, recalculated on each change; a cost below 0 compares as 0. Basis: `rr.modifiers`, `rr.engagement-check`.
- Umbar Street Shadow (`16017:A:2`) (span-settled). The defending player discards an attachment he controls, his choice (`rr.the-word-choose`); with none nothing happens. Copied from Battle Warg.
- Umbar Square (`16018:A:1`) (span-settled for the Response, recommended for the main quest). The Response is optional, for any enemy defeated by any means (`faq.legolas-active-location`: "bypassing the active location"). The line names the main quest, so the progress goes on the main quest even while a side quest is the current quest. Not taken: the progress goes to the current side quest (`hrd.current-quest`). Basis: `hrd.current-quest`; https://hallofbeorn.com/LotR/Details/Southron-Champion-TSoH (no ruling). `AddStageProgress` puts it on the main quest card itself, past the active location and any current side quest.
- Umbar Square travel (`16018:A:2`) (span-settled). The cost reveals the top card of the encounter deck and resolves its staging; an empty deck is handled as `faq.last-encounter-card` says.
- Port of Harad (`16019:A:1`, `16019:A:2`) (recommended). X is the number of enemies engaged with any player, counted when read. The first player assigns all X damage among the characters in play of any player, one point at a time, as the user ruled for High Falls (19019:A:3) and The Withered Heath. Not taken: each owner assigns his characters' damage. With X = 0 the cost is paid at once. Basis: `rr.travel`, `rr.first-player`, `docs/scenarios/ered-mithrin/the-withered-heath.md` (user ruling 1). No source covers this card.
- Haradrim Estate (`16020:A:1`) (span-settled). While in the staging area no progress can be placed on it (`rr.cannot`); once active it takes progress.
- Haradrim Estate travel (`16020:A:2`) (recommended). The first player searches the encounter deck and discard pile for a Harad enemy (`rr.search`: may add; `faq.search-effects`: the deck is shuffled, the discard pile is not); with none found the cost is still paid and the players travel; the enemy engages the first player (the engage event is sent, so "engages you" Forced effects fire); then, only when the search found an enemy (`rr.then`), 1 progress goes on the main quest past the active location. Not taken: with no Harad enemy the players cannot travel here. Basis: `rr.travel`, `rr.search`, `rr.then`; https://visionofthepalantir.com/2019/09/08/escape-from-umbar/ (describes the search, nothing on none found).
- Enemy Pursuit (`16021:A:1`) (recommended). The first player chooses which option the players take; X is the number of progress tokens on the main quest when the effect resolves; with 0 progress both options do nothing; damage is assigned by the first player as for High Falls. Not taken: each player chooses. Basis: the printed line, `rr.first-player`; the thread above (only-one-option answer).
- Enemy Pursuit Shadow (`16021:A:2`) (span-settled). After this attack the attacker makes an additional attack; its earlier shadow cards are discarded and a new one is dealt if the deck has one. Basis: `faq.additional-attacks`, `faq.enemy-attacks-outside-combat`. Copied from Servant of Sauron.
- Southron Champion When Revealed (`16040:A:1`) (span-settled; recommended for an empty deck). The side quest goes to the staging area; the effect discards from the top of the encounter deck until a Harad enemy is discarded, adds it to the staging area with 1 resource token (it is "the Champion") and, while the side quest is in play, gives it +2 Threat, +2 Attack and +2 Defense and "Immune to player card effects"; the main quest stage cannot be defeated while the Champion is in play. Basis: `hrd.encounter-side-quests`, `hrd.immune-to-player-card-effects`, `faq.immune-expanded`. An encounter deck that runs out while it discards shuffles the encounter discard pile into it and goes on, as Guarded (X) and `faq.last-encounter-card` do (recommended; `DiscardUntil` with `reshuffle`, which stops with no Harad enemy in either pile). Not taken: the effect ends at once on an empty deck. The same reading holds for `16044:A:1`, `16067:A:1` and `16067:B:2`. Basis: `wor.guarded-x`, `emce.guarded-x`; no official ruling for a "discard until" effect that is not Guarded (X).
- Southron Champion (`16040:A:2`) (span-settled). The Champion takes no damage from any source, attacks included, unless the side quest holds at least 4 progress tokens; the side quest cannot be defeated while the Champion is in play, so it waits full until the Champion is defeated, and is then defeated at once. Basis: `rr.cannot`, the printed line.
- Southron Captain (`16041:A:1`, `16041:A:2`) (span-settled). In the staging area each other Harad enemy gets +1 Threat; engaged with you, each other Harad enemy engaged with you gets +1 Attack. Basis: `rr.modifiers`.
- Southron Soldier Shadow (`16042:A:2`) (span-settled). The first player chooses a non-unique enemy in the staging area and engages it (it triggers "engages you"), then deals it a facedown shadow card; with none nothing happens. This is Onslaught of Mordor's record (146040:A:2), printed the same, so its reading is copied: an encounter card has no controller, so the first player chooses (`rr.the-word-choose`). The enemy has not attacked this round, so it attacks later in this combat phase (`rr.step-6-4a`). Not taken: it attacks next round. No post covers a shadow-engaged enemy attacking.
- Nowhere to Hide (`16044:A:1`) (see the empty deck reading above). The first player discards until an enemy and puts it into play engaged with him.
- Nowhere to Hide Shadow (`16044:A:2`) (recommended). +1 Attack; +2 instead if the enemy engaged a player at any time in the current round (from the staging area, the encounter deck or a card effect); an enemy engaged in an earlier round gets +1. Not taken: "engaged this round" means engaged in the encounter phase only. Copied from Hound of Sauron. Basis: `rr.engaged`.
- In Enemy Territory (`16045:A:2`) (span-settled). While in the staging area, no player holds more than 5 cards in hand; a hand over 5 discards down to 5 at once, the player's choice, also after later draws. Basis: `rr.constant-ability`; https://visionofthepalantir.com/2019/09/08/escape-from-umbar/ (play report, same reading). The table holds the flag `handLimit`, and the haradrim family announces `handOverLimit` for a seat over it at the next settle.
- In Enemy Territory (`16045:A:3`) (span-settled). The Response is optional, offered to the first player when the side quest is defeated: each player draws 1 card; the card goes to the victory display (Victory 10) and the quest deck does not advance. Basis: `hrd.side-quests-in-play`, `rr.response`, `docs/scenarios/ered-mithrin/fire-in-the-night.md` (user ruling 4).
- Injured Companion (`16046:A:1`, `16046:A:2`) (span-settled). Each player chooses for himself: raise threat by 1 for each character he controls, or discard one character he controls. The Shadow raises each player's threat by 1 if the attack destroys a character, hero or ally. Both lines are copies.
- Enfeebled (`16047:A:1`) (span-settled). Each player exhausts a character he controls (his choice; none with no ready one). Until the end of the combat phase no exhausted character can ready, including a character exhausted or entering play later in that phase; it does not reach the refresh phase; if revealed after the combat phase nothing applies. Basis: `rr.lasting-effects`, `faq.lasting-effects`, `rr.cannot`. The class holder of a lasting effect on later characters reads player card effects (`cannotReadyByPlayerCard`), the only readying of the combat phase, until the end of the combat phase.
- Enfeebled Shadow (`16047:A:2`) (recommended). The character that defended the attack cannot ready until the end of the round, the refresh phase included; with no defender nothing. Not taken: any character that defends at some time is held. Basis: the printed line, `rr.lasting-effects`; https://visionofthepalantir.com/2019/09/08/escape-from-umbar/ (reads Enfeebled only).
- Stages 1B and 2B (`16066:B:1`, `16067:B:1`, `16066:B:2`, `16067:B:3`) (span-settled). A stage holds at most 15 progress tokens, though it cannot advance (the card caps what `faq.progress-without-advancing` allows); it cannot be defeated while any player is engaged with an enemy; defeating 2B wins the game. The flag `stageCapped` does it.
- Flee the City (`16067:B:2`) (span-settled). Once at the beginning of each quest phase, if any player is not engaged with an enemy, one enemy is discarded from the deck and added to the staging area (one, not one for each player).
