# Challenge of the Wainriders

Vengeance of Mordor part 6 (Challenge of the Wainriders, `CotW`), standalone only, on Easy and Normal (Q09.6, E09.6). Encounter sets Challenge of the Wainriders, Rolling Plains and Riders of Rhûn (`vom.setup-challenge-of-the-wainriders`; the last two are in A Shadow in the East). The quest is six stages in a circuit, and the scenario adds the Race keyword, Racing X tests, and the objectives The Challengers and The Wainriders.

- **Setup.** Stage 1A prints "Setup: Build the circuit (see insert). Place The Wainriders and The Challengers objective cards at this stage. The first player adds Wainrider Champion to the staging area. Each other player adds 1 different location to the staging area. Shuffle the encounter deck." (`22075:A:1`). The reader gives the steps `buildCircuit` and `placeCircuitObjectives`, a staging clause for the Champion and `eachPlayerAddsLocation` with `exceptFirst`. The circuit is `vom.the-circuit`.
- **Piles.** The six quest cards are the quest deck (DragnCards sets stages 2 to 6 aside; five overrides deal them as quest cards, stages 1 to 3 on the lower row and 4 to 6 on the upper). The Challengers and The Wainriders stay set aside until the Setup places them; Wainrider Champion is staged (an override). The Champion's Cunning shares collector number 73 with Wainrider Chariot, so it takes the code `220730` (an override).
- **Win and lose.** The Challengers wins by completing the circuit twice before The Wainriders does, and loses if The Wainriders complete two circuits first (`22065:A:2`).

## Data

- Race stages cannot be defeated outside the quest phase, progress cannot be placed on them by player card effects, and a defeated stage stays in play with its progress removed at the end of the phase (`vom.race-keyword`).
- Each stage's Forced effect makes a racing X test (`vom.racing-x-tests`). Wainrider Champion has Threat and Defense X, 2 per player, and is Indestructible.

## Readings

The statuses are those of the Readings bullet of `docs/scenarios/vengeance-of-mordor.md`. Each key is the scan-read ledger line (`data/printed/vengeance-of-mordor.json`), `<code>:<side>:<n>`. Sources read: the Challenge of the Wainriders rulesheet (`vom.the-circuit`, `vom.race-keyword`, `vom.racing-x-tests`, `vom.racing-x-tests-example`), ALeP = https://alongextendedparty.com/semi-official-errata-faq-1-10/ (item 3.352), https://visionofthepalantir.com/2021/05/25/challenge-of-the-wainriders/ (a play article; one comment, no designer answer), the FFG forum archive thread https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/307473-beating-challenge-of-the-wainriders/ (found by search, not opened). A line not listed here is span-settled.

- Damage on a stage the Wainriders leave (`22064:A:1`) (researched). The damage is removed from a defeated stage like progress, and the Wainriders do not complete a second circuit automatically: ALeP 3.352 (7 April 2020, Caleb Grace): https://alongextendedparty.com/semi-official-errata-faq-1-10/.
- Ahead (`22067:A:2`, `22069:A:1`) (span-settled). The Challengers is ahead of The Wainriders when it has advanced through more quest stages than they have; at the same stage neither is ahead. Basis: `vom.the-circuit`.
- A failed racing test (`22076:B:2` to `22080:B:2`) (span-settled). A stage whose test fails is still defeated and The Challengers advance; the excess progress is not placed on the next stage; the failure effect applies. Basis: `vom.racing-x-tests-example`; stage 6 prints its own failure (not defeated, all progress discarded).
- Results (`22072:A:2`) (span-settled). STRAIGHT, TURN and WILD are the corner marks of encounter cards; a WILD counts as both; a card with no mark counts for neither. Ravine Ledge in the staging area adds 1 to every requirement, and its Travel test needs 1 TURN, so 2 while it is in the staging area. Basis: `vom.racing-x-tests`; the post above says "essentially two".
- Discarding for the test (`22075:B:2`) (recommended). The first player discards the top X cards one at a time; an empty deck is refilled from the discard pile and the discarding goes on. Not taken: the test stops at an empty deck. Why recommended: `rr` gives the refill for a reveal only; no ruling covers a discard.
- Narrow Pass (`22070:A:1`) (recommended). The Response applies to damage placed on the Champion by any source, including excess damage the Wainrider Chariot sends; the damage on the Wainriders' stage is removed by the same amount, never below 0. Not taken: only combat damage. Why recommended: the post above raises timing and gives no answer; no official source settles it.
- Wainrider Chariot (`22073:A:2`) (recommended). "Excess damage dealt by attached enemy" is the damage beyond the defending character's remaining hit points. Not taken: all damage the attack deals. Why recommended: no source defines the excess; the post above reads it as excess.
- Champion's Cunning (`220730:A:1`) (recommended). "If no attacks were made" counts attacks the Champion makes against each engaged player; when no player is engaged it makes none and the damage is placed. Not taken: the Champion engages first. Why recommended: the post above says this usually makes them advance; no official source settles it.
