# The Fortress of Nurn

Vengeance of Mordor part 9 (The Fortress of Nurn, `TFoN`), standalone only, on Easy and Normal (Q09.9, E09.9). Encounter sets The Fortress of Nurn, Ulchor's Guard, Under Guard and The Power of Mordor (`vom.setup-the-fortress-of-nurn`; the last three are in A Shadow in the East). The quest is two stages; the scenario adds the four Castle side quests (Storm the Castle) and the encounter side quest The Dungeons of Dol Rhugar (22158).

- **Setup.** Stage 1A prints "Setup: Player decks cannot exceed 50 cards. Create The Power of Mordor deck (see insert) and set it next to the quest deck. Each player puts 1 copy of Ulchor's Guard into play engaged with him. Add Thane Ulchor and all 4 copies of the Storm the Castle side quest to the staging area. Place the top card of The Power of Mordor deck faceup under each side quest in play. Each player places 8 cards from top of his deck under the quest deck and each side quest in play." (`22159:A:1`). The ledger holds the printed "exceed" (the card prints "excede"). The reader gives the steps `limitDeckSize`, `buildPowerOfMordorDeck`, `putCopyEngagedEachPlayer`, `placePowerCardUnderSideQuests` and `placeDeckCardsUnder`, and a staging clause for Thane Ulchor and the four Storm the Castle cards (`vom.storm-the-castle`).
- **Piles.** DragnCards loads the four Storm the Castle cards on its map; the cycle definition stages them. Ulchor's Guard (4 copies) is set aside for the Setup to put into play; The Power of Mordor cards are the second encounter deck.
- **Storm the Castle.** Each card is double-sided: Storm the Castle on one face, a different side quest on the other (Castle Garrison, Lethal Counterattack, No Quarter, Stronghold of Mordor). Hall of Beorn has the front picture only, so the scan list (`scripts/data/printed/scope.ts`) pins DragnCards' reverse picture and the ledger reads each reverse from it.

## Data

- Thane Ulchor is immune to player card effects, has Threat X (1 more than the Castle side quests in the victory display) and cannot leave the staging area at stage 1B.
- Stage 1B cannot be defeated unless it is the end of the round and 3 Castle side quests are in the victory display; stage 2B caps Ulchor's damage at the progress on it and wins when he is defeated.

## Readings

The statuses are those of the Readings bullet of `docs/scenarios/vengeance-of-mordor.md`. Each key is the scan-read ledger line (`data/printed/vengeance-of-mordor.json`), `<code>:<side>:<n>`. Sources read: the Fortress of Nurn rulesheet (`vom.storm-the-castle`, `vom.power-of-mordor-deck-the-fortress-of-nurn`), https://theroadlotr.wordpress.com/2020/10/21/the-fortress-of-nurn-playthrough/ (a playthrough, no designer answer), https://lotr-lcg-quest-companion.gamersdungeon.net/ (a fan site), ALeP = https://alongextendedparty.com/semi-official-errata-faq-1-10/ (no entry for the scenario). A line not listed here is span-settled.

- Storm the Castle (`22161:A:2`) (span-settled). When it becomes the current quest the faceup encounter card under it is revealed and resolves its staging, and the card is flipped; the side quest on the other face is the current quest until the end of the phase and stays in play until it is defeated. The players randomize the four cards before the Setup stages them so the back is unknown. Basis: `vom.storm-the-castle`.
- Cards under the quest deck and the side quests (`22159:A:1`) (recommended). Each player places 8 cards from the top of his deck under the quest deck and 8 under each of the four side quests, 40 in all, in that order; a card under a side quest returns to its owner's deck, shuffled, when the side quest is defeated, and the player then draws 1 card. Not taken: 8 cards in all. Why recommended: the playthrough above says "8 under each rather than 8 total"; no official answer or agreed thread settles it.
- Player decks (`22159:A:1`) (recommended). A deck of more than 50 cards cannot start the scenario: the deck check refuses the start. Not taken: the deck is trimmed. Why recommended: no source says whether the Setup refuses or trims; the printed words are "cannot exceed". No official ruling settles it.
- Soldier of Nurn and Fortress Walls (`22149:A:1`, `22153:A:2`) (recommended). The player chooses which Castle side quest in play receives the card; with no Castle side quest in play nothing is placed. Not taken: the first player chooses. Why recommended: the card says "a Castle side quest" with no chooser; no source settles it.
- Castle side quests in the victory display (`22148:A:2`, `22150:A:2`, `22155:A:1`) (span-settled). The count is the side quests with the Castle trait in the victory display, whichever face is up. Basis: the printed words and the trait on both faces.
- Stage 1B (`22159:B:2`) (recommended). The 3 progress tokens that bar the stage from being chosen as the current quest are the stage's own; the stage is defeated only at the end of a round with 3 Castle side quests in the victory display. Not taken: the stage is defeated in the quest phase. Why recommended: the card says "unless it is the end of the round"; no source settles the timing with progress.
