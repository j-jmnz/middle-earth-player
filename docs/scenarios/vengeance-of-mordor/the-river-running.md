# The River Running

Vengeance of Mordor part 1 (A Shadow in the East, `ASitE`), standalone only, on Easy and Normal (Q09.1, E09.1). Encounter sets The River Running, Riders of Rhûn, Easterling Raiders and Rolling Plains (`vom.setup-the-river-running`; the insert of the box prints the set list). The quest is two stages, and the scenario adds the encounter side quests Warriors of the East (21024) and An Arduous Journey (21029).

- **Setup.** Stage 1A prints "Setup: Set Warriors of the East aside, out of play. Add Easterling Pursuit and 1 copy of River Running to the staging area. Each player adds 1 different Easterling enemy to the staging area. Shuffle the encounter deck." (`21075:A:1`). The reader gives an aside clause, a staging clause and the step `eachPlayerAddsTraitEnemy` (trait Easterling).
- **Piles.** Easterling Pursuit and one River Running are staged; Warriors of the East is set aside. DragnCards stages one Easterling Outrider, Easterling Raider, Rider of Rhûn and Easterling Captain; four overrides leave them in the encounter deck, where the Setup finds them (the counts then equal Hall of Beorn's).
- **Stage 2A.** Warriors of the East is added to the staging area, then X enemies (X the players) are discarded from the shuffled deck and added (`21076:A:1`).
- **Win.** Stage 2B: "When this stage is defeated, the players win the game." (`21076:B:3`).

## Data

- Stage 1B gets +5 quest points per player and adds +1 Threat to the staging area for each resource on Easterling Pursuit.
- Stage 2B takes -10 engagement cost from each enemy in the staging area, and no more than 5 progress a round while a player is engaged with an enemy.

## Readings

The statuses are those of the Readings bullet of `docs/scenarios/vengeance-of-mordor.md`. Each key is the scan-read ledger line (`data/printed/vengeance-of-mordor.json`), `<code>:<side>:<n>`. Sources read: the insert of A Shadow in the East (no rule of its own for the scenario beyond the Setup), the Vision of the Palantir page https://visionofthepalantir.com/2020/08/22/the-river-running/ (a play article with no designer answer in its comments), ALeP = https://alongextendedparty.com/semi-official-errata-faq-1-10/ (no entry for the scenario). A line not listed here is span-settled (its basis is the printed line and the rule span it names).

- Different Easterling enemy (`21075:A:1`) (span-settled for "title", recommended for the pick). "1 different" is a title, not a card (user ruling 7, `docs/scenarios/ered-mithrin/roam-across-rhovanion.md`); each player in player order picks one non-engaged Easterling enemy from the encounter deck without revealing it, and the deck is shuffled after. Not taken: a random enemy. Why recommended: the page above advises the choice by player count and states no rule; no official answer or agreed thread settles who picks.
- Easterling Pursuit (`21012:A:1`) (recommended). The resource is placed at the end of each round; with 3 or more the objective removes 3, and a count above 3 keeps the rest. The enemies are the discarded enemies of a deck shuffled with the discard pile, and with no enemy in the deck or discard pile nothing is added. Not taken: all resources removed. Why recommended: the page above gives "the objective can receive more than 3 resources, in which case 3 are still removed" and no official source says it; no ruling settles it.
- Warriors of the East (`21024:A:2`, `21076:A:1`) (recommended). The side quest is added to the staging area by stage 2A, not revealed, so its Surge does not trigger; its Forced effect attaches the top Easterling treachery in the discard pile to each Easterling enemy that enters play after it. Not taken: a Surge when added. Why recommended: the page above says "added instead of revealed, so the Surge won't go off"; no official answer settles it.
- Easterling Captain (`21017:A:2`) (recommended). The players choose which Easterling attachment to discard when it would take damage, the damage is cancelled, and a Captain with none takes the damage. The treachery attached when it enters play is attached without resolving its When Revealed text. Not taken: the first attachment is discarded. Why recommended: the page above advises discarding the Horse first, which means the players choose; no official source settles it.
- Exposed Riverbank (`21015:A:1`) (recommended). While in the staging area it places a resource on Easterling Pursuit after a "when revealed" effect is canceled; it does not replace the canceled effect. Not taken: the canceled treachery still places its own resource. Why recommended: the page above says the Riverbank places one "instead of the treachery"; no official answer settles it.
- Easterling Outrider (`21013:A:2`) (researched). The player the Outrider engaged chooses one: place 1 resource on Easterling Pursuit, or it makes an immediate attack on that player. The Forced triggers however it was engaged (ALeP 3.327, 27 August 2019, Caleb Grace: https://alongextendedparty.com/semi-official-errata-faq-1-10/). Not taken: the first player chooses, or a random choice.
- Easterling Captain, entering play (`21017:A:1`) (recommended). The Forced fires whenever the Captain enters play (revealed, added at Setup, added by Easterling Pursuit or by stage 2A). Cards are discarded from the top of the encounter deck until an Easterling treachery (Horse, Sword of Rhûn, Recurve Bow) is discarded; it is attached without its When Revealed or Surge. With none, the whole deck is discarded and nothing attaches. Not taken: resolve its When Revealed, or shuffle the discard pile in first. Why recommended: https://hallofbeorn.com/LotR/Details/Easterling-Captain-ASitE and the page above show no ruling; no official answer settles it.
- Easterling Horse (`21019:A:2`) (recommended). The candidates are Easterling enemies in play (staging or engaged, any player) without a Horse; the one with the lowest current engagement cost, the first player choosing among ties. An engaged enemy is returned to staging. With no candidate the Horse gains Surge. Not taken: staging enemies only, or the printed cost. Why recommended: neither the page above nor https://visionofthepalantir.com/2025/09/25/qotw-the-river-running covers the Horse, and no official answer settles it.
- Sword of Rhûn (`21022:A:3`) (recommended). The candidates are Easterling enemies in play (staging or engaged) without a Sword of Rhûn; the one with the lowest current Attack, the first player choosing among ties. Not taken: staging only, or the printed Attack. Why recommended: no source covers it and no official answer settles it (https://visionofthepalantir.com/2020/08/22/the-river-running/).
- Recurve Bow (`21023:A:3`) (recommended). The candidates are Easterling enemies in play (staging or engaged) without a Recurve Bow; the one with the highest current engagement cost, the first player choosing among ties. Not taken: staging only, or the printed cost. Why recommended: no source covers it and no official answer settles it (https://visionofthepalantir.com/2020/08/22/the-river-running/).
- Warriors of the East, the attack (`21024:A:3`) (recommended). The first player chooses among the Easterling enemies in staging tied for the highest Attack. Not taken: a random pick. Why recommended: no source covers the tie and no official answer settles it (https://visionofthepalantir.com/2020/08/22/the-river-running/).
- An Arduous Journey, the exhaust (`21028:A:1`) (recommended). Each player in player order chooses a ready character he controls, exhausts it and deals 1 damage to that same character; a player with no ready character does neither. Not taken: an exhausted character may be chosen and takes the damage. Why recommended: no source covers the card and no official answer settles it (https://visionofthepalantir.com/2025/09/25/qotw-the-river-running).
- An Arduous Journey, the ready cap (`21029:A:1`) (recommended). The cap is active while the side quest is in play (staging or current). It follows the reading of `07036:A:1` (`docs/scenarios/the-ring-maker/into-fangorn.md`, `CapRefresh`): at the beginning of the refresh phase each player holds back, one at a time, the exhausted characters he controls beyond 6 that the refresh step would ready; the held ones stay exhausted for the phase. Readies by a card effect are not capped. Not taken: the literal count of every ready in the refresh phase from any source, or active only while current. Why recommended: the same printed wording is already settled so on `07036:A:1` and `08099:B:3`; no source covers the card and no official answer settles it (https://visionofthepalantir.com/2025/09/25/qotw-the-river-running).
- The Crossing of Araw, the progress cap (`21076:B:2`) (recommended). While a player is engaged with an enemy, no more than 5 progress in all can be placed on this stage each round, from any source; the count restarts each round and extra progress is lost. Not taken: only the quest phase counts. Why recommended: the same cap is read in `docs/scenarios/angmar-awakened/the-wastes-of-eriador.md` from a community answer only; the 2025 article lists an achievement variant, not a ruling, and no official answer settles this wording (https://visionofthepalantir.com/2025/09/25/qotw-the-river-running).

## Table

Each piece a record creates, with the line that creates it (`docs/agents/agent-brief.md`, Scenario procedure, The Table step). The engine decides each number; the table only says it.

- **Pursuit dial** (`21012:A:1`; `21075:A:1` Setup stages the card): the resources on Easterling Pursuit against the 3 that make the end of the round add enemies (`src/play/readouts/mordor/river.ts`, words in `river-words.ts`). It warms at 2 and is never critical: the Forced effect removes 3 at once. One dial for all seats (the card is one in the staging area), so no held dial for each seat. The same resources add Threat on stage 1B (`21075:B:1`); the note says so. The tokens also show on the card itself.
- **Progress cap dial** (`21076:B:2`): stage 2B's 5 progress a round while a player is engaged with an enemy, the same readout as The Woodland Realm's cap (`src/play/readouts/standalone-scenarios/woodland.ts`); it appears only while the cap is held and says "near" when it is full.
- **Easterling attachments on enemies** (`21017:A:1`, `21019:A:2`, `21021:A:1`, `21022:A:1`, `21023:A:1`, `21024:A:2`): ordinary attachments on an enemy in the staging area or engaged; the table draws an attached card under its host in every zone the host can be in, so no zone or widget. The sentences over the picks that choose the host name the stat that decides it (`src/play/words/asks/the-river-running.ts`).
- **Side quests** (`21024`, `21029`): Warriors of the East and An Arduous Journey are cards in the staging area with their quest points (8 and 6) shown as any quest card's. The refresh cap of `21029:A:1` is a tally of one phase and a Choose the player answers (`CapRefresh`), worded like The Ring-maker's (`07036`, `08099`); no tracker.
- **Set aside** (`21075:A:1`): Warriors of the East waits out of play until stage 2A adds it; no zone is drawn for it.
- **Owner on a pick that spans seats**: the Travel picks (`21014:A:2`, `21026:A:2`) and the tie picks (`21019`, `21022`, `21023`, `21024:A:3`, `21017:A:2`) are the first player's for the group and not `owned`, so the sheet adds who chooses. The picks of one player's own (`21013:A:3` shadow, `21013:A:2` Choose, `21028`, `21029:A:1`, `21029:A:2`, the Setup pick of `21075:A:1`) are `owned`, so two-handed the sentence leads with the player.
- **The One Ring** is not dealt here.
- Test: `src/play/scenarios/the-river-running-table.test.ts`; dial words in `src/play/readouts/mordor/river-words.ts`.
