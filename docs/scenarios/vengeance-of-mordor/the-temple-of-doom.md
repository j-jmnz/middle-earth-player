# The Temple of Doom

Vengeance of Mordor part 3 (A Shadow in the East), standalone only, on Easy and Normal (Q09.3, E09.3; DragnCards labels the deck "Temple of Doom"). Encounter sets The Temple of Doom, Ulchor's Guard, Twisted Tunnels, Easterling Raiders and The Power of Mordor (`vom.setup-the-temple-of-doom`). The quest is a stage 1 and four stage 2 cards; the scenario adds Thane Ulchor and Tribute to Mordor.

- **Setup.** Stage 1A prints "Setup: Shuffle each of the stage 2 quest cards together and place them facedown under this stage. Create The Power of Mordor deck (see insert) and set it next to the quest deck. Add Thane Ulchor and Tribute to Mordor to the staging area. Shuffle the encounter deck." (`21080:A:1`). The reader gives the steps `shuffleStageTwosUnder` and `buildPowerOfMordorDeck` (the set The Power of Mordor, `vom.power-of-mordor-deck-the-temple-of-doom`) and a staging clause.
- **Piles.** The five Power of Mordor cards are the second encounter deck (`encounterDeck2`); Thane Ulchor and Tribute to Mordor are staged; the four stage 2 cards are quest cards dealt in any order.
- **Quest stages.** "Quest stage" counts the double-sided cards from the quest deck in the victory display and no side quest (`vom.quest-stages-vs-side-quests`): stage 1B adds itself to the victory display when it advances, so it counts.

## Data

- Thane Ulchor has Indestructible and is immune to player card effects; Hall of Horrors and Thane Ulchor read X from the quest stages in the victory display.
- Each stage 2B stops progress while there are 4 quest stages in the victory display; Tribute to Mordor cannot be the active quest while Thane Ulchor has hit points.

## Readings

The statuses are those of the Readings bullet of `docs/scenarios/vengeance-of-mordor.md`. Each key is the scan-read ledger line (`data/printed/vengeance-of-mordor.json`), `<code>:<side>:<n>`. Sources read: the insert of A Shadow in the East (`vom.power-of-mordor-deck-the-temple-of-doom`, `vom.quest-stages-vs-side-quests`), https://visionofthepalantir.com/2020/11/19/the-temple-of-doom/ (a play article with one reader correction, no designer answer), ALeP = https://alongextendedparty.com/semi-official-errata-faq-1-10/ (no entry for the scenario). A line not listed here is span-settled.

- Thane Ulchor's X and engagement (`21054:A:2`) (span-settled). X is 1 more than the quest stages in the victory display, and he cannot be engaged, by a player or an effect, unless X is 5; the first stage 1 and three stage 2 cards make 4, so he is engageable at the last stage. Basis: `vom.quest-stages-vs-side-quests`; the post above reaches the same count but calls its own wording loose.
- Power of Mordor cards (recommended). A revealed card of the Power of Mordor deck is an encounter card that resolves as revealed; Khamûl the Easterling, which has no Victory number, goes to the encounter discard pile when defeated, not back to the Power of Mordor deck. Not taken: a discarded card returns to its own deck. Why recommended: the post above says Khamûl "can possibly return" through the encounter deck; no official source says where a discarded Power of Mordor card goes.
- Tribute to Mordor (`21053:A:1`) (span-settled). With 4 or more damage tokens it removes 4 and reveals the top card of the Power of Mordor deck; with none to reveal the players lose the game. Basis: the printed line (ledger misprint note).
- Hall of Horrors (`21056:A:2`) (span-settled). Each player deals all X damage to one character he controls. Basis: the printed words "a character he controls"; the post above corrected "amongst" to a single character.
- Stage 1B (`21080:B:1`, `21080:B:2`) (span-settled). The quest phase is skipped, the resource is placed at the end of the planning phase, and the second advances to the topmost stage 2A. Basis: the printed line.
- Easterling Raiders (`21020` to `21024`) reuse the readings of `docs/scenarios/vengeance-of-mordor/the-river-running.md`, Readings (the Easterling Raiders).
- Lost in the Dark (`21065:A:1`) (recommended). The card is an encounter side quest (quest points 8, Victory 10, printed with a landscape face and "stage" in its text, scan `21065.A.jpg`), so "this stage" is this side quest card. The cards under each threat dial go back, shuffled into their owner's deck, when the side quest is defeated; they stay under the dial if it leaves play any other way. Not taken: the main quest stage (the scan gives no such stage) or any exit of the card. Why recommended: no source covers this card (ALeP has Lost in the Dark 2B of Over the Border only); the printed words "this stage" with a side quest are loose, and "defeated" is the word the rules use for a side quest completed.
- Lost in the Dark (`21065:A:2`) (researched). A threat raise of any amount, one raise per effect, discards one card from under that player's dial; a raise by 0 does not count; with none under the dial nothing happens. Basis: ALeP 3.345 (an effect that raises threat "by 1 for each" is one raise), https://alongextendedparty.com/semi-official-errata-faq-1-10/.
- Power of Mordor objectives (`21068`, `21069`, `21070`) (span-settled). Under the Watchful Eye, The Armies of Mordor and Covered in Darkness are objective cards: a revealed objective stays in the staging area (`rr.staging-area`, `rr.step-3-3`), so their Forced and constant lines hold while they are in play; the scan of Covered in Darkness (`21070.A.jpg`) prints Objective and Doomed 5 and no discard text.
- Khamûl the Easterling (`21066:A:2`) (recommended). The printed line is a constant effect with no trigger word: whenever Khamûl is in play and not engaged with the first player he engages the first player at once, so he is engaged when he enters the staging area and stays engaged until he leaves. Not taken: only when revealed. Why recommended: no source says; the plain present tense is read as in Ulchor's Guard.
- The Tower of Barad-dûr (`21067:A:2`) (span-settled). "Cannot travel here" bars only the Travel step to it, as printed (scan `21067.A.jpg`); an effect that makes it the active location without travel is not barred by this line. Basis: `rr.travel`.
- Tribute to Mordor (`21053:A:1`) (recommended). Damage tokens from every card and the end-of-round Forced total on Tribute; the 4-or-more check runs only inside that Forced, at the end of the round. Not taken: a check at each placement. Why recommended: no source covers a mid-round count; the printed "Then" ties the check to the Forced.
- Tribute to Mordor (`21053:A:2`) (recommended). "Cannot be the active quest" bars the choice of Tribute as the current quest while Ulchor's hit points minus his damage is above 0; Ulchor is Indestructible, so when the damage equals his hit points Tribute can be chosen. Not taken: Ulchor must leave play. Why recommended: no source says how hit points remaining reads for an Indestructible enemy.
- Thane Ulchor (`21054:A:3`) (recommended). One damage token on Tribute for each character Ulchor's attack destroys (the Forced trigger names a destroyed character, so it fires once for each); nothing if Tribute is not in play. Not taken: one for each attack. Why recommended: no source covers several characters in one attack; the trigger names a destroyed character; same reading as `docs/scenarios/vengeance-of-mordor/danger-in-dorwinion.md`, Readings (Submit or Die).
- Place of Evil (`21057:A:1`) (recommended). The first player makes the choice between the extra token and Surge. Not taken: each player chooses. Why recommended: no source says who chooses; the first player decides a shared choice in Danger in Dorwinion (Messenger from Mordor).
- Ulchor's Guard (`21058:A:1`) (recommended). The text is constant: while Ulchor is engaged with a player the Guard, wherever it is, engages that player at once and stays engaged if Ulchor leaves. Not taken: only when revealed. Why recommended: no source covers it; "While" names a continuous condition.
- Ulchor's Rage (`21059:A:1`) (recommended). Ulchor attacks the first player from the staging area (an attack outside combat, `rr.attacks-outside-the-combat-phase`, is no engagement), his shadow card is dealt and he stays in staging; with Ulchor not in play, Rage gains Surge. Not taken: Ulchor must be engaged. Why recommended: no source covers it; "cannot be engaged" does not bar an attack.
- Fanaticism (`21060:A:2`) (recommended). The candidates are Easterling enemies in the staging area or engaged without a copy of Fanaticism; ties of the lowest current Defense go to the first player; with none it gains Surge. Not taken: printed Defense. Why recommended: no source covers ties or the set; same as `21022:A:3` in `docs/scenarios/vengeance-of-mordor/the-river-running.md`.
- Twisted Tunnel (`21061:A:1`) (recommended). The enemy discarded is added to the staging area with no When Revealed; with none in the deck the deck is discarded and no shuffle follows. Not taken: reshuffle. Why recommended: no source covers an empty deck; same as `21049:A:2` in `docs/scenarios/vengeance-of-mordor/danger-in-dorwinion.md`.
- Narrow Opening (`21062:A:1`) (recommended). One card in all, of any type, played from hand each round, per player; a card put into play by an effect is not played. Not taken: one of each type. Why recommended: no source covers it; the printed line says "play" and "1 card".
- Crumbling Passage (`21063:A:1`) (recommended). The previous active location returns to the staging area with its progress kept. Not taken: its progress removed. Why recommended: no source covers it; `vom.losing-control-of-a-location` removes progress only where it says so.
- Under the Watchful Eye (`21068:A:2`) (recommended). One raise of 1 for each draw of any number of cards (a draw of 0 does not count); the opening hand and the mulligan at Setup do not count. Not taken: 1 for each card. Why recommended: no source names this card; ALeP 3.345 reads "by any amount" as one event.
- Covered in Darkness (`21070:A:2`) (recommended). While it is in play, the player card effects together lower each player's threat by at most 1 each round; game effects are not limited. Not taken: 1 for each effect. Why recommended: no source names this card; the same cap wording as Hillside Manor in Danger in Dorwinion (`21037:A:1`) and `mec113.dire`.
- Stage 2B The Legacy of Mordor (`21081:B:1`) (recommended). Each player in player order picks a hero, takes 1 damage on it and exhausts it; the hero cannot ready or be healed until the start of the refresh phase. A player with no ready hero still picks one. Not taken: only ready heroes. Why recommended: no source covers it; no thread names the card.
- Stage 2B Memory of the Dark Years (`21083:B:1`) (recommended). The encounter deck's top X cards are discarded (X read when it resolves), and 2 is added to the staging total threat for each different printed card type among them: enemy, location, treachery, objective or side quest, each its own type (`rr.card-types`); the added threat holds until the end of the phase. Not taken: until the end of the round (no difference in play, the quest phase is where staging is counted). Why recommended: no source covers the length; the printed line says only "add 2".
- Stage 2B Shrine to Sauron (`21084:B:1`) (recommended). Each player discards the top X cards of his deck, then discards from his hand and from play any card with the same title as one he just discarded, without a choice; only his own cards. Not taken: match by card code, or the cards of other players. Why recommended: no source covers it; "copies" is the same title in all of the game's rules.
- Stage 2B The Lord of Middle-earth (`21085:B:2`) (recommended). X is the sum of the printed costs of the cards discarded (no cost, a variable cost and an X cost count 0); the cap is on the total progress placed on the stage until the end of the round, whatever places it, on this stage only (a side quest that is the current quest is not capped); with X = 0 no progress can be placed. Not taken: the cost as paid. Why recommended: no source covers it; `rr.cost` names the printed cost.
