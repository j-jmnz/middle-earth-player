# Danger in Dorwinion

Vengeance of Mordor part 2 (A Shadow in the East), standalone only, on Easy and Normal (Q09.2, E09.2). Encounter sets Danger in Dorwinion, Servants of Sauron, City of Rhûn and Under Guard (`vom.setup-danger-in-dorwinion`). The quest is three stages, and the scenario adds Plot objectives, Cultist enemies and the encounter side quests Shadow of Fear (21043) and Unwelcome Travelers (21052).

- **Setup.** Stage 1A prints "Setup: Set each Plot objective and each Cultist enemy aside, out of play. Each player searches the encounter deck for a different location and adds it to the staging area. One of those locations must be Side Street. Shuffle the encounter deck." (`21077:A:1`). The reader gives one aside clause naming the three Plot objectives (Bribes 21033, Submit or Die 21034, Sauron Worship 21035) and the three Cultist enemies (Priest of Sauron 21030, The Governor 21031, Black Númenórean 21032), `eachPlayerAddsLocation` and the step `mustBeLocation` (Side Street 21049).
- **Piles.** DragnCards loads the Plot objectives as a second shared deck and the Cultist enemies as a third; the cycle definition sets both aside. The locations the players add are searched in the encounter deck (the staging pile is empty).
- **Stage 2A and 3A.** Stage 2A adds 1 random set-aside Plot objective and reveals 1 encounter card per player; stage 3A adds 1 random set-aside Cultist and X enemies (X one less than the players) from the shuffled deck. The Hall of Beorn Easy counts equal the Normal ones for the six cards.

## Data

- Evidence of the Cult has Guarded (enemy) (`hrd.guarded-x`, `hrd.guarded-x-example`); Priest of Sauron, The Governor and Black Númenórean are Victory 6 and immune to player card effects.
- Stage 2B (21078) prints its quest points as 0 (the scan shows a 0 in the quest-points shield, read against `https://s3.amazonaws.com/hallofbeorn-resources/Images/Cards/A-Shadow-in-the-East/The-Shadow-of-Mordor-2B.jpg`), so `data/cards/ASitE.json` is right and needs no override; the preview says "No quest points" for it, which is right, and the gate (21078:B:2) is the stage's only hold.
- Stage 3B: the Cultist takes at most X damage each round, X the printed quest points of the active location; when it is defeated the players win.

## Readings

The statuses are those of the Readings bullet of `docs/scenarios/vengeance-of-mordor.md`. Each key is the scan-read ledger line (`data/printed/vengeance-of-mordor.json`), `<code>:<side>:<n>`. Sources read: the insert of A Shadow in the East, https://visionofthepalantir.com/2025/10/01/qotw-danger-in-dorwinion/ (a quest-of-the-week post whose comments are player reports), ALeP = https://alongextendedparty.com/semi-official-errata-faq-1-10/ (no entry for the scenario). A line not listed here is span-settled.

- Random set-aside Plot and Cultist (`21078:A:1`, `21079:A:1`) (recommended). The player who resolves the card draws the random card from the set-aside pile without looking, and the pile of a type that is empty adds nothing. Not taken: the first player picks. Why recommended: no source says who draws; the post above and the cards print "random" only, and no official ruling settles it.
- Secret Cultist (`21039:A:1`) (recommended). It attaches to the ally with the highest Attack as it is now among the allies in play (the printed line says "highest attack" and, unlike a line that says "printed", does not name the printed value, `rr.printed`); on a tie the first player chooses; with no ally it is discarded with no effect. The attached ally is an enemy engaged with its owner, with a blank text box and its own Attack, Defense and hit points; the card prints no Threat for it, so its Threat is 0. Not taken: highest printed Attack; the ally's Willpower as its Threat (the Fallen Into Evil rule for a hero). Why recommended: no source settles ties or a missing ally, and the printed line names neither a printed value nor a Threat; the post above has players describing Gandalf the White with a Staff.
- Seedy Tavern (`21047:A:1` to `21047:A:3`) (recommended). The facedown cards under it belong to no player's hand; "explored" includes an exploration in the staging area, so each facedown card is revealed whenever the location is explored there or as the active location. Not taken: only an active exploration reveals them. Why recommended: no source covers a Tavern explored in the staging area; the printed text says "explored" without "as the active location", unlike Hideous Deeps.
- Leader of the Cult (`21079:B:1`) (recommended). With no active location the cap X is 0 and the Cultist takes no damage. Not taken: no cap. Why recommended: the card gives no value for a missing active location; no source settles it.
- Stage 1B and 2B (`21077:B:1`, `21078:B:1`) (span-settled). A resource is placed each time the active location leaves play as an explored location, whatever the explorer. Basis: `rr.explored`.
- Side Street at Setup (`21077:A:1`) (recommended). The first player takes Side Street and the others choose from the rest; in solo the player takes Side Street only. Not taken: the players decide together. Why recommended: no source says who takes Side Street, and the printed line has no order.
- Messenger from Mordor (`21040:A:1`) (recommended). The first player chooses between the threat raise and the immediate attack; the attack is made from the staging area and the Messenger stays there. Not taken: each player chooses, or a random choice. Why recommended: no source says who chooses; the card says "either ... or" and the attack names the first player.
- The Governor (`21031:A:2`) (recommended). With The Governor in play and engaged with another player, the Forced moves it to the first player; with it not in play nothing happens. Not taken: it stays with its engaged player. Why recommended: no source covers an engaged Governor; the printed line names the first player.
- Black Númenórean (`21032:A:2`, `21032:A:3`) (recommended). A shadow card with a shadow effect stays faceup on him after the attack and is discarded when he leaves play; at each attack the new card resolves once, at the step where shadow effects resolve (after the defenders are declared), and then each older faceup card resolves its shadow effect too, in the order dealt, "as if it had been dealt this round", against the current attack and not before the defender is declared. Not taken: keep every shadow card; resolve the new card twice; resolve the held cards as the attack begins. Why recommended: no official ruling names the card, and the post above only shows a game against him.
- Bribes (`21033:A:2`) (recommended). The players are processed in player order and the resources are placed once every discard is done, and only when every deck held the X cards (a deck with fewer cards stops the discard short, `rr.then`). Not taken: place per player; place with a short deck. Why recommended: no source covers it, and the order does not change the total.
- Submit or Die (`21034:A:2`) (recommended). The Forced fires for each character an enemy's attack destroys (a defender or a hero from undefended damage, a redirect target included): one damage token each; a character killed by a shadow effect is not counted, and no card of the scenario's four encounter sets has such a shadow effect; the players lose at players plus 4 tokens. Not taken: only a defender counts; one token for each attack; count a shadow-effect kill. Why recommended: no official source covers undefended damage.
- Sauron Worship (`21035:A:3`) (recommended). The Forced uses the end-of-round window, after the refresh, and a player at the new elimination level is eliminated at once. Not taken: placed before the refresh phase ends. Why recommended: no ruling gives the exact step; the post above agrees on the end of round.
- Hillside Manor (`21037:A:1`, `21037:A:2`) (recommended). While it is in staging, the player card effects together lower each player's threat by at most 1 each round (a reduction of 3 becomes 1); other reductions are not limited. The Travel cost discards 1 random card from each hand, and a player with an empty hand pays nothing. Not taken: 1 for each effect; no Travel with an empty hand. Why recommended: no official ruling; the post above agrees with the total.
- Disappearances (`21038:A:1`) (recommended). Each player discards all allies from hand in player order; only if no player discarded a card does it gain doomed 2. Not taken: per player. Why recommended: no ruling on "no cards are discarded this way" for several players; the post above shows the discard is per player.
- City Forum (`21046:A:3`) (recommended). The Travel cost raises each player's threat by 1 for each resource as one raise; with no resource the cost is 0 and is paid. Not taken: a cost of 0 cannot be paid. Why recommended: no source covers a cost of 0.
- Darkness in the East (`21048:A:2`) (recommended). A threat raise of the defending player at any time from the start of the round counts as "this round" (the Doomed keyword, a When Revealed, a cost). Not taken: only a raise in the combat phase. Why recommended: no source defines "this round" for a threat raise; the printed line says "this round".
- Side Street (`21049:A:2`) (recommended). The location is added to staging without its reveal (no When Revealed, no Travel); with no location in the deck and discard the whole deck is discarded and nothing is added. Not taken: reveal the location. Why recommended: no ruling on an empty deck; the post above has no answer.
- Catacombs (`21050:A:2`) (recommended). The Travel cost can be paid with an empty encounter deck; an enemy discarded is put into play engaged with that player with no When Revealed and no Surge. Not taken: no Travel with an empty deck. Why recommended: no source covers it.
- Under Guard (`21051:A:1`) (recommended). It resolves the Travel effect of the active location as an effect, without paying the cost and without moving; with no Travel text nothing happens. Not taken: pay the Travel as a cost and change the active location. Why recommended: no source says; the card says "resolve".
- Unwelcome Travelers (`21052:A:2`) (recommended). The Forced fires when the first player makes it the current quest; the discard stops at the first enemy, and with none the deck is discarded without a shuffle. Not taken: the Forced fires on reveal. Why recommended: no source covers it; the card names the current quest.
- Rhûn Backstabber (`21044:A:2`) (researched). The shadow raises the defending player's threat once, by the number of enemies engaged with him, the attacker included. Not taken: separate raises of 1. Basis: ALeP 3.345 (Caleb Grace, 28 Feb 2020: an effect that raises threat "by 1 for each" is totalled and raised at once), https://alongextendedparty.com/semi-official-errata-faq-1-10/.

## Table

Each piece a record creates, with the line that creates it (`docs/agents/agent-brief.md`, Scenario procedure, The Table step). The engine decides each number; the table only says it.

- **Stage gate dial** (`21077:B:2`, `21078:B:2`): the resources on stage 1B against the 2 that let it be defeated, and on stage 2B against the players plus 3. A goal dial (the players' way out, never warms) with a "near" warning while the stage holds the progress that would defeat it (its quest points; stage 2B prints 0, so any progress) but too few resources (`src/play/readouts/mordor/dorwinion.ts`, words in `dorwinion-words.ts`). The resources come from the Forced effects of `21077:B:1` and `21078:B:1`.
- **Bribes dial** (`21033:A:1`, `21033:A:2`): the resources on Bribes, each adding 1 Threat to the staging area. No limit; a tap says what the Forced effect does with them. Shown while Bribes is in the staging area.
- **Sauron Worship dial** (`21035:A:2`, `21035:A:3`): the damage tokens on Sauron Worship, each lowering every player's elimination level by 1. No limit; the note says that one more is placed at the end of each round.
- **Hillside Manor seat mark** (`21037:A:1`): while Hillside Manor is in the staging area each seat's dial label is "Cap n/1" (solo) or "P1 n/1" (two-handed, short so the bar's caption keeps the count), the threat player card effects took off it this round (`playerThreatLoweredBy`) against the 1 allowed; it never warns. The count restarts each round.
- **Cards facedown under Seedy Tavern** (`21047:A:3`, `21047:A:2`): a pill "N facedown" on the location, never which cards, spoken as "N encounter cards facedown underneath" (`src/play/readouts/mordor/tavern.ts`, one call in `heldMark` of `src/play/cards/card-effects.ts`). The Forced effect reveals each when the location is explored.
- **Shadow cards held by Black Númenórean** (`21032:A:2`, `21032:A:3`): a kept shadow card stays faceup attached to the enemy, so the table draws it under its host in every zone the enemy can be in, as any shadow card; no zone or widget.
- **Converted ally under Secret Cultist** (`21039:A:1`): the ally becomes an enemy engaged with its owner (the card `<code>~enemy`) with Secret Cultist attached to it, drawn under its host as any attachment; no zone or widget.
- **Set aside** (`21077:A:1`, `21078:A:1`, `21079:A:1`): the Plot objectives and the Cultist enemies wait out of play and a random one is added by the stage; no zone is drawn for them.
- **Owner on a pick that spans seats**: the Secret Cultist tie (`21039:A:1`), the Messenger's Choose (`21040:A:1`) and the Seedy Tavern pick (`21047:A:3`) are the first player's for the group and not `owned`, so the sheet adds who chooses. The picks of one player's own (`21038:A:2` shadow, `21042:A:3` shadow, `21044:A:1` and its discard, the Setup search of `21077:A:1`) are `owned`.
- **The One Ring** is not dealt here; its Setup record (`141085:A:1`) makes no tracker.
