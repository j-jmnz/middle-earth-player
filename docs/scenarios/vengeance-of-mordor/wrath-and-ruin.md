# Wrath and Ruin

Vengeance of Mordor part 4 (Wrath and Ruin, `WaR`), standalone only, on Easy and Normal (Q09.4, E09.4). Encounter sets Wrath and Ruin, Easterling Raiders and City of Rhûn (`vom.setup-wrath-and-ruin`; Easterling Raiders and City of Rhûn are in A Shadow in the East). The set and the scenario share a title, so the booklet reader keeps "Wrath and Ruin" whole (`setsOf`).

- **Setup.** Stage 1A prints "Setup: Add Thane Ulchor and Gate of Dorwinion to the staging area. Each player adds a different location to the staging area. Make a copy of Makeshift Fortification the active location. Shuffle the encounter deck." (`22025:A:1`). The reader gives a staging clause, `eachPlayerAddsLocation` and an active clause. The card (scan 22025.A) prints "Shuffle the encounter deck."; the "enocounter" typo is Hall of Beorn's, and the ledger holds the printed word.
- **Piles.** DragnCards stages six locations; one Makeshift Fortification is the active location (an override moves it from the staging pile; ALeP 3.363 says it comes from the encounter deck), the rest are the locations the players add. DragnCards has no line for Warriors of the East (21024), a card of Easterling Raiders; an override adds one copy at both difficulties, which equals Hall of Beorn's count.
- **Taking control.** Stages 1B and 2B print "Forced: When a location is explored, the first player takes control of it." (`vom.controlling-locations`, `vom.losing-control-of-a-location`).

## Data

- Gate of Dorwinion is Victory 5, immune to player card effects, with X Threat the controlled locations plus 1; stage 2B wins the game when the players control it at the end of the round.

## Readings

The statuses are those of the Readings bullet of `docs/scenarios/vengeance-of-mordor.md`. Each key is the scan-read ledger line (`data/printed/vengeance-of-mordor.json`), `<code>:<side>:<n>`. Sources read: the Wrath and Ruin rulesheet (`vom.controlling-locations`, `vom.losing-control-of-a-location`), ALeP = https://alongextendedparty.com/semi-official-errata-faq-1-10/ (items 3.343 and 3.363), https://visionofthepalantir.com/2021/04/17/wrath-and-ruin/ (a play article; one comment, unanswered). A line not listed here is span-settled.

- Control instead of the victory display (`22025:B:2`, `22026:B:3`) (researched). A location that is explored takes the first player's control instead of going to the victory display, even a location with a Victory number (Seedy Tavern): ALeP 3.343 (20 February 2020, Caleb Grace): https://alongextendedparty.com/semi-official-errata-faq-1-10/.
- Makeshift Fortification (`22025:A:1`) (researched). The copy made active comes from the encounter deck, not from the locations the players add; a 4-player game has 4 non-unique locations in the staging area after Setup: ALeP 3.363 (10 December 2020, Caleb Grace): https://alongextendedparty.com/semi-official-errata-faq-1-10/.
- Locations the players add (`22025:A:1`) (span-settled for "title", recommended for the pick). Each player in player order picks one non-unique location from the encounter deck, different in title, without revealing it, and the deck is shuffled after (user ruling 7). Not taken: a random location. Why recommended: ALeP 3.363 gives only the count; no official source says who picks.
- Ransacked House (`22016:A:1`) (recommended). Any player may use the Encounter Action, the topmost discard enemy engages that player, and the player takes control of the House; taking control this way is not an explored location, so Thane Ulchor's Forced is not triggered. Not taken: only the first player. Why recommended: the post above reads it so; no official source settles it.
- Contested Square (`22015:A:2`, `22015:A:3`) (recommended). Its progress is placed only while it is in the staging area; while controlled it returns to the staging area after a character its controller controls is destroyed. Not taken: progress while controlled. Why recommended: the post above reads it so; no official ruling settles it.
- Travel to a controlled location (`22025:B:2`, `22026:B:3`) (recommended). A location a player controls is not a travel target; the players travel only to a location in the staging area (`rr.travel`), and a location they control leaves the staging area. `vom.controlling-locations` and `vom.losing-control-of-a-location` say nothing about travel. Not taken: Assault on Osgiliath's ruling (Caleb Grace, FFG: its scenario rules allow travel to a controlled location), which is a ruling for that scenario. Why recommended: no source says a controlled location may be traveled to in this scenario. Engine: `travelTo` on TakeControlOfLocation, set only by Assault on Osgiliath's record.
- Gate of Dorwinion travel (`22014:A:3`) (recommended). "Stage 2B has at least 12 progress on it" is true only at stage 2; at stage 1 the players cannot travel to it. Not taken: progress on stage 1B counts. Why recommended: the card names stage 2B; no source settles another reading.
- Stage 1B and 2B end of the round (`22025:B:3`) (recommended). "More locations under the players' control than in the staging area" counts the locations in the staging area at that moment, the active location excluded (it is not in the staging area). Not taken: the active location counts. Why recommended: no source settles the active location; the staging area does not hold it.
- Thane Ulchor (`22011:A:2`) (recommended). After the active location is explored, the first player chooses. Option 1 discards from the top of the encounter deck until a location is discarded and adds it to the staging area; the other discards stay discarded, and with no location in the deck nothing is added. Option 2 is an immediate attack by Ulchor on that player, dealt a shadow card (`faq.enemy-attacks-outside-combat`). Not taken: a deck reshuffled when it runs out, or an attack that engages Ulchor. Why recommended: ALeP has no entry; no source covers a deck with no location, and no rule reshuffles a deck inside an effect.
- Choice to return a location (`22012:A:1`, `22020:A:1`, `22026:A:1`) (recommended). A player who controls no location cannot take the "return a location" option, so Champion of Rhûn attacks him, Caught Off Guard puts an enemy into play engaged with him, and Drive them Out returns nothing and engages an enemy. Not taken: a player may pick the impossible option and lose nothing. Why recommended: no source says anything about an impossible option; the cycle file reads We've Been Seen the same way.
- Highest Threat location (`22013:A:2`, `22022:A:2`, `22026:A:1`) (recommended). The controller of the destroyed character, or the player the card names, returns the location he controls with the highest Threat; on a tie that player picks; with none nothing happens. Not taken: the first player picks. Why recommended: no source covers ties; the card says "he", the controller.
- Ransacked House (`22016:A:2`) (recommended). While controlled it returns to the staging area after its controller loses control of any other location; it does not hear its own return. Not taken: it also hears its own return. Why recommended: no source covers it; the House returning itself would be a loop.
- Captured Tower (`22018:A:1`) (recommended). While it is the active location, encounter card effects cannot be canceled: no effect of an encounter card and no whole card can be canceled (`faq.cancel-encounter-effect`, `faq.cancel-encounter-card`). Not taken: only effects, not cards. Why recommended: no ruling names this card; ALeP 3.330 (the Master Ring) reads the same words as absolute.
- Captured Tower (`22018:A:2`) (recommended). The player who takes control shuffles the encounter discard pile into the deck, discards from the top until an enemy is discarded and puts it into play engaged with him; the other discarded cards stay discarded, and with no enemy nothing is put into play. Not taken: the discarded cards go back into the deck. Why recommended: no source covers a deck with no enemy; the card names one shuffle and one discard run.
- Fury (`22019:A:2`) (recommended). Fury counts as a Condition attachment; when the attached enemy would be destroyed, all damage is healed from it, it stays in play and Fury is discarded (no victory display and no defeat trigger). Not taken: the enemy is defeated and Fury is discarded. Why recommended: no source covers a replacement of a destroy; the printed "instead" replaces it.
- Fury (`22019:A:3`) (recommended). When Revealed, Fury attaches to the Easterling enemy without Fury that has the lowest current engagement cost, in the staging area or engaged with any player; ties go to the first player; with none Fury is discarded. Not taken: staging area only. Why recommended: no source covers it; same reading as the Easterling Horse in `docs/scenarios/vengeance-of-mordor/the-river-running.md`.
- Under Siege (`22022:A:1`) (recommended). X is fixed when the card is revealed (1 more than the locations the players control then) and does not change when control changes later in the phase. Not taken: X read live. Why recommended: no source covers it; `rr.the-letter-x` leaves a value set by the card as it is set.
- Stage 2B (`22026:B:2`) (recommended). The players cannot travel to Gate of Dorwinion unless they control strictly more locations than the staging area holds, the active location excluded, with the same reading as stage 1B (`22025:B:3`). Not taken: the active location counts. Why recommended: no source settles the active location; the staging area does not hold it.

## Table

Each piece a record creates, with the line that creates it (`docs/agents/agent-brief.md`, Scenario procedure, The Table step). The engine decides each number; the table only says it. The pieces are drawn in `docs/design-system-table.md` (Wrath and Ruin's table pieces).

- **A location a player controls** (`22025:B:2`, `22026:B:3`, `22016:A:1`): the engine keeps it in that player's `play` list, so the table draws it in that player's play row. Its preview, its spoken label ("controlled by you" or "controlled by Player 2") and the banner and log lines for control taken or lost are Assault on Osgiliath's (`readouts/against-the-shadow/osgiliath.ts`, shared by key on both scenarios). Code: `readouts/mordor/wrath.ts` (`wrathDetail`).
- **Control dial** (`22025:B:3`, `22026:B:2`): the locations the players control against more than the locations in the staging area hold (the Gate counts, the active location does not). A goal dial that never warms: nothing here is a danger. Its note names the controlled locations, how many more are needed and X.
- **Gate dial** (`22014:A:3`, `22026:B:2`): stage 2B's progress against the 12 that let the players travel to Gate of Dorwinion, on stage 2B only, after the Control dial. A goal dial.
- **X** (`22014:A:2`, `22022:A:1`): no widget of its own. The Control dial's note says X (1 more than the locations controlled), Gate of Dorwinion's preview gives its Threat now, and Street Fighting's and Ulchor's Revenge's own X (the locations one player controls) is said in each seat's mark note and in the damage choice.
- **Seat marks** (`22021:A:1`, `22023:A:3`): two-handed one mark for each seat, "P1 2", the locations it controls, the count kept in the caption; the note names them and says X for that seat. True solo has no mark (the Control dial holds the one count). No mark warns.
- **Contested Square and Ransacked House** (`22015:A:3`, `22016:A:2`): while controlled, the preview says the Forced line they gain (the card's printed line is unchanged).
- **Gate of Dorwinion** (`22014:A:2`, `22014:A:3`): its preview gives its Threat now and what stops a travel (12 progress on stage 2B, more locations controlled than in the staging area).
- **Owner on a pick that spans seats**: Fury, the Easterling ties (`21022:A:3`, `21023:A:3`) are the first player's for the group and not `owned`; the returns and the Setup location of one player's own are `owned`; the Ulchor attack rows name Thane Ulchor, the player and his Attack.
- **Setup records** `141085:A:1` (The One Ring) and `22025:A:1` (Ulchor, the Gate and the active Makeshift Fortification from the scenario's piles, each player's location) make no other tracker, zone or held card.
