# The Steward's Fear

Against the Shadow part 4 (pack `TSF`, MEC18), standalone only, on Easy and Normal (Q03.4, E03.4). Encounter sets The Steward's Fear, Streets of Gondor and Brigands (`ats.setup-the-stewards-fear`; the two other sets are from Heirs of Númenor and are shared with Peril in Pelargir). The Underworld X keyword is `ats.underworld` with `ats.underworld-example`: cards of the Underworld deck are stacked facedown under the location, and when the location leaves play they are revealed one at a time and added to the staging area.

- **Setup.** `ats.setup-the-stewards-fear-underworld-deck` builds a separate Underworld deck from every enemy of Streets of Gondor and Brigands and the three Clue objectives, removes the three Villain enemies and the three Plot objectives from the encounter deck, and sets one of each kind aside facedown at random (the "hidden" cards); the other two of each kind leave the game unseen. The seed picks the hidden pair, so one seed always hides the same Villain and Plot.
- **Piles.** The `setAside` pile holds what the Setup removes from the encounter deck: the Underworld deck (Pickpocket 3, Zealous Traitor 3, Lossarnach Bandit 2, Umbar Assassin 1 on Normal and none on Easy, and the Clue objectives A Scrap of History, Secret Map and A Prisoner), the three Villains (06015 to 06017), the three Plots (06029 to 06031) and Roots of Mindolluin (06019). DragnCards loads the Underworld deck as the second shared deck (`sharedEncounterDeck2` in `scripts/data/cycle-defs.ts`). Override in `data/overrides.json`: The Fourth Star (06022) is in the encounter deck (DragnCards sets it aside; the Setup and the rulesheet name no such step); the scan of stage 1B ("Search the encounter deck for The Fourth Star") confirms it.
- **Errata.** None for a card of this scenario.
- **Cards of other scenarios.** The Streets of Gondor and Brigands cards (05037 to 05045) keep their records of Peril in Pelargir (`docs/scenarios/against-the-shadow/peril-in-pelargir.md`); the Alcaron's Scroll parts of those readings do not apply here, because no Scroll is dealt.

### The Underworld deck and Underworld X

- `06011:A:1` (span-settled). The Underworld deck is the enemies of Streets of Gondor and Brigands (Pickpocket x3, Zealous Traitor x3, Lossarnach Bandit x2, Umbar Assassin x1, none on Easy) and the three Clues, shuffled and kept apart, facedown, out of play. The other cards of those sets stay in the encounter deck. Not taken: another composition. Basis: ats.setup-the-stewards-fear-underworld-deck.
- `06011:A:1` (span-settled). Roots of Mindolluin is out of play from the Setup and comes into play only through `06012:B:1`. One Villain and one Plot are set aside facedown at random; the other two of each are removed from the game unseen; the whole Setup resolves before stage 1B. Basis: rr.in-play-and-out-of-play, rr.setup, ats.setup-the-stewards-fear-underworld-deck.
- `06018:A:1`, `06019:A:1`, `06020:A:1`, `06021:A:1`, `06022:A:1` (span-settled). When a location with Underworld X enters play, the first player stacks the top X cards of the Underworld deck facedown under it. "Enters play" covers a reveal from the encounter deck, a search (Lost in the City, `06011:B:1`) and a location made active from the set-aside zone (Roots of Mindolluin); a location that is only returned to the staging area stays in play, so it takes nothing again and keeps its stack. Not taken: only a revealed location. Basis: ats.underworld, rr.search.
- `06018:A:2`, `06018:A:1` (span-settled). Sewers: the When Revealed resolves first (1 card under the active location, if able), then the card enters play and Underworld 1 stacks 1 card under Sewers itself. Not taken: Underworld first. Basis: rr.staging, ats.underworld.
- Underworld X, the stack (span-settled). When a location leaves play for any reason, explored or not, and whatever location it is (Sewers stacks a card under the active location: a City Street or a Market Square too), every facedown card under it is revealed one at a time (the staging steps of `rr.staging`: keywords such as Surge and Doomed, When Revealed, the effects that hear a reveal) and added to the staging area. The first player reveals; the next card is revealed once the reveal before it is over, and nothing is revealed while an effect is on the stack, an attack or a strike is being resolved or a response window is open. An open action window does not hold the cards back: it waits under the reveal and comes back when it is over (Secret Map explores a location in an action window and the cards come out at once). The stage that the location's token advances waits until the stack is revealed, because the location left play first. A card of the Underworld deck that would be discarded goes to the encounter discard pile. Not taken: the stack is discarded unexplored; the cards are only moved to the staging area. Basis: ats.underworld, rr.reveal.
- Underworld X, the order (recommended). The cards are staged in the order the engine holds them under the location (the order they were made), which is a function of the shuffle; the rules give no order. Not taken: last taken first, or first taken first (the players cannot tell the cards apart before they are staged, so the order is not a choice). Basis: ats.underworld names none.
- Underworld X, fewer cards than X (recommended). The location takes the cards that remain; with none left the keyword has no effect (the rulesheet states only the empty case). Not taken: no effect unless X cards remain. Basis: ats.underworld.
- The deck is separate (span-settled). Its cards are not searched, not dealt shadow cards and not shuffled until `06013:B:1`. After that shuffle the Underworld deck no longer exists and a later Underworld takes nothing; the stacks stay under their locations. Basis: ats.setup-the-stewards-fear-underworld-deck, ats.underworld.
- `06023:A:2` (researched). The top card of the Underworld deck is revealed in full (its keywords and When Revealed) and added to the staging area; a card with the Trait Clue is placed in the encounter discard pile instead; with the deck empty nothing happens and Surge still resolves. A revealed Underworld location enters play and takes its stack. Not taken: a Clue goes back to the deck. Basis: ats.underworld ("placed in the encounter deck discard pile"); FFG forum thread 222592.

### The quest stages

- `06011:B:1` (span-settled). The Fourth Star is searched for in the encounter deck only and made the active location at once; it enters play by the search, so Underworld X applies with X the players; then the encounter deck is shuffled. Not taken: Underworld only for a revealed location. Basis: ats.underworld, rr.search.
- `06011:B:2`, `06012:B:2` (span-settled). The token is placed when the active location leaves play as an explored location, from any source of progress (the quest, Secret Map). A location returned to the staging area or discarded otherwise gives none. Not taken: only the quest phase. Basis: rr.explored. The Fourth Star's Response (`06022:A:3`) comes after this Forced effect.
- `06011:B:3`, `06012:B:3` (recommended). The stage advances as soon as the 4th resource token is on the card, in any phase, once nothing is resolving (a Clue claimed in the planning phase advances it then). Not taken: only at the end of the quest phase. Basis: rr.quest-advancement by analogy; no source names the timing. The count starts at 0 on `06012`; tokens do not carry over from stage 1 (recommended; rr.quest-advancement by analogy).
- `06012:A:1` (span-settled). The hidden Plot is revealed, and so added to the staging area (faq.reveal-and-add, rr.reveal, rr.staging); the three Plots have no When Revealed. The Plot's constant lines apply at once; a line that acts "in each staging step" first acts in the next staging step (recommended; the cards' own text).
- `06012:B:1` (span-settled). Roots of Mindolluin enters play as the active location (Underworld 1 applies); the old active location returns to the staging area and stays in play (its stack stays, no explored token). Not taken: the returned location leaves play. Basis: rr.active-location, rr.in-play-and-out-of-play.
- `06013:A:1` (span-settled). The hidden Villain is revealed, and so added to the staging area, not engaged. Basis: faq.reveal-and-add, rr.reveal.
- `06013:B:1` (span-settled). The Underworld deck is shuffled into the encounter deck; the stacks under locations stay. Basis: ats.underworld.
- `06013:B:2` (span-settled). "A Villain" is any Villain in play (staging or engaged); progress above the quest points stays on the stage while one is. Defeating the last stage wins the game, as every last stage does; the record has no instruction for it. Not taken: progress is capped. Basis: faq.progress-without-advancing.

A canceled event is still played, so Bane attacks after it too. Not taken: it waits for the combat phase. Basis: rr.cancel and rr.event-cards (a canceled event is still played), rr.forced; docs/brief.md (Core round).

## Engine notes

The conditions and verbs are in the family `pelargir` (`src/catalog/families/pelargir.ts`, `src/rules/families/pelargir/`). The one shared line is the family-only list of `src/rules/interpret/condition-kinds/family-only.ts`, which names the two conditions.

- The Underworld deck is the cards of `state.setAside` that hold `stackedAt` (0 for the top); no zone and no pile. `BuildUnderworldDeck` and `HideOneAside` (Setup, `underworld.ts`) use the game's seed; the table lists their cards as facedown set-aside cards (`asideSecrets` of the family manifest). A card that leaves the deck loses `stackedAt`. `TakeUnderworld` moves cards to the zone `under` with the host; `RevealUnderworld` starts a normal reveal frame; `ShuffleUnderworldIntoEncounterDeck` ends the deck.
- Underworld X is written as records on each Underworld location, not as a parsed keyword: a Forced `encounterEnteredPlay` (reveal, a location made active) and a Forced `searchedIntoPlay` (a search) with `TakeUnderworld`. The cards under a location that leaves play are revealed by a settle sweep of the family (`releaseUnderworldStacks`), which holds for any location (a stack can lie under a City Street) and needs no record. `RevealSetAside` reveals the hidden Plot and Villain. The keyword chip of the card preview is read from the card's own `TakeUnderworld` record (Table).
- `RevealTopThenDiscard` (`reveal-discard.ts`): the card goes faceup to the encounter discard pile and a reveal frame starts at its Doomed step (the step that places a card in the staging area is skipped), so its Doomed, When Revealed, the Forced `revealed` effects and Surge work as in a reveal. It is not given `familyRevealed`, `watchReveal` or the first-enemy note, and it is never in the staging area.
- `PickRandom` (`pick-random.ts`): the seed chooses among the pick's candidates. `RemoveQuestTokens` (`quest-tokens.ts`) takes progress and resource tokens off the current quest card.
- `06030:A:2` is a constant record with the condition `deckEmpty` (`empty-deck.ts`: a living player has no card in his deck) and `LoseGame`, which the core ends the game for once nothing is resolving (`constantWin`).
- The stages' four tokens use the flag `advanceNow` of `families/angmar/stage.ts` with a `tokens` condition and the condition `underworldReleased` (no facedown card lies under a location that is out of play), so the stage advances after the stack of the location that was just explored is revealed.
- **Limits.** A Response that cancels a card revealed by `RevealTopThenDiscard` is offered as for any revealed card. An enemy revealed by it is not noted as the first enemy of the round. The cards under a location are revealed in the engine's card order.

## Table

Each piece is made by a line above and drawn only while that line makes it; none is a control (no control types damage, resources, threat, progress or life). Code: `src/play/readouts/against-the-shadow/stewards-fear.ts`, `src/play/words/scenarios/against-the-shadow/stewards-fear.ts`, the hooks `src/play/store/selectors.ts` (the stack), `src/play/cards/card-effects.ts` (the pill), `src/play/cards/dungeon-preview.ts`, `src/play/cards/card-keywords.ts` and `src/play/cards/table-preview.ts`; test `src/play/scenarios/the-stewards-fear-table.test.ts`; every hook keys on this scenario's id, on its `TakeUnderworld` records or on its Plots' codes, and the test proves another scenario's table shows none of them. The design system (`docs/design-system-table.md`, The Steward's Fear underworld pieces) describes them.

- **The stack under an Underworld location** (`06018:A:1`, `06019:A:1`, `06020:A:1`, `06021:A:1`, `06022:A:1`, `06018:A:2`, `ats.underworld`): the facedown cards are drawn under the location in the staging area or as the active location, one counted peek, with the pill "2 facedown" and the preview line "2 facedown underworld cards lie under it. When it leaves play, they are revealed one at a time and added to the staging area." The card Sewers puts under the active location shows the same on whatever location that is (City Street, Market Square). A tap on a facedown card opens its host (its slot has no card code; a hold and a right click do nothing); no face, name or picture of a card under a location is ever said.
