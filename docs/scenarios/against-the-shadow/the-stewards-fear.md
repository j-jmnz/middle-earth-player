# The Steward's Fear

Against the Shadow part 4 (pack `TSF`, MEC18), standalone only, on Easy and Normal (Q03.4, E03.4). Encounter sets The Steward's Fear, Streets of Gondor and Brigands (`ats.setup-the-stewards-fear`; the two other sets are from Heirs of Númenor and are shared with Peril in Pelargir). The Underworld X keyword is `ats.underworld` with `ats.underworld-example`: cards of the Underworld deck are stacked facedown under the location, and when the location leaves play they are revealed one at a time and added to the staging area.

- **Setup.** `ats.setup-the-stewards-fear-underworld-deck` builds a separate Underworld deck from every enemy of Streets of Gondor and Brigands and the three Clue objectives, removes the three Villain enemies and the three Plot objectives from the encounter deck, and sets one of each kind aside facedown at random (the "hidden" cards); the other two of each kind leave the game unseen.
- **Piles.** The `setAside` pile holds what the Setup removes from the encounter deck: the Underworld deck (Pickpocket 3, Zealous Traitor 3, Lossarnach Bandit 2, Umbar Assassin 1 on Normal and none on Easy, and the Clue objectives A Scrap of History, Secret Map and A Prisoner), the three Villains (06015 to 06017), the three Plots (06029 to 06031) and Roots of Mindolluin (06019). DragnCards loads the Underworld deck as the second shared deck (`sharedEncounterDeck2` in `scripts/data/cycle-defs.ts`). Override in `data/overrides.json`: The Fourth Star (06022) is in the encounter deck (DragnCards sets it aside; the Setup and the rulesheet name no such step). Its source is the Hall of Beorn text of stage 1A, which the Read task confirms against the scan.
- **Errata.** None for a card of this scenario.
