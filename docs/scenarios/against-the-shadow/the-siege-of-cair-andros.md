# The Siege of Cair Andros

Against the Shadow part 3 (pack `HoN`), standalone only, on Easy and Normal (Q03.3, E03.3). Encounter sets The Siege of Cair Andros, Southrons, Mordor Elite and Ravaging Orcs (`ats.setup-the-siege-of-cair-andros`). Southrons is shared with Into Ithilien and Assault on Osgiliath, Mordor Elite with Assault on Osgiliath and The Morgul Vale, Ravaging Orcs with Encounter at Amon Dîn and The Blood of Gondor. Five stages: The Defense, Reinforcing the Banks, Breakthrough at the Approach, Breakthrough at the Citadel and The Last Battle.

- **Rules.** `ats.cair-andros-location-damage` (damage on locations is separate from progress), `ats.cair-andros-defense-carry-over` (undefended damage of the stage 1 Defense does not carry over), `ats.cair-andros-remove-stage` and `ats.cair-andros-remove-stage-example` (removing a stage from the quest deck). Battle and Siege are `hon.battle` and `hon.siege`; The Citadel (05033) has the Battleground trait.
- **Piles.** Stage 1A's Setup adds The Approach, The Citadel and The Banks to the staging area (05033 to 05035, the `staging` pile). No override.
- **Errata.** The Power of Mordor (`faq.errata.05061`, Mordor Elite): the Read task reads the printed card against it.

Not taken: staging area only. Basis: rr.in-play-and-out-of-play (active location is in play); card text; V18 says "including any active location". Sources: V18, HOB:Orc-Scramblers.
- `05030:shadowText` (span-settled). Shadow resolves when dealt: 2 damage to The Citadel if it is in play (staging or active); else the attacking enemy gets +2 Attack. - `05031:text` (span-settled). Doomed 2 raises each player's threat 2 on reveal even if the WR does nothing. Not taken: doomed only if WR resolves. Basis: rr.doomed-x; faq.cancel-encounter-effect (keyword is separate from the WR).
- `05031:text` (span-settled). WR: only the ACTIVE location, and only if it has the Battleground trait, takes 3 damage; a Battleground in the staging area takes none. Not taken: all Battlegrounds. Basis: card text ("the active location"). Sources: HOB:Battering-Ram.

Not taken: split or all tied locations. Basis: rr.first-player (first player selects among multiple eligible targets); card data threats 1/2/3.

- `05033:text` (researched). Constant: at 11 or more damage The Citadel is removed from the game. It is not explored, not in the victory display (no VP), not in the encounter discard, and its Response does not fire (no stage removed). Checked as soon as damage is placed, in the staging area or as the active location. Not taken: removal counts as "leaves play as an explored location", or goes to the discard. Basis: rr.removed-from-game; card text; V18 ("If the locations were destroyed by the encounter deck, they also leave play but are not added to the victory display"; "You will also not discard the accompanying quest stage"). Sources: V18, HOB:The-Citadel.

- `05033:text` (researched). Response after The Citadel leaves play as an explored location (explored in the staging area by a card effect, or active by quest or travel): remove stage 4. "Remove a stage" = take it out of the quest deck and set it aside; it is no longer part of the scenario deck; defeating the stage before it advances to the next numerically sequential stage still in the deck. Offered immediately after the explore has resolved (docs/brief.md, Keywords implemented once). Not taken: stage stays in the deck. Basis: B p.4 ("In this scenario, players may be instructed to remove a stage from the quest deck... advance to the next numerically sequential stage that remains in the quest deck"); V18 ("Remember to remove stages if players explore a Battleground location"). Sources: B, V18.
- `05034:text` (researched). Same as above, stage 3 (Approach). Not taken: same. Basis: B. Sources: B.
- `05035:text` (researched). Same as above, stage 2 (Banks). Not taken: same. Basis: B. Sources: B.
- `05033:text` (recommended). "if able" when the stage to remove is the CURRENT quest card (e.g. The Citadel explored while stage 4 is active, possible because stage 1B can also be beaten with 9 progress): the stage cannot be removed, the Response does nothing. Not taken: the current stage is removed and the players advance. Basis: no source says; B only describes removing a later stage. Sources: B, V18, VN: none addresses a removal of the stage in play; the card says "if able" and B's "players are that much closer" language is about stages ahead. Add to Readings to confirm. Not taken: later entrants unaffected (V18 says so). Basis: rr.lasting-effects (encounter or quest card lasting effects apply to cards entering later); rr.character-cards; V18 disagrees with the span, the span wins. Sources: V18 (disagrees).

- `05072:text` (span-settled). Setup: The Approach, The Citadel, The Banks are placed in the staging area directly (not revealed: no WR, no staging step), then the encounter deck is shuffled. Basis: rr.setup; card text. Sources: HOB:The-Defense.
- `05072:back` (researched). Siege (hon.siege). Each undefended attack's whole damage (shadow bonuses included) goes to the lowest-Threat Battleground location in play instead of to a hero; none goes to a hero; damage left over when the location is removed does not carry to the next location; ties: first player picks. Not taken: hero still takes it; carry-over. Basis: hon.siege; B p.4 ("damage dealt to the lowest threat location from an undefended attack does not carry-over to the next Battleground location"); rr.first-player; VN ("damage from these attacks will go onto the lowest threat Battleground location in play instead"). Sources: B, VN, HOB:The-Defense.
- `05072:back` (researched). Damage on a location is separate from progress, does not count toward exploring, and is only read by card effects (the removal thresholds). Not taken: damage reduces quest points. Basis: B p.4 ("Such damage is separate from any progress placed on the location... The damage does nothing in and of itself"). Sources: B.
- `05072:back` (researched). "If there are no Battleground locations in play, immediate advance to the next stage": checked constantly (also when the last one is removed or explored); the next stage is the next numerically sequential stage still in the quest deck. Quest points 9 let progress also advance stage 1. Not taken: advance to stage 2 only. Basis: B p.4 (next sequential remaining stage); card text. Sources: B, HOB:The-Defense.
- `05072:back` (recommended). ORDER when the last Battleground is EXPLORED: the explored location's Response (remove its stage) resolves BEFORE the advance, so clearing all three on stage 1 goes straight to stage 5. Not taken: advance first (constant before Response), so the stage the last location would remove (stage 2 after The Banks) is reached and the removal fails. Basis: VN ("Clear all three yourself, and you go straight to stage 5"; achievement "Be Done Before Lunch": clearing all three on stage 1). Sources: VN, QOTW https://visionofthepalantir.com/2026/05/22/qotw-recap-the-siege-of-cair-andros/: no official text orders "immediate advance" against a Response; rr.timing-of-ability-resolution would put constant first, so the community reading is against the generic order. Add to Readings to confirm. If a location is removed by damage there is no Response, so the advance goes to the next stage still in the deck.

Not taken: extra card first; applies in the round reached. Basis: rr.step-3-3; card text. Sources: no source on order: the card gives none. Order is unobservable except through Surge and WR chains.

Forced after an unsuccessful quest (threat higher than committed Attack; a tie is neither): no player raises threat; each player chooses and discards 1 character they control (hero or ally; a hero is discarded, not defeated for threat); a player with none discards nothing; the Forced fires between the comparison and the threat raise. Not taken: players discard and also raise threat. Basis: rr.step-3-4; rr.character-cards; card text "instead of". Sources: no ruling found; a search surfaced Hunt for Gollum thread https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/129869-the-hunt-for-gollum-%E2%80%94-stage-1b-questing and a "quest successfully" Forced thread (snippets only, not read in full) saying a quest-stage Forced fires before tokens are placed. The `instead` replacement is the same family as Urdug's Gambit, which the engine may already have.

Siege; the stage itself adds 5 Threat to the staging area total while it is the current quest (constant, like a card in the staging area; counts in every quest resolution and in Power of Mordor's count as 0 cards). Not taken: only at resolution. Basis: hon.siege; card text.
- `05076:back` (span-settled). Siege. WR: reveal 1 card per player and add to the staging area; each is staged as a normal reveal (Surge, Doomed and WR resolve; a treachery goes to the discard). Not taken: cards added without WR. Basis: rr.staging, rr.step-3-3.
- `05076:back` (researched). Constant: with 4 or more victory points collected, The Last Battle gains Battle and loses Siege; it replaces the printed keyword, as the card text of The Cross-roads does. Points = Victory X of every card in the victory display (Banks 1, Approach 2, Citadel 3, Lieutenant of Mordor 3); a card removed from the game or in the discard counts nothing. Re-checked at every point change, so Battle can switch on mid-stage (willpower replaced by Attack). Not taken: Willpower/Defense chosen once on reveal; points of player cards also count. Basis: faq.errata.06123 (Cross-roads gains siege and loses battle: text overrides keywords); faq.victory-display; rr.victory; V18 (Lieutenant is the only other VP card; "4 victory points required to swap the Siege keyword for Battle") ; the Nightmare rule that player-card VPs do not count is a Nightmare card, not here. Sources: V18, VN, HOB:The-Last-Battle.
- `05076:back` (span-settled). Win: defeating this stage (15 quest points) wins the game. Basis: card text. Sources: HOB:The-Last-Battle.
- `05056:text` (span-settled). "Allies cannot defend against Lieutenant of Mordor" is a constant on the enemy: no ally may be declared as its defender; heroes may. Basis: rr.cannot; card text. Sources: HOB:Lieutenant-of-Mordor.
- `05056:text` (span-settled). WR resolves ONLY the When Revealed effect of the topmost treachery in the encounter discard (the last one placed there, including a treachery discarded after being a shadow card); no Doomed/Surge of it; that treachery stays in the discard pile and is not "revealed" again. Not taken: Doomed and Surge also resolve; the card is re-revealed. Basis: faq.cancel-encounter-effect (a keyword is separate from the WR effect); rr.staging. Sources: HOB:Lieutenant-of-Mordor.
- `05056:text` (span-settled). "if able": no treachery in the discard, or its WR has no legal effect, does nothing. Basis: card text.
- `05056:text` (span-settled). Lieutenant is not in the staging area while its WR resolves, so a copied Power of Mordor does not count it (staging comes after the WR). Not taken: counts it. Basis: rr.staging (step 3 places the card after the WR).

Not taken: only the copied effect. Basis: card text; rr.cannot; V18 ("immune to all cancellation"). Sources: V18, HOB:Lieutenant-of-Mordor.
- `05056:text` (researched). Source text runs "...Mordor.When Revealed:" together: the scan separates the constant from the WR (check on the scan). Victory 3. Basis: HOB:Lieutenant-of-Mordor. Sources: HOB:Lieutenant-of-Mordor.
