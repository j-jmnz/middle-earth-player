# The Siege of Cair Andros

Against the Shadow part 3 (pack `HoN`), standalone only, on Easy and Normal (Q03.3, E03.3). Encounter sets The Siege of Cair Andros, Southrons, Mordor Elite and Ravaging Orcs (`ats.setup-the-siege-of-cair-andros`). Southrons is shared with Into Ithilien and Assault on Osgiliath, Mordor Elite with Assault on Osgiliath and The Morgul Vale, Ravaging Orcs with Encounter at Amon Dîn and The Blood of Gondor, so the records of those sets must hold there too. Five stages: The Defense (05072), Reinforcing the Banks (05073), Breakthrough at the Approach (05074), Breakthrough at the Citadel (05075) and The Last Battle (05076). Battle and Siege are `hon.battle` and `hon.siege`; Archery is `hon.archery`. The rules of this scenario are `ats.cair-andros-location-damage` (damage on a location is separate from its progress), `ats.cair-andros-defense-carry-over` (undefended damage does not carry over), `ats.cair-andros-remove-stage` and `ats.cair-andros-remove-stage-example` (removing a stage from the quest deck).

- **Piles.** Stage 1A's Setup adds The Approach, The Citadel and The Banks to the staging area (05033 to 05035, the `staging` pile) and shuffles the encounter deck. The piles do the Setup, so `05072:setup:1` is empty (as `05068:setup:1`). No override.
- **Errata.** The Power of Mordor (`faq.errata.05061`): the scan-read ledger line has no "Then", so it already is the erratum.
- **Cards of other scenarios.** The Southrons (05052 to 05055) and The One Ring (141085) have records from Into Ithilien and need none here. Approaching Cair Andros (05071) is not dealt here.

## Engine notes

The flags and verbs are in the family `pelargir` (`src/catalog/families/pelargir.ts`, `src/rules/families/pelargir/`).

- `removedAtDamage` (a constant on each Battleground location, the amount is the number): the family's settle sweep (`siege.ts`) removes a location in play whose damage reaches it, to the zone `removed`, and clears the active slot.
- `undefendedToBattleground` (a constant on stage 1): the family's `combat.undefended` hook returns the lowest Threat Battleground locations in play as the targets of an undefended attack's damage.
- `advancesWithoutBattleground` (a constant on stage 1): the same sweep calls `advanceStage` when no Battleground location is in play, once the quest is set up.
- `RemoveStage { code }` (`stage-remove.ts`): the stage is set aside; the current stage is set aside and the quest advances. `skippedStage` (`stage-skip.ts`) takes, in this scenario, the first later stage still in the quest deck.
- `ResolveTopmostTreachery { uncancelable }` and `ShuffleStagingIntoEncounterDeck` (`staging.ts`): the second leaves out the card being revealed, the cards with a frame open on the stack (a Lieutenant of Mordor that copies it) and any treachery, because a revealed card lies in the staging area while its When Revealed resolves, and sets the effect's X.
- `cannotSpendResources` (a constant on a hero) and `losesSiege` (a constant on the current stage) are read by two shared files, each with one added clause that no other record can reach: `payerOk` in `src/rules/families/costs/pay.ts` and `spendablePools` in `src/rules/families/costs/verbs.ts` (a locked pool pays nothing), and `currentQuestHas` in `src/rules/families/quest/conditions.ts` (the stage that loses Siege does not have it). The direct spenders read the same lock through `resourcesLocked` (`families/pelargir/spending.ts`): `PayResources` (`families/encounter/items.ts`, Rumour from the Earth), `SpendFrom` (`families/play/leave.ts`, Elfhelm), `SpendFromPool` (`families/defense/index.ts`, Livery of the Tower) and `SpendResources` from one pool. A locked hero in those three stops the effect that needed the payment. Discards of resources (`DiscardResources`, Captain Sahír, Shelob) and moves are not spending and stay.
