# Encounter at Amon Dîn

Against the Shadow part 6 (pack `EaAD`), standalone only, on Easy and Normal (Q03.6, E03.6). Encounter sets Encounter at Amon Dîn and Ravaging Orcs (`ats.setup-encounter-at-amon-din`; Ravaging Orcs is from Heirs of Númenor and shared with The Siege of Cair Andros and The Blood of Gondor). The Villagers X keyword is `ats.villagers` with `ats.villagers-example` and `ats.villager-tokens-discarded`; the two objectives that hold the tokens are `ats.setup-amon-din-objectives`.

- **Setup.** Stage 1A prints "Set Ghulat aside, out of play. Put Lord Alcaron into play. Make Burning Farmhouse the active location. Add the Rescued Villagers and Dead Villagers objectives to the staging area. Then, shuffle the encounter deck and reveal 1 encounter card per player and add it to the staging area." (`06066:A:1`, read from the scan).
- **Piles.** The data places Rescued Villagers (06068) and Dead Villagers (06069) in `staging`, Burning Farmhouse (06075) in `activeLocation`, Lord Alcaron (06070) and Ghulat (06071) in `setAside`, and the two stages in the quest pile. DragnCards loads Lord Alcaron in player 1's play area where the Setup puts him into play, so `scripts/data/cycle-defs.ts` maps that group (`player1Play1`) to `setAside`. The Setup record gives Alcaron to the first player, gives Burning Farmhouse its tokens, shuffles and reveals.
- **Errata.** None for a card of this scenario. No `data/overrides.json` row.
- **Cards of other scenarios.** The Ravaging Orcs (05062 to 05064) keep their records of The Siege of Cair Andros (`docs/scenarios/against-the-shadow/the-siege-of-cair-andros.md`, Readings): no line of them is wrong here. The One Ring (141085) keeps its records; its loss is not held by Ghulat (Engine notes, Limits).

### Villager tokens

- `06066:B:1`, `06075:A:1`, `06076:A:1`, `06077:A:1` (span-settled). Villagers X places X resource tokens on the card when it enters play: a location revealed into the staging area, the active location of the Setup, a location a search puts into play, and the quest card when it is revealed (stage 1 at the start of the game). The tokens are the card's `resources` and are villager tokens: no payment, Prowl or effect that counts, spends or discards resources reads them (ats.villagers). Not taken: tokens placed only when a location becomes active. The records are Forced records of the location (`encounterEnteredPlay`, `searchedIntoPlay`); the Setup record places the tokens of Burning Farmhouse, which the piles put in play without an entry event. No keyword file is read.
- `06066:B:1` (researched, community blog only). The Villagers value is the number placed on entry, not a cap: Alcaron and Panicked! put tokens on a location that already holds more (R2: "does not represent the maximum"). Not taken: a location holds at most X.
- `06069:A:1`, `06070:A:2` (span-settled). A discarded token returns to the token bank (ats.villager-tokens-discarded). A token that moves (explore, the replacement of the quest progress, Panicked!, Alcaron's placing) is not discarded. Damage tokens that Ghulat and Marauding Orc put on Dead Villagers are no discard: Alcaron cannot answer them.
- `06068:A:1`, `06069:A:1` (recommended). A location that leaves play any way but explored (discarded, shuffled back, removed) takes its tokens with it to the bank, and no damage token is placed: the card names only the explored case and nothing is "discarded from a location" by name. Not taken: the tokens are discarded (damage on Dead Villagers) or move to Rescued Villagers. No source addresses it (R1, R2, Hall of Beorn). Reading taken for `06069:A:1`: `ats.villager-tokens-discarded` ("When a villager token is discarded, return that token to the token bank") says what happens to a discarded token, not when a token is discarded, and `06069:A:1` names a token "discarded from a location, objective, or quest stage": a card that discards the location discards the card, not its tokens. The other reading (1 damage token on Dead Villagers for each token on the location) is not taken. R3 and R4 (https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/270842-encounter-at-amon-din) do not address it.

### Savagery of the Orcs

- `06066:A:1` (span-settled). Setup order: Ghulat set aside, Alcaron put into play and given to the first player, Burning Farmhouse the active location with its tokens, the two objectives in the staging area, the deck shuffled, 1 card revealed for each player (a revealed card's Villagers are placed as it enters play; a surge card adds one). Basis: ats.setup-amon-din-objectives, ats.villagers, rr.surge.
- `06066:B:2` (recommended). The replacement: when progress would be placed on the quest, no progress is placed and as many tokens move from the quest card to Rescued Villagers as the progress, at most the tokens it holds; the rest of the progress is lost. Not taken: the surplus carries to stage 2 or stays on the quest. Basis: rr.replacement-effects and the card's "an equal number"; no source names the surplus (R1, R2, Hall of Beorn). Only progress that reaches the quest meets it: the active location takes its share first and the remainder of an explored location goes to the quest (faq.legolas-active-location, rr.explored). The move is no discard: no damage token, no Alcaron (card word "move"; `06069:A:1` says "discarded").
- `06066:B:3` (recommended). A state check: when no villager token is on the quest card, the quest advances at once, also if none stands there when it is revealed. Not taken: advance only after a later quest resolution. The card's "if" has no timing and no source gives one. Tokens can also leave the quest by a discard (`06069:A:1` names "quest stage"): Dead Villagers hears it, Alcaron may save one, and the stage advances at zero (span-settled).

### Protect the Villagers and the end of the game

- `06067:A:1` (span-settled). Ghulat is added to the staging area from the set-aside pile, not revealed (no When Revealed) and not engaged.
- `06067:B:1` (span-settled). An undefended attack is aimed at Rescued Villagers while stage 2B is current. The damage that would go to a hero is replaced by discarding as many tokens from Rescued Villagers (X is the damage after the undefended all-damage rule, rr.step-6-4-3); with fewer tokens, all of them; each discard puts a damage token on Dead Villagers. No hero is damaged. Basis: rr.replacement-effects and the card's "instead". It covers an enemy's undefended attack only, not damage by a card effect.
- `06067:B:1` (recommended). With no token left, or X = 0, nothing is discarded and the hero is still not damaged (the damage is replaced by a discard of zero). Not taken: the hero takes it. No source names the empty case.
- `06067:B:2`, `06071:A:3` (span-settled). "When the players defeat this stage, end the game" is held while Ghulat is in play: "the game cannot end" is absolute (rr.cannot). The defeated stage stays the current quest card, the game goes on, and the end runs once Ghulat leaves play. Not taken: the game ends at once.
- `06067:B:2` (recommended). With Ghulat in play after the defeat there is no next stage: play goes on (rounds, Ghulat's attacks and his damage tokens) and the comparison is made with the counts of the moment he leaves play. Not taken: the end is checked only at the defeat. Basis: rr.winning-the-game, rr.cannot; the blog's order (Ghulat in the Victory Display, then the end) is the only voice (R2, R1).
- `06068:A:2`, `06069:A:2` (researched; the printed lines and one relayed official reply). The printed win line asks for "more villager tokens here than damage tokens on Dead Villagers" and the loss line for "more damage tokens here than villager tokens on Rescued Villagers": neither fires on a tie. No `ats.*` span states the tie; the pack insert says the players need to collect "more" villager tokens (R1). An FFG reply relayed in the forum (https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/270842-encounter-at-amon-din, post of 2018-03-05: "if the number of tokens on Rescued Villagers is the same as Dead Villagers, then you have neither won nor lost", and the rules say nothing more) says a tie is not a win; the community there and in R4 plays it as a loss (the Legendary leagues do). The game has ended (stage 2B was defeated) and the app has no result between won and lost, so a tie is **lost**: the record loses on "not more". Not taken: a tie wins (rr.winning-the-game: the final stage was defeated), or play goes on. The official reply is second-hand and says "neither", which the app cannot show: see Readings to confirm.
- `06068:A:2` (recommended). "The end of the game" is the game's end by stage 2B (or an effect), not a check each round.
- `06070:A:3` (span-settled). If Lord Alcaron leaves play (destroyed, discarded, removed, his controller eliminated: rr.player-elimination) the players lose, held while Ghulat is in play (rr.cannot).
- `06070:A:3` (recommended). The loss that Ghulat held back is not forgotten: the players lose when Ghulat leaves play, because the end is blocked, not cancelled. No source speaks of it.
- `06071:A:3` (researched). Ghulat leaves play when he is defeated to the Victory Display (Victory 2) or any other way; the held end then runs (R2, R5 above).
- `06071:A:3` (recommended). With every player eliminated and Ghulat in play the game ends in a loss anyway (rr.player-elimination); the hold does not keep a game with no player going. See Engine notes, Limits. Which reading the sources support: none settles it. `rr.cannot` ("absolute") read to the letter supports holding every end, and `rr.player-elimination` ("If all players are eliminated, the game ends in a loss for the players") is as unconditional; no FAQ or forum post names Ghulat with an elimination (R3, R4 and https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/270842-encounter-at-amon-din do not). The record holds the ends the scenario writes and the engine ends the game for elimination, because no player is left to play on (recommended).

### Rescued Villagers, Dead Villagers and Lord Alcaron

- `06068:A:1` (span-settled). A location that leaves play as an explored location (the active location, or a location in the staging area explored by an effect; into the discard pile or the Victory Display) moves all its tokens to Rescued Villagers, a move (rr.explored: "not the same as discarding it"). A Victory location still moves its tokens (rr.victory). Moved tokens can later be discarded or taken by Panicked!.
- `06069:A:1` (span-settled). Each token discarded from a location, an objective (Rescued Villagers too) or the quest stage puts one damage token on Dead Villagers, one for each token, as a Forced effect, so before Alcaron's Response is offered (rr.forced, rr.response). Damage tokens never come off.
- `06070:A:1` (span-settled). The first player gains control of Lord Alcaron, an objective ally: he stands in the first player's play area from the Setup and is not committed unless the player commits him (ats.ally-objectives); control stays with that player when the first player token passes (rr.ownership-and-control).
- `06070:A:2` (span-settled for the order, recommended for the damage). The Response comes after the Forced effect: the damage token is on Dead Villagers when Alcaron exhausts to place the discarded token on a location (the staging area or the active location, also the one the token came from), and it stays. Not taken: the Response replaces the discard and no damage token is placed (R3, a table custom). One token for each use; he must be ready and a location must be in play; with several tokens discarded at once he saves one (R2). The token he places makes the location hold more than its Villagers value (see above). R3 (https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/166266-encounter-at-amon-din-alcarons-response-and-dead-villagers-card) is community only: its two posters read the card strictly (the damage token first) and one would play it the thematic way; no staff reply.

## Engine notes

The verbs, the trigger and the flags are in the family `pelargir` (`src/catalog/families/pelargir.ts`, `src/rules/families/pelargir/`; the render templates are in `scripts/data/effects/render/families/pelargir.ts`; engine tests `src/rules/families/pelargir/amon-din.test.ts`). One shared line: `PROGRESS_REPLACED` in `src/rules/quest/progress-replaced.ts` has the row `progressToVillagers`, which does nothing unless a stage holds the flag.

- **Villagers X** is records (Readings, Villager tokens). Villager tokens are the `resources` of a card that is no hero: the payments, the Prowl discard and every effect on a hero's pool read the pools of heroes (and allies that hold resources), so they cannot reach a token. The verbs that act on one card's resources name a card by a reference or code (`RemoveQuestTokens` reaches the current quest card, and no card of this scenario names it).
- **`DiscardVillagerTokens`** (`villagers.ts`). Takes up to `amount` tokens off the card, queues the trigger `villagerDiscarded` once for each token (the tokens of one call share an occurrence) and sets the effect's X to the number discarded. The event names no card, so every card in play hears it: the Forced effect of Dead Villagers and the Response of Alcaron. **`MoveVillagerTokens`** moves tokens between two cards in play, adds to X and announces nothing.
- **`PickFewestHitPointsLeft`** (`fewest.ts`). The hero or ally in play with the fewest hit points left, the first player choosing among a tie; the controller of the picked card acts for the rest of the effect (the queue's items change seat).
- **The quest card's replacement** (`06066:B:2`). The flag `progressToVillagers` is in `PROGRESS_REPLACED`: the stage's progress is not placed and its Forced record hears `progressReplaced` with the amount and moves the tokens. The flag `advancesWithoutVillagers` is read by a settle sweep (`advanceWithoutVillagers`): it advances the quest when the card holds no token, not during the Setup and not while an effect, an attack or an event is open.
- **The undefended attack** (`06067:B:1`). The flag `undefendedToVillagers` makes Rescued Villagers the only target of an undefended attack (`combat.undefended`). The damage is placed on it as on any card, and `combat.damaged` (`villagersDamaged`) takes it off again and discards that many tokens, one event each; the step log keeps no damage on Rescued Villagers.
- **The end of the game.** Stage 2B holds its win (`holdsWin`) and its Forced record sets `gameEnding` when it is defeated. The constants of Rescued Villagers (`WinGame`) and Dead Villagers (`LoseGame`) hold while `gameEnding` is set and Ghulat is not in play, so Ghulat leaving play makes them fire at that settle. Alcaron's Forced record sets `endingLost` when he leaves play; `alcaronLoss` (`ending.ts`, a settle sweep) loses the game once the flag is set and Ghulat is not in play.
- **Limits.** The hold of Ghulat covers the ends this scenario writes: stage 2B's defeat, Alcaron's loss and the comparison of the objectives. The core's own ends are not held: The One Ring leaving play, an empty player deck and the elimination of all players end the game at once. A hold for them would be a change in `src/rules/core/` or `src/rules/interpret/constants.ts`. A location that leaves play unexplored keeps its `resources` number in its new zone until it enters play again, where the count is cleared: the tokens are in the bank in every rule that reads them.

## Table

Pointers for the Table step (`docs/agents/agent-brief.md`, Scenario procedure); each piece is drawn only in this scenario and read from the records and the table, never a control.

- **Villager tokens**: a count on a location (staging and active), on the quest card (stage 1B) and on Rescued Villagers, read from `resources` (`src/play/canvas/token-spots.ts`, `src/play/cards/table-preview.ts`), and the Villagers chip on the preview (`src/play/cards/card-keywords.ts`).
- **The two objectives**: a readout that compares Rescued Villagers (tokens) and Dead Villagers (damage tokens) with the verdict wording (more rescued than dead wins; a tie loses), from stage 1 (`src/play/readouts/scenario-dials.ts`).
- **Lord Alcaron**: the ally row from the Setup, the choice sheet of his Response (the location to place the token on), and a note on his preview that the players lose if he leaves play.
- **Ghulat**: Attack X as the current damage tokens on Dead Villagers, the line "the game cannot end while he is in play", and a banner on the stage when the game waits for him to leave play (also when Alcaron has left play).
- **The undefended attack** in stage 2B: the defender sheet shows "N villager tokens from Rescued Villagers" in place of a hero (`src/play/choice/combat-sheets.ts`).
- **Asks**: Alcaron's Response and its location, Craven Eagle's prevention, Panicked!'s location for each player.
- **Savagery of the Orcs**: the quest readout shows the tokens left on the card in place of quest points while stage 1B is current (`src/play/phases/quest-stat.ts`).
- **The Resolution**: won with more rescued than dead; lost with a tie or fewer, or when Alcaron left play.
