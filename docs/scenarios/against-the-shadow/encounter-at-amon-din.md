# Encounter at Amon Dîn

Against the Shadow part 6 (pack `EaAD`), standalone only, on Easy and Normal (Q03.6, E03.6). Encounter sets Encounter at Amon Dîn and Ravaging Orcs (`ats.setup-encounter-at-amon-din`; Ravaging Orcs is from Heirs of Númenor and shared with The Siege of Cair Andros and The Blood of Gondor). The Villagers X keyword is `ats.villagers` with `ats.villagers-example` and `ats.villager-tokens-discarded`; the two objectives that hold the tokens are `ats.setup-amon-din-objectives`.

- **Piles.** Hall of Beorn's text of stage 1A sets Ghulat aside, puts Lord Alcaron into play, makes Burning Farmhouse the active location, adds the Rescued Villagers and Dead Villagers objectives to the staging area, then reveals 1 encounter card per player. The data: `staging` Rescued Villagers (06068) and Dead Villagers (06069), `activeLocation` Burning Farmhouse (06075), `setAside` Lord Alcaron (06070) and Ghulat (06071). DragnCards loads Lord Alcaron in player 1's play area where the Setup puts him into play, so `scripts/data/cycle-defs.ts` maps that group (`player1Play1`) to `setAside`.
- **Errata.** None for a card of this scenario.

## Readings

The readings of Encounter at Amon Dîn use the statuses defined in the Readings bullet of `docs/scenarios/against-the-shadow.md`. The keys are the research keys, `<code>:<field>`. The lines are written from the source text, not from the scan-read ledger, so the Read task of Encounter at Amon Dîn confirms each against its scan.

- 06068:text(explored) (span-settled). "If a location leaves play as an explored location, move any villager tokens from that location to Rescued Villagers." Explored = progress >= quest points; location goes to the discard pile (or Victory Display with Victory); this is "not the same as discarding it" so Dead Villagers does not fire. Applies to the active location and to a staging-area location (explored by a card effect). Not taken: explored tokens are discarded. Basis: rr.explored; card text. Sources: rr.explored; rr.victory.
- 06068:text(not explored) (recommended). Not taken: a leave-play not explored discards the tokens (damage on Dead Villagers). Basis: no source found: insert and card text name only the explored case; searched Hall of Beorn, forum archive, rulespal, blogs, none addresses it. - `06069:text` (researched). Dead Villagers: any villager token discarded from a location, objective or quest stage puts 1 damage token on Dead Villagers per token (also fires for tokens discarded from Rescued Villagers, an objective). The damage token is placed after the discard has happened; damage tokens are never removed. Not taken: fires once per discard event, not per damage. Basis: card text; visionofthepalantir "Dead tokens cannot be removed". Sources: https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/04/11/encounter-at-amon-din/.
- 06069:text(end) (recommended). At end of game: loss if damage tokens > villager tokens on Rescued Villagers (stated with 06068; a tie is a loss by the rescued card "more ... than" test). Not taken: tie neither wins nor loses. Basis: Rescued Villagers needs strictly more to win; Dead Villagers triggers only on strictly more; with a tie neither fires. Forum and blog treat tie as loss (blog: "Grim Rule" tells players they lost). Sources: https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/04/11/encounter-at-amon-din/ ; https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/96730-encounter-at-amon-din-victory-condition-what-of-a-draw ; blog is the author's reading and the forum has no official reply; both agree on loss, but the cards' text alone says neither fires.
- 06070:text(control) (span-settled). The first player gains control of Lord Alcaron (an Objective Ally in play at Setup under the first player). Not taken: encounter-controlled. Basis: card text. Sources: card text; rr.ownership-and-control.
- 06070:text(Response) (recommended). Response: after a villager token is discarded, exhaust Alcaron: place THAT token on a location (any location in play, active or staging) instead; it is then not discarded: the token stays in play, but the Dead Villagers damage token has already been placed or not depends on timing: strict reading places the damage token first (discard resolved), then Alcaron saves a token. Not taken: thematic: the Response replaces the discard (no damage token). Basis: forum thread: strict rules reading "after it has been discarded" gives damage first; the poster plays thematically. visionofthepalantir: "Alcaron can only save a discarded villager"; tokens added to Dead Villagers by Ghulat or Marauding Orc cannot be saved; one token per use (Trapped Inside discards simultaneously, only one saved). Sources: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/166266-encounter-at-amon-din-alcarons-response-and-dead-villagers-card ; https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/04/11/encounter-at-amon-din/ ; forum has no official reply and the two community voices differ on timing. Standing user ruling (Response offered immediately after trigger has occurred and resolved, docs/brief.md Keywords implemented once) points to the strict reading (damage token placed, then Alcaron offered); Alcaron then places the token and the damage token stays. Orc Ravager then counts as having discarded a token (no surge). Add to Readings to confirm.

Ghulat X = number of damage tokens on Dead Villagers (live count; attack, a printed variable); Forced: when Ghulat attacks, 1 damage token on Dead Villagers (before damage; the attack's attack strength is read per the card's X at damage); Alcaron cannot save it (no token is discarded).
