# Assault on Osgiliath

Against the Shadow part 7 (pack `AoO`), standalone only, on Easy and Normal (Q03.7, E03.7). Encounter sets Assault on Osgiliath, Mordor Elite and Southrons (`ats.setup-assault-on-osgiliath`). Control of locations is `ats.controlling-locations` and `ats.losing-control-of-locations`: a location that leaves play as an explored Osgiliath location goes in front of the first player, in play, with its text active. The scenario has two active locations at times (`faq.two-active-locations`, FAQ 1.34); an active location can give the quest Siege or Battle (`hon.siege`, `hon.battle`).

## Data

- **Piles.** Hall of Beorn's text of stage 1A has each player choose 1 enemy and 1 unique location and add them to the staging area, so no pile holds a Setup card. The scenario has one quest stage (06091). No override.
- **Errata.** Counter-attack (06106, `faq.errata.06106`): the printed card already says "either", so the card and the erratum agree.
- **Printed lines.** The Readings are keyed by the scan-read ledger's line ids (`data/printed/against-the-shadow.json`); the scan of each Osgiliath card was read against the data (stats, Setup, 1B and the parenthetical of 06099:A:2 agree).
- **Shared cards.** The Southrons and Mordor Elite cards (05052 to 05061) are read in `into-ithilien.md` and `the-siege-of-cair-andros.md`. One interaction here: Orc Vanguard and Orc War Camp read "in the staging area", and a controlled location is not in it, so Orc War Camp's bar ends once control is taken (rr.in-play-and-out-of-play, ats.controlling-locations).

## Engine notes

The control of locations is in the family `pelargir` (`src/catalog/families/pelargir.ts`, `src/rules/families/pelargir/location-control.ts`, `undefended.ts` and `progress-bar.ts`; the render templates are in `scripts/data/effects/render/families/pelargir.ts`; line tests `src/campaign/scenarios/assault-on-osgiliath/lines-*.test.ts` and `spec.test.ts`, the table helper `test/scenarios/assault-on-osgiliath/table.ts`). Shared code holds three inert additions, each read only when a record of this scenario sets its flag or filter: the filter `controlled` on a location pick (`src/rules/interpret/pick.ts`), the bar on a player card's progress (`placeProgress` in `src/rules/interpret/effect-verbs.ts`, its callers in `player-verbs.ts` and `families/quest/places.ts` say whether a player card places it) and the render's two trigger lines.

- **Control** (`06091:B:2`, `ats.controlling-locations`). `TakeControlOfLocation` moves the location to the first player's `play` list, faceup, ready, with no progress, and notes the seat on the card (the flag `controlledBy`, the seat plus 1). A location lies there for the first player alone: he is the only one who takes control. Its text works there (a Response of Ruined Square, the Forced effects of the Gate cards). A location explored while it is controlled is taken again by the sweep with no loss (it never leaves control by its own text), and 06091:B:2 finds it in play.
- **A pick of controlled locations** (`type: location`, `controlled: true`): the locations in the `play` lists of the seats the filter names (`control: you` is the acting player's own), never the staging area or the active slots. `highestThreat` then reads the printed Threat as it is when the effect resolves.
- **Loss of control** (`ats.losing-control-of-locations`). `LoseControlOfLocation` returns a named location to the staging area. Every other way out of the play area is seen by one settle sweep (`controlSweep`): `ReturnToStaging` on the card itself, a travel to it, West Gate becoming the active location, and the discard of an eliminated player's cards (core `clearEliminated` sends the location to the encounter discard pile; the sweep puts it in the staging area). A loss removes the progress, clears the controller and announces `controlLost` for the seat that held it; Ruined Tower hears it (`06103:A:2`).
- **Travel to a controlled location** (Caleb: the rules allow it). The family's `travel.reach` lists the controlled locations and its `travel.cost` is the first step of the travel: the location is returned to the staging area (the control is lost), then its own Travel line and the travel run as for any staged location. Ancient Harbor and The Old Bridge cannot be traveled to at all (`Restrict travel`).
- **`attackUndefended`** (`06096:A:2`, `06097:A:2`, `06098:A:3`, `06099:A:3`). The family's `combat.damaged` hook announces it for the defending player once the attack's damage is dealt and the attack had no defender (none declared, or the defender left play). It runs for an attack that deals no damage as well (a 0 Attack enemy; tested through a real attack).
- **The bar on progress** (`06091:B:1`). The table flag `noProgressInStagingByPlayer` (the stage's constant) bars the progress a player card's effect places on a location in the staging area; the active location, a location in a play area and any encounter or location effect are not barred.
- **Ruined Tower** (`06103:A:1`). With exactly one active location it may be traveled to as the second one (`travel.second`, as Fisherman's Dock does); the record's cost exhausts a character when one location is active. It is the only card that gives a second slot, so West Quarter and East Quarter are never active together and the quest never has Siege and Battle at once (the recommended reading of `06100:A:1` is not needed: no record holds a condition for it).
- **Ruined Tower's progress split** (`06103:A:1`, `faq.two-active-locations`). The players divide the progress of a quest between the two active locations as they choose, as for Fisherman's Dock: `splitRooms` in `src/rules/families/smaug/split.ts` names both (the code `06103` beside the Dock's). A location the split explores announces its leaving play (a fault found here: a split never announced the locations it explored, so no Forced effect heard them).
- **A Haradrim Elite chosen at Setup attacks** (`06091:A:1`, `05053:A:1`). `SearchMove` announces `addedToStaging` for an ambush enemy only, so the Setup record names the card it found (`remember`) and `AnnounceAddedToStaging` (family `pelargir`, opt-in: no other record names it) announces it, so the Elite's Forced effect is heard. Southron Support's search in Into Ithilien has the same gap and is not changed here.
- **Easy mode** (`06091:A:1`, `ltp.easy-mode`, `easy-mode.easy-mode`). Hall of Beorn's `easyQuantity` of this set is 0 for the four unique locations, but the scenario's own Easy counts (the E03.7 deck, `easy` in `data/scenarios/assault-on-osgiliath.json`) keep all four, so the Setup has locations to offer on Easy. The scenario's counts are what the deal reads; `easyQuantity` is not read.
- **The controller of a destroyed character** (`06093:A:1`, `06100:A:2`, `06101:A:2`, `06104:A:2`). The Forced effect of Uruk Soldier hears `characterDestroyedByAttack` for the seat that controls the destroyed character; the three Shadow effects start with `ActForDestroyedController`, so the player who controls the destroyed character (a Sentinel defender may belong to another player than the one attacked) picks the location he returns.
- **Ruined Square's Response** (`06102:A:2`) hears an enemy destroyed in the staging area as well as an engaged one (a test destroys one with Thalin as it is revealed).
  - The win check at the end of the round (`06091:B:3`) is a Forced record on the stage: no Osgiliath location is in the staging area or active.
