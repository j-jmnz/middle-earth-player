# Assault on Osgiliath

Against the Shadow part 7 (pack `AoO`), standalone only, on Easy and Normal (Q03.7, E03.7). Encounter sets Assault on Osgiliath, Mordor Elite and Southrons (`ats.setup-assault-on-osgiliath`). Control of locations is `ats.controlling-locations` and `ats.losing-control-of-locations`: a location that leaves play as an explored Osgiliath location goes in front of the first player, in play, with its text active. The scenario has two active locations at times (`faq.two-active-locations`, FAQ 1.34); an active location can give the quest Siege or Battle (`hon.siege`, `hon.battle`).

- **Piles.** Hall of Beorn's text of stage 1A has each player choose 1 enemy and 1 unique location and add them to the staging area, so no pile holds a Setup card. The scenario has one quest stage (06091). No override.
- **Errata.** Counter-attack (06106, `faq.errata.06106`): the Read task reads the printed card against it.

- `06091:back:1` (span-settled). Player card effects (events, attachments, hero abilities) cannot place progress on any location in the staging area; the active location, and locations in a player's play area, are not in the staging area so are not barred; encounter-card and location effects (Ancient Harbor, Old Bridge, Ruined Square) are not player card effects and can place progress in the staging area. Not taken: bar also covers active/controlled locations. Basis: rr.in-play-and-out-of-play, rr.staging-area, VotP "Progress by means of player card effects cannot be placed on any location in the staging area". Sources: https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/04/20/assault-on-osgiliath.
- `06091:back:2` (researched). On exploration the first player takes control: the location leaves the active slot/staging with all progress removed and lies in the first player's play area; it is still in play (not staging, not active, not discarded); its text stays active; Forced resolves before any Response (a Response such as Leave No Trace cannot remove it). Not taken: location goes to the discard pile. Basis: INSERT text above; rr.in-play-and-out-of-play (faceup cards in a player's play area are in play). Sources: https://rulespal.com/the-lord-of-the-rings-the-card-game-assault-on-osgiliath/rulebook ; https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/04/20/assault-on-osgiliath.
- `06091:back:2` (span-settled). "Explored" counts wherever progress reaches quest points, so a location explored in the staging area by Ancient Harbor / Old Bridge / Ruined Square also "leaves play as an explored location" and the first player takes control. Not taken: taking control only for the active location. Basis: rr.explored (progress >= quest points, placed in discard pile) ; forum report that one hero can win by progress on a staging-area Ancient Harbor https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/88236-assault-on-osgiliath-experiences/page/2 (community only, no ruling). Sources: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/88236-assault-on-osgiliath-experiences/page/2.
- `06091:back:2` (researched). Progress can still be placed on a controlled location; when it reaches its quest points the first player takes control again (progress removed, stays controlled) rather than discarding it. Not taken: controlled locations take no progress. Basis: FFG staff (Caleb) forum answer, 2013-09. Sources: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/90773-controlled-locations-an-official-response ; https://alongextendedparty.com/semi-official-errata-faq-1-10/ (item 3.099).
- `06091:back:2` (researched). A controlled location can be traveled to (e.g. West Gate / Ruined Tower travel text); travel moves it out of the player's area, so control is lost when it leaves his play area (progress removed). Not taken: cannot travel to a controlled location. Basis: FFG staff (Caleb): "I like your idea of traveling to a location you already control... the rules for the scenario do allow it. You would lose control of the location when it leaves your play area". Sources: same two URLs.
- `06091:back:2` (researched). Losing control (any return to staging): remove all progress from it, place it in the staging area; a player eliminated returns every Osgiliath location he controls to the staging area. Not taken: progress stays. Basis: INSERT text above. Sources: https://rulespal.com/the-lord-of-the-rings-the-card-game-assault-on-osgiliath/rulebook.
- `06091:back:2` (recommended). Control stays with the player who took it when the first-player token passes (no transfer at refresh; "takes control" is one event, "the player who controls X" on cards then names that player). Not taken: control follows the first-player token. Basis: no source says either; insert says "locations under any player's control" and "a player returns a location he controls"; Aragorn (mec112.aragorn) shows a card must say so to transfer. Sources: searched FFG archive threads above, VotP, HoB; none address token passing. Recommend: stays; confirm.
- `06091:back:2` (span-settled). When the first player is a player in multiplayer, only he gets locations; Uruk Soldier / Southron Phalanx / Counter-attack "each location that player controls" then bite the first player hardest. In a game where the first player is eliminated, locations return to staging (insert). Basis: INSERT. Sources: rulespal URL above.
- `06091:back:3` (recommended). Win check: "at the end of the round" = the end-of-round window after the refresh phase, all Osgiliath locations in play (staging, active and controlled) must be controlled, so any Osgiliath location in staging or active blocks the win; locations in the encounter deck/discard are out of play and do not count. Not taken: check right after combat (VotP gloss "after combat"). Basis: rr.in-play-and-out-of-play ; card text; VotP says "(after combat)" but is a blog gloss; forum users disagree on timing (https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/88236-assault-on-osgiliath-experiences/page/2, "Might need an official ruling"). Sources: VotP, the experiences thread; no official ruling found. Recommend end-of-round window per card text; confirm. Edge: zero Osgiliath locations in play at the check is vacuously a win, cannot arise after Setup.
