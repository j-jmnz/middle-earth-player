# The Blood of Gondor

Against the Shadow part 8 (pack `BoG`), standalone only, on Easy and Normal (Q03.8, E03.8). Encounter sets The Blood of Gondor and Ravaging Orcs (`ats.setup-the-blood-of-gondor`; Ravaging Orcs is shared with The Siege of Cair Andros and Encounter at Amon Dîn). Hidden cards are `ats.hidden-cards` and `ats.hidden-cards-turn-faceup`; the FAQ entry on hidden cards turned faceup is `faq.blood-of-gondor-hidden-cards`. Captured! (stage 2) has Battle (`hon.battle`).

- **Piles.** Hall of Beorn's text of stage 1A adds The Cross-roads and the Black Númenorean to the staging area and puts the objective allies Faramir and Lord Alcaron into play. The data: `staging` Black Númenorean (06119) and The Cross-roads (06123), `setAside` Faramir (06132) and Lord Alcaron (06133). DragnCards loads both allies in player 1's play area (`player1Play1` as `setAside` in `scripts/data/cycle-defs.ts`).
- **Errata.** The Ambush (`faq.errata.06117`, stage 1B: a choice between turning the hidden cards faceup and taking a hidden card) and The Cross-roads (`faq.errata.06123`: the current quest card gains Siege and loses Battle). The Read task reads each printed card against its erratum.
- **Rulesheet.** The hidden cards spans (`bog`) are read from the pinned rulesheet. The Quest Companion transcription and the community FAQ compendium the research also used are community material.

The readings of The Blood of Gondor use the statuses defined in the Readings bullet of `docs/scenarios/against-the-shadow.md`. The keys are the research keys, `<code>:<field>`. The lines are written from the source text, not from the scan-read ledger, so the Read task of The Blood of Gondor confirms each against its scan.

- `hidden.take` (researched). take = top of encounter deck to the taker's play area facedown, unseen; empty deck: shuffle the encounter discard pile into it first. Basis: QC Rulings > Hidden Cards (insert text). Sources: QC; visionofthepalantir (same empty-deck rule).
- `hidden.owner` (researched). a hidden card is in the taker's play area, owned by no one but counted per player; eliminated player's hidden cards are discarded. Not taken: keep them. Basis: QC insert text. Sources: QC.
- `hidden.turn-enemy` (researched). turned faceup one at a time; enemy engages that player immediately (one at a time, ignoring engagement cost); it is NOT "revealed" (no When Revealed, no surge, no staging). Not taken: treat as revealed. Basis: QC insert; UFAQ "enemies that are turned face-up are not 'revealed'" (official answer, French forum thread cited). Sources: QC; UFAQ; forum 234287.
- `hidden.turn-treachery-location` (researched). by default treachery and location turned faceup are discarded at once with no When Revealed and not added to staging. Not taken: resolve them. Basis: QC insert text. Sources: QC. The card text of Lying in Wait overrides this for its own effect (next rows).
- `hidden.new-card-during-turn` (span-settled). cards taken during a "turn each hidden card faceup" effect (Evil Crow engage) stay facedown; only the cards in front of the player at that moment are turned. Not taken: turn them too. Basis: faq.* span (the FAQ spans of data/rules/entries.json, Q "When playing The Blood of Gondor ... another hidden card"). Sources: the FAQ spans of data/rules/entries.json; QC.
- `hidden.order-vs-shadow` (researched). at beginning of combat, ambush-turned enemies and shadow dealing are simultaneous: first player chooses the order; may deal shadow cards first, then turn hidden cards faceup (so a newly engaged enemy gets no shadow card). Not taken: always turn first. Basis: UFAQ (official answer by FFG's Caleb, forum 96446, 21 Jan 2014). Sources: forum 96446; UFAQ.
- `06117:back` (span-settled). "must either turn each of his hidden cards faceup, or take 1 hidden card": a per-player choice made in player order at the beginning of the combat phase (Faramir's Combat Action cannot be used before the choice). Not taken: must do both (original misprint "turn ... or take" read as both). Basis: faq.errata.06117; official answer Caleb 13 Jan 2014 "Stage 1B is intended to be a choice". Sources: the FAQ spans of data/rules/entries.json; forum 96446. Hall of Beorn text already has the either/or wording (no comma), so the build may already read it; the printed card lacks "either" in the original print: check the scan.
- 06117:back (quest phase) (span-settled). each player takes 1 hidden card at the beginning of quest phase (2B has the same line). Basis: card text. Sources: card text.
- `06117:text` (span-settled). Setup: Crossroads and Black Númenorean to staging; Faramir and Lord Alcaron put into play, the first player gains control (their own text), then shuffle and reveal 1 per player. Not taken: gains control only at once. Basis: card text, Hall of Beorn. Sources: https://hallofbeorn.com/LotR/Details/The-Ambush-TBoG.
- 06117 combat ordering of 1B vs Archery/other beginning-of-combat effects (researched). simultaneous: first player orders. Basis: UFAQ same official answer. Sources: UFAQ.
- `06118:text` (researched). When Revealed: Faramir and Lord Alcaron are placed under Captured! (still in play; players have no control, so their abilities and bold text cannot be used; their leave-play loss text still applies if one would leave); then each player turns each hidden card faceup (enemies engage, treachery/location discarded). Not taken: they leave play (and lose the game). Basis: card text; visionofthepalantir: "stay in play under no player's control, so their bold text doesn't trigger". Sources: visionofthepalantir. Same mechanism as MEC109 Stage 4 span (mec109.stage-4: under a card = still in play, nobody controls it).
- `06118:back` (recommended). Battle; at beginning of quest phase each player takes 1 hidden card; a player who has 5 hidden cards must turn them faceup (checked as soon as he reaches 5, including a card taken during the round, e.g. Evil Crow, travel cost, shadow); 15 points ends the game as a win. Not taken: check only at the quest-phase beginning. Basis: card text; visionofthepalantir "Players must flip their Hidden cards when they receive a fifth". Sources: visionofthepalantir only (fan review); no official answer found in FAQ, UFAQ, forum 96446. Put in Readings to confirm.
- `06118 5-card turn-faceup` (researched). the 5 turned cards are turned faceup one at a time under the insert rules (enemies engage; the card that made the fifth is included). Basis: QC insert. Sources: QC.
- `06118 + 06123 Cross-roads` (span-settled). Captured! has Battle; The Cross-roads (in play) makes the current quest card gain Siege and lose Battle: players quest with Defense while Cross-roads is in play, not explored or removed. Not taken: both apply. Basis: faq.errata.06123 ("gains siege (and loses battle)"); visionofthepalantir. Sources: the FAQ spans of data/rules/entries.json; visionofthepalantir.
- 06132:text (Faramir) (recommended). "first player gains control" at setup (stage 1); Combat Action: exhaust to turn 1 hidden card faceup, where the card chosen is any hidden card in the controller's own play area (only first player is in position; see alternative). Not taken: any player's hidden card. Basis: card text; no ruling found on whose. Sources: forum 96446, 234287, UFAQ, visionofthepalantir say only "1 hidden card"; none says whose. Recommend: any hidden card in the game, chosen by the controller, as the card names no owner. Put in Readings to confirm. (Agent recommendation: in solo or one-hidden-card-per-owner cases both readings agree.).
- `06132:text enemy case` (researched). if the card is an enemy, Faramir deals 3 damage to it BEFORE it engages; if destroyed, it never engages; no When Revealed, and a destroyed Brutal Uruk's Forced does not trigger. Not taken: engage first, then damage. Basis: UFAQ official answer (sdajce forum, official): "Faramir will deal 3 damages to that enemy before it is considered to be engaged. If the enemy is destroyed by that effect, then it never engages." Non-enemy turned faceup follows the insert (discarded). Sources: UFAQ; forum 234287 (same quote); visionofthepalantir.
- 06132:text (leave play) (span-settled). "If Faramir leaves play, the players have lost the game": triggers on any leaving of play (death, discard, return); applies in stage 2 while under Captured!. Not taken: only in stage 1. Basis: card text. Sources: card text.
- 06133:text (Lord Alcaron) (span-settled). Action: exhaust to return an enemy engaged with you (the controller) to staging; it can be any enemy engaged with that player, including a just-engaged hidden-turned enemy. Not taken: any player's enemy. Basis: card text ("engaged with you"). Sources: card text; visionofthepalantir says "pick any enemy engaged with a player" (fan wording, not authority).
- 06133:text (leave play) (span-settled). players lose the game when he leaves play. Basis: card text. Sources: card text.
- `06132/06133 Orc Ambusher interaction` (span-settled). Orc Ambusher's discard: non-objective allies only, so Faramir and Alcaron are exempt. Basis: card text. Sources: card text.
- `06123:text The Cross-roads` (span-settled). official errata: "The current quest card gains siege (and loses battle)." Hall of Beorn text already reads this way (errata flag is set); the scan prints the erratum wording, with lowercase "siege" and "battle" and "X is equal to the number of players". Basis: faq.errata.06123. Sources: the FAQ spans of data/rules/entries.json.
- `06117:back The Ambush` (span-settled). errata above; the scan already prints "either" and the comma, so the ledger holds the printed wording. Basis: faq.errata.06117. Sources: https://hallofbeorn.com/LotR/Details/The-Ambush-TBoG (lists the errata, FAQ 1.06, 02/06/2014).
- `source typos` (recommended). HoB has "hiddien" (06119), "Brutual" (06121), "Easter Road" (06127), "destoys" (06130), "Easter", "a characters" (06130): the scans print "hidden", "Brutal Uruk", "Eastern Road" and "destroys a character": they are transcription slips, and the ledger holds the printed words. Basis: HoB data/raw/hallofbeorn/EncounterCards.json. Sources: no source gives the printed text; the scan read settles (not a user question).
- 06119:text Black Númenorean (span-settled). threat X = total hidden cards in the game (all players' play areas); Forced at end of round: each player raises threat by 1 per hidden card in his OWN play area; Victory 3 and Cross-roads Victory 3 are keywords in HoB data (not in the source text lines above). Basis: card text. Sources: HoB; the Nightmare version differs (extra Forced on engage), not this scenario.
- `06119 X` (researched). recompute as hidden cards change; hidden cards in an eliminated player's area are discarded so no longer counted. Basis: QC insert. Sources: QC.
- `06120:text Orc Ambusher` (span-settled). Forced after it engages you (including by hidden turnup): discard 1 non-objective ally you control, if able. Basis: card text. Sources: card text.
- `06120:shadowText` (span-settled). +1 Attack, or +3 Attack instead if the attack is undefended (not cumulative: 3, not 4). Not taken: +4. Basis: card text ("instead"). Sources: card text.
- `06121:text Brutal Uruk` (recommended). after it attacks and destroys a character, that character's controller turns all hidden cards in his play area faceup (one at a time, insert rules); each enemy turned faceup this way is dealt a shadow card and its attack is resolved as normal (it attacks that player right away, after Brutal Uruk's attack, before the next enemy attack; defender and shadow resolution as usual; engaged enemies already in the combat attack step are unaffected). Not taken: newly engaged enemies wait for the next combat phase. Basis: card text "resolve their attacks as normal". Sources: forum 96446, 234287, UFAQ, visionofthepalantir say nothing on this card. No source on order or on who defends. Recommend: attack order is the order they were turned, each resolved fully (one at a time) after Brutal Uruk's attack finishes; the shadow card is dealt per enemy and resolved at that attack. Readings to confirm.
- `06121 destroyed-character case` (span-settled). "destroys a character" includes a hero (the controller turns his hidden cards) and an ally; if the character was an ally and the controller is the first player (Faramir/Alcaron cannot be attacked: captured at stage 2 and not controlled). Basis: card text. Sources: card text.
- `06122:text Evil Crow` (span-settled). Forced when it engages a player: that player takes 1 hidden card (stays facedown even if the engage came from a "turn all faceup" effect). Not taken: turn it too. Basis: faq (the FAQ spans of data/rules/entries.json Q+A about Evil Crow). Sources: the FAQ spans of data/rules/entries.json.
- `06122:shadowText` (recommended). Shadow: the defending player takes 1 hidden card (the shadow effect applies to the defender; the card taken goes to the defending player's play area). Not taken: attacking enemy's controller. Basis: card text "Take 1 hidden card" with rr shadow rules: the player resolving the attack. Sources: none of the sources states the player; the defending player is the only player the attack is "against". Readings to confirm.
- `06128:text The Dark Woods` (recommended). Archery X where X = players; Forced after it leaves play as an explored location: first player discards 1 hidden card from his own play area (he chooses which: they are all facedown and unseen, so any card); the discarded card is turned up only as far as discard demands (it goes to the encounter discard pile; not turned faceup, so not engaged or resolved); if the first player has none, nothing. Not taken: the first player picks a card from any player. Basis: card text. Sources: no source found on a discarded hidden card being revealed or not. Recommend: discarded facedown, nothing resolves (discard is not "turn faceup"). Readings to confirm.
- `06128 archery span` (span-settled). Archery X per hon.archery span. Basis: hon.archery. Sources: data/rules.
- 06124..06127 Roads (Travel cost) (recommended). the first player must take hidden card(s) (1; Eastern Road 2) to travel here; he takes them as part of the travel cost; if the encounter deck must be reshuffled the insert rule applies; the players cannot travel to a road if the first player is unable (never unable, since deck reshuffles; if both deck and discard are empty he cannot). Not taken: pay in any order. Basis: rr travel rules; QC insert. Sources: rr.* travel span covers cost generally; no source on an empty deck and empty discard. Readings to confirm.
- `06125:text / 06127` (span-settled). Western and Eastern Road get +5 quest points while in the staging area (they are 4 and ? quest points otherwise; the bonus is not cumulative with being active). Basis: card text. Sources: card text.
- `06129:text Lying in Wait` (recommended). When Revealed: first player turns each hidden card in HIS play area faceup (one at a time; only his own); for each turned enemy, engage as usual; for each turned treachery, resolve its When Revealed by this card (not the discard-without-resolving default) then discard it; each turned location is added to the staging area (not discarded); enemies are not "revealed" so no When Revealed of enemies; the Lying in Wait text overrides the insert default for treachery and location. Not taken: treachery and location discarded without effects (insert default). Basis: card text "Resolve the 'When Revealed' effects of each encounter cards turned faceup ... If a location is turned faceup, add it to the staging area"; insert says otherwise but card text wins as the more specific text. Sources: forum 101651 (users alogos, Catastrophic09; non-official, they disagree on enemies: they resolve When Revealed and add locations, enemies per insert engage). No official answer; a card text overriding a rule default is the standing order (authority: card wins). Readings to confirm.
- `06129 surge on turned-faceup treachery` (recommended). Mordor Looms' Surge and Conflict's Surge do not fire when they are turned faceup by Lying in Wait (turned faceup is not revealed; surge says "revealed"; only the When Revealed effect resolves). Not taken: surge fires. Basis: rr.surge ("When an encounter card with the surge keyword is revealed ... reveal 1 additional encounter card"); UFAQ "enemies that are turned face-up are not revealed" (enemy-only). Sources: rr.surge text; no source on this exact case. Readings to confirm.
- `06129 Conflict at the Crossroads turned faceup` (recommended). When Revealed resolved in the current phase (the staging effects "until end of the phase"); its own surge self-gain applies only when there are no engaged Orcs and it is revealed normally; when turned by Lying in Wait it adds Orc threat to staging, nothing else. Basis: card text. Sources: same reasoning as the row above.
- `06129 location turned faceup order` (recommended). locations added to staging at the time each is turned; treachery When Revealed resolved right after it is turned, then the next hidden card. Not taken: all turned first, then effects. Basis: card text, insert "one at a time". Sources: QC insert ("one at a time"); no source on batching.
- `06130:text Mordor Looms` (span-settled). Surge; When Revealed: each player takes 1 hidden card (Surge resolves after, rr.surge/ltp.surge). Basis: card text; ltp.surge, rr.surge. Sources: data/rules.
- `06130:shadowText` (span-settled). Shadow: if this attack destroys a character, that character's controller turns each of his hidden cards faceup; resolved after the attack's damage; newly engaged enemies engage as the insert says (no shadow card unless Brutal's own text). Basis: card text. Sources: card text.
- `06131:text Conflict at the Crossroads` (span-settled). When Revealed: until the end of the phase, each Orc enemy engaged with a player adds its Threat to the staging area total (engaged enemies count toward the total for the quest phase's staging comparison); if none, it gains surge (so surge is checked when revealed, after the When Revealed is checked for orcs). Not taken: adds threat of enemies engaged with each player. Basis: card text. Sources: card text.
- `06131:shadowText` (recommended). Shadow: the defending player takes 1 hidden card. Not taken: attacker's controller. Basis: card text. Sources: same reason as 06122 shadow; none state it. Readings to confirm.
- 06134 (not present) (researched). Easy mode: remove 2 Conflict at the Crossroads, 2 Orc Ambusher, 2 Brutal Uruk, 3 Orc Rabble (Ravaging Orcs). Basis: quest companion transcription of Easy Mode. Sources: QC Difficulty level; data/rules Easy Mode sheet is the owner, check it.
- `06127 Eastern Road` (span-settled). the first player takes 2 hidden cards. Basis: card text. Sources: card text.
- `06126/06124 Roads` (span-settled). single hidden card travel cost. Basis: card text. Sources: card text.
