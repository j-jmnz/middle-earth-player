# The Morgul Vale

Against the Shadow part 9 (pack `TMV`, MEC23), standalone only, on Easy and Normal (Q03.9, E03.9). Encounter sets The Morgul Vale, Mordor Elite and Creatures of the Forest (`ats.setup-the-morgul-vale`; the last two are from Heirs of Númenor, shared with the scenarios named in `docs/scenarios/against-the-shadow.md`). Captain enemies are `ats.captain-enemies`: each of the three stages has a Captain that must be destroyed to advance, and the stage's own line stops it leaving play any other way. The To the Tower objective and the removal of Faramir are `ats.setup-the-morgul-vale-to-the-tower`.

- **Piles.** The data: `staging` To the Tower (06147), Murzag (06151) and Morgul Vale (06155), `setAside` the Captains Lord Alcaron (06152) and Nazgûl of Minas Morgul (06153) and The White Bridge (06156). They are set aside as the DragnCards list Q03.9 has them; the Read task checks each against its stage's text. No override.
- **Errata.** Impenetrable Fog (`faq.errata.06157`): the Read task reads the printed card against it.

## Readings

The readings of The Morgul Vale use the statuses defined in the Readings bullet of `docs/scenarios/against-the-shadow.md`. The keys are the research keys, `<code>:<field>`. The lines are written from the source text, not from the scan-read ledger, so the Read task of The Morgul Vale confirms each against its scan.

Not taken: Captain may leave play by effects and stage still advances. Basis: MEC23 insert "Captain Enemies" (card text printed on the quest card wins).
