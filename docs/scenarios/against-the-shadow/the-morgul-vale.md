# The Morgul Vale

Against the Shadow part 9 (pack `TMV`, MEC23), standalone only, on Easy and Normal (Q03.9, E03.9). Encounter sets The Morgul Vale, Mordor Elite and Creatures of the Forest (`ats.setup-the-morgul-vale`; the last two are from Heirs of Númenor and are read in `the-siege-of-cair-andros.md` and `into-ithilien.md`). Each of the three stages has a Captain that the players must destroy to advance, and the stage's own line keeps it in play until then (`ats.captain-enemies`). The To the Tower objective and the removal of Faramir are `ats.setup-the-morgul-vale-to-the-tower`.

## Data

- **Piles.** The data: `staging` To the Tower (06147), Murzag (06151) and one Morgul Vale (06155); `setAside` Lord Alcaron (06152), Nazgûl of Minas Morgul (06153) and The White Bridge (06156); `encounter` the other two copies of Morgul Vale. These are the DragnCards lists Q03.9 and E03.9 and match the Setup line of stage 1A. No override.
- **Printed lines.** The Readings are keyed by the scan-read ledger's line ids (`data/printed/against-the-shadow.json`). The Setup line of 06144 prints "Add To the Tower, Murzag, and 1 copy of Morgul Vale to the staging area", with no search.
- **Quest points.** The three stages print no quest points, so no progress can complete a stage (`06144:B:1`).
- **The White Bridge.** The scan (06156 A) prints Threat X (the banner) and quest points X (the orange X), and the one line "X is the number of progress tokens on To the Tower." The data holds no Threat for it and `questPointsX`, as it holds no Threat for The Cross-roads (06123, Threat X); a record sets both numbers. No override.
- **Errata.** Impenetrable Fog (`faq.errata.06157`): the scan prints "either places 3 progress tokens ... or reveals" with no comma before "or reveals", so the ledger holds the printed wording.
- **Source typos.** The printed capital in "To The Tower" (06158:A:2) and the apostrophe in "each player's threat" (06160:A:1) are kept in the ledger.
- **Shared cards.** Mordor Elite (05056 to 05061) and Creatures of the Forest (05049 to 05051) keep the readings of their own files.

## Readings

### Setup and the stages

- `06144:A:1` (set aside) (span-settled). Lord Alcaron, Nazgûl of Minas Morgul and The White Bridge are set aside: out of play, not in the encounter deck, not revealed, targeted or damaged until a stage text adds them. Not taken: they stay in the encounter deck. Basis: `rr.in-play-and-out-of-play`; `ats.captain-enemies` ("a corresponding Captain enemy for each stage").
- `06144:A:1` (add) (span-settled). "Add" is not "reveal": To the Tower, Murzag and one Morgul Vale enter the staging area at Setup with no When Revealed, no keyword and no shadow, before the quest card turns to side B. The piles place them. Not taken: reveal them. Basis: `rr.setup`; `rr.staging`; `faq.reveal-and-add`.
- `06144:A:1` (To the Tower) (span-settled). To the Tower is searched out of the encounter deck and added to the staging area; the piles place it, and the encounter deck is shuffled at Setup as usual. Not taken: a separate card. Basis: `ats.setup-the-morgul-vale-to-the-tower`; `faq.search-effects`.
- `06144:A:1` (the copy of Morgul Vale) (researched). The "1 copy" is one of the 3 copies of the set, taken from the encounter deck into the staging area (staging Threat after Setup: Murzag 2 and Morgul Vale 3, as the blog counts). Not taken: a fourth card from outside the set. Basis: the pile data (2 copies in the encounter pile, 1 in the staging pile). Sources: https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/05/07/the-morgul-vale/ https://hallofbeorn.com/LotR/Details/The-Rearguard-TMV.
- `06144:B:1` (cannot leave play) (span-settled). While stage 1 is current, Murzag cannot be shuffled into the encounter deck, returned to a deck or hand, discarded or removed from the game; only destruction takes him out of play. Moving between the staging area and an engagement is not leaving play. Not taken: effects still shuffle or discard him. Basis: `ats.captain-enemies`; `rr.cannot`; the MEC23 rules sheet. Sources: https://hallofbeorn-resources.s3.amazonaws.com/Images/LotR/Rules/MEC23.pdf.
- `06144:B:1` (defeated) (researched). "Defeated" means destroyed (damage at or above hit points): the Captain goes to the encounter discard pile, with no victory display, and after a reshuffle he is an ordinary card again once his stage has passed. Not taken: removed from the game on defeat. Basis: `rr.destroy`; `rr.victory`; the blog ("Murzag isn't added to the victory display"); thread 105253 (GrandSpleen). Sources: https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/05/07/the-morgul-vale/ https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/105253-morgul-vale-help/ https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/93961-nazg%C3%BBl-of-minas-morgul.
- `06144:B:1` (advance) (researched). The stage advances as soon as Murzag is destroyed, before any Response to the destruction; the next stage's side A text ("add Lord Alcaron") resolves first and the Responses stay available after it. Not taken: Responses first, advance at the end of the window. Basis: `rr.quest-advancement`; community (PocketWraith: advancing "interrupts and supersedes everything"; cmabr002 agrees the window stays open). Sources: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/199062-tactagorn-morgul-vale.
- `06144:B:1` (no quest points) (recommended). The stages print no quest points (data `questPoints` null), so progress placed on the quest never advances a stage; only the Captain's destruction does. The quest resolution still runs and progress still goes to an active location first. Not taken: treat the missing points as 0 and advance at once. Basis: `rr.quest-advancement` (a stage needs quest points); `ats.captain-enemies`. Sources: https://hallofbeorn.com/LotR/Details/The-Rearguard-TMV (shows no points; no source discusses progress on a stage with none).
- `06145:A:1` (span-settled). When stage 2 is revealed, Lord Alcaron (set aside) is added to the staging area, not revealed. His engagement cost is 10, so at the next engagement check (`rr.engagement-check`, in the encounter phase) every player with threat 10 or more is checked and he engages the first player in order who qualifies; a player may engage him earlier at the optional engagement step. A stage that advances during combat leaves him in the staging area until the next round. Not taken: he engages at once. Basis: `rr.engagement-check`; `rr.optional-engagement`. Sources: https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/05/07/the-morgul-vale/ (the blog says "combat phase"; the rules put engagement checks in the encounter phase).
- `06145:B:1` (span-settled). As 06144:B:1 for Lord Alcaron; his destruction advances to stage 3. Basis: `ats.captain-enemies`.
- `06146:A:1` (span-settled). Nazgûl of Minas Morgul (engagement cost 1, so he engages at the next engagement check) and The White Bridge are added to the staging area, not revealed. Not taken: revealed. Basis: `rr.engagement-check`; `ats.captain-enemies`. Sources: https://hallofbeorn.com/LotR/Details/Nazgul-of-Minas-Morgul-TMV.
- `06146:B:1` (timing) (span-settled). A new stage follows side A (add the Nazgûl and The White Bridge), then flips to side B, and the back's When Revealed resolves at once. Not taken: it waits for the quest phase. Basis: `ltp.quest-advancement`.
- `06146:B:1` (cards revealed) (span-settled). Each of the 1 card per player is revealed in full (`rr.reveal`, `faq.reveal-and-add`): When Revealed and keywords resolve, then an enemy, location or objective goes to the staging area (an enemy is not engaged) and a treachery goes to the discard pile. Not taken: revealed enemies engage. Basis: `rr.reveal`; `rr.staging`; `faq.reveal-and-add`.
- `06146:B:2` (span-settled). The Nazgûl cannot leave play unless destroyed, as Murzag at stage 1. Basis: `ats.captain-enemies`; `rr.cannot`.
- `06146:B:3` (span-settled). When the Nazgûl is destroyed the players win at once, with no wait for the end of the round; no stage follows. Basis: the printed line; `ats.captain-enemies` ("To advance from each stage and win the game, the players must defeat each Captain"). Sources: https://hallofbeorn-resources.s3.amazonaws.com/Images/LotR/Rules/MEC23.pdf.

### To the Tower and The White Bridge

- `06147:A:1` (researched). To the Tower stays in the staging area all game: it cannot be shuffled, returned, discarded or taken by a player; it is an objective with no stats and is never engaged. It counts as an encounter card in the staging area for The Power of Mordor's count, and is not shuffled. Not taken: it leaves play as other objectives do. Basis: `rr.cannot`; the blog ("immune to The Power of Mordor, but still counts toward the encounter cards needed in the staging area"). Sources: https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/05/07/the-morgul-vale/ https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/126185-question-about-murzag.
- `06147:A:2` (every Faramir) (span-settled). Every card named Faramir that the players own (the hero 06081 and 144002, the ally 01014 and 21005) is removed from the game when To the Tower enters play at Setup, whether in a deck, a hand, a discard pile or in play; none can be used later. Not taken: only a Faramir in play. Basis: `ats.setup-the-morgul-vale-to-the-tower` ("no version of Faramir can be used by the players"); `rr.removed-from-game`. Sources: https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/05/07/the-morgul-vale/.
- `06147:A:3` (zone) (span-settled). The loss holds for To the Tower lying in the staging area, where its own line keeps it (`06147:A:1`); the record's self test names the zone (`inStaging`), because the engine's `matches` test reads none. Not taken: ten tokens on a card of that title anywhere in play. Basis: `06147:A:1`; `rr.in-play-and-out-of-play`.
- `06147:A:2` (threat) (recommended). The removal comes at Setup after the heroes are placed and the threat dial is set (`rr.setup`): a removed Faramir hero keeps its threat on the dial, and a seat left with no hero is eliminated. Not taken: recompute the starting threat, or refuse the deck. Basis: the order of `rr.setup`. Sources: https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/05/07/the-morgul-vale/ (advises against bringing him; no source speaks of the dial).
- `06147:A:3` (researched). The loss is immediate when the tenth token is placed (a state check, "10 or more"), after every placement: Impenetrable Fog's 3 tokens and tokens placed during an attack included. Not taken: checked at the end of the round. Basis: the printed line; the blog ("when the tenth progress token is placed on this card, the players have lost the game"). Sources: https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/05/07/the-morgul-vale/.
- Tokens "on To the Tower" (span-settled). They go on that objective and never on the active location or the quest. Not taken: redirected to the active location. Basis: `faq.legolas-active-location` (it covers only progress on the quest).
- `06156:A:1` (researched). X is the number of progress tokens on To the Tower, counted live: the Threat and the quest points of The White Bridge move as tokens are added or removed. Not taken: X fixed when the card is revealed. Basis: `rr.modifiers`; the scan (Threat X and quest points X); the blog. Sources: https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/05/07/the-morgul-vale/ https://hallofbeorn.com/LotR/Details/The-White-Bridge-TMV.
- `06156:A:1` (no token) (recommended). With no token on To the Tower The White Bridge has Threat 0 and 0 quest points: staged, it adds nothing to the staging area, and as the active location no progress goes on it (it has no room), so it stays active until a token makes its quest points at least 1 and the next progress explores it; the progress goes to the stage, which never advances by progress. Not taken: a location with 0 quest points is explored at once. Basis: `rr.explored` (progress equal to the quest points); no source speaks of X = 0. Sources: https://hallofbeorn.com/LotR/Details/The-White-Bridge-TMV.

### Morgul Bodyguard

- `06148:A:1` (redirect first) (researched). When damage would be dealt to a Captain, the whole amount is placed on 1 Morgul Bodyguard instead; the Nazgûl's reduction to 1 does not apply to the Bodyguard, because the damage is never dealt to the Nazgûl. Not taken: reduce to 1 first, then redirect. Basis: the printed line; `rr.damage`; thread 110883 (alogos, PsychoRocka and richsabre agree; GrandSpleen and Saramund leave it open) and the blog ("doesn't lower the damage dealt to the Bodyguards by 1"). Sources: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/110883-nazgul-of-minas-mogrul-damage-reduction-bodyguard https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/05/07/the-morgul-vale/.
- `06148:A:1` (moved and placed damage) (researched). Damage moved or placed onto a Captain (Infighting, effects that place damage) counts as dealt and also goes to a Bodyguard. Not taken: moved damage bypasses the Bodyguard. Basis: the designer (Caleb, relayed by GrandSpleen): "Any time you would place damage on an enemy, that damage is 'dealt' to that enemy". Sources: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/93961-nazg%C3%BBl-of-minas-morgul.
- `06148:A:1` (amount) (span-settled). The amount is fixed first (combat damage is the attack less the Captain's Defense, `rr.damage`) and then assigned to the Bodyguard; the Bodyguard's own Defense does not reduce it. Not taken: the Bodyguard's Defense applies. Basis: `rr.damage`; the blog ("ignoring its defence"). Sources: https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/05/07/the-morgul-vale/.
- `06148:A:1` (which Bodyguard, spill) (recommended). One damage instance goes whole to one Bodyguard and any excess over its 6 hit points is lost, not passed on; the engine picks the Bodyguard that holds the most damage (the first in play on a tie), because a choice in the middle of a damage step has no place to be asked. Not taken: the player whose effect deals the damage picks it; the Captain takes the excess. Basis: no source speaks of the chooser or the excess. Sources: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/110883-nazgul-of-minas-mogrul-damage-reduction-bodyguard.
- `06148:A:1` (no Bodyguard) (span-settled). The line is a constant of a Bodyguard in play (staging area or engaged): with none in play the Captain takes the damage normally. Not taken: the damage is lost. Basis: `rr.in-play-and-out-of-play`.
- `06148:A:2` (two or more players) (researched). The attacking enemy engages the next player (`rr.in-player-order`) and makes an immediate attack against that player: the current attack ends there, its damage does not resolve and its other shadow cards are discarded when the new card is dealt. Not taken: both attacks resolve. Basis: the designer (Caleb, relayed by Narsil0420): "The effect ends the current attack and immediately begins a new one"; `faq.additional-attacks`. Later posters disagree (PsychoRocka plays both attacks, Laco cites FAQ 1.7). Sources: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/94076-morgul-bodyguard-shadow-effect.
- `06148:A:2` (one player, "if able") (researched). With one player there is no next player, so the enemy is not able: the shadow effect does nothing and the attack resolves normally. Not taken: the enemy attacks the same player again. Basis: Caleb, relayed ("If there is no other player for the enemy to attack, then it is not able"); `faq.next-player`. Sources: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/94076-morgul-bodyguard-shadow-effect.
- `06148:A:2` (eliminated player) (span-settled). The next player is the next player still in the game: an eliminated player is not counted and not engaged (`faq.next-player`, `rr.in-player-order`). The app has one or two seats, so with two players and the other one eliminated there is no next player, the shadow effect does nothing and the attack resolves normally. Not taken: the enemy engages the eliminated seat or the attack is cancelled. Basis: `faq.next-player`; `docs/brief.md`, Core round (`seatCount` is 1 or 2).
- `06148:A:2` (the immediate attack) (span-settled). The immediate attack is dealt its own shadow card at its start and the enemy stays engaged with the next player. Not taken: it returns to the first player after. Basis: `faq.enemy-attacks-outside-combat`; `faq.additional-attacks`; `rr.engaged`. Sources: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/131389-questions-that-need-to-be-answered.
- `06148:A:2` (Forced "after attacks") (recommended). Murzag's and Lord Alcaron's Forced effects fire for the immediate attack, since each attack that begins is an attack; the interrupted first attack counts as an attack for "when" and its "after" fires as it ends. Not taken: only the immediate attack counts. Basis: no source covers the interaction. Sources: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/94076-morgul-bodyguard-shadow-effect.

### Morgul Sorcerer and Morgul Tracker

- `06149:A:1` (one card per placement) (span-settled). The Sorcerer is dealt one shadow card for each placement, whatever the number of tokens ("any number"): Impenetrable Fog's 3 tokens give one card. Not taken: one card per token. Basis: the printed line; the blog says "each time a progress token is placed", the printed words win. Sources: https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/05/07/the-morgul-vale/.
- `06149:A:1` (in the staging area) (span-settled). A Sorcerer in the staging area is in play and is dealt the card; it keeps it until it attacks, in addition to the card dealt at the start of combat. Not taken: only an engaged Sorcerer. Basis: `rr.in-play-and-out-of-play`; `faq.enemy-attacks-outside-combat`; the user ruling in `docs/scenarios/dream-chaser.md` (a staged enemy is dealt a shadow card like an engaged one).
- `06149:A:1` (several Sorcerers) (span-settled). Each Sorcerer triggers and the first player orders them. Basis: `rr.forced`; `rr.timing-of-ability-resolution`.
- `06149:A:1` (its own shadow) (recommended). Tokens placed by the Sorcerer's own shadow effect trigger it and the other Sorcerers: the card dealt during the attack stays facedown for the next attack and does not resolve in this one. Not taken: it resolves at once. Basis: nothing found on a card dealt in the middle of an attack. Sources: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/131389-questions-that-need-to-be-answered.
- `06149:A:2` (timing) (span-settled). The shadow resolves at step 6.4.2, after the defender is declared: the defending player places 1 token on To the Tower, or the attack is treated as undefended: a declared defender stays exhausted and takes no damage, and all the damage goes to one hero the defending player picks (`rr.step-6-4-3`). Not taken: the choice before the defender is declared. Basis: `rr.step-6-4-1`; `rr.step-6-4-2`; `rr.step-6-4-3`. Sources: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/131389-questions-that-need-to-be-answered.
- `06149:A:2` (already undefended) (recommended). If the attack is already undefended the player is not asked and places no token. Not taken: he must place the token. Basis: no source found; "treat as undefended" is already true.
- `06150:A:1` (span-settled). The Forced effect triggers only when the player optionally engages the Tracker (`rr.optional-engagement`), not by an engagement check or by an effect that engages it. Not taken: any engagement. Basis: `rr.optional-engagement`; the printed line.
- `06150:A:2` (span-settled). +1 Attack for this attack; if undefended when the shadow resolves (no defender declared), 1 token is placed whatever the damage. Not taken: a token only if damage is dealt. Basis: `rr.step-6-4-1`; `rr.step-6-4-2`; the printed line.

### Murzag, Lord Alcaron, the Nazgûl

- `06151:A:1` (reveal) (researched). "Reveal" is a full reveal (`rr.reveal`): keywords and When Revealed resolve, a treachery is discarded, a location goes to the staging area and an enemy is put into play engaged with the defending player, not staged; it attacks in that phase's enemy attack step. Not taken: non-enemy cards are discarded unseen. Basis: Caleb, relayed twice ("reveal and add to the staging area is just long-hand for reveal"); `faq.reveal-and-add`. Sources: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/94687-morgul-vale-captain-murzag-forced-effect-question https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/126185-question-about-murzag.
- `06151:A:1` (shadow card) (researched). The enemy put into play is not dealt a shadow card for this phase's attack (shadow cards are dealt at the start of combat). Not taken: it is dealt one. Basis: Caleb, relayed ("it will not be dealt a shadow card"). Sources: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/94687-morgul-vale-captain-murzag-forced-effect-question.
- `06151:A:1` (defending player) (researched). "Defending player" is the player Murzag attacked, also when another player's Sentinel character defended. Not taken: the defender's controller. Basis: community only (Teamjimby). Sources: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/126185-question-about-murzag.
- `06151:A:1` (no attack, empty deck) (span-settled). A Murzag whose attack never starts (Feint, "cannot attack") triggers nothing; one that starts an attack triggers after it ends, also when the shadow card turned the attack undefended. The encounter discard pile is shuffled into an empty deck in the quest phase only, so Murzag's attack in the combat phase with an empty deck reveals nothing, and an attack made in the quest phase shuffles it first. Not taken: the discard pile is shuffled in any phase; it triggers on a canceled attack. Basis: the Feint text; `rr.forced`; `rr.running-out-of-cards` ("during the quest phase").
- `06151:A:1` (attacked player eliminated) (recommended). When Murzag's attack eliminates the player attacked, the printed line still reveals the top card, for the first living seat: an enemy goes to the staging area with no engagement and no attack (no player is left to engage, and an eliminated player's enemies return to the staging area: `rr.player-elimination`), a location goes to the staging area and a treachery resolves and is discarded, and its Surge resolves. Not taken: nothing is revealed. Basis: the printed line has no condition on the player; no official ruling or discussion found on this case. Sources read: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/126185-question-about-murzag https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/94687-morgul-vale-captain-murzag-forced-effect-question https://www.fantasyflightgames.com/media/ffg_content/lotr-lcg/support/LotR-FAQ.pdf.
- `06152:A:1` (each attack) (researched). A token is placed at the start of each attack he begins, before the shadow card; each additional and each immediate attack places another. Not taken: one token per round. Basis: the printed line; the blog ("each time he attacks"); thread 266088 (NathanH). Sources: https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/05/07/the-morgul-vale/ https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/266088-lord-alcaron-forced-effect.
- `06152:A:1` (Feint, canceled) (span-settled). A Feinted Alcaron makes no attack, so no token. A token already placed when the attack starts stays if the attack is stopped later (recommended). Not taken: a token only if the attack completes. Basis: the Feint text; the printed line "when ... attacks"; no source on canceling after the start. Sources: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/105253-morgul-vale-help/.
- `06152:A:2` (trigger) (researched). The condition is checked when the shadow card is flipped at step 6.4.2: a card with no shadow effect (also one with no Shadow line) triggers the additional attack; a card with a shadow effect, even a canceled one, does not. Not taken: checked when the card is dealt facedown. Basis: the printed line; community (GrandSpleen: his Forced text triggers off the shadow card). Sources: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/105253-morgul-vale-help/.
- `06152:A:2` (chain) (researched). The additional attack follows this attack's end: the old shadow cards are discarded and a new one is dealt and resolved (`faq.additional-attacks`); each new blank card gives another attack with no cap, and each attack places a token (`06152:A:1`). Not taken: one extra attack. Basis: `faq.additional-attacks`; NathanH ("Yes, he keeps making attacks"); PocketWraith (old cards discarded). Sources: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/266088-lord-alcaron-forced-effect https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/194002-do-additional-attacks-receive-shadow-cards.
- `06152:A:2` (Feint) (researched). A Feinted Alcaron is never dealt a shadow card for the attack, so the Forced effect does not fire. Not taken: it fires. Basis: community only (GrandSpleen). Sources: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/105253-morgul-vale-help/.
- `06152:A:2` (several shadow cards) (recommended). Each blank card dealt to him is its own trigger: with extra shadow cards (Orc Vanguard) each blank card gives one more additional attack, made one after another, each dealt a shadow card of its own, and an additional attack whose own card is blank gives another on top of those. Not taken: one extra attack in all. Basis: `rr.forced`; the orchestrator's decision to follow the recorded reading; no source found.
- `06153:A:1` (span-settled). No attachment can attach to the Nazgûl, from a player or from the encounter deck: an attachment played or put into play on him is refused or discarded. Not taken: it attaches and does nothing. Basis: `rr.attachment-cards`; `rr.cannot`. Sources: https://hallofbeorn.com/LotR/Details/Nazgul-of-Minas-Morgul-TMV.
- `06153:A:2` (per instance) (researched). Each separate damage instance dealt to him is cut to 1 (one attack, one Hail of Stones, one Infighting each 1), with no limit per round. Not taken: 1 damage a round. Basis: community, clear and agreed (Spurries, ronaldo24: the text has no "round"); the blog. Sources: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/93961-nazg%C3%BBl-of-minas-morgul.
- `06153:A:2` (moved and placed) (researched). Damage moved or placed on him is dealt and is reduced to 1. Not taken: moved damage is not reduced. Basis: Caleb, relayed by GrandSpleen. Sources: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/93961-nazg%C3%BBl-of-minas-morgul.

## Engine notes

The new verbs and flags are in the family `pelargir` (`src/catalog/families/pelargir.ts`, `src/rules/families/pelargir/vale/` and the files of the family named below; the render templates are in `scripts/data/effects/render/families/pelargir.ts`).

- **Progress by title** (`06149:A:2`, `06150:A:2`, `06152:A:1`, `06155:A:2`, `06157:A:1`, `06158:A:2`, `06159:A:2`). `PlaceProgressOnNamed` places the tokens on the card in play with the title, the staging area included, through the engine's own placement, so the tokens of one effect are one placement (`progressPlaced` is announced once, as `06149:A:1` and `06147:A:3` read it). The amounts read the tokens live with `{ stat: "progress", ofNamed }`.
- **The loss at 10** (`06147:A:3`). A Forced effect on `progressPlaced` with To the Tower as the subject and a condition on its progress, scoped to the staging area. It fires after each placement, so Impenetrable Fog's 3 tokens count as one.
- **Faramir** (`06147:A:2`). The Setup record picks To the Tower and announces it as added to the staging area (`AnnounceAddedToStaging`: the piles place the cards, so nothing announces them); its own Forced effect runs `RemoveAllNamedFromGame`: each player card with the title, in a deck, a hand, a discard pile or play, goes to the zone `removed` with its attachments and no leaving of play is announced. The threat dial is not touched.
- **Cannot leave play** (`06144:B:1`, `06145:B:1`, `06146:B:2`). The stage's constant gives the Captain `cannotLeavePlay` (holder by name). Three guards follow the rule that a player is never offered a target whose effect cannot happen. A Pick (or an Each) whose following instructions take the card out of play, with no destruction, leaves a Captain out (`leavesPlayPicked`; the verbs it counts are in `takesPickedOut`, `src/rules/families/pelargir/vale/cannot-leave.ts`), and an ability with no other target is not offered (`src/rules/interpret/pick.ts`, `effect-frames.ts`, `src/rules/player/potential.ts`). The staging area shuffled into the encounter deck (`05061:A:2`) counts the Captain and To the Tower and moves neither (`staging.ts`: `moveEncounter` would clear the tokens on To the Tower before `moveCard` refused it). A settle sweep (`captainKept`) puts the current stage's Captain back in the staging area when it lies out of play and no `destroy` step of the intent names it; it waits while an attack or a strike is pending or the stage is cleared. Returning a Captain to the staging area, or engaging it, is not leaving play. To the Tower holds `cannotLeaveStaging`, which `moveCard` refuses.
- **The advance** (`06144:B:1`, `06145:B:1`, `06146:B:3`). A Forced effect on `enemyDestroyed` with the Captain as the subject advances the quest, so the stage advances before any Response to the destruction; the win of the last stage is a triggered effect. The stages print no quest points, so a quest that succeeds puts its progress on the stage and never advances it.
- **The Bodyguard** (`06148:A:1`). A constant of the Bodyguard gives each Captain, staged or engaged, `shieldedByBodyguard`; `damageGoesTo` (`src/rules/families/defense/damage.ts`) sends the damage assigned to such a card to the Bodyguard in play with the most damage, the first in play on a tie, before the Captain's own limits apply, whatever the source (combat, effects, moved and placed damage). The Nazgûl's reduction (`damageLimit` 1) therefore never touches the damage a Bodyguard takes. A Bodyguard that takes more than its hit points is destroyed and the rest is lost.
- **The attack switched** (`06148:A:2`). The Shadow record marks the attacker `attackSwitched` until the end of the attack and leaves, for the end of the attack, `EngageNextPlayer` (announced as an engagement) and `ImmediateAttack`, each only with another player in the game and the attacker engaged. `resolveAttack` (`src/rules/combat/shadow.ts`) ends the attack at once when the enemy holds the flag: no damage, no shadow card still facedown resolves, and the new attack is dealt a card of its own, which discards them. The first attack uses up the enemy's attack of the round; the immediate attack does not.
- **Murzag** (`06151:A:1`). `RevealTopEngaging` runs the engine's reveal frame for the player attacked (the seat of the `attackResolved` event, so also when a Sentinel of another player defended) with the frame's `engage`: an enemy is engaged with that player and dealt no shadow card, and attacks in the same combat phase; a location goes to the staging area, a treachery is resolved and discarded. The extra card a surge reveals is staged as any other. When the attack eliminated the player attacked, the frame runs for the first living seat without `engage`: the enemy goes to the staging area and the Surge resolves.
- **Impenetrable Fog's reveal** (`06157:A:1`). The option "reveal" requires the condition `encounterCanReveal` (read by the pelargir family, `conditions.ts`): the deck holds a card, or it is the quest phase and the discard pile can reset the deck. With none the first player has only the tokens, and the engine offers no choice.
- **Alcaron** (`06152:A:1`, `06152:A:2`). `attacks` places a token for each attack he begins, immediate and additional attacks included; `attackResolved` with a blank shadow card makes `AttackPerBlankShadow`: one immediate attack for each blank card the attack was dealt (counted when the attack ends, before the next attack discards them), one after another, each dealt a card of its own (`families/pelargir/vale/additional.ts`). The verb begins the first attack and leaves itself, with the attacks that remain, for the end of that attack; an attack of the enemy already in progress takes them after it.
- **The surge chain** (`06160:A:1`, `06159:A:1`). The `deckReset` hook of the family (`surge-chain.ts`) holds the reset when the deck is empty and every card of the discard pile is a card whose reveal is still open, in a game that has To the Tower (the check reads the records of the cards in the game), unless Terror Drives Them is in the pile. Dead City Looms with no token as the last card therefore ends its chain; Terror Drives Them places a token on each pass and the chain ends in the loss at the tenth.
- **The White Bridge** (`06156:A:1`). Its constant sets Threat and quest points from the tokens, as The Cross-roads sets its Threat; the card prints no Threat, and the modifier applies in the staging area and as the active location.

## Limits

- **Cannot leave play, an effect with no pick.** An effect that takes no Pick and moves a Captain out of play (a discard of "the attacking enemy", a sweep of every enemy in play other than the staging shuffle) is undone by the sweep, not stopped: the Captain returns to the staging area, face up, controlled by nobody. What it lost on the way is not restored: its damage and tokens, its attachments, its shadow cards, and the Forced and Response effects that heard it leave play. A Pick followed by a conditional leaving instruction (an instruction with a `when`) does not leave the Captain out either; the sweep undoes that case too.
- The Bodyguard is picked by the engine (the most damaged), not by the players.
- A set-aside Captain, Lord Alcaron and the Nazgûl, is out of play until its stage adds it; the sweep never restores a set-aside card.
- A seat left with no hero after the removal of Faramir is eliminated by the engine's own rule.
