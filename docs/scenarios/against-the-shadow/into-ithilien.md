# Into Ithilien

Against the Shadow part 2 (pack `HoN`), standalone only, on Easy and Normal (Q03.2, E03.2). Encounter sets Into Ithilien, Brooding Forest, Creatures of the Forest and Southrons (`ats.setup-into-ithilien`). Brooding Forest is shared with The Drúadan Forest, Creatures of the Forest with The Morgul Vale, Southrons with The Siege of Cair Andros and Assault on Osgiliath. The ally objective rules (Celador) are `ats.ally-objectives`; Archery is `hon.archery`.

- **Piles.** Stage 1A's Setup adds Celador to the staging area, makes Ithilien Road the active location and has each player search for a copy of Southron Company and add it to the staging area. The data stages Celador (05025) and the four Southron Company (05027), and makes Ithilien Road (05028) the active location. Override in `data/overrides.json`: Southron Company is `staging` with 4 copies in both modes, one for each of up to four players; DragnCards' Q03.2 stages all 4 and E03.2 stages 1 and leaves 3 in the encounter deck. The normalizer drops the Easy encounter copies a per-player Setup stages (`stagedPerPlayer`, `scripts/data/normalize/scenario-piles.ts`).
- **Errata.** Blocking Wargs (`faq.errata.05029`): the printed card is read against the erratum by the Read task.

The readings of Into Ithilien use the statuses defined in the Readings bullet of `docs/scenarios/against-the-shadow.md`. The keys are the research keys, `<code>:<field>`. The lines are written from the source text, not from the scan-read ledger, so the Read task of Into Ithilien confirms each against its scan.

- `05025:text:a` (researched). Celador is an ally objective: a character AND an objective; in the staging area he counts his stats (willpower, or attack under Battle, defense under Siege) for quest resolution, is "committed" for every effect that reads "character committed to the quest", and no player controls him there. Not taken: He is an objective only (no stats, not hit by character-committed effects). Basis: `ats.ally-objectives`: "An ally objective card is considered to be both an objective and an ally... commits it to the quest when it is in the staging area... count their stats and assist the players when resolving a quest. Any card effect that affects characters committed to the quest can also affect these ally objective cards.". Sources: `ats.ally-objectives`; FFGFORUM-CEL; VOTP.
- `05025:text:b` (researched). While in the staging area Celador cannot attack, defend, exhaust for costs (not controlled by a player); he can be damaged and healed by effects that do not need control of the target. Not taken: Players may exhaust or use him in the staging area. Basis: `ats.ally-objectives` (commit by text only); FFGFORUM-CEL (community, one answer, accepted by the asker: "you do not control him until you reach the third quest stage"). Sources: FFGFORUM-CEL (community only, clear; not official).
- `05025:text:c` (recommended). Forced trigger "a character leaves play": any character (any player's hero or ally, also an Ithilien Guardian, not enemies/objective non-allies) leaving play deals 1 damage to Celador; one trigger per character that leaves play; "players quest unsuccessfully" = quest resolution where total threat exceeds total committed strength (rr.step-3-4); tie or zero progress with equal values is not unsuccessful. Not taken: "A character" excludes allies Celador himself (when he leaves, the next clause removes him anyway); or a failed quest means threat >= strength. Basis: rr.step-3-4 (compare strength; successful only when higher); LOTRBLOG and VOTP restate the text only. Sources: Searched HoB, FFG forum, VOTP, LOTRBLOG: no official or agreed ruling on which characters or on ties. Agent recommendation: follow rr.step-3-4 wording; count each character separately.
- `05025:text:d` (span-settled). "If Celador leaves play, remove him from the game" replaces going to a discard pile; removed-from-game is out of play for the rest of the game (rr.removed-from-game); stage 1B's skip test then fails and the players advance to stage 2. Not taken: Celador is discarded. Basis: rr.removed-from-game; 05068:back.
- `05025:text:e` (researched). Control: while the effect of stage 3B moves him to the first player he stops being committed (`ats.ally-objectives`), he can then be committed in the quest phase like any ally. Not taken: He stays committed. Basis: `ats.ally-objectives`: "the card is no longer considered to be in the staging area, and is no longer committed to the quest (unless its controller commits it during the quest phase)". Sources: `ats.ally-objectives`.
- `05026:text:a` (researched). Same as 05025:text:a/b for Ithilien Guardian (ally objective, committed by text while in the staging area). Basis: `ats.ally-objectives`. Sources: `ats.ally-objectives`; VOTP.
- `05026:text:b` (span-settled). "When Revealed: Add to the staging area and Ithilien Guardian gains surge": it is revealed in staging, placed in the staging area by this effect, gains the surge keyword (rr.gains), and surge resolves right after its When Revealed (ltp.surge: surge after the when-revealed effect) so one more encounter card is revealed. Not taken: Surge is a printed keyword (so it would also trigger on any later reveal). Basis: rr.surge, rr.gains, ltp.surge, rr.objective-cards.
- `05026:shadowText` (researched). Shadow: 2 damage is dealt to the attacking enemy (direct damage, not combat damage); if that destroys the enemy, the enemy cannot finish the attack: no damage is dealt to the defender. Not taken: The attack still resolves. Basis: rr.shadow-effects, rr.step-6-4-2 (shadow resolves before damage is determined). "attack is cancelled when enemy dies" is only in VOTP. Sources: VOTP (fan guide, agreement with rules order; no official text). Mark would be `recommended` if the engine team wants an official source: none was found.
- `05027:text` (researched). While the current quest card has the battle or siege keyword the Company gets +2 Threat and +2 Attack; the test uses the current quest card's keywords and is re-evaluated continuously (stage 4B gains Siege when a threat is 37+, so the bonus then applies). Not taken: The bonus is only checked at reveal. Basis: rr.gains (gained keyword counts), faq.lasting-effects (encounter-card effects are recalculated); VOTP: "If the current quest card has Siege or Battle on it, they get a boost of 2". Sources: VOTP; LOTRBLOG ("+2 attack and +2 threat while the current quest card has the battle or siege keyword").
- `05027:shadowText` (recommended). "+1 Attack (+2 instead if the current quest card has a keyword)": any keyword on the current quest card (battle, siege, archery X from 2B) gives +2; stage 3B and 1B... the 1B side has battle; stage 3B has none (only the +1 applies); stage 4B has none until it gains siege. Not taken: "Keyword" means only battle or siege. Basis: Card wording: any keyword; stage backs 05068 (Battle), 05069 (Siege. Archery X), 05071 (gains Siege). No source says otherwise. Sources: Searched BGG/FFG forum/HoB/VOTP: no ruling on "a keyword"; wording is plain, reading follows it.
- `05028:text` (span-settled). While Ithilien Road is the active location, each enemy in the staging area has engagement cost 0 (modifies the printed cost; it is not an engagement check rule) so an engagement check engages every staging enemy against the first player whose threat is 0 or higher; at stage 3B enemies still do not engage. Not taken: Cost 0 only for engagement checks, optional engagement is separate. Basis: rr.active-location, rr.engagement-check, rr.step-5-3, 05070:back; VOTP: "the engagement cost of all enemies in the staging area will be considered to be 0".
- `05028:shadowText` (recommended). After the attack (after damage is dealt, at the end of that attack, rr.step-6-4-4) the attacking enemy returns to the staging area; it is then not engaged, may engage again at the next encounter phase (cost 0 only if Road is active) and may have its remaining attacks cancelled; shadow card discarded as normal (ltp.shadow-discard). Not taken: Return happens at once when the shadow resolves, so the enemy deals no damage. Basis: rr.shadow-effects, rr.step-6-4-4, ltp.shadow-discard. "after it attacks" fixes the timing at the end of the attack. Sources: Searched Google (4 queries), HoB pages, VOTP, LOTRBLOG, FFG forum: no ruling on this card. Agent recommendation: wording "after it attacks".
- `05029:text` (span-settled). Surge; When Revealed: 1 damage to each character committed to the quest, including ally objectives Celador and Ithilien Guardian (they are committed by text) and all players' characters; surge after the when-revealed effect. Not taken: Objective allies are not hurt. Basis: faq.errata.05029 (errata: "Surge. When Revealed: Deal 1 damage to each character committed to the quest." Omit the parenthetical) ; `ats.ally-objectives`. Sources: HoB source text already omits the parenthetical (matches the errata); the printed card may carry it: check the scan, the errata (faq.errata.05029) wins.
- `05029:shadowText` (span-settled). 1 damage to the defending character (before combat damage; an undefended attack gives no defender: no effect). Basis: Card text; rr.step-6-4-2.
- `05046:text` (researched). Action: any player may exhaust a Ranger character they control (traits Ranger: Celador, Ithilien Guardian and player cards); Celador and the Guardians cannot be exhausted for it while in the staging area (not controlled). Not taken: Objective allies in the staging area may be exhausted. Basis: rr.ownership-and-control; FFGFORUM-CEL ("paying Overgrown Trail's cost requires control"). Sources: FFGFORUM-CEL (community; the thread's own example is this card).
- `05046:shadowText / 05047:shadowText` (recommended). Remove X progress tokens from the current quest, X = the attacking enemy's current Threat (with modifiers such as Southron Company +2); if fewer progress tokens exist, remove all; active location is not affected. Not taken: X is printed Threat. Basis: Card wording "attacking enemy's Threat"; rr.gains / faq.lasting-effects (current value). Sources: No source found (HoB, FFG forum, VOTP). Agent recommendation: current Threat.
- `05047:text` (span-settled). Immune to player card effects: ignores all player card effects (no progress from player cards, no damage, no engage/travel by player card); framework effects (quest progress, travel by rule) still work. Basis: faq.immune-expanded, faq.legolas-immune-location.
- `05048:text` (recommended). Each player in turn order removes 1 character he controls from the quest, if able (a player with no committed character removes nothing); Celador and Guardians are not controlled so are not removed by step 1; THEN if any player has no characters committed to the quest, remove all characters from the quest: committed characters under every player go back (they stay in play, exhausted); Celador and Guardians are committed by their own text, so they stay committed (a constant effect cannot be removed by this). Not taken: Step 2 removes ally objectives too, or ally objectives count for "any player has a committed character". Basis: HoB raw text; rr.ownership-and-control; `ats.ally-objectives` (committed by their text, a constant). Sources: HoB page (card has no rulings), VOTP, LOTRBLOG, FFG forum: no ruling on ally objectives. Agent recommendation: "has no characters committed" counts only that player's controlled characters; objective allies are controlled by no player. Note: HoB web page shows only the first sentence; the raw HoB and dragncards data hold both.
- `05049:text` (span-settled). Until end of round Spider gets +1 Attack for each character (any player's) not currently committed to a quest, recomputed as characters commit or leave (a lasting encounter effect: faq.lasting-effects); counts only characters in play, includes exhausted ones; Celador and Guardians in the staging area are committed so are not counted; characters in a side quest commit count as committed. Not taken: Count fixed at reveal. Basis: faq.lasting-effects ("A lasting effect created by an encounter card ability is recalculated if the game state changes") ; `ats.ally-objectives`. Sources: VOTP says count is taken "when it enters play" (fan summary); the FAQ span wins.
- `05050:text` (researched). First player chooses a questing hero (a hero committed to the quest, not an ally), deals 2 damage, and removes it from the quest (the hero stays in play unless destroyed); no questing hero = no effect. Not taken: A questing character (ally) can be chosen. Basis: Card text ("questing hero"); VOTP: "deals 2 damage to a hero and removes that hero from the quest". Sources: VOTP (fan summary, agrees with card).
- `05050:shadowText` (recommended). Defending player raises threat by X = attacking enemy's current Threat (undefended attack: the active player). Basis: Card text; rr.step-6-4-2. Sources: No source found. Agent recommendation: the player attacked ("defending player") is the engaged player.
- `05051:text` (recommended). Raise each player's threat by the total number of questing characters (all players' characters committed to the quest, plus Celador and the Guardians while they are committed from the staging area); surge applies if the current quest has battle or siege at reveal time. Not taken: Each player raises by the count of his own questing characters; or objective allies not counted. Basis: `ats.ally-objectives` ("Any card effect that affects characters committed to the quest can also affect these ally objective cards"); FFGFORUM-WW (community: reads as all questing characters across players, no official reply). Sources: Searched FFG forum, VOTP, HoB, rules: no official answer; the thread leans to the total but nobody confirms. Agent recommendation: the total, objective allies included.
- `05051:shadowText` (span-settled). Each player raises threat by the number of enemies engaged with HIM (own engaged enemies). Basis: Card text.
- `05052:text` (researched). Archery X, X = number of players: at the start of each combat phase, deal X damage to character cards in play, divided as the players choose (first player decides a dispute); cumulative for each card; may include Celador and the Guardians (they are characters in play). Not taken: Objective allies cannot take archery damage. Basis: hon.archery ("damage to character cards in play ... any player's control"); `ats.ally-objectives` (ally objective is an ally). Sources: `ats.ally-objectives`; hon.archery.
- `05052:shadowText` (span-settled). +1 Attack; +3 Attack instead if the attacker has the Harad trait (Southron Company, Haradrim Elite, Mûmak and itself all have Harad). Basis: Card text; traits from data/cards.
- `05053:text` (span-settled). Forced on entering play: the Elite makes an immediate attack from the staging area against the first player; it is dealt a shadow card at the start; the four combat steps run with an action window after each; the shadow card is discarded after the attack; it may be defended by the first player's characters (Sentinel of other players: yes); it stays in the staging area (never engaged). Not taken: No shadow card for this attack. Basis: faq.enemy-attacks-outside-combat; rr.attacks-outside-the-combat-phase; faq.peril-attacks (all players may defend; peril case). Sources: FFGFORUM-HE; FFGFORUM-SH (communities citing the FAQ).
- `05053:shadowText` (span-settled). The attacking Elite attacks again after this attack resolves; a new shadow card is dealt for that attack; chains if that shadow is another Haradrim Elite shadow. Not taken: No new shadow card. Basis: Card text; rr.attacks-outside-the-combat-phase.
- `05054:text:a` (span-settled). No attachments can attach to Mûmak (player attachment cannot attach to it). Basis: Card text; rr.attachment-cards.
- `05054:text:b` (researched). At most 3 damage a round: damage that would take the total placed this round above 3 is not dealt (any source: combat, ranged, direct); the count resets at the end of the round; damage already on it before this round does not count. Not taken: Cap is 3 total damage on Mûmak. Basis: Card text; VOTP: "it can only take 3 damage per turn, including damage dealt outside combat" (fan write-up). Sources: VOTP (fan, one source, reading matches wording); HoB page no rulings. Searched FFG FAQ, BGG, forum: nothing more.
- `05055:text` (span-settled). Doomed 3: when revealed each player raises threat by 3 (rr.doomed-x, even if the When Revealed effect is cancelled: faq.cancel-encounter-effect); When Revealed: each player (in player order, first player first) searches the encounter deck AND discard pile for 1 Harad enemy and adds it to the staging area, "if able", a player that finds none adds none; the same copy cannot be chosen twice; shuffle the encounter deck. Not taken: Search only for one player or only the deck. Basis: rr.doomed-x, faq.cancel-encounter-effect, rr.search (searching is optional to find: "may add") ; traits from data/cards (Harad: 05027, 05052, 05053, 05054). Sources: none needed. Note: "must search" vs rr.search "may add": the card says "if able".
- `05068:text` (span-settled). Setup: add Celador to staging area (he is the objective, committed by his text), search the encounter deck for Ithilien Road and make it the active location (not travel), each player searches for 1 Southron Company and adds it to staging, shuffle; Southron Company count is 1 per player (4 copies exist). Basis: rr.search, rr.active-location.
- `05068:back` (span-settled). Battle (hon.battle) , 15 quest points; when completed: if Celador is in the staging area advance to 3A (stage 2 skipped), else advance to stage 2A; "complete this stage" means the quest points are reached. Not taken: Skip stage 2 whenever Celador is anywhere. Basis: hon.battle; card text; Celador is out of the game or controlled = not in the staging area. Sources: VOTP: "If Celador dies, stage 3 is discarded and the players move to stage 2" (agrees).
- `05069:back` (span-settled). Siege (hon.siege) and Archery X (hon.archery, X = players) while this stage is current; after completion advance to 4A (3 is bypassed). Basis: hon.siege, hon.archery. Sources: VOTP agrees.
- `05070:back` (researched). When Revealed: the first player takes control of all Ranger objectives in the staging area (Celador, Ithilien Guardians: trait Ranger from data/cards); they are moved to his play area, stop being committed (`ats.ally-objectives`). Constant: no engagement checks (rr.step-5-3) and no optional engagement (rr.step-5-2) while this stage is current; effects that engage an enemy are not stopped. Not taken: Control passes every round. Basis: `ats.ally-objectives`; rr.step-5-2; rr.step-5-3; VOTP: "This will be the only time that these allies can come under the control of any player". Sources: `ats.ally-objectives`; VOTP; LOTRBLOG.
- `05071:back` (researched). While any player's threat is 37 or higher this stage gains Siege (constant, rechecked): quest strength uses defense (hon.siege) and Southron Company gets the +2/+2 and +2 shadow; Forced: at the end of each round raise each player's threat by 2 (after other end-of-round effects: faq.simultaneous-effects, first player orders them); "If the players defeat this stage, they have won the game" (stage 4B completed). Not taken: Siege is checked only at the start of the round. Basis: rr.gains; faq.simultaneous-effects; faq.lasting-effects; VOTP. Sources: VOTP; LOTRBLOG.
