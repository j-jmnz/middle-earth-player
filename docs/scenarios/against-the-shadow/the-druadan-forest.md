# The Drúadan Forest

Against the Shadow part 5 (pack `TDF`, MEC19), standalone only, on Easy and Normal (Q03.5, E03.5). Encounter sets The Drúadan Forest and Brooding Forest (`ats.setup-the-druadan-forest`; Brooding Forest is from Heirs of Númenor and shared with Into Ithilien). The Prowl X keyword is `ats.prowl` with `ats.prowl-example`: when a card with Prowl is revealed from the encounter deck, the players as a group discard X resources from their heroes' pools, or as many as they can. The last stage carries the Siege keyword (`hon.siege`).

- **Setup.** Stage 1A prints "Search the encounter deck for Drû-buri-Drû and set him aside, out of play. Shuffle the encounter deck." (`06042:A:1`, read from the scan). Stage 3A adds him to the staging area (`06044:A:1`), without a reveal.
- **Piles.** The `setAside` pile holds Drû-buri-Drû (06045), so the Setup record only shuffles the encounter deck. The quest pile holds the three stages. No override.
- **Errata.** None for a card of this scenario.
- **Cards of other scenarios.** The Brooding Forest cards (05046 to 05048) keep their records of Into Ithilien (`docs/scenarios/against-the-shadow/into-ithilien.md`): no line of them is wrong here. Their lines that act differently on this table are under Readings.

### Prowl

- `06047:A:1`, `06047:A:2`, `06048:A:1`, `06049:A:1`, `06054:A:1` (span-settled). The players as a group discard X resources from the pools of their heroes, all they can when fewer remain, with no penalty for the shortfall. Each player decides for his own heroes, in player order, one resource at a time, as the example of ats.prowl-example has it ("Player 2 decides not to discard any resources, so Player 3 discards 2"): a player may stop while the players after him hold enough for the rest, and must go on when they hold too few (verb `GroupDiscardResources`). The discard is not a spend (the lock of Orc Vanguard does not stop it). X is the number of players for the Elite (`06047:A:2`), 1 for the Hunter and the Thief, 2 for Stars in Sky. Not taken: each player discards X; the first player picks every hero (the printed example shows each player deciding for his own heroes; rr.first-player gives him the final say on a disagreement, which the turn order replaces). Basis: ats.prowl, ats.prowl-example.
- Prowl on a card not revealed (span-settled). A card put into the staging area without a reveal (Drû-buri-Drû by `06044:A:1`, a card an effect moves) does not trigger Prowl. Prowl applies to a treachery too (`06054:A:1`). Basis: ats.prowl ("revealed from the encounter deck").
- `06042:B:1` (span-settled). Each card revealed is a normal reveal, one at a time: Prowl, When Revealed and Surge apply. Not taken: Prowl skipped for cards a quest effect reveals. Basis: faq.reveal-and-add, rr.step-3-3, rr.surge, ats.prowl.
- `06049:A:1` (span-settled). Prowl first, then the surge card. Not taken: Surge first. Basis: rr.staging, rr.surge.
- Prowl against When Revealed (recommended). Prowl is no When Revealed effect, so a cancel of the When Revealed effects does not cancel it (faq.cancel-encounter-effect keeps a keyword apart from the effect). Its order against the When Revealed of Stars in Sky is open: rr.staging puts the keyword and When Revealed in one step and orders neither, and the insert and the preview give no order. The engine resolves the When Revealed first and the Prowl next, because a Forced `revealed` record is heard after the When Revealed step. Not taken: Prowl first (a keyword step in `windows/frames.ts`, closed to a scenario).
- Prowl as a record (recommended). The three enemies hold a Forced `revealed` record (`byEncounterDeck`, subject self), which resolves after the card's When Revealed step (these enemies print none) and before the card is placed and before Surge; Stars in Sky holds the same Forced record apart from its When Revealed record, so a cancel of the When Revealed effects leaves the Prowl (tested). Not taken: a keyword step in the reveal frame. Basis: no span orders a keyword against a record; the frame code is closed to a scenario. Cost: for Stars in Sky the pay-or-leave step comes before the Prowl discard, not after it.

### The quest stages

- `06042:A:1` (span-settled). Drû-buri-Drû is out of play before any reveal, so Prowl and the reveals never meet him. The record shuffles the encounter deck. Basis: ats.setup-the-druadan-forest, rr.staging.
- `06043:B:1` (recommended). Archery damage goes to allies while an ally is in play that can take it, heroes when none is. Not taken: "if able" checked once for the whole archery. Basis: hon.archery, the printed line. Sources: the preview says allies take it first; no official answer.
- `06044:B:1` (span-settled). The keyword Siege applies by hon.siege (the committed characters' total Defense, as modified, replaces the total Willpower; the staging threat still opposes). The parenthesis is reminder text and adds no rule; the record is empty. Basis: hon.siege, rr.keywords.
- `06044:B:2`, Willpower (recommended). Every attack a character makes on an enemy (hero or ally, any player) counts its current Willpower, as modified (the +1 of Drû-buri-Drû included), in place of its Attack; bonuses to Attack are not added; a Willpower of 0 deals 0. The enemy's Defense is still subtracted. Not taken: Attack modifiers also add. Basis: the printed line; no ruling on modifiers.
- `06044:B:2`, progress (span-settled). Only the damage a character's attack deals becomes progress tokens on the enemy. Damage from an effect, a shadow card or archery stays damage. Not taken: all damage becomes progress. Basis: "damaged this way".
- `06044:B:2`, threshold (researched). The enemy is full when its progress equals its printed hit points; damage on it does not change that, and progress is never damage, so it never destroys the enemy. Not taken: the remaining hit points. Basis: the clarification of the stage 3B wording that the thread starter relays from Caleb in https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/221161-stage-3b-the-druadan-forest, 28 May 2016: "In order to add an enemy to the victory display at stage 3B, you need to place progress on that enemy equal to its printed hit points." It says the printed value, which the line's plain "its hit points" leaves open; the page does not state who Caleb is, and a member there argues the wording is poor. The test of the line names the reading.
- `06044:B:2`, the victory display (recommended). A full enemy goes to the victory display (it has no Victory keyword, the stage says so) and ALL its progress, the excess included, goes on the quest, counted toward the quest points. The enemy leaves first, then the tokens land. Not taken: only hit-point many tokens move. Basis: "those progress tokens" has no cap; faq.victory-display; rr.discard-pile-and-victory-display.
- `06044:B:2`, piling (span-settled). Progress may pile above the quest points of stage 3B; the stage is not defeated while an effect holds it back. Basis: faq.progress-without-advancing.
- `06044:B:3` (span-settled). The players win when stage 3B is defeated and Drû-buri-Drû is in the victory display. Not taken: the win counts and he is ignored. Basis: ledger `06044:B:3`, `06045:A:3`.
- `06044:B:3`, a destroyed Drû-buri-Drû (recommended). Destroyed by damage that is not a character's attack (he has 6 hit points and the stage turns attacks into progress, so this is rare), he goes to the encounter discard pile, and the players cannot win; the engine does not rescue the game. Not taken: he returns. Basis: rr.destroy. Sources: the preview calls it unresolved; no official answer.
- Stage bar (recommended). The stage card also holds the bar on winning (`06044:constant:3`, condition: Drû-buri-Drû not in the victory display), because the bar on his own card works only while he is in play. Not taken: the card of 06045 alone. Basis: rr.destroy, the reading above.

### Drû-buri-Drû

- `06045:A:1` (span-settled). No ally is declared as a defender against his attack. Basis: the printed line.
- `06045:A:2` (span-settled). While he is in the victory display, every character in play (heroes and allies, all players) has +1 Willpower and +1 Defense. Not taken: a card out of play gives nothing. Basis: faq.victory-display.
- `06045:A:3` (span-settled). While he is not in the victory display, no win is possible. Basis: the printed line.
- Nightmare (recommended, only if Nightmare is built). Out of scope: the Nightmare card limits damage to one a turn and no ruling says whether progress counts. Sources: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/301152-two-questionsdruadan-forest-nightmare-and-lonely-mountain.

### The enemies

- `06046:A:1` (span-settled). The Drummer is a Wose and counts itself. Each Drummer gives +2 Threat to every Wose in the staging area, itself and Drû-buri-Drû included, cumulative; an engaged enemy gets none. Not taken: the Drummer does not count itself. Basis: the printed line.
- `06046:A:2` (recommended). Every Wose engaged with the defending player gets +1 Attack until the attack ends, the attacker included when it is a Wose. A Wose engaged with another player gets nothing. The card prints no duration. Taken: until the end of the attack, because the shadow card is discarded after the attack resolves (faq.enemy-attacks-outside-combat; the discard of an attack's shadow cards, faq.additional-attacks) and the community default for a shadow effect with no duration is the attack (Seastan, GrandSpleen on https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/88894-how-long-do-the-shadow-effects-last/; others there say until the combat phase ends, and none cites an official source, so it is not settled). Not taken: until the end of the combat phase (each engaged Wose gets +1 Attack for its own attack that phase). No rule span, no FAQ entry and no Hall of Beorn ruling (https://www.hallofbeorn.com/LotR/Details/Druadan-Drummer-TDF) gives the duration; rr.shadow-effects and rr.lasting-effects name none. The Woses it affects are those engaged when the shadow resolves; a card that becomes engaged during the same attack is not affected (rr.lasting-effects would add it), which no card of this scenario can make happen.
- `06047:A:3`, `06049:A:2` (recommended). The shadow card resolves when it is dealt: a player pays 1 resource or the enemy returns to the staging area after its attack. The defending player decides, and the payment may come from the pool of any player's hero (the engine asks the defending player). With no resource to pay the enemy returns without a question. Not taken: only the defending player's heroes pay. Basis: the printed "any player", rr.cost. Sources: no official answer in the insert or the card pages.
- `06048:A:1`, `06048:A:2` (span-settled). Archery X is the number of heroes of every player with no resources in their pool, counted live when the archery is dealt (the keyword's own value moves with the heroes' pools). Not taken: counted once at the reveal. Basis: the printed line, hon.archery.
- `06048:A:3` (span-settled). The attacker gets +2 Attack (a template writes it).
- `06047:A:1` Archery 2 (span-settled). The Archery 2 is the card's keyword and adds to every other archery source. Basis: hon.archery.

### The locations

- `06050:A:1` (span-settled). Every player card of any type costs 1 more to play, from any zone, while the location is in play (staging area or active). The extra resource is paid with the rest from a matching hero. Actions and Responses that are not played cards do not cost more. Not taken: only while the location is active. Basis: the printed line, rr.resource-match.
- `06050:A:2` (span-settled). The Travel cost is one ready hero of any player, exhausted; the first player decides. With no ready hero the players cannot travel here. Not taken: each player exhausts one. Basis: rr.travel, rr.step-4-2.
- `06051:A:1` (span-settled). The location blocks the gains of card effects only, as the flag `noCardResources` of each hero does (not the table's `cannotGainResources`, which also stops the resource phase); the resource phase still gives each hero its resource (tested). Basis: faq.moving-resources-is-not-adding, the Barrow-wight precedent in `docs/scenarios/standalone-scenarios/fog-on-the-barrow-downs.md`.
- `06051:A:2` (span-settled). Each player, the first player first, pays 1 resource from his own heroes' pools. When any player cannot, the players cannot travel here. Not taken: only the players who can pay. Basis: rr.travel.
- `06052:A:1`, `06052:A:2` (span-settled). Each Wose in play (staging area or engaged, any player) gains archery 1 while the Glade is in play; two Glades each give 1 and stack. X is the total archery value (printed, gained and what the card gives itself) of the Wose with the most, counting the Glade's gains, recounted as play changes; 0 with no Wose in play. Not taken: the Glade's gain left out of X. Basis: hon.archery, rr.keywords.

### The treacheries

- `06053:A:1` (recommended). Each player, the first player first, for each hero he controls: he pays 1 resource from that hero's own pool or the hero takes 1 damage. A hero with an empty pool takes the damage. Men in the Dark gains surge when no hero took damage: damage that a Response canceled or a rule prevented is not damage taken (verb `DamageCardThenSetX`). Not taken: a player pays from any hero's pool. Basis: rr.cost. Sources: the preview (heroes pay to prevent damage); no official answer.
- `06053:A:2` (span-settled). +1 Attack for each hero the defending player controls with no resources, counted when the shadow card resolves. Not taken: all heroes in play. Basis: the printed line.
- `06054:A:2` (recommended). Each questing hero: its controller pays 1 resource from that hero's own pool or the hero is removed from the quest (it stays exhausted). A hero that is not committed is not affected. This resolves before the Prowl 2 discards: the engine hears the When Revealed first (see Prowl). Not taken: payment from any pool. Basis: rr.cost. Sources: no official answer.
- `06054:A:3` (recommended). All the resources of the defending character's pool are discarded. An ally has no pool, and an undefended attack has no defending character, so nothing happens. Not taken: an undefended attack hits a hero. Basis: no ruling.
- `06055:A:2` (recommended). Each player, the first player first, pays 1 resource for each attachment he controls (wherever it is attached), in all from his heroes' pools and none partly, or discards all of them. With no attachment nothing is asked. Not taken: pay for some and discard the rest. Basis: the printed "or discard all". Sources: no official answer.
- `06055:A:3` (recommended). The defending player chooses the attachment of the defending character that is discarded; nothing happens for an undefended attack or a character with none. Not taken: the encounter player chooses. Basis: no ruling.
- `06055:A:1` (span-settled). Surge is read from the card; its card is revealed after this card is staged. Basis: rr.surge.

## Engine notes

The flags, the verb and the amount are in the family `pelargir` (`src/catalog/families/pelargir.ts`, `src/rules/families/pelargir/`). Three shared lines: the optional amount field `archeryOf` in `src/catalog/effect-schema-amount.ts`, and two calls in `src/rules/combat/combat.ts` (`attackStrength` reads the attack stat through `attackStatOf`, `strikeEnemy` places the attack damage through `placeAttackDamage`), which do what they did before in a game whose table holds no flag.

- **Stage 3 flags** (`06044:B:2`). The record of The Passage Out sets `attackWithWillpower`, `attackDamageAsProgress` and `progressToVictory` on the table (`stage-attack.ts`, `stage-progress.ts`). `attackStatOf` reads Willpower while the first flag holds, and `placeAttackDamage` places progress tokens in place of the damage of a character's attack while the second holds; an enemy that cannot take damage takes no progress. The settle sweep of the family moves each enemy in play with progress at least its printed hit points to the victory display and puts all its progress on the current quest (`progressToVictory`, inert unless the game reads the flag).
- **The bar on winning** (`06044:B:3`, `06045:A:3`). Two `Restrict advance` records with the condition "Drû-buri-Drû is not in the victory display": one on his card, one on the stage card. The progress of an enemy that fills lands after he is in the display, so a stage already full is defeated at that moment.
- **`EachPlayerSpends`** (`06051:A:2`, `group-spend.ts`). Queues a spend for each living player, first player first, from his own heroes' pools. The family's travel hook says the Travel cost is unpayable when any living player holds fewer resources than the amount.
- **`hasResources: false`** (`06048:A:2`, `06053:A:2`, `no-resources.ts`). The pick hook of the family leaves out each hero whose pool holds a token; the core reads only the `true` form.
- **`archeryOf`** (`06052:A:2`, `archery-of.ts`). The amount hook returns the highest archery total (printed, gained, `archeryBonus`) among the cards the pick finds, 0 with none.
- **Prowl** is the verb `GroupDiscardResources` (`group-discard.ts`): each player in player order chooses a hero of his own with resources and discards one resource at a time, and may stop while the players after him hold enough; a Choose with the options `discard` and `stop`, then a hero pick (nothing is asked with one hero or when he must go on). No keyword code is read. The chip on the card preview is under Table.
- **`DamageCardThenSetX`** (`06053:A:1`, `damage-noted.ts`). Deals the damage as DamageCard does, and sets the X marker after the responses to it were heard only when the card holds more damage than before.
- **Limits.** (1) The Distribute intent assigns all the archery points at once against the list of allies at that moment: an ally the first points destroy does not send the later points to another ally or a hero, and a player may assign more points than an ally can take. (2) The Prowl of Stars in Sky comes after its When Revealed, not before. (3) The attack damage that becomes progress is not damage, so a Response that waits for "after an attack damages an enemy" does not hear it, and no card of the scenario has one. (4) A shadow effect that raises the Attack of the engaged Woses reaches those engaged when it resolves. (5) `placeAttackDamage` (`stage-attack.ts`) copies only part of the guards of `placeDamageHere`: it leaves out Dragon Scales, the damage cap and a resource cancel. No card of this scenario reaches them; the full list of guards is in `src/rules/core/keywords.ts`, a core file the family does not change.
