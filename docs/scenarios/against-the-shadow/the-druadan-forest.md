# The Drúadan Forest

Against the Shadow part 5 (pack `TDF`, MEC19), standalone only, on Easy and Normal (Q03.5, E03.5). Encounter sets The Drúadan Forest and Brooding Forest (`ats.setup-the-druadan-forest`; Brooding Forest is from Heirs of Númenor and shared with Into Ithilien). The Prowl X keyword is `ats.prowl` with `ats.prowl-example`: when a card with Prowl is revealed from the encounter deck, the players as a group discard X resources from their heroes' pools, or as many as they can. The last stage carries the Siege keyword (`hon.siege`).

- **Piles.** Hall of Beorn's text of stage 1A has the players search the encounter deck for Drû-buri-Drû and set him aside; stage 3A adds him to the staging area. The `setAside` pile holds Drû-buri-Drû (06045). No override.
- **Errata.** None for a card of this scenario.

## Readings

The readings of The Drúadan Forest use the statuses defined in the Readings bullet of `docs/scenarios/against-the-shadow.md`. The keys are the research keys, `<code>:<field>`. The lines are written from the source text, not from the scan-read ledger, so the Read task of The Drúadan Forest confirms each against its scan.

- 06047:text (Prowl X) (researched). Trigger on reveal from the encounter deck; players as a group discard X resources (X = player count) from any heroes' pools, split as they choose; if fewer than X in total, discard all they can. Not taken: each player discards X; or only the first player decides. Basis: MEC19 insert Prowl X keyword text (URL in header); no rr/faq span holds Prowl. Sources: MEC19.pdf (header URL); https://www.hallofbeorn.com/LotR/Details/Druadan-Elite-TDF.
- 06048:text (Prowl 1) (researched). Same Prowl rule with X = 1: group discards 1 resource from any hero pool, none if the group has none. Not taken: each player discards 1. Basis: MEC19 insert. Sources: MEC19.pdf; https://hallofbeorn.com/LotR/Details/Druadan-Hunter-TDF.
- 06049:text (Prowl 1) (researched). Same as Prowl, applied at reveal; Surge resolves after staging per rr.surge. Not taken: Prowl waits until after the surge card. Basis: MEC19 insert; rr.surge. Sources: MEC19.pdf.
- 06054:text (Prowl 2) (researched). Prowl applies to a treachery that is revealed from the encounter deck, discard 2 as a group. Not taken: Prowl is for enemies only. Basis: MEC19 insert says "encounter card with the prowl keyword". Sources: MEC19.pdf.
- 06047:text, 06048:text, 06049:text, 06054:text (order of Prowl vs When Revealed/staging) (recommended). Prowl resolves at the reveal, first, then the card's When Revealed / staging, then surge (rr.surge). Prowl is not cancelled by cancelling the When Revealed effect. Not taken: Prowl after staging. Basis: faq.cancel-encounter-effect (a keyword such as Doomed is separate from the When Revealed effect); insert gives no order. Sources: MEC19.pdf; https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/04/25/the-druadan-forest/; https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/84563-watch-out-for-woses/; web search found no order ruling. Low impact: only matters when a hero's last resource is also needed by a When Revealed cost (Stars in Sky).
- 06048:text (Prowl: chosen heroes) (researched). The players choose, with no restriction to a single hero or to the first player. Not taken: first player decides. Basis: MEC19 insert example (players decide among themselves). Sources: MEC19.pdf.
- 06042:back (reveal 1 per player; Prowl on those cards) (span-settled). Each card revealed here is a normal encounter reveal: Prowl, When Revealed, Surge all apply, one card at a time. Not taken: Prowl skipped for cards added by a quest effect. Basis: MEC19 "revealed from the encounter deck"; faq.last-encounter-card for staging order. Sources: MEC19.pdf.
- `06042:text` (span-settled). Search encounter deck for Drû-buri-Drû, set aside out of play, shuffle. He is not in the deck for Prowl or reveal. Basis: card text.
- `06043:back` (recommended). Archery damage goes to allies while any ally in play can take it; per hon.archery players still divide the points; heroes only when no ally is in play ("if able" checked point by point, so an ally killed mid-assignment is gone for the next point). Not taken: If able is checked once for the whole archery. Basis: hon.archery; card text. Sources: https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/04/25/the-druadan-forest/; no official answer found. Per-point check is the plain reading of "if able".
- 06044:back (Siege keyword) (span-settled). The keyword applies as hon.siege: committed characters count total Defense instead of Willpower when quests resolve; staging threat still opposes. Not taken: Siege reminder text replaces quest rule. Basis: hon.siege; the card prints "Siege." as a keyword. Sources: https://hallofbeorn.com/LotR/Details/The-Passage-Out-TDF (shows "Siege." plus the three sentences); https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/04/25/the-druadan-forest/ (players quest with defense, attack with willpower).
- 06044:back (willpower for attacks) (recommended). During this stage every character attack (player attack declared on an engaged enemy) uses the character's Willpower in place of Attack, including modifiers to Willpower; attack bonuses do not add. Not taken: attack modifiers also add. Basis: card text only. Sources: https://hallofbeorn.com/LotR/Details/The-Passage-Out-TDF; https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/04/25/the-druadan-forest/; no ruling on modifiers found. A character with Willpower 0 deals 0.
- 06044:back (progress on enemy instead of damage) (span-settled). Only damage dealt by a character's attack becomes progress tokens; damage from any other source (effects, Shadow, archery) stays damage. Not taken: all damage becomes progress. Basis: card says "damaged this way". Sources: card text; https://hallofbeorn.com/LotR/Details/The-Passage-Out-TDF.
- 06044:back (enemy full of progress) (recommended). At progress >= hit points, the enemy goes to the victory display and ALL its progress tokens (including any excess) go on the quest; counts toward the 14. Not taken: only tokens equal to its hit points move; or it also needs damage rules for defeat. Basis: card text "place those progress tokens"; faq.victory-display (victory display is out of play, not discard). Sources: https://hallofbeorn.com/LotR/Details/The-Passage-Out-TDF; no ruling on excess. The card says "those progress tokens" without a cap.
- 06044:back (does the enemy go to the victory display, not discard, even without a Victory keyword) (span-settled). Yes, it goes to the victory display and stays there. Not taken: to the encounter discard pile. Basis: card text; faq.victory-display; rr.victory.
- 06044:back (progress at 14 but no Drû-buri-Drû) (span-settled). Progress may pile above 14 (faq.progress-without-advancing) but the stage is not defeated, so the game is not won, until Drû-buri-Drû is in the victory display. Not taken: stage is defeated at 14 and the game is lost or stuck. Basis: faq.progress-without-advancing; 06045 text ("players cannot win").
- `06044:text` (span-settled). Add Drû-buri-Drû from set-aside to staging as When Revealed of the stage; not revealed from the deck, so no Prowl or surge. Basis: card text; MEC19.
- 06045:text (Allies cannot defend) (span-settled). Allies cannot be declared defenders against him. Basis: card text.
- 06045:text (victory display bonus) (span-settled). Static +1 Willpower and +1 Defense to every character (heroes and allies, all players) while he is in the victory display; a victory-display card may still affect play. Not taken: out-of-play cards give no effect. Basis: faq.victory-display ("some card effects may still interact with them"). Sources: https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/04/25/the-druadan-forest/.
- 06045:text (cannot win) (span-settled). While he is not in the victory display, no win (including the stage 3B win) is possible; the stage's win only counts when he is there. Not taken: the win counts and he is ignored. Basis: card text.
- 06045 (Nightmare variant) (recommended (only if Nightmare is built)). Out of scope: Nightmare Drû-buri-Drû has a one-damage-per-turn clause; the thread says no ruling whether it limits progress. Basis: forum. Sources: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/301152-two-questionsdruadan-forest-nightmare-and-lonely-mountain.
- `06046:text` (span-settled). Drummer is a Wose and counts itself: +2 Threat to each Wose enemy in staging, cumulative with several Drummers. Not taken: does not count itself. Basis: card text; trait Wose on the card. Sources: https://www.hallofbeorn.com/LotR/Details/Druadan-Elite-TDF (Wose trait pattern; confirm Drummer trait in data).
- `06046:shadowText` (span-settled). +1 Attack to the attacking enemy if it is a Wose engaged with the defending player. Not taken: any Wose enemy. Basis: card text.
- 06047:text (Archery 2) (span-settled). Archery 2 as hon.archery, cumulative with other archery cards; with Glade of Cleansing it is 3. Basis: hon.archery.
- 06047:shadowText, 06049:shadowText (recommended). Shadow effect after the attack resolves (damage dealt): the attacker returns to staging unless any player pays 1 resource from a hero pool; the payer's choice is open to all players, paid before the enemy is returned. Not taken: returns before damage; only the defending player may pay. Basis: card text; faq.additional-attacks for "after it attacks" sequencing. Sources: no official answer found (searched MEC19 insert, Hall of Beorn card pages). Reading follows the printed "any player".
- 06048:text (Archery X) (span-settled). X counts heroes in play (every player's) with 0 resources, counted when the archery damage resolves in the combat phase start, not when the card is revealed. Not taken: counted once at reveal. Basis: card text; hon.archery.
- `06048:shadowText` (span-settled). Attacking enemy gets +2 Attack. Basis: card text.
- 06050:text (cost +1) (span-settled). Each player card played (any type, any player) costs +1 while the location is in play; not limited to the active location. Not taken: only while active. Basis: card text.
- 06050:text (Travel) (span-settled). Travel cost: exhaust a hero (any hero of any player; players choose); if no ready hero, cannot travel. Not taken: all players exhaust. Basis: rr.travel.
- 06051:text (no gain) (span-settled). Blocks hero gains from card effects only; the resource phase gain (rr.step-1-2) is not a card effect and still happens. Not taken: blocks it too. Basis: rr.step-1-2; card text.
- 06051:text (Travel) (span-settled). Each player pays 1 resource from a hero pool; if any player cannot pay, the players cannot travel (rr.travel). Not taken: only players who can pay. Basis: rr.travel.
- 06052:text (Glade of Cleansing) (span-settled). Each Wose enemy (in any zone) has Archery 1 while Glade is in play; Threat X = total archery value of the highest-archery Wose enemy in play (own keyword plus Glade's, cumulative per hon.archery); X = 0 with no Wose in play. The hon.archery sum counts the Glade's own grants, so X is also counted after the grant. Not taken: Archery 1 only from Glade of Cleansing in the active slot. Basis: hon.archery; card text; HoB shows Threat X (5). Sources: https://www.hallofbeorn.com/LotR/Details/Glade-of-Cleansing.
- 06052:text (two Glades in play) (recommended). Each Glade grants Archery 1 separately; stack. Not taken: does not stack. Basis: hon.archery ("effects are cumulative"). Sources: no official ruling on same-name locations; cumulative rule for archery cards is the nearest span.
- 06053:text (Men in the Dark) (recommended). Each hero (all players) is paid by its controller: pay 1 resource from that hero's own pool, or that hero takes 1 damage; if no hero took damage, Men in the Dark gains surge. Not taken: a player pays from any hero's pool. Basis: rr.resources; card text. Sources: no official source found. A hero with 0 resources must take damage. Surge from the card is read after staging (rr.surge). Source "gains surge" counts when each hero paid or had nothing to pay for? Taken: a hero that cannot pay takes damage, so surge is off.
- `06053:shadowText` (span-settled). +1 Attack per hero the defending player controls with no resources. Not taken: all heroes in play. Basis: card text.
- 06054:text (When Revealed) (recommended). Each hero currently committed to the quest: its controller pays 1 resource from that hero's own pool or removes it from the quest (still exhausted). Not taken: pay from any hero. Basis: rr.resources; card text. Sources: no official source found. After the 2 Prowl discards.
- `06054:shadowText` (recommended). Discard all resources from the defending character's pool; no effect if it is an ally (no pool). Not taken: cannot be dealt against allies. Basis: card text. Sources: no official ruling; allies have no resource pool.
- 06055:text (When Revealed) (recommended). Each player chooses: pay 1 resource per attachment he controls (from any of his hero pools, total paid across heroes, no partial pay) or discard all attachments he controls; with no attachments pays 0. Not taken: may pay for some and discard rest. Basis: card text ("or discard all"). Sources: no official source found. Surge per rr.surge.
- `06055:shadowText` (recommended). The defending player discards an attachment attached to the defending character (the defending player chooses which); nothing if none. Not taken: encounter player chooses. Basis: card text; faq (none found). Sources: no official source found.
