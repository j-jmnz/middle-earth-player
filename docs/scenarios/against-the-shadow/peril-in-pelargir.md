# Peril in Pelargir

Against the Shadow part 1 (pack `HoN`, Heirs of Númenor), standalone only, on Easy and Normal (DragnCards decks Q03.1 and E03.1). Encounter sets Peril in Pelargir, Streets of Gondor and Brigands (`ats.setup-peril-in-pelargir`). Streets of Gondor and Brigands are shared with The Steward's Fear (`docs/scenarios/against-the-shadow/the-stewards-fear.md`). The quest cards carry the Battle keyword (`hon.battle`); Umbar Assassin (05044) has Archery 2 (`hon.archery`).

- **Piles.** Stage 1A's Setup searches the encounter deck for The Leaping Fish and Alcaron's Scroll, makes the Fish the active location and attaches the Scroll to a hero (the normalizer reads it in `scripts/data/normalize/setup-against-the-shadow.ts`). Overrides in `data/overrides.json`: Alcaron's Scroll (05019) is `attachedChosen` and not staged as DragnCards loads it; the five Harbor Thug (05020) are in the encounter deck, since the Setup stages no enemy (DragnCards stages 4 on Normal and none on Easy). The Fish (05021) is the `activeLocation` pile. The Setup record `05065:setup:1` sets the Scroll aside out of the encounter deck and attaches it, claimed, to the hero the first player chooses. Sources: the Hall of Beorn scenario export and the DragnCards lists Q03.1 and E03.1, as the override reasons say.
- **Errata.** None for a card of this scenario (no FAQ errata span names one).

## Readings

The readings of Peril in Pelargir use the statuses defined in the Readings bullet of `docs/scenarios/against-the-shadow.md`. Each key is the scan-read ledger line it reads (`data/printed/against-the-shadow.json`), `<code>:<side>:<n>`, with a note in brackets where one line holds several readings; the FAQ is the FAQ 1.9 PDF. The records are in `src/effects/authored/against-the-shadow/peril-in-pelargir.ts` and read each as written here, but for the three differences under Engine notes.

### 05019 Alcaron's Scroll

- `05019:A:1` (researched). "If unattached, return Alcaron's Scroll to the staging area": the Scroll is an objective. When the card it is attached to leaves play (enemy destroyed or removed, hero destroyed), the Scroll goes to the staging area and is NOT discarded with its host. Same when the effect of another card leaves it attached to nothing. Not taken: Discard it with its host (rr.attachment-cards, "simultaneously discard") Basis: FAQ 1.17 Unclaimed Objectives ("treated like an attachment ... remains attached to that card until it leaves play, at which point it will return to the staging area"); the printed line itself. Sources: FAQ 1.9 https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/lotr_faq_19.pdf; https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/110322-pickpockets-shadow-effect-and-the-scroll (GrandSpleen, unofficial, same reading from the printed passive)
- `05019:A:1` (Action half) (span-settled). "it gains: 'Action: Exhaust a hero to attach ...'": the Action exists while the Scroll is unattached in the staging area (an in-play area, so an action can be used from it); any player may exhaust any ready hero of theirs; the hero's controller gains control of the Scroll. Not taken: Only the first player; only while in staging is not required. Basis: rr.in-play-and-out-of-play; rr.attachment-cards ("that player gains control of the attachment"); FAQ 1.07 (claiming an objective gives control)
- `05019:A:2` (damaged) (researched). "damaged by an enemy attack" = damage tokens were placed on the hero by that attack (rr.damage step 3). Damage cancelled or reduced to 0 by defense does not fire the Forced. Not taken: Cancelled damage still counts (alogos/GrandSpleen) Basis: rr.damage; rr.cancel; Caleb (FFG developer) on Muck Adder, quoted second-hand: damage must actually be placed. Sources: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/93529-do-new-enemies-engaging-during-combat-also-attack (Caleb's answer is relayed by one poster; applied by extension from Muck Adder; confirm)
- `05019:A:2` (redirected damage) (recommended). Damage the Thug or Collateral Damage shadow redirects to the Scroll hero is damage "by an enemy attack": the Forced fires and the Scroll attaches to the attacking enemy. Not taken: The redirect is not "damaged by the attack" Basis: rr.damage; shadow text of 05020 / 05024. Sources: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/93529-do-new-enemies-engaging-during-combat-also-attack (GrandSpleen, one voice, unofficial, same scenario). No source states it; plain reading, damage dealt by the attack.
- `05019:A:2` (timing vs destruction) (recommended). "When ... is damaged": the Forced resolves as damage is placed, while the hero and the Scroll are still in play; the Scroll attaches to the enemy, then the hero is destroyed if damage is at or over hit points. Not taken: Hero destroyed first, Scroll goes to staging. Basis: rr.forced (must be in play at the trigger); rr.hit-points; rr.destroy. Sources: no source found (forum threads above; Hall of Beorn Alcaron's Scroll page lists no ruling). Ends the same way for 3B only if the enemy survives.
- `05019:A:2` (attached to enemy) (span-settled). While attached to an enemy the scenario controls the Scroll; it moves with the enemy (engaged, returned to staging by Lurking in Shadows, etc.); it counts as "attached to an enemy", not "attached to a hero". Not taken: n/a. Basis: rr.ownership-and-control; FAQ 1.17.

### 05020 Harbor Thug

- `05020:A:1` (trigger) (researched). "raises his threat" fires on every threat raise of the Scroll hero's controller: round end 7.3, Harbor Storehouse, Collateral Damage, Local Trouble, card costs. Not taken: Only effects, not the framework raise. Basis: rr.step-7-3; FAQ Q "Dungeon Torch ... raise his threat by 2 or 3 during the refresh phase" (the refresh raise is called a raise) Sources: FAQ 1.9 https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/lotr_faq_19.pdf; https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/76048-peril-in-pelargir-questions (Kroepian, unofficial, agrees). The Vision of the Palantir review also says "whenever that player raises his threat": https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/03/13/peril-in-pelargir/ (found by search, snippet only).
- `05020:A:1` (from where) (span-settled). Harbor Thug engages from the staging area or from another player's engagement area (it moves to the Scroll player). Not taken: Only a staged Thug engages. Basis: rr.engaged ("enters a player's play area from another game location, that enemy has engaged that player"; stays engaged "until ... moved to another play area") Sources: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/76048-peril-in-pelargir-questions (Kroepian, yes, unofficial, agrees with the span)
- `05020:A:1` (no attack on engage) (span-settled). Engaging by this Forced makes no attack by itself. Engaged in the encounter phase or at round end: it attacks in the next combat phase. Engaged during combat before enemy attacks resolve (a Local Trouble raise): it is engaged and has not attacked, so it is eligible to attack this combat, with no shadow card (shadow cards are dealt only in 6.2). Engaged after it was dealt a shadow card elsewhere: see below. Not taken: Immediate extra attack on engage; or never attacks until next round. Basis: rr.step-6-2; rr.step-6-3; rr.step-6-4a ("eligible enemy ... has not yet attacked this round"); rr.shadow-effects; FAQ 1.32. Sources: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/78173-peril-in-pelargir-questions (GrandSpleen: no extra/immediate attack); https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/93529-do-new-enemies-engaging-during-combat-also-attack (Caleb relayed: attacks, no shadow card; agrees with the spans)
- `05020:A:1` (engaged mid-attack) (researched). A Thug that changes player mid-attack, keeps its shadow card and finishes that attack with its new state. Not taken: Shadow card discarded. Basis: FAQ 1.32 Mid-Attack Control or Engagement Change ("that attack still resolves with the card still participating from its new state") Sources: FAQ 1.9 https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/lotr_faq_19.pdf; https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/121037-peril-in-pelargir-question (GrandSpleen cites 1.32; I confirmed the entry text in the PDF). Not a `faq.*` span.
- `05020:A:2` (shadow, redirect) (researched). "Any damage dealt by this attack is dealt to the hero with Alcaron's Scroll attached, if able": all damage of the attack goes to the Scroll hero (any player's hero), in place of the defender or the undefended assignment, before damage is placed. The defender takes nothing. Not taken: The defender still takes it. Basis: the printed line; rr.step-6-4-3. Sources: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/107153-shadow-effect-of-harbor-thug-who-receives-the-damage (klaymen_sk, alogos; no dissent; unofficial community only, so it also goes to Readings to confirm)
- `05020:A:2` (no Scroll hero) (researched). If no hero has the Scroll (staging, on an enemy), "if able" fails and the defender takes the damage as normal. Not taken: The attack deals no damage. Basis: the printed line ("if able") Sources: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/107153-shadow-effect-of-harbor-thug-who-receives-the-damage (klaymen_sk, same)

### 05021 The Leaping Fish

- `05021:A:1` (gained Forced, when active) (span-settled). The Fish gains the Forced only while the Scroll is attached to a hero when the beginning of the quest phase triggers. A Scroll attached later in the phase does not fire it. The Fish may be the active location or in the staging area (both are in play). Not taken: Only as the active location. Basis: rr.forced and faq.triggering-forced-effects ("in play and active at the time its trigger occurs"); rr.in-play-and-out-of-play (staging area and active location are in play)
- `05021:A:1` (X, empty deck) (span-settled). X = number of players; discard X from the top; each discarded enemy is added to the staging area, with no When Revealed (it is added, not revealed); other cards stay in the encounter discard pile. If the deck runs out the discard pile is shuffled into a new deck and the discard continues. Not taken: Stop at the empty deck. Basis: rr.running-out-of-cards ("out of cards during the quest phase"); rr.reveal; rr.staging.
- `05021:A:1` (discarded cards leave the game) (span-settled). Discarding an objective or unique card from the top: none expected (Scroll and the Fish are set up out of the deck). Not taken: n/a. Basis: the Setup of 05065.

### 05022 Pelargir Docks

- `05022:A:1` (span-settled). While Docks is the ACTIVE location (not staged), every enemy in play gets +1 Attack and +1 Defense: engaged enemies of every player, enemies in the staging area, and enemies that enter later. Face-down shadow cards and enemies in the deck or discard pile are out of play. Not taken: Only engaged enemies. Basis: the printed line; rr.in-play-and-out-of-play.

### 05023 Harbor Storehouse

- `05023:A:1` (who triggers) (span-settled). Triggers only while Storehouse is in play (staging or active) when a location is discarded from the top of the encounter deck. A Storehouse discarded by that same effect is out of play and does not trigger. Not taken: Triggers from the deck as well. Basis: rr.forced; faq.triggering-forced-effects; rr.in-play-and-out-of-play.
- `05023:A:1` (which discards) (recommended). Counts a location discarded from the top by the Fish, by Collateral Damage, and by any other effect that names "from the top of the encounter deck". Does NOT count a shadow card discarded from an enemy at the end of combat, nor a location discarded from the staging area. Each location is one trigger (raise each player's threat by 1); with two Storehouses in play each triggers. Not taken: Count shadow cards too. Basis: the printed line ("from the top of the encounter deck"); rr.forced ("each forced ability must resolve as completely as possible") Sources: no source on shadow-card discards; https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/86498-collateral-damage-peril-in-pelargir (Caleb, about Collateral Damage only). Plain reading: a shadow card is dealt from the top and discarded from an enemy.
- `05023:A:2` (count) (span-settled). "an additional shadow card for each Thug enemy in play": count Thug enemies in play when the shadow effect resolves: staging area and every player's engagement area, including the attacker. A face-down Thug dealt as a shadow card is not in play. Not taken: Count only engaged Thugs. Basis: rr.in-play-and-out-of-play; the printed line.
- `05023:A:2` (how dealt) (recommended). The additional cards are dealt face-down to the attacking enemy at that moment, then flipped one at a time and each shadow effect resolves; all are discarded at the end of the combat phase. Not taken: Only the last or only the first resolves. Basis: rr.step-6-4-2 (only the first card is named); FAQ ("unresolved shadow card ... discarded at the end of the combat phase"); faq.errata.01091 (an additional card is dealt, for Beastmaster, at step 1) Sources: no source covers a shadow effect that deals more shadow cards. The FAQ 1.9 PDF was read; a web search for the ruling returned only the Beastmaster erratum.

### 05024 Collateral Damage

- `05024:A:1` (order) (researched). "Discard 2 cards from the top ... Discard an additional 2 cards for each copy of Collateral Damage in the discard pile. Then, raise each player's threat by 2 for each location discarded by this effect": sentence by sentence. (1) discard 2; (2) count copies of Collateral Damage in the encounter discard pile NOW, including copies discarded by (1), but not the revealed copy (it is placed after its effects, rr.treachery-cards); discard 2 per copy; (3) raise threat by 2 for each location discarded by (1) and (2). Copies discarded by (2) add no more. Not taken: All at once; count before the first discard. Basis: rr.treachery-cards; Caleb (FFG): "When Revealed effects are resolved on a sentence-by-sentence basis ... If a copy of Collateral Damage is discarded by the first sentence, then you would count that copy" Sources: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/86498-collateral-damage-peril-in-pelargir (Caleb's post relayed by the asker, the one reply that came from the developers); dissent in https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/78173-peril-in-pelargir-questions (unofficial, older, no Caleb). The printed "Then," now makes the order explicit.
- `05024:A:1` (empty deck) (span-settled). If the deck empties during the discards (it is the quest phase, staging), shuffle the encounter discard pile into a new deck and keep discarding; locations discarded earlier in this effect still count for the raise. The copy of Collateral Damage being resolved is not in the discard pile at that point. Not taken: Stop at the empty deck. Basis: rr.running-out-of-cards; rr.step-3-3 ("shuffled and reset back into the encounter deck") Sources: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/76048-peril-in-pelargir-questions (Kroepian, unofficial, agrees). Open edge: after a reshuffle, copies of Collateral Damage in the new deck are not in the discard pile, so they add nothing; recommended, no source.
- `05024:A:1` (Storehouse) (researched). Locations discarded by the effect also trigger Harbor Storehouse (05023) if it is in play, in addition to this card's own raise. Not taken: n/a. Basis: the 05023 row; Caleb's thread. Sources: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/86498-collateral-damage-peril-in-pelargir.
- `05024:A:2` (shadow) (researched). Same reading as 05020 shadow: damage goes to the hero with the Scroll, if able. A treachery dealt as a shadow card has only its shadow text active and is discarded at end of combat. Not taken: n/a. Basis: see 05020:A:2; FAQ Q "Banks of the Anduin ... only its Shadow text is considered to be active" Sources: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/107153-shadow-effect-of-harbor-thug-who-receives-the-damage; FAQ 1.9 PDF.

### 05037 Pickpocket

- `05037:A:1` (Pickpocket, attacks) (span-settled). "Forced: When Pickpocket attacks": the defending player discards 1 resource from a hero's pool of his choice, and 1 card at random from his hand; each part resolves as far as possible (no resources: still discards the card). Not taken: All or nothing. Basis: rr.forced ("resolve as completely as possible"); the printed line.
- `05037:A:2` (shadow, "his attachments") (researched). The defending player chooses 1 attachment he controls to discard. Local Trouble (an encounter card attached to his hero) is not his: the controller of the hero does not control it. (Undefended: all of his attachments.) Not taken: Local Trouble is discardable. Basis: FAQ 1.06 Control of Non-objective Encounter Cards ("that character's controller does not gain control of the attachment") Sources: FAQ 1.9 https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/lotr_faq_19.pdf; https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/76048-peril-in-pelargir-questions (Kroepian corrected, unofficial, agrees)
- `05037:A:2` (Scroll) (recommended). Alcaron's Scroll on his hero is an objective attachment under his control (he claimed it), so it can be the chosen attachment, and "discard all" includes it. A discarded Scroll is "unattached" and returns to the staging area (it is not placed in a discard pile). Not taken: The Scroll is not an attachment, so never eligible; or it goes to the encounter discard pile. Basis: FAQ 1.07; FAQ 1.17; printed 05019:A:1. Sources: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/110322-pickpockets-shadow-effect-and-the-scroll (GrandSpleen, one voice, unofficial, same reading). No official ruling on a discard of the Scroll; goes to Readings to confirm.

### 05038 City Street

- `05038:A:2` (City Street travel) (span-settled). While a City Street is in the staging area, players can travel only to a location with the TITLE City Street. "City" is a trait, so The Leaping Fish and Harbor Storehouse (trait City) are barred. Applies to player card effects that make players travel. Not taken: Trait City. Basis: the printed line ("does not have the title"); rr.traits.

### 05039 Market Square

- `05039:A:2` (Market Square travel) (span-settled). Travel cost: each player pays 1 resource from one of his heroes' pools. If any player cannot pay, the players cannot travel to it. Not taken: Only the first player pays. Basis: rr.travel ("If the players cannot pay the Travel cost, then they cannot travel"); the printed line.
- `05039:A:3` (shadow) (span-settled). Defending player discards every resource in the pools of all his heroes (the defender's pools only). Not taken: n/a. Basis: the printed line.

### 05040 Lost in the City

- `05040:A:1` (span-settled). Each player in player order searches the encounter deck and the discard pile for 1 City-trait location, adds it to the staging area (no When Revealed, rr.search), if able; then the deck is shuffled once, the discard pile is not. A unique location already in play (The Leaping Fish active or staged) cannot be found: it cannot enter play. "Cannot be canceled" is read as printed. Not taken: Search the deck only; shuffle after each player. Basis: faq.search-effects; rr.search; rr.in-player-order; rr.unique-cards; rr.reveal.
- `05040:A:2` (shadow) (span-settled). Defending player discards his whole hand. Not taken: n/a. Basis: the printed line.

### 05041 Local Trouble

- `05041:A:1` (attach) (span-settled). Attach to the hero with the highest threat cost that has no Local Trouble; ties: the first player chooses among the tied heroes. If every hero has one, nothing can attach: it is placed in the encounter discard pile as a treachery. Not taken: Each player attaches to own hero. Basis: rr.first-player ("targets a single ... card, and there are multiple eligible targets, the first player selects"); FAQ 1.03; rr.attachment-cards ("cannot enter play if there is no eligible card"); rr.treachery-cards. Sources: FAQ 1.9 https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/lotr_faq_19.pdf.
- `05041:A:1` (counts-as text) (recommended). "raise its controller's threat by 1" once for each exhaust, each ready (refresh included), and each ability the attached hero triggers: every triggered ability of that hero's own text box (Action, Response, Forced); abilities of other cards or attachments do not count. Not taken: Only during combat; or only Actions. Basis: rr.triggered-ability (Action, response, forced are triggered abilities); the printed line. Sources: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/78173-peril-in-pelargir-questions (GrandSpleen, unofficial, says any time it readies, not only combat); no source on "triggers an ability" and Forced. Goes to Readings to confirm.

### 05066 Fighting in the Streets

- `05066:B:1` (span-settled). Battle reminder: same as 05065:B:1. The line prints the keyword and reminder text, a keyword part owned by the card keywords. Not taken: n/a. Basis: hon.battle.
- `05066:B:2` (recommended). "The players cannot advance to the next stage unless Alcaron's Scroll is attached to a hero.": progress keeps being placed on the quest while the Scroll is not on a hero (no upper limit); the stage advances the moment the Scroll is attached to a hero if quest points are already reached. The block holds against any effect that advances the stage. Not taken: Progress is not placed / the stage resets. Basis: faq.progress-without-advancing; rr.quest-advancement ("immediately advance ... as soon as"). The "advance when the Scroll attaches later" step has no source. Sources: no source covers advancing after a blocked stage; the span says only that progress still goes on. Plain reading of "immediately advance as soon as".
- `05066:B:3` (target) (recommended). When Revealed: attach the Scroll to the enemy in play with the highest engagement cost: enemies in the staging area and engaged enemies of every player are in play; ties: the first player chooses. The Scroll leaves where it is (hero, staging) and attaches to that enemy. Not taken: Engaged enemies only; the Scroll stays on its hero. Basis: rr.in-play-and-out-of-play; rr.first-player; FAQ 1.03; rr.attachment-cards. - `05066:B:3` (otherwise) (recommended). "if able. Not taken: Scroll stays on its hero when no enemy. Basis: rr.staging-area; 05019:A:1; the printed line. ### 05067 Escape to the Quays

- `05067:A:1` (span-settled). When Revealed: each player in player order searches the encounter deck and the discard pile for 1 enemy and adds it to the staging area (not revealed: no When Revealed, no surge); shuffle the deck, not the discard pile. Not taken: Reveal the enemy. Basis: faq.search-effects; rr.search; rr.reveal; faq.reveal-and-add.

Engagement by checks, by the Thug's Forced, or by a player card (Son of Arnor) is still allowed and is not optional engagement, so the harsher clause of 05042-05044 applies. ## Engine notes

Where the engine does not read a reading above to the letter, and why.

- `05019:A:2` (timing vs destruction): the Scroll attaches to the enemy when the hero survives the damage. When the damage is lethal and no Response in play or in hand could cancel it, the engine destroys the hero as the damage is placed, before the Forced effect is heard, so the Scroll goes to the staging area (the reading not taken). A hero whose destruction waits for a cancel Response hears the Forced effect first. The fix is a hold of lethal damage in `src/rules/core/keywords.ts` (`placeDamageHere`), a shared engine file this scenario does not change.
- `05041:A:1` (counts-as text): the engine announces an exhaust that is the cost of an Action only through the Action (`abilityTriggered`), not as `exhausted` (`docs/agents/effect-authoring.md`, Not expressible yet): a hero that exhausts to pay for another card's Action raises the controller's threat once through the ability, not once more for the exhaust.
- `05024:A:1` (empty deck): a deck that runs out during the effect is reset from the discard pile as the reading says, but a location discarded before the reset is then in the new deck and no longer counts for the raise.
- `05066:B:2` and `05067:B:3` (later attach): the stage advances at the next settle with no window open (`advanceFreed`, `src/rules/quest/stage-reveal.ts`), so a Scroll attached in an action window advances it when the window closes.
