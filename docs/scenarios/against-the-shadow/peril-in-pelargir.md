# Peril in Pelargir

Against the Shadow part 1 (pack `HoN`, Heirs of Númenor), standalone only, on Easy and Normal (DragnCards decks Q03.1 and E03.1). Encounter sets Peril in Pelargir, Streets of Gondor and Brigands (`ats.setup-peril-in-pelargir`). Streets of Gondor and Brigands are shared with The Steward's Fear (`docs/scenarios/against-the-shadow/the-stewards-fear.md`). The quest cards carry the Battle keyword (`hon.battle`); Umbar Assassin (05044) has Archery 2 (`hon.archery`).

- **Piles.** Stage 1A's Setup searches the encounter deck for The Leaping Fish and Alcaron's Scroll, makes the Fish the active location and attaches the Scroll to a hero (the normalizer reads it in `scripts/data/normalize/setup-against-the-shadow.ts`). Overrides in `data/overrides.json`: Alcaron's Scroll (05019) is `attachedChosen` and not staged as DragnCards loads it; the five Harbor Thug (05020) are in the encounter deck, since the Setup stages no enemy (DragnCards stages 4 on Normal and none on Easy). The Fish (05021) is the `activeLocation` pile. Sources: the Hall of Beorn scenario export and the DragnCards lists Q03.1 and E03.1, as the override reasons say.
- **Errata.** None for a card of this scenario (no FAQ errata span names one).

## Readings

The readings of Peril in Pelargir use the statuses defined in the Readings bullet of `docs/scenarios/against-the-shadow.md`. The keys are the research keys, `<code>:<field>`. The lines are written from the source text, not from the scan-read ledger, so the Read task of Peril in Pelargir confirms each against its scan.

- `05019:text:1` (recommended). Scroll "unattached" = on no card; it is returned to the staging area and there gains the Action; a hero attaches it by exhausting. The Action is usable only while it is in the staging area. Not taken: Scroll stays where it is until an effect moves it. Basis: card text; rr.attachment-cards. Sources: https://hallofbeorn.com/Cards/Details/Alcarons-Scroll (no ruling); no FAQ or forum entry on it. Agent recommendation: a card whose host leaves play or is discarded becomes unattached (enemy killed, hero dead) and goes to staging.
- `05019:text:2` (researched). "Damaged by an enemy attack" means damage was placed (rr.damage step 3). Damage cancelled or prevented (Frodo, Spirit cancel) does not trigger the Forced attach. Trigger fires after damage, scroll goes to the attacking enemy, also when the shadow redirected the damage to the scroll hero. Not taken: Cancelled damage still counts (GrandSpleen). Basis: rr.damage; FFG Caleb on Muck Adder (relayed in thread). Sources: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/93529-do-new-enemies-engaging-during-combat-also-attack (Caleb's Muck Adder answer is quoted second-hand and applied by extension; confirm). Not on the FAQ spans.
- `05019:text:3` (span-settled). Scroll attached to an enemy: the enemy's controller is the scenario; the attached scroll moves with the enemy (rr.ownership-and-control). Basis: rr.ownership-and-control.
- `05020:text:1` (researched). Forced fires whenever a player's threat rises (any amount, any source: round end 7.3, Harbor Storehouse, Collateral Damage), for the player whose hero holds the scroll; the Thug moves from staging OR from another player's engagement area to that player (an enemy engaged elsewhere also moves). Not taken: Only enemies in staging engage. Basis: rr.engaged; rr.step-7-3. Sources: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/76048-peril-in-pelargir-questions (Kroepian, unofficial: yes, moves from another player too; community, one voice) ; https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/226943-engagement-semantics.
- `05020:text:2` (researched). When the Thug engages by this Forced effect it makes no immediate extra attack; it attacks in the next combat phase. During combat (mid-attack engagement) it makes an attack but receives no shadow card. Not taken: Immediate attack on engage. Basis: faq.enemy-attacks-outside-combat; Caleb (FFG) reply in thread. Sources: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/93529-do-new-enemies-engaging-during-combat-also-attack (Caleb official, relayed: "will make an attack" but no shadow card, as not engaged at dealing); https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/78173-peril-in-pelargir-questions (unofficial: no immediate attack at round end). No FAQ span covers mid-combat engagement; confirm.
- `05020:text:3` (span-settled). Thug engaged "while stuck" (Ranger Spikes): text-less case, not needed; engaging is not an engagement check. Basis: rr.engaged.
- `05020:shadowText:1` (researched). Damage is dealt to the hero with the scroll (any player's hero) if able; if no hero has the scroll (in staging or on an enemy), the defender takes it as normal. Damage target changes before assignment (rr.damage step 2). Not taken: Defender still takes it. Basis: Shadow text; rr.damage. Sources: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/107153-shadow-effect-of-harbor-thug-who-receives-the-damage (consensus, unofficial).
- `05020:shadowText:2` (researched). A Thug engaged mid-attack by an action keeps its dealt shadow card and attacks the new player during that player's attacks. Not taken: Shadow card discarded. Basis: faq 1.32 as cited in thread (not a rules span in data). Sources: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/121037-peril-in-pelargir-question (unofficial, cites FAQ 1.32; check the FAQ span id before relying).
- `05021:text:1` (researched). Gains the Forced only while Scroll is attached to a hero (not on an enemy, not in staging); X = number of players; the discarded cards are the top X of the encounter deck; each discarded enemy goes to staging; other discarded cards stay in the encounter discard pile (a discarded Scroll objective: no rule seen, recommend it stays discarded -> leave to staging? see note). Empty deck: shuffle discard pile into a new deck and continue. Not taken: Stop when the deck is empty. Basis: rr.encounter-deck; card text. Sources: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/76048-peril-in-pelargir-questions (Kroepian, unofficial: shuffle the discard to rebuild when deck runs out); card text https://hallofbeorn.com/Cards/Details/The-Leaping-Fish. Note: the Scroll is a searched-for card, cannot be in the deck at that time.
- `05021:text:2` (recommended). Leaping Fish applies the Forced from the active location or from staging alike ("If Alcaron's Scroll is attached..." is a constant, location need only be in play). Not taken: only when active. Basis: card text. Sources: no source covers it; HoB lists no ruling.
- `05022:text:1` (recommended). +1 Attack and +1 Defense for every enemy (staging, engaged, all players) while Docks is the active location; includes enemies engaged by any player and enemies revealed later. Not taken: Only engaged enemies. Basis: card text; rr.immune not involved. Sources: no ruling found (https://hallofbeorn.com search of Peril in Pelargir cards, forum threads above). Plain reading.
- `05023:text:1` (researched). Forced triggers once per location discarded from the top of the encounter deck (by Leaping Fish, Collateral Damage, other discards from the deck top, including Storehouse's own discard? No: it must be in play to trigger, a Storehouse discarded this way is not in play) ; raises each player's threat 1 per location. Not triggered by shadow cards discarded after an attack, nor by locations discarded from staging. Not taken: Triggers for the discard pile as well. Basis: card text; Caleb's ruling on Collateral Damage (locations just discarded count). Sources: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/86498-collateral-damage-peril-in-pelargir (Caleb, FFG, relayed); in-play condition for Forced is an agent reading, recommend.
- `05023:shadowText:1` (recommended). One additional shadow card for each Thug enemy in play (staging, engaged by any player), counted when the shadow effect resolves; the additional shadow cards are dealt to the attacking enemy then resolved. Not taken: Count only engaged Thugs. Basis: card text; faq.additional-attacks does not apply. Sources: none found. Ask the Read task to follow the printed scan; "in play" covers staging and every player's area.
- `05024:text:1` (researched). Sentence by sentence: (1) discard top 2; (2) count copies of Collateral Damage in the encounter discard pile NOW (after step 1, so a copy just discarded counts; the revealed copy is not yet in the discard pile; copies discarded by step 2 add nothing); discard that many x2 more; (3) raise each player's threat by 2 per location discarded by THIS effect (steps 1+2 only, not earlier locations). Not taken: All-at-once: copies in pile before the first discard only. Basis: Caleb (FFG) via thread. Sources: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/86498-collateral-damage-peril-in-pelargir ; https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/96534-collateral-damage (both relay Caleb: sentence by sentence). Dissent: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/78173-peril-in-pelargir-questions (unofficial, superseded). Not in any faq.* span.
- `05024:text:2` (recommended). Empty encounter deck while discarding: shuffle the encounter discard pile into a new deck and continue (locations discarded earlier in this effect count; the rebuilt deck must not count discards twice). Not taken: Stop. Basis: rr.encounter-deck; Kroepian (unofficial). Sources: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/76048-peril-in-pelargir-questions (unofficial only). The revealed copy resolves, then is discarded after the effects.
- `05024:text:3` (researched). Cards discarded by the effect count toward Storehouse trigger (see 05023). Other copy: only the revealed copy resolves When Revealed. Basis: Caleb thread. Sources: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/96534-collateral-damage.
- `05024:shadowText:1` (researched). Same as Harbor Thug shadow: damage dealt to the hero with the scroll, if able. Basis: see 05020:shadowText:1. Sources: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/107153-shadow-effect-of-harbor-thug-who-receives-the-damage.
- `05065:text:1` (recommended). Setup: search for Leaping Fish and Scroll (both removed from the deck), make Fish the active location, attach Scroll to a hero: with several players the first player chooses the hero (agent reading; the players choose). Encounter deck is shuffled after search (faq.search-effects). Not taken: Each player attaches? (single Scroll: no). Basis: faq.search-effects; card text. Sources: no source says who chooses; the one Scroll goes to one hero. Rule: first player decides unless the card says otherwise (agent recommendation).
- `05065:back:1` (span-settled). Battle: quests count total attack (hon.battle), staging area enemies and locations still count threat. When Revealed: each player must search for a Harbor Thug and add it to staging (up to the number of players; "if able" absent, no failure rule); shuffle. Not taken: Only one Thug total. Basis: hon.battle; faq.search-effects. Sources: none needed beyond the spans (HoB https://www.hallofbeorn.com/LotR/Details/The-Leaping-Fish-Quest-HoN).
- `05066:back:1` (recommended). Battle. The scan prints a second line, "The players cannot advance to the next stage unless Alcaron's Scroll is attached to a hero." (the source text lacks it). When Revealed: attach Scroll to the highest engagement-cost enemy in play (staging area included; ties: first player chooses; Scroll comes from wherever it is, including off a hero); if none in play, add to staging. Not taken: Engaged enemies only. Basis: hon.battle; card text. Sources: no ruling found. Reading "in play" includes staging; tie break by first player (rr.step-5-3 uses the first player for the same kind of tie). Attached Scroll then follows the enemy per 05019.
- `05067:text:1` (span-settled). When Revealed: each player searches the encounter deck and discard pile for 1 enemy and adds it to staging; shuffle deck (discard pile is not shuffled, faq.search-effects). Basis: faq.search-effects.
- `05067:back:1` (span-settled). "Enemies cannot be optionally engaged" = optional engagement step is skipped for every enemy; Forced mechanic and the Thug/Zealous/Bandit/Assassin "not optionally engaged" clauses then always take the harsher reading when engaged by engagement check (2 damage / 2 resources / discard hero). Basis: rr.optional-engagement; rr.step-5-2.
- `05067:back:2` (recommended). Forced: first enemy revealed each round makes an IMMEDIATE attack against the player who controls the scroll. Source text ends "from the staging area" (HoB) which does not parse; "controls" = the player whose hero holds it; if the Scroll is attached to an enemy or in staging, no player controls it. Immediate attack: shadow card dealt (faq.enemy-attacks-outside-combat), enemy then stays in staging (no engagement). Not taken: Attack the first player when scroll unattached; enemy engages. Basis: faq.enemy-attacks-outside-combat; HoB https://www.hallofbeorn.com/LotR/Details/Escape-to-the-Quays. Sources: the scan must be read: the "from the staging area" tail may be a transcription error or part of the effect; no discussion found. Recommend: attack happens only if a player controls the Scroll; else it is skipped.
- `05067:back:3` (span-settled). Stage cannot be defeated unless Scroll is attached to a hero; defeat = game won. 15 quest points. Basis: card text.
- `05037:text:Pickpocket:1` (recommended). Forced when it attacks: defending player discards 1 resource from one hero's pool (his choice, if any) and 1 random card from hand; each part resolves if able. Not taken: All or nothing. Basis: card text. Sources: no ruling; threads silent.
- `05037:shadowText:1` (researched). Defending player discards 1 attachment he controls (own attachments only, encounter-card conditions like Local Trouble not eligible: "his"); undefended: discards all of his attachments. Not taken: Local Trouble discardable. Basis: card text. Sources: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/76048-peril-in-pelargir-questions (Kroepian corrected, unofficial: no).
- `05038:text:1` (span-settled). Surge (rr.surge). While City Street is in staging, players cannot travel to a non-City-Street location; City is "title", so a card titled City Street. Not taken: Trait City. Basis: rr.surge; card text. Sources: HoB card text (title, not trait).
- `05038:shadowText:1` (span-settled). Attacking enemy gets +2 Attack for this attack. Basis: card text.
- `05039:text:1` (span-settled). Immune to player card effects (faq.immune-expanded: incl. travel, damage, progress); Travel cost: each player spends 1 resource from one of his heroes' pools; a player who cannot pay prevents travel. Basis: faq.immune-expanded; card text.
- `05039:shadowText:1` (span-settled). Defending player discards all resources from his heroes' pools (all heroes). Basis: card text.
- `05040:text:1` (span-settled). Each player searches deck AND discard pile for 1 City location, adds it to staging; shuffle deck; cannot be canceled; "if able" per player; Hall of Beorn trait City lists The Leaping Fish and City Street. Discard pile not shuffled. Basis: faq.search-effects; faq.cancel-encounter-effect.
- `05040:shadowText:1` (span-settled). Defending player discards his hand. Basis: card text.
- `05041:text:1` (researched). Attach to the hero with highest threat cost lacking a Local Trouble (ties: first player chooses). Condition grants: when attached hero exhausts, readies or triggers an ability, controller raises threat 1 (each event separately; "triggers an ability" = an Action/Response/Forced on the hero, not the Condition). Raises per ready in refresh too. No eligible hero: nothing (treachery discarded). Not taken: Only during combat. Basis: card text. Sources: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/78173-peril-in-pelargir-questions (GrandSpleen, unofficial: raises on any ready incl. refresh); tie rule agent recommendation.
- `05042:text:1` (span-settled). "Engages a player" includes optional engagement (rr.engaged); 1 damage to each ally the player controls; 2 if engaged any other way (engagement check, an effect such as Harbor Thug). Not taken: Only checks. Basis: rr.engaged.
- `05043:text:1` (span-settled). Same: 1 resource from each hero's pool, 2 resources when not optionally engaged. Basis: rr.engaged.
- `05044:text:1` (span-settled). Archery 2 (hon.archery). Forced on engage: that player deals 3 damage to a hero (their choice); not optionally engaged: that hero is DISCARDED instead (player chooses which hero). Basis: hon.archery; rr.engaged.
- `05045:text:1` (span-settled). Return all engaged Brigand enemies to staging (all players); if none returned, gains surge (reveal one more card). Basis: rr.surge.
- `05045:shadowText:1` (recommended). Return the attacking enemy to staging after it attacks; it keeps no shadow effect; the attack is complete. Basis: card text. Sources: no source; plain reading. Returned enemy is not engaged and can re-engage at engagement checks.
