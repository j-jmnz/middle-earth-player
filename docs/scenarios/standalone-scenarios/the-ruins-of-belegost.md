# The Ruins of Belegost

The 2015 print-on-demand scenario (pack `TRoB`, RingsDB's code for it, position 6 of cycle 50), Normal and Easy, the sixth of the cycle. Hall of Beorn files it under the product `2015` beside Murder at the Prancing Pony, so each product line names `scenarioTitle`. No Nightmare deck exists for it: `scripts/data/nightmare-defs.ts` and `data/raw/nightmare` have no set for it, and Hall of Beorn's Nightmare counts only repeat Normal (https://hallofbeorn.com/LotR/Scenarios/The-Ruins-of-Belegost). Hall of Beorn lists no rulesheet and `data/rules` holds none; the scenario's rules are the quest cards and the four-page Scenario Rules insert, whose pages 2 to 4 are the DragnCards rules cards (read from their pictures, below).

- **Data.** Hall of Beorn numbers its cards `00001` to `00030`, which collide with The Massing at Osgiliath's; RingsDB lists none of the pack's cards. `overrides.productCodes` gives them `506` and the collector number, as the Stone of Erech's are `503` (`50601` to `50607` the seven quest cards, `50608` Stalking the Ruins, `50609` to `50614` the six Loot objectives, `50615` Naurlhûg, `50616` Lurker of the Depths, `50621` Naurlhûg's Lair, `50617` to `50620` and `50622` to `50630` the rest of the encounter deck). Gimli (01004, tagged with the box in Hall of Beorn's player cards) stays the Core Set hero. The piles are DragnCards' deck Q99.06 for Normal and its own Easy twin E99.06 for Easy, checked against the quest card's printed Setup (`50601:A:1`): Stalking the Ruins and one Abandoned Mine for each player are staged (`perPlayer`), and Lurker of the Depths, Naurlhûg, Naurlhûg's Lair, the Sword of Belegost and the five other Loot objectives (Map of Ered Luin, Keys of Belegost, Book of Aulë's Children, Dwarven Torch, Blue Mountain Gem) wait in the set-aside pile; the other 12 titles (the encounter deck, 31 cards on Normal) are dealt as the Setup says by the record. The Setup's "Randomly select 1 of the 5 remaining Loot objectives and place it facedown underneath Stalking the Ruins ... Shuffle the others into the encounter deck" is read by two Setup steps (`hideLoot`, `shuffleOthersIn`, `scripts/data/normalize/setup-standalone-scenarios.ts`); the five stay in the set-aside pile for the scenario's record to hide and shuffle (the Sword is set aside by the line before). DragnCards' two "Scenario Rules" rows are reminder cards, not cards the scenario deals, and the build skips rows of type Rules.
- **Easy.** E99.06 differs from Q99.06 by the three cards Hall of Beorn gives no Easy copies: Flooded Hall, Old Stone Troll and Spawn of Thangorodrim (2 copies each on Normal, 0 on Easy), so the data agrees with Hall of Beorn's counts.
- **Stages.** The quest deck branches: 1B goes to 2C when Keys of Belegost is attached to a hero, else 2A; 2B to 3C when the Book of Aulë's Children is attached, else 3A; 2D to 3E when the Book is attached, else 3C; 3B to 4A when the Dwarven Torch is attached, else 3C; 3D to 4A when the Map of Ered Luin is attached, else 3E; 3F to 4A when the Blue Mountain Gem is attached, else 3A. The data lists the seven cards in printed order (`1A`, `2A`, `2C`, `3A`, `3C`, `3E`, `4A`); the branches are the scenario author's.
- **Scenario Rules cards.** Pages 2 to 4 of the insert (DragnCards' pictures `Scenario Rules 1` and `2`, read from the pictures, researched: the card pictures are the source; page 1 is not available online). Discover X: when a location with the Discover keyword becomes the active location, the first player resolves in order: look at the top X cards of the encounter deck, X being the active location's Discover value; choose 1 looked at objective with the Loot keyword and attach it to the active location as a guarded objective; randomly select 1 looked at card with the Hazard trait, reveal it and add it to the staging area; place any remaining looked at cards in the encounter discard pile. If fewer than X cards remain in the encounter deck when resolving Discover, shuffle the encounter discard pile into the encounter deck and continue. Loot: objectives with the Loot keyword can only enter play by resolving the Discover keyword on a location; a Loot card revealed from the encounter deck is discarded and another card is revealed from the top. Taking control of Loot: when the encounter card guarding a Loot objective leaves play, the first player must immediately attach the Loot objective to a hero he controls; a Loot objective attached to the active location when the players make enough progress to explore it and advance to the next stage is taken by the first player before advancing. Immune to player card effects: such a card ignores the effects of all player cards and cannot be chosen as a target of player card effects.
- **Ledger.** The 37 printed sides are read from the scans (`data/printed/standalone-scenarios.json`). Hall of Beorn's text has: "The player cannot advance" for the printed "The players cannot advance" (50601 B, 50603 B), "advace" and "Stage 2A" for "advance" and "stage 2A" (50601 B), "Otherwise advance" for "Otherwise, advance" (50604 B), "on the active location" for "of the active location" (50605 B), "Shuffle the Sword of Belegost" for "Shuffle Sword of Belegost" (50607 A), single quotes for the printed double quotes (50607 B, 50624 A), "1 progress token" for "1 resource" on Stalking the Ruins (50608 A), "fist player" for "first player" (50610 to 50614), "that hero's resource pool" for "that hero's pool" (50611), a comma for the full stop in "Immune to player card effects. Cannot take damage." (50615), and Lurker of the Depths missing its first two lines, "Cannot have attachments." and "cannot be engaged or take more than 4 damage each round." (50616). Two printed typos are kept under `misprints`: the full stop missing at the end of Naurlhûg's Forced line (50615 A, line 3) and "Blue Mountain Goblins gains surge" (50620 A, line 1).
## Readings

- **Readings.** The scenario shares Discover X and Loot with The Ghost of Framsburg (`emce.discover`, `emce.loot`), whose records the scenario reuses (`Discover`, `Loot`, `ClaimWhenFree`, `GuardObjective`, the pick fields `lootKeyword` and `controlled`; Ered Mithrin, The Ghost of Framsburg, above). The Scenario Rules insert's steps are word for word the `emce.discover` steps, so no new Discover text applies (researched: the insert pictures; https://visionofthepalantir.com/2021/01/04/the-ruins-of-belegost lists the same steps). No FAQ span, Hall of Beorn ruling, designer answer or forum thread rules on this scenario, so every reading below is the card's text or `recommended` (agent recommendation).
  1. 50601:A:1 Setup (and 50608/50621): "Randomly select 1 of the 5 remaining Loot objectives ... facedown underneath Stalking the Ruins ... Shuffle the others into the encounter deck". The seed picks the one; the other four are shuffled in unseen; the card underneath is out of play, as any card under another is (`rr.in-play-and-out-of-play`), until Naurlhûg's Lair is explored (50621:A:3) adds it to the staging area faceup. Literal (https://visionofthepalantir.com/2021/01/04/the-ruins-of-belegost agrees).
  2. "Control a Loot objective" (50601:B:1, 50602:B:1, 50603:B:1, 50617:A:3, 50619:A:1, 50620:A:2): a Loot objective is controlled by the player whose hero it is attached to; one attached to a location or an enemy as a guarded objective, or lying in the staging area, is controlled by nobody. "Attached to a hero" (50618, 50620, 50625) is the same set. Recommended, as Ered Mithrin's `controlled`: the card says "attaches it to a hero he controls" and counts objectives attached to heroes.
  3. Loot "If unattached, the first player claims X and attaches it to a hero he controls" (50609 to 50614:A:2) and "Taking control of Loot" (insert): the same as Ered Mithrin's Loot, a claim by `ClaimWhenFree` once nothing guards the objective, so a Loot objective that lies in the staging area free of encounters (the one under Stalking the Ruins added by the Lair, one a guard released, one a hero's leaving or a discard returned to the staging area) is claimed at once, and a Loot objective on the active location is claimed when that location leaves play (explored), before the quest advances: the explore comes first, so the claim comes first. Literal for the guard; a detached Loot returning to the staging area and being claimed again is `recommended` (as Ered Mithrin's `returnsToStagingArea`; no source rules on it).
  4. Discover value changes: 50605:B:1 "The Discover value of the active location is increased by 1" is a constant while the stage is current, added to the value the keyword and 50617:A:2, 50629:A:1 read; with no active location the value is 0 (so X is 1 for those two cards). Every location this scenario deals has a Discover keyword, so the +1 reaches every active location it can meet.Community only (https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/218028-nameless-cave-dweller-in-ruins-of-belegost: with no active location the value is 0, so 1). 50622:A:3 (Abandoned Mine) and 50623:A:3 (Flooded Hall) change how the keyword resolves: Abandoned Mine looks at X more cards (X the number of players, so more cards are looked at than the value); Flooded Hall's first player reveals one more looked-at Hazard for each other player (a one-player game none; fewer Hazards than that among the looked cards, all of them). Each extra Hazard is revealed at random, one at a time, and added to the staging area; the cards not taken go to the encounter discard pile. Literal.
  5. 50606:B:2 "After resolving the Discover keyword, if no Hazard card was revealed by that effect, reveal the top card of the encounter deck": the `discovered` trigger (amount 0), the first player revealing one card from the top as in the staging step; a Loot card revealed is discarded and another revealed. Literal (Ered Mithrin's Hidden Staircase reads the same event).
  6. 50604:B:1 "The first Hazard card revealed each round gains surge": any card with the Hazard trait revealed, from the staging step, a card effect or Discover's reveal, counts; the first of the round gains surge. The first Hazard of the round may be revealed by Discover during the travel phase: that card gains surge and reveals the next card. Recommended (`FirstTraitSurge`, trait Hazard, any type; no source says whether Discover's reveal counts: a reveal is a reveal, `rr.reveal`).
  7. 50601:B:2 "Forced: At the beginning of the travel phase, if there are no locations in play, return the topmost location in the encounter discard pile to the staging area": locations in play are those in the staging area and the active one; "return" puts the discard pile's last card of that type into the staging area (not revealed, no When Revealed); with none in the pile nothing happens. Recommended (`PutTopmostFromEncounterDiscard` with `type: location`; the same words as "Put the topmost Orc enemy in the discard pile into play").
  8. Stage branches (50601:B:3 to 50606:B:3): "When this stage is defeated, if X is attached to a hero, advance to stage N" is the stage's Forced effect that sends the quest to the printed stage; "otherwise" is the other printed stage (1B to 2C or 2A; 2B to 3C or 3A; 2D to 3E or 3C; 3B to 4A or 3C; 3D to 4A or 3E; 3F to 4A or 3A, as the card prints it; the blog's labels differ and are not used). The stage has no default successor: both branches are Forced effects with conditions. The stage letters (the printed "stage 2A") are the data's `2A`...: letter and level, `2C` the second stage of level 2. Literal; the engine reads both branches as one `AdvanceIfAttached` record of the stage (`attachedNext`), a Loot objective attached to a hero being one a hero holds. The branches lead round (3A to 3C to 3E to 3A), so a stage the quest played before comes back from the set-aside pile with no progress, side A up (agent recommendation: the cards never say a stage is used once). 50603 B:3 and 50602 B:2 branch on the Book of Aulë's Children "attached to a hero", also literal.
  9. 50603:A:1 When Revealed: "Shuffle Lurker of the Depths and the encounter discard pile into the encounter deck. Discard cards from the top of the encounter deck until a location is discarded. Add the discarded location to the staging area." The location is added, not revealed (no When Revealed, no Discover: Discover waits until it becomes the active location); a deck with no location leaves the discard pile as it is. Literal. 50604:A:1 and 50605:A, 50606:A: shuffle the discard pile into the deck and reveal 1 encounter card per player, one for each player in turn order, the staging step's order and the reveal of the first card at its own turn. Literal.
  10. 50603:B:1 "at least 3 resources here" and :B:2 "After the active location is explored, place 1 resource here": the resources are tokens on the quest card; a progress token is not a resource. Literal. 50608:A "place 1 resource on Stalking the Ruins" the same: resources, not progress (the printed card; Hall of Beorn's "progress token" was wrong).
  11. 50608:A:1 "add or return Naurlhûg to the staging area" with 3 or more resources: set aside, Naurlhûg is added; engaged with a player, it returns to the staging area (it stays one card, damage kept); already in the staging area, nothing. Recommended (Hall of Beorn's pages give no more). 50608:A:2 "set Naurlhûg aside, out of play" removes it from wherever it is, with its damage and shadow cards, so a later Stalking adds it new. "Discard all tokens" with none on it resolves in full ("all" of none is none; `rr.effects`: "resolve as much of each aspect of the effect as they are able"), so the set aside follows (`rr.then`). Recommended: no ruling covers a "Then" after an empty discard (https://alongextendedparty.com/semi-official-errata-faq-1-10/, items 2.12, 2.27, 3.093 rule on others).
  12. 50615 Naurlhûg X is the number of resources on Stalking the Ruins, read when asked; "Forced: When Naurlhûg makes an attack, the defending player assigns X damage among characters he controls": at the start of the attack, after the defender is chosen and before the shadow card or the defenders are chosen, from the damage the printed Attack gives separately; the damage is not combat damage (it is dealt even when no one defends), is assigned by the defending player among his characters (heroes and allies), cannot be redirected by Sentinel, and with X of 0 does nothing. Naurlhûg's "Cannot take damage" holds until Naurlhûg's Lair is in the victory display (50607:B:1: it "loses the text"), then the damage is dealt normally; 50607:B:3 the players win when Naurlhûg is defeated. Recommended on the timing: the card says "When ... makes an attack", read as the attack's start (`faq.enemy-attacks-outside-combat`, like Ered Mithrin's "When Crypt Stalker attacks").
  13. 50607:B:2 "After Naurlhûg attacks, if it was dealt a shadow card with no shadow effect, it engages the next player. Then it makes an immediate attack. Do not deal it a shadow card": the attack that just ended, shadow card face up with no Shadow text (a blank card counts); in a one-player game there is no next player (`faq.next-player`), so nothing happens; the new attack is an immediate attack against the next player, with the 50607 X damage again and no shadow card dealt (`faq.additional-attacks` does not apply: nothing is dealt). Literal, the next player being the player to the left of the one attacked.
  14. 50616 Lurker: "cannot be engaged" (no engagement check or effect engages it); "While in the staging area it is considered to be engaged with each player": as the Watcher in the Water is (Mount Gundabad's Dagnir, Urdug): it adds no Threat, any character may attack it, and it attacks each player in the combat phase; "cannot take more than 4 damage each round" is a ceiling for the round (the `damageCap` flag of Dagnir: damage placed on it in a round stops at 4). Recommended: the card says "each round", so the round counter resets at the round's end; no source says whether it resets earlier.
  15. 50617 Nameless Cave Dweller (Doomed 1 is on 50627 Echoes in the Dark only): its X (printed stats are X) is 1 more than the active location's Discover value; "+2 Attack instead" with a Loot objective controlled, per item 2. 50618 Old Stone Troll: -10 engagement cost per Loot objective attached to a hero (any hero, any player), the engagement cost not below 0; the stat is read at each engagement check. "Only the engaged player can declare attackers": as the Witch-king's (`pelennor.ts`). 50619 Orc: each player who controls at least 1 Loot, in turn order from the first player, is attacked in turn by an immediate attack (a shadow card dealt for each attack); a player with no Loot is not attacked; the Shadow effect ends the attack, engages the next player and attacks (none with one player). 50620 Goblin: surge while any Loot is attached to a hero; the Shadow effect puts it into play engaged with the player and deals it a shadow card, as Pelennor's "put into play engaged with you and deal it a shadow card".
  16. 50621 Naurlhûg's Lair: Discover 5; "Immune to player card effects"; Forced when explored adds the Loot from under Stalking the Ruins to the staging area faceup; Travel places X resources (X the players) on Stalking the Ruins. 50622:A:2 Abandoned Mine in the staging area: no progress can be placed on the other locations in the staging area (the players choose none of them for the progress of a card; Abandoned Mine can hold progress); the active location is not in the staging area, so progress still goes to it. Literal. 50623:A:2 Flooded Hall in the staging area: the cost of each ally the players play is up by 1 (`IncreaseCost`).
  17. 50624:A:2 Darkened Tunnel: "While Darkened Tunnel is in the staging area, it gains: 'Forced: After a "when revealed" effect is canceled, discard the top card of the encounter deck. If the discarded card has the Hazard trait, resolve its staging as if it was just revealed from the encounter deck.'" The trigger is the `whenRevealedCanceled` event of Nightmare's 906304, which every card in play hears; the discarded Hazard card is revealed in full (When Revealed, Surge, Doomed) from the discard pile, an enemy or location entering the staging area, a treachery resolving and then discarded; a non-Hazard card stays discarded. Recommended: "resolve its staging" names a reveal (`rr.reveal`; the card is the discard's top and the reveal comes from there).
  18. 50625 Sunken Treasury: +1 Threat for each Loot objective attached to a hero; Travel: each player discards 1 random card from his hand (`DiscardRandomFromHand` with `who: eachSeat`); Shadow: the player exhausts a character he controls, a ready one he chooses (`Exhaust`; no character ready, nothing). 50626 Secret Chamber (a Hazard location): "After the players travel to Secret Chamber, if no Loot objective is attached to Secret Chamber, search the encounter discard pile for a Loot objective and attach it to Secret Chamber as a guarded objective": Ered Mithrin's `AttachLootFromDiscard` with the active location as the guard (the first player chooses one of the discard pile's Loot objectives; none in the pile, nothing); Travel raises each player's threat by 2.
  19. 50627 Echoes in the Dark: "Add 1 to the total Threat in the staging area for each character controlled by the first player until the end of the phase": the number of characters (heroes and allies) the first player controls when the card is revealed, counted once; Shadow: discards 1 random card from the hand; if it was an event the enemy gets +2 Attack, otherwise nothing; an empty hand, nothing. 50628 Spawn of Thangorodrim: "If Naurlhûg is in play" is in the staging area or engaged; it attacks the first player from where it is; otherwise 1 resource goes on Stalking the Ruins and the 3-resource check adds Naurlhûg ("add", not "return": a set aside Naurlhûg); the check also runs after the resource of the Forced effect on Stalking. Shadow: an additional attack against the defender after this one, dealt a new shadow card (`faq.additional-attacks`: the attacker's earlier shadow cards are discarded before a new one is dealt). Span-settled.
  20. 50629 Concealed Spikes: X is 1 more than the Discover value; each player assigns X damage among characters he controls, in player order. Shadow: X is the number of attachments the defender controls (the Loot objectives attached to his heroes count as attachments, "Counts as an attachment", 50208:A:2's pattern; player card attachments on his characters too); "discard the highest cost attachment you control to cancel": the player chooses among attachments tied for the highest printed cost (an objective has no cost, so counts 0), a discarded Loot objective goes to the encounter discard pile, where only Secret Chamber's search finds it; the effect is canceled only when a card is discarded. Recommended: no source says where a discarded Loot goes; the discard pile is where Secret Chamber's search looks for it.
  21. 50630 Covered Pit: each player discards an ally he controls (a player with none, nothing; two or more, the player chooses). Shadow: "If attacking enemy is non-unique, attach a Loot objective you control to it as a guarded objective": the defender chooses one of the Loot objectives attached to his heroes (none, nothing); the objective is attached to the enemy as its guard and stays attached while the enemy is in play (`AttachGuard`); "if this attack destroys a character, return attacking enemy to the staging area" is a separate sentence and applies whatever the first sentence did, after the attack; it keeps the guarded objective (in the staging area, the objective is a guarded objective until the enemy leaves play). Literal.
  22. 50609 Sword of Belegost: "Loot. Restricted." (a hero with other restricted cards discards one of them: `rr.restricted`); "Response: Exhaust Sword of Belegost to cancel a shadow effect just triggered": the cancel Response to a shadow effect of an enemy attacking the player or a character of his, offered to the first player or the Sword's controller; the effect is cancelled, the shadow card stays dealt and is discarded as usual. 50610 Map: "non-unique location gets -2 Threat until the end of the phase" (Threat not below 0). 50611 Keys: a hero chosen receives 1 resource. 50613 Torch: -1 Attack and -1 Defense to a non-unique enemy until the end of the phase. 50614 Gem: the chosen player's threat drops by 1. The Actions are the controller's (the hero it is attached to, so that player). Literal.
  23. 50607:A:1 When Revealed (the quest card): Naurlhûg and Naurlhûg's Lair are added to the staging area and the Sword of Belegost and the encounter discard pile are shuffled into the encounter deck; the Lair is not revealed, so its Discover does not resolve until it is the active location. 50607 is the last stage: the stage has no quest points (the data prints none) and is never defeated by progress; the players win when Naurlhûg is defeated (`WinGame`, 50607:B:3).
## The table

- **The table.** The Table step (`docs/agents/agent-brief.md`, Scenario procedure; lines `50603:B:1`, `50603:B:2`, `50608:A:1`, `50615:A:2`; `src/play/readouts/standalone-scenarios/belegost.ts`, words `belegost-words.ts`; test `belegost.test.ts`): two top-bar dials after the seat dials. The Stalking dial counts the resources on Stalking the Ruins against the 3 that bring Naurlhûg (read from the card's own record), warms when the next quest phase brings him and, with Naurlhûg in play, says his X (50615:A:2); a tap shows the card. On stage 2D a goal dial counts the resources on the stage against the 3 it asks for (50603:B:1, 50603:B:2). The Loot objective facedown under Stalking the Ruins (50601:A:1) is the peek every game draws under a staging card, and it stays hidden: no readout says which Loot it is. The Loot objectives attached to heroes and to locations are the attachments and guarded objectives the table already draws. No pick needs more than the owner names the choice tray already gives. Checked with `pnpm browser shot --fixture belegost-lost --at 100 --landscape --tap text=Continue` (the Stalking dial reads 2).
- An enemy in the staging area that is considered engaged with a player is engaged with him for a card that reads "engaged with", and is no engagement event (docs/brief.md, Considered to be engaged).
