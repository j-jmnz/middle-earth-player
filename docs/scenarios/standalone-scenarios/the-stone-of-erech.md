# The Stone of Erech

The 2013 print-on-demand scenario (pack `TSoE`, Hall of Beorn lists it under the year `2013`), Normal and Easy. No Nightmare deck exists for it: the Nightmare source has no set for it (`scripts/data/nightmare-defs.ts`), Hall of Beorn lists no Nightmare card set for it, and its scenario page's Nightmare column only repeats the Normal and Easy quantities (https://hallofbeorn.com/LotR/Scenarios/The-Stone-of-Erech). Hall of Beorn lists no rulesheet for it (its resources are the Rules Reference alone) and `data/rules` holds none. The only source for its Night objective and Spectral rules is RulesPal's transcription of the scenario's rulesheet, a community source, so those readings are marked "researched, community (RulesPal transcription)".

- **Data.** Hall of Beorn numbers its 27 cards `00001` to `00027`, which are the earlier standalone packs' codes and RingsDB lists no card of it, so `overrides.productCodes` gives them `503` and the two-digit collector number: `50301` to `50303` the quest stages, `50304` Derufin, `50305` The Lord of the Dead, `50306` The Stone of Erech, `50307` Tarlang's Neck, `50308` to `50310` the Night objectives Eventide, Dusk and Midnight, `50311` Relic from the Dark Years, `50312` to `50327` the other encounter cards (50 is RingsDB's cycle 50, 3 the pack's position in it). The piles are DragnCards' deck Q99.03; it has no Easy deck, and Hall of Beorn's Easy counts equal the Normal ones, so Easy loads the same deck (community: 45 encounter cards in both modes, https://visionofthepalantir.com/2020/07/17/the-stone-of-erech/; no official source says otherwise). The encounter deck is the 38 cards of the set that no Setup line sets aside, Relic from the Dark Years among them. Tarlang's Neck is the active location (`overrides.scenarioPiles`, `50301:A:1`); Derufin, the Lord of the Dead, the Stone and the three Night objectives are set aside, out of play, and Eventide is placed into play next to the current quest (`scripts/data/normalize/setup-standalone-scenarios.ts` reads both Setup sentences).
- **Ledger.** The 30 printed sides (`data/printed/standalone-scenarios.json`) are read from the scans, not from Hall of Beorn's text, which prints "Tarlang's Next" for the printed Tarlang's Neck (50301 and 50307), "engaged all" for "engage all", "Exhuast", "insread", "attacks and Oathbreaker", "attacked" and "Attack enemy" for the printed wording, and a few capitals and a period before a parenthesis.
- **Readings.** Each is marked as `.claude/rules/authority.md` (Open points) asks; RulesPal's transcription of the rulesheet (community) was read at https://rulespal.com/the-lord-of-the-rings-the-card-game-the-stone-of-erech/rulebook, the community analysis at https://visionofthepalantir.com/2020/07/17/the-stone-of-erech/ and https://talesfromthecards.wordpress.com/2014/01/16/surviving-the-stone-of-erech/, and Caleb Grace's answers as compiled in https://alongextendedparty.com/semi-official-errata-faq-1-10/.
  - **Night objectives** (researched, community: RulesPal transcription). One Night objective is in play at a time, next to the current quest, and the players cannot claim it. A "Dusk." line is active only while Dusk is in play and a "Midnight." line only while Midnight is in play; Midnight's value replaces Dusk's ("instead"), and Eventide has no lines. The end-of-round Forced places 1 progress; a Night objective with 4 or more progress (Eventide, Dusk) goes to the victory display and the next comes into play with no progress, checked whenever progress lands, so Banks of Morthond's shadow and The Gloaming can trip it (recommended: agent recommendation; the community session report shows it mid-round). A Night objective that enters play during an end-of-round Forced does not place progress that round (`rr.forced`). Midnight is never capped and raises each player's threat by the progress now on it. "Dusk/Midnight." means Dusk or Midnight, not Eventide.
  - **Spectral** (researched, community: RulesPal transcription). A character attacking a Spectral enemy uses its current Willpower in place of its Attack to set attack strength; the enemy's Defense is still subtracted and direct damage is unaffected. Which modifiers count (Willpower modifiers yes, Attack bonuses no) is an agent recommendation, mirroring the engine's Battle. The Relic from the Dark Years (Midnight Action by any player, attached to a hero that player controls) removes Spectral from an Oathbreaker the attached hero attacks, for that attack and until the end of the phase (researched, community; "this very attack" is an agent recommendation).
  - **Battle on stage 3B** (researched, community: RulesPal transcription): "Midnight. Battle" is gained only while Midnight is in play. Stage 3B takes no progress of any kind on the quest card unless a player controls Derufin; progress on the active location is unaffected. Stage 1B cannot be defeated while Tarlang's Neck is in play, and excess progress when a stage is defeated is lost (researched, Caleb Grace, FAQ 3.169).
  - **Derufin** (recommended: agent recommendation, no ruling). After the players travel to The Stone of Erech the first player takes control of him: he leaves the Stone and enters their area ready as an ally. If he leaves play, wherever he is, the players lose.
  - **Group choices** (recommended: agent recommendation; the first player decides, as elsewhere in the app): stage 2B's choice is one per round (all players skip this round's planning phase, or no hero collects resources this round); Tarlang's Neck's Forced is one choice (end the encounter phase, or engage all enemies in the staging area, offered even with none).
  - **The Lord of the Dead** (researched, Caleb Grace, FAQ 2.74): every printed text box (abilities and keywords, not stats or Traits) of the characters of the player engaged with him is blank, from the moment he is engaged. **Haunted Valley**: ready heroes of every seat lose the four sphere icons, and the Planning Action is paid with a hero its player controls (`rr.cost`). **Murmurs of Dread** is a lasting effect recalculated as characters enter (`faq.lasting-effects`). **Groping Horror**: the hand passes to the next seat simultaneously. **Blackroot Graves** and **Restless Dead** move the topmost Undead enemy of the encounter discard pile without revealing it (recommended: as Wainrider Captain, The Massing at Osgiliath).
  - **The Stone of Erech's Threat** is X, twice the number of players (read from the scan: the data row has no Threat).
  - **Driven by Fear** (literal): the first player attaches it to any hero he controls, a hero that already carries a copy included (no copy filter is printed). **Groping Horror**'s Midnight surge is a constant of the card, so a canceled When Revealed does not cancel it (the surge is read as the card is resolved, before the treachery leaves the staging area). **Relic from the Dark Years**: the attack's Forced is heard only for a hero host.
  - **The table** (the Table step, `docs/agents/agent-brief.md`, Scenario procedure; lines `50301:A:1`, `50303:B:2`, `50304:A:1`, `50308:A:1`, `50308:A:2`, `50309:A:1`, `50309:A:2`, `50310:A:1`; `src/play/readouts/standalone-scenarios/erech.ts`, words `src/play/readouts/standalone-scenarios/erech-words.ts`): the Night objective in play is a top-bar dial named for it (Eventide, Dusk, Midnight), its progress against the 4 that brings the next Night in, read from the card's own limit (`50308:A:2`, `50309:A:2`); it warms within 2 of the limit and turns critical at 1, and its words name the Night that comes in. Midnight has no limit, so its dial never warms and its words say that the end of the round places 1 more progress and each player raises their threat by what it then holds (`50310:A:1`). A tap on either dial shows its card large, and each Night's dial is its own, so a Night that comes in shows no "lowered" badge. A second dial, Derufin, says Stone while he is attached to the Stone and Ally once a player controls him (`50304:A:1`), warning on stage 3B while no player controls him that no progress can be placed (`50303:B:2`); the seat that controls him wears a mark ("Derufin", "P2 · Derufin" two-handed) saying that if he leaves play the players lose. On stage 3B the quest readout says what the active location takes and that the stage takes none while no player controls Derufin; an attack on a Spectral enemy shows the attackers' Willpower as its strength and says so. The Night objective is drawn by its dial, not as a card on the table. The Spectral keyword is a chip on its enemies, struck through while the Relic has taken it away, with its own rule text (no rules span holds it). No other piece is added: the Stone is a location and Derufin a card in the staging or a play area like any other.

## Readings to confirm

- The Stone of Erech, agent recommendations or community only, not user-confirmed: the Night objectives' 4-progress check is passive and the next one enters with no progress; Spectral counts Willpower modifiers, not Attack bonuses; Relic from the Dark Years covers the attack it is played in; Derufin enters ready under the first player; stage 2B's and Tarlang's Neck's choices are the first player's; Restless Dead and Blackroot Graves move, not reveal; the Night objective, Spectral and stage 3B Battle rules come from RulesPal's transcription, a community source (`docs/scenarios/standalone-scenarios/the-stone-of-erech.md`, The Stone of Erech).
