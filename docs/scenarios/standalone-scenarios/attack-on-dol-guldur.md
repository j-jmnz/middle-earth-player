# Attack on Dol Guldur

The 2017 print-on-demand scenario (pack `AoDG`, RingsDB's code for it, position 9 of cycle 50; Hall of Beorn lists it under the year `2017`), Normal and Easy, the ninth of the cycle. It is played in the Standard Game Mode, 1 to 4 players. The Epic Multiplayer Mode (3 teams at 3 stage 3 cards at once, each with its own staging area and encounter deck) is not played, for the reason given under The Siege of Annúminas. No Nightmare deck exists for it, and neither Hall of Beorn nor DragnCards (no Rules row for the pack) holds a rulesheet or insert; the rules are the quest cards, the two mode cards and the printed Siege value of each enemy and location.

- **Data.** Hall of Beorn numbers its cards `00001` to `00032`, which collide with The Massing at Osgiliath's; RingsDB lists none of the pack's cards. `overrides.productCodes` gives them `509` and the collector number (`50901` to `50906` the quest cards 1A, 2A and the stage 3 choices 3A, 3C, 3E and 3G, `50907` Dol Guldur, `50908` Power of Lórien, `50909` Host of Lórien, `50910` to `50913` the Lieutenants Bane of Amon Lanc, Sorcerer of Dol Guldur, Chieftain Morlug and Dungeon Dweller, `50914` to `50930` the rest of the encounter deck, `50931` Standard Game Mode, `50932` Epic Multiplayer Mode). The piles are DragnCards' deck Q99.10, with no Easy twin: Hall of Beorn's Easy counts equal the Normal ones, so Easy loads the same deck. The scenario's `modeCard` is Standard Game Mode (`50931`, read with the quest card's Setup); `50932` is in the catalog and the ledger and excluded from the deal (`overrides.scenarioExcluded`). Dol Guldur and Power of Lórien start staged; the Host of Lórien and the four Lieutenants are set aside (1A Setup); Dol Guldur Marauder has 4 copies, the 1A Setup stages 1 per player and the rest shuffle into the encounter deck.
- **Mode cards.** Printed, Standard Game Mode front: "Setup: Add Dol Guldur to the staging area, attach a threat dial to it, and set its value to 5. Then, increase its value by 5 for each player. For the rest of the game, that threat dial is the siege tracker and it displays the city strength of Dol Guldur. Flip this card over." Back: "While Dol Guldur's city strength is 0, each Lieutenant enemy loses indestructible." "While the players are at stage 3, this card gains: 'Forced: After resolving the Forced effect on Dol Guldur, randomly select a stage 3 that was not in play this round and resolve its Forced effect. Then, discard all progress from the current stage 3 and advance to a new stage 3 of your choice.'" "At the end of the round, if Dol Guldur's city strength is 0 and there are no Lieutenant enemies in play, Dol Guldur is captured and the players win the game." The Epic Multiplayer Mode card (not built) sets up 3 staging areas and 3 teams, adds a response to give control of Host of Lórien or Power of Lórien to another player, and loses the game for the group when the last player at a stage is eliminated.
- **Siege value and city strength.** City strength is Dol Guldur's value on its threat dial (5 plus 5 per player: 10 true solo, 15 for two). Each enemy and location of the encounter deck prints a Siege value in a box at the bottom right, beside Victory; the data holds none of them, so the effect records read them from the scans: Host of Dol Guldur 4, Mirkwood Olog-hai 3, Beast of Taur-nu-Fuin 2, Amon Lanc Archer 1, Dol Guldur Marauder 1, Battle Warg 1, and the four Dol Guldur locations (Gate of Dol Guldur, The Sorcerer's Tower, The Shadow Road, Dungeon Door) 2 each. Forest Battleground, Blackened Woods, the treacheries and the Lieutenants print none. Dol Guldur's own text (50907) reduces the city strength by the total siege value of each location explored and each enemy destroyed this round, at the end of the round at stage 3 only. Archery X and Immune to player card effects are read as in The Siege of Annúminas.
- **Ledger.** The 40 printed sides (both mode cards, all 6 quest cards and the 24 encounter cards) are read from the scans (`data/printed/standalone-scenarios.json`). Hall of Beorn's text differs in four places: 50906 B "an ally an puts it into play" (printed "an ally and puts"), 50913 A lacks "Immune to player card effects." on the Indestructible line (printed on all four Lieutenants), 50920 A "The player cannot travel" (printed "The players cannot travel"), and 50931 A "increase it's value" (printed "its value"). No printed typo is kept as a misprint.
- **Readings.** Numbered; the status is `literal`, `span-settled`, `researched` or `recommended` (agent recommendation). Official sources and `faq.*` spans say nothing on this scenario beyond `faq.lasting-effects`, `faq.additional-attacks` and the Rules Reference. Read: the FFG forum archive threads https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/286633-win-condition-for-attack-on-dol-guldur-standard-game-mode and https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/279060-attack-on-dol-guldur-confused-on-win-condition (players only, no FFG answer), the fan analysis https://visionofthepalantir.com/2023/07/21/attack-on-dol-guldur/, Hall of Beorn's card pages (https://www.hallofbeorn.com/LotR/Details/The-Siege-of-Dol-Guldur-AoDG, https://www.hallofbeorn.com/LotR/Details/Standard-Game-Mode-Campaign-AoDG) and the FFG news post https://www.fantasyflightgames.com/en/news/2018/2/15/attack-on-dol-guldur/ (no rules text).   1. "Each stage", "each team", "at this stage", "at any stage" and "each player at each stage" read as the one stage and the whole table; the Epic sentences are not played (`literal`, as The Siege of Annúminas, reading 2).
  2. The Siege value is a printed box, not a text line, so it is on no ledger line and in no source. `overrides.cards` gives each card a keyword field `siegeValue` (a new numeric field of the printed keywords; the `siege` flag of `hon.siege` on a quest card is another thing): Host of Dol Guldur 4, Mirkwood Olog-hai 3, Beast of Taur-nu-Fuin 2, Amon Lanc Archer, Dol Guldur Marauder and Battle Warg 1, Gate of Dol Guldur, The Sorcerer's Tower, The Shadow Road and Dungeon Door 2; the other cards have none (`literal`: read from the scans; https://visionofthepalantir.com/2023/07/21/attack-on-dol-guldur/ gives the same four enemy values).
  3. City strength is the resource tokens on Dol Guldur, as in The Siege of Annúminas (reading 4): the mode card's Setup record places 5 and then 5 for each player, the threat dial is the readout, "reduce by X" removes tokens down to 0, "increase by 3" adds them. No card loses the game at any strength (`literal`).
  4. X of Dol Guldur's Forced (50907:A:1) is the siege value of every location explored and every enemy destroyed this round, by any means and at any stage, counted when it happens into a round tally and cleared at each round's end after the Forced resolves. The Forced exists only at stage 3, so nothing is taken off at stage 1 or 2; the round the players reach stage 3 counts its earlier destroyed enemies and explored locations (`recommended` for that round; the rest `literal`; the fan analysis agrees that a defeat at stage 1 reduces nothing). A Lieutenant or a card with no value adds 0.
  5. End of the round at stage 3, in this order: the stage's Forced, Dol Guldur's Forced (the reduction), the win check (50931:B:3), then the mode card's random-stage Forced (50931:B:2). The cards fix only the middle order; the win check precedes the advance so a win is not lost to a new stage and Lieutenant (`researched`, community only: both forum threads, a player chooses the order of triggers and the passive win check comes before the advance; not an official ruling).
  6. 50931:B:2, "randomly select a stage 3 that was not in play this round": a Standard game has one stage 3 at a time, so the stage 3 in play this round is the current one, and the pick is a seeded random choice of the other three. Its Forced effect resolves as printed with "this stage" the selected card, which holds no progress: the "remove 5 progress" option cannot be taken, so the penalty happens, and the first player's "additional 5 progress" option is not offered. "Each player at each stage" is each player. Then all progress on the current stage 3 is discarded and the first player advances to a new stage 3 of his choice, any of the other three (`recommended`; an unattended stage with no progress is the Epic design, and the fan analysis names the effect without explaining it).
  7. 50903:A:1 to 50906:A:1: "the Lieutenant enemy at this stage" is the Lieutenant of the stage 3 the players leave (in play, in the staging area or engaged), which loses every token and goes out of play (set aside); coming from stage 2B there is none. The new stage's Lieutenant is added to the staging area unless it is in a victory display. At 5 or less city strength it makes an immediate attack against each player in turn order (`literal`; https://visionofthepalantir.com/2023/07/21/attack-on-dol-guldur/ agrees). The Lieutenants' engagement cost goes down by the progress on the current stage 3 (50903:B:1 to 50906:B:1).
  8. The stage 3 Forced lines (50903:B:2 to 50906:B:2): the first player chooses between "remove 5 progress from this stage" and the penalty; an option nobody can do is not offered, and the "additional 5 progress" step is offered only after the first 5 came off and only while 5 more progress is on the stage (`recommended`; as The Siege of Annúminas, reading 16; https://visionofthepalantir.com/2023/07/21/attack-on-dol-guldur/ reads 10 progress for 3A's extra reduction). 50903: penalty +3 city strength, extra -3. 50904: each player discards all but 1 card from his hand; extra: a chosen player searches his deck for a card, adds it to his hand and shuffles. 50905: each player discards from the encounter deck until an enemy is discarded and puts it into play engaged with him (the discard pile is reshuffled into the deck if the last card goes); extra: destroy a chosen non-unique enemy in play (it adds its siege value to the round's tally). 50906: each player discards an ally he controls; extra: a chosen player searches the top 10 cards of his deck for an ally and puts it into play (`literal`).
  9. 50901:B:1 resolves in the card's order: place 1 resource, move all progress from the stage to Power of Lórien (which holds it as a card in the staging area), then advance at 3 resources (`literal`, "If"). 50901:B:2 is a Response to the end of the round: each player's threat goes up by 1 as its cost (`rr.cost`), then each player draws 1 card and adds 1 resource to a hero of his choice. The stage's own advance resolves before any Response (`rr.forced`), so no Response is possible on the round the stage advances (`literal`; the fan analysis agrees).
  10. 50902:A:1: the first player may remove 10 progress from Power of Lórien to take control of Host of Lórien (into his play area from the set-aside area). Only after that may he remove an additional 20 to put Power of Lórien into his play area under his control; in every other case Power of Lórien is discarded, whether he declined, could not pay or took no Host (`researched`: https://visionofthepalantir.com/2023/07/21/attack-on-dol-guldur/ reads 10 for the Host and 30 in all for the Power; the discard in every other case is `recommended`).
  11. 50902:B:1: each player, in turn order, searches the encounter deck and discard pile for a Dol Guldur location and adds it to the staging area (a player with none to find adds none), the deck is shuffled, and the first player chooses the stage 3 to advance to: any of the four (`literal`).
  12. Gate of Dol Guldur, The Sorcerer's Tower, The Shadow Road and Dungeon Door gain their line while in the staging area: the players cannot travel to it or place progress on it unless the current stage is 3B (Gate), 3D (Sorcerer's Tower), 3F (Shadow Road) or 3H (Dungeon Door), the back sides of the stage 3 cards 50903 to 50906. At stage 1 or 2 none can be travelled to (`literal`). The gained lines are the card's own effects (a threat reduction cap of 1 per round on the Gate, healing 1 per character per round on the Dungeon Door, a reveal after a canceled When Revealed on the Tower, an event attached facedown on the Shadow Road); they work only while the card is in the staging area.
  13. 50922:A:2: an event played during the quest phase is attached facedown to The Shadow Road after it resolves, in place of its discard; with two copies in the staging area the first player chooses which takes it (the engine resolves simultaneous Forced effects oldest card first, so the older copy takes it). Each facedown card is +2 Threat (`recommended`; `rr.event-cards` puts the event in the discard pile after it resolves, and the Forced then takes it from there).
  14. 50928:A:1 and :2, 50930:A:2 and other lasting effects of an encounter card are recalculated while the game changes (`span-settled`, `faq.lasting-effects`): "each damaged character gets -1 Willpower" and "-1 Defense for each damage on it" follow the damage on the card until the end of the phase. 50930:A:1: no more than 5 progress can be placed on the current quest this round, from every source; surge while the city strength is 5 or less is read when it is revealed (`literal`).
  15. "Either ... or ..." lines (50915, 50916, 50917, 50918, 50919, 50927): an option the player cannot do is not offered, and a player with neither does nothing; the player the card names chooses (`recommended`, as The Siege of Annúminas, reading 16).
  16. 50910:A:2: with an empty hand nothing is discarded and X is 0; X is the printed cost, 0 for a card with none. 50925:A:1: X is the printed Threat of the discarded card, 0 for a card with none or a missing top card. 50926:A:2: ties for the highest engagement cost are broken by the first player; with no enemy in the staging area nothing happens. 50924:A:1 counts the allies of the player with the most (`recommended`).
  17. 50915:A:1: an excess point of damage is a point of the attack's damage beyond the character's remaining hit points (`literal`, `rr.damage`). 50911:A:2: "each copy of that card" is each card in play of the discarded card's title, controlled by any player (`literal`, `rr.unique-cards`).
  18. 50931:B:1: while city strength is 0 each Lieutenant has no Indestructible, whatever its printed keyword; a Lieutenant's damage stays on it until its stage changes (`literal`, https://visionofthepalantir.com/2023/07/21/attack-on-dol-guldur/). 50931:B:3 is a win at the end of the round, not a Forced effect that waits for a trigger (`literal`).
  19. 50909:A:3 and :4: Host of Lórien quests without exhausting; if it leaves play for any reason it is removed from the game (`literal`). 50908:A:1: while a player controls Power of Lórien it gains a Response that adds it to the victory display (its cost) to cancel and discard an encounter card just revealed from the encounter deck (`literal`; `rr.victory`).
- **The table.** The Table step (`docs/agents/agent-brief.md`, Scenario procedure; lines `50907:A:1`, `50931:A:1`, `50931:B:1`, `50931:B:3`; `src/play/readouts/standalone-scenarios/guldur.ts`, words `guldur-words.ts`; test `guldur.test.ts`): one top-bar dial after the seat dials. The City dial reads the city strength, the resource tokens on Dol Guldur, on its disc, with no ring: it counts down to the win at 0 and has no limit above, so only its warnings colour it. Its note says the round's tally, the Siege value of the enemies destroyed and the locations explored this round, which comes off at the end of a stage 3 round (before stage 3 it says nothing comes off), and that at 0 the Lieutenants lose Indestructible and the players win at the end of a round with none in play. It warns near at 5 or less and critical when, at stage 3, the tally is as much as the strength left; a tap shows Dol Guldur, then the Standard Game Mode that lies beside the quest deck. The tally is a number on the game state (`siegeTally`) that only this dial and Dol Guldur's Forced read. Power of Lórien, the Host of Lórien, the Lieutenants and the facedown cards under The Shadow Road are cards in the staging, a play area or under their host; no record creates a held dial or a zone. Every pick that spans seats (Servant of Sauron's attacker, the stage 3 extras' player) leads with "Player N" two-handed.
- **Not built.** The Epic Multiplayer Mode (50932) and the Epic game: no Standard card needs them.
