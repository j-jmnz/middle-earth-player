# Fog on the Barrow-downs

The 2014 print-on-demand scenario (pack `FotBd`, RingsDB's code for it), Normal and Easy, the fifth of the cycle. Hall of Beorn files it under the product `2014` beside The Old Forest, so its product line names `scenarioTitle` too. No Nightmare deck exists for it: `scripts/data/nightmare-defs.ts` and `data/raw/nightmare` have no set for it, and Hall of Beorn's Nightmare count (42) only repeats Normal (https://hallofbeorn.com/LotR/Scenarios/Fog-on-the-Barrow-downs). Hall of Beorn lists no rulesheet and `data/rules` holds none; the sources for its rules are RulesPal's transcription (https://rulespal.com/the-lord-of-the-rings-the-card-game-fog-on-the-barrow-downs/rulebook) and the quest cards, so the readings below are marked "researched, community (RulesPal transcription)".

- **Data.** Hall of Beorn numbers its cards `148001` to `148021`, RingsDB's own codes for the pack (cycle 50, position 5 holds only 148021, Ho! Tom Bombadil!), so they collide with nothing and no `productCodes` rule is needed: `148001` to `148004` the four stages (stage 4A has four copies, one for each player who enters a Great Barrow), `148005` Hollow Circle, `148006` Standing Stones, `148007` Great Barrow, `148019` Tom Bombadil, `148020` the campaign card, `148021` the boon. Tom Bombadil is the same printed card as The Old Forest's `147022` (Hall of Beorn serves one scan, one sha256, for both, set Fog on the Barrow-downs 41/41) and DragnCards loads one Tom Bombadil in both decks; each scenario deals the code of its own box, and The Old Forest's data does not move. Aragorn (01001) is tagged with the card set in Hall of Beorn's player cards and stays a Core Set card. The piles are DragnCards' deck Q99.05: stage 1A's Setup sets each copy of Great Barrow (5), Standing Stones and Hollow Circle aside; the 34 other cards of the set (Tom Bombadil among them) are the encounter deck. The load list's Frodo Baggins and The One Ring in player 1's play area are not dealt, as in The Old Forest.
- **Easy.** DragnCards has no Easy deck (no E99.05), so Easy loads Q99.05 and `overrides.scenarioCounts` takes out what Hall of Beorn's Easy counts remove (42 Normal and 33 Easy encounter cards; its page notes only that I'm Waiting for You! is not in Easy mode): 2 I'm Waiting for You! (2 to 0), 2 Great Barrow (5 to 3, in the set-aside pile), and one copy each of Ancient Barrow (3 to 2), Barrow-wight (4 to 3), Frozen by Fear (3 to 2), Dreadful Song (2 to 1) and Stone Ring (4 to 3), 9 in all. The gold mark is printed per copy, so a scan cannot confirm which copies; Hall of Beorn's counts are the only source (recommended: agent recommendation).
- **Campaign card and boon.** Fog on the Barrow-downs (148020, "The Lord of the Rings Part 1.2": "You are playing Campaign Mode", while any player is at stage 4B the first player cannot lose control of the first player token, Resolution earns the Ho! Tom Bombadil! boon) and the boon (148021: Setup adds it to the first player's hand; Response adds it to the victory display to cancel the "when revealed" effects of an encounter card just revealed) belong to Campaign Mode after The Old Forest. The cycle is standalone only with no campaign log, so the build deals neither (`campaignPlaced` is empty) and their three printed sides are in the ledger with every line inert (Campaign Mode only); the two cards are excepted in `scripts/checks/box-dealt.test.ts`. Hall of Beorn prints the campaign card's second line as a "Setup:" the card does not have. The standalone game ends at stage 3B ("If the players defeat this stage, they win the game").
- **Ledger.** The 23 printed sides of the dealt cards are read from the scans (`data/printed/standalone-scenarios.json`). Hall of Beorn's text has "Great Barrows" (148003 A), "the a Great Barrow" and a missing comma (148006), "The player cannot travel hereo." for "The players cannot travel here." (148007), "heros" (148013), "Then shuffle" for "Then, shuffle" (148019), single quotes and "Willpower is 0" for the printed double quotes and "Willpower is reduced to 0" (Dreadful Song), and "Setup:" on the campaign card.
- **Readings.** From RulesPal's transcription (researched, community): the Staging step reveals encounter cards one at a time in player order, "you" on a card being the revealing player and a Surge card giving that player an additional card; Peril means that player resolves the card alone without conferring and the others cannot act or trigger responses; a player told to create a staging area sets aside a separate area in front of himself, each area resolves as if only the players sharing it were present, players cannot affect players or cards outside their area, each player in an area reveals one card in the encounter phase and "each player" effects reach only that area; stage 3B and its staging area stay in play until defeated and a phase is skipped there when no player is at stage 3B; combining staging areas moves each enemy and location of one area into the other, enemies engaged with the player staying engaged; "Immune to player card effects" ignores all player card effects and cannot be targeted by them. The transcription says nothing on Easy mode (recommended: agent recommendation). The split-staging-area rules are new to the engine: the scenario author reads `148003:B:1` to `148003:B:3`, `148004:B:2`, `148007:A:2`, `148009:A:1`, `148005:A:2` and `148006:A:2` against them.
- **Line readings** (Read task; status after each; "community" = RulesPal, Vision of the Palantír and forum posts, not official; sources in the closing line).
  - Areas apart (every line that says "each player", "you" or "this stage" once a player is trapped): Fog's own rule insert, quoted on the FFG forum and the same paragraph as `mec108.create-staging-area`, applies: each phase resolves as if only the players sharing a staging area were in the game, players cannot affect players or cards outside their area, a player reveals 1 card for each player sharing his area, and an encounter card's effect, "each player" included, reaches only the players at its stage; the first player of an area is the one nearest the token among its players; the players as a group still cannot have 2 copies of a unique card. The printed "during the encounter phase, players only reveal 1 card" is read as the staging step, the only step that reveals. Status: span-settled (`mec108.create-staging-area`, the Mount Gram engine reads it so).
  - Doomed (Frozen by Fear 148017, 1): an encounter card's Doomed X "still affects each player" whatever the area (the insert; RulesPal), so Frozen by Fear raises every living player's threat by 1, unlike Mount Gram's Doomed, which stays in the area. A Doomed on a player card raises only the players at the same stage (Caleb Grace, FFG, by email to a forum poster, Oct 2015: "players and player cards cannot be affected by player cards at a different stage"). Status: researched (official for the player card; the insert for the encounter card; https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/124254-doomed-cards-in-separate-staging-areas).
  - Partial split (`148007:A:2`, `148004:B:2`): only the player who revealed a Great Barrow leaves; the others stay at stage 3B with the shared staging area, and 3B and its staging area stay in play until it is defeated, with no player at it too (a phase at a stage with no player is skipped). Great Barrow stays in the staging area at 3B and the new area starts empty; the player takes one of stage 4A's copies, side B up, and its When Revealed resolves for him. "You" on a Great Barrow added by Standing Stones' Travel is the first player of the area that travelled. Status: researched (RulesPal; the FFG designer's email in the Semi-official FAQ item 3.181 says the Great Barrow "is still in the 3B staging area"; https://alongextendedparty.com/semi-official-errata-faq-1-10/; https://visionofthepalantir.com/2019/03/13/fog-on-the-barrow-downs/ for the Standing Stones case).
  - A player already at stage 4A who reveals a location (a Great Barrow included) has it canceled and discarded by 4B's Forced effect, so he is never trapped twice at once (`148004:B:1`: "When a location is revealed at this stage", the revealer is the seat at the stage; "including modifiers" is the Threat the card would count in the staging area). Status: literal.
  - Stage 4A When Revealed (`148004:A:1`): "End your quest phase" is that player's alone (no more cards revealed for him, his quest not resolved; the others finish theirs); the Wight is the first one the discards reach, added to his own area, not engaged; X counts the enemies engaged with him before that Wight; he chooses which X allies stay (fewer than X: none discarded). Status: literal (Vision of the Palantír agrees on X and on the Wight staying in the staging area). With the encounter deck empty before a Wight is discarded, the discard pile is shuffled in (`faq.last-encounter-card`); with no Wight anywhere none is added. Status: recommended (agent recommendation; no ruling found).
  - Stage 4B defeated (`148004:B:2`): the player leaves 4A at the beginning of that round's travel phase and joins stage 3B (no When Revealed: 3B is already in play); the enemies and locations of his area move to 3B's area, engaged enemies stay engaged; 1 progress goes on a Great Barrow at 3B (the one that trapped him if still there, else any; with 1 quest point it is explored and goes to the victory display); none there: nothing. When 4B leaves play because its player is eliminated, one Great Barrow in play goes to the victory display instead (no progress step; the stage at 3B is not discarded with him). Status: researched (official: the FFG designer's email, "1 copy of Great Barrow should be added to the victory display from play when 1 copy of stage 4B leaves play (whether from being defeated or the player at that stage being eliminated)", Semi-official FAQ item 3.181; the community errata proposal at https://alongextendedparty.com/community-errata-free-to-choose-list/ gives the progress wording for elimination). Defeating a 4B does not win the game (only 3B's own line says so).
  - Stage 4A copies: the 4 printed copies are one for each player; a copy left by defeat or elimination goes back to the quest deck with no progress, so a player trapped again, or a 2-seat game trapped more than 4 times, still has one. Status: recommended (agent recommendation; no ruling found).
  - Stage 3B (`148003:B:1` to `B:3`): progress cannot be placed on the stage while any Great Barrow is in play (a staged one; the victory display and the discard pile are not in play), which stops the surplus of a quest resolution and a card effect but not progress on the active location, Standing Stones included; the Forced effect hears every elimination, the trapped player's and one by threat included, while 3B is the stage in play, and raises the players sharing 3B's area by 10 (none at 3B: nobody; each elimination is its own occurrence); the stage cannot be defeated unless every living player shares its area and Standing Stones is in the victory display, and defeating it wins the game. Status: literal (Vision of the Palantír: "any player being eliminated, even in their own stage 4, raises everybody at stage 3's threat by a whopping ten").
  - Stage 3A When Revealed (`148003:A:1`): in the text's order: the first player adds Standing Stones, each other player reveals 1 card, then the discard pile and the set-aside Great Barrows are shuffled in, so no Great Barrow is revealed by those cards. Status: literal (Vision of the Palantír).
  - Stages 1 and 2 (`148001`, `148002`): the Setup's set-aside is the data's piles and only the shuffle is the record; 1B skips the quest phase and places 1 progress at the end of each round (it advances then); 2A adds Hollow Circle and each player reveals 1 card; 2B's Forced effect raises every player's threat at the end of the refresh phase (nobody is apart yet); 2B cannot be defeated until Hollow Circle is in the victory display. Status: literal.
  - Hollow Circle and Standing Stones Travel (`148005:A:2`, `148006:A:2`): each player (the first player) searches the deck and the discard pile (and Standing Stones the victory display too, where a Great Barrow loses its Victory points by leaving it) for a Wight (a Great Barrow) and puts it into play engaged (adds it to the staging area); the deck is shuffled whether or not one was found. Both, and Great Barrow, are immune to player card effects. Status: literal; the shuffle recommended (agent recommendation; as `147001:A:1`).
  - Stone Ring (`148009:A:1`): both clauses count the Wights engaged with players at the stage Stone Ring is at; each such Wight gets +1 Attack and +1 Defense, and the first sentence (the constant, not the Forced effect) of each Wight name engaged with one of them applies to each player at that stage. Status: span-settled (`mec108.create-staging-area`: effects stay in the area).
  - Ancient Barrow (`148008`): Threat X is 1 plus the facedown cards under it (Threat X on the scan); after it enters play each player at its stage places an ally he controls facedown (his choice; none: nothing); the allies go to their owners' hands when it leaves play explored, and no other way out of play exists (stage 4B discards a revealed location before it enters play). Status: literal.
  - North Downs (`148010`): while staged, the characters of the players at its stage defend against Wight enemies with their Willpower in place of Defense (allies included); its Shadow's "each player" is each player at the defender's stage. Status: literal; the area span.
  - Wights (`148011` to `148013`): the first sentence is a constant on the engaged player: threat cannot be reduced; cannot draw cards from card effects (the Resource-phase draw is a framework step, `rr.effects`); heroes cannot gain resources from card effects (collecting is not). The Forced effect hears the attack that destroys a character, the character's controller raising, discarding a random card or discarding every resource of each hero. Status: literal (`rr.effects`).
  - I'm Waiting for You! (`148014`): each Wight engaged with a player at the revealer's stage makes an immediate attack against that player, one after the other in player order; none made: it gains surge. Not in Easy. Chill Fog (`148016`): "resources in play" are the resources in the heroes' pools (`rr.resources`), each player at the revealer's stage discarding his and raising his threat by 1 for each; none discarded in all: surge. Status: literal; span-settled for the area.
  - Long Groping Arm (`148015`, Peril): the lasting effect makes each Wight engaged with the revealer, one engaged afterwards by the search included, add its Threat to the total in his staging area until the end of the phase (`rr.lasting-effects`); "no enemies engaged" means no enemy of any kind, then the Wight is searched for, put into play engaged and the deck shuffled. Status: literal.
  - Frozen by Fear (`148017`): players at the revealer's stage cannot trigger Action or Response effects until the end of the round; a player who joins that stage later in the round is not held (agent recommendation, rare). Status: span-settled; the joiner recommended.
  - Dreadful Song (`148018`, Peril): the revealer attaches it to a hero he controls that is committed to the quest and has no Dreadful Song; with none the treachery is discarded. The attached hero's Willpower is 0 for as long as it is attached, and at each end of the round its controller raises his threat by 1 for each Wight engaged with him. Status: literal.
  - Tom Bombadil (`148019`): the same printed text as The Old Forest's `147022`, read as that card; the player chosen is one sharing the revealer's area, the first player of that area choosing (not a Peril card). Status: recommended (agent recommendation; as The Old Forest).
  - Engine readings (Build task; agent recommendation). A stage no player is at (stage 3B once every player is trapped, or its player eliminated) is parked: it stays in play with its staging cards, its active location goes back to the staging area and nothing resolves for it until a player returns to it. Stage 4B's Forced effect on a location reveal raises the threat by the location's Threat as it counts in the staging area, then discards it; no card is revealed in its place. When stage 4B is defeated the player chooses the Great Barrow among those at stage 3B (the printed text says "a Great Barrow at that stage"; none preferred). Frozen by Fear also stops the held players from playing events, whose Action or Response is the play. Stage 3B and stage 4B both hold their defeat as a final stage does (`holdsWin`): 3B's own Forced effect wins the game, and stage 4B is left by its own Forced effect, never by a win.
  - Finish readings (agent recommendation unless marked). Great Barrow, Hollow Circle and Standing Stones print "Immune to player card effects", which Hall of Beorn lists no keyword for: three `data/overrides.json` entries turn Immune on (status: literal, from the scans). Stage 3B's "Progress cannot be placed here" bars every placement on the stage while a Great Barrow is in play, the quest's and a card's (`putOnStage`, `noProgressPlaced` on the stage). Stage 4A's `DiscardUntil` carries an opt-in `reshuffle` flag: it shuffles the encounter discard pile into an empty deck and goes on when the discard pile holds a Wight, and ends with nothing found when neither pile does (`faq.last-encounter-card`, `rr.running-out-of-cards`; no other scenario's DiscardUntil changes, because a general reshuffle would move the goldens of the scenarios that use it; status: recommended, see Line readings for `148004:A:1`).
  - **The table.** A zone or widget exists only for a card line that creates it (`docs/design-system-table.md`, Top bar). Stage 3B parked (no player at it; `148003:B:1`, `148007:A:2`, `148004:B:2`) is in no seat's row, so it is a top-bar dial, "Parked 3B", whose note names the stage and the cards of its staging area and says nothing resolves there until a player comes back from stage 4B, and whose tap shows the stage large with its staging cards listed after it; the cards of that area are drawn in no seat's row and are not on the table for a pick, which opens as the tray. Each seat's own stage 4A, staging row, quest card and active location are the shown seat's (as Breaking of the Fellowship), and a player trapped there wears the mark "Trapped" ("P2 · Trapped"). The advance screen tells the trap as one ("Trapped", who stays at stage 3B, when he comes back, button Continue), the return is a banner beat ("Player 2 is back at stage 3B…"), and stage 4B's cancel of a revealed location is a line of the banner and the log. The quest readout says no progress goes on stage 3B while a Great Barrow is in play and, when the progress would fill it, that it is held until every player is at it and Standing Stones is in the victory display (`148003:B:1`, `148003:B:3`), so it never promises progress or a win the engine withholds; the foresight of a quest that eliminates a player names stage 3B's 10 threat raise (`148003:B:2`). Code: `src/play/readouts/standalone-scenarios/barrow-downs.ts`, `src/play/store/selectors.ts`.
  - **The Great Barrow for an eliminated player's stage 4B (`148004:B:2`).** The designer's ruling sends one Great Barrow in play to the victory display; the player who was eliminated cannot choose it, so the first player (a player still in the game) does. Status: recommended (agent recommendation; no source says who chooses; not user-confirmed).
