# The Hunt for the Dreadnaught

The 2020 print-on-demand scenario (pack `THftD`, RingsDB's code for it; Hall of Beorn lists it under its own name), the fourteenth and last of the cycle, Normal and Easy alike. It is a fleet scenario: each player commands a ship-objective, the Corsair deck feeds Boarding, and the quest ends when the Dreadnaught is destroyed. The sea rules are the Dream-chaser ones (`dcce.ship-objectives`, `dcce.ship-enemies`, `dcce.prepare-your-fleet`, `dcce.corsair-deck`, `dcce.boarding`). The box has no rulesheet in `data/rules` and Hall of Beorn and DragnCards hold no insert; the rules are the three mode cards, the two quest cards and the box rulebook, whose text RulesPal transcribes (https://rulespal.com/the-lord-of-the-rings-the-card-game-the-hunt-for-the-dreadnaught/rulebook) and whose scenario is reviewed at https://visionofthepalantir.com/2022/01/16/the-hunt-for-the-dreadnaught/. No FAQ entry, official ruling or designer answer names the box (the FFG forum thread https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/311373-the-hunt-for-the-dreadnaught holds only speculation from before the release).

- **Data.** RingsDB's own codes are kept (no `productCodes`): `40001` Easy Mode, `40002` Standard Mode, `40003` Hard Mode, `40004` stage 1 (Searching the High Seas), `40005` stage 2 (Destroy the Dreadnaught), `40006` to `40011` the six fleet ships (Eithiliant, Golden Wing, Pride of Lebennin, Silent Mist, Swan Ship of Dol Amroth, Twilight's Call, each with an Upgraded side B), `40012` Gondorian Warship (4 copies), `40013` the Dreadnaught (Elite side A, Damaged side B), `40014` to `40019` the six unique Corsairs (Captain of the Haven, Commander of the Dreadnaught, Descendant of Castamir, Hydraken, Ophidian, Varjax), `40020` to `40025` the 13 non-unique Corsairs of the Corsair deck (Brash Corsair 1, Corsair Plunderer 3, Daring Buccaneer 3, Fierce Marauder 3, Southron Sailor 2, Umbar Captain 1), `40026` to `40032` the seven Corsair ship-enemies (11 copies), `40033` Sea Monster (2), `40034` to `40039` six locations (15), `40040` to `40046` seven treacheries (15) and `40047` the contract A Perilous Voyage (a player card, not dealt). The piles are DragnCards' deck Q99.15 with no Easy twin: its `sharedEncounterDeck` is the 43-card encounter deck (13 ship-enemies and Sea Monsters, 15 locations, 15 treacheries), `sharedEncounterDeck2` the Corsair deck (`corsairDeck`), `sharedQuestDeck` the two stages and `sharedSetAside` the three mode cards, the six ships, the four Gondorian Warships and the seven unique enemies. The product is `ledgerKeywords` and `sidedDragnRows` like the Dream-chaser boxes. Hall of Beorn files Sea Monster as a ship-enemy; its scan prints ENEMY (a Creature), so `overrides.cards` sets the type. Hall of Beorn has no stat fields for ships: the printed stats of the fleet (both sides), the Gondorian Warship, the seven Corsair ships, Sea Monster and the Dreadnaught are `overrides.cards` rows read from the scans (the ledger notes repeat them). The Dreadnaught prints no hit point number on either side, only the global value "15 per player among all groups", so its `health` stays null and a record reads it; its Damaged side has Threat 3, Attack 5, Defense 3 and Victory 99. Stage 1B prints `*` quest points ("a global quest point value of 8 per player among all groups"), so the card takes `questPointsX` and a record sets the number.
- **Mode cards.** The three mode cards are Hall of Beorn's `GenCon_Setup` cards (`campaign` type in the catalog). The scenario's `modeCard` is Standard Mode (`40002`), the cooperative game of one table (1 to 2 seats in the app): `overrides.scenarioExcluded` keeps the mode cards out of the count check, as the Siege's Standard Game Mode is kept, and it stands beside the quest deck after Setup with its back faceup. Easy Mode (`40001`: two Black Sails and a Corsair Warship set aside, Cobas Haven staged and not discarded for, 1 resource per hero, an extra draw in each resource phase, the Quest Action without the reveal) and Hard Mode (`40003`: three Tolfalas Landings staged, threat reduction capped at 1 each round, a threat raise when a character is healed or readied by a player card effect) are in the catalog and the ledger, and the app's Easy difficulty deals Easy Mode and Normal deals Standard Mode (reading 1; Hard Mode is not dealt, `scripts/checks/box-dealt.test.ts`). The Epic Multiplayer Mode (any number of groups of 1 to 4 players, one copy of the box for each, an organizer who tracks stage 1B progress and the Dreadnaught's damage, "any group" in the card text) needs several tables and is not built; "in any group" and "among all groups" read as the one table.
- **Setup (Standard Mode).** Printed, front: "Setup: • Players prepare their fleet. • Prepare the Corsair deck. • Set aside all 7 unique enemies (including the Dreadnaught). • Shuffle the encounter deck and discard cards from the top until 2 locations are discarded (3 locations instead if there are 3 or more players). Add those locations to the staging area. • Flip this card over and place it next to the quest deck." Back: "Quest Action: Deal 10 damage to a non-unique enemy engaged with a player in any group. Reveal the top card of the encounter deck. (Group limit once per game.) If a player controls no ship-objective, that player is eliminated from the game." The build reads the bullets as one paragraph (the bullets are dropped), "Players prepare their fleet" and "Prepare the Corsair deck" as the Dream-chaser steps (the ships are the box's own, so the Dream-chaser's Fleet is dealt for the Dream-chaser scenarios only), "Set aside all 7 unique enemies" as the seven set-aside enemies and the discard sentence as `discardToStaging` with a count of 2 (3 from 3 players).
- **Fleet and Corsair deck (rulebook).** Each player takes control of one of the six unique ship-objectives, Basic side faceup; the unused ships and the four Gondorian Warships stay set aside. Ship-objectives are encounter cards that act as objective allies and attack and defend against ship-enemies only; only ship-objectives defend against a ship-enemy, and an undefended ship-enemy attack's damage goes to a ship-objective the player controls. The 13 Corsair deck cards are non-unique non-Ship Corsair enemies in a separate facedown deck with its own discard pile (reshuffled when the deck runs out) that enter play only through card effects such as Boarding. Boarding X resolves when a Boarding ship-enemy engages a player from the staging area. Pillage X: after the enemy attacks a player, the player discards the top X cards of his deck. Massive: the enemy cannot leave the staging area, is engaged with every player and attacks each player in turn in the combat phase. The Dreadnaught's hit points and stage 1B's quest points are global values (15 and 8 per player among all groups); in the app they are those numbers times the players at the table.
- **Quest stages.** 1B (global quest points; "No more than 5 per player progress can be placed on this stage each round. The players can only advance at the end of the quest phase.") and 2B ("No more than 5 per player progress can be placed on this stage each round." / Action: remove 5 per player progress to shuffle 1 set-aside Gondorian Warship into a group's encounter deck / Response: when the Dreadnaught attacks you, raise your threat by 4 to cancel the attack / "When the Dreadnaught is destroyed, the players win the game."). The player icon after a number is written `PerPlayer` in the ledger, as Hall of Beorn writes it.
- **Ledger.** The 58 printed sides (the three mode cards, 2 quest cards, 6 ships on both sides, the Gondorian Warship, the Dreadnaught on both sides and 31 other encounter cards) are read from the scans (`data/printed/standalone-scenarios.json`). Hall of Beorn's text differs in about 40 lines: Standard Mode (a Setup line "Set the following enemies aside: ..." for "Set aside ..."; the Quest Action lacks "Reveal the top card of the encounter deck"); the Upgraded sides of the six ships and the Dreadnaught's Damaged side run their lines together and Hall of Beorn spells the icons in brackets; Golden Wing A lacks "Setup:"; Silent Mist "a cost 3 or more" (printed "a cost of 3 or more"); the Dreadnaught A lacks "Massive." and reads "global hit point value (15 hit points for each player ...)" and "hit pints" (printed "a global hit point value of 15 per player among all groups" and "hit points"), side B "5 damage per player" (printed 5 with the player icon); Hydraken "cards effects"; Corsair Skiff "eithe" and "Bording"; Corsair Skirmisher "gaines"; Cobas Haven "Boading"; Isle of Fortune "1 resource per player" (printed "1" with the player icon, "resource(s)"); Mouths of Anduin "is gains"; Tolfalas Landing "Tofalas"; Ballista Barrage "players threat"; Violent Tempest "assigns X among" and "to the defending character" (printed "assigns X damage among" and "to defending character"); the quoted effects of Grappled!, Taking on Water, Mouths of Anduin and Tolfalas Landing are in double quotes. Swan Ship of Dol Amroth's Upgraded side prints "After Swan of Dol Amroth flips": kept under `misprints`. Everything else matches. No scan was unreadable.
## Readings

- **Readings.** Numbered; the status is `literal`, `span-settled`, `researched` or `recommended` (agent recommendation, not user-confirmed; the section Readings to confirm, at the end of this file, lists them). No `faq.*` span, official ruling or designer answer names the box; no BoardGameGeek, reddit or Hall of Beorn thread returned a rule. Read: the box rulebook as RulesPal transcribes it (https://rulespal.com/the-lord-of-the-rings-the-card-game-the-hunt-for-the-dreadnaught/rulebook; it does not give the Setup order, the Dreadnaught's flip or the ship elimination), the review https://visionofthepalantir.com/2022/01/16/the-hunt-for-the-dreadnaught/ (the three modes, the flip, Massive, the damage cap, Pillage and the elimination, in play-through words), Hall of Beorn's Easy, Standard and Hard Mode pages (https://www.hallofbeorn.com/LotR/Details/Easy-Mode-THftD, https://hallofbeorn.com/LotR/Details/Standard-Mode-THftD, https://www.hallofbeorn.com/LotR/Details/Hard-Mode-THftD; card text only) and the FFG release post https://www.fantasyflightgames.com/en/news/2020/9/21/the-hunt-for-the-dreadnaught/ (no rules). The sea cards the box reprints keep the earlier readings (A Storm on Cobas Haven, The City of Corsairs, Voyage Across Belegaer, Flight of the Stormcaller; Ships, Boarding X and the Corsair deck, above): Southron Sailor 40024 (11060), Corsair Skiff 40027 (12022), Corsair Skirmisher 40028 (12125), Corsair Warship 40029 (11058), Slave Ship 40031 (12152), Boarding Party 40041 (11059), Taking on Water 40045 (12132) and Cobas Haven 40035 (12130) have the same text and the same records.
  1. Mode card by difficulty: Normal deals Standard Mode (`40002`), Easy deals Easy Mode (`40001`), the scenario's own printed easy mode; Hard Mode (`40003`) is not offered, as the app has no Hard setting. The rulebook says "choose which difficulty mode to play, find its corresponding Setup card and remove the other two" (`researched`, community: RulesPal and the review; the mapping of the app's Easy setting to the Easy Mode card is `recommended`: https://www.hallofbeorn.com/LotR/Details/Easy-Mode-THftD prints its Setup). The data deals the mode card by difficulty (`ScenarioData` gains the Easy card beside `modeCard`); `scenarioExcluded` keeps `40001` and `40003` out of the count check.
  2. Easy Mode's "Add 1 resource to each hero's resource pool" (`40001:A:5`) is the one resource of the Easy rule (`easy-mode.easy-mode`): a hero starts with 1 resource, not 2. Easy Mode's back (40001:B:1) is a constant: each player draws 1 additional card in the resource phase (`rr.resource-phase`, `literal`). The record adds it and Setup step 2's Easy resource is skipped while the mode card is Easy Mode (`recommended`; the card prints the rule's step, as the Hall of Beorn page says "as regular Easy mode").
  3. Setup order: the opening hands are drawn and the mulligan is taken first, then the whole mode card Setup runs, as the engine resolves every Setup (the Setup lines of heroes and contracts too). The fleet is prepared first, then the Corsair deck, the locations and the rest. A choice of a ship before the opening hands would need a window the start of a game has not (every start is followed by the mulligan's Keep), so the two ships' hand lines work on the hand the player holds: Pride of Lebennin ("when you draw your starting hand ... You cannot take a mulligan") shuffles the hand into the deck and draws 6, then draws 5 and shuffles 5 cards of the hand back, so a mulligan taken before the ship was chosen has no effect; Silent Mist ("Your starting hand size is 8 cards") draws 2 more once the fleet is taken (`recommended`; the rulebook gives no order; the printed line "cannot take a mulligan" wins over the fallback: the mulligan stays offered and does nothing for this ship).
  4. Prepare your fleet: each player in player order from the first player chooses one of the six ships, Basic side up, and takes control of it; a lone player takes one ship. No Heading card. Unused ships and the four Gondorian Warships stay set aside, not removed. Each ship's Setup lines resolve for its controller once every player has a ship, in player order (`researched`: RulesPal's "each player chooses and takes control of one of the six unique ship-objectives"; `dcce.prepare-your-fleet` for the order).
  5. 40002:A:4 "discard cards from the top until 2 locations are discarded (3 if there are 3 or more players)": the encounter deck is shuffled, cards are discarded one at a time to the encounter discard pile until N locations have been discarded, then those locations are added to the staging area without being revealed (no When Revealed, no Surge). If the deck runs out first the discarding stops; the discard pile is not reshuffled in Setup (`literal`; the empty deck is `recommended`, it cannot happen with 15 locations in 43 cards).
  6. The Corsair deck is the non-unique, non-Ship Corsair enemies: 40020 to 40025 (13 cards). Sea Monster (a Creature enemy) stays in the encounter deck and goes to the encounter discard pile; the unique Corsairs are set aside and, with Victory 10, go to the victory display when destroyed. `PrepareCorsairDeck` and the Corsair discard redirect read the trait Corsair here (`researched`: RulesPal "the 13 non-Ship, non-unique Corsair enemies"; the engine's earlier "any non-ship enemy" fits the Dream-chaser boxes, which have no other).
  7. 40006:A:3 Eithiliant Setup: the player chooses a hero, then another hero they control; the first gains the other's printed Traits until the end of the game (a lone hero gains nothing). 40007:A:3 Golden Wing Setup: reduce threat by 3 (not below 0, after the starting threat is set), and the chosen hero gains ranged until the end of the game (`literal`; the second hero is the same player's choice, `recommended`).
  8. 40008:A:2 Pride of Lebennin: the starting hand is drawn as 6 + 5 cards, the player chooses 5 cards of the hand to shuffle into the deck (the deck is shuffled once), and has no use of a mulligan (reading 3). 40009:A:2 Silent Mist: the starting hand and a mulligan redraw are 8 cards (`literal` text; the choice of the 5 is `recommended`, as a random 5 would not be a filter).
  9. Ship Actions. 40009 Silent Mist "Play an event from your hand with a cost of 3 or more, reducing its cost by 1" (2 on B): the event's printed cost is 3 or more (an X cost is not), the player pays the reduced cost with a resource match, and the event is played in this Action window as its own text allows (`PlayFromHand` `reduce`, `min` 0). 40010 Swan Ship "Spend 2 resources to ready a character" (1 on B, "ready a card"): the resources come from the controller's heroes' pools, any hero, the player chooses; "a card" is any exhausted card in play (hero, ally, attachment, contract, Ship objective; the review reads it so). 40011 Twilight's Call "Play an ally or attachment from your hand": paid as normal; the A constant waives the resource match for the first card the controller plays each round, B for every card. "Limit once per round" is a limit on the Action for each ship (`literal`; "a card" is `researched`, https://visionofthepalantir.com/2022/01/16/the-hunt-for-the-dreadnaught/).
  10. 40009:B:2 "events from your discard pile and/or your collection ... (following deckbuilding rules)": up to 2 events, from the discard pile and from the player's collection, the cards the catalog holds as deck-legal events. The copies of a title in the deck, hand, discard pile and play plus the card added never pass 3, or the card's own deck limit when the catalog states a lower one (`rr.deckbuilding`; the printed card carries `deckLimit`). The card from outside the game is a card the game did not hold (as Spyglass in The Thing in the Depths, above). `recommended` (no source on a ship's use of the collection; the Collection screen is not read, as owning a card is never required).
  11. 40008:B:2 Pride of Lebennin B: heal 2 damage from any character in play (hero, ally or Ship objective), and add up to 2 cards the player chooses from the encounter discard pile to the victory display, whatever their type (the Corsair discard pile is not the encounter discard pile) (`literal`). 40006:B:2 Eithiliant B: shuffle up to 6 cards of the player's discard pile into the deck; place 6 progress on a location in the staging area or the active location (progress placed on a staged location is placed there, not on the quest; `literal`). 40007:B:2 Golden Wing B: 6 damage to an enemy in play, any player's engaged or staged: the ship is an encounter card, so the damage passes an enemy's immunity to player card effects, and the Dreadnaught's damage cap still applies (`literal`, `dcce.immune`). 40011:B:2 Twilight's Call B: the controller draws 3 cards (a ship's draw is not a player card effect for Tolfalas Landing: `recommended`).
  12. Belfalas Shipyard 40034:A:1: after it is explored as the active location, the players "as a group" spend 5 resources, from any heroes' pools, the first player choosing who pays each (`rr.first-player`; `recommended`); then any Ship objective in play is healed of 5 damage, readied and, if it shows its Basic side and has an Upgraded side, flipped to it (the Gondorian Warship has none; a ship already Upgraded is healed and readied only). The Response is offered only when 5 resources can be spent. It keeps the ship's damage and tokens, and a flipped ship is the same card (`recommended`).
  13. "After X flips" (the six B Responses) is heard by the flipped ship's controller as that Response's trigger, only the B side's records; each ship flips once (a Basic ship only), and a ship dealt Upgraded by a campaign never flips here (`literal`).
  14. 40001:B:3, 40002:B:2, 40003:B:3, "If a player controls no ship-objective, that player is eliminated from the game": a constant checked at each settle once the fleet is prepared, not during Setup; a Gondorian Warship or a Skirmisher taken as a ship counts (it is a ship-objective). An eliminated player's cards leave the game (`rr.player-elimination`); the last player eliminated loses the game (`literal`; the Warship protecting a player is `researched`, https://visionofthepalantir.com/2022/01/16/the-hunt-for-the-dreadnaught/).
  15. The Quest Action (40001:B:2, 40002:B:1): an Action any player may take in a quest phase action window, offered from the mode card beside the quest deck like the Action of a campaign card (`rr.action`; as Spider Den's, above), group limit once per game (the whole table once); the target is a non-unique enemy engaged with any player (not one in the staging area); 10 damage; then, in Standard Mode, the acting player reveals the top card of the encounter deck into the staging area as in a Staging step (Surge adds one; `recommended` for who reveals). Hard Mode's back is not dealt.
  16. The global values (40013:A:2, :A:1 and :B:2, 40004:B:1, :B:2, 40005:B:1, :B:2): "N PerPlayer" is N for each player who started the scenario, an eliminated player included: hit points 15 each, so 15 or 30; a damage cap of 5 each per round, so 5 or 10; Archery 1 each; stage 1B 8 quest points each; the progress cap 5 each per round; and the 5 progress each that stage 2B's Action removes. "Among all groups" and "in any group" are the one table (`researched`: the box rulebook as RulesPal transcribes it, "Global values are calculated based on the total number of participating players among all groups at the start of the event" and "The player symbol next to a value multiplies that value by the number of players in that group who started that scenario", https://rulespal.com/the-lord-of-the-rings-the-card-game-the-hunt-for-the-dreadnaught/rulebook; the Rules Reference and the FAQ say nothing of an eliminated player and a value per player). The records read the amount `startedPlayers`; the engine's other "per player" values keep counting the living players. The cap counts damage placed on the Dreadnaught since the round began, from every source, and the excess is not dealt; it starts again each round (`recommended`).
  17. Massive (40013): the enemy stays in the staging area, counts its Threat there, and is engaged with every player at once: every player may attack it from the staging area in the combat phase (the flag `attackableEngagedWithAll`; ranged characters of the others may join), no engagement check ever engages it, and in the combat phase it attacks each player in turn from the first player, once each, with a shadow card dealt for each attack. "Engaged player" on the unique Corsairs and Southron Sailor's "each Ship enemy engaged with you" read it as engaged with each: Southron Sailor adds the Dreadnaught's Threat to the staging area total once more (`researched`: RulesPal and the review for Massive; the Sailor is `recommended`, no source). Elite side: Archery 1 for each player at the start of the combat phase (cumulative with other Archery); the Damaged side has none printed.
  18. The flip (40013:A:3): at the end of the round, with the round's other end-of-round Forced effects, when its remaining hit points are below half its starting hit points (30 hit points: 14 or fewer left; 15 hit points: 7 or fewer), it flips to the Damaged side keeping its damage and tokens. It never flips back (`researched`: the review, "flips ... once more than half its hitpoints are gone"). A hit that would take it under half in a round leaves it Elite until that round's end.
  19. 40013:B:3: after it flips, each player in turn order puts 1 set-aside unique Corsair, drawn by the game's seed (a different one for each player), into play engaged with them. It is not revealed (no When Revealed or Boarding) and dealt no shadow card then. Putting an enemy into play engaged with a player is an engagement for "After X engages you" Forced effects: Descendant of Castamir (set aside, so reachable only this way) would never fire otherwise (`recommended`; no source).
  20. 40014 to 40019: "Engaged player cannot attack the Dreadnaught" and Umbar Captain's "cannot attack Ship enemies" bar the player's characters (heroes, allies and Ship objectives) from being declared as attackers against that enemy or those enemies; effects that deal damage are not attacks; the other players attack as usual (`literal`). Ophidian: after one character is declared as an attacker against him, no other character is that round, even if that attack is cancelled (`recommended`).
  21. Pillage X (40014, 40021 to 40023, Belfalas Shipyard's Shadow): after the enemy's attack on a player has resolved, defended or not, that player discards the top X cards of the deck, one at a time (all of them when fewer remain). It happens once an attack was made; a canceled attack makes none. The Forced lines of 40021 to 40023 resolve once for each event card discarded this way, one after the other, the player choosing the card, hero or character where the line asks (`researched`: RulesPal and the review for the keyword; "once for each event card" is `recommended`, "an event card is discarded" is singular). Captain of the Haven's Forced eliminates an engaged player with an empty deck at the end of the combat phase (`literal`).
  22. 40015 Commander: after it attacks the player (in the combat phase or from a Forced), the Dreadnaught makes one additional attack on that player at once, with its own shadow card, which stage 2B's Response may cancel (`span-settled`, `faq.additional-attacks`, `faq.enemy-attacks-outside-combat`; the shadow card is `recommended`). 40016 Descendant: the top 2 cards of the Corsair deck go into play engaged with the player (the deck refills as in Boarding) and each is dealt 1 shadow card now (`literal`). 40017 Hydraken: after he attacks, once each round, the engaged player raises their threat by 4 and he makes an additional attack dealt its usual shadow card and 2 more, so 3 shadow cards in all, each resolving (`literal`: "Deal him 2 additional shadow cards for this attack"; `span-settled`, `faq.additional-attacks`: an additional attack is dealt a shadow card of its own, the extra cards are added to it).
  23. 40018 Ophidian: "When a character defends against Ophidian, deal 2 damage to that character", a Ship objective included (it is a character, `dcce.ship-objectives`), once the defender is declared. 40019 Varjax: an excess point of combat damage is a point beyond the character's remaining hit points (`rr.damage`, as The Siege of Annúminas); for each, the defending player, the player attacked whether or not the attack was defended, deals 1 damage to a Ship objective they control (the player chooses; none, none) (`recommended`).
  24. Brash Corsair 40020: each time the engaged player exhausts a Ship objective, by any means (as 12132's "When attached objective exhausts"), it makes an attack on that player at once, after the action that exhausted the ship is done (so an exhaust to defend does not start a second attack inside the first; `recommended`). Black Sails 40026 Shadow: if the attacking enemy is a Ship, it returns to the staging area after this attack; it makes Boarding again on its next engagement from the staging area (`literal`).
  25. Sea Monster 40033: "Ship objectives may attack and defend against Sea Monster" lets Ship objectives attack and defend against this one enemy that is not a ship; its When Revealed deals 4 damage to the Ship enemy or Ship objective in play, any player's, with the fewest remaining hit points (the first player breaks a tie; nothing when none is in play). It is an encounter card effect, so it passes the Ship objective's immunity (`literal`; the tie `recommended`, as The Siege of Annúminas reading 16).
  26. Locations. Isle of Fortune 40036: enters play with 1 resource for each player; "after a Ship enemy enters play" is any entry into play (revealed, put into play engaged, searched into the staging area), not a return to the staging area; when explored the first player chooses the hero whose pool takes the resources (`literal`; the hero `recommended`). Mouths of Anduin 40037: each copy in the staging area gives itself the Forced line, so two copies each reveal a card after a canceled When Revealed; as for the Tower in The Siege of Annúminas reading 12 (`recommended`; the review is unsure that they stack). Tolfalas Landing 40038: "draws any number of cards from a player card effect" is a draw by a hero, ally, attachment, event or contract, not by a ship or encounter card and not the resource phase draw; each copy in the staging area raises the threat of the player by the number of cards actually drawn (`recommended`). Wicked Cove 40039: all locations in the staging area, itself included, are immune to player card effects while it is there; the Shadow's immunity and +2 Defense last until the end of the round and follow `faq.lasting-effects`. Cobas Haven 40035 as 12130.
  27. Treacheries. Ballista Barrage 40040: Doomed 1, then each Ship enemy in play gains Archery 2 until the end of the round, including one that enters play later that round (`span-settled`, `faq.lasting-effects`); the Shadow's damage is assigned by the defending player among characters they control, Ship objectives included, 3 with 10 or more cards in their discard pile. Out of the Mist 40043: "the enemy with the highest engagement cost" is chosen among the enemies in the staging area (the Dreadnaught is one), the first player breaking a tie; it engages the first player (a Ship makes Boarding) and attacks at once; with no enemy, or when no attack began (the count of the attacks an effect made, the attack of a staged enemy included: `AttackWithPicked counted`), Out of the Mist gains Surge (as 50926 in Attack on Dol Guldur reading 16; `recommended`). Overworked 40044: the text boxes of the non-Ship allies in play, and any that enter this round, are blank but for Traits until the end of the round (`span-settled`, `faq.lasting-effects`, `rr.blank`); a player controls "7 or more allies" counting Ship objectives, which are allies (`dcce.ship-objectives`; the "non-Ship" in the first sentence shows the card counts ships as allies; `researched`, the earlier Skirmisher reading). Violent Tempest 40046: 1 damage to each enemy in play, the Dreadnaught's cap included, and each player's X is the number of exhausted characters they control when it resolves, Ship objectives included (`literal`). Grappled! 40042 and Taking on Water 40045: as Taking on Water 12132, the Action's "3 characters" are 3 characters the acting player controls and exhausts as the cost; Grappled! keeps its host from readying (`recommended`, as the earlier reading).
  28. Gondorian Warship 40012: when revealed (from the encounter deck, once stage 2B shuffled it in), the first player chooses the player who takes control, a lone player taking it (`rr.first-player`; as Send for Help, above); it is a Ship objective with no Upgraded side, so its controller is no longer eliminated for lack of a ship. Destroyed, it is set aside again (`literal`); used as a shadow card it deals 5 damage to the attacking enemy and goes to the encounter discard pile (`literal`). 40005:B:2 shuffles a set-aside copy into the encounter deck (the one table is the only group), costing 5 progress for each player from the stage; with none set aside it does nothing and cannot be taken.
  29. Quest stages. 40004:B: "The players can only advance at the end of the quest phase" holds the advance in every window but the end of the quest phase, even when a card effect fills the stage in another phase (the condition `phaseEnded`: the quest phase has just ended and its boundary is not yet crossed); progress beyond the cap is not placed; progress over the quest points at the advance is lost. 40005:A:1 adds the set-aside Dreadnaught to the staging area, Elite side up, not revealed. 40005:B:3 "raise your threat by 4 to cancel the attack" is a Response of the attacked player, a cost, taken when the Dreadnaught's attack is declared, before defenders (`rr.cost`); 40005:B:4 wins the game when the Dreadnaught is destroyed, on either side (`literal`).
  30. Ships and the Setup lines of the ship cards: each hero's bonus ("+1 Willpower", "+1 hit point", "+1 Defense", ranged) is the ship's constant for its controller's heroes while it is in play and shows that side; a ship's damage and tokens stay when it flips.
