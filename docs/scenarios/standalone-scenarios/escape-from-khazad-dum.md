# Escape from Khazad-dûm

The 2020 print-on-demand scenario kit (pack `EfKD`, RingsDB's code for it, position 13 of cycle 50; Hall of Beorn lists it under its own name), Normal and Easy alike, the thirteenth of the cycle. It is a kit like The Mines of Moria, with which it can share encounter cards in an advanced build: seven quest cards, 14 encounter sets of three cards (5 copies each) and the double-sided Dark Pit / Durin's Bane card. Its rulebook has no setup text; the Quick-build rules are "Create your quest deck by choosing one stage 2 and one stage 3 and placing them in numerical order under stage 1. Create your encounter deck by choosing 7 different encounter sets and shuffling them together. Choose cooperative or competitive play" (`researched`, `https://rulespal.com/the-lord-of-the-rings-the-card-game-escape-from-khazad-dum/rulebook`, a transcription of the printed rulebook; community source, not user-confirmed). The app plays a cooperative Quick-build game only (competitive play, 1v1 or 2v2 on traded custom scenarios, is not built). No Nightmare deck exists for it.

- **Data.** Hall of Beorn numbers its cards `00001` to `00053`, which collide with earlier packs; RingsDB lists none of the pack's cards. `overrides.productCodes` gives them `513` and the collector number (`51301` Out of the Depths, `51303` to `51305` the three stage 2 cards, `51307` to `51309` the three stage 3 cards, `51310` Dark Pit, `51311` Durin's Bane, `51312` to `51353` the 42 encounter cards). The piles are DragnCards' deck Q99.14, with no Easy twin: Easy loads the same deck. DragnCards names the pack without the accent (`overrides.dragnPackAliases`), loads the encounter cards in its `sharedQuestDeck` group (the scenario's `groups` read it as the encounter pile), loads the seven quest cards and Dark Pit set aside (`overrides.scenarioPiles` puts the quest cards in the quest pile and Dark Pit in the staging area, as the 1A Setup says) and omits Durin's Bane, the back of Dark Pit (the scans number them 1/2 and 2/2; the `back` field of the Dark Pit rule names it; the cardgame.tools row of Dark Pit holds Durin's Bane as its back image). Hall of Beorn lists the quest cards and those two cards in the scenario file, so `encounterSets` names the 14 sets of the kit besides. Hall of Beorn gives the 42 encounter cards no set; each is printed with its set number (15 to 28, in the orange circle; the set's cards are numbered x/5, one for each copy), so `overrides.cards` gives each card its set ("Escape from Khazad-dûm 15" to "Escape from Khazad-dûm 28", three cards each in code order) and its printed cost, which Hall of Beorn leaves empty. The load list deals all 14 sets (70 cards).
- **Setup.** The 1A Setup is "Add Dark Pit to the staging area. The opposing team searches the encounter deck for one different 0-cost enemy or location per player and adds them to the staging area. Shuffle the encounter deck." The search is The Wizard's Quest's (reading 1 there): the cards are found by the Setup record, and the 0-cost applies to enemies and locations alike.
- **Quest stages.** 1B (10 quest points): "Forced: When Dark Pit is explored, flip it over and set Durin's Bane aside, out of play. Each player searches the top 5 cards of his deck for a card, adds it to his hand, and shuffles his deck. This stage cannot be defeated while Dark Pit is in play." The three stage 2 cards share the 2A text with The Mines of Moria's (the opposing team searches the top 5 cards of the encounter deck for a card with cost 1 or less, cost 2 or less with two or more players, sets it aside, shuffles, reveals it; its "when revealed" effects cannot be canceled): Old One Lair (2B, 10 quest points: When Revealed, the opposing team searches the encounter deck and discard pile for an enemy with cost 2 or less, adds it to the staging area and places a resource on it, shuffle; the enemy with a resource is immune to player card effects and progress cannot be placed on this stage while it is in play), A Wrong Turn (15: When Revealed, each player chooses 1 card in his hand and shuffles the rest into his deck) and A Way Up (20: add the active location's Threat to the staging area; during the travel phase the players must travel to a location, if able). The three stage 3 cards share the 3A text "When Revealed: Add the set-aside Durin's Bane to the staging area. It cannot take damage this round. If there is more than 1 player, it makes an immediate attack against the first player." The Bridge of Khazad-dûm (10; no more than 5 progress each round; Forced: at the beginning of the quest phase Durin's Bane attacks the first player and the undefended or excess damage is placed here as damage; with damage equal to or greater than its quest points the players lose, defeating it wins), Last Lord of Moria (15; Durin's Bane engages the first player and cannot take more than X damage each round, X the progress tokens here; this stage cannot be defeated while Durin's Bane is in play; defeating it wins) and The Rear Guard (20; players' threat cannot be reduced by player card effect; Durin's Bane is considered engaged with each player and attacks each in turn order in the combat phase with a shadow card for each attack; defeating it wins).
- **Dark Pit and Durin's Bane.** Dark Pit (Underground, unique location; Threat 1, 1 quest point): "Immune to player card effects. Travel: the opposing team searches the top 5 ... (the 2A search)". Durin's Bane (Balrog, unique enemy; engagement 50, Threat 4, Attack 8, Defense 3, 15 hit points, Victory 5): "Immune to player card effects. Cannot be optionally engaged. Forced: After Durin's Bane attacks and destroys a character you control, raise your threat by 2 and heal 2 damage from Durin's Bane." The Mountain's Roots prints Threat X ("X is 1 more than the stage number of the main quest"): the card's threat stays null in the data and a record sets it, as for The Mines of Moria's Chamber of Mazarbul.
- **Opposing team.** The lines that name it (the Setup, the stages, Dark Pit, Nest of Vermin, Frightful Den, Lost and Alone, Many Roads, Goblin Dungeon, Black Uruks, Dreadful Gap, Vast and Intricate, Watchful Eyes, A Foe Beyond and others) follow The Wizard's Quest, reading 1.
- **Ledger.** The 58 printed sides (7 quest cards, Dark Pit, Durin's Bane and 42 encounter cards) are read from the scans (`data/printed/standalone-scenarios.json`). Hall of Beorn's text differs in 22 lines: the shared 2A text ("It's"), Old One Lair ("searchs", "a resources"), A Way Up (no "to a location"), The Bridge of Khazad-dûm (no comma), Last Lord of Moria ("per round"), The Rear Guard ("effects"), Dark Pit ("2 or more"), Durin's Bane ("Immuned"), Black Bats ("Eithe"), Old Stone-troll ("attachs"), Bottomless Chasm ("the player must choose Bottomless Chasm"), Lost and Alone ("charactert"), Hobgoblin ("Hobgobling"), Unspeakable Thing ("thing"), Shadow Dweller ("character"), Elder Nameless Thing (no closing quote), Goblin Dungeon (no "under Goblin Dungeon"), Orc Raiders ("Orc Raider"), Nameless Thing ("guarded attached") and Mithril Lode ("Mitril", no closing quote).
- **Engine.** The records are in `src/effects/authored/standalone-scenarios/escape-from-khazad-dum.ts`, the new verbs in the Wizard's Quest family (`src/rules/families/wizards-quest/khazad.ts`): RemoveDefender (Fatigue), SearchTopShadow (Black Uruks) and ReplacesRevealCancel (Undisturbed Bones, read by CancelEffect), with the flags `allShadowsUncancelable` and `attackPerShadow`, the fields `FlipOver setAside` (Dark Pit), `ShuffleHandIntoDeck keepPicked` (A Wrong Turn), `AttackFromStaging excessToStage` (The Bridge of Khazad-dûm), `EngageFirstPlayer target`, `HoldPickedUnder returnsExhausted` (Goblin Dungeon) and `Restrict defend anyDefender` (Rock Adder). The spec rows, asks and goldens follow the records.

- **The table.** The Table step (`docs/agents/agent-brief.md`, Scenario procedure; lines `51301:B:1`, `51307:B:2`, `51307:B:3`, `51340:A:1`, `51351:A:1`; `src/play/readouts/standalone-scenarios/khazad.ts`, `docs/design-system-table.md`, Top bar): the damage on the stage 3B is a top-bar dial against the 10 that lose the game, which warms when Durin's Bane's next attack could lose it; Durin's Bane set aside by Dark Pit's flip is a word dial ("Aside", a tap shows its card) until a stage 3 card adds it; the card Nameless Thing guards wears the held pill on its host and its preview says it is discarded, not returned, when the host leaves play; Goblin Dungeon's ally reuses the held pill of Carried Away and its preview says it returns to play exhausted. The Mountain's Roots' Threat X is the card's own number, read live. No other tracker, limit or zone is new; the asks of the picks the first player settles name each choice.

## Readings

**Readings.**

1. **The opposing team in a cooperative game** (`researched`): as The Wizard's Quest, reading 1, and the Escape from Khazad-dûm rulebook says it in its own words: "the first player should resolve that effect in the manner that will provide the greatest challenge for the players" (https://rulespal.com/the-lord-of-the-rings-the-card-game-escape-from-khazad-dum/rulebook). The engine chooses; no prompt is shown.
2. **Which sets and stages are used** (`recommended`): as The Mines of Moria, reading 2. The rulebook's Quick-build is one stage 2 and one stage 3 under stage 1 and 7 different encounter sets (https://rulespal.com/the-lord-of-the-rings-the-card-game-escape-from-khazad-dum/rulebook); the app deals 7 of the 14 sets from the seed and the first player chooses the stage 2 and stage 3 cards. The 1A Setup is the Quick-build (`BuildQuickDeck` keep 7 and two `ChooseQuestStage` steps) before the Setup search. Competitive play is not built.
3. **The opposing team's choice, line by line** (`recommended`, extends The Mines of Moria reading 3; no source names a measure): a search for an enemy or location takes the highest printed Threat (an enemy: then the highest Attack), then the card nearest the top (the Setup search, Old One Lair, Nest of Vermin, Frightful Den, Many Roads). The top-5 searches of 2A and Dark Pit's Travel take the legal card nearest the top. Vast and Intricate takes the non-unique location in the staging area with the highest Threat. Watchful Eyes takes the hero with the highest Willpower (The Woodland Realm reading 17). Goblin Dungeon takes the ally with the highest printed cost in play (one ally, not one for each player). Lost and Alone takes each player's questing character with the highest Willpower. A Foe Beyond takes the attachment with the highest printed cost (The Mines of Moria reading 10). The card chosen by Dreadful Gap, and the location Many Roads shuffles back, is the card in the encounter discard pile with the highest Threat (an enemy: then Attack), else the one most recently discarded. Black Uruks' search takes the legal card with the highest Attack if it is an enemy, else the card nearest the top. A card that is not found changes nothing, and the encounter deck is still shuffled where a line says so.
4. **Encounter cost** (`literal`, data): as The Wizard's Quest, reading 4; `overrides.cards` carries the 42 printed costs. "Cost 1 or less" reads the printed cost of any card type; "0-cost" reads a printed 0 (a card with no printed cost counts 0).
5. **The 2A and Dark Pit searches** (`literal`, rr.search): as The Wizard's Quest, reading 5, and The Woodland Realm's Spider Den Travel (51110:A:2). A Travel line that searches, sets aside and reveals a card is an effect, not a cost: the players travel to Dark Pit whatever the deck holds.
6. **The 1A Setup** (`literal`, as The Wizard's Quest, reading 6): Dark Pit starts in the staging area (the deal); the opposing team then searches the encounter deck for one different 0-cost enemy or location for each player, adds them to the staging area, and the deck is shuffled.
7. **Dark Pit and 1B** (`literal`, rr.explored; precedent Rhosgobel, The Wizard's Quest reading 7, with the one two-sided card): exploring Dark Pit (1 quest point) flips it: the card turns over and Durin's Bane is set aside out of play, not added to the staging area (3A adds it). Each player then searches the top 5 cards of his own deck for any card, adds it to his hand and shuffles his deck (a player with fewer than 5 cards searches those; none found changes nothing, the deck is still shuffled). The player chooses the card, so the choice tray asks. Dark Pit has no Victory, so it goes to the encounter discard pile as the flip leaves play. The stage cannot be defeated while Dark Pit is in play, as Rhosgobel's (51001:B:2), nor while the flip of an explored Dark Pit is still pending: its explored event (`locationExplored`, or `activeExplored` when it was the active location) waits to be heard, or the Forced effect is running (the condition `exploredPending`). A stage full at the moment Dark Pit is explored therefore advances only once the flip is done. A Dark Pit that left play in any other way with no flip pending holds nothing, so the stage is never blocked for good.
8. **Old One Lair, 2B** (`literal` for the effect; precedent The Wizard's Quest, 51003:B:1 and :B:2, with an enemy for a location): the opposing team takes an enemy with cost 2 or less (reading 3), adds it to the staging area and places a resource on it; the deck is shuffled. While it holds the resource it is immune to player card effects, and progress cannot be placed on this stage while it is in play. The block is on the stage only: the active location and a side quest still take progress (https://visionofthepalantir.com/2023/06/23/escape-from-khazad-dum/, community, agrees). Quest-phase progress is lost, not kept, while it is in play. With no such enemy found the stage is not blocked.
9. **A Wrong Turn, 2B** (`literal`; the chooser is each player, named: https://visionofthepalantir.com/2023/06/23/escape-from-khazad-dum/ reads it as "choose one card from their hand to keep and shuffle all others into their deck"): each player keeps one card of his hand and shuffles every other card of it into his deck, then the deck is shuffled once. A hand of one card or none changes nothing. He draws no card back. The player chooses, so the choice tray asks.
10. **A Way Up, 2B** (`span-settled` by mec109.stage-4, `mustTravel`, 142021:B:1): "Add the active location's Threat to the staging area" is read live: the Threat of the active location counts in the staging area total while it is active (11072:A:1). "The players must travel to a location, if able": the travel step offers no way to decline while a location in the staging area can be traveled to and no active location is in play; the players choose which one and still pay its costs (https://visionofthepalantir.com/2023/06/23/escape-from-khazad-dum/: "forced to travel during the travel phase").
11. **Bottomless Chasm** (`span-settled`, 19130:A:2 Narrow Stair, 01088:A:1 precedent `travelOnly`): while it is in the staging area and there is no active location, the players travel to it and nowhere else, if able. With two copies in the staging area they choose between those. Its +2 Defense shadow lasts until the end of the phase.
12. **The 3A line** (`literal`): Durin's Bane (set aside by 1B) enters the staging area as a new card with no tokens; it cannot take damage until the end of the round; with more than 1 player it makes an immediate attack against the first player (a shadow card is dealt for it, faq.additional-attacks). The attack is an ordinary one: its undefended or excess damage goes on the heroes, not on 3B, because 3B's line speaks of "this attack", the Forced one (reading 14).
13. **Durin's Bane** (`literal`): "Immune to player card effects" and "Cannot be optionally engaged" as printed (Moria's Watcher, 51210). Its Forced line fires when its attack destroys a character: that character's controller raises his own threat by 2, and Durin's Bane heals 2 damage (Dagnir 19073:A:1). It fires for an undefended attack that kills a hero as for any other, and not for a character destroyed in any other way. Its Victory 5 is printed; defeating the stage wins the game whatever it holds (reading 17).
14. **The Bridge of Khazad-dûm, 3B** (`literal`, `recommended` for "excess"; https://hallofbeorn.com/LotR/Details/Nearing-the-Gate-The-Bridge-of-Khazad-dûm-EfKD gives the text only, https://visionofthepalantir.com/2023/06/23/escape-from-khazad-dum/ says "if 10 or more damage lands on the quest card before it clears, the players lose"; no ruling on excess damage was found): at the beginning of each quest phase Durin's Bane (wherever it is, in the staging area or engaged) makes an attack against the first player (a shadow card is dealt, rr.step-6-2). The damage of an undefended attack is placed as damage tokens on this stage and on no hero. For a defended attack the damage goes on the defender as always, and the damage beyond what is needed to destroy it (rr.damage: hit points less the damage already on it; the same count as Hill Troll's excess, 01082:A:1) is placed on this stage as well. Shadow effects that raise the damage count. The players lose when the stage holds damage equal to or greater than its quest points (10); a stage defeated wins the game. The players lose at once, as the damage is placed (the engine's `loseAtDamage`). "No more than 5 progress each round" is 142042:B:1 read for this stage (The Woodland Realm reading 9; The Mines of Moria reading 12).
15. **Last Lord of Moria, 3B** (`literal`; `recommended` for the timing of "engages"): Durin's Bane engages the first player: it engages as soon as the stage is current and whenever it is in the staging area while this stage is current (Captain Sahír, 11076:A:1, and Gollum, 146049:A:2, are the precedent), ignoring its engagement cost. "Cannot take more than X damage each round, X the number of progress tokens on this stage" is read live; with no progress it takes none; damage over the cap is not placed (the Revenant limit, 11181:A:3; `damageCap`, 11177:A:4). Its 3A line runs before the engage is heard: the card is added, then the 3A steps that follow (cannot take damage until the end of the round, the immediate attack with more than one player) pick it staged or engaged, since the Forced engage has already moved it. "This stage cannot be defeated while Durin's Bane is in play", as Moria's reading 21; defeating the stage wins the game.
16. **The Rear Guard, 3B** (`literal`; precedent The Mines of Moria reading 7, Old Man Willow 147006:B:1; https://visionofthepalantir.com/2023/06/23/escape-from-khazad-dum/ agrees with the threat line): Durin's Bane is considered engaged with each player and attacks each player in turn order in the combat phase with a shadow card dealt for each attack. It is read like The Watcher at 5 progress, but with no progress condition: from the moment this stage is current, every player may declare attackers against it, it adds no Threat to the staging area, and it cannot leave the staging area (the rule that holds The Watcher; Old Man Willow prints it). No player's threat can be reduced by a player card effect (18071:B:1). A shadow card dealt to Durin's Bane resolves as to any attacker.
17. **Winning, and why the Bane can stay** (`literal`): each stage 3 card wins the game when it is defeated: the players place its quest points in progress (10, 15 or 20). Last Lord also needs Durin's Bane out of play first; The Bridge and The Rear Guard do not, so Durin's Bane need not be killed there (https://visionofthepalantir.com/2023/06/23/escape-from-khazad-dum/). Moria's reading 21 holds: a stage that cannot be defeated advances as soon as nothing holds it.
18. **Cave Spider and the other "either" lines with no named player** (`recommended`; as The Mines of Moria reading 19; Rules Reference rr.the-word-choose): Black Bats' When Revealed and Cave Spider's Forced line are the first player's choice (the Spider's Forced line: the defending player's; the attack is against one player), and the choice tray asks. Cave Spider's other option is a +2 Attack for this attack. Black Bats' Shadow is the defending player's.
19. **Orcish Howls and Lost and Alone** (`recommended`; as The Mines of Moria reading 19, Grabbed by Spiders): Orcish Howls exhausts X characters, X the Uruk enemies in play (staging area or engaged): the first player chooses them among the ready characters of every player, all of them if there are fewer. Lost and Alone removes, for each player, the questing character with the highest Willpower (reading 3).
20. **Undisturbed Bones** (`recommended`; https://visionofthepalantir.com/2023/06/23/escape-from-khazad-dum/ reads it as preventing a cancel, no ruling found; rr.cancel for what a cancel is): it attaches to the main quest card (never a side quest, `AttachToQuest main`) and stays there until the stage changes (the stage leaving play discards it). When a "when revealed" effect would be canceled, the cancel does not happen: Undisturbed Bones is discarded and the effect resolves. The ability that tried to cancel is still used and its cost still paid (rr.cancel). It answers the first cancel only, as it is gone then. Alternative: the cancel happens and Bones is discarded as well; the words "instead" rule it out.
21. **Fatigue** (`recommended`; no ruling: https://hallofbeorn.com/LotR/Details/Fatigue-EfKD carries the text only): When Revealed, each player exhausts a character he controls (his choice, a ready one if any); until the end of the round player card effects cannot ready any character (`cannotReadyByPlayerCard`; framework readying in the refresh phase still happens). Shadow: the defending character leaves the attack (it stays exhausted: its cost of defending is paid), and the defending player may declare a different character as the defender, who exhausts to defend; he need not, and then the attack is undefended. The removed character cannot be declared again for this attack.
22. **Rock Adder, Cave-troll, The Great Bridge** (`recommended` for The Great Bridge, `literal` for the others): Rock Adder is not defended by any character of any player (no defender can be declared; the attack is undefended); Cave-troll's allies cannot attack or defend (19068:A:1, 143023:A:2). While The Great Bridge is in the staging area, each declaration of attackers against an enemy has one character; another character, even of the same player, may attack the same enemy in a later attack (Pelennor's 145050:A:1 reading: the limit is on one attack). No source on The Great Bridge was found (https://hallofbeorn.com/LotR/Details/The-Great-Bridge-RD is another card).
23. **Branching Paths** (`literal`; https://visionofthepalantir.com/2023/06/23/escape-from-khazad-dum/ says only "quite high threat to begin with"): it counts each location in the staging area, itself included when it is there, and gets no more than +3 Threat in all. It gets the bonus wherever it is (staging area, active location). The Threat is read live (Moria's Chamber of Mazarbul reading 8).
24. **The Mountain's Roots** (`literal`, as The Mines of Moria reading 8, Chamber of Mazarbul): its Threat X, and the X of its Travel line, is the stage number of the main quest plus 1, read live. Each player discards that many cards from the top of his deck, one player after another (an empty deck discards what it has, rr.running-out-of-cards).
25. **Deep Deep Dark, Western Hall, Unspeakable Thing, Shadow Dweller** (`literal`; precedent 18057:A:2, 142055:A:2, 19157:A:2, 09034:A:1): "character you control" is any character, ally or hero; X from a discarded card is its printed value, 0 for a card with none, and an empty deck discards nothing (X 0). The damage Shadow Dweller assigns, and Deep Deep Dark's choice, are the defending (or each) player's.
26. **Chieftain of the Pit** (`literal`; precedent 50220:A:2 for the global scope): while it is in play no shadow effect can be canceled, whoever's enemy it is on, not only its own. Nameless Thing and the Elder Nameless Thing follow 18050:A:2 (a guarded attachment under the enemy) and 09026:A:2 (a Forced line it gains while in the staging area). Nameless Thing's guarded card is discarded, to its owner's discard pile, when it leaves play (the printed line says "discard that card"; Hobgoblin returns its card to the hand, so `GuardWithDeckTop` has the field `discards` and the marker verb is `DiscardGuarded`).
27. **Black Uruks** (`literal`; precedent 10129:A:2 for the count): +1 Attack for each shadow card dealt to it for this attack, read live while it attacks, and the extra cards of an additional-shadow effect count. Its Shadow takes the top-5 card named in reading 3 and deals it to the attacking enemy as a shadow card (this does not reveal it). Orc Horn Blower, Orc Raiders and Chieftain's additional shadow cards are dealt one by one (faq.additional-attacks).
28. **Goblin Dungeon** (`recommended`; precedent Carried Away, The Wizard's Quest reading 9, with a location as the host): one ally, the one named in reading 3, lies faceup under Goblin Dungeon, its tokens discarded, still its owner's, out of play and with blank text; it returns exhausted to its owner's control when Goblin Dungeon is explored. If Goblin Dungeon leaves play any other way the ally returns exhausted at once. With no ally in play nothing happens. While it lies there no copy of its title can be played.
29. **Mithril Lode** (`literal`; precedent Amon Forn 09026:A:2 for a line gained while active): while it is the active location it has the Response; when it is explored, each player adds 1 resource to the pool of a hero he controls. The player chooses the hero, so the choice tray asks, naming the owner. A Response is optional: the player may decline.
30. **Other lines** (`literal`; cited): Swarm of Bats and Rock Adder's Shadow as printed; Twisting Passage (51249:A:1, 51152:A:1: "add it to the staging area", not "reveal"); Many Roads (51204:B:1, "different 0-cost location per player", no limit of 2); Silent Caverns (51143:A:1); Orc Raiders (143056:A:1); Chance Encounter (51014:A:1; Shadow: the additional attack comes after this one, faq.additional-attacks); Mithril Lode and Elder Nameless Thing, readings 26 and 29; New Devilry (Woodland 51114: text box blank, Traits kept, rr.blank; the same sentence without "printed"); Dark Places (09058:A:1, the Threat of the active location, surge with none; Shadow X the stage number); Forked Passage (01114:A:1: one more threat at the refresh phase, the same moment as the printed raise); Lightless Grotto's Travel is an effect, not a cost: with no enemy in the staging area nothing is engaged.
31. **Watchful Eyes** (`recommended`; "Limit 1 per hero" is part of the card's own attached text, no source): the opposing team attaches it to the hero with the highest Willpower that has no Watchful Eyes yet; with every hero holding one, none is attached.
32. **Elder Nameless Thing's gained Forced effect** (`recommended`; precedent 51007:B:1, which reads "after the players have committed characters to the quest" as the beginning of the staging step): while it is in the staging area it attacks the first player at the beginning of the staging step.
33. **Black Uruks and the Travel of Lightless Grotto** (`recommended`): +1 Attack for each shadow card is read while the stat is read and only while Black Uruks is the attacking enemy (the flag `attackPerShadow`); Lightless Grotto's Travel engages the chosen enemy with the first player, who chooses it.
