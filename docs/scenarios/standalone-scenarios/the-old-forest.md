# The Old Forest

The 2014 print-on-demand scenario (pack `TOF`), Normal and Easy. Hall of Beorn files it and Fog on the Barrow-downs both under the product `2014`, so the product line names its scenario title (`scenarioTitle` in `scripts/data/campaign-defs.ts`). No Nightmare deck exists for it: the Nightmare source has no set for it (`scripts/data/nightmare-defs.ts`, `data/raw/nightmare`) and Hall of Beorn's Nightmare quantities only repeat the Normal ones (https://hallofbeorn.com/LotR/Scenarios/The-Old-Forest). Hall of Beorn lists no rulesheet for it and `data/rules` holds none; the only source for its rules is RulesPal's transcription, a community source (https://rulespal.com/the-lord-of-the-rings-the-card-game-the-old-forest/rulebook), so those readings are marked "researched, community (RulesPal transcription)".

- **Data.** Hall of Beorn numbers its 24 cards `147001` to `147024`, RingsDB's own code for the pack (cycle 50, position 4 holds only 147024, Old Bogey-stories), so no `productCodes` rule is needed: `147001` the stage 1, `147002` to `147005` the four stage 2s, `147006` the stage 3, `147007` Old Man Willow, `147011` Withywindle, `147022` Tom Bombadil, `147023` the campaign card, `147024` the boon. Aragorn (01001) is tagged with the card set "The Old Forest" in Hall of Beorn's player cards and stays a Core Set card. Hall of Beorn prints "The Wiched Willow" for 147006 and serves its scans at that spelling, which answers 403; `overrides.cards` names it "The Wicked Willow" with the working addresses, and `overrides.scenarioStages` adds it to the stages because Hall of Beorn's scenario file lists stages 1 and 2 only. The piles are DragnCards' deck Q99.04: Old Man Willow and Withywindle are set aside, the 33 other cards of the set (Tom Bombadil among them) are the encounter deck, and five locations (one each of Misleading Path, Deep Gulley, Wall of Trees, Strange Woods and Oppressive Forest) are the copies the Setup search finds, kept as `searched` staging placements like the other scenarios' explorer searches. The Setup line "Each player searches the encounter deck and adds 1 different location to the staging area" is read in `scripts/data/normalize/setup-standalone-scenarios.ts`. Tom Bombadil's scan is printed in the set Fog on the Barrow-downs (41/41) though Hall of Beorn and DragnCards deal it with The Old Forest.
- **Easy.** DragnCards has no Easy deck (no E99.04), so Easy loads Q99.04 and `overrides.scenarioCounts` takes out what Hall of Beorn's Easy counts remove (its scenario page shows 41 Normal and 34 Easy encounter cards): 2 Falling Branches (3 to 1), 2 Long Trailer (4 to 2), 1 Song of Sleep (2 to 1), and one copy each of Misleading Path and Oppressive Forest (2 to 1; the searched copy goes, the deck copy stays), 7 in all. The gold mark is printed per copy, so a scan cannot confirm which copies; Hall of Beorn's counts are the only source (recommended: agent recommendation, not user-confirmed).
- **Campaign card and boon.** The Old Forest (147023, "The Lord of the Rings Part 1.1": "You are playing Campaign Mode", Setup attaches Old Bogey-stories, Resolution earns the boon if Old Man Willow holds at least 12 damage) and the boon Old Bogey-stories (147024) belong to Campaign Mode after A Shadow of the Past. The cycle is standalone only with no campaign log, so the build deals neither (`campaignPlaced` is empty, the "(Campaign)" list entry and the boon are skipped) and their three printed sides are in the ledger with every line inert (Campaign Mode only) and the two cards are excepted in `scripts/checks/box-dealt.test.ts`; the standalone game ends at stage 3B ("If the players defeat this stage, they win the game").
- **Ledger.** The 28 printed sides of the dealt cards are read from the scans (`data/printed/standalone-scenarios.json`). Hall of Beorn's text has "beginning of the planning phase" for the printed "end of the planning phase" (147002 B), "as a result of questing" for "as the result of" on the four stage 2Bs, "The player cannot win" for "The players cannot win" (Withywindle), "gains :" (Deep Gulley) and "Then Shuffle" (Tom Bombadil).
- **Readings.** From RulesPal's transcription (researched, community): the Staging step reveals encounter cards one at a time in player order, "you" on a card being the revealing player and a Surge card giving that player an additional card; Peril means that player resolves the card alone without conferring, and the others cannot act or trigger responses; stage 2B moving to "a different random stage 2A" shuffles the other stage 2 cards, picks one, returns the old stage 2 to the quest deck and discards its progress and attachments; "Immune to player card effects" ignores all player card effects and cannot be targeted by them; Indestructible cannot be destroyed by damage. The transcription says nothing on Easy mode and nothing further on Doomed (recommended: the Rules Reference's Doomed rule applies as for the other scenarios). X for the stage 2B victory test is five times the number of players, counted in victory points on locations in the victory display (printed). Which of two stage 2A cards the engine draws is a seeded random pick (recommended: agent recommendation).
- **Line readings** (Read task; status after each, sources in the closing line; "community" = Vision of the Palantír and Unfinished Trails, not official).
  - Staging, keywords (every line): the engine's one staging order is the Saga rule (`mec113.saga-staging-rules`, `mec109.peril`, `faq.peril-attacks`; RulesPal agrees): each seat reveals in turn, "you" is the revealing seat, Surge gives that seat another card, Doomed X raises every seat's threat. Surge, Peril, Doomed 2, Indestructible and Immune to player card effects are keyword lines the keyword framework owns (`mec109.indestructible`, `rr.immune`, `mec109.immune-to-player-card-effects`, `rr.doomed-x`). Status: span-settled.
  - Who chooses: a "choose" on a Peril card (Swaying Without Wind, Song of Sleep) is the revealing player's, as Saruman's Voice (`143068:A:2`); on any other encounter or quest card the first player's (`rr.the-word-choose`; Tom Bombadil as the same text on `22010:A:2`, the stage 2A search). Status: recommended (agent recommendation; no ruling found).
  - Setup (`147001:A:1`): each player in turn searches the encounter deck (the discard pile is empty) for a location of a different title and adds it to the staging area, then the deck is shuffled; "set aside" is the data's piles, as `19109:A:1`. Status: literal.
  - Stage 1B (`147001:B:1`): stage 1A prints 0 quest points, so 1B resolves as the opening of the game, as `146072:B:1` does: each player draws 1, then the quest advances to one of the four stage 2s at random with the game's seed. Status: literal; `stages.test.ts` pins that it resolves right after the Setup's search is answered.
  - Different random stage 2A (`147001:B:1`, `147002:B:2` to `147005:B:2`): from stage 1B the quest takes one of the four stage 2s; from a stage 2B it takes one of the other three (never the same one), the stage 2 left goes back into the quest deck with its progress and attachments discarded, and the new one arrives with no progress and its When Revealed resolves. Not Roam Across Rhovanion's each-once order. Status: researched, community (RulesPal https://rulespal.com/the-lord-of-the-rings-the-card-game-the-old-forest/rulebook: "shuffles each stage 2 (except for the one currently in play)... The previous stage 2 is placed back in the quest deck... Discard any progress or attachments"; https://visionofthepalantir.com/2019/03/10/the-old-forest/).
  - The stage 2B Forced effect (`147002:B:2` to `147005:B:2`): it fires once after a successful quest resolution that placed progress anywhere, on the active location, on Wall of Trees or on the stage ("placing progress", not "placing progress here"; a stage 2 prints no quest points, so the stage itself never needs the progress); then, with X or more Victory points in the victory display (X is 5 times the players), the quest advances to 3A, else to a different random stage 2A. Only locations score there (no dealt enemy prints Victory). Status: researched, community (https://unfinishedtrailsblog.wordpress.com/2019/08/02/saga-guide-part-2-the-old-forest/, https://visionofthepalantir.com/2019/03/10/the-old-forest/: "it doesn't matter if the active location is cleared or not").
  - Stage 2A When Revealed (`147002:A:1` to `147005:A:1`): when the staging area holds fewer locations than the players, the first player searches the encounter deck and discard pile for a location and adds it to the staging area; the deck is shuffled whether or not a location was added. Status: literal (https://visionofthepalantir.com/2019/03/10/the-old-forest/: the deck is reshuffled each time).
  - Stage 2B costs: `147002:B:1` each player exhausts a hero he controls (none ready: none; `01077:A:1`, `144052:A:3`); `147003:B:1` "at the beginning of the round" is the start of the Resource phase, the round's first window, removing all progress from every location in play (staging and active); `147004:B:1` a random location of the victory display goes to the encounter discard pile (any trait); `147005:B:1` and Withywindle (`147011:A:3`) one more encounter card in the staging step, revealed by the first player (`41006:B:1`, `142067:B:1`). Status: literal, except the round's beginning (recommended: agent recommendation; no other card prints it).
  - Stage 3 (`147006:A:1`, `B:1`, `B:2`): Old Man Willow and Withywindle come from set aside to the staging area (added, not revealed); then the first player reveals players minus 1 encounter cards (0 alone, 1 with two seats; ordinary reveals). Old Man Willow holds the Watcher flags (cannot leave the staging area, engaged with each player for attacks, attackable by any, no Threat in the staging area, following the Watcher in the Water's reading (`142024:A:2`) by `rr.engaged`; `10126:B:1`, `19125:A:3`), and a shadow card is dealt and resolved for each attack as usual (Thaurdir's held shadows are not needed). The defeat is the win. Status: literal.
  - Withywindle (`147011:A:2`): the players cannot win while it is in play, by `Restrict advance` as `09017:A:3`; "in play" excludes the victory display and the discard pile (`rr.in-play-and-out-of-play`), so once Old Man Willow has discarded it from the display the stage can be defeated until it returns. Status: literal.
  - Old Man Willow (`147007:A:2`): the Forced effect hears his own attack, discards a random location from the victory display (any trait) to the encounter discard pile, and the defending player raises his threat by its Victory value; none there: nothing. Status: literal (community agrees, Unfinished Trails).
  - Long Trailer (`147008:A:1`): in the staging area, after the active location is explored (the active location only), it makes an immediate attack against the first player; each Long Trailer does. Status: literal; `18016:A:3`.
  - Grasping Root (`147009:A:1`): after it engages a player by any means, that player assigns X damage among characters he controls, X the locations in the victory display. Army of Flies (`147010:A:2`, `147010:A:3`): the player picks a non-objective attachment he controls; the shadow likewise (the copy `142034:A:2` omits `nonObjective`, so it is written afresh). Status: literal.
  - Misleading Path (`147012`): the travel line is `01088:A:1` and `18046:A:2`; the Shadow discards each ally card in the defending player's hand. Deep Gulley (`147013:A:1`): in the staging area, after a player optionally engages an enemy that player raises his threat by the enemy's current Attack when the Forced effect resolves (`rr.modifiers`). Strange Woods (`147014`): no character is healed while it is in the staging area (`09055:A:2`); the Shadow raises each player's threat by 2 (the printed "each player", unlike `09056:A:2`) if the attack destroys a character. Status: literal.
  - Wall of Trees (`147015`): the scan prints Threat X, Quest points 4 and Victory X, X the players (Hall of Beorn's Victory 254 is its code for X: `overrides.cards` must clear it and a record gives the Victory, `gainsVictory`; the score and the stage 2B test read it); progress goes on it before the quest while it is in the staging area (`142052:A:1`). Status: literal (scan 147015.A; community: Victory worth 1 to 4 by the players).
  - Oppressive Forest (`147016`): while in the staging area each player cannot play more than 1 card each round: cards played, any phase, none put into play (`rr.play-and-put-into-play`; unlike `00017:B:2`). Its Shadow adds it to the staging area when no location is there (`50223:A:3`). Status: span-settled for played against put into play; per round is literal (community: "per turn, not phase").
  - Swaying Without Wind (`147017:A:2`): the revealing player chooses a location in the victory display and it returns to the staging area; none returned (none in the display) gains surge. Status: recommended on the chooser (see above), else literal.
  - Falling Branches (`147018`): each player chooses a character he controls that is committed to the quest, removes it from the quest and deals it 2 damage (3 to an ally); with none committed nothing happens. The Shadow is +2 Attack when the defending character is an ally, else +1 (an undefended attack +1). Status: recommended (agent recommendation; as Steaming Vent and Saruman's Voice above; no ruling found).
  - Song of Sleep (`147019:A:2`): the revealing player chooses a hero he controls that is committed to the quest and has no Song of Sleep (Limit 1 per hero), removes it from the quest and attaches the card (a Condition with blank text and cannot ready, `144060:A:1`); with none, nothing attaches and the treachery is discarded. Status: recommended (agent recommendation; no ruling found).
  - Lost and Witless (`147020:A:2`): all progress on staging locations is discarded first (the active location keeps its own, and takes the old one's progress back as it returns); then a non-unique active location, if there is one, is switched with a staging location chosen at random with the game's seed (none in staging: nothing). The switch is not travel (`MakeActive`). Status: literal (community: "randomly switches").
  - Ending and Failing (`147021:A:2`): Doomed 2 on reveal; at the end of that phase each player raises his threat by the enemies in the staging area counted then, Old Man Willow included (he lies in the staging area though considered engaged). Status: researched, community (https://visionofthepalantir.com/2019/03/10/the-old-forest/: "in Stage 3 this is guaranteed to be at least 3 threat").
  - Tom Bombadil (`147022:A:1`): the first player chooses a player who takes control of Tom ready and committed; at the end of the round Tom is discarded, then the encounter discard pile (Tom in it) is shuffled into the encounter deck, only if the discard happened (`rr.then`: Tom already out of play leaves the pile alone); the effect cannot be canceled. Status: literal (as `22010:A:2`; community agrees).
  - Not found: no official source (FFG rules insert, FAQ, Hall of Beorn, designers) has any of these; RulesPal's page is a transcription and the two blogs are fans' reading.
- **The table.** No new zone, flag or widget. Stage 2 (`147002:B:2` to `147005:B:2`) has no quest points, so its test is on the top bar: a Victory dial reads the Victory points of the locations in the victory display against the players times 5 (read from the stage's own record), a goal dial that warns within one location (3 points at most) of the number, and a tap opens the victory display's list (`readouts/standalone-scenarios/old-forest.ts`). The stage's preview says what the number sends the quest to in place of "No quest points", and the advance banner says which side of the test the advance fell on ("7 of 10 Victory points in the victory display: the quest goes to a different stage 2A", or that the number was reached and the quest goes to stage 3A). On stage 3B the quest readout adds "; Withywindle holds the win" when the progress would fill the stage while Withywindle is in play (`147011:A:2`). Old Man Willow's preview and long press say he is engaged with each player from the staging area, attacks every player, can be attacked by any character and adds no Threat, in place of a "Cannot be engaged" pill.
- An enemy in the staging area that is considered engaged with a player is engaged with him for a card that reads "engaged with", and is no engagement event (docs/brief.md, Considered to be engaged).
