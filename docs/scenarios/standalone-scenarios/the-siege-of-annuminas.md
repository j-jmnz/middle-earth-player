# The Siege of Annúminas

The 2016 Gen Con event scenario (pack `TSoA`, RingsDB's code for it, position 8 of cycle 50; Hall of Beorn lists it under the year `2016`), Normal and Easy, the eighth of the cycle. It is played in the Standard Game Mode, 1 to 4 players. The Epic Multiplayer Mode (3 to 12 players in 3 teams at 3 stages at once, each stage with its own staging area and encounter deck) is not played: it needs several games on one table and a team of players for each, which the app's single table does not hold. No Nightmare deck exists for it (`data/raw/nightmare` has no set for it), and Hall of Beorn lists no rulesheet; the rules are the quest cards, the two mode cards and the five-page Scenario Rules insert, whose pages 1 to 5 and the FAQ are DragnCards' three Rules rows (read from their pictures, below).

- **Data.** Hall of Beorn numbers its cards `00001` to `00032` (and Boromir `02095` and Faramir `06081`), which collide with The Massing at Osgiliath's; RingsDB lists none of the pack's cards. `overrides.productCodes` gives them `508` and the collector number (`50801` Standard Game Mode, `50802` Epic Multiplayer Mode, `50803` to `50805` the Standard stages 1A to 3A, `50806` to `50808` the Epic stages 1C to 3C, `50809` to `50811` the Epic stages 1E to 3E, `50812` Annúminas, `50813` to `50816` the Lieutenant of Angmar, Battering Ram, Enemy Camp and Siege Tunnel, `50817` Host of Angmar, `50818` Dúnedain of Annúminas, `50819` to `50832` the rest of the encounter deck). Boromir and Faramir are RingsDB's own cards and keep their codes. The piles are DragnCards' deck Q99.08, labelled "Standard The Siege of Annúminas" (`deck.label`), with no Easy twin: Hall of Beorn's Easy counts equal the Normal ones, so Easy loads the same deck. Q99.09 is the Epic deck. DragnCards spells the pack "The Siege of Annuminas" without the accent, so `overrides.dragnPackAliases` maps its rows.
- **Mode cards.** Hall of Beorn types them `GenCon_Setup`; they are `campaign` cards like the Mountain of Fire's Epic Multiplayer Mode, and the Hunt for the Dreadnaught's three mode cards are. The scenario's `modeCard` is Standard Game Mode: the build reads its Setup with the quest card's (Annúminas starts in the staging area, so `overrides.scenarioPiles` moves it from DragnCards' set-aside pile; `overrides.scenarioExcluded` keeps the card out of the count check). Its back is in play beside the quest deck all game. Printed, front: "Setup: Arrange the quest cards into three separate quest decks: 1A-3A, 1C-3C, and 1E-3E. Remove the 1C-3C deck and the 1E-3E deck from the game. Search the encounter deck for Annúminas, add it to the staging area, and set its city strength to 20. Flip this card over and place it next to the quest deck." Back: "Forced: When the players advance to stage 2A (and before resolving its 'when revealed' effect), shuffle each set aside Dúnedain of Annúminas into the encounter deck. Then, shuffle X copies of Host of Angmar into the encounter deck, where X is one less than the number of players in the game, to a minimum of 1." and "If a player is eliminated from the game, deal 9 damage to Annúminas." The Epic stages (`50806` to `50811`) and `50802` are in the catalog and the ledger and are excluded from the scenario's stages (`overrides.scenarioExcluded`), because the Standard Setup removes them from the game.
- **Scenario Rules cards.** Read from the pictures of DragnCards' three Rules rows (https://dragncards-lotrlcg.s3.amazonaws.com/cards/English/6c89f835-68f8-4882-90ea-83f84953fabf.jpg, 8bc0199c-09ff-463f-a6b5-886d14d843cc.jpg, 9a89ea1f-1835-4ed8-918e-28d248771378.jpg, each with a `.B.jpg` back). Page 1: the scenario was designed for the Gen Con 2016 special event; it can be played in the standard game mode and the epic multiplayer mode; the expansion symbol (a tower) comes before each card's collector number. Page 2: standard mode supports 1 to 4 players and needs 1 copy of the scenario; epic mode 3 to 12 players and 3 copies; to play standard mode follow the Setup of the card labelled "Standard Game Mode". Pages 3 and 4 (Epic only): 3 teams of 1 to 4 players, one team at each stage, each stage its own game and encounter deck; the players at a stage are the only players in the game at that stage; end-of-round effects resolve in alphabetical stage order. Page 4 also prints Immune to player card effects: such a card ignores the effects of all player cards and cannot be chosen as a target of a player card effect. Page 5: "City strength is a value that represents the amount of damage the city of Annúminas can take before it is overrun by the enemy. To set the city strength of Annúminas during setup, take a threat tracker, place it next to Annúminas, and set its value to the correct amount. That threat tracker is the city strength tracker for the rest of the game. Any time Annúminas takes X damage, reduce its city strength by X. If the city strength of Annúminas reaches 0, the players lose the game." Archery X: while a card with Archery is in play, players must deal damage to character cards in play equal to the archery value at the beginning of each combat phase; the damage can be dealt to characters under any player's control and divided among the players as they see fit. FAQ (the last card): a "group" is the 3 to 12 players in the event, a "team" the 1 to 4 players at one stage; a card is in a victory display if it is in the victory display of any team in the group; damage on an enemy stays when it moves to another stage; the first player who takes control of a Dúnedain of Annúminas at stage 1 does not resolve its When Revealed effect; a destroyed Dúnedain of Annúminas goes to the encounter deck discard pile.
- **Ledger.** The 43 printed sides (both mode cards, all 9 quest cards and the 21 encounter cards; the 1C to 3C and 1E to 3E stages and the Epic card too) are read from the scans (`data/printed/standalone-scenarios.json`). Hall of Beorn's text differs in: 50801 A "Remove the 1C-3C deck and 1E-3E deck" (printed "and the 1E-3E deck"); 50801 B the second sentence run on after "1." and "Then" lacking its comma; 50802 A "three separate staging area, one for each quest" (printed "staging areas, one for each quest deck"), "adds it to the staging area" (printed "their staging area") and "Flip over all 3 Setup cards"; 50802 B "2D" for "the staging area of stage 2D" and the quoted effect in single quotes (printed double); 50803 A the Setup list "each copy Host Angmar" (printed "each copy of Host of Angmar"); 50804 A "until their are X enemies are discarded" (printed "until X enemies are discarded"); 50807 A and 50810 A "players in the game" and "discarded this way" (printed "players at this stage" and "discarded in this way"); 50805 A "Each player reveals" (printed "Each other player reveals"); 50805, 50808 and 50811 B "the Lieutenant of Angmar is in the victory display" (printed "If Lieutenant of Angmar is in a victory display"); 50808 B "Process" (printed "Progress"); 50809 A "One of these enemies" (printed "those"); 50831 A no "When Revealed:" prefix. Paragraphs Hall of Beorn ran together are split into lines (50801 B, 50809 B, 50810 B, 50811 B). Four printed typos are kept under `misprints`: "the the quest deck" (50801 A), "each copy Dúnedain of Annúminas" (50803 A, 50806 A, 50809 A), "the the top" (50806 A) and "enocunter" (50809 A).
- **Not built yet.** The records, spec rows, asks and goldens of the scenario come after its data. The Standard Game Mode is the whole scenario: stages 1A to 3A, the encounter deck and the mode card are complete without the Epic Multiplayer Mode. No Standard card puts Enemy Camp or Siege Tunnel into play (only the Epic stages do), so those two stay set aside; their lines still get records, tested on a scene-kit table. Mechanics new to the engine: the mode card kept beside the quest deck by the scenario (reading 3) and the city strength readout. Archery already exists (`mec109.archery`, `archeryTotal` in `src/rules/combat/combat.ts`, a card in the staging area counts), and so do the other verbs the cards need.

- **Readings.** Numbered; the status is `literal`, `span-settled`, `researched` or `recommended`. Official sources and the insert say nothing on Standard mode beyond the printed text (Hall of Beorn's card pages, the FFG forum thread https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/225988-siege-of-annuminas-gencon-and-fellowship-event/ and the FFG news article read: none has a ruling); the fan analysis https://visionofthepalantir.com/2022/01/07/the-siege-of-annuminas/ agrees with readings 2, 8, 13 and 14 and is not official.
  1. Epic Multiplayer Mode (50802, 50806 to 50811) is not built; Standard Game Mode (one team, one stage, 1 to 4 players) is complete without it (`literal`, insert page 2).
  2. Stage, team and group wording in the Standard stages and encounter cards ("players at this stage", "each enemy at each stage", "all teams lose", "each team discards from their encounter deck") reads as the one table: the players in the game, every enemy in play (staging area and engaged), the players lose, the one encounter deck. The insert FAQ defines a team as the players at one stage and a group as the 3 to 12 players of the event (`researched`: the insert, https://visionofthepalantir.com/2022/01/07/the-siege-of-annuminas/ says stage 2A's X is the number of players less one).
  3. The Standard Game Mode card (50801) is a constant of the scenario, not a pile: `ScenarioData.modeCard` (written by the build from the cycle definition's `modeCard`) puts it in `ScenarioDefinition.modeCard` at the end of Setup, side B faceup in the `modeCard` zone beside the quest deck, where the engine already keeps the Hard/Expert Mode card. Its three lines are owned by records on 50801: the front Setup (`setup` timing, run before the quest card's Setup; the pile deals Annúminas to the staging area, the record sets the strength to 20) and the two back lines (`forced` and a player-eliminated trigger). The card joins `dealtCodes`, so ownership coverage asks for all three lines and its box-dealt exception goes; 50802 and the Epic stages keep theirs (`recommended`: agent recommendation, a design choice on the ledger, no rule is in question).
  4. City strength is the resource tokens on Annúminas: Setup places 20, "deal X damage to Annúminas" removes X tokens (never below 0), "increase the city strength by 10" places 10, and a constant record loses the game when none are left (`literal`, insert page 5: "reduce its city strength by X", "reaches 0"; the Defense of Helm's Deep tracker keeps tokens on its objective, mec112.defense). The table shows it as a readout with its limit.
  5. 50803:B:2 Response: remove 10 progress from stage 1B is the cost, so the Response is offered only with 10 or more progress on it; the strength then rises by 10. Stage 1 has no quest points, so progress never advances it (`literal`, rr.then).
  6. 50804:A:1 X is the number of players less 1, at least 1, counted over players still in the game (`literal`; `DiscardUntilEnemies` `perPlayer 1, less 1, atLeast 1` is how 2A of The Wastes of Eriador reads the same words). The 50801 back reads "players in the game" the same way.
  7. 50804:B:2: a tie for the highest Attack in the staging area is the first player's choice (rr.first-player); "no attacks are made" means no enemy is in the staging area (`recommended`, agent recommendation). The enemy attacks from the staging area against each player in turn order; each attack resolves in full.
  8. 50804:B:3 has one stage in Standard mode, so no stage is chosen: each player heals 1 damage from a character he controls (`recommended`).
  9. 50805:B:2 names an enemy "at another stage". Standard mode has no other stage, so no enemy can be chosen and the Response is never offered (`rr.the-word-choose`: no valid target, the ability cannot be initiated). The record keeps the printed instructions and the stage filter, which no Standard game satisfies; the other reading (any enemy in play) adds power the card does not print (`recommended`).
  10. 50805:B:3 resolves last in the end of the round, after the Forced effects (rr.forced: Forced effects take priority) and the Responses; a city strength of 0 loses at once, wherever it happens (`recommended`; https://visionofthepalantir.com/2022/01/07/the-siege-of-annuminas/ says the players must survive the round).
  11. 50812:A:1 counts every enemy in play when it resolves, the Lieutenant of Angmar and the Host of Angmar in the staging area included (`literal`, reading 2).
  12. 50813:A:2 reads the progress on the current quest card (stage 3B); 50813:A:3 sends the Lieutenant to the one staging area, then it attacks the first player from there (`literal`; Pack Leader in The Wastes of Eriador has the same first line).
  13. 50814:A:2 places the resource first, then X is read: 2 damage the first round, then 3, 4 and on (`literal`, "Then", rr.then; the fan analysis agrees).
  14. 50818:A:2 "any player may spend 1 resource": a Pick of a player (not the first player), who pays 1 resource from one of his heroes; with no taker the Dúnedain is discarded. A destroyed Dúnedain of Annúminas goes to the encounter discard pile (`literal`, insert FAQ; cookbook, Readings the review catches).
  15. 50803:A:1 "one of those locations must be Gate of Annúminas": `SearchMove` with `require: "50819"`, so the first player's search takes it and the others are free; alone, the one location is the Gate (`literal` by sibling: 142022:A:1, 145030:A:3).
  16. "Either ... or ..." lines (50817, 50824, 50825, 50826, 50829, 50830, 50831): an option the player cannot do at all is not offered, and a player with neither does nothing (`recommended`, the way the engine reads the other scenarios' either/or lines).
  17. 50801:B:1 resolves when the players advance to stage 2, before 2A's When Revealed (`literal`): the Dúnedain and the X copies of Host of Angmar are shuffled in, then 2A's discard step sees them. 50801:B:2 deals 9 damage each time a player is eliminated; the last player's elimination loses the game first (`literal`).
  18. 50815:A:2 and 50816:A:2 read under each stage 3 and each team as the one stage 3 and the one team (reading 2); no Standard card brings either card into play (`literal`).
  19. 50804:B:2 reveals the top card when no attack was made in this way (the effect's own count of attacks), not when the staging area holds no enemy afterwards; 50805:B:1 bars progress while Battering Ram (a location) is in play, the active location included; 50820:A:2 tests its 3 resources before the discard and deals the damage and reveals on that one check (rr.then) (`literal`; rr.in-play-and-out-of-play).
  20. 50814:A:1 gives Battering Ram "Immune to player card effects" although the source lists no keyword for it (its line carries X first): the record sets the flag while it is in play (`literal`; the other locations and enemies of the box have it as a keyword line of its own).
  21. 50822:A:1 bars the cancel of 'when revealed' effects only (`rr.cancel`): while Hills of Evendim is in the staging area a Shadow effect can still be canceled (`literal`).
  22. 50820:A:3 City Wall: with an empty encounter deck outside the quest phase the Travel cost cannot be paid, so the players cannot travel there; the deck is not reset for the cost (`rr.travel`, `rr.running-out-of-cards`; Caleb Grace, ALeP 3.243 and 3.358, https://alongextendedparty.com/semi-official-errata-faq-1-10/; `researched`).
- **The table.** The Table step (`docs/agents/agent-brief.md`, Scenario procedure; lines `50801:B:2`, `50812:A:1`, `50812:A:2`, `50814:A:2`, `50820:A:2`; `src/play/readouts/standalone-scenarios/annuminas.ts`, words `annuminas-words.ts`; test `annuminas.test.ts`): one top-bar dial after the seat dials. The City dial reads the city strength, the resource tokens on Annúminas, on its disc, with no ring: it counts down to the loss at 0 and has no limit above, so only its warnings colour it. Its note says the damage the end of the round brings (1 for each enemy in play, the engaged ones too; Battering Ram's X; City Wall's 4 when its next resource is its third) and that a player eliminated costs 9. It warns near at 10 or less and critical when the round's damage is as much as the strength left; a tap shows Annúminas, then the Standard Game Mode (the mode card of the scenario, lying beside the quest deck on its back: `modeCard`, Setup), so its Forced and the 9 damage for a player lost can be read; the Dúnedain of Annúminas and the other cards are cards in the staging or a play area. The heroes held under stage 3 by Enemy Camp (`50815:A:2`) belong to the Epic game, which is not built: no Standard card brings Enemy Camp into play, so no held dial is added. Every pick that spans seats (the Hordes of Angmar's return and search, Sorcery of Carn Dûm, Defend the City's attacker) is owned by the player and leads with "Player N" two-handed.
