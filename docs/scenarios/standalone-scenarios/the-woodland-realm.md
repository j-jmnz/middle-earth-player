# The Woodland Realm

The 2018 Gen Con print-on-demand scenario kit (pack `TWR`, RingsDB's code for it, position 11 of cycle 50; Hall of Beorn lists it under its own name), Normal and Easy alike, the eleventh of the cycle. It is a kit like The Wizard's Quest: seven quest cards, 14 encounter sets of three cards and the double-sided Spider Den / Mendor card, and the players build the scenario from it. The rulebook (`https://rulespal.com/the-lord-of-the-rings-the-card-game-the-woodland-realm/rulebook`) gives the same Quick-build (one stage 2 and one stage 3 under stage 1, 7 different encounter sets shuffled together; random choice allowed), Advanced-build (35 cards, at least 10 enemies, 10 locations and 10 treacheries, at most 3 copies of a non-unique card, cost 21 in all, cards of The Wizard's Quest allowed) and competitive modes as The Wizard's Quest. The app plays a cooperative Quick-build game only. No Nightmare deck exists for it.

- **Data.** Hall of Beorn numbers its cards `00001` to `00053`, which collide with earlier packs; RingsDB lists none of the pack's cards. `overrides.productCodes` gives them `511` and the collector number (`51101` Captured by Spiders, `51103` to `51105` the three stage 2 cards, `51107` to `51109` the three stage 3 cards, `51110` Spider Den, `51111` Mendor, `51112` to `51153` the 42 encounter cards; collector numbers 2 and 6 are the shared A sides printed on the stage 2 and stage 3 cards). The piles are DragnCards' deck Q99.12, with no Easy twin: Easy loads the same deck. DragnCards loads the seven quest cards and Spider Den set aside; `overrides.scenarioPiles` puts the quest cards in the quest pile and Spider Den in the staging area, as the 1A Setup says. Spider Den and Mendor are one two-sided card (DragnCards, the rulebook): `overrides.dragnCardCodes` gives Spider Den `51110` and its back `51111`. Hall of Beorn's scenario file lists no quest card and only those two cards, so the scenario's `encounterSets` names the 14 sets of the kit. Hall of Beorn gives the 42 encounter cards no set; each is printed with its set number (a green circle, 1 to 14; the set's cards are numbered x/5, three different cards, 5 copies in all), so `overrides.cards` gives each card its set ("The Woodland Realm 1" to "The Woodland Realm 14", cards `51112` to `51114` the first). The same rows give each card its printed cost (the box at the bottom right, which Hall of Beorn leaves empty), which the stage cards' searches read. The load list deals all 14 sets (70 cards).
- **Setup.** The 1A Setup is "Add Spider Den to the staging area. The opposing team searches the encounter deck for one different 0 cost enemy or 0 cost location per player and adds them to the staging area. Shuffle the encounter deck." The second sentence is the Setup step `searchZeroCostPerPlayer` (The Wizard's Quest uses it too). In a cooperative game there is no opposing team (The Wizard's Quest, reading 1).
- **Quest stages.** Printed, stage 1B (10 quest points): "Forced: When Spider Den is explored, flip it over and put Mendor into play under the first player's control, ready. This stage cannot be defeated unless the first player controls Mendor." The three stage 2 cards share the 2A text: "When Revealed: The opposing team searches the top 5 cards of the encounter deck for a card with cost 1 or less (cost 2 or less if there are two or more players), sets it aside, and shuffles the encounter deck. Reveal the set aside card. Its 'when revealed' effects cannot be canceled." and each has 20 quest points and the Forced line "After characters are committed to the quest, no more than 5 progress can be placed here this round (10 progress instead if Mendor is committed to the quest)": Lost in the Dark (the first treachery revealed each round gains Doomed 1 and its 'when revealed' effects cannot be canceled), "Don't Leave the Path" (progress cannot be placed on locations in the staging area; the players must travel to a location during the travel phase, if able), Ambushed at Night (each enemy in the staging area gets -10 engagement cost, cannot be optionally engaged and cannot take damage). The three stage 3 cards share the 3A text with "cost 2 or less (cost 3 or less if there are two or more players)": The Forest of Great Fear (15; When Revealed: the opposing team searches the encounter deck and discard pile for an enemy, adds it to the staging area, then places a resource on an enemy in play with cost 2 or higher, which gets +2 Attack and +2 Defense and is immune to player card effects while this stage is in play; the stage cannot be defeated while that enemy is in play; defeating it wins the game), To the Elvenking's Halls (X, the highest threat level of any player; each player's threat cannot be reduced by more than 1 each round; Forced: after the players quest successfully, if 10 or more progress was placed here this phase, reveal the top card of the encounter deck; defeating it wins) and Escape from Taur-nu-Fuin (20; Forced: at the beginning of the quest phase, discard from the top of the encounter deck until a location is discarded and add it to the staging area; defeating it wins).
- **Spider Den and Mendor.** Spider Den (Forest, threat 1, 1 quest point): "Immune to player card effects." and "Travel: The opposing team searches the encounter deck and discard pile for a 0 cost enemy and adds it to the staging area, if able (1 cost or less instead if there are two or more players). Shuffle the encounter deck." Mendor (Objective-Ally, Silvan, Scout; Willpower 2, Attack 2, Defense 2, 5 hit points): "Ranged. Sentinel. The first player gains control of Mendor. If Mendor leaves play, the players lose the game."
- **Opposing team.** Printed lines that name the opposing team: the 1A Setup, the three 2A and three 3A sides, 3B The Forest of Great Fear, Spider Den, Chieftain Ufthak, Abandoned Camp, Black Forest Bats, The Spiders' Ring, Caught in a Web, The Goblins' Caves, Driven by Shadow, Eyes of the Forest, Vastness of Mirkwood, Massing at Night. Reading 1 of The Wizard's Quest settles them.
- **Ledger.** The 58 printed sides (7 quest cards, Spider Den, Mendor and 42 encounter cards) are read from the scans (`data/printed/standalone-scenarios.json`). Hall of Beorn's text differs in: 1A ("adds it"), the three 2A sides ("teach", "(2 or less"), the three 3A sides ("It's 'when revealed'", "(3 or less"), 3B The Forest of Great Fear ("the places", "witha"), Spider Den ("1 cost or less if"), Gathering Gloom (no full stop), Caught in a Web ("2 resource"), The Goblins' Caves ("The Goblin's Caves"), Giant Web ("any previous location"), Dry Watercourse ("in in") and Ambushing Orcs ("Engaged", no "and"). One printed typo is kept as a misprint: The Spiders' Ring prints "The Spider's Ring" in its Forced line. The trait line of The Goblins' Caves prints "Mountan", which is not a text line and is not recorded.
- **The table.** Two limits are shown on the table, each a top-bar dial after the seat dials (`readouts/standalone-scenarios/woodland.ts`, `woodland-words.ts`; `docs/design-system-table.md`, Top bar). "Cap" (test id `woodland-cap`) is stage 2's limit on the progress placed on the stage this round (ledger lines 51103:B:2, 51104:B:3, 51105:B:2, reading 9): it appears once the staging step set the cap (5, or 10 with Mendor committed, reading 21), counts what the round has placed, and is a plain limit: it says "near" at the cap and never warms. "Reveal" (test id `woodland-phase`) is stage 3B's tally of the progress placed on it in the quest phase against 10, the number that reveals a card after a successful quest (51108:B:3, reading 14): a goal dial, full at 10. The cap on each player's threat reduction (51108:B:2, 51143:A:1) is the threat readout's (`readouts/threat-level.ts`); Mendor is an ally in the first player's play area, and the resource Forest of Great Fear places is a token on the enemy it names. No control types a number.

**Readings.**

1. **The opposing team in a cooperative game** (`researched`): as The Wizard's Quest, reading 1. The rulebook's Cooperative Play and Rules Clarifications say the first player resolves "the opposing team chooses" and "the opposing team searches" in the manner that gives the players the greatest challenge, and that an effect counting players counts only the active team (`https://rulespal.com/the-lord-of-the-rings-the-card-game-the-woodland-realm/rulebook`). No prompt is shown for it.
2. **Which sets and stages are used** (`researched` for the random sets, `recommended` for the stages): as The Wizard's Quest, reading 2. The rulebook's Quick-build says "choosing one stage 2 and one stage 3" and "choosing 7 different encounter sets" and allows a random choice in a cooperative game (rulebook URL of reading 1); `https://visionofthepalantir.com/2023/03/06/the-woodland-realm/` describes picking seven of the fourteen sets and three stage cards. The app deals 7 of the 14 sets from the seed and the first player chooses the stage 2 and stage 3 cards. Easy and Normal are the same deck. Advanced-build and the competitive modes are not built.
3. **The opposing team's choice, line by line** (`recommended`, extends The Wizard's Quest reading 3; no source names a measure): the engine chooses for the first player, never asks. A search for an enemy or location takes the highest printed Threat (an enemy: then the highest Attack), then the card nearest the top of the deck (Spider Den Travel, The Forest of Great Fear 3B, Chieftain Ufthak, The Spiders' Ring, The Goblins' Caves, and the 0 cost enemies of Massing at Night, 51141). The top-5 searches of 2A, 3A and Vastness of Mirkwood (51138) take the legal card nearest the top. A choice among enemies (the resource of The Forest of Great Fear, Driven by Shadow) takes the enemy with the highest Threat, then the highest Attack; a questing character (Black Forest Bats) the one with the highest Willpower; a hero for Caught in a Web the hero with the highest Willpower that has no copy; Abandoned Camp's card in the encounter discard pile the enemy or location with the highest Threat (an enemy: then Attack), else the card most recently discarded; the card discarded from each hand (Eyes of the Forest, 51132) the card with the highest printed cost, ties by the card nearest the left of the hand. A card that is not found changes nothing. Alternative: the first player picks through the choice tray.
4. **Encounter cost** (`literal`, data): as The Wizard's Quest, reading 4. Each encounter card prints a cost; `overrides.cards` gives the 42 costs read from the scans. "Cost 2 or higher" (3B) and "cost 1 or less" (Ufthak) read the printed cost, an enemy's or a treachery's.
5. **The 2A and 3A searches** (`literal`, rr.search): as The Wizard's Quest, reading 5. "Cost equal to or less than the stage number of the main quest" (Vastness of Mirkwood, 51138) reads the stage number of the current quest card (1, 2 or 3 here); the card found is revealed with no bar on canceling, since its line does not print one.
6. **The 1A Setup** (`literal`): as The Wizard's Quest, reading 6. Spider Den starts in the staging area (the deal); one different 0 cost enemy or location per player is added, not revealed.
7. **Mendor's control** (`span-settled`, sotm.ally-objective-wilyador and dwd.ally-objective-arwen, which say that "The first player gains control of ..." follows the first player token, and faq.first-player-elimination): the first player controls Mendor as an ally; at the end of each round the new first player gains control. He is an objective-ally with no owner. "If Mendor leaves play, the players lose the game" is a Forced loss for any way of leaving play (the same lines as Arwen Undomiel, 04014:A:3). A "discard an ally you control" effect (Lost and Witless, 51147 Shadow) may take Mendor from his controller; a player with no other ally must discard him and the players lose. No FAQ or forum entry names Mendor (the kit's FAQ, Hall of Beorn's pages and `https://visionofthepalantir.com/2023/03/06/the-woodland-realm/` were read).
8. **Spider Den flips into Mendor** (`literal`): 1B's Forced effect, when Spider Den is explored, turns the one two-sided card over: Spider Den does not go to the discard pile, and Mendor enters play ready, under the first player's control, with none of Spider Den's tokens. Stage 1B (10 quest points) cannot be defeated until the first player controls Mendor, so the players explore Spider Den first. Spider Den's Travel runs on the travel and its own immunity to player card effects keeps player effects from clearing or traveling to it.
9. **The stage 2B progress cap** (`recommended`, no source): the cap of 5 (10 while Mendor is committed to the quest) counts all progress placed on the stage this round, whatever places it, and is read at each placement, so removing Mendor from the quest mid-phase lowers it. "After characters are committed" is read as the cap holding from the end of the commit step; progress cannot normally be placed on the stage before it. `https://visionofthepalantir.com/2023/03/06/the-woodland-realm/` ("you can quest with Mendor in order to place double that amount of progress this turn") agrees. Alternative: the cap is fixed once at the commit step.
10. **Lost in the Dark** (`span-settled` by the same line of 50721:A:2, and the review above: "the Doomed 1 and immunity to cancelation only counts for the first treachery each round"): the first treachery revealed each round, here or in a search, gains Doomed 1 (added to a Doomed it already prints) and its When Revealed effects cannot be canceled.
11. **Ambushed at Night** (`literal`): each enemy in the staging area gets -10 engagement cost, so it engages at the engagement checks of the encounter phase; "cannot be optionally engaged" bars only the player's choice to engage it, not the checks. It cannot take damage while it is in the staging area.
12. **"Don't Leave the Path"** (`literal`): the line is the printed text of 142021:B:1 (the same mandatory travel, the engine's `mustTravel`); no progress can be placed on a location in the staging area, whatever places it.
13. **Shuffling after a search, 51107:B:1, 51122:A:2, 51125:A:2, 51141:A:1** (`recommended`; `https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/206167-encounter-deck/` (two players: "you always reshuffle the encounter deck after looking through it for a card"), `https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/253143-shuffle-the-encounter-deck-outside-of-questing-phase` (one player quotes the opposite, second hand), `https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/182893-deck-search/` (no answer); `rr.search` says nothing and no official text found settles it): the encounter deck is shuffled after each of these searches, as after every other search of it in the app (01126:B:1, 142022:A:1, 142040:A:1, 41041:A:1), though these lines print no shuffle. The discard pile is not shuffled into it. The Forest of Great Fear's search of the deck and discard pile finds an enemy of any cost and adds it to the staging area, then the deck is shuffled. Then (rr.then), only if an enemy was found, one resource goes on an enemy in play, in the staging area or engaged, with printed cost 2 or higher, the enemy just found included. Only that enemy has the resource; while this stage is in play it gets +2 Attack and +2 Defense and is immune to player card effects, and the stage cannot be defeated while it is in play. With no such enemy no resource is placed and the stage can be defeated as printed. The stage's own quest points (15) and its win on defeat are as printed.
14. **To the Elvenking's Halls, 3B** (`literal`): X (quest points) is the highest threat level of any player now. "Each player's threat cannot be reduced by more than 1 each round" holds for every reduction, not only by player card effects (the flag `threatReductionCap`, 11217:A:1). The Forced effect reveals the top card in full (rr.reveal) after a successful quest when the progress placed on this stage this phase is 10 or more.
15. **Escape from Taur-nu-Fuin, 3B** (`researched`, `https://visionofthepalantir.com/2023/03/06/the-woodland-realm/`: the Forced effect "will reset the encounter deck if it is empty"; the same wording of emce.guarded-x): if the encounter deck is empty while discarding, the discard pile is shuffled into it and the effect continues; with no location in the deck and the discard pile it stops. The location found goes to the staging area faceup, not revealed. This differs from The Wizard's Quest reading 11 (The Tower of Sorcery), which has no such source.
16. **Enchanted Stream** (51119:A:2, `recommended`, no source): "those heroes" are the heroes exhausted by this Travel effect; they cannot ready while Enchanted Stream is the active location (the refresh step included). While the stream is in the staging area, no progress can be placed on the main quest (as Cracked Pillar, 41006:B:2).
17. **Caught in a Web** (51123:A:1, `recommended`, mirkwood-paths precedent of the same attachment text): it attaches to a hero without a copy and exhausts it; the hero cannot ready unless its controller first spends 2 resources from the hero's pool. A hero that cannot pay stays exhausted.
18. **Ambushing Orcs, 51151:A:2** (`recommended`, no source): "the lowest engagement cost enemy in the staging area" is read on the printed cost with the staging area's current modifiers; a tie goes to the defending player's choice. The enemy engages the defending player and is dealt a shadow card.
19. **Other copied lines** (`literal`): Wasted Provisions (51153) as 18041:A:1; Dark-wood Grove, Mountains of Mirkwood, Dry Watercourse, Wild Wargs, Pack of Wargs, Beast of Taur-nu-Fuin, Old Forest Road, Mirkwood Path, Giant Web and Chieftain Ufthak as their sibling records named in the pack. Competitive and Advanced-build play: not built.
20. **A Travel effect that makes another location active** (`recommended`, no source; Dark-wood Grove's Travel reveals the top card, and Giant Web, 51131:A:1, makes itself the active location): the players cannot travel while a location is active, so the travel to the location does not take place, the location stays in the staging area and the travel opportunity is over. No FAQ or forum entry names the case (the rulebook of reading 1 was read). Alternative: the revealed location returns to the staging area and the travel goes on.
21. **How the stage 2 cap is applied** (`recommended`, a limit of the engine's trigger names; refines reading 9): the Forced line is a record that fires when the staging step begins (after characters are committed) and sets the cap to the end of the round, 10 when Mendor is committed at that moment, else 5. A later removal of Mendor from the quest does not lower it (the alternative of reading 9).
