# Murder at the Prancing Pony

The 2015 print-on-demand scenario (pack `MatPP`, RingsDB's code for it, position 7 of cycle 50), Normal and Easy, the seventh of the cycle. Hall of Beorn files it under the product `2015` beside The Ruins of Belegost, so its product line names `scenarioTitle` too. No Nightmare deck exists for it: `data/raw/nightmare` has no set for it, and Hall of Beorn's Nightmare counts only repeat Normal (https://hallofbeorn.com/LotR/Scenarios/Murder-at-the-Prancing-Pony). Hall of Beorn lists no rulesheet and `data/rules` holds none; the scenario's rules are the three quest cards and the five-page Scenario Rules insert, whose pages 2 to 5 are DragnCards' four Rules rows (read from their pictures, below).

- **Data.** Hall of Beorn numbers its cards `00001` to `00029` (and Legolas, `01005`), which collide with The Massing at Osgiliath's; RingsDB lists none of the pack's cards. `overrides.productCodes` gives them `507` and the collector number (`50701` to `50703` the three quest cards, `50704` to `50708` the five Hideouts, `50709` to `50713` the five Suspects, `50714` Taken by Surprise, `50715` The Prancing Pony, `50716` to `50729` the rest of the encounter deck). Legolas (`01005`, tagged with the box in Hall of Beorn's player cards) stays the Core Set hero. Hall of Beorn's scenario file gives the scenario the number 6, the same as The Ruins of Belegost; the data keeps it. The piles are DragnCards' deck Q99.07 for Normal and its own Easy twin E99.07 for Easy, checked against the quest card's printed Setup (`50701:A:1`): The Prancing Pony is the active location, one Ramshackle Manor is staged, and the five Suspects, the five Hideouts and both Taken by Surprise wait in the set-aside pile (the encounter deck is 36 cards on Normal, 27 on Easy). The Setup is read by three Setup steps (`findSuspectsAndHideouts`, `hideSuspectAndHideout`, `buildInvestigationDeck`) and the active-location and staging sentence (`scripts/data/normalize/setup-standalone-scenarios.ts`); the twelve stay in the set-aside pile for the scenario's record to hide one Suspect and one Hideout and to build the Investigation deck. DragnCards' four "Murder at the Prancing Pony" rows of type Rules are the insert pages, not cards the scenario deals.
- **Easy.** Hall of Beorn's scenario export gives every card an Easy count equal to its Normal count, and its card export gives Easy 0, while its scenario page and DragnCards' E99.07 agree: Easy has 40 cards against Normal's 50 (Dark Alley 3 to 1, Market Square 3 to 2, Greenway Footpad 4 to 2, Chetwood Outlaw 3 to 2, Smoke and Mirrors 3 to 1, Deadly Accusations 2 to 1, Taken by Surprise 2 to 1). `overrides.scenarioEasy` records the seven counts (researched: https://hallofbeorn.com/LotR/Scenarios/Murder-at-the-Prancing-Pony, DragnCards E99.07; not confirmed by a printed source, because the Easy mark is printed on single copies).
- **Title collision.** The Prancing Pony (`50715`) has the title of A Knife in the Dark's `141045`, so a record template cannot find a card by that name. `141025:constant:1` (the Fellowship's "cannot advance while The Prancing Pony is in play") is now an authored record with the same instruction the template wrote.
- **Scenario Rules cards.** Pages 2 to 5 of the insert (DragnCards' pictures, read from the pictures, researched: the card pictures are the source; page 1 is not available online). The Investigation deck: begin by following the Setup on stage 1A; when told to "build the Investigation deck", take the 4 remaining Suspect enemies and the 4 remaining Hideout locations, plus each copy of Taken by Surprise, and shuffle them together without looking at them. The players may not look at the Investigation deck except when resolving the Investigate keyword or when a card effect says so. The Investigation List (page 5 of the rules) names all 5 Suspect enemies (Todd the Troll, Susie the Spider, Wolf-pelt Jake, Johnny Goblin-fingers, Old Orc-eyes) and all 5 Hideout locations (Bill Ferny's House, Staddle Hiding Hole, Combe Storehouse, Archet Trading Post, Chetwood Campsite) with a box before each; players check names off to find the set-aside Suspect and Hideout by elimination. Investigate X: when an active location with the Investigate keyword is explored, the first player shuffles the Investigation deck and looks at the top X cards, then places them back on top in the same order; at stage 2B the Investigate keyword on the active location is resolved before the second Forced effect on that stage. Immune to player card effects: such a card ignores the effects of all player cards and cannot be chosen as a target of player card effects. Making the accusation (stage 2B to 3A): the first player names 1 Suspect and 1 Hideout, then reveals the facedown, out of play Suspect and Hideout and adds them to the staging area; if both are the ones named, the accusation is correct; if not, search the Investigation deck for each just named card that is not already in play, add it to the staging area, and raise each player's threat by 3.
- **Ledger.** The 32 printed sides are read from the scans (`data/printed/standalone-scenarios.json`). Hall of Beorn's text has: "investigation deck" for "Investigation deck" (50702 B), no Oxford comma in "discard pile, and victory display" (50704 A), single quotes for the printed double quotes round the Suspects' Forced text (50706 A) and the Hired Muscle text (50726 A), no comma before "and +1 Defense" (50708 A), "1 other Brigand" for "1 other Brigand enemy" (50710 A), "shadow cad" for "shadow card" (50713 A), "the Prancing Pony" for "The Prancing Pony" (50715 A), "2 additional card" for "2 additional cards" (50719 A), "Bree-land Information" for "Bree-land Informant" (50722 A), "optionally engaged" for "optionally engage" (50725 A), and in 50726 A "attacked" for "attached" and "the bottom of the Investigation deck" for "the bottom card of the Investigation deck". No printed typo is kept as a misprint. The Suspects' keyword line is `Suspect. Brigand.` and the Hideouts' is `Hideout.`.
- **Mechanics.** New to the engine: the Investigation deck (a facedown deck of 8 and Taken by Surprise, looked at and shuffled, never drawn), the Investigate X keyword, the Suspect and Hideout traits' rules (Suspects cannot take damage while a Hideout is in play; each Hideout in the victory display changes the Suspects), the accusation, and travelling to a Hideout. The new rules and verbs are in the Readings below.

  1. 50701:A:1 Setup: the seed picks 1 Suspect and 1 Hideout (two seeded rolls, in code order), which stay out of play facedown in the set-aside pile; the other 4 Suspects, the other 4 Hideouts and each Taken by Surprise form the Investigation deck, a zone of its own beside the quest deck (a named pile, as the Orc deck of The Weather Hills, `mec108.orc-deck`), shuffled by the seed. The hidden pair is never named to the player: no engine step, log line, words line or readout names a facedown card of the set-aside pile or of the Investigation deck, and only a look window names the cards it shows. Literal (the insert; https://visionofthepalantir.com/2020/05/22/murder-at-the-prancing-pony/ agrees).
  2. Looking at the Investigation deck (Investigate, 50715:A:1, 50719:A:2, 50722:A:1, 50726:A:2): a look window shows the cards to the looking seat and changes nothing; the deck is shuffled only when the Investigate keyword resolves, never by another look. "Look at the top 2", "the bottom card" and "2 additional cards" do not shuffle; the cards looked at stay where they are, in the same order. Researched, community discussion only (https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/193711-murder-at-the-prancing-pony: "the only time you shuffle the investigation deck is as a part of resolving the Investigate key word"); also in the insert text (shuffle, then look, then put back in the same order).
  3. Investigate X (50716 to 50720:A:1): a Forced effect of the location when it is explored as the active location, in the Discover pattern: the first player shuffles the Investigation deck and looks at the top X cards; with fewer than X cards left, as many as there are. At stage 2B it resolves before the stage's second Forced effect (the insert's clarification; literal). Researched (the insert via https://rulespal.com/the-lord-of-the-rings-the-card-game-murder-at-the-prancing-pony/rulebook).
  4. "The Investigate value of the active location" (50727:A:1, 50729:A:1, 50729:A:2): the printed Investigate number of the active location plus any change a card gives; 0 with no active location or one without the keyword (The Prancing Pony). 50703:B:1 "Ignore the Investigate keyword when the active location is explored" stops the keyword resolving at the explore (and so the Forced effects that hear it, 50719:A:2, 50720:A:2) and does not change the value those three lines read. Recommended (no source says; the ignore is worded for the explore only; as `discoverValue` of The Ruins of Belegost).
  5. 50719:A:2 Old Warehouse: the Forced effect resolves when the keyword triggers, before the shuffle and the look: the first player chooses to look at 3 cards (1 + 2) or to reduce each player's threat by 3 (not below 0). Recommended (https://visionofthepalantir.com/2020/05/22/murder-at-the-prancing-pony/ names the choice, not the order; "when triggering" puts it first).
  6. 50714:A:1 Taken by Surprise: a Forced effect of the card in the Investigation deck that hears every look at that deck, by any card, and resolves once for each copy among the cards looked at, after the effect that looked has resolved in full (`rr.forced`; the player has seen the cards first). It shuffles the encounter discard pile into the encounter deck, discards until an enemy is discarded and puts that enemy into play engaged with the first player, with no reveal (no When Revealed, no Surge, no engagement check; it is not revealed from the encounter deck, so a cancel of a revealed card does not apply), then removes Taken by Surprise from the game, but only when the enemy was put into play: the removal follows "Then" and resolves only if the part before it resolved in full (`rr.then`). With no enemy in the deck and discard pile, nothing is put into play and the card stays in the Investigation deck. Researched, review only (https://visionofthepalantir.com/2020/05/22/murder-at-the-prancing-pony/: "isn't revealed from the encounter deck", a Forced effect, not a When Revealed); the timing after the look is recommended.
  7. 50702:B:1 "choose 1 random card from the Investigation deck and remove it from the game (without looking at it)": a seeded pick after a failed quest; the card is removed unseen and unnamed (a Taken by Surprise removed this way does not resolve). With an empty deck nothing happens. Literal (https://visionofthepalantir.com/2020/05/22/murder-at-the-prancing-pony/).
  8. 50702:B:2 stage 2B: stage 2 has no quest points, so progress placed on it never advances it; the Forced effect hears the progress a successful quest places on the stage itself (`questProgressPlacedOnStage`; a quest whose progress all goes to locations does not trigger it), and the first player chooses: reveal an encounter card and stay, or make the accusation and advance to 3A. Recommended (no quest points printed; the printed trigger reads as a successful quest that places progress on the stage).
  9. Making the accusation (insert page 4; 50702:B:2): the first player names 1 Suspect and 1 Hideout (any of the five, a card already in play included), then the facedown out-of-play pair is turned faceup and added to the staging area (no reveal: none of the ten has a When Revealed). If both match, nothing more. If either does not, the Investigation deck is searched for each named card that is not in play (the victory display is out of play) and each one found is added to the staging area, and each player's threat is raised by 3 once, not once per wrong name. A named card removed from the game or not in the deck is not found. Researched (https://rulespal.com/the-lord-of-the-rings-the-card-game-murder-at-the-prancing-pony/rulebook: "If either is wrong ... raise each player's threat by 3"; https://visionofthepalantir.com/2020/05/22/murder-at-the-prancing-pony/: "every player should raise their threat by 3"; the once reading is recommended).
  10. 50715:A:1 The Prancing Pony: a Forced effect when it is explored: the first player looks at the top 2 cards (no shuffle, reading 2) or each player draws 2 cards. Literal.
  11. 50720:A:2 Village Stable: a Response after the Investigate keyword on it resolved: the cost is adding Village Stable to the victory display (it is still in play, being explored); the looked-at card (the top card, since X is 1) that is a Suspect or a Hideout moves from the deck to the staging area faceup. Optional, as any Response. Researched, review only (https://visionofthepalantir.com/2020/05/22/murder-at-the-prancing-pony/; https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/193711-murder-at-the-prancing-pony shows players using it).
  12. 50728:A:1 and 50728:A:2 Rising Body Count: "shuffle Rising Body Count into the Investigation deck" is allowed (the Investigation deck does not leave play), from the staging area (when revealed) or from the shadow zone (shadow); the card is a card of the deck from then on and has no effect there. The Hideout's Immune text does not protect it from its own effect (it is not a player card). Researched, community only (https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/227990-murder-at-the-prancing-pony-rising-body-count).
  13. 50726:A:2 Hired Muscle: attach to a Brigand enemy in play (any zone) without a copy of Hired Muscle attached; the enemy returns to the staging area; the card counts as a Condition attachment (the printed parenthesis). A Suspect prints "Cannot have attachments", so it is never a host: with no other Brigand the treachery is discarded. Its granted Forced effect looks at the bottom card of the Investigation deck when the attached enemy is defeated. Literal; the discard with no target is recommended.
  14. 50722:A:1 Bree-land Informant: after it attacks, if the shadow card it was dealt has no shadow effect (`shadowBlank`), the defending player raises their threat by 1, spends 1 resource (from a hero they choose) to look at the bottom card, and the Informant is discarded only if the text before "Then" resolved in full (`rr.then`); a player who cannot spend a resource still raises the threat, gets no look, and the Informant stays in play. Span-settled.
  15. 50713:A:2 Old Orc-eyes: the Forced effect hears an attack that resolved with a shadow card of no shadow effect (`shadowBlank`) dealt to him; the additional attack is against the same player, is dealt no shadow card, and cannot start another additional attack (it was dealt no shadow card). Literal.
  16. 50709:A:2 Todd the Troll: damage beyond the remaining hit points of the character his attack damages (not a ship, not a location) becomes a threat raise for the defending player by the same amount, in place of damage that is lost; the character is still destroyed. Recommended (no source).
  17. 50712:A:2 Johnny Goblin-fingers: when he attacks, before the defender is declared, the defending player discards 1 random card from their hand and raises their threat by its printed cost (an event or card with no printed cost, or an empty hand, raises it by 0). Recommended.
  18. 50711:A:2 Wolf-pelt Jake: after an attack by him that destroys an ally (the ally was destroyed by that attack's damage), he returns to the staging area. Literal.
  19. 50721:A:2 Bill Ferny: the first treachery revealed each round, from the encounter deck by any reveal, gains Doomed 1 (each player's threat raised by 1) and its effects cannot be canceled. A card shuffled in or played from the Investigation deck is not revealed. Recommended.
  20. 50703:B:3: while any Hideout is in play (the staging area or the active location; the victory display is not play) Suspect enemies cannot take damage. The players win the game the moment no Suspect enemy is in play (a Suspect returned to the staging area is still in play), checked while stage 3B is the current quest, so a game with no Suspect in play on reaching 3B cannot happen (the accusation always adds the hidden Suspect). Recommended.
  21. The Hideouts' victory-display lines (50704 to 50708:A:2) are constants that hold while the Hideout is in the victory display (`sourceIn victory`): 50704 makes each Suspect and each shadow card dealt to a Suspect immune to player card effects (the shadow card's effects cannot be canceled by a player card); 50705 stops a player not engaged with a Suspect declaring attackers against it (as the Witch-king's engaged-player rule); 50706 gives each Suspect "Forced: After this enemy engages a player, it makes an immediate attack"; 50707 caps the damage on each Suspect at 5 a round; 50708 gives each Suspect +1 Threat, +1 Attack, +1 Defense. Literal.
  22. The Hideouts' Travel lines: 50704:A:3 searches the victory display too; 50716:A:3 each player raises threat by 1 (all or none can travel); 50718:A:3 each player spends 1 resource from a hero they choose (a player with none cannot, so the players cannot travel); 50719:A:3 reveals the top card of the encounter deck; 50720:A:3 each player discards 1 random card from their hand (a player with none cannot pay, as 50625:A:3 of The Ruins of Belegost). Recommended where a cost cannot be paid (Travel cost, `rr.travel`).
  23. The Investigation List (insert page 5): a shared aide, one list for all players (https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/193711-murder-at-the-prancing-pony); the app marks a Suspect or Hideout once a look window has shown it, or it is in play, in the victory display or in the discard pile, and never marks a card the players have not seen (the engine sets `seen` on each card a look window shows; a card removed unseen stays unchecked). The dial "List" counts the checked names out of 10 (`docs/design-system-table.md`, the Investigation List entry); the Investigation deck is a pile of the second slot showing the back and the count only (`docs/design-system-table.md`, the Investigation deck entry). Recommended (the Table step; no engine rule).
