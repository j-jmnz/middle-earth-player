# The Oath

Mirkwood Paths part 4 and The Dark of Mirkwood part 1, campaign card 41050. The Oath is dealt through the Campaign Setup Checklist with no saga setup, in two campaigns that share its scenario file: in Mirkwood Paths it continues the log Escape from Dol Guldur wrote (`tdom.mirkwood-paths`: the pool on its heroes, each Mendor's Support in its owner's deck, the burdens in the encounter deck, all through the usual steps), and a new `the-dark-of-mirkwood` playthrough starts it on a blank log (`tdom.mini-campaign`), taking in a finished campaign's carry-over (Mendor, Mendor's Support) when the player chooses. A standalone game has no campaign card, no Adaptation and no Stalking Goblin. Every printed line of every dealt card has a record (`src/effects/authored/the-oath.ts`; the Adaptations, Stalking Goblin and Guilty Conscience in `src/effects/authored/pool/TDoM.ts`; the Adaptations' "Setup. Attach to a hero." is the generated setup template, which reads the period as well as the colon).

- Campaign card, Setup (41050:A:2, Campaign Mode only): each seat in turn chooses 1 Adaptation boon (41044 The Fervor of Lore, 41045 The Might of Tactics, 41046 The Power of Spirit, 41047 The Secret of Leadership; the box holds one of each, so a seat cannot take another's) and a hero of its own to carry it (`ChooseCards`, asked on the briefing like any campaign choice). An Adaptation is not Permanent, so the log does not record its hero: the pool keeps it as the seat's player-backed card, and a later scenario's checklist shuffles it into that seat's deck, where its Setup line finds it and attaches it to a hero. Stalking Goblin (41041) is shuffled into the encounter deck now and added to the pool (`AddToPool`), so steps 7 and 8 do not deal it again this scenario; later scenarios shuffle it into the encounter deck from the pool. A lost scenario writes none of this.
- The Adaptations: the attached hero gains the Lore, Tactics, Spirit or Leadership resource icon (41044:A:2 to 41047:A:2). Each Action is "Limit once per campaign. Record in Campaign Log when used." (the limit `oncePerCampaign`): using it adds the card's code to the game's copy of the log's `usedOnce` fact, a won scenario writes that back, and a scenario that starts with the code in `usedOnce` does not offer the Action (a lost one writes nothing, so its uses do not stay). 41044: discard 3 cards from your hand (you choose them) to heal all damage from a hero; 41045: deal 1 damage to the attached hero to deal 3 damage to an enemy engaged with you; 41046: exhaust the attached hero to place 3 progress on a location; 41047: spend 1 resource from the attached hero's pool to ready a hero. An Action is offered only when its cost and its target can be had, and a used Adaptation's preview says "its Action is used for this campaign". A standalone game cannot hold an Adaptation.
- Campaign card, Response (41050:A:3): the ledger reads "Response: At the end of each round, place 1 resource token here" (the scan; Hall of Beorn's "Forced" is a transcription difference and RingsDB has no row for the card: confirmed against the scan). So it is optional: after the Forced effects of the end of the round, the first player is offered it, once a round, and may pass (`PlaceToken`; the tokens are the card's own, not a hero's pool). This is a round-end Response window (`endOfRound` heard by Responses); no other card hears it. The top bar shows the tokens ("Tokens", `campaign-tokens`) while the campaign card is in the game and has such a record. Resolution (41050:B:1, a won scenario in Campaign Mode only): with 6 or more tokens (the `tokens` condition reads the card's tokens) the Guilty Conscience burden is earned. The card prints no "record the tokens" line, so the log records no count (the log's `counters` field is unused by this card). The end-of-round Response sheet is headed "End of round" and its note says what the tokens lead to ("6 or more tokens at the end earn Guilty Conscience.", read from the record). A won game whose Resolution asks nothing (this one) docks "See the Resolution", not "Choose rewards"; the Resolution sentence also names the Adaptations it records as used this campaign.
- Guilty Conscience (41043, Surge, burden treachery): When Revealed it is attached to the hero with the highest printed threat cost among the heroes of every player (the first player chooses among those tied: `AttachSelf` `highestThreatCost`); it counts as an Adaptation attachment. Forced: at the end of each round its hero's controller raises threat by 2. Action: the hero's controller spends 2 resources from the hero's pool to discard it (an encounter card attached to a hero is used by that hero's controller).
- Stalking Goblin (41041:A:1): after it engages a player the encounter deck, then the discard pile, then "your collection" are searched for a Goblin Sniper (by title: Anduin's 01083 counts too) and it is added to the staging area, and the encounter deck is shuffled. The app tracks no collection, so the collection holds every card the game knows: a copy is made when neither the deck nor the pile has one (`SearchCollection`). As a Shadow card it is added to the staging area. Its stats are the scan's, not Hall of Beorn's: Threat 2 and engagement cost 10, Attack 2, Defense 2, 2 hit points (an override in `data/overrides.json`, which may set a card's `threat` and `engagementCost`: Hall of Beorn files the burden enemy's Threat icon as Willpower and has no engagement cost, so the engagement check could not engage it). Ungoliant's Swarm (01136) has the same gap and its own override (scan: Threat 3, engagement cost 33, Attack 2, Defense 2, 5 hit points).
- Quest. Stage 1 (41001:A:1) puts The Eaves of Mirkwood in the staging area and sets Goblin Troop aside (the scenario's piles deal it; the record describes it). Stage 2B (41002:B:1, B:2): each player finds a Forest location; the stage cannot be defeated unless a Goblin Trail is in the victory display. Stage 3 (41003:A:1, B:1): the first player adds the set-aside Goblin Troop and each other player (the condition `firstPlayer`, negated) finds a Goblin enemy; the stage cannot be defeated while Goblin Troop is in play, and defeating it wins the game. Goblin Trail's Response (41014:A:2, "place 6 progress on the current quest") puts the progress on the stage that is current when it resolves. Advancing is immediate and the new stage's When Revealed is part of it (rr.quest-advancement, rr.triggering-condition), so when the progress that explores the Trail also fills stage 2, stage 3's When Revealed (Goblin Troop into play) resolves before the Trail's Response and the 6 progress cannot clear stage 3 while Goblin Troop is set aside. The engine does this for an explored location whose Response puts progress on the quest (a record with `ProgressQuest` on `leftPlay`); every other explored location keeps offering its Responses before the stages it advanced to reveal, as Fellowship, Passage and Anduin games replay them.
- Locations and enemies: Forest Gate's Response (41012:A:1, The Dark of Mirkwood) reads "the first player may draw 2 cards" on the scan, which Hall of Beorn and DragnCards drop to "draws" on that printing only (the Core and Revised Core printings read "may draw" on both); Tangled Grove's Threat is the number of locations in the staging area (41011:A:1) and each player discards a card at random to travel there (the players cannot travel there while one has no card); Spider Den's Quest Action (41013:A:1) is any player's: search the deck and discard pile for a Spider enemy, it engages that player (`SearchMove` `engaged`: it enters the player's play area, so Forced effects that hear an engagement hear it), and Spider Den is discarded; Goblin Trail's Travel reveals a card (as Mountains of Mirkwood); Abandoned Camp's Travel discards the top encounter card and adds it to the staging area if it is an enemy, and its Response discards the first player's top card and puts it into play if it is an ally; The Eaves of Mirkwood, while the active location, keeps any Response that cancels a When Revealed or Shadow effect (`CancelEffect`) and any paid cancel from being offered (the table flag `encounterUncancelable`); the window stays open for a Response that does not cancel (Round Shield's Defense, 19118:response:1); Goblin Sniper, Caught in a Web, Driven by Shadow and Forest Gate read as their Anduin and Passage twins do. Goblins are Upon You! cannot be canceled while it resolves.
- Other encounter lines: Goblintown Scavengers discards the top card of each player's deck and its Threat rises by each printed cost for the phase (an empty deck adds nothing); Surprising Speed returns an engaged enemy of each player and, if none was returned, gains surge and doomed 1; Eyes in the Dark has each player choose between raising threat by 1 for each questing character and discarding one.
- Table (new pieces, each created by a line above): the campaign card's tokens on the top bar (41050:A:3); an Adaptation whose Action is used says so in its preview (41044:A:3); the Campaign Log shows "Used this campaign" (the cards whose once-per-campaign Action was used) once a scenario is won, and the path's Pool line and the Log's Boons row mark a spent Adaptation "· used" (41044:A:3). The briefing's Adaptation choice has a muted sentence, each option's Action line from the ledger, and a Deal bar that says "Choose an Adaptation for seat N above." until it is answered. No control types damage, resources, threat, progress or life.
- Easy removals come from the Easy Mode sheet and the printed marks (`data/scenarios/the-oath.json`).
