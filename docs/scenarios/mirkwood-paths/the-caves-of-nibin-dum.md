# The Caves of Nibin-Dûm

Mirkwood Paths part 5 and The Dark of Mirkwood part 2, campaign card 41051: the last part of both campaigns, so a win completes each. `tdom.setup-the-caves-of-nibin-dum`. It is dealt through the Campaign Setup Checklist with no saga setup, on the log The Oath wrote (the Adaptations in the seats' decks, Stalking Goblin and Guilty Conscience in the encounter deck, the once-per-campaign uses in the game's facts) or, in The Dark of Mirkwood, on the log holding only The Oath. A standalone game has no campaign card, no "No Time to Waste!", no Shiny Distraction and no Ranger Sense, but the first quest card's own Setup still deals the Cave Torch. Every printed line of every dealt card has a record (`src/effects/authored/the-caves-of-nibin-dum.ts`; 41042, 41048 and 41049 in `src/effects/authored/pool/TDoM.ts`; the Goblins cards 41019 to 41026 are The Oath's). Its vocabulary is the `caves` family (`src/catalog/families/caves.ts`, `src/rules/families/caves/`).

- Campaign card, Setup (41051:A:2, Campaign Mode only): each player adds a "No Time to Waste!" to his hand once his starting hand and mulligan are done (`AddToHandAfterMulligan`), each copy joins the Campaign Pool, and Shiny Distraction is shuffled into the encounter deck and joins the pool. The scan's first line, "You are playing Campaign Mode", is inert.
- Cave Torch (41029:A:1): the Setup's attach follows `rr.restricted` like any attach. A hero that already holds 2 Restricted attachments discards one of the three at once, the choice of the hero's controller (`docs/brief.md`, Keywords implemented once).
- Campaign card, Resolution (41051:B:1): the final threat of the player with the highest threat (the condition `highestThreat`: every seat of the game, an eliminated player's threat raised to 50 by rr.scoring, so it never earns). Under 40, each player earns a Ranger Sense now (it joins the pool and is shuffled into his deck by the next checklist) and a Ranger Sense for each player is carried over to the next campaign (`CarryOver`, `eachPlayer`); at 40 or more nothing is earned or kept. A lost scenario writes nothing. The Resolution sentence on the confirm screen says the win completes the campaign when the scenario is the last of its campaign, and names the Ranger Sense kept (`settledSentence`).
- Quest. Stage 1 (41004:A:1): Goblin Chieftain and Cracked Pillar are set aside by the scenario's piles; the Cave Torch comes out of the encounter deck (the `attachedChosen` pile) and the first player attaches it to one of his heroes, dealt by the Setup record (`AttachSetAside`, run at the start of the game with the stage's When Revealed). Stage 1B: each player finds a different location. Stage 2 (41005): Goblin Chieftain joins the staging area and each player finds an enemy in the deck or discard pile; in the encounter phase every enemy's engagement cost is 0 (the table flag `engagementCostZero`, read by `modifiedStat`); the stage's quest points are 4 for each enemy in play. It prints 0 quest points, so **a stage that arrives with 0 quest points is not met on arrival**: its When Revealed has not resolved yet, and the check comes with the next progress (`advanceStage`; the two other stages that print 0, 01121 and 01128, are held back by their own Restrict). Stage 3 (41006): Goblin Chieftain is set aside again (`SetAsideInPlay`) and Cracked Pillar joins the staging area; one more card is revealed each quest phase; no progress goes on the Pillar in the staging area (`noProgressPlaced`); the stage prints no quest points and advances when the Pillar leaves play explored. Stage 4 (41007): Goblin Chieftain returns; with 8 progress on the stage it loses "Cannot take damage" (`losesNoDamage`); the stage cannot be defeated while it is in play, and defeating it wins.
- Goblin Chieftain (41027): Threat, Attack and Defense are the stage number (the amount `stage`: the card's place in the quest deck, 1 to 4); it cannot take damage or have attachments; after it attacks the first player chooses to remove X progress from the quest or return it to the staging area. Policy note for the golden: it only stays engaged to be killed when the stage keeps 8 progress after X come off.
- Cracked Pillar (41028): in the staging area the first player may attack it in the combat phase as if it were engaged with him, its Threat as its Defense (`attackableAsEngaged`, read by `canAttack`); it has no hit points, so damage only piles up on it; 4 damage are removed to travel there (the travel cost check reads it). The players' attack sheet says "enemy or location" when it is among the options.
- Cave Torch (41029): Action: exhaust it to place 1 to 3 progress on a Dark location ("up to 3": placing none is no use, so the choice starts at 1); Forced: after it exhausts, the top encounter card is discarded and an enemy goes to the staging area. Its exhaust is the Action's first instruction, not a paid cost; the Forced effect hears it either way, since every exhaustion (an exhaust cost, a commit to the quest, an attack, a defense) is announced to "After ~ exhausts" (`src/rules/windows/exhaust.ts`, rr.exhausted). Cavern Warg's Response, Lightless Passage's Travel cost and Lost in the Dark exhaust it the same way; Lost in the Dark is the choice of the player who controls the Torch (nobody chooses when nobody does).
- Encounter cards: Great Cave-troll's excess combat damage removes progress from the quest (`excessToQuestProgress`); Cavern Warg's Shadow returns it to the staging area after an attack that destroyed a character; Goblin Tunnels (in the staging area) takes a progress token off the quest when a Goblin is revealed from the deck; Lightless Passage's Shadow cancels the combat damage dealt to the attacker (`combatDamageCanceled`); Branching Paths adds 1 Threat to each Dark location in the staging area, and when explored the players choose 1 of the top 3 encounter cards to reveal, the others going to the bottom (`BottomOtherLooked`); Collapsed Mine limits progress on the quest to 4 each round (`questProgressCap`, the smallest cap held) and its Shadow raises the defender's threat by the damage the attack dealt (the damage is kept on the attack only while a Shadow effect waits to read it); Goblin Dungeon's explored Response puts an ally from the top 5 cards of the first player's deck into play; Crumbling Stairs takes no progress in the staging area and, explored, discards the top encounter card and puts a location into play as the active location; Crumbling Ruin has each player exhaust a character and discard a card, and discard the character when the card's printed cost reaches its remaining hit points; Lost in the Dark may stop progress on the quest for the phase (`noQuestProgress`, a lasting modifier the table holds: `ModifyStat` with `target: "table"`; the active location still takes it); Watchful Eyes counts as a Condition attachment and reveals a card at the end of the combat phase when its hero is exhausted.
- A finished campaign's path keeps its action bar: it says the campaign is won ("Ered Mithrin won. The players win the campaign!": the name and the campaign card's printed Resolution line, the name alone when the card prints none) with a Start a new campaign button that opens the Campaigns tab scrolled to New playthrough (`start=1`), where a campaign's setup screen offers the carry-over, and when the log holds a carry-over it says what waits for the next campaign ("A Ranger Sense for each player waits for your next campaign."). The campaign list, the history card and the save line call the finished playthrough "Campaign won". The Resolution screen of the last scenario shows the printed line under the title in heading type with "Ered Mithrin is complete: 9 of 9 scenarios won." under it, and labels Confirm "Finish the campaign". The Resolution sentence names the campaign it completes ("...and completes The Dark of Mirkwood.").
- Known readings and gaps. Lightless Passage's Shadow (41033:A:2) is recorded with the literal duration `endOfAttack` (rr.lasting-effects: a Shadow effect lasts until the end of the attack): an enemy attack ends before any character attacks that enemy in the combat phase, so the flag has expired when the attacker could take combat damage, the Shadow does nothing in practice, and only a unit test (`the-caves-of-nibin-dum/torch.test.ts`) shows the flag. Collapsed Mine's cap (41035:A:1) counts the progress that reaches the quest card: the card says "placed on the quest", and rr.active-location says progress that would be placed on the quest is instead placed on the active location, so the active location is outside the cap; this is the engine's reading of those two spans, not a ruling. The round's progress is noted from its start in a game whose records read a cap (`trackQuestPlaced`, set by `syncConstants`; `noteQuestPlaced` in `src/rules/families/caves/progress.ts`), so progress placed on the quest before Collapsed Mine entered the staging area counts against it.
- Shiny Distraction (41042, Surge, Doomed 1): each player may add 1 resource to each of his heroes' pools and then raises his threat by 5.
- "No Time to Waste!" (41048) and Ranger Sense (41049): "When playing this card, choose one" is the existing choice among an event's Actions on different lines; the first Action of each removes the card from the game and from the Campaign Pool (`RemoveSourceFromGame`, `RemoveFromCampaignPool`: the log drops that seat's own entry when the scenario is won), the second draws a card. "No Time to Waste!" is a Quest Action (threat down by 3 for each player, an encounter card revealed).
- Table (new pieces, each created by a line above): none that needs a widget. The Pillar among the attack choices (41028:A:1) and the words of its choices (`src/play/words/scenarios/caves.ts`) are its only pieces under `src/play`; the quest card's title reads the quest points the engine reads (41005:B:2).
- Easy removals come from the Easy Mode sheet and the printed marks (`data/scenarios/the-caves-of-nibin-dum.json`).
