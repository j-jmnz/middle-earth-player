# Escape from Dol Guldur

Campaign card 01131. It is the third part of Mirkwood Paths, dealt through the Campaign Setup Checklist with no saga setup: Journey Along the Anduin's pool reaches it (each Valor and Scarred on the hero that carries it, attached again at step 5) and so does its `prisoner` fact. The campaign card's Setup puts Mendor into play (`PutObjectiveAllyIntoPlay`) and the first player chooses the hero that gets Appointed by Fate (01134, `AttachChosen`, asked on the briefing like any campaign choice; the card is not added to the pool). A standalone game has no campaign card, no Mendor and no Appointed by Fate, and its prisoner is chosen at random. Every printed line of every dealt card has a record (`src/effects/authored/escape-from-dol-guldur.ts`; Appointed by Fate and the pool Mendor in `src/effects/authored/pool/RevCore.ts`). Its families are `objectives`, `prisoner` and `dungeon` (`src/catalog/families`, `src/rules/families`).

- Guarded (`rr.guarded`, `ltp.guarded`; 01108 to 01110:A:1): an objective with the Guarded keyword gets the next card of the encounter deck attached when it is revealed (`GuardObjective`, a When Revealed record on the objective) and in Setup (01123:A:1: after the encounter deck is shuffled, each of the 3 objectives in the staging area gets one, run beside the quest card's other Setup). The next card that is not itself an objective guards. When the next card is an objective that also has the Guarded keyword, the card after it guards the first objective, and then the second objective is revealed and its own Guarded keyword takes the card after that (`rr.guarded`: "use the next card of the encounter deck to fulfill the original keyword effect, and then resolve the guarded keyword on the second card"). An objective without the keyword stays on top of the deck. A revealed enemy or location is placed in the staging area attached to the objective (`CardInstance.guard`): it counts its Threat there, is engaged, travelled to and attacked like any other, and is unlinked from the objective when it leaves play (defeated, explored, discarded). A treachery resolves as it is revealed and the objective gets no attachment (`ltp.guarded`; `rr.guarded` says the same in other words: dealt with when its effects resolve). The Setup also turns the Nazgûl faceup, out of play (`TurnFaceUp`, 01123:A:1).
- Claiming (01108 to 01110:A:2): an Action any player may use on an objective in the staging area that is free of encounters (no card attached; the condition `freeOfEncounters`). Its cost raises the acting player's threat by 2; they choose a hero they control and `ClaimObjective` attaches the objective to it (zone `attached`, controlled by that player, `claimed`; "Counts as an attachment" is a `CountsAs` record). Each is Restricted (`rr.restricted`): a hero holds two, so a third claim on one hero has its controller choose which Restricted attachment returns. These three print "If detached, return ... to the staging area" (the flag `returnsToStagingArea`), so a detached one returns to the staging area, controlled by nobody: its hero leaves play, a card discards it (Cavern Guardian's Shadow: "Discarded objective cards are returned to the staging area", 01103:A:2) or a Restricted slot is over. A claimed objective that prints no such return (Athelas 02063, the Clues of Rhudaur 10110, 10112, 10114) is an attachment: it is discarded with its hero (`rr.attachment-cards`) to the encounter discard pile, as the Gate Key (12071:A:1) is the only other card that prints its return (the semi-official FAQ 1.23, "Any objective card that attaches to another card is treated as an attachment in addition to its other card types", and 1.17, which returns only a guarded objective to the staging area, https://alongextendedparty.com/semi-official-errata-faq-1-10/; community, agent recommendation). `claimed` is announced about the objective (01131:B:1).
- The claimed objectives' own lines: Gandalf's Map (01108:A:3) stops its hero attacking and defending; Dungeon Torch (01109:A:3) raises the attached hero's controller's threat by 2 at the end of each round (`RaiseThreat` with `who: hostController`); Shadow Key (01110:A:3) puts 1 damage on its hero at the end of each round. An objective in the staging area does neither.
- Dungeon Jailor (01101:A:1): after the players quest unsuccessfully it shuffles 1 unclaimed objective of the first player's choice from the staging area into the encounter deck (its guard is discarded with it: an attached card leaves play with its host). The failed quest is announced once for each seat, so the record is limited to once a round: the Forced effect is one effect, whatever the number of players.
- Prisoner (`prisoner` family; 01123:B:1, 01124:B:1, 01131:A:3 and B:1):
  - The stage's When Revealed turns 1 hero chosen at random among all the heroes the players control (the game's seed) facedown (`TakePrisoner`). It and each card attached to it go to the captive zone the Ring-bearer under the Watcher uses (`south/captive.ts`), flagged `prisoner`: still in play, controlled by nobody, blank (`textBlank`), out of its player's heroes (so it is not picked, not damaged, collects no resources and no player attacks, defends or quests with it). The table draws it facedown in its player's play area, with Mendor under it.
  - Campaign Mode: the campaign card's Forced effect (01131:A:3) answers `prisonerTaken`: the hero the Campaign Log names (`prisoner` fact, Journey Along the Anduin's Resolution) is the prisoner instead when the players control it (`SelectLoggedPrisoner`: the random one comes back as it was; with no such hero the random one stays), the cards attached to it are turned facedown only in a campaign game ("Each card attached to that hero is turned facedown" is the campaign card's line 01131:A:3; the stage's 01123:B:1 turns the hero alone, so a standalone game keeps them faceup), Mendor is captured with it (`CaptureWithPrisoner`: facedown under the prisoner, still in play, blank) and the card flips to side B.
  - Its Response (01131:B:1) after the first objective is claimed (the condition: it is the only claimed objective) frees Mendor faceup with 1 damage under the first player (`FreeCaptive`, once a game).
  - The rescue is stage 2B's Response after any amount of progress is placed on it (`progressPlaced`, 01124:B:1): `RescuePrisoner` turns the hero faceup with 1 damage on it among its player's heroes, its attached cards faceup in that player's play area, and announces `prisonerRescued`. The Nazgûl of Dol Guldur, set aside faceup, hears it from out of play (the only trigger a set-aside card hears; `rr.in-play-and-out-of-play`) and moves into the staging area (01102:A:2).
- Stage rules: 1B (01123:B:2 and 3) and 2B (01124:B:2 and 3): the players, as a group, play at most 1 ally card each round (`LimitPlays`: the cards the reducer recorded as played this round, whichever seat played them; allies put into play are not played), and the stage cannot advance without a claimed objective (1B) or while a hero is the prisoner or with fewer than all 3 objectives claimed (2B; the condition `prisoner`). Stage 3B (01125:B:1 and 2): at the beginning of each quest phase each player's top deck card becomes an Orc Guard; it cannot be defeated while Nazgûl of Dol Guldur is in play, and the last stage's defeat is the win.
- Orc Guard (01107:A:1 after travelling to Tower Gate, 01125:B:1): `PlaceOrcGuard` takes the top card of a player's deck out of the deck, facedown, held under an enemy stand-in the engine makes (`orc-guard`, printed.ts: Attack 1, Defense 1, 1 hit point, no Threat, engaged with that player as if it had just engaged them). It attacks, is dealt shadow cards, counts as an engaged enemy, and when it leaves play (defeated or otherwise) the card under it goes faceup to its owner's discard pile and the stand-in leaves the game. The table names it "Orc Guard"; no card art is needed. A Forced effect "After travelling to ~" resolves before the travel's Responses (`rr.forced`): every real travel announces `traveled` to Forced effects as well.
- Iron Shackles (01105:A:1): attached to the top of the first player's deck, in play, controlled by nobody (`AttachToDeck`, `Seat.deckAttached`; the table draws it under the deck). The next time a player would draw 1 or more cards from that deck the draw does not happen and the card is discarded to the encounter discard pile instead: one draw action (Draw of any number of cards, the resource phase, DrawEach) replaced as a whole, one Shackles per draw action (a draw that does not happen, an eliminated player's or one a card forbids, discards nothing; cards drawn for a destroyed enemy are one draw action too: one check, in `drawMany`). Its Shadow effect resolves the When Revealed effect of the card. "Counts as a Condition attachment" is a record for the ledger line; nothing reads it for a card attached to a deck.
- Nazgûl of Dol Guldur (01102): no attachment can be played on it (`cannotHaveAttachments`); after a shadow effect dealt to it resolves (announced as `shadowEffectResolved` once the attack's shadow cards have resolved, before damage; a shadow card with no effect is not announced) the engaged player chooses and discards 1 character he controls (a destroy, so a seat with no hero left is eliminated). Under the Shadow (01104:A:1): until the end of the phase the staging area's total Threat is raised by the number of players (`RaiseStagingThreat`, the table's `moreStagingThreat`; `lessStagingThreat` still subtracts); its Shadow raises the defending player's threat by the number of enemies engaged with them, Orc Guards included.
- Resolution (01131:B:2): when Mendor is still in play (the condition `inPlay`; captive or not), Mendor and a Mendor's Support for each player are written to the log's `carryOver` (`CarryOver`); when he is not, nothing is, and a lost scenario writes nothing. A Mendor in the Campaign Pool is put into play at the start of each scenario by his own record (`01135:campaignSetup:1`, which takes the ledger line 01135:A:2, the only line of his card that speaks of control), so checklist step 8 does not shuffle him in. The Resolution screen's last sentence names the cards kept: "Mendor and a Mendor's Support for each player are kept for your next campaign."
- Table (new pieces, each created by a line above): the encounter card guarding an objective lies over it in the staging area (01123:A:1), and the objective's preview says "Guarded by ..." or "Free of encounters: you can claim it"; the facedown prisoner with Mendor under it is in its player's play area (01123:B:1, 01131:A:3) with a small hero mark on its back, its owner's dial says "Aragorn is the prisoner, face down", a tap opens its preview and the reader table names the hero; Iron Shackles peeks out above the deck, which wears a chain and opens the card on a tap (01105:A:1); the Orc Guard stand-in's preview gives its printed stats and says the facedown deck card under it returns to its owner's discard pile (01107:A:1); a second ally refused by the group limit names the card that limits ("The Necromancer's Tower: the players play at most 1 ally card each round, and one was played this round."); a third Restricted attachment asks "Aragorn can hold 2 Restricted attachments. Choose 1 to remove: an objective returns to the staging area, any other card is discarded."; the Campaign Log shows the prisoner only until this scenario is completed, and one phrase ("Mendor and a Mendor's Support for each player") names the carry-over on the Resolution, the Log and the campaign setup screen. No control types damage, resources, threat, progress or life.
- Easy removals come from the Easy Mode sheet and the printed marks (`data/scenarios/escape-from-dol-guldur.json`).
