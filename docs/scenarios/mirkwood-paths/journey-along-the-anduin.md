# Journey Along the Anduin

Campaign card 01130. It is the second part of Mirkwood Paths, dealt through the Campaign Setup Checklist with no saga setup: Passage Through Mirkwood's earned pool reaches it (each player's Mendor's Support is shuffled into their deck, the burden into the encounter deck). A standalone game has no campaign card, no Mendor, no Valor and no Scarred. Every printed line of every dealt card has a record (`src/effects/authored/journey-along-the-anduin.ts`, Valor and Scarred in `src/effects/authored/pool/RevCore.ts`); no widget, zone or counter is added to the table.

- Setup (01126:A:1, "Each player reveals 1 card from the top of the encounter deck, and adds it to the staging area"): the first quest card's Setup record runs after the deck is shuffled, in a frame of its own, one reveal for each player in turn (`mec109.setup`, `rr.reveal`; a card with Surge reveals one more). The stage then turns to side B and its When Revealed resolves (01126:B:1): the stage searches the encounter deck and discard pile (the engine's search) for 1 Hill Troll, unless one is in play, adds it to the staging area and shuffles. Stage 1B cannot be defeated while any Hill Troll is in play (01126:B:2). The start of the game runs the first stage's When Revealed for every scenario.
- Stage 2B (01127:B:1): the staging step reveals 1 additional card (the table holds `revealMore` while the stage is current) and no engagement checks are made in the encounter phase (the table holds `noEncounterEngagementChecks`, a bar limited to the encounter phase, where Daybreak's and the Safe locations' `noEngagementChecks` holds in every phase; an enemy's Ambush check in the quest phase is still made, but one in the encounter phase is not, as the stage says "during the encounter phase" and Ambush is an engagement check, `dwd.ambush`; `recommended`: no official or forum source rules on it; read https://hallofbeorn.com/LotR?EncounterSet=Journey+Down+the+Anduin, https://lotrlcgblog.wordpress.com/2018/04/23/journey-along-the-anduin-quest/, https://visionofthepalantir.com/2020/11/29/journey-along-the-anduin-2/); each player may still optionally engage 1 enemy. Stage 3B (01128:B:1 to 3): 2 encounter cards for each player are revealed into the staging area when it is revealed; the staging step of the quest phase is skipped (`skipStaging`, held while the stage is current, "for the remainder of the game"); the players win once no enemy is in play (a constant `WinGame`, read when nothing is resolving, so the reveals arrive first). Stage 3B prints 0 quest points and is won no other way, so progress cannot advance it (a `Restrict advance`, as 01121:B:2 in Passage Through Mirkwood).
- Hill Troll (01082:A:1): the combat damage its attack deals beyond the remaining hit points of the character it damages (damage dealt beyond `hit points - damage already there`, after Toughness) raises its controller's threat by that much; the damage itself still goes on the character (`excessToThreat`, `src/rules/families/defense/excess.ts`; every place an enemy attack places damage on a character goes through it; only damage that was placed counts, so a character that takes no damage or takes life instead has no excess). Damage a card later cancels does not call the threat back.
- Wolf Rider (01081:A:2, Shadow, ledger: "Shadow: Wolf Rider attacks the defending player. That player may declare 1 character as a defender. Deal Wolf Rider its own Shadow card. After combat, return Wolf Rider to the top of the encounter deck."): the shadow card attacks the defending player. One attack is in progress at a time, so it attacks as soon as the attack it was dealt to has ended, not inside the shadow step (an engine decision: no rules span orders an attack inside a shadow step): it comes into play engaged with the defending player, is dealt a shadow card of its own (`rr.attacks-outside-the-combat-phase`, `faq.enemy-attacks-outside-combat`), and the player may declare a defender. "After combat" is read plainly: Wolf Rider stays engaged until the end of the combat phase (a Delay to it, `rr.step-6-11`), and then, if it is still in play, returns to the top of the encounter deck; a Wolf Rider the players defeat first goes where a defeated enemy goes, and its shadow card is discarded with it (`ltp.shadow-discard`). It counts as having attacked this phase (it makes no second attack) and, as an attack an effect made, uses up no attack of its own. Two Wolf Riders dealt to one attack (Massing at Night) attack one after the other, each after the "after" instructions of the one before. Its defense sheet says "Wolf Rider's shadow effect: it attacks you now, and goes back to the top of the encounter deck after combat."
- Wargs (01085:A:1): when they attacked and were dealt a shadow card with no shadow effect, they return to the staging area after the attack (the condition `shadowBlank`; a card dealt none is not blank). Goblin Sniper (01083:A:1): while it is in the staging area with another enemy there, in the encounter phase, it cannot be optionally engaged; at the end of the combat phase in the staging area, each player deals 1 damage to 1 character he controls (01083:A:2). Marsh Adder (01084:A:1) raises the attacked player's threat by 1 each time it attacks. Despair (01086) removes 4 progress from the quest, or all it has; its Shadow sets the defending character's Defense to 0 for the attack. Misty Mountain Goblins (01111) remove 1 progress after attacking, and 1 (3 when undefended) as a Shadow. Massing at Night (01112): each player reveals 1 additional card (X additional cards, X the number of players); its Shadow deals the attacker 1 shadow card for each player. Eastern Crows (01115): shuffled back into the encounter deck when defeated; Pursued by Shadow (01117) raises each player's threat by 1 for each character he controls that is not committed; its Shadow returns an exhausted ally (a player's choice: Mendor is one) to its owner's hand, or raises the defender's threat by 3 with none. Evil Storm (01116) and Treacherous Fog (01118) read the player's threat from 35 up.
- Locations: Banks of the Anduin (01113:A:1) goes to the top of the encounter deck, not the discard pile, when it leaves play (explored too). The Brown Lands (01087:A:1) takes 1 progress when it becomes the active location by traveling (the engine's `becameActive`, which an effect that makes a location active also fires). The East Bight (01088:A:1): while there is no active location the players must travel to it, may not decline and may not travel elsewhere, if they can pay its (empty) cost (`Restrict travelOnly`). Gladden Fields (01114:A:1): each player raises threat by 1 more at the end of the refresh phase while it is the active location. Enchanted Stream (01095) is Passage Through Mirkwood's record.
- Campaign card 01130, Campaign Mode only. Setup: Mendor is put into play under the first player (as 01129), and a copy of Valor (01133) and of Scarred (01138) for each player is set aside (`SetAsideEachPlayer`; "each copy" is read as one for each player, since each trigger gives each player one). Response (01130:A:3): after a Hill Troll is defeated, each player attaches 1 set-aside Valor to a hero he controls, if able (the first player's Response, optional like any; a hero that already carries a Valor is not offered: Limit 1 per hero). Forced (01130:A:4): after a character is destroyed by a Hill Troll's attack (the combat damage of its attack; announced as the attack ends, so a lethal wound a Response could still cancel is settled first), each player attaches 1 set-aside Scarred to a hero he controls, once per game (a Forced effect with a limit spends it when it fires, `oncePerGame`). If Mendor leaves play the players lose the game. Valor (01133:A:2): Permanent; after its hero is declared as an attacker, the player may exhaust Valor to heal 1 damage from the hero and deal 1 damage to the defending enemy. Scarred (01138:A:2): Permanent; after a character you control is destroyed, you raise your threat by 1.
- Resolution (01130:B:1, a won scenario only): every Valor and Scarred attached to a hero in play is added to the Campaign Pool on that hero (one entry for each copy: two heroes of one player each keep theirs), and the next scenario's checklist attaches each to its hero again (step 5; a fallen hero's copies leave the pool). The hero in play with the most damage is taken prisoner, a tie settled at random with the scenario's seed (the pick is part of the replayed game), and the log's `prisoner` fact holds it: the Campaign Log screen shows a Prisoner row, and the Resolution screen's last sentence names the prisoner ("Confirming records Valor on Aragorn in the campaign and unlocks Escape from Dol Guldur. Aragorn, the hero with the most damage, is taken prisoner."; earned cards are listed with commas and an "and" only before the last). A hero that fell is not a candidate. A loss writes nothing.
- Nightmare Troll Attack (900208:A:2): the Shadow resolves the same attack against each player: each player not engaged with the Troll names a hero, which takes the Attack the Troll struck with (the attack notes it at the damage step) as combat damage of the Troll, an undefended attack's (`StrikeUndefended`). No second shadow card is dealt, as the card says "this attack"; `recommended`: Vision of the Palantir's Nightmare Journey Along the Anduin review confirms the attack is made against each other player and undefended for one not engaged with the Troll, but says nothing of the shadow card or of which hero takes it, and no FAQ, forum or Hall of Beorn page rules on either; read https://visionofthepalantir.com/2019/05/18/nightmare-journey-along-the-anduin, https://hallofbeorn.com/LotR/Details/The-Lower-Depths (no related ruling), the FFG LotR FAQ (searched through search results; its PDF address returned 404).
