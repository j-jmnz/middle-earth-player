# The Hobbit

The data, rulings and readings of The Hobbit cycle, scenario by scenario. Product rules, the Domain and the Core round are in `docs/brief.md`; the authority order is in `.claude/rules/authority.md`.

The saga cycle of 2012 and 2013: the two saga boxes The Hobbit: Over Hill and Under Hill (`OHaUH`, MEC16: We Must Away, Ere Break of Day; Over the Misty Mountains Grim; Dungeons Deep and Caverns Dim) and The Hobbit: On the Doorstep (`OtD`, MEC24: Flies and Spiders; The Lonely Mountain; The Battle of Five Armies). Like Dwarrowdelf it is `kind: "cycle"` (`data/campaigns/the-hobbit.json`): no campaign card, no booklet, no Campaign Mode, every game standalone on Easy and Normal (user decision, 2026-11-23, for the cycle's six scenarios). It lists under Coming later until one scenario is playable (`src/campaign/playable.ts`). The cycle is data: `scripts/data/cycle-defs.ts` (its six scenarios with the DragnCards decks `Q0A.n` and `E0A.n`), `scripts/data/campaign-defs.ts` (its two products), `scripts/data/normalize/setup-hobbit.ts` (the Setup sentences of the six stage 1A cards), and the folder `the-hobbit` for authored effects and seed finders; `scripts/data/the-hobbit.test.ts` holds its piles, counts and Nightmare decks.

- **Data.** The rules inserts (`ohauh`, `otd`) are pinned as rules docs with `hob.*` spans (`data/rules/index.json`): each scenario's encounter-set paragraph is `hob.setup-<scenario id>`. The scenario files carry no Setup span (`setupSpan`): the sets are Hall of Beorn's list checked against the load list, as in Dwarrowdelf, because an insert's set names hold commas and "and" (We Must Away, Ere Break of Day; Flies and Spiders). The printed ledger is `data/printed/the-hobbit.json`: every side of every card the six scenarios deal, both Bilbo Baggins heroes, the two Baggins-sphere events and the nine treasure cards, read from the scans (`scripts/data/printed/scope.ts`, `hobbitCodes`). Nightmare is out of scope: the decks 9055 to 9060 are built as cards (`scripts/data/nightmare-defs.ts`) but name no scenario, so the scenario files carry no Nightmare block and the ledger no Nightmare lines. Naming a scenario in a row builds the block (the removals resolve), and the Nightmare ledger lines are then read from the proxy pictures.
- **Rules shared by the scenarios of a box** (each box has its own copy, the words differ): Bilbo Baggins and the Baggins sphere (`hob.bilbo-baggins-ohauh`, `hob.baggins-sphere-ohauh`, `hob.bilbo-multiplayer-ohauh`; `hob.bilbo-baggins-otd`, `hob.baggins-sphere-otd`, `hob.bilbo-multiplayer-otd`), Treasure (`hob.treasure-ohauh`, `hob.treasure-otd`), the saga expansion's player cards (`hob.saga-player-cards-ohauh`, `hob.saga-player-cards-otd`), Immune to player card effects (`hob.immune-to-player-card-effects`) and Indestructible (`hob.indestructible`).
- **The shared rules are built once** (the section The shared rules below): the cycle's folder `src/effects/authored/the-hobbit/` holds the records of the treasure cards (`the-hobbit.ts`), the first player's Bilbo Baggins is `ScenarioData.sagaStart` (`src/campaign/saga.ts`), and the words of the shared cards are `HOBBIT_BILBO_ASKS` and `HOBBIT_TREASURE_ASKS` (`src/play/words/hobbit-asks.ts`), which each scenario's asks file spreads.
- **Keyword and effect rules by scenario.** We Must Away: `hob.sack-deck`, `hob.sack-x`, `hob.sack-x-example`. Over the Misty Mountains Grim: `hob.active-and-inactive-encounter-decks`. Dungeons Deep and Caverns Dim: `hob.riddle-area`, `hob.riddles`, `hob.riddles-steps-2-and-3`. Flies and Spiders: `hob.venom-poison`, `hob.unconscious-characters`, `hob.staging-area-creating`, `hob.staging-area-combining`. The Lonely Mountain: `hob.burgle`, `hob.burgle-example`. The Battle of Five Armies: `hob.multiple-stages-in-play`, `hob.current-quest-stage`, `hob.battle`, `hob.siege`. The Easy counts are `easy-mode.the-hobbit`.
- **Piles.** The piles are the DragnCards load lists' (Q0A.1 to Q0A.6, E0A.1 to E0A.6), checked against the stage 1A card's printed Setup. Bilbo Baggins (player 1's play area in every list) is dealt in no pile: the first player takes control of him at the start of every game. We Must Away sets aside the seven Sack objectives, the three Trolls and Troll Cave (the Sack deck is built from the set-aside Sack cards); Over the Misty Mountains Grim's second encounter deck (Misty Mountain Goblins and The Great Goblin) is the `encounterDeck2` pile; The Lonely Mountain sets aside the five Erebor treasure cards (stacked under The Lonely Mountain at Setup); Flies and Spiders sets aside The Spider's Glade and three copies of the treasure Bilbo's Magic Ring.
- **Treasure sets.** A treasure card's set icon is the scenario where it is discovered: the troll icon of We Must Away (Sting, Orcrist, Glamdring), the spider web of Flies and Spiders (Bilbo's Magic Ring), the dragon of The Lonely Mountain (the five Erebor treasures). Each insert prints the treasure icons a scenario accepts as pictures only (no text): one icon in Over the Misty Mountains Grim, Dungeons Deep and Caverns Dim and Flies and Spiders, two in The Lonely Mountain, three in The Battle of Five Armies. They are the sets of the earlier scenarios of the cycle that discover treasure (We Must Away; then Flies and Spiders; then The Lonely Mountain).
- **Readings.** Each scenario below lists what its cards, rulesheet and sources leave open or settle, with sources (`.claude/rules/authority.md`, Open points).

## We Must Away, Ere Break of Day

Pack `OHaUH`, deck Q0A.1 / E0A.1. Encounter sets We Must Away, Ere Break of Day and Western Lands (`hob.setup-we-must-away-ere-break-of-day`). The 1A Setup sets the Sack cards (Sack trait), the three unique Troll enemies and Troll Cave aside; `hob.sack-x` shuffles a Sack card that leaves play back into the sack deck.

- The seven Sack cards are the `setAside` pile (a Sack deck is a Setup record's work, not a pile of its own); DragnCards loads them in its second shared deck (literal).
- A Sack card's printed text is two paragraphs (the attach rule, then what the attached character cannot do); Hall of Beorn runs them together, so the ledger has two lines (scan).

## Over the Misty Mountains Grim

Deck Q0A.2 / E0A.2. First deck: Over the Misty Mountains Grim and Western Lands; second, inactive deck: Misty Mountain Goblins and The Great Goblin (`hob.setup-over-the-misty-mountains-grim`, `hob.active-and-inactive-encounter-decks`). Hall of Beorn lists the sets of the first deck only, so `encounterSets` names all four (`scripts/data/cycle-defs.ts`).

- Stone-Giant: DragnCards stages one copy; the printed 1B advances at once to stage 2A, whose When Revealed (131027:B:1) finds a copy in the encounter deck. All three copies start in the encounter deck (`data/overrides.json`, scenarioPiles; printed card).

## Dungeons Deep and Caverns Dim

Deck Q0A.3 / E0A.3. Sets Dungeons Deep and Caverns Dim and Misty Mountain Goblins (`hob.setup-dungeons-deep-and-caverns-dim`); the riddle area is `hob.riddle-area`.

- Gollum and Bilbo's Magic Ring: DragnCards stages them; stage 2A's own Setup (131030:A:1) searches the encounter deck for them and places them in the riddle area, so they start in the encounter deck (`data/overrides.json`, scenarioPiles; printed card).
- The Goblins' Caves in Easy: DragnCards' E0A.3 holds 1, the Easy Mode sheet removes 1 of 3 (Easy 2, Hall of Beorn agrees); the sheet wins (`easy-mode.the-hobbit`, overrides scenarioEasy).
- Hall of Beorn's pile counts of the Misty Mountain Goblins set needed DragnCards' rows resolved by pack number: DragnCards names the pack "The Hobbit - Over Hill and Under Hill" (`overrides.dragnPackAliases`).

## Flies and Spiders

Pack `OtD`, deck Q0A.4 / E0A.4. Sets Flies and Spiders and Wilderland (`hob.setup-flies-and-spiders`; the insert of The Battle of Five Armies spells it Wilderlands).

- The 1A Setup lets each player shuffle 1 copy of Bilbo's Magic Ring into the deck; the load list sets aside 3 copies of the treasure card, the box holds 1 (Hall of Beorn quantity 1). The data keeps the load list's 3 (recommended; no source found, searches of the FFG FAQ, RulesPal and forum text gave no ruling; the Setup record decides how many copies a table of N players gets). Listed in Readings to confirm.

## The Lonely Mountain

Deck Q0A.5 / E0A.5. Sets The Lonely Mountain and Wilderland (`hob.setup-the-lonely-mountain`).

- Easy counts of Fighting Among Friends (2 removed) and Weighed Down (1 removed): DragnCards' E0A.5 keeps both copies of each; the Easy Mode sheet, Hall of Beorn and the other two Wilderland decks (E0A.4, E0A.6) agree on the removal (overrides scenarioEasy).
- The treasure stack (`hob.burgle`) is the five set-aside Erebor treasure cards.

## The Battle of Five Armies

Deck Q0A.6 / E0A.6. Sets The Battle of Five Armies and Wilderland (`hob.setup-the-battle-of-five-armies`); stages 2 to 4 are in play together (`hob.multiple-stages-in-play`).

- Hall of Beorn lists stages 2 to 5 and leaves out stage 1A; it is added by `overrides.scenarioStages` and takes its place by stage number. Hall of Beorn and DragnCards' load list disagree on the title: the card prints "The Gathering of the Clouds" (scan 132075.A), and `overrides.cards` names it so and corrects the picture address, which answers 403 under Hall of Beorn's spelling.
- **Stage 1** (132075; `recommended`, no source settles it). The scan prints a dash in the quest points of both sides, so Hall of Beorn has none. The Setup (add Bolg, shuffle, reveal 1 encounter card for each player) runs, then stage 1 shows side B and its When Revealed runs after the reveals (`families/quest/setup.ts`, as for every stage 1 with a Setup); `PutStagesIntoPlay` puts stages 2 to 4 into play and sets stage 1 aside, so stage 1 never advances. Alternative: it never advances (a stage with no points). While the Setup reveals, stage 1 is the only stage in play. Searched the FFG forum archive and a playthrough log: none says.
- **Bolg** (132053:A:1; `literal`). He starts in the staging area, as DragnCards stages him: the data build reads the Setup's "add it to the staging area" as a pile placement and refuses an `overrides.scenarioPiles` entry that moves him to the encounter deck, so the Setup record only shuffles and reveals. "X is the number of players" is his Threat. A card revealed during the Setup resolves its When Revealed, and "until the end of the round" lasts to the end of round 1, so the first Goblin the Setup reveals gains Surge and none later in round 1 (`researched`, a forum thread quoting FAQ 1.19: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/175562-battle-of-five-armies-2-questions; the FAQ PDF is not pinned, and its URL answers 404).
- **Stages 2, 3 and 4 in play together** (132075:B:1; `span-settled`, `hob.multiple-stages-in-play`; `faq.side-quest-current-quest-text` says the text of each quest card in play is active). Each stage's text is active while it is in play. Stage 1 leaves play. The stages are three quest cards at once, each with its own progress.
- **Current quest stage** (`span-settled`, `hob.current-quest-stage`). The first player chooses it at the beginning of each round, before resources are collected: the same choice the encounter side quests ask for (`currentQuestId`). Progress goes only on it (the active location first, `rr.active-location`); "the current quest" in every effect is that stage; Battle (stage 3) and Siege (stage 4) work as for a side quest (`hon.battle`, `hon.siege`, built). The Setup round has no choice (stage 1 is the only stage); round 1's choice is the first one.
- **Complete** (132076:B:1, 132077:B:1, 132078:B:1; `span-settled`, `faq.progress-without-advancing`, `rr.quest-advancement`). A stage is complete when its progress equals or exceeds its 8 quest points; it stays in play and may take more progress, but it does not advance alone. When stages 2, 3 and 4 are all complete the players advance to stage 5 at once. A progress removal that leaves a stage under 8 makes it incomplete again (Gundabad Climber, Ruins of Dale and the like). The community reads it the same way (https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/79753-question-about-the-battle-of-five-armies-quest: "you cannot progress to 5a until 2b, 3b, and 4b all have 8 or more tokens on that all at the same time"; no official answer found). Status: `researched`, community only; listed under Readings to confirm.
- **Quest stage in play with no progress** (132054:A:2, 132055:A:2, 132057:A:1, 132058:A:2, 132059:A:2; `literal`). The count is the stages in play with no progress token on them, read when the effect resolves: 0 to 3 while stages 2 to 4 are in play. Stage 1 counts during the Setup and stage 5 once it is in play (`recommended`: the cards say "each quest stage in play"; alternative: only stages 2 to 4 count).
- **No progress at the end of the round** (132076:B:2, 132077:B:3, 132078:B:3; `literal`). A Forced effect at the end of the round, only while the stage is in play with 0 progress. Siege's discard takes every resource from every hero's pool, Bilbo's too.
- **Travel costs of Ruins of Dale, Ravenhill and The Front Gate** (132061:A:3, 132062:A:3, 132063:A:3; `literal`). The cost is 1 Baggins resource when the named stage is the current quest, 3 otherwise; a player who does not control Bilbo spends from the Baggins resources of his pool (`hob.bilbo-multiplayer-otd`). Their Forced removes 2 progress from the named stage at the end of each round, if there is progress to remove.
- **Heedless of Order** (132066:A:2; `researched`, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/175562-battle-of-five-armies-2-questions, community): the first player chooses a stage other than the current one to be current until the end of the round; with no other stage the card gains Doomed 3. Revealed during the Setup it gains Doomed 3 (stage 1 is the only quest; "TwiceBorn: Correct").
- **Stage 5** (132079:B:1 to B:3; `literal`). Bolg is in the staging area, and while he is, he is engaged with the first player (he attacks in the combat phase, and he is not optionally engageable). He is Immune, and cannot take damage while a Bodyguard of Bolg is in play (132054:A:1, a Bodyguard in the staging area or engaged); stage 5 is the last stage and the players win when Bolg is destroyed. Before stage 5 he can be attacked in the staging area only if the engine offers it; the engine follows the cards (Immune stops card effects, an attack is no effect; forum, no official ruling: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/79707-battle-of-the-five-armies-questions). Progress cannot be placed on stage 5, so questing no longer moves the quest. `recommended` for the engagement timing (before stage 5 he stays in the staging area and engages only by the stage 5 text).
- **The table** (`hob.multiple-stages-in-play`, `hob.current-quest-stage`; ledger lines 132076:B:2, 132077:B:2, 132077:B:3, 132078:B:2, 132078:B:3, 132079:B:2; `docs/design-system-table.md`, Top bar): the three stages in play together are three dials after the seat dials (`readouts/armies.ts`), each with its progress against its 8 quest points, the current stage marked, the Battle or Siege it prints, and a warning while it holds no progress, since its Forced effect at the end of the round then applies. The first player's choice of the current stage at the start of each round is a choice sheet with a row for each stage and its progress (`words/scenarios/armies.ts`). Stage 5 shows no dial: no progress can be placed on it and Bolg, in the staging area, is the card that matters. All three stage cards are in the quest area at once.
- **Wilderland set** (132035 to 132038, records in `src/effects/authored/the-hobbit/set-wilderland.ts`, shared with Flies and Spiders and The Lonely Mountain).
  - Fighting Among Friends (132037:A:1; `span-settled`, `rr.neutral-cards`): the first player names one of Leadership, Lore, Spirit and Tactics; a neutral non-Baggins character belongs to none of them, so it is removed from the quest too.
  - Weighed Down (132038:A:1; `span-settled`, `rr.lasting-effects`, `faq.lasting-effects`): each player of the staging area it is revealed to holds the lasting effect on the characters he controls, now and entering play later; the penalty is read each time the stat is read, so a character exhausted or readied later in the round gains or loses it (stat condition `holderExhausted`). A:2 (`literal`): only a defended attack that destroys its defender raises the threat; an undefended attack that destroys a hero does not.
  - Dark Bats (132036; `recommended`): it attaches to the damaged or defending character and counts as a Condition attachment while attached (`CountsAs`, `AttachSelf` to `subject` or `defender`); a character it destroys is out of play, so the card stays an enemy; an undefended attack has no defending character, so the Shadow card is not attached.
  - Hidden Path (132035:A:2; `span-settled`, `hob.staging-area-creating`): the 2 Baggins resources are paid from the heroes of the Baggins sphere (`SpendResources` with `sphere: baggins`, `group`); once the staging areas are separate only the players who share the location's staging area may pay, from the heroes they control (`spendablePools`, `src/rules/families/costs/verbs.ts`, reads the actor's area). This replaces the earlier recommended reading that Bilbo's pool pays in every area.

## The shared rules

Built once for the six scenarios: Bilbo Baggins and the Baggins sphere, Treasure, the saga player cards, Immune to player card effects and Indestructible. Each reading says its status (`.claude/rules/authority.md`, Open points): `literal` (the printed line), `span-settled` (a `hob.*` span), `researched` (an online source settles it) or `recommended` (no source settles it).

- **Bilbo Baggins** (`hob.bilbo-baggins-ohauh`, `hob.bilbo-baggins-otd`; `span-settled`). The first player takes control of the box's Bilbo Baggins at the start of every game: `sagaStart` of each scenario names him (131001 for the three Over Hill and Under Hill scenarios, 132001 for the three On the Doorstep ones), and `loadoutOf` (`src/campaign/briefing.ts`) puts him in front of the first seat's heroes. He has a threat cost of 0 and does not count against the starting hero limit, so the first seat may hold three heroes of its own beside him (131001's card text leaves the hero limit to the insert; 132001:A:1 prints it). No other version of Bilbo is a starting hero or in a deck (`secondVersion`), and the Baggins hero is refused in any scenario that grants none (`bagginsOutsideHobbit`). The Fellowship sphere stays refused (the cycle is no saga, `mec109.the-fellowship-sphere`).
- **Bilbo and the first player token** (`hob.bilbo-multiplayer-ohauh`, `hob.bilbo-multiplayer-otd`; 131001:A:1, 132001:A:1; `span-settled`). When the token passes, the new first player gains control of Bilbo, the resources in his pool and the cards attached to him (the `ControlledBy` constant, `src/rules/families/play/sweep.ts`). When Bilbo was the last hero under a player's control and leaves it, that player is eliminated at once (`eliminateEmpties` after a hero changes control). A player who does not control Bilbo plays Burglar Baggins with a Baggins resource from his pool (131019:A:1).
- **Resources Bilbo gains** (131001:A:2, 132001:A:1; `literal`). The Over Hill and Under Hill card prints "cannot gain resources from player card effects" and its insert "through player card effects" (`hob.baggins-sphere-ohauh`): the card wins. A treasure is a player card, so that Bilbo gains no resource from Orcrist (131021:A:3) even from his own box. The On the Doorstep Bilbo prints "from non-treasure cards" (`hob.baggins-sphere-otd`): a treasure's effect adds resources to him (Bilbo's Magic Ring, 132019:A:2). The resource he collects in the resource phase is no card effect.
- **Treasure cards** (`hob.treasure-ohauh`, `hob.treasure-otd`). A treasure is a player card with card type Treasure, a cost of 0 and no sphere. A deck adds it at the Setup when its set is one the scenario lists (`ScenarioData.treasures`, from `scripts/data/cycle-defs.ts`: the troll set of We Must Away for the next three scenarios and The Lonely Mountain, the spider web set of Flies and Spiders from The Lonely Mountain on, the dragon set of The Lonely Mountain in The Battle of Five Armies), one copy of a title, outside the 50 card minimum (`validateLoadout`, `treasureSet`, `treasureCopies`). It is in the deck, drawn into the hand and played like an attachment onto the host its "Attach to" line names (`playsOntoHost`, `src/rules/core/printed.ts`; `recommended`: the spans never say how a treasure is played, the card prints "Attach to ...", `hob.treasure-ohauh` calls the cards "added to a deck" and the Setup of Flies and Spiders has the players shuffle a copy into the deck). Its text calls treasures apart from attachments ("non-treasure, non-objective attachments", 131056:A:1), so a pick of attachments never takes one (`literal`).
- **Discovery in a cycle with no log** (`recommended`, listed under Readings to confirm). The first condition of both spans, "discovered ... through game text in a previous scenario using the same group of heroes", needs a record the cycle does not keep (every game is standalone, no Campaign Mode). The second condition, the set icon, is kept, and the first is waived: any treasure of a listed set may be added at the Setup, with no record of its discovery. The alternative is to record each won game's discovered treasure with its heroes in History and admit only those; it needs a record no screen reads yet. Sources read: the two rulesheets (`hob.treasure-ohauh`, `hob.treasure-otd`), https://talesfromthecards.wordpress.com/2013/04/24/the-hobbit-on-the-doorstep-review-attachments-and-treasures/ ("Once a scenario tells you that you have 'earned' a treasure, you can include them as part of your decks for future quests"), https://www.unboxedtheboardgameblog.com/post/we-must-away-ere-break-of-day and the FFG forum thread https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/301078-the-hobbit/: none addresses a standalone game or an app, and the FAQ (`faq.*`) has no entry. Flies and Spiders' own Setup gives each player a copy of Bilbo's Magic Ring whatever was discovered (132067:A:1); its stage 3B discovers it for the later scenarios (132070:B:1).
- **The treasure lines** (each `literal` unless said). Sting (131020:A:3) and Bilbo's Magic Ring (132019:A:3) answer the host's declaration as defender: the host exhausted says it exhausted to defend, as the Fellowship's Sting does (142011:A:3). The Ring's cancel is "this attack deals no damage": the response comes after the defender is declared, before damage, so "cancel all damage" is that attack's damage not dealt, as for Frodo's One Ring (142001:A:1). Orcrist and Glamdring answer an attack that destroys an Orc (131021:A:3, 131022:A:3); Thrór's Battle Axe (132022:A:3) deals its 1 damage to another enemy engaged with its controller, so the enemy just destroyed is never a choice. Thrór's Hunting Bow gives Ranged (132023:A:2) and +2 Attack while the attack is a ranged one (132023:A:3: `rr.ranged`: an enemy engaged with another player, or an attack another player declared). The Arkenstone (132020:A:2): the player chooses X when the action resolves (1 to the threat that leaves them in the game) and raises their threat by X, then the next ally they play this phase of the attached hero's sphere costs X less (`recommended`: the engine pays a raise of threat as a fixed cost, so X is chosen as the first step of the effect; the action needs an ally in hand). Thrór's Golden Cup adds itself to the victory display as its cost and draws 3 cards (132024:A:2).
- **Saga player cards** (`hob.saga-player-cards-ohauh`, `hob.saga-player-cards-otd`; `recommended`, listed under Readings to confirm). The Baggins sphere cards (Bilbo and the two events) are "intended for use only" with the box's scenarios. "Intended" is no deck rule and deckbuilding follows the Rules Reference (STATUS, Standing decisions), so Burglar Baggins and The Lucky Number stay deck-legal; no hero pays for them outside the Hobbit scenarios, since only Bilbo holds a Baggins resource. The treasure cards are refused outside the scenarios that list their set.
- **Immune to player card effects and Indestructible** (`hob.immune-to-player-card-effects`, `hob.indestructible`; `span-settled`). Both read as `mec109.immune-to-player-card-effects`, `faq.immune-expanded` and `mec109.indestructible` do: the keyword framework (`src/rules/interpret/immune-targets.ts`, `src/rules/core/keywords.ts`) already implements them, and the tests cite the Hobbit spans beside the other ones.

## Ledger readings

Where a scan and Hall of Beorn's text differ, the card wins (`.claude/rules/authority.md`); the ledger holds the printed reading. The differences found:

- Paragraphs Hall of Beorn runs together are separate lines (Sack cards, Riddle lines, Burgle lines, Travel and Forced lines of locations); quoted text on a card is written with straight double quotes.
- Keyword lines missing from the text: Doomed 1 (A Nice Pickle, No Campfire), Surge (Goblin Runners); Dark Bats' Shadow line; Smaug the Golden's "Cannot be engaged."; Ruins of Dale, Ravenhill and The Front Gate print "(1 Baggins instead ...)" with the icon only.
- Quest card typos in Hall of Beorn's text, written as printed: Lost in the Dark 2B, Captured by Spiders 3B, The Burglar's Turn 2B, The Gathering of the Clouds 1B, The Clouds Burst 5B, "unconscious characters" on the Flies and Spiders stages.
- Printed misprints, kept under `misprints`: Ravenous Warg prints "Ravenous Wargs attacks" (132059:A:1); A Warm Welcome 1B prints "At the end of round" (132071:B:2).
- The OHaUH insert says Bilbo "cannot gain resources through player card effects" (`hob.baggins-sphere-ohauh`); the card prints "from player card effects" (131001:A:1). The card wins.
