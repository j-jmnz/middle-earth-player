# Vengeance of Mordor

The data, rulings and readings of the Vengeance of Mordor cycle, scenario by scenario. Product rules, the Domain and the Core round are in `docs/brief.md`; the authority order is in `.claude/rules/authority.md`.

The cycle of 2019 and 2020: the deluxe expansion A Shadow in the East (`ASitE`, three scenarios) and its six Adventure Packs Wrath and Ruin (`WaR`), The City of Ulfast (`TCoU`), Challenge of the Wainriders (`CotW`), Under the Ash Mountains (`UtAM`), The Land of Sorrow (`TLoS`) and The Fortress of Nurn (`TFoN`). Like Haradrim it is `kind: "cycle"` (`data/campaigns/vengeance-of-mordor.json`): no campaign card, no booklet, no boon or burden and no Campaign Mode, so every game is standalone. Nightmare is not built. The cycle is data: `scripts/data/cycle-defs.ts` (its nine scenarios with the DragnCards decks `Q09.n` and `E09.n`; `Q08` is Wilderland's), `scripts/data/campaign-defs.ts` (its seven products), `scripts/data/normalize/setup-vengeance-of-mordor.ts` (the Setup sentences of the stage 1A cards the other readers do not read), and `scripts/data/vengeance-of-mordor.test.ts` (its piles, counts and spans). The printed ledger is `data/printed/vengeance-of-mordor.json`.

- **Data.** Each scenario's encounter sets are printed in the insert of A Shadow in the East (`asite`, MEC77) or the pack's rulesheet (`war`, `tcou`, `cotw`, `utam`, `tlos`, `tfon`, MEC78 to MEC83), as the span `vom.setup-<scenario id>`. The PDFs are scans with a faulty text layer, so each document is read by eye from the rendered pages (`handRead` in `data/rules/columns.json`) and holds only the paragraphs a span reads. The piles are the DragnCards load lists' (Q09.1 to Q09.9, E09.1 to E09.9), checked against the stage 1A card's Setup; the departures are overrides in `data/overrides.json`, each with its reason and source, and are named in the scenario's file. The Setup readers use the ledger's printed line, so a printed typo that Hall of Beorn corrected or kept is settled by the scan (`misprints` in the ledger).
- **Rules the cycle owns.** Contracts (`vom.contracts`), Sméagol / Gollum (`vom.smeagol-gollum`), The One Ring and the Master trait (`vom.the-one-ring`), quest stages against side quests (`vom.quest-stages-vs-side-quests`), taking and losing control of locations (`vom.controlling-locations`, `vom.losing-control-of-a-location`), the circuit, the Race keyword and Racing X tests (`vom.the-circuit`, `vom.race-keyword`, `vom.racing-x-tests`, `vom.racing-x-tests-example`), The Power of Mordor deck (`vom.power-of-mordor-deck-the-temple-of-doom`, `vom.power-of-mordor-deck-the-fortress-of-nurn`), Storm the Castle (`vom.storm-the-castle`) and two strategy tips that print rules (`vom.strategy-tip-the-city-of-ulfast`, `vom.strategy-tip-under-the-ash-mountains`). Side quests, Archery X, Immune to player card effects, Indestructible and Guarded (X) are the Haradrim cycle's and the booklets' (`hrd.*`, `mec109.indestructible`); the insert of A Shadow in the East prints them again, so no `vom` span repeats them.
- **FAQ spans and errata.** The FAQ has no span for a card of the cycle and lists no erratum for it. The semi-official A Long Extended Party FAQ (`https://alongextendedparty.com/semi-official-errata-faq-1-10/`, the designer's answers with their sources) has the entries named in the scenario files; each is a `researched` reading.
- **Shared encounter sets.** The first scenario that deals a set holds its readings; a later scenario reuses them. Easterling Raiders: The River Running, The Temple of Doom, Wrath and Ruin, The Land of Sorrow. Rolling Plains: The River Running, Challenge of the Wainriders, The Land of Sorrow. Riders of Rhûn: The River Running, Challenge of the Wainriders. City of Rhûn: Danger in Dorwinion, Wrath and Ruin. Servants of Sauron: Danger in Dorwinion, The City of Ulfast. Under Guard: Danger in Dorwinion, The City of Ulfast, The Fortress of Nurn. Twisted Tunnels: The Temple of Doom, Under the Ash Mountains. Ulchor's Guard: The Temple of Doom, The Fortress of Nurn. The Power of Mordor: The Temple of Doom, The Fortress of Nurn. A card keeps one record in every scenario that deals it, so a fix in one scenario's reading moves the others' saved games.
- **Mechanics the engine has not read before.** The Race keyword and Racing X tests with the circuit of quest stages (Challenge of the Wainriders), locations under a player's control (Wrath and Ruin), a Power of Mordor deck beside the encounter deck, plot objectives and Cultist enemies set aside and drawn at random, a guarded objective-ally under a Camp location with an enemy under it, the four double-sided Storm the Castle side quests with a faceup card under each, cards placed under the quest deck and under side quests, a deck trimmed or capped at Setup, and the player contracts, Sméagol / Gollum and The One Ring of the insert. Each is built by the scenario that first deals it (`docs/agents/agent-brief.md`, Scenario procedure); none is built by the data. No keyword is needed by every scenario that a scenario does not own.
- **Readings.** Each scenario file lists what its cards, rulesheet and sources leave open or settle, with sources (`.claude/rules/authority.md`, Open points). A reading is `span-settled` (a rule span or the printed line settles it), `researched` (an official online source settles it) or `recommended` (no official source settles it); a `recommended` reading carries the URLs read and why none settled it, and stands as the answer. The research rows were written against the scan-read ledger and the sources; the scenario's Read task confirms every line.

## Player card readings

The player card readings use the same statuses. The keys are `<code>:<line>`, written from the scan-read ledger (`data/printed/player-cards.json`).

- Contracts (`21074`, the Fellowship contract of the insert, and the later ones) (span-settled). A contract is a player card that is never in the deck and does not count toward the minimum deck size; each player may put one into play with the heroes at the start of the game, side A faceup, if the deck meets its restrictions. Basis: `vom.contracts`, `docs/brief.md` (Player side quests, contracts and saga cards).
- Sméagol / Gollum (`21072`, `21073`, `21071`) (span-settled). A player who starts with Sméagol shuffles two copies of Stinker into the encounter deck; Stinker flips Sméagol to Gollum, who enters play engaged with the owner, ready, and engages the first player; a defeated Gollum flips back, exhausted, under his owner's control. Sméagol cannot start a game of a saga expansion or the Shadows of Mirkwood cycle. Basis: `vom.smeagol-gollum`. If Sméagol is the last hero and Stinker flips him, the player is not eliminated, and undefended damage is lost (researched, ALeP 3.333: https://alongextendedparty.com/semi-official-errata-faq-1-10/).
- The One Ring and the Master trait (`21001`, `21009`) (span-settled). The One Ring is attached to a hero right after the heroes are placed; with two or more copies the first player decides which is put into play and each other copy is removed from the game; it is immune to non-Master card effects, and a Master card can be used only by the player who controls it. Basis: `vom.the-one-ring`. ALeP 3.354 (researched: https://alongextendedparty.com/semi-official-errata-faq-1-10/): player card Setup effects resolve at the time the rules insert gives, so The One Ring's is at once after the heroes. A mulligan shuffles back only the cards of the opening hand: the Master card the Setup fetched stays in the hand (recommended). Read: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/301588-the-long-arm-of-mordor-and-setup-effects-and-mulligan (Oct 2019, a player's answer that the Master card is kept; two players call keeping one card through a mulligan odd but accept it) and https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/306124-timing-of-setup/ (a designer ruling on Thurindir, reported second-hand: the side quest searched before the hand is kept when the six drawn cards are shuffled back; the designer later moved hero Setup to step 7 and said a current ruling overrules a past one). Why recommended: no official source names The One Ring and the mulligan, and the one designer ruling is second-hand, about another card, and superseded on timing; the community answers agree and ALeP 3.354 puts the Setup before the hand. BoardGameGeek, r/lotrlcg and the Hall of Beorn forum gave nothing on it.
- Player card Setup effects (researched). Contracts and other player Setup effects resolve at the time their insert says; ALeP 3.354 (18 April 2020) reverses 3.347 and 3.348: https://alongextendedparty.com/semi-official-errata-faq-1-10/.
- The Master Ring on a side quest (`21009`) (recommended). The Master Ring cancels a whole card, so a revealed encounter side quest is canceled and another card is revealed in its place; "cannot be canceled" text is absolute (ALeP 3.330). Not taken: a side quest's "when revealed" text is protected from the whole card. Why recommended: ALeP 3.395 (18 December 2025, Caleb Grace, who says he answers as he would resolve it, not as a ruling) is the only source: https://alongextendedparty.com/semi-official-errata-faq-1-10/. No FAQ entry or agreed thread settles it.
