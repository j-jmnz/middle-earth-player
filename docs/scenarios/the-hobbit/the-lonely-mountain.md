# The Lonely Mountain

Deck Q0A.5 / E0A.5. Sets The Lonely Mountain and Wilderland (`hob.setup-the-lonely-mountain`).

- Easy counts of Fighting Among Friends (2 removed) and Weighed Down (1 removed): DragnCards' E0A.5 keeps both copies of each; the Easy Mode sheet, Hall of Beorn and the other two Wilderland decks (E0A.4, E0A.6) agree on the removal (overrides scenarioEasy).
- The treasure stack (`hob.burgle`) is the five set-aside Erebor treasure cards.
- **Readings of the cards** (sources: the Easy and rules spans named; FFG forum archive threads https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/129461-on-the-doorstep-questions, /topic/98174-smaug-the-magnificent, /topic/80902-pretending-to-sleep-on-lonely-mountain-stage-3b, /topic/195264-lonely-mountain-suggestions, /topic/304983-hobbit-on-the-doorstep; the semi-official FAQ https://alongextendedparty.com/semi-official-errata-faq-1-10/; no entry of the FFG FAQ or Rules Reference names the scenario).
  - Setup (132071:A:1; `literal`). The piles already hold the search: Smaug the Golden and The Lonely Mountain start in the staging area, Smaug the Magnificent and A Bare Patch are set aside. The record shuffles the five Erebor treasures and stacks them facedown under The Lonely Mountain.
  - A burgle attempt (`hob.burgle`, `hob.burgle-example`; `span-settled`). Step 1 discards, it does not reveal: no When Revealed, no staging, no surge, no "when revealed" trigger (Croaking Crows) for the discarded cards. A burgle effect is read only for the card step 1 stops at. Step 2 shuffles the first player's deck once and reveals its top card; it stays on the deck and stays "the revealed card" (`researched`: FFG answer quoted in the forum thread 129461, a fan reporting it); only The Lonely Mountain's action changes it (takes the revealed card into hand, reveals the next one without a shuffle). Step 3: the first player discards exactly the printed number of cards from his hand that match, or the attempt fails ("may" lets him decline). A deck with no card to reveal fails the attempt, also after the action takes the last card (`recommended`: the action's "reveal the next card" has nothing to reveal).
  - The action window of a burgle attempt (`The Lonely Mountain` 132043:A:2, `researched`): players take actions after step 2 and before step 3, the semi-official FAQ (2.57).
  - Burgle step 1 when the encounter deck runs out (`researched`, community): the discard pile is shuffled back into the encounter deck, as in any quest phase (semi-official FAQ, 3.083; every burgle attempt happens in the quest phase). Alternative: the attempt fails.
  - What "match" means (`recommended`; no source found, searches of the forum archive and the FAQ gave no ruling): a card matches the revealed card's sphere when both have the same sphere, neutral with neutral and Baggins with Baggins; its type when the card types are equal; its cost when the printed costs are equal (an X cost matches nothing). A sphere-and-type line needs both.
  - Smaug the Golden (132040; `literal`). X (his Threat) is the number of committed characters without the Burglar trait, counted as it changes; he is in the staging area and cannot be engaged. At stage 2B the first player's failed attempt makes him attack the first player: an attack outside the combat phase, dealt a shadow card, with the first player choosing the defender (`span-settled`: `faq.enemy-attacks-outside-combat`).
  - The sixth progress on Smaug the Golden at stage 2B (132040:A:3, 132072:B:1; `recommended`): the stage advances to 4A at once and the quest's burgle attempt is not made (the stage text is no longer in play, `faq.triggering-forced-effects`). The forum is split (thread 129461: advance, then burgle; a later poster: no burgle); no official answer. Alternative: place the progress, make the attempt, then advance.
  - Pretending to Sleep at a stage with no Smaug the Golden (132048:A:2; `researched`, community: thread 80902): the progress cannot be placed, so the "Then" half is dropped and the surge still happens. With him in play the first player may spend X Baggins resources from Bilbo's pool or advance to stage 4A; with fewer than X he advances (`recommended`: "spend X or advance" is the first player's choice). Advancing to 4A while at 4A does nothing.
  - Smaug the Magnificent (132041; `researched`, community: thread 98174, alogos, accepted by the asker). He is in the staging area, so his Threat 6 counts against the players' quest and he is a target for staging-area effects. He is "considered engaged" with the first player as the Balrog (142044:A:2) is: he attacks only the first player in the combat phase, who declares his attackers (the others only with ranged), with the first player token passing to the new first player. His additional attack (a shadow card with a burgle effect, whether or not it has shadow text; the Forced effect is heard when he attacks, with the shadow card dealt for that attack, so the additional attack follows the attack in progress) discards the shadow card first (`span-settled`: `faq.additional-attacks`) and deals a new one; the attack is not undefended (fan answer, thread 129461).
  - A Bare Patch (132039). It starts set aside and enters the staging area with Smaug the Magnificent at stage 3A; the response triggers from there. Attached, he has -3 Defense (`literal`). When Smaug the Magnificent is set aside at stage 4B, a Bare Patch attached to him is set aside with him and a Bare Patch still in the staging area is set aside too, so stage 3A adds both again (`recommended`: `rr.objective-cards` keeps an objective in play, `rr.attachment-cards` discards an attachment whose host leaves; neither returns it to the set-aside pile). Alternative: it stays in the staging area, and stage 3A adds only what is not in play.
  - Stage 3B (132073:B:2; `recommended`): the forced check is made whenever progress is placed on the stage or damage is dealt to Smaug the Magnificent; the remaining hit points are 20 less his damage. Progress above 20 is kept (`faq.progress-without-advancing`). Defeating stage 3 wins the game; nothing is stored for the treasure (no log).
  - Stage 4B (132074:B:1; `literal`): the progress on stage 4 goes with it when the quest deck resets to stage 2B; Smaug the Golden returns with no progress (his progress was removed at 132074:A:1). Treasure taken into hand stays.
  - The Lonely Mountain (132043; `span-settled`): with a card under it its Threat is 12 less 2 for each, then halved and rounded up with Secret Entrance active (`rr.modifiers`: additive modifiers first, halving after, a fraction rounded up, below 0 is 0). Under it the five treasures are the stack; with none it is plain. The action spends 2 Baggins resources from Bilbo's pool (the first player controls him) and Dragon-Spell punishes that spending.
  - Treasure from under The Lonely Mountain (132072:B:1; `literal`): the first player takes the top card of the stack into hand; with an empty stack he takes none.
  - Fighting Among Friends (132037:A:1; `span-settled`, `rr.neutral-cards`; the reading is the Wilderland set's, in `docs/scenarios/the-hobbit/the-battle-of-five-armies.md`, The Battle of Five Armies): the first player names one of Leadership, Lore, Spirit and Tactics; a neutral non-Baggins character belongs to none of them, so it is removed from the quest too, and only characters committed to the quest are removed.
  - Weighed Down (132038:A:1; `span-settled`): `faq.lasting-effects`: an encounter card's lasting effect is recalculated, so a character that exhausts later in the round also gets the minus.
  - Known to an Ounce (132049:A:1; `literal`): each player discards 2 random cards (all he holds if fewer); each Dragon in play, in the staging area or engaged, gets +1 Threat for each event discarded in this resolution, by all players together, until the end of the phase.
  - Belching Fire (132050:A:1; `literal`): each Dragon in the staging area attacks one after the other, no shadow card, the defending player the one with the most Willpower committed; a tie is the first player's choice (`rr.first-player`). The attacked player chooses the defender.
  - Dragon-Spell (132051:A:1; `literal`): the hero with the most resources that has no Dragon-Spell attached; a tie is the first player's choice (`rr.first-player`); with no such hero the card is discarded (`recommended`: `rr.attachment-cards`, an attachment cannot enter play with no target). The damage applies to every spending of that hero's resources, for any card or ability.
  - Secret Entrance (132045:A:2; `literal`, the printed line is the erratum's wording): the first player exhausts a ready hero (any hero he controls, Bilbo too) and names enemy, location, treachery or objective; the top card of the encounter deck is looked at, not revealed; with an empty deck it is not the named type (`recommended`). The Lonely Mountain's Threat is halved while it is the active location. "(The players cannot travel again this round.)" is printed, so it holds (`literal`): the engine allows one Travel in the travel phase, so after this Travel, kept or returned to the staging area, the players do not travel again in that phase. A travel that a card effect makes later in the round is not barred (`recommended`, agent recommendation, no ruling found: the engine has no restriction that lasts to the end of the round, and the only travel effect in the cycle's scenarios is this one's own); listed under Readings to confirm.
  - Great Hall (132044:A:1) puts 1 progress on Smaug the Golden at the end of each round while it is active, and Desolation of Smaug (132046:A:1) gives surge to the first treachery card revealed each round while it is active (`literal`).
  - Hidden Path (132035:A:1, A:2; `literal`): -1 Threat to every location in the staging area, The Lonely Mountain included (nothing below 0); the 2 Baggins resources come from Bilbo's pool.
  - Dark Bats (132036:A:1, A:2; `literal`): once attached it is a Condition attachment, no longer an enemy, and is discarded with its host; a shadow-card Dark Bats attaches to the defending character.
  - **The table** (the Table step, `docs/agents/agent-brief.md`, Scenario procedure; the dials are `docs/design-system-table.md`, Top bar). The stack the Setup makes (132071:A:1) and The Lonely Mountain's Threat it lowers (132043:A:1) are the dial "Stack": the cards still under the Mountain out of 5, with the Threat now and printed in its note (the card's own Threat badge shows the -2 for each). Smaug the Golden's progress against the 6th that advances the players (132040:A:3) is the dial "Golden", which warms within 2 of the limit. The held cards are no zone of the table: a card under the Mountain is a count, as the enemies under Reeking Moat are. A burgle attempt's revealed card and its action window (132043:A:2, `hob.burgle`) are a sheet that names the card, what step 3 asks of the first player and the Mountain's Action; step 3's Choose and Picks name the number to discard and whose choice it is, two-handed too (`src/play/choice/burgle-sheets.test.ts`). A choice that the first player makes names the first player.
