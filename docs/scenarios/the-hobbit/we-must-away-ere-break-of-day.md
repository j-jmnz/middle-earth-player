# We Must Away, Ere Break of Day

Pack `OHaUH`, deck Q0A.1 / E0A.1. Encounter sets We Must Away, Ere Break of Day and Western Lands (`hob.setup-we-must-away-ere-break-of-day`). The 1A Setup sets the Sack cards (Sack trait), the three unique Troll enemies and Troll Cave aside; `hob.sack-x` shuffles a Sack card that leaves play back into the sack deck.

- The seven Sack cards are the `setAside` pile (a Sack deck is a Setup record's work, not a pile of its own); DragnCards loads them in its second shared deck (literal).
- A Sack card's printed text is two paragraphs (the attach rule, then what the attached character cannot do); Hall of Beorn runs them together, so the ledger has two lines (scan).
- **The sack deck** (`hob.sack-deck`, 131023:A:1; `span-settled`). The seven Sack objectives (131044 to 131050) are set aside at the Setup and shuffled face down into the sack deck, a deck of its own that is no part of the encounter deck. The 1B Setup and the span say the same.
- **Sack X** (`hob.sack-x`, `hob.sack-x-example`; `span-settled`). The first player draws the top X cards of the sack deck one at a time and resolves each card's When Sacked before the next; an empty sack deck ignores the keyword; a Sack card that leaves play for any reason is shuffled into the sack deck. Where a Troll prints "Forced: After William engages a player, sack 2" (131032:A:2, 131033:A:2, 131034:A:2) the Forced effect is the trigger (`literal`); 131051 (When Revealed, Shadow) says "Sack 1" in its own text.
- **When Sacked targets** (`hob.sack-x`, `hob.sack-x-example`; `span-settled` for the choice, `literal` for the rest). A target is a character in play (a hero or an ally; Bilbo Baggins is one, 131036:A:2 lets him act with a Sack on him), never a card titled Gandalf (except for A Worn Sack, which prints no exclusion), never one with a Sack attached, and never one that cannot have attachments (Beorn, the span's example). The comparison uses printed values (cost, attack, defense, hit points, willpower), the number of attachments on the character, or the resources in a hero's pool (Bilbo's Baggins resources count). The first player chooses among tied characters. When none is eligible (A Smelly Sack with no ally in play) the card is shuffled back into the sack deck.
- **No printed value** (`recommended`): a character with no printed attack (a dash) counts as 0, so it can be the highest only when every eligible character has 0. No source speaks of it.
- **Remove all Troll enemies and Sack cards from the game** (131025:B:1; `literal`). Every Sack card is removed wherever it lies (in play, attached or in the sack deck); the next line then wins the game, so the choice has no effect on play.
- **Stage 2B** (131024:B:1, `researched`, plus a ruling). Either condition advances the quest: no Troll enemy left in play, or no card left in the encounter deck. The quest's 1 quest point needs no progress for the first condition. Source: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/88412-we-must-away-ere-break-of-day-2b (players agree; no FFG answer). Listed in Readings to confirm. An empty deck advances the stage as soon as it has no card (rr.forced), so before the quest phase reset of an empty encounter deck (rr.running-out-of-cards, faq.last-encounter-card) and not at the end of the staging step or the phase: the sack family's settle hook announces `encounterDeckEmpty` whenever the deck is empty and no frame is open, and `resetEncounterDeck` waits while a card in play hears that event (agent recommendation; the ruling the orchestrator recorded). A deck emptied by the stage's own progress discards, by the last card of the staging step or by a shadow card dealt in combat advances at once.
- **Stage 2B progress** (131024:B:2; `literal`). The players' progress goes on the active location first; the quest then discards that many cards from the top of the encounter deck instead of taking it, to the encounter discard pile, as many as the deck holds; an empty deck advances the stage by the line above.
- **Troll enemies' shared lines** (`researched`, community: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/70231-hobbit-troll-questions, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/290209-if-tom-bert-or-william-leave-play). The three constant lines (131032:A:1, 131033:A:1, 131034:A:1) apply to every Troll enemy in play while their card is in play, and stop when that card leaves play. William's: any damage, from any source, to a Troll that is not engaged with a player is not dealt, in the staging area too. Alternative: only the card's own Troll. Listed in Readings to confirm.
- **One attacker at a time** (131034:A:1; `researched`: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/70359-attacking-while-tom-is-in-play-hobbit-1, community only). A Troll takes an attack declared by one character only; the players may declare another character's attack against it in the same phase after that attack. Alternative: one attack on a Troll in all. Listed in Readings to confirm.
- **Troll Key and Troll Purse** (131042:A:1, 131043:A:1; `researched`: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/298646-hobbit-saga-we-must-away-ere-break-of-day-problem-with-the-ending-spoilers). A discarded copy goes to the staging area (the errata the line prints), whether it was discarded from play or from the encounter deck by stage 2B's progress (the Setup of An Unexpected Party makes the table note the cards placed in the discard pile, and the copy hears `encounterDiscarded`); a copy unattached in the staging area attaches to a Troll enemy as soon as one is in play, if able (so in stage 1 a revealed copy waits there). The claim Responses are the first player's alone (no other player is offered them) and use Bilbo Baggins' pool: the Purse costs 1 Baggins resource, the Key exhausts him (`literal`). The Purse's claim (131042:A:2) is offered as the host Troll is destroyed (`hostDestroyed`, announced by the destruction), before the Purse returns to the staging area and attaches to another Troll (rr.timing-of-ability-resolution: a Response to the destruction is offered on that event; agent recommendation, no source says otherwise); when the first player does not claim it, it returns. The Key's claim (131043:A:2) is offered after a player's attack damaged its host Troll (`enemyDamagedByAttack`), a Troll that is engaged being the only one that can be damaged. A claim takes the objective from its host or from the encounter discard pile (`ClaimObjective fromHost`).
- **Troll Cave** (131037:A:2; `literal`). The first player spends 5 Baggins resources from Bilbo's pool (2 when Bilbo has the Troll Purse attached) as the travel cost, and only while Bilbo has the Troll Key attached. It is in the victory display only when explored, so the players who advance by 2B without exploring it win without the three treasures (131025:B:1; nothing is stored: no log). The forum thread above reports the same.
- **Hungry Troll** (131054:A:1; `literal`). The first player chooses the Troll in the staging area and engages it himself; the card gains Surge only when no Troll was in the staging area as it resolved (so a lone staged Troll is engaged and there is no Surge); the Troll's Forced lines then run (the sack and the return at the end of the combat phase).
- **Return to the staging area** (131032:A:3 and its twins; `literal`). At the end of the combat phase the engaged player may raise his threat by 1; if he does the Troll stays engaged, otherwise it returns to the staging area.
- **Hobbit-lands, Dreary Hills and Lone-Lands** (131038:A:1, 131039:A:1, 131040:A:1; `recommended`). The three print the same trigger: "After placing 1 or more progress tokens on ..." (Hobbit-lands a Response, the other two Forced). All three are read the same way: they fire on the placement that explores the location too (`progressPlacedOnSelf` for the Forced records; the Response hears the card leave play explored, the same moment, `rr.explored`). The reason is the card itself: Hobbit-lands has 1 quest point, so every placement on it explores it, and a reading in which the exploring placement is silent would leave its Response dead. The rule spans do not settle it: `rr.explored` and `rr.forced` do not say whether a trigger on the placement that explores still resolves. No FAQ, Hall of Beorn or Vision of the Palantir page speaks of these cards (https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/03/25/we-must-away-ere-break-of-day/, https://hallofbeorn.com/LotR/Details/We-Must-Away-TH read). Alternative: the exploring placement is silent for all three (Hobbit-lands then never fires). Listed in Readings to confirm.
- **Pathless Country 04044 and Hives and Hives 900509 against the Western Lands cards** (`docs/scenarios/dwarrowdelf/road-to-rivendell.md`, `docs/scenarios/shadows-of-mirkwood/conflict-at-the-carrock.md`). Pathless Country's Forced line acts on the card's own progress tokens, so once the placement explores it there is nothing left to remove; its file reads the exploring placement as silent (`literal`; the one forum answer, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/63041-pathless-country, is a player, no FFG answer), and that reading leaves its line alive on every placement that does not explore it (5 quest points). Hobbit-lands has 1 quest point, so its print shows that these words fire on the exploring placement (every placement explores it); that is why the three Western Lands cards fire there. Hives and Hives ("Forced: After any amount of progress is placed on Hives and Hives, deal 1 damage to each character in play.") removes no progress and is the same case as Dreary Hills, but main reads it the opposite way (silent on the exploring placement; `rr.forced`, agent recommendation). It is left as it is, because another scenario's saved games would move; which reading both follow is the user's decision.
- **Dreary Hills' and Lone-Lands' Response** (131039:A:2, 131040:A:2; `literal`). The Response of an encounter card may be triggered when it changes nothing, since the rule that withholds such an ability names player cards (`rr.triggered-ability`). In this scenario Bilbo is always in play while the game runs, so no game reaches the case.
- **Pay-to-cancel Shadow effects** (131051:A:2, 131054:A:2; `literal`). "The first player may spend 1 (2) Baggins resources to cancel this effect": the Shadow effect runs in a frame that offers the cancel as it begins, paid from Bilbo Baggins' pool (the only pool of the Baggins sphere), by the first player (rr.cancel).
- **The Sack pick of Troll Camp** (131036:A:2; `literal`). The Action's pick reaches a Sack card attached to a character by an encounter card (no controller, not claimed): `PickSpec.onCharacter`. The removed card goes back into the Sack deck (hob.sack-x).
- **Bert's bar** (131033:A:1; `literal`). Only the play of an attachment card on a Troll is barred (flag `noAttachmentPlays`); an objective or an encounter card an effect attaches (the Key, the Purse, a Sack card) still attaches.
- **Gandalf** (131050:A:1; `literal`). Six Sack cards print "(excluding Gandalf)"; A Worn Sack prints none and no span bars Gandalf from every Sack (`hob.sack-x`, `hob.sack-x-example`), so a hero titled Gandalf with the most resources can take it.
- **Treachery and location lines** (`literal`, nothing open): No Campfire (131055:A:2) and Roast 'Em or Boil 'Em? (131053:A:1: 2 damage to each ally, not 1 and then 1) are read as printed; the card gaining Doomed 2 (131051:A:1) raises each player's threat by 2 as it is revealed (`rr.doomed-x`).

- **The table** (`docs/design-system-table.md`, Piles; the Table step of `docs/agents/agent-brief.md`, Scenario procedure). Each zone or widget below exists because a record creates it, and each is read in `src/play/`.
  - The Sack deck (131023:A:1, `hob.sack-deck`) is a pile of the second slot (`sackDeck`: `src/play/store/pile-ids.ts`, layout.ts, `pile-piece.native.tsx`): a back with its count, facedown, opening no list.
  - A readout (131023:A:1, 131032:A:2): the top-bar dial "Sacks" (`src/play/readouts/hobbit/sack.ts`, tested in `sack.test.ts`) counts the Sack cards left in the deck of the 7 the Setup builds and says how many are attached. It is a plain count with no ring and no warning: the full deck is the start and an empty deck is good news (Sack X is ignored, `hob.sack-x`). A tap reads the two numbers.
  - Stage 2B (131024:B:1, B:2; `src/play/readouts/hobbit/roast-mutton.ts`): progress on it discards encounter cards and never fills its quest point, so the table shows no quest-point strip on it and the advance overlay says "Advances when no Troll is in play, or the encounter deck is empty (N left)".
  - The deciding number of a Sack tie (131044:A:1 to 131050:A:1; `src/play/choice/sack-rows.ts`): each tied character's row shows the stat the card compares (the printed Attack, the attachments, the resources).
  - A held dial for each seat: none. No Sack card or Troll line keeps a number per player; the Baggins resources are on Bilbo Baggins' own card.
  - The held card under its host (131044:A:2 to 131050:A:2): a Sack card attached to a character is drawn under it, in the play area and in the staging area, as any attachment is, whoever controls the host; read with `pnpm browser shot "/standalone/table/{save}" --fixture day-lost --at 62 --size 1280x720 --tap text=Continue` (A Worn Sack and A Strong Sack under their heroes). The Key and the Purse (131042:A:1, 131043:A:1) lie under the Troll they are attached to, and under Bilbo Baggins once claimed.
  - The owner of a pick that spans seats: none. The first player makes every choice the scenario asks (the target of a Sack card, the Troll a Key or Purse attaches to, the Troll Hungry Troll engages), so the sentences name no player (`src/play/words/asks/we-must-away-ere-break-of-day.ts`).
