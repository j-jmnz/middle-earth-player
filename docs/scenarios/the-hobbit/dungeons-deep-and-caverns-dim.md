# Dungeons Deep and Caverns Dim

Deck Q0A.3 / E0A.3. Sets Dungeons Deep and Caverns Dim and Misty Mountain Goblins (`hob.setup-dungeons-deep-and-caverns-dim`); the riddle area is `hob.riddle-area`.

- Gollum and Bilbo's Magic Ring: DragnCards stages them; stage 2A's own Setup (131030:A:1) searches the encounter deck for them and places them in the riddle area, so they start in the encounter deck (`data/overrides.json`, scenarioPiles; printed card).
- The Goblins' Caves in Easy: DragnCards' E0A.3 holds 1, the Easy Mode sheet removes 1 of 3 (Easy 2, Hall of Beorn agrees); the sheet wins (`easy-mode.the-hobbit`, overrides scenarioEasy).
- Hall of Beorn's pile counts of the Misty Mountain Goblins set needed DragnCards' rows resolved by pack number: DragnCards names the pack "The Hobbit - Over Hill and Under Hill" (`overrides.dragnPackAliases`).
- **The riddle area and the two stages** (`hob.riddle-area`, `ltp.quest-advancement`, `faq.progress-without-advancing`; `span-settled`). Stage 1A's Setup adds Lake in the Cavern and creates the riddle area; stage 2A's Setup then searches the encounter deck for Gollum and Bilbo's Magic Ring, places Gollum and Bilbo in the area and attaches the Ring to Bilbo (131030:A:1; Gollum and the Ring are in the deck at the start, see above). Each quest card follows the ordinary rule: its Setup on side A, then it is flipped, so both play as 1B (14 quest points) and 2B (9) from the start. Progress past the quest points stays on the card, and the players advance to stage 3 at once, when the second of the two is complete (`rr.quest-advancement`: "as soon as"; a play report at https://talesfromthecards.wordpress.com/2014/03/13/thorin-and-company-dungeons-deep-and-caverns-dim/ advanced the moment 2B reached 9). While Bilbo is in the area the first player still controls him, but he cannot quest, attack or take attachments played by the players, takes damage only from Gollum and defends only against Gollum (`hob.riddle-area`; the stage's record sets `cannotQuest`, `cannotAttack`, `noPlayerAttachments` and `bilboLimitedToGollum` on him, `span-settled`). The Setup text "create a riddle area" is no data step: `createRiddleArea` is parsed (`scripts/data/normalize/setup-text.ts`) but the scenario file does not carry it, so the records place the cards.
- **What leaves the area at stage 3** (131031:B:1, B:2; `researched`, community only). Stage 3B's "The first player gains control of Bilbo Baggins" and "Gollum engages the first player" are the quest effects that take the two out of the area (the rulesheet: cards in the area "cannot leave the riddle area except through specific quest card effects"; the records give no other way out, so no player or encounter card effect moves them), with the Ring still attached to Bilbo, and stage 2 is removed with the stage 1 card. After that Bilbo can quest, defend and be healed, and Gollum is an enemy the players can engage and attack (the Tales from the Cards report above, 3B). The alternative, the area staying until Gollum is destroyed, has no source.
- **Gollum follows the first player token** (131031:B:2; `researched`: an official booklet on the same words, one community answer). "Gollum engages the first player" is its own paragraph of stage 3B, no When Revealed effect, and Gollum engages each new first player as the token passes (the Refresh phase), as the same words do on Gollum of The Passage of the Marshes (`mec112.gollum-smeagol`: "after he engages the first player during the refresh phase") and of Mount Doom. A BoardGameGeek answer to https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1243937/dungeons-deep-and-caverns-dim-fire-gollum-engages (read through a search summary only, the page refused the fetch) says the same: a passive ability, so he follows the token. The alternative, one engagement when stage 3 is revealed, has no source: a line with no timing word is a constant effect.
- **Answering a riddle** (`hob.riddles`, `hob.riddles-steps-2-and-3`; `span-settled`). The first player chooses between the card's ordinary effect and its riddle only when the card is revealed from the encounter deck: a riddle card dealt as a shadow card, put into play by a search (Come Down Little Bird) or discarded by It Likes Riddles? gives no choice at that time. Answering replaces resolving the card, and the card goes to the encounter discard pile (the rulesheet's example, and a forum answer: "you either answer the riddle or resolve the card as normal, not both", https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/250750-riddles-quest-in-ohauh/). The first player names the items, then shuffles his deck and discards the top X, then places 1 progress on stage 2 for each discarded card that matches every named item. The card types are ally, event, attachment and treasure; the spheres leadership, spirit, lore, tactics and Baggins; a card with no sphere (neutral cards, treasures) matches no named sphere. A riddle names before it shuffles, so no card effect can set the top of the deck (https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/74108-hobbit-quest-3-issues).
- **An answered riddle card is not added to the staging area** (`hob.riddles`: the rulesheet's example, "adding Wild Wargs to the staging area or answering the riddle"; `span-settled`). The card is added only when the first player resolves it, so a Forced or Response that hears a card added to the staging area is not heard for an answered riddle card.
- **Naming a cost** (`hob.riddles-steps-2-and-3` says "any number"; `recommended`). The tray offers 0 to 6, the printed costs of the player cards (an X or no cost never matches a named number, so naming a higher number only throws the guess away). Sources read: the two spans, the FAQ (`faq.*` has no riddle entry), https://visionofthepalantir.com/2019/09/04/dungeons-deep-and-caverns-dim/ (cost named, no ruling): none says more.
- **Bilbo's Magic Ring** (131079:A:2; `span-settled`). The action is used while answering, after the top cards are discarded, once for each Baggins resource spent: each spends 1 resource from Bilbo's pool and discards 1 more card, checked for a match like the others (the rulesheet's example spends two resources one after the other). Gollum's Forced is read once the riddle ends, so extra discards can still find the match (same example). Only the first player uses it (`span-settled`, `rr.cost`, `hob.riddles-steps-2-and-3`): `rr.action` lets any player initiate an objective's Action, but the Baggins resource comes from Bilbo's pool, Bilbo is the first player's (`hob.riddle-area`), and `rr.cost` says a cost is paid with game elements the paying player controls, so no other player can pay it. The extra card is the riddle's own additional discard, from the deck of the first player who answers (the example spends the resources from Bilbo and discards from Tom's deck, Tom being the first player). The Ring here is an encounter card, not a player card: Bilbo's "cannot gain resources from player card effects" does not stop its A:3 adding a resource (`literal`; the Ring is not a treasure, `hob.treasure-ohauh`). A:3 prints "Exhaust ... and raise each player's threat by 2 to add 1 Baggins resource": both parts are its cost, paid before the effect and whatever happens to it (`literal`).
- **Gollum's attack on Bilbo** (131074:A:1; `recommended`, community only). Each answered riddle with no match is one attack, with Gollum's printed Attack, no shadow card, and Bilbo as the defender: nothing else can defend while Gollum is in the area (`hob.riddle-area`: "defend (except against Gollum)"). Bilbo exhausts to defend, as any defender does (`rr.step-6-4-1`); an exhausted Bilbo leaves the attack undefended, and its damage is dealt to Bilbo, the only hero in the area (`rr.step-6-4-3`, which would let the first player pick a hero, is read against the card, which says "attacks Bilbo Baggins"). So two failed riddles in one round hurt twice, and Bilbo's loss ends the game (`131001:A:3`). Sources read: https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/69995-hobbit-3-defending-against-gollum and https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/75414-defending-bilbo-in-dungeons-deep-and-caverns-dim (community: Bilbo exhausts, an exhausted Bilbo takes it undefended; "Bilbo does not exhaust" is the minority reading; nobody says where undefended damage goes) and https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/89499-hobbits-saga-riddles-area-question. No official answer.
- **Bilbo as the hero for damage that is not Gollum's** (131030:B:3, `hob.riddle-area`; `recommended`). While the stage says Bilbo cannot take damage from cards other than Gollum, he is not one of the heroes a player may pick for an undefended attack of another enemy or for "deal/assign damage to a character" of the player's choice (131070's Shadow, Grip, Grab! Pinch, Nab!). `rr.cannot` makes the bar absolute, and a hero that takes no damage cannot take "all damage from the attack" (`rr.step-6-4-3`, `ltp.undefended-attack`: "to one of the heroes"), so picking him would cancel the attack for free. The other heroes of the seat remain the choices; when Bilbo is the seat's only hero, the attack goes undefended and deals no damage (the rule for a seat with no hero to take it: "If you have no heroes, there's no damage to be placed", GrandSpleen, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/204228-undefended-attack-on-a-player-with-no-heroes). No span, `faq.*` entry or Hall of Beorn text covers a hero that cannot take damage; the threads on this quest (https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/75414-defending-bilbo-in-dungeons-deep-and-caverns-dim, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/89499-hobbits-saga-riddles-area-question, https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/72859-dungeons-deep-and-caverns-dim) only quote the card's "cannot ... take damage from cards other than Gollum", and the Hall of Beorn page (https://hallofbeorn.com/LotR/Details/Dungeons-Deep-and-Caverns-Dim-Nightmare-DDaCDN) has no ruling. The reading covers an undefended attack and a chosen-damage effect. Archery damage is not reached in this scenario: no card of it has the Archery keyword. `rr.damage` makes assigning and placing two steps, which is the case for the alternative reading: the player names him and the placement is refused. The alternative (the player may name him and the damage is lost) has no source and favours the players.
- **Stage 2's bar, Not Fair! Not Fair! and The Wargs' Glade** (131030:B:2, 131084:A:1, 131077:A:1; `span-settled` by `rr.cannot`, against one community report). Stage 2B stops progress being "added to, or removed from, this quest except by answering riddles": the stage holds `noProgressPlaced` and `noProgressRemoved`, which bar card effects on that card only (the active location and stage 1 are not barred). Answering a riddle puts progress on it. `rr.cannot` ("the word 'cannot' is absolute, and cannot be countermanded by other abilities or effects") is a Rules Reference definition: Not Fair! Not Fair! answers no riddle, so its removal is countermanded by the stage's "cannot" and takes nothing off stage 2. The card still names a type and discards the top 3 encounter cards, and it still cannot be canceled. The alternative, the card being the specific text that removes progress, rests on one play report that removed 2 progress from 2B with it (https://talesfromthecards.wordpress.com/2014/03/13/thorin-and-company-dungeons-deep-and-caverns-dim/, round 12): a report of play, not a ruling, and it loses to the Rules Reference (`.claude/rules/authority.md`). Other searches (BoardGameGeek, the FFG forum archive, https://wardenofarnor.wordpress.com/2015/11/10/breaking-the-game-dungeons-deep-and-caverns-dim/) have no ruling on the pair. The Wargs' Glade's "the current quest" is stage 1 (stage 2 takes no removal from it); its Forced line is heard after any Creature enemy's attack, engaged or not.
- **The Wargs' Glade on a destroyed defender** (131077:A:1; `span-settled`, `rr.damage`, `rr.forced`): "After a character takes damage from an attack made by a Creature enemy" is true when the damage destroys the character (docs/brief.md, Core round): 1 progress comes off the current quest.
- **It Likes Riddles?** (131081:A:1; `literal`). The first player has no choice on this card (its When Revealed says "must choose to answer"). With at least 1 match he discards encounter cards until one with a riddle is discarded, and answers that riddle, which is not optional ("Then, answer that riddle"). With no match there is no second riddle. Each answered riddle counts for Gollum's Forced. The FFG forum thread https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/286051-it-likes-riddles-loophole argued for an optional second riddle; no staff answered and its poster accepted the card's wording. If the encounter deck runs out during the discards the discard pile is shuffled into it (`rr.encounter-deck`) and the discarding goes on (`recommended`; the search stops when no riddle card is left). The card itself is not "another card": shuffled back, it is not found and answered again.
- **What's In My Pocket?** (131083:A:1; `literal`): the first player answers the riddle and no card cancels that (the effect cannot be canceled).
- **Riddles on the other cards** (`literal`). Wild Wargs gets +2 Attack for the attack its shadow card is a riddle card (131076:A:1, until the attack ends, `rr.shadow-effects`); Great Gray Wolf's Forced, once dealt a riddle shadow card, returns every Creature enemy to the staging area at the end of the combat phase, itself included, even when it is gone by then (131075:A:1; `recommended`: the line says "all Creature enemies", and the Forced is a delayed effect). A riddle card dealt as a shadow card has no shadow effect (the encounter set prints none, https://visionofthepalantir.com/2019/09/04/dungeons-deep-and-caverns-dim/). At stage 3 "All riddle effects are ignored": the Riddle lines of the cards are void and the cards are resolved as ordinary cards with no choice. The two Forced lines on the shadow riddle remain (they are no riddle effect). Treachery cards gain surge at stage 3.
- **Extra reveals** (131029:B:2; `researched`, community only). The staging step reveals 1 more encounter card per player at the start of the step, so 2 per player in all, each with its riddle choice; the count is set before the step, so the reveals go on if stage 3 is reached part way (the Tales from the Cards report: "the number of cards is set before staging"). The extra cards stop with stage 3.
- **The table** (`readouts/the-hobbit/riddles.ts`; ledger lines 131029:A:1, 131030:A:1, 131030:B:2, 131030:B:3, 131031:B:1; test id `riddle-stage`). The riddle area is no zone the table draws: stage 2 is one top-bar dial after the seat dials, its progress against the 9 that completes it as the players' way out (the ring never warms; the note says how many more it needs and that only a riddle adds progress), and a tap shows the stage and Gollum. Bilbo Baggins stays among the first seat's heroes with the Ring drawn under him as any attachment is, and his seat's threat dial wears the mark "Riddle area" ("P1 · " first two-handed) with a note of what he cannot do there, and his preview lists each limit the stage sets on him. While Gollum attacks from the riddle area he is drawn in the engagement area as the attacker, with the hero he hits beside him; no shadow card is announced for that attack. While the first player decides whether to answer a riddle card just revealed, the quest figure leaves that card's threat out, since answering keeps it out of the staging area. Stage 3 takes both away. The riddle sheets are worded by `words/scenarios/the-hobbit/riddles.ts` (Choice tray, `docs/design-system-table.md`).
- **Chosen characters** (131061:A:1, A:2; `literal`). Goblin Driver's "1 character he controls" includes Bilbo: the card is an encounter card, so the riddle area's immunity does not apply, and the first player controls him.
