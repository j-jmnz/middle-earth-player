# Roam Across Rhovanion

- **Roam Across Rhovanion** (part 5, 12184). The campaign card's Setup: a player may search their deck for a unique boon ally, with Melanwar's line the first player removes 3 non-unique enemies other than Grey Mountain Giant from the encounter deck (`RemoveFromEncounterDeckIfLogged` with `except`), and once the quest card's Setup has put Urdug under the first player (`TakeControlOfSetAside`, `afterSetup`) the first player puts their trust in Urdug (`PutTrust`: a resource on him, `PlaceResourceTokens`) or a copy of Urdug's Cunning is shuffled into the encounter deck. The back of the card stays in the campaign zone: the same question after the first player claims an objective, Urdug gets +1 Willpower, Threat, Attack and Defense for each resource on him (either side, `resourcesOn`), and Deep is ignored. A claim is any of the three ways the players gain an objective: Tiny and Urdug gain control (`GainControlWhenFree` with `claim`, `rr.objective-cards`: "If a player claims an objective, then that player gains control of that objective"), Durin's Key is attached to a hero the first player chooses and Urdug's Horn to Urdug once nothing guards them (`ClaimWhenFree`, a settle sweep); each is announced as `claimed`. The Resolution: with 3 or more resources on Urdug each player earns Misplaced Trust, shuffled into their deck after the starting hand (`Earn` with `afterHand`), else Urdug's Cunning; then each player in turn, the first player first, chooses a location in the staging area, a different title from the players before them if able, and a copy of it joins the pool's encounter cards (`ChooseStagedLocationForPool`; a burden location is not offered, Abandoned Ruin joins as a burden). The stages: stage 1 sets Urdug under the first player with the other objectives set aside by the piles, and its active location explored with the first player controlling him advances the quest to a random stage 2; the three stages 2 are played in a random order, each once, and a defeated one leaves the game (`AdvanceToRandomStage`, copied onto the stage as `randomNext`, the game's seed; the quest then goes to stage 3). Each searches the encounter deck and discard pile for its guard (Grey Moorland, Grey Mountain Giant, Deep Ravine), adds it to the staging area and attaches the set-aside objective (Tiny, Urdug's Horn, Durin's Key) to it as a guarded objective; a search that finds no copy still adds the objective, unguarded, to be claimed at once. The players cannot travel to the Moorland guarding Tiny or the Ravine guarding the Key without 5 progress on the stage (`Restrict` travel with `guarding`), the Giant guarding the Horn takes no damage without it, and no stage 2 is defeated unless the players control what its line names. Stage 3 flips Urdug and Tiny to their enemy sides in the staging area, removing damage and keeping resources and attachments (`FlipToEnemySide`); quest progress cannot be placed from questing; a failed quest places the amount the threat would have risen by on the stage instead of raising threat (the Forced effect hears the failed quest, whose event carries the amount as X, waives the threat and `AddStageProgress`), and the players lose at 5 progress; the stage is never defeated by progress, and the players win when Urdug has as much damage as hit points (Indestructible keeps him in play). While Urdug is engaged he attacks at once at the beginning of the quest phase, and if that attack damages a character (`damagedByAttack`, `characterDestroyedByAttack`, whose events name the attacking enemy) Urdug and Tiny return to the staging area; while Tiny is in play Urdug cannot take damage. Deep Ravine's cap is on all the locations in the staging area together (`progressCapShared`). Sneaking Off (19044) puts Urdug under a location of the first player's choice as a guarded objective (`GuardReturnedBy`) or adds 5 Threat. User rulings (2026-11-05, 2026-11-06): (1) a search for the guard of a stage 2 that finds no copy (every copy is in play) still brings Tiny, the Horn or the Key into the staging area, unguarded, and the first player claims it at once (the settle sweep; a claim, so the campaign card asks about trust), so the stage can still be defeated; (2) "each enemy with 1 or more guarded attachments" (19049:B:1) counts any enemy that guards a card, the Giant guarding Urdug's Horn included, as well as one holding a player card under it (Hobgoblin); (3) Sneaking Off needs Urdug under the first player's control: with him already guarded in the staging area, or on his enemy side, it adds 5 Threat; (4) gaining control of Tiny or Urdug and Urdug returning after Sneaking Off are claims like the others, and each asks about trust again; (5) Deep Ravine's cap of 1 progress a round is in total across all the locations in the staging area. (6) Wild Creatures counts a player as unable to engage when every enemy in the staging area is barred to them, not only when the staging area holds no enemy; (7) at the Resolution (12184:B:4) a location title may repeat when fewer titles than players are in the staging area (a title, not a card, is "different"); (8) only players still in the game choose a location, and every player still earns Misplaced Trust or Urdug's Cunning. Words: `src/play/words/scenarios/rhovanion.ts`, the banner of the random stage in `src/play/store/outcome.ts`, Urdug's preview in `src/play/cards/rhovanion-preview.ts` and the Resolution's location choice in `src/campaign/resolution-view.ts`. Urdug counts wherever he is in play (the stat bonus, the trust and the Resolution read him engaged as well as in the staging area).
- **Urdug's attack on a hero it destroys** (19053:A:2; `span-settled`, `rr.damage`): "If this attack deals damage" holds when the damage destroys the hero: Urdug and Tiny return to the staging area.
