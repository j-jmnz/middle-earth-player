# Lost in Mirkwood

- **Lost in Mirkwood** (part 2, 12168). The four Objective-Locations of Searching for a Way Out (18029 to 18032) are objectives that count as locations: the players travel to them, they take progress as the active location (4 quest points, read from the scan; `data/overrides.json`) and an explored one goes to the victory display with its Victory 4 (`rr.victory`, user ruling, 2026-11-05), while they stay objectives for Guarded, the staging area and immunity (`printed.objectiveLocation`, `isLocationCard`). "Advance to the next stage A / C" is `AdvanceToLetterStage`; Haldan, dealt set aside, comes under the first player at stage 1's Setup (`TakeControlOfSetAside`); the Spiders of Mirkwood and Dol Guldur Orcs sets wait in their piles until stage 2 shuffles one in; the Resolution discards cards until X of those sets' cards are discarded and adds them to the pool (`DiscardToPoolFromSets`). X is the number of players still in the game ("one less player" for an eliminated seat, span-settled by `rr.player-elimination`), so a two-handed game that lost a seat adds 1, and the deck running dry is a different, rarer end. Progress on the quest while no Objective-Location is in play is discarded from the encounter deck and then the topmost Objective-Location of the discard pile enters play (`progressDiscards` and `PutObjectiveLocationAfterDiscard`). Stages 1 to 3 print no quest points: the quest advances only through the Objective-Locations, and "when the players advance from this stage" is the stage's `questCardDefeated` (a jump past stages names the stage it left, `stageLeft`). Stage 3A, Carried Away (18074): each player's chosen hero loses its tokens and attachments and lies facedown under the enemy discarded to (`CarryAwayHero`, the captive zone: in play, controlled by no one, `guardedHero`); a player is not eliminated while an enemy holds their only hero (`rr.player-elimination`: heroes killed); the enemies guarding a hero cannot take non-combat damage; a guard that leaves play in any way, defeated or discarded, returns the hero to its owner's control, exhausted (`ReleaseGuardedHero`, heard as `leftPlay`; user ruling, 2026-11-05), and advancing discards the heroes still held (`DiscardGuardedHeroes`). An enemy attacking a seat with no hero in play (every hero held) is undefended and deals no damage. "Guarding a card" on Unseen Danger's Shadow (18036:A:2) covers an enemy guarding an objective or a hero. Stage 3C, Truly Lost (18075): after each player has discarded to a non-objective location, each player's hand lies facedown under a different location of the staging area (an Objective-Location is one; `PlaceHandUnderLocation`, the actor chooses); a location gets +1 quest point for each facedown player card under it (`eachAttached` with `facedownUnder`), returns them to their owners' hands when explored in the staging area or as the active location; a location that leaves play unexplored leaves them facedown, out of hand, and advancing discards every facedown player card under a location (user ruling, 2026-11-05). A player may place their hand under any staging-area location with no hand under it yet (user ruling, 2026-11-05). Stage 4A, The Forest of Great Fear (18076, 8 quest points): the enemy with the most printed hit points in the set that stayed set aside enters the staging area as the nemesis, the first player choosing among a tie (`SearchNemesis`, `nemesis` until it leaves play); it gets +2 Threat, Attack and Defense, is immune to player card effects, cannot take damage with fewer than 8 progress on the stage, blocks the stage while in play, returns to the staging area and attacks the first player at the end of each round (`NemesisAttacks`), and defeating it wins the game. Melanwar's "if you quested unsuccessfully this round" (12169:A:2) is the `questFailed` condition: the seat's quest failed this round (willpower below the staging area's threat; a tie is not unsuccessful, user ruling, 2026-11-05: a quest with nothing committed against Threat above 0 is unsuccessful, against 0 Threat it is a tie and neither; `resolveQuest`), kept in `quested.failed` in a game whose cards read it. A hero whose owner was eliminated while it was held goes to that owner's discard pile when its guard leaves play instead of returning (`rr.player-elimination`); the cards returned from under a location are faceup in the hand again; only the facedown hands under a location are discarded on the advance, not a card attached facedown under another card. On the phone, each is cited to its ledger line (`docs/design-system-table.md`, Lost in Mirkwood and Choice tray): the preview of an enemy holding a hero and that seat's held dial (18074:B:2), of a location with a hand under it (18075:B:2, 18075:B:3) and of the nemesis (18076:B:1 to 18076:B:3), the Twilight Hall search offer and the claim of the freed treasure (12168:A:2), and an Objective-Location's quest-point badge and travel row (18029:A:2).

## Encounter card readings

- **Lost in Mirkwood encounter cards.** Unseen Danger (18036:A:1) discards the character it removed from the quest when the printed Threat of the card it discarded is greater than that character's Willpower, not the discarded encounter card. While Accursed Forest (18038:A:1) is in the staging area only 'when revealed' effects cannot be canceled (`rr.cancel`): a Shadow effect can still be canceled.
- **Stages 2A and 2C on a destroyed character** (18072:B:2, 18073:B:2; `span-settled`, `rr.damage`, `rr.forced`): "When a Spider (Orc) enemy attacks and damages a character" is heard when the damage destroys the character (docs/brief.md, Core round). At 18073:B:2 the player whose character it was discards the card, as for a character that survives, and it is that player in a game of two, not the first player.
- **Lost in Mirkwood stages 2A and 2C** (18072:A:1, 18073:A:1). The stage shuffles the encounter deck even when its encounter set has no card left to add, and that shuffle counts as resolved, so the players still reveal 1 encounter card per player (`rr.then`; `recommended`, agent recommendation, the same reading as Hazardous Terrain in `docs/scenarios/shadows-of-mirkwood.md`; Caleb Grace, ALeP 3.114 and 3.119, https://alongextendedparty.com/semi-official-errata-faq-1-10/, speak of effects that target nothing, not of this shuffle).
