# Mount Gundabad

- **Mount Gundabad** (part 8, 12193; `emce.gundabad-quest-deck`, `emce.bypassing`, `emce.setup-mount-gundabad`, `emce.caves-deck-gundabad`). The quest deck is the seven Exploring Gundabad stage 2s, shuffled with side A up beneath stage 1 (`ShuffleStages`) with no stage 3: each prints `FlipAtQuestPhase`, which marks it a stage of a quest deck (`stages[i].deck`), so the quest advances to the top of the deck, a stage defeated goes to the victory display (so does The Side Door) and the last one defeated wins. A stage arrives side A up and only its side A text works until it is flipped at the beginning of the quest phase (`FlipCurrentQuest`, `unflippedStage`, `src/rules/families/quest/order.ts`); stage 1B's Forced effect at the end of the planning phase looks at the top 2 stages, the first player puts 1 on the bottom (`LookAtStages` with `bottom`, which asks before the advance as Voyage's does) and advances. At the end of the combat phase the first player answers for the group whether to bypass the current stage (`BypassStage`; offered only with another stage in the quest deck): it loses its progress, goes to the bottom of the quest deck with side A up and the top stage is revealed, its When Revealed resolving; it is not defeated, so no "when this stage is defeated" Forced effect hears it (`stageLeft` `bypassed`). 19142 must be bypassed at the end of the combat phase with 4 or fewer quest cards in the victory display (the count includes The Side Door). Throat of the Mountain (`SearchQuestDeck`) lets the first player search the quest deck: the chosen stage becomes the current quest, side B up at once, and the quest deck, with the stage that was current put back in it as a bypassed stage is, is shuffled. Dagnir (X is 1 more than the quest cards in the victory display) takes at most X damage a round from effects other than a quest card's (`damageCap`, `damageCapSparesQuest`; the 5 damage of 19139:B:2 is neither held to the cap nor counted against it), and from 5 quest cards on is considered engaged with each player, as the Watcher in the Water is; Dagnir's Slave bars the damage of any effect but a quest card's (`cannotTakeNonQuestDamage`; combat damage is not an effect and still counts). Wormsbane is attached to a hero the first player chooses at Setup, Broken side up; The First Forge exhausts that hero when it becomes active and it cannot ready while The First Forge is the active location (`whileSourceActive`), and once explored the first player flips Wormsbane to its Reforged side (`FlipInPlace`) and attaches it to any hero; a hero that leaves play with Wormsbane attached loses the game (an attachment hears its hero leave play, `hostLeftPlay`). 19142:B:2 is the attacking player's Response (`answeredBy` `eventSeat`; an attack with several attackers offers it for each): when Dagnir has no hit points remaining the player may discard a Reforged Wormsbane attached to a hero they control to add her to the victory display, and the players win. The campaign card's Setup shuffles Dragon-sickness in with 6 or more treasures logged and the Melanwar and Beldis removals (2 non-unique locations and 1 Caves location, then 3 treacheries, Dragon-sickness among them); its side B ignores Discover and a unique location cannot be discarded by an encounter card's effect (`cannotBeDiscardedByEncounter` on the unique locations, read by the Discard verb; the Deep keyword is a rule and still discards). The Resolution takes a treasure of 3 (`ChooseRandomTreasure`), discovers each treasure the players control, removes A Growing Threat from the pool and, with 6 or more treasures logged, adds Dragon-sickness to it. Dragon-sickness attaches to a character with a treasure attached (the first player chooses) and every unguarded treasure in play, on a character or free in the staging area, is attached to that character (`AttachTreasuresToHost`; a Restricted overflow is discarded); after the character exhausts its controller raises their threat by 1 for each treasure attached to it. Each stage's Forced effects are heard as stated: "after characters are committed" as the staging step begins (`stagingStepBegins`); the first treachery revealed each round cannot have its When Revealed canceled (`UncancelableWhenRevealed` `firstRevealedEachRound`, noted as it is revealed); Foreboding Darkness limits each player to 1 card played a phase (`LimitPlaysPerPhase`, `state.played`; a card put into play is not played); Frenzied Warrens discards the top 5 cards and adds a random enemy among them (`StageRandomDiscarded`); the Caves location a stage defeated brings is the first player's search (`ActivateFromCaves`). Readings (decided, none put to the user): (1) the stage 1B reveals the first stage 2 at the end of the planning phase and flips it at once, since the quest phase begins as it arrives (the engine hears the beginning of a phase before the end of the one before, so the stage's own Forced effect has passed); stages revealed later flip at the next quest phase. (2) "Flip over the top card of the quest deck" (19140:B:2) is the advance to the top card after the look, side A up like every revealed stage 2, flipped at the next quest phase; the alternative, turning it to side B at once, is what Throat's "flip it over" says of the card it chooses, and 19140 says no such thing of the stage it brings. (3) The stage Throat replaces returns to the quest deck as a bypassed one does, rather than leaving the game, since nothing removes it. (4) A bypass is the first player's answer for the group, and "the players may" with no other stage to reveal offers nothing (rr.effects). (5) "Quest cards in the victory display" counts The Side Door and every stage 2 defeated, the only quest cards that scenario puts there. (6) The Resolution reads its lines in printed order, so the treasure just discovered (B:2, B:3) counts toward B:5's 6 recorded; the alternative, the log as it stood, would drop Dragon-sickness from the pool for a log of 5. (7) "Wormsbane he controls" (19142:B:2) is a Wormsbane attached to a hero the player controls; the Broken side is not enough. (8) Dagnir's Slave bars only effects of cards other than a quest card, so the attack on her and the quest card's damage still land; Dagnir "against each engaged player" (Wilderland's Bane) is the player she is engaged with, or every player once she is considered engaged with each, and with no attack her engagement cost falls by 30 until the end of the round (`noneReturned`). (9) The Side Door's advance asks the first player even with two players, and the choice is not owned. (10) The words (`src/play/words/scenarios/gundabad.ts`, `gundabad-beats.ts`, `src/play/choice/gundabad-sheets.ts`), told only in a game whose quest is a quest deck (`inGundabad`, read from `stages[i].deck`, never a card code), so a record of another game with the same shape (Ominous Fate, Lost Armory, Passage Through Mirkwood's pick) keeps its ordinary words; Dragon-sickness is told in every game since it joins the later scenarios' encounter decks. The seven stages print one title, so a sheet, a beat and the refusal name a stage with a short reading of its side B read from its records ("Exploring Gundabad (attacks)", `stageName`); the printed subtitles are not in the card data. A pick names the player two-handed ("Player 1:", "Player 1, Dagnir's Hoard:") and the first player's choice for the group is not owned, so the sheet adds who chooses. The bypass note says the progress the stage loses, that it goes to the bottom of the quest deck and is not defeated (no victory display, and its "when defeated" effect does not happen, said only for a stage that has one), that the next stage is revealed and how many wait, and what staying keeps; a forced bypass leads with why it is forced and a stage the look or the search chose is listed by its reading (the look adds "to the bottom"); the flip at the beginning of the quest phase, a bypass and a stage arriving side A are told in the banner from the state before and after (no step of their own); the first treachery of a round is noted as it is revealed (`firstTreachery`); the Responses of The First Forge, Throat of the Mountain, Dagnir's Hoard and the Great Worm say what they do and what passing leaves undone, and the two that leave the game unwinnable (passing The First Forge's, which keeps Wormsbane Broken so the players cannot win without the Reforged side, and the Great Worm's) arm the Pass (`passCaution`); a row that would destroy a character arms "Tap again" ("is destroyed", or "falls and the game is lost" for a hero who carries Wormsbane), for a stage's damage to a questing character and for each damage of an AssignDamage, and Eyes in the Dark's discard of that hero says the game is lost (read from the engine, `discardLoss`); the refusal of a second card in a phase names the stage, the limit, the player and the phase (the `playReason` family hook); the Resolution's treasure choice lists what Dragon-sickness does (it joins when the treasures recorded, this one and the ones the players control reach 6, and it says the count), then that A Growing Threat leaves the pool (only when the pool holds it), the order the settled sentence uses, and declining the treasure says the same counted without it (`afterwardsWords`, read from the record's `codes` and `when`). (11) Dagnir's Hoard's Response shuffles a deck only for the player who searches it (the shuffle is part of the search, "search his deck ... and shuffle his deck"); a player who declines leaves hand and deck as they are. (12) The Great Worm prints no quest points and the other stages are all defeated or bypassed before it can be the last, so a game is won by slaying Dagnir with the Reforged Wormsbane: every golden win does, and a game whose Wormsbane never reaches a hero who can slay her is a long game or a lost one. (13) The Reforged side is Restricted (19144:A:1), so a hero who already carries two Restricted attachments when it is flipped onto him discards one of the three (rr.restricted); "treasures the players control" in the Resolution's count are the controlled treasures not yet in the log.
- An enemy in the staging area that is considered engaged with a player is engaged with him for a card that reads "engaged with", and is no engagement event (docs/brief.md, Considered to be engaged).
