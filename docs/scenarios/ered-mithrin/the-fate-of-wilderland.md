# The Fate of Wilderland

- **The Fate of Wilderland** (part 9, 12195; the last part; `emce.campaign-log`, `emce.boon-allies`, `emce.treasure`, `rr.engaged`). Stages 1 to 3 have no quest points, so questing never defeats them: the quest is fought on The Goblins' Assault, an objective in the staging area that holds progress. Stage 1B advances at the end of the round when The Goblins' Assault holds 2 plus 2 per player (after it gets 1 progress for each enemy in the staging area, Urdug among them, engaged enemies not), stage 2B at 4 plus 4 per player (it also gets 1 progress after any enemy attacks), and stage 3B makes the players lose at 8 plus 8 per player (after 1 progress for each enemy in play, engaged ones included): "X or more" is at least X. The players win by destroying Urdug (19157:A:3, a triggered effect of Urdug's own). The Setup and each When Revealed of a stage discard from the top of the encounter deck until X enemies, X the number of players, are discarded and add them to the staging area (`DiscardUntilEnemies` as a quest card's Setup runs in a frame after the deal). Urdug: his Defense is +2 for each other enemy in play, in the staging area or engaged; he cannot be dealt damage unless he has resolved an attack in the combat phase now under way (`attackedThisPhase`: in the combat phase his entry, `id` or `id@seat` when he attacks each player, is among the attacks resolved; an immediate attack, Urdug's Command from the staging area, counts in the phase it was made in and is noted apart from those attacks, which it does not use up, only in a game with a card that asks), so the players must let him attack first. He cannot leave the staging area (`cannotBeEngaged`). With 5 resources per player on The Heroes' Defense (at least) he is considered engaged with each player as the Watcher in the Water is: he adds no Threat, nobody engages him, any character may attack him and he attacks each player. Its Response places a resource after any enemy is destroyed, wherever it was; Gate of Gundabad in the staging area bars every placing (`noResourcesPlaced`, read by `PlaceResourceTokens`): the Response is then not offered (its only effect is barred, so its record needs the flag unset), and Dwarven Watchtower, whose resource option requires the flag unset on The Heroes' Defense (`namedFlag`), offers only the progress removal (a choice with one option that can change the game is not asked). When Urdug attacks, the shadow card is dealt first and then the top card of the encounter deck is discarded; its printed Threat is his bonus for that attack only. The "either exhaust or place progress" Forced effects (Urdug's Elite, Fierce Vanguard, Warg-rider) are answered by the player the enemy engaged or attacked, Slopes of Gundabad's by the player who revealed it, Dwarven Watchtower's by the first player. Gate of Gundabad's Travel searches the victory display as well as the encounter deck and its discard pile (`SearchStageCard`; the card is moved to the staging area). Hilltop Battlements' Forced effect discards the top X cards and adds every enemy among them (`StageDiscarded`). Dark Clouds' +1 Threat is an encounter card's lasting effect (`ModifyStat` target `stagingCards`, a class holder): an enemy or location that reaches the staging area later in the phase has it too, and one that leaves it loses it. Its "if the players quest unsuccessfully this phase" waits for this phase's quest (`Delay` until `questFailed`): revealed in the encounter phase, the quest is behind it and nothing is placed. "Stage number of the main quest" is 1, 2 or 3. The Goblins' Assault gets its progress after the players quest unsuccessfully, 1 for each player: the Forced effect prints no limit, and the group's one failed quest is heard by the first player's seat alone, so the progress is placed once (a quest heard by every seat would place it for each). The campaign card's Setup lets each player in turn (all of them, not only the first to take one) search their deck for a boon or treasure card of any type and add it to the hand after the mulligan (`SearchBoonOrTreasureToHand`, a pool boon being in the deck by then), then removes 3 non-unique enemies (Turayn's line) and 3 treacheries (Beldis's line) from the encounter deck. Deep and Discover are ignored. Winning runs the one Resolution line, "The players win the campaign!": it asks nothing and writes nothing, no part follows (`nextStep` is `finished`) and the screen's last sentence completes the campaign. The win shows as any campaign's last win does (The Caves of Nibin-Dûm, the finished campaign's path and Resolution screen). "5 resources for each player" and "X or more" count the players still in the game: a seat eliminated by its threat is no longer a player, so Urdug is engaged with each remaining player at the lower number, and the stage's number falls with it. The words (`src/play/words/scenarios/fate.ts`, `fate-beats.ts`) are told only in a game that deals one of the scenario's own verbs (`RemoveProgressFrom`), and read the records' shape and the numbers on the table: each choice that makes a player pay or place progress says why it opened and what The Goblins' Assault holds ("3 of 4 progress, +2 at the end of the round (1 for each enemy in the staging area | in play). At 4 the goblins push the quest on", "At 16 the game is lost" on the last stage; stage 2, which gets its progress from attacks, has no "+N"), the numbers read from the stage's records and the table; on the last stage a row, or Pass and the Watchtower's resource row, arms "Tap again" when what the objective holds, the progress the row places and the end of the round's progress (1 for each enemy in play, Urdug always among them) reach the number that loses; the Watchtower's Response and The Heroes' Defense's name both counts, and the Watchtower names the card that bars a resource; each branch names its card ("Fierce Vanguard gets +2 Attack until the end of the round", "Return Warg-rider to the staging area: it does not attack you this phase"); Urdug's attack names whom it is on, what a destroyed character brings the objective to when that loses on the last stage (Urdug's Command), and the card discarded and its Threat; the Attack on Urdug says his Defense and what raises it, that he takes no damage until he has attacked this phase (and he has not), or that he can be damaged and that destroying him wins; the banner adds the objective's count, the resources placed, the card discarded and its Threat, and Dark Clouds' effect (no progress when it is revealed after the quest).
- An enemy in the staging area that is considered engaged with a player is engaged with him for a card that reads "engaged with", and is no engagement event (docs/brief.md, Considered to be engaged).
