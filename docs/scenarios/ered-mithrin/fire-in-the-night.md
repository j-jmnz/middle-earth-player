# Fire in the Night

- **Fire in the Night** (part 6, 12187; `emce.side-quests`, `emce.side-quests-in-play`). Encounter side quests are quest cards with an encounter card back (card type `encounter-side-quest`). The eight are dealt as a deck of their own (the `sideQuestDeck` pile, `NAMED_PILES.sideQuests`; the quest card's Setup shuffles it with the game's seed), and Dagnir is dealt into the staging area. Their quest points (5 for Draw Her Fire, Defend the Town and Rout the Goblins, 10 for the others) are read from the scans (`data/overrides.json`: Hall of Beorn exports only their Victory 10). Stage 1B's Forced effect at the end of the planning phase reveals the top side quest into the staging area (`RevealSideQuest`, a reveal frame of its own: no Response is offered to cancel it, and a Response that cancels the When Revealed effects of a side quest is barred, `emce.side-quests`) and Dagnir attacks Hrogar's Hill (`AttackStage`): an attack outside the combat phase against the first player's seat with no shadow card, which the first player may defend; the damage of an attack left undefended, and the damage beyond a defender's remaining hit points (the Stone-troll reading), is placed on the stage (`pendingAttack.toStage`, `excessToStage`; `ExcessToStage` is the Shadow of Powerful in Wrath, `undefendedToStage` on `AttackFromStaging` the When Revealed of The Dragon's Fury). Damage to or healing of the main quest card is `DamageCard` and `Heal` with the target `questStage`. At the beginning of the quest phase, once the Forced effects have resolved, the first player is asked once a round whether a side quest in the staging area is the current quest until the end of the phase (a frame of its own, `askCurrentQuest`, options "main" and each side quest; `MakeCurrentQuest` sets the `currentQuest` flag and the trigger `becameCurrentQuest` is heard); progress that remains after the active locations goes on it instead of the main quest, what it cannot take is discarded, and a full side quest is defeated by a settle sweep (`defeatFullSideQuests`): it is added to the victory display, the quest deck does not advance and its `questDefeated` records answer (a Response is the first player's, as a Response of any encounter card is). A game that deals no side quest deck reads none of this. Stage 1B cannot be defeated while Dagnir has hit points remaining, the players win when he is destroyed (`enemyDestroyed`, as for Fire-drake) and lose when the stage holds as much damage as it has quest points (`loseAtDamage`). Dagnir takes no more than X damage a round from effects that are not a quest card's (`damageCap` with `damageCapSparesQuest` on Dagnir alone: damage dealt by a side quest or a quest card, `DamageSource` "quest", is not capped and does not count toward his cap; every other card's cap holds quest damage too), is never engaged (`cannotBeEngaged`), and with 3 side quests in the victory display is considered engaged with each player as the Watcher in the Water and Cold-Drake are (`engagedWithAll`, no Threat, any player's characters attack him). Fortify the Defense is `defenseIgnored` on Dagnir (read by `defenseAsDefender`), Rally the Woodmen `alliesCannotAttack`, Draw Her Fire `shadowsImmune`, Hold the Door `threatReductionBarred` ("cannot be reduced", any effect). The campaign card's Setup puts Beldis under the first player and removes 3 non-unique locations with Turayn's line and 3 non-unique enemies with Isolde's; side A also ignores the Deep keyword. Beldis's Action (`anyPlayer`) spends 1 resource of the player who triggers it, marks that player's next box beside her in the campaign log (`MarkBeldisUse`, the condition `beldisBox`: four a player, `campaignFacts.beldisUses`, which a won game writes back) and heals 3 damage from a character. The Resolution: Beldis joins the pool or the log takes "Beldis's care has bolstered the heroes' spirits" (`BoonAllyOrLogLine`), with at least 5 progress on Hrogar's Hill the first player chooses 1 of 3 random undiscovered treasures (`ChooseRandomTreasure` with the condition `stageProgress`), the treasures the players control are discovered, and each player may remove a non-burden card from the pool (`RemoveFromPool`, `choice: "eachPlayerNonBurden"`). On `12187` the inert line is the Note (line 4) and the Deep rule is a record. Rulings (user, 2026-11-06): (1) Dagnir's cap leaves out the damage of the main quest card and of side quests, so Face the Dragon's 5 damage is not capped; all other damage, combat included, is; (2) at 3 side quests in the victory display Dagnir is considered engaged with each player and adds no Threat to the staging area, as the other enemies considered engaged with each player do; (3) "Dagnir cannot leave the staging area" is that no player can engage him, with no separate bar on moving him; (4) the Response of a defeated side quest is optional and offered to the first player; (5) only the placing of progress is redirected to the current side quest: every other effect that names "the current quest" still reads the main quest card; (6) Powerful in Wrath reads the active location's Threat before it is discarded (the surge condition too, so a Threat that is the location's own constant is still read), and gains surge when there is no active location or its Threat is 0; (7) Hold the Door bars every threat reduction, whatever its source. Each has a test (`src/campaign/scenarios/fire-in-the-night/cards.test.ts`, `stages.test.ts`). The table has no side quest deck or current-quest mark yet (issue #187). Words (`src/play/words/scenarios/night.ts`, tests `src/play/words/scenarios/night.test.ts` and `src/play/choice/night-sheets.test.ts`): the first player's question at the beginning of the quest phase names the side quests and says what keeping Hrogar's Hill does; a side quest revealed from its deck is told in the banner under the end of the planning phase as "Revealed ... from the side quest deck: it is added to the staging area.", with "Its When Revealed effect cannot be canceled by player cards." when it has one; the defense sheet of the attack on Hrogar's Hill names the first player as the defender, says no shadow card is dealt, what a defender and an undefended attack do to the Hill and how much damage it holds, shows "+N to Hrogar's Hill" on a defender whose hit points the damage exceeds, and arms the row and Leave undefended when the damage would fill the Hill; the commit sheet names a side quest that is the current quest; the first player's question shows each side quest's progress (and that Dagnir then attacks, for Draw Her Fire); the Response sheet of a defeated side quest says the quest deck does not advance and that passing gives the Response up; Beldis's Action row states the boxes the acting player has left (of 4) and that a win records the mark, and the Resolution's confirm sentence lists, for each player whose count rose this game, the boxes marked beside Beldis ("Player 1 marks 1 box beside Beldis (3 of 4 marked)", solo "You mark ..."); the Resolution's removal says each player is asked in turn, the first player first, that only non-burden cards are offered and that Remove nothing keeps the pool as it is.
