# Design system — Night

Visual authority for chrome. Product rules stay in [brief.md](brief.md). Structure stays in [architecture.md](architecture.md). Every screen phase uses these tokens and components. Do not invent a palette, a type scale, a radius, or a second styling system.

This replaces the earlier Parchment system. Where `docs/phases/phase-17-design-system.md` says "system face only", "no downloaded font", or "light only", this file supersedes it. The one embedded typeface and the dark theme are deliberate decisions. The reference mockups are `Fellowship Companion v2.dc.html` in the design project.

## Direction

A game, not a form. The phone is a lamp-lit table at night: deep blue-black, warm gold light, parchment where something is written down.

- **Night** is the page. Every screen sits on a scene backdrop that gives depth: a gradient sky, a soft glow, sometimes a mountain ridge.
- **Gold is the Ring.** Gold marks one thing per screen: the current node, the primary button, the selected choice. If two things are gold, one of them is wrong.
- **Parchment is what gets recorded or decided.** The splash, the campaign log, and every choice sheet on the table are paper with ink.
- **Card art does the illustrating.** Hero tiles, deck covers, and the inspect screen use the real cached card pictures. Do not draw characters or scenery in SVG or Skia beyond the backdrop scenes and the ring. The game's own printed icons are the exception: see Icons.
- **Few words.** A label says what something is, in plain words: no codes, no JSON-ish labels, no ALL CAPS tables. The collector code shows only on the card screen and as row detail in a pack.

## Performance

Feel outranks decoration. These are exit conditions.

- A list is a FlashList. A row is one pressable, text, at most one image decoded at 32×44, and small game icons (see Icons). No blur, no shadow, and no gradient on a row. The name still shows when the file is missing.
- The backdrop is one static Skia canvas per screen (`backdrop.native.tsx`), drawn once per window size, behind the content. It never sits inside a list row or scrolls. The web build draws the same scene in CSS (`backdrop.tsx`).
- Press feedback is an immediate fill in the same frame. Nothing animates on the scroll path. Sheets and phase changes use Reanimated and snap under reduced motion.
- Motion has one home: `src/play/motion-tokens.ts` (see Table, Motion). No duration, curve or spring is written anywhere else in `src/play`, every length passes through `motionScale(speed, reduced)`, and every token is at most 500 ms (the title sweep, 900 ms, and phase 24's spotlight are the two exceptions). The canvas draws again only when what it shows changes: a state's motions run on one clock that stops, with no idle loop, no breathing glow and no timer that redraws while nothing moves. A card that does not move is drawn plain, with no animated value, so a full table costs nothing between states.
- The first screen paints from SQLite. Marcellus is embedded at build time by the `expo-font` config plugin, so there is no runtime font load and no wait. No icon font is loaded at runtime: the game icons are committed SVG and PNG files (see Icons). No other new package, except `expo-haptics`, which the user allowed on 2026-10-25 for the table's ticks (Table, Haptics).
- The table is one Skia canvas. Card pictures stay on that canvas. Chrome text and buttons are React Native. `src/play` cannot import `src/screens`. The canvas uses the `felt` and `feltLight` tokens, copied into play when the table is built.

## Type

Two faces: **Marcellus** (display, `font-display`) for names and moments, and the **system face** for everything you read in a sentence. Five sizes. Do not add a sixth.

| Name | Class | Size / line | Face | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| display | `text-display` | 34 / 40 | Marcellus | Screen title, outcome word (Victory) |
| title | `text-title` | 24 / 30 | Marcellus | Campaign and scenario names, sheet titles, card name |
| body | `text-body` | 17 / 24 | System | Rows, sentences, errors, buttons |
| label | `text-label` | 13 / 18 | System | Meta, tabs, neighbor steps, counts |
| eyebrow | `text-eyebrow` | 12 / 16, tracking 2.4 | Marcellus, uppercase | One short tag above a title: Current, Scenario 1, Combat |

The primary button label is Marcellus body size, uppercase, widest tracking. Numbers the player watches (threat, life, progress) are Marcellus. Card text is never typeset: on the table it is the picture.

## Spacing, radius, border

Tailwind's 4-point steps. Page inset `px-6` (24), top `pt-safe`, controls at the bottom `pb-safe`. Gap under a title 16 to 24. Panels pad 20 (`p-5`). Minimum target 44 (`min-h-11`); rows, tabs, and the primary are 56 (`min-h-14`).

| Radius | Class | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 12 | `rounded-tile` | Hero and choice tiles, pressed fill on text buttons |
| 20 | `rounded-card` | Panels, deck covers, card picture on inspect |
| 28 | `rounded-t-sheet` | Paper sheets |
| pill | `rounded-pill` | Primary button, segmented control, chips, tokens |

Borders are 1 pt `border-hairline` on night. The current panel uses `border-goldLine`. The selected choice tile uses a 1.5 pt gold border.

Shared pieces live in `src/screens/chrome.tsx`. They are views and text. They do not fetch, and they do not import `src/rules` or `src/play`.

Option lists use bone `TextButton`s; gold marks the one action or a link such as Booklet and Log. Close on the booklet reader sits at the bottom in thumb reach.

## Icons

The game's printed icons, added on the user's request in phase 11. They come from online sources first and must be high resolution (vector, or a raster at least 256 px on its short side; 96 px for a token drawn only on a card); the booklets and the card scans were the fallbacks. `pnpm data:sync icons` fetches the sources into `data/raw/icons` (pinned by sha256 in `data/sources.json`); `pnpm data:build` cuts them out offline into `assets/icons` and writes `src/play/icons/generated.ts` (each icon's source and size) and `src/play/icons/files.generated.ts` (the asset requires); `pnpm data:check` covers both.

| Icons | Source | Format |
| --- | --- | --- |
| willpower, attack, defense, hit points, threat, shadow, unique, cost; Leadership, Tactics, Spirit, Lore, Fellowship, Baggins, Neutral; hero, ally, attachment, event, treasure, player side quest, contract; the One Ring | RingsDB icon font `ringsdb.svg` (glyphs named in the font or through `app.css` `.icon-*:before`) | Vector, one SVG per glyph, flipped from the font's y-up units |
| quest points, engagement cost | DragnCards token art (`dragncards-lotrlcg` S3 `tokens/`) | PNG, 256×256 |
| resource, damage, progress tokens | DragnCards token art (`dragncards-lotrlcg` S3 `tokens/`) | PNG, 146×146 |
| player and encounter card backs | DragnCards card backs (`dragncards-lotrlcg` S3 `cardbacks/`, named in its plugin's `jsons/cardBacks.json`) | JPEG, 429×600 |

The resource, damage and progress tokens are DragnCards' token art, 146 px (user request, 2026-10-25: the real token art, not drawn coins). They are drawn only on a card, at 32 pt or less, so their bar is three times that, 96 px, not the 256 px of an icon in chrome; each raster in `scripts/data/icons.ts` carries its own bar. The table draws them on the canvas with the count over them, and the log and the outcome banner show the same art beside a line. The card backs are the printed ones (user request, 2026-10-25): the One Ring on a player card, the Eye on an encounter card, 429×600 like a card scan, so their bar is a scan's 600 px; they are written to `assets/icons` as `playerBack.jpg` and `encounterBack.jpg`, listed in `CARD_BACKS`, and required through `CARD_BACK_FILES`. Encounter card types (enemy, location, treachery, objective, quest) print no type icon and stay words. There is no travel token in this box: progress on a location is the progress token, and Travel stays a word on the rail.

Tinting: a one-colour vector takes a token (bone or muted on night, ink or inkLabel on paper); a sphere icon takes its sphere token. The PNG token art keeps its printed colours. An icon always sits beside its word or carries an accessibility label (an icon and a number read as "Willpower 2"), so the words Maestro asserts stay on screen. In React Native the icon is `GameIcon` (expo-image, SVG or PNG, `tintColor`); on the table canvas it is a Skia path from the same glyph.

Where: the dock (one icon per tab, user request 2026-10-25), the card screen (type, unique, sphere, cost or threat, willpower, attack, defense, hit points, engagement cost, quest points), pool rows, deck editor rows and sphere chips (`SphereMark`), the pool filter's Cost, hero tiles and the briefing (`SphereMark`), the threat rings, the quest card's quest points, the choice sheets (willpower on a quest or Hide commit and in the Hide numbers, attack in a defense header, defense on each defender and on an attacked enemy, attack on each attacker, engagement cost on optional engagement, threat and quest points on travel, hit points left on archery), the log sheet's threat and Hide lines, the campaign log's Threat Modifiers, and the sphere icon on a table card drawn as its name while its file is missing.

## Card pictures

The cached RingsDB picture is the illustration everywhere it appears.

- **Inspect:** full card, max 300 pt wide, centered, `rounded-card`, gold halo shadow (inspect only). Pinch to zoom. Name in Marcellus below, collector code top-right.
- **Preview:** a long press (350 ms) on a face-up card on the table, or on a card row in a pack or the card pool, shows the card as large as the screen allows over night at 82%, with Details (gold, opens the card screen) and Close under it. It grows in on a spring from 82% scale and shrinks back in 150 ms; reduced motion snaps both. A tap anywhere or Android Back closes it. While the scan is missing, the name and the card text sit on paper (`CardPreview`, src/play/card-preview.tsx).
- **Preview on the table (landscape):** the card stands at the left as tall as the screen allows (`previewSideSize`), and a column at its right names it, lists the effects on it now under a gold eyebrow Effects on it ("+1 Attack from Dagger of Westernesse, until the end of the phase"), and lists the card with its attachments and face-up shadow cards (32×44 thumb, name, Attachment or Shadow card): a tap on one shows it in the card's place. A long press on an attachment opens on that attachment. Details opens the card screen for the card shown. The column is not a scrim: a tap there does not close the preview.
- **Hero tile** (briefing, deck editor header, defend sheet): the card picture cropped to the art, `contentFit="cover"` with the focus near the top, a night gradient over the bottom third, the hero's short name in Marcellus.
- **Deck cover:** the three hero pictures side by side, cropped to art, fading into night, deck name on top.
- **Row thumb:** 32×44, `rounded`, raised fill while the file is missing.
- Encounter cards have no RingsDB picture. On the table they use the bundled scans; elsewhere they are text.

### Table — landscape (user request, 2026-10-25; replaces phase 11's portrait table)
The table is played in landscape only (`orientation` on its stack screen), with the status bar hidden and the stack's edge swipe off (a drag from the hand's edge must not leave the table; Leave the table in the menu does). Top bar, 44 pt, inside both side safe-area insets, three zones and nothing else (phase 19; `src/screens/table-top-bar.tsx`): the threat dials at the left, the phase rail centered on the bar, and one menu button at the right. The dials: the threat of each seat as a dial (user request, 2026-10-25: threat is the number that ends a game, so it must be hard to miss): a 40 pt threat-red pill with the threat icon and the number in Marcellus at title size, a meter along its foot that fills toward 50, the elimination level (`rr.threat-elimination-level`), and Threat beside it. It warms near elimination: from 40 the pill is tinted threat red with a danger edge, from 45 it is solid threat red and a screen reader hears "near elimination" (`src/play/threat-level.ts`). When the number changes the dial pops (`dialPopRise` to 1.22, then `dialPopSettle`, a spring) and a badge under its word says the change for three seconds, "−6" on gold when it fell, "+2" on threat red when it rose, read as "Threat lowered by 6, now 24"; reduced motion keeps the dial still and shows the badge. In two-handed play the labels are P1 and P2, read as "Player 1 threat", the shown seat's label in gold. Life, then the Burden deck (Burden), are the same pill with a gold edge, no meter and no levels, when declared. The rail (phase 20; `src/play/phase-rail.tsx`) is one hairline pill of two lines: "Round 3" in muted label, a dot and the phase in Marcellus caps gold with the soft glow, over the step in bone label ("Round 3 · QUEST" over "Staging"; the pill's word for the phases outside the seven is Beginning or End, and Setup and the mulligan show the step alone). The pill alone is the rail whenever the phone is under 760 pt inside the insets or the rail is narrower than `RAIL_WIDE` (about 430 pt); there the round reads "R3". Wider, the previous and next phase names sit at its sides in muted label, each with a chevron drawn as views (no printed icon fits), and a small gold S stands by the next chevron while Stops are on and one of the player's stops lies ahead in this phase (`rail-stop-ahead`; both sides keep the room for it, so the pill never moves); when the neighbors are not shown the S stands at the end of the pill's top line instead, in room the floor keeps. When the phase changes the neighbor names slide one place (`railSlide`, 280 ms ease-out; the old one leaving to the left) and the pill's edge flashes gold (`railFlash`, 300 ms); a step change cross-fades the step (`railStep`, 200 ms); all of it snaps under reduced motion, and what is on the table when it opens appears without sliding. The rail is centered on the bar, which sits inside the safe-area insets, so it is centered on the screen only when the two side insets are equal: both sides get the same width (`src/play/top-bar-layout.ts`). The rail never truncates the pill: its floor, `RAIL_FLOOR` (about 210 pt), is computed from the pill's own words, "Round 99 · ENCOUNTER" at the phase and label sizes and the longest step name, plus the pill's chrome, and a test checks every step name and phase against it. It is the one measured floor: phase 19's was the longest step name at the body size (232 pt), so the two-seat bar is no worse than before. Only when the dials are wider than the room that leaves on each side (two seats with Life and the Burden deck, labeled "Burden" and read as "Burden deck") does the left grow at the rail's expense, down to the floor, and the rail sit off center; if they still do not fit, the rail wins and the dials scroll sideways, none cut. Until the bar and the dials are measured the rail is held invisible rather than drawn on a guess, so it does not jump when the table opens. A tap on the rail opens the phase carousel (Panels) and a second tap closes it; the pill's edge is goldLine at rest, so it reads as a button, and turns gold while the strip is open, and the rail is heard as a button that expands. When the dials scroll, a seven-band fade to the felt at the left zone's right edge shows a cut dial can be scrolled to. The menu button is 44 pt with three bone dots drawn as views (no printed icon fits; gold while the menu is open), label "Menu" (`table-menu`). **Motion.** The table moves like a game, not a form, and never holds the player up: every token is at most 500 ms, the title sweep apart. A sequence of tokens runs longer (a played card 760 ms, a revealed one about 790, a destroyed one about 570, a hand of six 870), and the player never waits on it; Quick runs them all at 0.6. A rule is already resolved when its motion plays. Every length, curve and spring is a token in `src/play/motion-tokens.ts`; `motionScale(speed, reduced)` multiplies each one: 1 for Normal, 0.6 for Quick, 0 under reduced motion, which snaps everything. **Animation speed** (Normal, Quick) is a table setting (`animationSpeed`, `table-settings.ts`, stored with the others in `user.db`) shown in the Table settings panel (`settings-speed`, `settings-speed-normal`, `settings-speed-quick`). Pure code works out what each card does (`motion.ts`: `flip`, `delay`, `arc`, `land`, and the cards that left) and its pose at a moment (`motion-pose.ts`, `motion-curves.ts`); the UI thread draws one clock per state (`card-motion.native.tsx`), so the canvas draws again only when what it shows changes and no timer runs while nothing moves.

A spring is named by its damping ratio and the time it takes to settle; the canvas evaluates the same damped oscillator Reanimated integrates, so a state has one clock. The timers that are not motion stay outside the scale: the top bar's 400 ms measure fallback, the dock's second-tap window, the banner's 650 ms beats and 4.5 s hold, the 350 ms settle after a new moment, and the 150 ms the sweep waits to see whether the table passes the phase.

- **Move.** An arrival eases out past its slot a little and settles (`cardArrive`); a card that leaves eases in (`cardDepart`). A card that was elsewhere on the table moves from there.
- **Draw.** A card leaves the deck on a shallow arc (a 24 pt rise at mid-path), showing its printed back, the One Ring, and turns to its face at the midpoint. Cards drawn together leave one after another, 90 ms apart.
- **Reveal.** An encounter card comes off the encounter deck showing the Eye, turns over, then pops (1.12, a 120 ms hold, a spring back to 1). A dealt shadow card does not flip: it stays face down. A flip shows only the printed back the canvas already has, never a face the player may not see.
- **Play.** A card from the hand lifts to 1.12 (`lift`), glides to its slot (`glide`) and lands squashed to 0.96, springing back to 1 (`land`). The landing ticks once (`tickLand`).
- **Exhaust and ready.** The quarter turn is a spring with a light overshoot (`exhaust`).
- **Leave.** A card destroyed or discarded from play shakes (three steps of 4 pt, 50 ms each), then shrinks toward its pile (`cardLeave`, ease in for the travel and the size) while it fades out on an ease-out curve, so it does not linger at full strength: 570 ms in all; one discarded from the hand, an event played, or returned to a deck glides to the pile in `cardDepart`; a card set aside fades where it stood.
- **Token pop.** When a damage, resource or progress count changes (`tokenDeltas`, `token-deltas.ts`), its token pops, 1 to 1.4 and back on a spring (about 300 ms), once its card has arrived. A count that fell to 0 has no token left to pop, so only its number floats. Progress placed on a quest card or a location pops its "progress / quest points" strip the same way.
- **Floating number.** A number rises 20 pt from the token and fades over 700 ms: "+2" in gold for a gain, "−1" in danger red for damage or a loss (the `danger` token, which reads on the felt where threat red does not), Marcellus 13 pt with a night outline. The numbers are the reducer's; the canvas draws a string and a size it was handed.
- **Phase title sweep.** When the round moves into a new phase, its name in Marcellus display crosses the canvas center (fade in, slide 40 pt, fade out, 900 ms in all; `phase-sweep.tsx`). Taps pass through it. It is skipped under Quick (the scale is not 1), under reduced motion, when the phase went backward (Undo), when the new state moves cards or changes a count (the title would cover the pops and turns on the player's row), and when Stops passed the whole phase (a second phase change within 150 ms takes the first one's place).
- **Seat handoff** (two-handed play). When the acting seat changes, its dial pulses once in a gold ring (1 to 1.12 and back, `seatPulse`) and its hand slides up into place (`seatHand`) while the rest of its table fades in over the same time, so the two seats cross-fade; nothing is dealt.
- **Hurry.** A touch on the canvas finishes the running card motions at once (their progress is set to 1, and the pending landing tick is dropped, so no haptic follows a snapped card) and is then handled as usual; the token pops and floating numbers have a clock of their own and keep running; it never delays or swallows a tap, a drag or a long press, and it touches neither the tray's outside-tap fold nor the More popover's outside-tap close. Nothing animates while the carousel strip scrolls, the rail does not animate when the table opens, and no motion moves the dock's pinned buttons.

**Gestures.** A tap picks for the open moment, or, on a card the moment does not ask about, shows it large. A long press (350 ms) shows it large. A card that can be played, committed, traveled to or engaged can be dragged; the drop picks its target. No drag or drop sends an intent, and a tap sends one only in a moment with one answer (One answer, one tap).

The top bar stays live, so a dial or the rail replaces the panel. One reading layer at a time, the carousel included (`reading.ts`): a new one replaces the last. When the choice tray is open too, the panel sits over it and closing the panel shows the tray again. The threat panel shows the seat's threat of 50, how the engagement checks read it, and each staging enemy's name with its engagement cost and what the engine's checks would do with it now: Engages in ink with a 3 pt threat-red rule at the row's left, Engages Player 2, Stays or Cannot be engaged in inkMuted. **Undo.** Undo is the first of the dock's quiet pills, in thumb reach. **Haptics.** A tick when a button arms, a confirm when an intent is sent or taken back, a reject when the reducer refuses one, and a light tick when a card played from the hand lands (`tickLand`, at most one per new state): on iOS the selection, light impact and warning feedback; on Android the system's Segment tick, Confirm and Reject constants (`performAndroidHapticsAsync`), which follow the phone's touch-feedback setting, never the vibrator. They add to the screen and never replace it; a phone without haptics ignores them.

**Banner.** After each intent that changed the table, a night panel centered on the screen at the top, just under the top bar and over the table and any open panel (user request, 2026-10-25), says what happened: the title in Marcellus and up to five centered lines with their marks, on a card with a soft shadow off the felt, a 3 pt band along its top and a 1 pt edge in the tone (gold for a quest that succeeded or a passed Hide test, threat red for a failed quest or an engagement, faint otherwise), 260 to 440 pt wide and never wider than the screen less 16 pt a side: "Quest failed · Willpower 3 against threat 5 · Threat +2", "Black Rider attacks · Attack 3 against Sam Gamgee's defense 2 · Shadow, Hasty Departure: … · 1 damage on Sam Gamgee", "Traveled to Bag End". The moment that ends a round (not setup) is followed by the round's summary in the same banner, titled "Round 3 ends": the threat before and after when it moved, progress placed, damage taken and dealt, encounter cards defeated or explored, cards drawn. **Log.** Log opens this round's engine steps on a paper panel, each with its cause in inkMuted under it (the rule title, or the card and its printed line), with the threat, willpower and attack icons and the token art as marks. ## States

| State | Treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Empty | A muted sentence that says what is missing. The title stays. |
| Loading | The splash is the startup wait. A later wait shows the title and the word Loading in muted. No spinner package. |
| Error | Danger sentence naming the card, the book, or the failed open. |
| Locked | Faint text, hairline ring, the word Locked. Not pressable. |
| Current | Gold ring or gold edge, plus the word Current when it is not obvious. |
| Completed | Gold disc, the word Completed or the reward names. |
| Refused | Danger sentence naming the card and the rule. The refused change does not happen. |
| Not ready | A deck that breaks a deckbuilding rule: hairline ring and the rule in danger ("Needs 3 more cards"). It saves; the briefing does not offer it. |
| Failed test | The word Failed in Marcellus plus the engine's numbers. |
| Not applied | A card with a printed line the app does not apply: the struck ring on the table, "Not applied" with the loss in a list row (Ability not applied, Cannot be played, Text not applied), the lines under the Not applied eyebrow on the card and in the preview, and the count on a deck ("2 cards with text not applied"). Never the word inactive. |
| Armed | A button that sends with nothing picked, after its first tap: the line above the buttons says what the second tap does ("Tap again: no one quests") in gold, with a 2 pt gold bar running down for three seconds (ten with a screen reader), and a light haptic tick. The button keeps its size, place and label. A moment with one answer arms nothing. |

An irreversible control (Deal, Confirm, a resolution choice) shows the consequence in a sentence directly above it. On the table no single tap sends an intent: a tap picks and a button confirms, and a button that sends with nothing picked is armed first; only a moment with one legal answer sends on one tap.
