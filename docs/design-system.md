# Design system — Lamplight

Visual authority for chrome. Product rules stay in [brief.md](brief.md). Structure stays in [architecture.md](architecture.md). Every screen phase uses these tokens and components. Do not invent a palette, a type scale, a radius, or a second styling system.

Lamplight replaces Night (user request, 2026-10-27: "too serious"; the playful first proposal was "too childish"; the screens were to be rethought, not only restyled, and stay dark and atmospheric). Night replaced the earlier Parchment system; where `docs/phases/phase-17-design-system.md` says "system face only", "no downloaded font", or "light only", this file supersedes it. The two embedded weights of Cinzel and the dark theme are deliberate decisions.

## Direction

A game, not a form. The phone is a lamp-lit table at night, and every screen opens on the picture of what it is about: the scenario's scene, the fellowship's faces, the card's painting. The words support the pictures; they do not replace them.

- **Night** is the page: near-black with cool shadow, a scene backdrop behind every screen (a moonlit sky, an ember, a vignette at the corners).
- **Gold is the Ring.** Gold marks the one thing to do and what is current or selected: the primary, the current step, the picked choice, the framed card, the filter chip that is on. Beside the one gold plate, gold is only words: the other actions that move the player on (a text button or a plate's word: Path, Log, Replay, Add cards, New playthrough, Duplicate, Keep) and a chip that says where the player stands (Scenario 2 of 6, Current, a card's count in the deck). A tag that does nothing when tapped (a special rule, a reward) is never gold.
- **Moonlight** names a section (`moon`, a cold blue-grey): Your fellowship, Difficulty, Other journeys. It is information, never an action.
- **Parchment is what gets recorded or decided.** The splash, the campaign log, and every choice sheet on the table are paper with ink.
- **Card art does the illustrating.** Hero portraits, scenario scenes, deck covers and the card screen use the real card pictures, cropped by rule (Card art). Do not draw characters or scenery in SVG or Skia beyond the backdrop scenes and the ring. The game's own printed icons are the exception: see Icons.
- **Few words.** A label says what something is, in plain words: no codes, no JSON-ish labels. The collector code shows only on the card screen and as row detail in a pack.

## Standards

What every screen does. A screen that breaks one is a finding in review.

1. **Art first.** A screen about a scenario opens on its scene; one about a deck on its heroes' paintings; one about a card on its picture. A campaign or a game names its fellowship with faces (`Portrait`). While a file is missing the art is a lit night surface with the words over it: nothing waits on a picture and nothing shows a hole.
2. **One clear next move, in thumb reach.** A screen's primary sits in its `ActionBar`, pinned at the foot, with the sentence it needs above it (what it waits for, what it will do). The content scrolls under a night fade. A tab screen whose primary belongs to one card (Begin on the Continue card) keeps it on that card.
3. **Status at a glance.** Progress is a bar (`JourneyBar`, `ProgressBar`), counts and stats are tiles (`StatTile`) and chips (`Chip`), a party is faces. Numbers are big and in Cinzel Bold; their words are small. A sentence says what a number cannot.
4. **Show the decision, fold the rest.** The briefing shows the chosen fellowship as one framed card and opens the deck list only to change it; Manage, Import and the archived lists are one quiet control away. Nothing is hidden behind a gesture alone.
5. **Back and the book are round buttons at the top** (`NavBar`, `IconButton`): a chevron and the printed Lore book, each with a spoken label ("Back to Decks", "Open the booklet"). Over a picture they sit on a night disc.
6. **Every target is 44 pt.** A smaller visible mark (a chip, a compact switch, a remove ×) keeps its 44 pt target with a hit slop or a larger pressable.
7. **Feedback in the same frame.** A press fills or sinks at once (the primary scales to 0.98). Nothing animates on the scroll path.

## Performance

Feel outranks decoration. These are exit conditions.

- A list is a FlashList. A row is one pressable, text, at most one small picture (a 30×42 thumb, a 44 pt portrait; a deck's row shows its heroes as up to three overlapping thumbs), and small game icons (see Icons). Inside a FlashList row nothing is lit and nothing casts: fills are solid tokens (`raised`, never `panelSurface`), no `boxShadow` (not even a dot's glow), no blur. The one gradient a row may draw is `nightFade` over a picture, so words read on it (a deck cover). A lit surface and its shadow belong to a card in a ScrollView (the Continue card, a stat tile, a panel). The name still shows when the file is missing. The card pool's grid is the one list of large pictures: three cards a row to browse and two while adding to a deck, each decoded at its drawn size (about 107×150 pt and 166×232 pt on a 390 pt phone) and kept decoded in memory, recycled by FlashList; a card draws again only when its own count or limit changes.
- The backdrop is one static Skia canvas per screen (`backdrop.native.tsx`), drawn once per window size, behind the content. It never sits inside a list row or scrolls. The web build draws the same scene in CSS (`backdrop.tsx`).
- A gradient is a style from `src/play/surfaces.ts` (`backgroundImage`, which React Native draws natively and react-native-web hands to the browser) over a solid fill of the same token, so a platform that draws no gradient still shows the right colour. The card screen's blurred backdrop is its one blurred image, drawn once behind the content.
- Press feedback is an immediate fill in the same frame. Nothing animates on the scroll path. Sheets and phase changes use Reanimated and snap under reduced motion.
- Motion has one home: `src/play/motion-tokens.ts` (see Table, Motion). No duration, curve or spring is written anywhere else in `src/play`, every length passes through `motionScale(speed, reduced)`, and every token is at most 500 ms (the title sweep, 900 ms, and the whole run of the combat spotlight, 1.5 s, are the two exceptions; the spotlight's own beats are each at most 500 ms). The canvas draws again only when what it shows changes: a state's motions run on one clock that stops, and the spotlight runs on a clock of its own only while it plays, with no idle loop, no breathing glow and no timer that redraws while nothing moves. A card that does not move is drawn plain, with no animated value, so a full table costs nothing between states.
- The first screen paints from SQLite. Cinzel SemiBold and Cinzel Bold are embedded at build time by the `expo-font` config plugin, so there is no runtime font load and no wait (the table canvas loads Cinzel Bold for its numbers through Skia, with the system face standing in for the moment it takes). No icon font is loaded at runtime: the game icons are committed SVG and PNG files (see Icons). No other new package, except `expo-haptics`, which the user allowed on 2026-10-25 for the table's ticks (Table, Haptics).
- The table is one Skia canvas. Card pictures stay on that canvas. Chrome text and buttons are React Native. `src/play` cannot import `src/screens`. The canvas uses the `felt` and `feltLight` tokens, copied into play when the table is built.

## Type

Two faces: **Cinzel** (display) for names, moments and the numbers the player watches, and the **system face** for everything read in a sentence. Cinzel's lower case is small capitals, so a name reads as engraved capitals while its string stays as written (Maestro and a screen reader see "Current", not "CURRENT"). Two weights of Cinzel, embedded: **SemiBold** (`font-display`) for titles and names, **Bold** (`font-display-bold`) for buttons, eyebrows, chips' numbers and every number. Seven sizes. Do not add an eighth.

| Name | Class | Size / line | Face | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| display | `text-display` | 28 / 34, tracking 0.6 | Cinzel SemiBold | Screen title, scenario name on its banner |
| title | `text-title` | 22 / 28, tracking 0.4 | Cinzel SemiBold (Bold for a number) | Campaign, deck and card names, sheet titles; the value on a stat tile, the threat dial |
| heading | `text-heading` | 15 / 22, tracking 0.3 | Cinzel SemiBold | A name on a row or a card (a scenario on the path, a deck in a list, a hero tile), the dock's moment title (on a 24 pt line), the NavBar title |
| body | `text-body` | 17 / 24 | System | Rows, sentences, errors, text buttons |
| label | `text-label` | 13 / 18 | System | Meta, counts, chips, sentences above a primary |
| eyebrow | `text-eyebrow` | 11 / 16, tracking 2 | Cinzel Bold, uppercase | One short tag above a title (gold: Scenario 1, Current, Adding to) or a section heading (moon) |
| micro | `text-micro` | 11 / 14, tracking 0.6 | System (Cinzel Bold for a number) | Tab labels, curve labels, the stage numbers |

The primary's label is Cinzel Bold 15 pt, uppercase, tracking 2.4 (14 pt, tracking 1.4, in the table's dock). A secondary plate's label is Cinzel Bold 13 pt, uppercase, tracking 1.6. Victory and Defeat are Cinzel Bold 44 pt, tracking 4, with a glow (gold, or threat red). Card text is never typeset on the table: there it is the picture.

Two widths the table counts are this type's (top-bar-layout.ts, dock-budget.ts): the rail's phase is Cinzel Bold capitals at 14 pt (0.76 em a capital at most; counted at 0.78), and the dock's title Cinzel SemiBold at 15 pt (0.61 em a character at most, 9.15 pt, plus the heading's 0.3 pt tracking: 9.45 pt; counted at 9.5 pt). The rail sets no tracking, so its 0.78 em is the capital alone.

## Spacing, radius, border

Tailwind's 4-point steps. Page inset `px-6` (24), top `pt-safe`, controls at the bottom in the `ActionBar` above the safe area. Gap under a title 12 to 20. Panels pad 20 (`p-5`), cards 16. Minimum target 44 (`min-h-11`); rows and the primary are 56 (`min-h-14`), a secondary plate 52 (44 compact).

| Radius | Class | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 8 | `rounded-chip` | A `Chip`, the plate inside a segmented track, a card in the pool's grid, the choice tray's damage pill |
| 12 | `rounded-tile` | Plates, tiles, chips that toggle, search fields, segmented tracks, stat tiles |
| 14 | `rounded-button` | The primary |
| 16 | `rounded-card` | Panels, cards, deck covers, the dock, the tray, popovers, card picture on inspect |
| 20 | `rounded-hero` | The Continue card |
| 28 | `rounded-t-sheet` | Paper sheets |
| pill | `rounded-pill` | Progress bars, portraits, small round marks, the phase rail |

Borders are 1 pt `hairline` on night. A framed card (the current panel, the Continue card, the chosen fellowship, the scenario to play, a game in progress) has a `goldLine` edge and the four gold `Corners`. A selected tile or cover has a 1.5 pt gold edge and a soft gold glow.

## Backdrops

`<Screen scene="…">` draws one scene behind the content. Scenes are data in `backdrop-scenes.ts`: base gradient, up to two glows, up to two ridges, an optional ring, and a vignette (how dark the corners fall). A screen that opens on a picture (`bleed`) draws the picture over the scene's top.

| Scene | Where | Feel |
| --- | --- | --- |
| dusk | Campaign list, Scenarios | A moonlit night over far mountains: a cold light high on the right, an ember low on the left |
| ember | Campaign path | Lamplight left, cold blue low right |
| vale | Briefing (under the scene banner) | Green vale in afternoon light |
| victory | Resolution (won), standalone outcome (won) | The Ring, lit, behind the outcome word |
| felt | Table chrome | Green-black felt, no vignette |
| plain | Decks, deck editor, card, packs, log, rules, errors | Quiet night, one cold light top-left and a faint ember at the foot |

A lost resolution uses `plain`. Do not add a scene for one screen; reuse one of these.

## Icons

The game's printed icons, added on the user's request in phase 11. They come from online sources first and must be high resolution (vector, or a raster at least 256 px on its short side; 96 px for a token drawn only on a card); the booklets and the card scans were the fallbacks. `pnpm data:sync icons` fetches the sources into `data/raw/icons` (pinned by sha256 in `data/sources.json`); `pnpm data:build` cuts them out offline into `assets/icons` and writes `src/play/icons/generated.ts` (each icon's source and size) and `src/play/icons/files.generated.ts` (the asset requires); `pnpm data:check` covers both.

| Icons | Source | Format |
| --- | --- | --- |
| willpower, attack, defense, hit points, threat, shadow, unique, cost; Leadership, Tactics, Spirit, Lore, Fellowship, Baggins, Neutral; hero, ally, attachment, event, treasure, player side quest, contract; the One Ring | RingsDB icon font `ringsdb.svg` (glyphs named in the font or through `app.css` `.icon-*:before`) | Vector, one SVG per glyph, flipped from the font's y-up units |
| quest points, engagement cost | DragnCards token art (`dragncards-lotrlcg` S3 `tokens/`) | PNG, 256×256 |
| resource, damage, progress tokens | DragnCards token art (`dragncards-lotrlcg` S3 `tokens/`) | PNG, 146×146 |
| player and encounter card backs | DragnCards card backs (`dragncards-lotrlcg` S3 `cardbacks/`, named in its plugin's `jsons/cardBacks.json`) | JPEG, 429×600 |

The resource, damage and progress tokens are DragnCards' token art, 146 px (user request, 2026-10-25: the real token art, not drawn coins). They are drawn only on a card, at 32 pt or less, so their bar is three times that, 96 px, not the 256 px of an icon in chrome; each raster in `scripts/data/icons.ts` carries its own bar. The table draws them on the canvas with the count over them, and the log and the outcome banner show the same art beside a line. The card backs are the printed ones (user request, 2026-10-25): the One Ring on a player card, the Eye on an encounter card, 429×600 like a card scan, so their bar is a scan's 600 px; they are written to `assets/icons` as `playerBack.jpg` and `encounterBack.jpg`, listed in `CARD_BACKS`, and required through `CARD_BACK_FILES`. Encounter card types (enemy, location, treachery, objective, quest) print no type icon and stay words. There is no travel token in this box: progress on a location is the progress token, and Travel stays a word on the rail.

Tinting: a one-colour vector takes a token (bone or muted on night, ink or inkLabel on paper); a sphere icon takes its sphere token. The PNG token art keeps its printed colours. ## Card art

The cached RingsDB picture is the illustration of a player card everywhere it appears; a quest card's scan (Hall of Beorn, bundled on release builds) is the picture of a scenario. Every crop is a region of the printed layout, the same on every card of its kind (`src/screens/art-crop.ts`, `cropFrame`, tested): the picture is drawn at the size that makes the region cover the box, centred on the region, never leaving a gap at an edge.

| Region | Of | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `FACE` | a player card: the portrait above the stat column's right, square | `Portrait`: a hero's face in a gold ring; `PortraitRow` overlaps them for a fellowship |
| `PAINTING` | a player card: right of the stat column, above the name banner | Hero tiles, deck covers, the deck editor's banner |
| `SCENE` | a quest card: under its title banners, above its text box, clear of the encounter-set badge | `SceneArt`: the scenario's picture on the Continue card, the path, the briefing's banner, the Scenarios rows, a game in progress |

- **Scene:** the first quest stage of the scenario (`scenarioOf(id).stages[0]`), its scan cropped to `SCENE`, a soft night at the top and night rising from its middle so the words below it read. While the scan is missing the box is a lit night surface.
- **Inspect:** full card, max 300 pt wide, centered, `rounded-card`, gold halo shadow (inspect only). Pinch to zoom. The card's own picture, blurred and at 45%, lights the top of the screen behind it. Name in Cinzel below, collector code top-right on a faint chip.
- **Preview:** a long press (350 ms) on a face-up card on the table, or on a card in a pack or the card pool, shows the card as large as the screen allows over night at 82%, with Details (gold, opens the card screen) and Close under it. It grows in on a spring from 82% scale and shrinks back in 150 ms; reduced motion snaps both. A tap anywhere or Android Back closes it. While the scan is missing, the name and the card text sit on paper (`CardPreview`, src/play/card-preview.tsx).
- **Preview on the table (landscape):** the card stands at the left as tall as the screen allows (`previewSideSize`), and a column at its right names it, lists the effects on it now under a gold eyebrow Effects on it ("+1 Attack from Dagger of Westernesse, until the end of the phase"), and lists the card with its attachments and face-up shadow cards (32×44 thumb, name, Attachment or Shadow card): a tap on one shows it in the card's place. A long press on an attachment opens on that attachment. Details opens the card screen for the card shown. The column is not a scrim: a tap there does not close the preview.
- **Hero tile** (briefing, deck editor, defend sheet): the painting, night rising over its lower 60%, the hero's name in Cinzel Bold and the sphere under it. A dense tile (four to a row in the briefing) gives the name the tile's whole width at 12 pt on up to two lines, with its marks (the sphere's printed icon, the Ring) on a line of their own above it, so a name is never cut (four Frodos look alike, and the tile is what confirms which one was picked). - **Row thumb:** 30×42, `rounded`, raised fill while the file is missing. A grid card: the full card, decoded at its drawn size; the name and type on night while the file is missing.
- Encounter cards other than quests have no picture off the table. On the table they use the bundled scans.

A sequence of tokens runs longer (a played card 760 ms, a revealed one about 790, a destroyed one about 570, a hand of six 870), and the player never waits on it; Quick runs them all at 0.6. A rule is already resolved when its motion plays. Every length, curve and spring is a token in `src/play/motion-tokens.ts`; `motionScale(speed, reduced)` multiplies each one: 1 for Normal, 0.6 for Quick, 0 under reduced motion, which snaps everything and plays no spotlight. **Animation speed** (Normal, Quick) is a table setting (`animationSpeed`, `table-settings.ts`, stored with the others in `user.db`) shown in the Table settings panel (`settings-speed`, `settings-speed-normal`, `settings-speed-quick`). Pure code works out what each card does (`motion.ts`: `flip`, `delay`, `arc`, `land`, and the cards that left) and its pose at a moment (`motion-pose.ts`, `motion-curves.ts`); the UI thread draws one clock per state (`card-motion.native.tsx`), so the canvas draws again only when what it shows changes and no timer runs while nothing moves.

The hand scrub's tokens (phase 25): `scrubRise` 220 ms ease-out cubic (the hand rises, grows to 1.5 and flattens, and the table's night fades to 35%), `scrubSpread` 220 ms ease-out cubic (the cards slide apart; it runs with the rise), `scrubLift` 120 ms ease-out cubic (the card under the finger lifts to 1.25; the one it left drops at once) and `scrubFall` 180 ms ease-in cubic (the hand settles back). Under reduced motion (scale 0) the hand shows risen and spread at once, the lift is instant and it settles at once; at Quick every length is 0.6. The canvas draws the scrub from shared values (`hand-scrub.native.tsx`): no idle loop and no redraw timer.

A spring is named by its damping ratio and the time it takes to settle; the canvas evaluates the same damped oscillator Reanimated integrates, so a state has one clock. The timers that are not motion stay outside the scale: the top bar's 400 ms measure fallback, the dock's second-tap window, the banner's 650 ms beats and 4.5 s hold, the 350 ms settle after a new moment (it starts again when a spotlight on it ends or is skipped), and the 150 ms the sweep waits to see whether the table passes the phase.

- **Move.** An arrival eases out past its slot a little and settles (`cardArrive`); a card that leaves eases in (`cardDepart`). A card that was elsewhere on the table moves from there.
- **Draw.** A card leaves the deck on a shallow arc (a 24 pt rise at mid-path), showing its printed back, the One Ring, and turns to its face at the midpoint. Cards drawn together leave one after another, 90 ms apart.
- **Reveal.** An encounter card comes off the encounter deck showing the Eye, turns over, then pops (1.12, a 120 ms hold, a spring back to 1). A dealt shadow card does not flip: it stays face down. A flip shows only the printed back the canvas already has, never a face the player may not see.
- **Play.** A card from the hand lifts to 1.12 (`lift`), glides to its slot (`glide`) and lands squashed to 0.96, springing back to 1 (`land`). The landing ticks once (`tickLand`).
- **Exhaust and ready.** The quarter turn is a spring with a light overshoot (`exhaust`).
- **Leave.** A card destroyed or discarded from play shakes (three steps of 4 pt, 50 ms each), then shrinks toward its pile (`cardLeave`, ease in for the travel and the size) while it fades out on an ease-out curve, so it does not linger at full strength: 570 ms in all; one discarded from the hand, an event played, or returned to a deck glides to the pile in `cardDepart`; a card set aside fades where it stood.
- **Token pop.** When a damage, resource or progress count changes (`tokenDeltas`, `token-deltas.ts`), its token pops, 1 to 1.4 and back on a spring (about 300 ms), once its card has arrived. A count that fell to 0 has no token left to pop, so only its number floats. Progress placed on a quest card or a location pops its "progress / quest points" strip the same way.
- **Floating number.** A number rises 20 pt from the token and fades over 700 ms: "+2" in gold for a gain, "−1" in danger red for damage or a loss (the `danger` token, which reads on the felt where threat red does not), Cinzel Bold 13 pt with a night outline. The numbers are the reducer's; the canvas draws a string and a size it was handed.
- **Phase title sweep.** When the round moves into a new phase, its name in Cinzel display crosses the canvas center (fade in, slide 40 pt, fade out, 900 ms in all; `phase-sweep.tsx`). Taps pass through it. It is skipped under Quick (the scale is not 1), under reduced motion, when the phase went backward (Undo), when the new state moves cards or changes a count (the title would cover the pops and turns on the player's row; a state that deals damage is such a state, so a title never runs under a spotlight), and when Stops passed the whole phase (a second phase change within 150 ms takes the first one's place).
- **Seat handoff** (two-handed play). When the acting seat changes, its dial pulses once in a gold ring (1 to 1.12 and back, `seatPulse`) and its hand slides up into place (`seatHand`) while the rest of its table fades in over the same time, so the two seats cross-fade; nothing is dealt.
- **Peek** (two-handed play). A peek and its way back have no motion (user request, 2026-10-27): the table is redrawn from the other seat at once, every card at rest. Only the seat's own zones change (the hand, the play area, the engaged enemies with their cards, and the deck and discard pile); staging, the quest, the locations and the encounter piles are shared and stay put. It is not the seat handoff: the handoff comes from the acting seat changing (`card-motion.native.tsx`), so no hand rises and no dial pulses; nothing is dealt, no token pops (the pops read two engine states, and a peek is one), no card lands and no haptic plays (`kindMoves` in `motion-kind.ts`). A peek that ends because the player confirmed something on the dock (a card picked, a peek, then Confirm) is a new state, so its own motions play, diffed from the layout the acting seat had, and the played card still lifts and lands, with its tick, and a drawn card is still dealt; and when the engine turns to the seat the table already shows, that hand is on screen and does not slide up again.
- **Hurry.** A touch on the canvas finishes the running card motions at once (their progress is set to 1, and the pending landing tick is dropped, so no haptic follows a snapped card), skips a combat spotlight that is playing (below), and that touch does nothing else (it picks, previews and drags nothing under the enlarged copies); any other touch is handled as usual; the token pops and floating numbers have a clock of their own and keep running; it never delays or swallows a tap, a drag or a long press outside a spotlight, and it is the first touch of a hold on the hand too, so a scrub that starts mid-draw finds the hand at rest, and it touches neither the tray's outside-tap fold nor the More popover's outside-tap close. Nothing animates while the carousel strip scrolls, the rail does not animate when the table opens, and no motion moves the dock's pinned buttons.

**Spotlight** (phase 24; `spotlight.native.tsx`, pure beats in `spotlight-beats.ts`, geometry in `spotlight-stage.ts`, the plan in `spotlight-plan.ts`). When an attack deals or blocks damage the table plays it large: an enemy against a defender, an enemy against an undefended hero, or the players' attackers against an enemy. Archery, damage from a card effect, an attack that was canceled and one that placed no damage step get none (the banner covers them): `clashesOf(prev, next, steps)` (`clash.ts`) reads the engine's `strike`, `damage` and `destroy` steps and every number on screen is theirs, so the UI computes none (two attacks on one target count up in turn from the state's own count). It is a top layer on the table's Skia canvas, drawn only while it plays (no idle loop, no timer that redraws), with the table's tokens: the canvas dims under night at 65%, and an RN scrim of the same night over the top bar and the dock catches the skip. The attacker is always at the left (the enemy for an enemy's attack, the players' attackers as a fan of up to three, then a small night chip "+n" with a hairline), the target at the right, both flown from their slots to the center and enlarged to 42% of the canvas height with a 24 pt gap; under each a big pill in Cinzel Bold, the attack strength with the attack icon (threat red for an enemy, gold for the players) and the defense with the defense icon (gold; none under an undefended hero, who has no defense). The state is already resolved: the spotlight never gates an intent, and the dock's next moment is ready under the scrim. While it plays the state's own card motions, the pops of the damage it placed, the banner and the 350 ms settle wait: the cards stand where they were, and all of it starts when the spotlight ends. A tap anywhere skips every attack of the moment and lands the table on the new state at once: on the canvas it rides the hurry (that touch does nothing else), on the scrim over the top bar and the dock a press (not a release) skips and reaches nothing under it, and Android Back skips first. The scrim fades in over 100 ms (`spotlightScrim`) and, on a skip, out over 100 ms; the moment the spotlight ends it stops catching touches, though its fill is still fading, so the dock is live at once. It is gone for good then, so the tray's outside-tap fold and the popovers' outside tap are as before. A reading layer (the carousel, a panel) closes when a spotlight begins. The enlarged cards are decoded again at their size while the spotlight plays (the shadow card's face too) and released when it ends; a card whose file is missing shows its name.

**Gestures** (phase 25; `src/play/hand-gestures.native.ts`, pure geometry in `hand-scrub.ts`). A tap picks for the open moment, or, on a card the moment does not ask about, shows it large. A long press (350 ms) on a card on the table shows it large; a discard pile's opens its list. A card that can be played, committed, traveled to or engaged can be dragged (a move of 6 pt starts it, at once; nothing waits for a hold); the drop picks its target. While the table peeks at the other seat a tap on any card (its own or a shared one) only opens its preview, nothing is picked or sent, and nothing can be dragged; a pile still opens its list, which is the viewed seat's. There is no swipe between seats: the seat is changed by its dial, so the drag-to-play path is never delayed. No drag or drop sends an intent, and a tap sends one only in a moment with one answer (One answer, one tap).

**Hold, slide and release** on the hand. A press on a hand card, or on the hand strip between cards, held still for 250 ms (a move of 6 pt or more before that is a drag, which plays a card exactly as above) starts a scrub, with one selection tick (`tickArmed`). The scrub starts on the card pressed (on the strip between cards, the one nearest the finger), and the nearest center takes over from the first move. The hand rises to show whole cards, grows to 1.5 times its size, flattens its fan and spreads across the canvas width, overlapping evenly and never leaving less than a 44 pt slice of any card (the scale holds at 1.5 up to about 12 cards; a longer hand gets a smaller scale instead). **Stops and Always use.** With Stops on (the phase carousel's switch), the step eyebrow starts with "Stops on ·", and an action window that is not one of the player's stops passes by itself unless a card usable there is new since the player was last asked.
