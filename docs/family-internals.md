# Mechanic family internals

What each mechanic family runs beyond its manifest, the zones and decks it owns, and the core files it hooks. The family pattern (vocabulary, engine, records) is in `docs/architecture.md`, Mechanic families; the families are listed in `src/catalog/families/index.ts`. Find a family here with `pnpm doc find` or by searching for its name.

## wilderland

`wilderland` (the Ered Mithrin campaign's treasures, boon allies and encounter cards in the Campaign Pool, and the decks, keywords and Guarded (X) its scenarios share: `src/rules/families/wilderland/`) runs `AttachRandomTreasure` in a game (a seeded draw, asked of the first player, attached as a guarded objective), answers the conditions that read the Campaign Log's lines and treasures, and leaves its Resolution verbs to `src/rules/setup/resolution.ts` and its Setup removal to `src/campaign/checklist.ts`. The decks beside the encounter deck are zones whose lists `src/rules/core/piles.ts` reads and writes through one accessor: the Corsair deck and its discard pile (`corsairDeck`, `corsairDiscard`), the Uncharted deck (`uncharted.deck`) and Ered Mithrin's `cavesDeck`, `evilCreaturesDeck`, `evilCreaturesDiscard`, `spidersDeck`, `orcsDeck` and Journey to the Cross-roads' `haradDeck` (`ScenarioState.piles`); `moveCard` joins and leaves them there, `core/decks.ts` refills a deck from its discard pile and `encounter/named-decks.ts` reveals its top card (the Corsair deck and the Evil Creatures deck share both), and `settle` keeps the Evil Creatures deck refilled. A card dealt into the Evil Creatures deck carries `CardInstance.deck`, so it goes to that deck's discard pile, even with Victory, when it would be discarded. Deep is a constant marker on a location: `quest/travel.ts` pushes a frame under the Responses to the travel that discards the location and makes the top Caves card the active location (`wilderland/deep.ts`). `IgnoreKeyword` on a card in play (the campaign card, the quest stage) is the scenario-level switch for Deep and Discover (`wilderland/markers.ts`). Mount Gundabad's quest deck is a quest whose stage 2s print `FlipAtQuestPhase` (`stages[i].deck`): the quest advances to the top of the deck, the stage defeated goes to the victory display, a stage arrives side A up and `unflippedStage` (`quest/order.ts`, read by `effectsOfCard`) keeps its side B text silent until `FlipCurrentQuest` flips it at the beginning of the quest phase; `wilderland/gundabad.ts` bypasses the current stage, searches the quest deck and flips Wormsbane in place, `gundabad-cards.ts` searches the Caves deck, stages a random discarded enemy and gathers treasures on one character, and `rounds.ts` notes the first treachery revealed each round and limits a player's plays in a phase. The Fate of Wilderland's `wilderland/assault.ts` searches the encounter deck, its discard pile and the victory display for a card, stages the enemies an effect discarded, takes progress off a card and answers `attackedThisPhase` (whether an enemy has resolved an attack in the combat phase now under way). Discover X is a Forced effect when a location becomes the active location, Loot a constant marker `startReveal` reads, and Guarded (enemy or location) on an objective is `GuardObjective` with `types`, which shares its search with the player attachments' Guarded (X) (`encounter/discard-until.ts`).

## island

`island` (the Island Map and Exploration of Temple of the Deceived, the Gate Key and the secrets: `src/rules/families/island/`, records in `src/effects/authored/temple-of-the-deceived.ts` and `pool/DCCE.ts`) adds the zone `islandMap` (a map location outside the staging area, in play; `syncIsland`, run by `settle`, moves the map's cards between the staging area and the map as the active location says) and keeps the map in `uncharted.island`.

## grapple

`grapple` (Grapple X, the encounter set a quest stage swaps in, the Stormcaller Elite's flip and its flip to an attack, The Thing in the Depths' campaign card and The Drowned Ruins' Sahír's Betrayal: `src/rules/families/grapple/`, records in `src/effects/authored/the-thing-in-the-depths.ts` and `the-drowned-ruins.ts`) adds the zone `grappled` (an enemy facedown on a location in the staging area: in play, outside the staging area, hosted by the location; `zones.ts` detaches it when the location leaves the staging area) and reads the Threat it adds in `modifiedStat`.

## dreamchaser

`dreamchaser` (the Dream-chaser campaign's XP: the Resolution verb `GainXp`, run by `src/rules/setup/resolution.ts`, with its counts in `src/rules/families/dreamchaser/index.ts`; what the log's marks mean to a game, a scalable card's level and an upgraded card's side, in `dreamchaser/facts.ts`; `GainXp` is allowed only in a Resolution record) has its records in `src/effects/authored/pool/DCCE.ts` for the Hard/Expert Mode card, and in each scenario's file for its campaign card.

## sea

`sea` (the ships, the fleet, the heading, the Sailing test, the Corsair deck, Boarding and the Stormcaller Elite of the Dream-chaser box: `src/rules/families/sea/`) keeps one optional piece of state, `sea` (the heading, the Heading card, the routes of the cards that never go to the encounter discard pile, the Sailing test in progress), and the zones `corsairDeck` and `corsairDiscard`; a game with no ship and no Stormcaller Elite card has neither, so no other game's state changes. A ship is an enemy or an objective ally carrying `PrintedCard.ship`; `moveCard` asks `sea/routes.ts` where a card really goes, `machine.ts` runs the Sailing test before the quest commitment and refills the Corsair deck, `events.ts` resolves Boarding as an engagement from the staging area is announced, and `combat.ts` and `legal.ts` read the ship rules. Its records for the Dream-chaser's Sailing line are in `src/effects/authored/families/sea.ts`.

## campaign

`campaign` (the Mirkwood Paths campaign cards' Setup and Resolution verbs: an objective ally put into play, cards set aside and attached later, cards added to the Campaign Pool on their hero, a prisoner named, cards carried to the next campaign, tokens placed on the campaign card and recorded) has its records in `src/effects/authored/passage-through-mirkwood.ts`, `journey-along-the-anduin.ts`, `the-oath.ts`, `pool/RevCore.ts` and `pool/TDoM.ts`. The Oath adds the limit `oncePerCampaign` (`src/rules/player/usage.ts`, `families/campaign/used.ts`: the use is a fact in the Campaign Log's `usedOnce`), the encounter family's `SearchCollection` and table flag `encounterUncancelable`, and a Response window at the end of the round (`src/rules/windows/triggers.ts`). The first quest card's own Setup and When Revealed at the start of the game run in `src/rules/families/quest/setup.ts`.

## burden

`burden` (the burden deck, the Ring-bearer's life, Flight to the Ford) and `south` (a second active location, the Ring-bearer captive under the Watcher, damage assigned among characters, The Ring Goes South) have no records file of their own: their records are in `src/effects/authored/flight-to-the-ford.ts` and `the-ring-goes-south.ts`.

## objectives

`objectives` (a Guarded objective with an encounter card attached, claimed by a player and attached to a hero), `prisoner` (a hero held facedown in the captive zone the south family also uses, Mendor captured with it, the rescue) and `dungeon` (Orc Guards made of deck cards, Iron Shackles on a deck, the group limit of allies, a staging threat raise) have no records file of their own: their records are in `src/effects/authored/escape-from-dol-guldur.ts`.

## caves

`caves` (The Caves of Nibin-Dûm: progress limits on the quest card, engagement cost 0, a location the first player may attack from the staging area, the excess of combat damage taken off the quest, a card removed from the game) has its records in `src/effects/authored/the-caves-of-nibin-dum.ts` and `pool/TDoM.ts`; the stage number is an amount, and a stage that arrives with 0 quest points is not met on arrival (`src/rules/quest/quest.ts`). How to write records for a family is `docs/agents/effect-authoring.md`.

## uncharted

`uncharted` (the Uncharted deck and its double-sided locations, the Grotto deck of The Drowned Ruins, Calphon, the bottom of a deck and the Campaign Setup of The Fate of Númenor: `src/rules/families/uncharted/`; the state `uncharted` and the zone `unchartedDeck`; its records are in `src/effects/authored/the-fate-of-numenor.ts`, `the-drowned-ruins.ts` and `pool/DCCE.ts`, `pool/DCCEDrownedRuins.ts`). A card whose two sides are two catalog cards (a Grotto location over its Underwater side, the Shrine to Morgoth) keeps the side it shows in `CardInstance.code` and the other in `CardInstance.other`; the Shrine, dealt apart from the deck, is paired in `uncharted.pairs`, and `uncharted.grotto` marks the deck as the Grotto deck, which the table names and draws as such (`zone.ts` routes a Grotto card, on either side, that would go to the encounter deck or discard pile into it, Grotto side up).

## aflame

`aflame` (Aflame X and The Havens Burn, Sahír's Advance, the Stormcaller Elite of Raid on the Grey Havens, Galdor's Counsel's level: `src/rules/families/aflame/`; the zone `under` hosted by the Burn; its records are in `src/effects/authored/raid-on-the-grey-havens.ts` and `pool/DCCE.ts`).

## stormcaller

`stormcaller` (Flight of the Stormcaller's area, a second quest deck of stages 2C to 4C beside the Stormcaller, the catch-up that wins, the progress that moves onto its stage and the commitments the ships and Corsairs hear: `src/rules/families/stormcaller/`) keeps its area in the sea state (`sea.stormcaller`: the Stormcaller, its current stage, the stages to come and the cards of the area) and the zone `stormcallerArea`, which is in play and is not the staging area; `settle` stands the area's cards in the staging area while the players are at the Stormcaller's stage and in the zone otherwise (`stormcaller/area.ts`), so no rule that reads the staging area reaches them and a player card effect cannot choose them; its records are in `src/effects/authored/flight-of-the-stormcaller.ts` and `pool/DCCE.ts`.

## pursuit

`pursuit` (The Uruk-hai's pursuit tracker: `ScenarioState.pursuit`, the verbs that set, raise and lower it, the amount X the quest stages read, and the condition `soleAttacker`: `src/rules/families/pursuit/`) and `captives` (the Captives of The Uruk-hai: heroes faceup in the captive zone under To Isengard! or an enemy, flagged `CardInstance.captive`, kept out of their owner's heroes and not killed, `values.ts` read by the `captive` pick filter; `src/rules/families/captives/`) have their records in `src/effects/authored/the-uruk-hai.ts`.

## fortress

`fortress` (Helm's Deep's Defense keyword, `mec112.defense`: `src/rules/families/fortress/`) answers the hook `questResult` (`resolveQuest` in `src/rules/quest/quest.ts` asks `familyQuestResult` first): while the current quest stage holds the flag `defenseKeyword` questing places no progress for the players, and the Threat that beats the committed Willpower is placed on the current quest through the active location, no threat raised; it keeps `ScenarioState.defensePlaced` (whether the stage itself took progress) for the condition `encounterPlacedOnStage`, announces `defenseQuestResolved` to the stage, bars attackers while the table holds `cannotDeclareAttacks` (the `combat.attack` hook) and answers `RemoveResourceTokens`, `playerUnengaged` and the pick filter `isSource`; its records are in `src/effects/authored/helms-deep.ts`.

## wizardry

`wizardry` (the Wizardry timing of The Road to Isengard, `mec112.wizardry`: `src/rules/families/wizardry/`) reads a Wizardry line as a `forced` record whose trigger is `wizardry`, so the line's printed timing in `data/` stays a constant: `play/hooks.ts` `triggerHears` lets it hear a location leaving play, the record's `subjectExplored` condition keeps it to an explored location (the staging area as well as the active location), and `TriggerWizardry` runs the same record for Machines of Isengard (the active location's) and for Saruman (a shadow card's, with `asOnlyPlayer`: each `ForEachPlayer` runs once for the acting player, `eachSeat` becomes the actor and the number of players is 1). `shadowWizardry` is announced about the attacking enemy for each face-up shadow card with a Wizardry line, as the shadow step ends (`combat/shadow.ts`, `wizardry/shadow.ts`), and the family keeps the progress placed on the current quest this phase while the table holds `trackQuestProgress` (`quest.ts` `putOnStage`) for `CancelPhaseProgress`.

## harad

`harad` (Journey to the Cross-roads, `mec112.setup-journey-to-the-cross-roads`: `src/rules/families/harad/`) places an enemy facedown under The Black Gate (`PlaceFacedownUnder`: the zone `under` hosted by the card with that code, `placedUnder` announced), answers the condition `facedownUnder` and the Pick filters `lowestAttack` and `lowestThreat`; its records are in `src/effects/authored/journey-to-the-cross-roads.ts`. The Harad deck is the wilderland pile `harad`, and `AddFromPile` with `reveal` reveals its top card (Surge and When Revealed resolve); a lasting `ModifyStat` with `target: enemies` and a `trait` is held by the class `enemies:<Trait>` (`core/holder-classes.ts`), which `archeryTotal` reads too.

## grey-company

`grey-company` (The Passing of the Grey Company, `mec113.phantom`: `src/rules/families/grey-company/`) cancels a Phantom enemy's combat damage in `combatDamage` (`defense/excess.ts`, the one place an attack deals its damage to a character) and raises the defending player's threat by as much, unless the enemy holds `losesPhantom`; it also answers the amounts `highestThreat` and `playersAtThreat`, the seat flag `cannotPlayCards` (the `play` hook), `DiscardHand` and the Travel cost `RemoveProgressFromQuest`. Phantom is a printed keyword (`keywords.phantom`, `src/catalog/keyword-line.ts`). A copy of Overcome by Fear on a player's threat dial is a card in that seat's play area under its control, attached to no card; the attachments family attaches and detaches it (`AttachToDial`, `DetachFromDial`, `RemoveSetAside`, the conditions `onDial` and `canAttachToDial`: `attachments/dial.ts`), the set-aside copies waiting in `ScenarioState.setAside`. The quest family's flag `threatFloor` stops a threat reduction at the starting threat level (`quest/threat.ts`). Hand pressure is the deck family's (`src/rules/families/deck/pressure.ts`): `cardsDrawn` is announced with the draw's source and a clock stamp (`GameEvent.at`, so a card that enters play after the draw does not hear it), `abilityTriggered` is announced when an Action, a Response or a Forced effect of a card triggers, and the settle `sweep` hook eliminates a player with 0 cards in hand while the table holds `eliminatedAtEmptyHand`.

## mount-doom

`mount-doom` (the Fortitude test of Mount Doom, `mec113.fortitude-tests`: `src/rules/families/mount-doom/`) is a frame beside the Hide test's (`fortitude`, step by step in `fortitude.ts`: the tester commits heroes with `CommitFortitude`, discards the top X cards of the encounter deck with the discard pile shuffled back in any phase, a Forced effect of a card discarded for the test (`discardedInFortitude`, Gollum) hears them, the Sauron's Eye icons (`keywords.eyeIcons`, read from the ledger) are counted, the players take actions, and the committed Willpower must be greater than the icons); its result is heard as `fortitudeFailed` or `fortitudePassed`, kept for the round in `ScenarioState.fortitudeFailures` and on the running effect for `lastFortitudeFailed` and `lastFortitudePassed`. The same family marks the first treachery of each quest phase (`FirstTreacheryImmuneSurge`, a `revealed` hook); the table flags `fortitudeEyes`, `noFortitudeCommit` and `noRefreshThreat` are read by the test and `src/rules/engine/refresh.ts`. A location a Setup makes active announces `becameActive` when its own Forced effect hears it (`announceSetupActive`), and the campaign family's `PlaceTokensOnCampaignCard` puts the recorded tokens on the campaign card kept in the game. A burden that functions like a player card (Poisoned Counsels, The Searching Eye) is the burden family's: `ShuffleCopyIntoEachDeck` (Setup, run by a Choose of the quest card's Setup: `setupRuns: "inChoose"`; `src/rules/families/burden/player-deck.ts`), the rest of the hand discarded by `DiscardEach`, the draw announced by `enteredHand`.
