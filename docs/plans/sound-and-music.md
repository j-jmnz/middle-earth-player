# Plan: the rest of the sound

The sound effects and the music are built and every effect slot plays (`docs/design-system-table.md`, Sound). Two decisions about them are open, for the user.

- **"VFX".** The first request named VFX, but its points were all audio. If visual effects are also meant (card glows, combat particles, phase transitions), they are a separate plan.
- **Hobbit score.** The Hobbit films' score would fit Mirkwood, Ered Mithrin and the dragon scenarios, outside the stated trilogy.
